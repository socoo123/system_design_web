30 秒开场、澄清与全部假设A 30-second opening, scope and assumptions

我先做一城外卖履约：支付已确认的订单保存为 pending_match，Match 从附近取候选，先过滤再比较到店路耗、负载和迟到风险。选人之后，在同一个权威事务里预占订单和骑手名额，保存 offer 与 Outbox；只有骑手业务 accept 提交才算 assigned。未接 offer 到期释放并换版本，旧 accept 被拒。位置常态每 5 秒上报，乱序按服务端会话与序号处理，过期点不能派单。高峰先保护已接单的履约，再按区收紧新准入。

I start with one-city food fulfillment. Persist paid orders as pending_match, recall nearby couriers, filter, then compare pickup road time, load and lateness risk. Atomically reserve the order and courier capacity with an offer and outbox. Only committed business accept means assigned. Expire unanswered offers, advance version and reject old accept. Report location every five seconds with session and sequence checks, exclude stale points, and tighten new zone admission while protecting accepted work.

澄清先问六件事：外卖还是点对点跑腿，订单何时算被平台接受，要不要看近实时位置，是否允许骑手同时带多单，接单目标偏准时还是接单量，取餐后谁能介入换人。这里选外卖、最多两份占用、准时优先。指派与抢单都是产品选择；本章用平台单人 offer 演示事务边界，不把它叫成某年的唯一标准答案。

Clarify food versus courier errands, platform acceptance semantics, live tracking, concurrent courier load, punctuality versus volume, and control of post-pickup handoff. We choose food delivery, at most two occupied slots and punctuality first. Platform offers and broadcast grabbing are product choices; a single-courier offer demonstrates the transactional boundary, not a universal yearly standard.

先把订单、位置与真实运力分开估Estimate orders, location and physical supply separately

下单平均速率 =500,000÷86,400≈5.79/s，乘 10 得峰值 57.87/s，口播约 58/s。一城订单入口并没有凭空变成百万 QPS。匹配时每单先召回 20 人再过滤到 8 人，首轮量是 57.87×20≈1,157 人次召回/s；路耗只计算 57.87×8≈463 个矩阵元素/s。一次请求可以带多个元素，元素数不是 HTTP 调用数；重试和跨区还会放大预算。

Average arrival is 500,000/86,400≈5.79/s; at 10× peak it is 57.87/s, about 58/s. Twenty recalled couriers per order give about 1,157 candidate visits/s, while eight route elements give about 463 elements/s. Elements are not HTTP calls; retries and cross-zone work add amplification.

位置常态 =10,000÷5=2,000/s，200 B/点给出 400,000 B/s=0.4 MB/s=3.2 Mb/s 原始负载。若临时变成 1 秒一票，则 10,000/s 与 2 MB/s，都是五倍。它会不会打穿 Loc Store 取决于压测出的预算、索引写放大和连接开销，不能单凭“1Hz”断言。硬上限负责挡住恶意或失控发送，限流器本身还要有骑手维度和整区维度。

Nominal location rate is 10,000/5=2,000/s. At 200 B, payload is 0.4 MB/s=3.2 Mb/s. One-second reporting raises it to 10,000/s and 2 MB/s, both 5×. Failure depends on measured budget, index amplification and connection overhead, not frequency alone. Enforce per-courier and zone-wide admission.

位置不必全量留盘。10,000×500 B=5 MB 仅是最新详情负载，尚未算索引、对象开销和复制；若常态连续跑 24 小时并全部留轨迹，2,000×86,400×200 B=34.56 GB/天。骑手在线数全天恒定是这个积分的最坏情况假设，现实按时间积分。履约行按 500,000×2 KB=1 GB/天，30 天为 30 GB，三份为 90 GB；attempt、Outbox、索引、日志和备份都另外计算。

Latest details alone are 10,000×500 B=5 MB, excluding indexes, object overhead and replication. Recording every nominal point for a full day gives 2,000×86,400×200 B=34.56 GB/day, assuming all 10,000 remain online all day. Fulfillment rows are 1 GB/day, 30 GB over 30 days, 90 GB in three copies. Attempts, outbox, indexes, logs and backups are extra.

吞吐预算要分别看四条曲线：订单创建、派单事务、位置写入、观看推送。3,000÷5=600 推送/s，×200 B=120 KB/s，这是用户出口负载；10,000 条骑手连接和 3,000 条观看连接合计 13,000 条，是连接资源。把全城每一个点推给每个观看者会膨胀成 2,000×3,000=6,000,000 点投递/s，正是订单级订阅要删掉的路径。

Track four rates: order creation, dispatch transactions, location writes and watched-point pushes. 3,000/5=600 pushes/s at 200 B is 120 KB/s, while 10,000 courier plus 3,000 viewer connections is 13,000 connections. Citywide point fanout would create 2,000×3,000=6,000,000 deliveries/s; order-scoped subscriptions remove that path.

履约入口：先有可恢复的单，再通知骑手Fulfillment: durable work precedes courier delivery

正常路径与权威Normal path and authority

用户的订单 o7 已有支付确认，Fulfillment API 用上游 order_id 幂等创建履约行。一个本地事务写 order(state=pending_match,version=7,match_deadline)、待匹配任务和 Outbox。提交后返回 202 与履约标识；同键重试读同一行，不复制新单。钱与履约跨服务的入口应靠上游持久支付事实与本地去重交接，不能只信手机传来的“已支付”。

For paid order o7, Fulfillment API idempotently creates by upstream order_id. One transaction writes pending_match, version 7, match_deadline, recoverable work and outbox. Return 202 plus the identifier after commit; same-key retries read the row. Cross-service payment handoff uses durable upstream payment facts and local deduplication, not a phone’s paid flag.

Match 领取待匹配任务，从 Loc Store 找附近候选，预占成功后又在 Fulfillment DB 保存 offer 与投递意图。Offer Gateway 这里合并画出扫描 Outbox 的 relay 和设备通道，职责仍是读已提交消息、重投、记录检查点。AWS 的 Outbox 原文说明了双写间隙与消费者去重要求 [5]。队列挂了，意图留在库里；进程重启不会把订单吞掉。

Match takes recoverable work, queries nearby candidates and commits offer plus delivery intent in Fulfillment DB. Offer Gateway combines the outbox relay and device channel visually: read committed messages, retry and checkpoint. The AWS outbox reference explains the dual-write gap and consumer deduplication [5]. A channel outage leaves the intent recoverable.

为什么分开状态，失败怎样恢复Why states differ, and how recovery works

202 的成功语义是“平台有一笔待处理履约”，不能同时写成“骑手已接单”。Gateway 发到设备，甚至收到传输 ACK，也只知道这一层收到了消息。用户看的是订单的当前业务状态，骑手查看的是当前 offer 列表。离线设备回来先查询当前快照，再处理仍有效的消息；通知里携带的订单地址也要避免在锁屏里暴露完整隐私。

202 means the platform owns recoverable pending fulfillment. Delivery and transport ACK do not prove business acceptance. Customers read current order state; couriers read current offers. Reconnected devices fetch a snapshot before processing still-valid messages. Notifications should avoid exposing full delivery addresses on a lock screen.

替代是把 Match 放在创建请求里同步完成：实现少一个任务领取，但 routing 或骑手交互会占住 HTTP 请求，失败后仍需持久工作恢复。位置库也只能是 Match 的旁支，不能画成“订单→位置→派单”的串行代理。位置丢失可以暂停候选产生；Fulfillment DB 权威不可用则停止新预占与接单确认，已有实物继续由当前骑手保管。

Synchronous matching during creation avoids a worker handoff but holds the request across routing or courier interaction and still needs durable recovery. Location is a side query, not a serial dispatch proxy. Missing location can pause candidate generation; unavailable fulfillment authority stops new reservations and acceptance while current couriers retain physical custody.

最小数据模型Minimal data model

白板只保留四类记录：order 的状态、版本、当前 attempt 与匹配期限；attempt 的骑手、offer 截止点、终态和生成时权重版本；courier_capacity 的容量、占用数与暂停标记；Outbox 的事件键与投递状态。位置详情另有 courier_id、session_epoch、seq、captured_at、received_at、accuracy 和 cell。最新位置是可重建投影，当前执行者与名额是事务事实，二者不能合并成一个 Redis 在线标志。

Keep order state/version/current attempt/deadline; attempt courier/offer deadline/result/weight version; courier capacity/occupied/paused; and outbox event key/checkpoint. Location details carry courier, epoch, sequence, capture/receipt time, accuracy and cell. Location is a rebuildable projection; current assignment and capacity are transactional facts, not one Redis online flag.

候选与打分：最近的人为什么不一定赢Recall and scoring: why nearest need not win

召回只是把搜索空间缩小Recall narrows the search space

取货点决定邻近查询。按区域格子或 GEO 半径召回有限 IDs，然后批量读位置详情，去重，排除当前会话不符、时间过期、资质不符、暂停服务与已满载者。Redis GEOSEARCH 提供半径或方框查询 [3]，没有配送里的“还能带几份”和“接这单会不会迟到”语义。它按地理距离筛出的最近若干人，也可能在河对岸，或正在反向送已取的餐。

Recall bounded IDs around pickup through cells or GEO, then batch-load and deduplicate details. Exclude invalid sessions, stale, ineligible, paused or full couriers. GEOSEARCH supports radius or box queries [3] without fulfillment load or lateness semantics. A geographically close courier can be across a river or carrying another order away from pickup.

资质与容量是硬门限，朝向通常是软特征。把反方向一律过滤会把能短距离掉头的人也删掉；把过期点仅加罚分却又保留，最终无人可选时仍会派给离线人。图里的 Filter 是 Match 内的逻辑步骤，Score 也是同一服务内的步骤；拆出盒子是为了看清约束，未要求每一步独立部署。空间索引细节沿用 Ch19，本章多的是双边资源竞争。

Eligibility and capacity are hard gates; heading is usually soft. Hard-rejecting every opposite heading excludes easy turns, while merely penalizing stale points still selects offline couriers when supply is scarce. Filter and Score are logical steps within Match, drawn separately for reasoning rather than mandated deployments. Ch19 supplies the spatial index; here the new problem is bilateral contention.

统一量纲，再比较可解释成本Use common units for an explainable cost

候选通过硬门限后，Routing 估到店分钟数。Google Routes 的矩阵接口返回多起点、多终点的距离与时长，而且响应元素可能不按序到达，须用起终点索引归位 [6]。本章八个人去一个取货点是 8×1=8 个元素。短 TTL 缓存可复用同路段估值，但缓存键必须带出发时间段或交通条件；每个元素失败独立处理，不能把缺失时长当成零。

Routing estimates pickup time after filtering. Google’s route matrix returns distances and durations for multiple origins and destinations; elements can arrive out of order and are indexed by origin/destination [6]. Eight origins to one pickup means eight elements. Cache by appropriate departure/traffic context, handle each failed element and never treat a missing duration as zero.

教学成本 J=到店分钟+4×迟到风险+2×负载。风险是这单及手上已有单的估计受影响比例，取 0 到 1；系数 4 表示分钟等价惩罚，负载系数 2 表示每个已有占用的分钟惩罚。A 到店 2 分钟、风险 0.5、负载 1，J=2+2+2=6；B 到店 4 分钟、风险 0.1、负载 0，J=4+0.4+0=4.4，因此选 B。风险校准与系数都是产品假设，不能宣称这是某厂模型。

Teaching cost J is pickup minutes+4×late risk+2×load. Risk lies in [0,1] and estimates harm to this order and existing commitments; weights convert risk and load to minute-equivalent penalties. A=(2,0.5,1) costs 6; B=(4,0.1,0) costs 4.4, so choose B. Risk calibration and coefficients are assumptions, not a company model.

公平也要有操作性。只追最小 J 会反复挑同一人，差区和远单被饿住；可以在仍满足准时与容量约束的候选中，用等待时间作同分规则，并把长时间未匹配单加优先级。另一种是 A4 的一秒短窗，把多单与多人批量配对，避免先到的一单占掉后面唯一可行的人。代价是最多多等这一秒、计算更复杂；窗口大小要计入 120 秒匹配期限。

Fairness needs an operational rule. Always minimizing J can starve couriers or difficult orders. Tie-break by idle time within capacity/punctuality constraints and prioritize old pending orders. A one-second batch can avoid a greedy order taking a later order’s only feasible courier, at the cost of delay and more computation within the 120 s match deadline.

打分结果仍要经过事务A score is still subject to a transaction

B 的空名额可能在路耗请求期间被另一单占走。预占时锁住订单和对应 courier_capacity，重验可派状态、version、当前 attempt、暂停标志与 occupied<capacity；用确定的锁顺序降低死锁，冲突后重新召回或选次优。行锁只保护所有更新者都使用同一规则的资源 [1]；“每区一个 Match”不能保护跨区借来的同一个 B。预约、accept、到期和完成都必须经过这个容量权威。

B’s free slot can disappear during routing. Lock the order and courier capacity, recheck state/version/current attempt/pause/occupied<capacity, use a consistent lock order and rematch after conflict. Row locks protect resources whose writers follow the same rule [1]. One Match per zone does not protect a borrowed B; all reservation and release paths use the same authority.

广播抢单把选择交给骑手，可以提高自主性和实现简单度，但不是“先到请求直接 SET assigned”。广播给 N 人会把通知量放大 N 倍；无论谁抢，最终仍原子预占双方。图默认一次单人 offer，减少通知与竞争；平台指派也不能自动保证全局最优。要用接单率、完成率、等待时长和不同区的分布验证选择，而不是凭“这是新答案”认定优劣。

Broadcast grabbing gives couriers agency and a simpler initial choice, but a first-arriving request cannot blindly set assigned. Broadcasting to N couriers multiplies delivery by N; the winner still atomically reserves both resources. Single-person offers reduce fanout and contention without guaranteeing a global optimum. Evaluate acceptance, completion and waiting distributions.

offer、accept 与到期：谁赢由同一笔事务决定Offer, acceptance and expiry serialize together

预占与业务接单的边界Reservation versus business acceptance

预占事务把 order o7 从 pending_match 改成 offered，创建 attempt a1(v7,B,deadline)，B 的占用加一，同事务写 offer Outbox。订单当前指针唯一，attempt 主键与事件键有唯一约束 [2]，容量行检查 0≤occupied≤capacity。容量占用包含 offered、assigned、picked_up；若原来是 0，offer 后是 1，accept 后仍是 1，完成后才回 0。给 accept 再加一次会让同一单虚占两个名额。

Reservation transitions o7 to offered, creates a1(v7,B,deadline), increments B’s occupancy and writes outbox atomically. Current order pointer, attempt and event uniqueness [2] plus 0≤occupied≤capacity constrain state. Occupancy includes offered, assigned and picked_up: 0→1 on offer, still 1 after accept, back to 0 on completion. Acceptance must not consume another slot.

骑手带 a1 和 v7 调 accept。事务先读已有结果：若同 a1 已经 assigned，就返回原成功，哪怕这次重试晚于 offer 截止点。若仍 offered，锁住订单与容量记录，读锁内实际数据库时间，验证当前 attempt、版本与截止点，然后提交 assigned。手机必须在业务提交成功后显示“已接单”；HTTP 结果丢失时查同一 attempt，不能换一个幂等键假装是新接单。

Courier calls accept(a1,v7). Return the existing success if that attempt is already assigned, even when the retry arrives after its offer deadline. Otherwise lock order and capacity, read actual database time under the lock, validate current attempt/version/deadline and commit assigned. Display accepted only after business commit; query the same attempt after a lost response.

服务端到期与迟到消息Server expiry and delayed messages

到期不是 Rider 主动发一条“我超时了”。服务端扫描 deadline≤当前时间的 offered，锁同一订单，再复查 a1 仍当前且未接受，才把 attempt 标 expired、释放 B 的一个占用、清当前指针、版本 7→8，并留下重新匹配意图。状态图的 expired 是 attempt 的终态，订单回 pending_match。Accept 先赢就取消到期效果；到期先赢，旧 accept 因指针或版本不符返回 409。

Expiry is a server scan, not a courier timeout message. For an overdue offered row, lock the same order and recheck a1 remains current and unanswered. Mark the attempt expired, release B once, clear the pointer, advance 7→8 and persist rematching work. expired is the attempt result; the order returns to pending_match. Accept first cancels expiry; expiry first fences old accept with 409.

PostgreSQL 的普通 SELECT 在 Read Committed 下只得到语句快照，不能靠先读一遍再无条件 UPDATE 实现互斥；锁定读或带状态/version 条件的更新才建立竞争边界 [10]。事务开始时刻也不能替代拿到锁后的实际时刻，长时间排队可能已经超过 deadline。截止点用服务端权威时间，不拿手机 ts 判定。多个到期 worker 可以重复扫描，但条件更新与同 attempt 的终态让释放只发生一次。

A plain Read Committed SELECT supplies a statement snapshot, not mutual exclusion [10]. Use locking reads or conditional state/version updates. Transaction-start time can be stale after waiting for locks; validate using actual authoritative time under the lock. Repeated expiry scans remain safe because terminal attempt/state checks permit one release.

重试原 offer 用同 a1，真正换人必须创建新 attempt 和新版本；两者不要混为一谈。B 拒绝或 offer 到期后短暂冷却，连续尝试最多三次，总匹配时间不超过 120 秒。三个 20 秒窗口合计 60 秒只是 offer 等待，召回、routing、队列与处理还要从剩余预算扣。工作领取的租约超时可以换 worker，但不能自动释放已 assigned 骑手，这是作业租约与业务归属的区别。

Retransmit an offer with the same a1; switching couriers creates a new attempt/version. Cool down B after rejection/expiry, cap automatic attempts at three and total matching at 120 s. Three 20 s waits consume 60 s before recall, routing, queue and processing. A worker lease expiry may change workers, never automatically release an assigned courier.

取餐是物理边界，数据库不能撤回实物Pickup is a physical boundary

assigned 后上报 pickup 或 delivered 都要带当前 attempt、骑手身份与版本，锁订单验证状态再推进；完成/取消与容量释放同事务，重复完成返回原结果。只有未接的 offered 默认自动到期。已接未取若要改派，应先控制撤销并核实未取餐；已取餐需要人工或双方确认的交接凭据。手机离线、GPS 过期和迟迟没送达都是告警依据，不能等价为“餐还在商家，可以重新发”。

Pickup and delivery carry current attempt/courier/version and advance state under the order lock. Completion or cancellation releases capacity in the same transaction; repeated completion returns the stored result. Only unanswered offered expires automatically. Reassigning accepted work requires controlled cancellation and custody verification; post-pickup handoff requires evidence, not inference from offline GPS.

位置写入：节流、乱序与新鲜度分开管Location writes: rate, ordering and freshness

设备会话与到达速率Device sessions and arrival rate

手机上报带服务端分配的 session_epoch、会话内单调 seq、captured_at、经纬度和 accuracy。重连同会话保留序号；重新登录或换设备由服务端生成更高 epoch。Loc Store 原子校验当前有效 epoch，并且只让更高 seq 更新最新点。仅比较手机时间戳会被时钟回拨、离线补传和第二台设备打乱；epoch 不是让手机自己挑一个更大的数字。

Reports carry server-issued session_epoch, monotone per-session sequence, capture time, coordinates and accuracy. Preserve sequence on reconnect; server advances epoch on a new device/session. Loc Store validates the active epoch and atomically accepts only higher sequences. Phone timestamp alone fails on clock changes, buffered uploads or multiple devices; the phone cannot self-select an epoch.

常态五秒采样兼顾匹配和电量；位移触发加密也要服从每人 1/s 的硬上限。静止可少发，但不能只有“动了才报”，否则活着的骑手也会过期，必须有心跳或静止状态采样。控制消息和位置用独立配额、连接池与工作队列，洪峰时优先保住 accept、pickup、delivered。慢客户端先丢旧位置，不丢当前接单动作。

Nominal five-second sampling balances matching and power; displacement-triggered reports still obey a hard 1/s cap. Stationary couriers need a heartbeat or stationary sample to remain online. Isolate quotas, pools and work queues for control versus location, preserving accept/pickup/delivery under bursts. Drop old points before business actions.

更新序号不等于采集时间足够新Newer sequence does not prove a fresh capture

seq42 即使比 41 大，也可能是网络恢复后上传的旧采样。教学 A6 假设时钟校准误差界 ±5 秒，保守年龄上界取 now_server−captured_at+5；超过 30 秒、来自明显未来或精度差于 50 米就不进入可派点。无法证明时钟误差界时，把位置标低可信并排除，而不是只看 received_at。这个门限用于工程新鲜度，不是对作弊 GPS 的物理证明；异常速度与风险审核仍是另一类检查。

Sequence 42 can contain a buffered old capture. With A6’s ±5 s clock bound, use conservative age now_server−captured_at+5. Exclude age above 30 s, implausibly future timestamps or accuracy worse than 50 m. Without a defensible clock bound, mark uncertain and exclude rather than trust receipt time. This is a freshness rule, not proof against spoofed GPS.

重复 seq41 或迟到 seq40 不覆盖新点，也不更新新鲜点的 captured_at、received_at 和有效期；连接心跳可以更新独立 liveness，但不能把旧 GPS 洗成新点。匹配时每次复查详情的会话、年龄、精度与业务在线状态，索引里有 ID 只是线索。Loc Store 的写确认只表示最新点已经被这一层接受，掉线后仍要重新采样才能继续参与匹配。

Repeated 41 or delayed 40 neither overwrites nor refreshes valid point timestamps or expiry. A heartbeat may update separate liveness without refreshing old GPS. Matching rechecks session, age, accuracy and business availability for every recalled ID. An index hit is only a lead; reconnect requires fresh sampling before matching.

GEO 成员与详情过期不是同一个生命周期GEO membership and detail expiry differ

Redis EXPIRE 是键级回收 [4]。给 rider:9 详情键设置 60 秒 TTL，不会顺带把 rider9 从另一 geo:zone 键删掉；给整张 GEO 键设置 TTL 又会一次删掉所有人的索引。空间成员由带版本的更新事件迁移与清理，跨格先把新格成员补齐再删旧格，容忍短暂重复并去重。投影短暂漏召回只损害匹配质量，绝不放宽权威容量检查；详情缺失或过期必须过滤。

EXPIRE reclaims a key [4]. A 60 s detail TTL does not remove a member from another GEO key; expiring the whole GEO key removes everyone. Use versioned membership updates and cleanup, adding the new cell before removing the old, tolerating duplicates. Brief omissions hurt recall quality without relaxing authoritative capacity. Missing or stale details fail the candidate gate.

索引更新 worker 用每骑手的 epoch/seq 版本拒绝旧迁移，保证旧事件不能把人搬回上一格；若分片投影不能原子迁移，保留短暂双成员并依赖详情核对真实格子。恢复时从当前有效详情重建，不从历史队列回放出五分钟前的人。位置层故障可以展示最后更新时间，暂停新候选；保住现有履约控制流比用旧点假装所有人都在线更可靠。

Fence membership updates by courier epoch/sequence. Across non-atomic shards, tolerate temporary dual membership and verify the current cell in details. Rebuild from current valid details instead of replaying stale historical locations. On location outage, show point age and pause new candidates while preserving fulfillment control.

用户轨迹与 ETA：新鲜点可以丢帧，履约状态不能Tracking and ETA: lossy points, durable fulfillment

订单级权限与快照恢复Order-scoped permission and snapshot recovery

Client 订阅 o7 前，Tracking API 先读 Fulfillment DB 校验订单归属与当前 attempt，再从 Loc Store 读当前骑手的新鲜点，沿 API 回用户。改派提交后重绑 attempt，位置推送里也带 order_version、attempt 与 seq；浏览器只显示当前授权的执行者。结束订单就撤销观看权限，不能留下一个永久 courier_id 通道让旧用户继续跟踪骑手。

Before subscribing to o7, Tracking API verifies ownership/current attempt in Fulfillment DB, then reads the current courier’s valid point and replies to the caller. Rebind after reassignment; pushes carry order version, attempt and sequence. Revoke tracking when fulfillment ends rather than leaving a permanent courier channel.

骑手需要上行位置和下行 offer，WebSocket 提供双向消息通道 [8]；原型也能用节流 POST 加独立下行，不必为了选协议改变事务。用户单向看轨迹用 SSE，标准有重连与 Last-Event-ID 机制 [7]，但它不自动提供完整历史回放。重新连接先验权限再取当前订单与最新点；履约状态查询要能补齐 dropped 推送，不能靠一条点流推断已经送达。

Couriers need upstream points and downstream offers; WebSocket provides bidirectional messaging [8]. A prototype can use throttled POST with separate delivery. SSE suits one-way viewers and supports reconnection/Last-Event-ID [7], without automatically retaining history. Reauthorize and fetch current snapshots after reconnect; durable status queries recover lost notifications.

观看推送只保留每连接的最新位置，慢连接覆盖旧槽位；3,000 个连接每五秒最多一票，约 600/s，不因骑手触发每秒上报而跟着增到五倍。送达、取消与改派事件不能被位置覆盖，要由持久状态和可恢复控制通知提供。若改用五秒轮询，也是 3,000÷5=600 读/s 的入口，还会增加鉴权与网络往返，协议选择需要按资源和产品需求比较。

Keep only the latest point per slow connection. Three thousand viewers at a five-second push limit remain about 600/s even when uploads burst to 1/s. Delivery, cancellation and reassignment use durable status and recoverable control events. Five-second polling produces 600 reads/s plus repeated authorization and round trips; choose protocols by resources and requirements.

ETA 是并行等待加串行路耗ETA combines concurrent waiting with sequential travel

未取餐 ETA 要考虑到店与出餐并行：骑手 4 分钟到店，商家还需 6 分钟，最早取餐时刻是 max(4,6)=6；再送 8 分钟，合计 14。直接 4+6+8=18 会把等待重复计算。多单还要加入已有取送停靠与新单插入代价，保留旧单的准时约束；餐已取就不再加一次出餐时间。ETA 的起点和当前阶段必须一致。

Pickup travel and food preparation overlap: 4 min arrival, 6 min preparation gives max(4,6)=6 before pickup; add 8 min delivery for 14. Summing 4+6+8=18 double-counts waiting. Multi-order routes include existing stops and insertion costs while protecting prior commitments. After pickup, remove preparation time and use the current stage.

Routing 失败时，先用仍新鲜且上下文适配的缓存路耗；没有可用值就返回 ETA 暂不可用或低可信区间。直线距离÷假定速度只给粗参考，直线距离×速度量纲是面积/时间，根本不是分钟。河道、禁行、拥堵和商家出餐都会让粗参考失准，所以它不能继续当硬承诺；位置已过期也不能因为“降级”重新进入 Match。

On routing failure, use only fresh context-appropriate cached duration; otherwise show unavailable or uncertain ETA. Straight-line distance divided by assumed speed is a rough estimate; multiplication has the wrong units. Barriers, restrictions, traffic and preparation invalidate a hard promise. Degradation never promotes stale points into matching.

饭点运力：有界排队、跨区借人与新准入Peak supply: bounded queues, borrowing and admission

真实供给比 worker 数更重要Supply matters more than worker count

A8 热区每秒来 20 单，当前能形成的可行派单只有 12 单/s，净增长 8 单/s；若开始有 600 个空位，600÷8=75 秒就满。12 指满足路线和骑手约束后的服务率，不是 Match 消费消息的速度。加两台 worker 能缩短 CPU 等待，却不能让已经满载的骑手多出名额，也不能把每小时的供给数字直接拿来和每秒到达相减。

A8 arrives at 20/s with feasible assignment service at 12/s, growing 8/s. Six hundred free slots fill in 75 s. Service means feasible courier assignments, not worker message consumption. More workers reduce CPU waiting without creating physical slots; normalize units before comparing supply and arrivals.

订单按取货区域排队，顺序以承诺期限与等待年龄综合决定，避免一直挑容易单。队列只是 pending_match 的索引，不能成为唯一事实；worker 丢失或热点迁移后从 DB 扫未终态单补齐。重复队列项按 order_id 与 version 收成同一工作，过期项读到已 assigned 就退出。每区单独配额、并发与等待上限，避免一个商圈占光全城数据库连接。

Queue by pickup zone and prioritize promise deadline plus age instead of only easy orders. Pending queues index durable rows rather than owning them; recover after worker loss or zone movement by scanning nonterminal DB work. Deduplicate by order/version and stop stale items already assigned. Isolate zone quotas, concurrency and wait limits.

从邻区借最多 2 单/s 的空闲能力，可行服务率变成 14/s，仍净增 6/s；只有把到达压到不高于可持续服务率，积压才不会继续涨。跨区的 B 必须使用同一 courier_capacity 权威，预约成本还要计入赶路；运力所属区改变不能复制新名额。持续跨区很贵时，改进边界和预测是后续选择，不要把广播全国骑手当救火按钮。

Borrowing at most 2/s raises service to 14/s but backlog still grows 6/s. Arrival must not exceed sustainable service to stop growth. Borrowed B uses the same capacity authority and accounts for repositioning travel; zone migration cannot clone slots. Revise boundaries/forecasts later, without citywide broadcast as an emergency shortcut.

准入拒绝与已支付失败分开Separate rejected admission from paid failure

新用户先取短期运力报价，再进入支付与履约提交；报价过期要重验。超限在尚未承诺时返回“当前运力不足”或 429，RFC 6585 定义 429 为请求过多，并允许 Retry-After [9]；运力业务失败也可以用明确产品状态，不能把所有无骑手都说成网络限流。报价与支付之间仍会竞争，严要求可预留有期限的准入额度，但这个额度也不是某个骑手的物理名额。

Obtain a short-lived capacity quote before payment and revalidate at submission. Before commitment, reject with a supply message or 429; RFC 6585 defines Too Many Requests with optional Retry-After [9]. A business supply failure can use its own state rather than masquerade as network throttling. Quote/payment races remain; a timed admission reservation is still distinct from a courier slot.

若钱已经确认而履约创建失败，上游保留 payment/order_id 对应的待补偿事实，下游幂等查询是否已有履约：存在就继续，不存在且决定撤销才发固定补偿键。进入 pending 的存量单，超过 120 秒或三次尝试仍失败，要持久写 failure 与通知/退款意图，让 Ch24 的资金流程接手。不能对已接单的用户只回 429 就把订单丢掉，也不能在是否创建未知时立即退款又继续派单。

If payment is confirmed but creation fails, upstream preserves a compensation fact and idempotently checks fulfillment. Continue existing work, or compensate with a fixed key once absence/cancellation is established. Pending work failing its 120 s/three-attempt bound persists failure plus notification/refund intent for Ch24. Never drop accepted orders with 429 or refund an unknown creation while dispatch continues.

降级顺序是减少非必要轨迹频率、限制 routing 并发、用可解释规则缩小搜索、拉长新报价的 ETA、收紧新准入；已承诺 ETA 改变要显式告知，不能后台悄悄改时间逃避违约。位置层失效则停新候选，订单权威失效停接单确认。故障恢复先处理最老存量工作，重试带抖动并受区级令牌约束，防止恢复瞬间重新制造一轮派单洪峰。

Degrade optional tracking, route concurrency and search scope first, then extend new quotes and restrict new admission. Notify changes to existing promises explicitly. Location loss stops new candidates; authority loss stops acceptance. Recover old pending work first with jittered retries under zone tokens to avoid a recovery storm.
