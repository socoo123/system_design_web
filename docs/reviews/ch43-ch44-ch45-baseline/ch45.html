1 · 限界上下文：同一个词，两个事实主人 1 · Bounded contexts: owners of different facts

中文

第一组图从一次结账请求出发。Order App 先在 Order Store 保存订单根、行快照与请求结果；需要跳转支付时，以 o42、金额和币种调用 Pay App。Pay App 创建自己的支付单 p9，提交 Pay Store 后返回托管 URL，结果经 Order App 回到用户。URL 是发起支付的入口，不能证明扣款已经发生。若创建支付单超时，订单仍可存在，调用者要查询原请求身份，不能删除订单就宣称回滚了外部动作。

限界上下文首先解决词义。Evans 的 Reference 把模型有效范围与团队语言联系起来；Fowler 的例子说明同一词在不同业务范围可有不同模型。[1][4] 本例中，订单的 paid 是对履约状态的判断；支付的 captured 是资金捕获事实；reconciled 是后来与渠道记录核对的结果。不能把“已收款”定义成“已完成日切对账”，否则所有成功支付都得等账单到来才成立，也不能只说“贷了一笔”而忽略复式配平。

第二问是生命周期。草稿可以改行，结账后价格冻结；未付订单会到期关闭，已付订单进入履约。支付单在订单关掉之后仍要接受渠道通知、记录退款与对账差异，因此不能让 Order 对象的销毁带着 Payment 的审计证据一起消失。生命周期差异是边界候选，不是机械拆分算法；最终要让业务专家确认谁能作出哪个决定，以及这两个事实允许不同多久。

第三问是不变量主人。Order 守总额、合法迁移与接受哪笔付款；Pay 守支付身份、配平分录与退款余额。共享 p9 和 o42 只是相关性，不能让支付团队拿订单表凭据直接 UPDATE paid。更细的权限边界是：入口验证租户与操作权限，合同携带受验证的关联身份，各模块对自己的写接口负责。把命令参数里的 order_id 当作充分授权，会让模型边界形同虚设。

上下文可先是同一部署内的两个模块，分别维护规则与写接口。只读报告按受控合同 JOIN，不等于可以跨模块改表；JOIN 并非 DDD 一律禁止的语法。独立部署时再补协议、恢复和兼容。按 User、Address、OrderItem 每个类拆服务，可能把同一不变量撕过网络。

同步调用的代价要从路径算：A5 接入往返 20 ms、订单事务 40 ms、支付调用整体 120 ms，串行条件预算 20+40+120=180 ms，余下 120 ms 给其他开销。它们不是三个 p99 可直接相加的保证，也不包括渠道真正扣款。若支付调用的尾延迟不可控，先返回持久订单和可查询的 payment_setup 状态，再异步补入口是可选方案；用户体验与失败承诺必须一起改。

English

Checkout persists Order locally, then calls Pay using o42, amount and currency. Pay commits p9 and returns a hosted URL through Order to the user. A URL initiates payment and does not prove capture. A timeout can leave both durable objects; query the original operation rather than claiming deletion rolled back a remote effect.

Evans connects model scope and team language; Fowler illustrates different meanings across contexts. [1][4] Here Order paid authorizes fulfillment, Pay captured describes money, and reconciled describes later comparison with provider records. Capture does not wait for daily reconciliation, and journal entries must balance.

Editable draft, frozen checkout, expiry and fulfillment differ from payment callbacks, refund and reconciliation. Closing Order must not destroy payment evidence. Lifecycle differences suggest boundaries, but experts must confirm decision ownership and tolerated disagreement.

Order owns totals, legal transitions and accepted capture; Pay owns payment identity, balanced journals and refundable amounts. IDs provide correlation, not authorization or permission to update another store. Validate tenant and operation authority at the interface.

Start with two modules in one deployment. Controlled reporting joins do not grant cross-module writes; JOIN is not universally banned. Deployment adds protocols, recovery and compatibility. Splitting each class can tear an invariant across the network.

A5 budgets 20+40+120=180 ms, leaving 120 ms for other overhead. These are conditional stage budgets, not summed p99 guarantees, and exclude the actual provider charge. If Pay setup has uncontrolled tail latency, return a durable order and queryable setup status, with a revised user contract.

2 · 聚合：把真不变量锁到一次提交 2 · Aggregates: enforce invariants at commit

中文

第二组图先画编辑命令，再把明细加到根旁边。总额等于各行数量乘冻结单价之和，合法状态限制能不能编辑，这些规则需要在同一次修改提交后成立。所以 Order Root 和 Lines 是同一聚合，外部写者经过根，持久层把相关行与根一起提交。聚合不是一张宽表，也不要求把所有数据一次无条件加载进内存；实现可以按命令需要取数据，但不能绕过完整规则去改一个子行。[1][2]

本例初始 total=8000，A 把第一行数量从 2 改到 3，根重新求和为 10999，再条件保存版本 v7→v8。价格从订单快照来，不能把今天商品目录的 3099 分偷换进昨天订单；金额不能在负数数量、不同币种或溢出的计算下照常保存。值对象的意义在这里很具体：Money 同时携带数值与币种，地址快照表达当时履约目的地，不靠一个裸字符串或浮点数去猜业务含义。

对象方法检查成功后，数据库仍可能被另一个写者改过。Vernon 的示例用版本保护持久对象。[2] A、B 都读 v7，A 先提交 v8，B 的 UPDATE 条件 version=7 影响 0 行，就要重载并判断命令还是否适用。只调用两个 Java 对象上的方法、没有持久并发控制，仍会丢更新。若选择行锁，则锁相关记录直到短事务结束，并准备死锁失败后的整笔重试；SQL 行锁与隔离的保证要按实现核实。[7][8]

版本冲突和重复命令是两种问题。A 已提交但连接断了，若用户重试“数量加一”，重新读 v8 再加一次就成了数量 4。k42 绑定租户、命令类别、对象 ID 与参数摘要，原子写入命令结果；重试同键同参返回已提交的 v8 结果，同键换参拒绝。验证失败可以明确返回未接纳，结果未知则查持久身份。幂等记录的生命周期要覆盖允许的重试窗口，资金侧的业务唯一身份更不能因缓存过期被清掉。

聚合过大通常来自导航方便：Order 引用完整 Customer、Payment、Warehouse，看起来方法调用少，实际任何改行都要加载和争用一整张对象图。Vernon 推荐外部聚合按身份引用，并指出读端可组合信息。[3] 聚合过小的症状则相反：明细和总额分两次提交，读者看到已经持久的非法组合。判别依据是提交时必须真的规则，而不是内存里有多少对象、数据库能不能 JOIN、某个团队是否有独立仓库。

“一次事务一个聚合”应当作为逼你解释一致性的默认原则。若一个真实业务命令必须原子改变同库两个小聚合，可以显式选择短事务，但要承认其争用与部署约束；不能把它伪装成 DDD 的普遍定理。反之，如果每次都要同时改十个聚合，优先复查是不是规则或边界没说明白。Repository 的重点只是让写入口保持聚合语义，不能给 OrderItemRepository 开一个随意 SET quantity 的旁门。下一组就用库存展示这个例外的成本。

English

Order Root and Lines share total and editability invariants. Writers go through the root and persist the related rows together. An aggregate need not be a wide table or unconditionally load every object; command-specific loading must still enforce all relevant rules. [1][2]

From total 8000, A edits quantity 2→3 and recalculates 10999 before saving v7→v8. Use the frozen price, not a current catalog price of 3099. Validate quantities, currency and overflow. Money carries amount/currency; an address value records the fulfillment destination at that time.

Domain validation does not prevent another persistent writer. Vernon uses versions. [2] A commits v8; B on v7 updates 0 rows and must reload/revalidate. Object methods alone can lose updates. Row locks are an alternative held through a short transaction, with whole-transaction deadlock retries under the actual database contract. [7][8]

Versions address concurrency, not duplicate commands. Retrying an increment after a lost committed response could create quantity 4. Bind k42 to tenant, command, object and payload hash; atomically store its result. Return the prior result for identical retries, reject changed payloads, and retain identity for the recovery horizon.

Oversized aggregates often follow navigation convenience into Customer, Payment and Warehouse. Vernon recommends identity references and allows read-side composition. [3] Undersized aggregates commit lines and totals separately, exposing an invalid combination. Commit invariants, rather than object count, JOIN capability or repository ownership, determine the boundary.

One aggregate per transaction is a design default demanding an explanation, not a universal SQL law. A real atomic command may update two small colocated aggregates, with explicit contention and deployment costs. Repeatedly updating ten suggests unclear boundaries. Keep repository write interfaces at aggregate semantics; inventory shows the exception next.

3 · 库存：同库预占是选择，热点是另一条约束 3 · Inventory: locality and contention are separate

中文

结账把可编辑 v8 冻结成 pending_pay v9，并以 k9 取得库存预占。在 A6 中，Order Store 与 Stock Store 是同一物理库里的逻辑持久区，Checkout 用一次本地事务保存订单、执行 available≥q 的条件更新、写入唯一预占和请求结果。图上绿色表示整笔结果，不能把“插入订单那条 SQL 已执行”提前当成提交 ACK。任何一个 SKU 不足、版本冲突或事务中断，整笔回滚。

库存聚合按 SKU 维护可用量与预占，Order 只持有 reservation_id、SKU 身份和价格快照。把它们放一个事务里，是“成功订单必须有有效预占”的工程合同；它们依旧可以有不同生命周期、不同命令和不同模型。如果部署后分成两个数据库，这个合同就失去本地原子性，不能靠同名 Repository 或普通 REST 把原事务的性质接回来。必须重新设计持久状态、准入与失败出口。

单个热 SKU 的争用不能靠更多服务实例消除。A7 排他占用 10 ms，忽略其他成本的乐观上限是 1/0.010=100 次/秒；300 次/秒需要 300×0.010=3 秒占用/秒，所以持续负载没有稳定排队解。把 Order Root 做成“全站订单和全部商品”会把无关订单也串起来。选择短事务、限额或预先划分库存份额，分别改变占用时间、进入速率或分配模型；最后一种仍需保证总份额不超过真实库存。

若业务选择独立库存服务，先持久创建可查询的 reservation 意图，调用者以同一身份申请预占；成功回执与过期规则明确后，订单再进入可付款状态。申请超时先查询原预占，不能直接再发一个新身份。取消与迟到申请会竞争，所以库存端需使“已取消”的身份阻止旧请求后来重新取得库存。只给预占加一个 TTL，没有取消栅栏和恢复扫描，会在用户关单后漏释放或重新锁货。

关单释放库存是可重复恢复的业务动作。若同库，可以与 Order 的状态决定一起事务提交；若异地，Order 本地保存释放义务，再按预占身份通知 Stock，Stock 幂等释放，结果最终追上。旧的 release 命令不能释放后来另一笔预占，重复命令也不能给 available 加两次。图只画 A6 的本地路径，远程预占是边界改变后的替代方案，详细补偿与恢复机制回 Ch18、Ch21 和 Ch41。

库存不足或冲突，由 Checkout 返回明确拒绝或有界重试。固定锁序、事务内不调用 PSP、整笔重评估可降低争用。[7][8] 回滚只撤本库未提交内容，无法撤销 Pay 已收款；下一组用持久合同连接两次提交。

English

Checkout freezes v8 into pending_pay v9 and reserves stock under k9. A6 uses one physical DB for logical Order and Stock stores. One transaction writes Order, conditionally reserves stock with available≥q, and persists reservation identity/result. A SQL statement succeeding is not commit ACK; failures roll back everything.

Stock owns availability and reservations; Order holds reservation and SKU IDs plus price snapshots. Atomic checkout is an explicit engineering contract while the models remain distinct. Moving them to separate DBs removes locality; repositories or REST do not restore the old transaction.

At 10 ms exclusive occupancy, the optimistic hot-row ceiling is 1/0.010=100/s. Demand 300/s needs 3 occupancy seconds per second and cannot sustain a stable queue. Larger roots serialize unrelated work. Shorter transactions, admission or allocated stock shares change occupancy, rate or business allocation; shares must conserve real stock.

With a separate stock service, persist/query the reservation identity, then make Order payable after a valid receipt. Query timed-out reservations before another attempt. Cancellation must fence delayed requests from reacquiring stock; TTL alone without cancellation state and recovery can leak or relock stock.

Closing releases inventory recoverably: locally in the same transaction, or remotely from a durable release obligation with reservation-scoped idempotency. Old release commands cannot release a later reservation; repeats cannot increment availability twice. The figure depicts A6 locality; remote recovery is the alternative covered by Ch18, Ch21 and Ch41.

Checkout returns rejection or bounded retry. Fixed lock order, no PSP I/O inside the transaction and full re-evaluation reduce contention. [7][8] Rollback cannot undo capture in Pay; durable contracts join the commits.

4 · 领域事件：从两次本地提交走出投递闭环 4 · Domain events: close the delivery loop

中文

领域事件表达业务已经承认的事实，例如 PaymentCaptured；内部事件可以触发本模块规则，对外集成合同则是一个独立、稳定的承诺。[1] PSP 回调进 Pay App，先按原始报文和渠道合同验签、校验支付尝试关联，再同事务保存 capture 身份、配平分录和 Outbox e17。账本的具体账户定义留在 Ch24，这里只要求同币种借贷配平和一笔资金动作有稳定身份，不能把对账完成误画成收款的前置条件。

直接改库再 publish 会留下丢失窗口：本地提交成功，进程死在发布前。先 publish 再改库又会出现未提交的幽灵事实。Transactional Outbox 把需要发布的内容放进同一个本地提交，由 Relay 从持久数据恢复。[5] 本例不依赖 Event Sourcing：当前状态和账本照常存表，Outbox 是投递意图。事件日志作为事实来源并重建状态，是另一种持久化决策，不能从用了领域事件自动推出。[11]

Relay 读 e17，发布给 Bus，收到符合持久合同的 broker ACK 后才标记原 Outbox 已发布。发布成功但 ACK 丢失，或者 ACK 到达后标记前崩溃，都可能重发 e17。图上红线停在 Relay：它知道要重试同一个事件，而不是换一个 event_id 冒充新事实。Relay 需要扫描未确认行和失败重试；若实现跳过旧行或只靠一次内存通知，就没有恢复保证。事务外发布也不会与本地数据库同时原子成功。

Order Adapter 收到消息，先校验合同与关联，在 Order Store 一次事务里插入 inbox、执行状态条件更新、保存处理结果；提交后才 ACK Bus。Idempotent Consumer 原文把已处理消息身份放进业务事务。[6] 若先提交 inbox，再单独改订单，崩溃后重投会被挡掉，却没有业务结果。若先 ACK 再提交，消息失去恢复入口。两处错误都不是多开几个 worker 能补救的吞吐问题。

同一支付身份还要有业务去重：渠道可能用两个不同事件 ID 描述同一 capture，仅靠 e17 会再执行一次。Pay Store 对渠道、商户与支付动作身份设唯一约束，并比较原金额币种；Order Store 对接纳的 p9 也保留明确结果。收到相同事实返回原结论；收到金额变化或另一笔支付，则进入异常或多收处理。事件身份表示投递项，业务身份表示钱的动作，二者不能混成一个“幂等 Redis 键”。

PSP ACK 是回调已接管，broker ACK 是持久接单，消费 ACK 是本地处理或隔离已提交；都不证明用户已看到 paid。异步化新增积压、兼容与查询责任。若不能容忍收款与接纳的差异，先改流程承诺，不能只说最终一致。

English

A domain event is an acknowledged business fact; a public integration contract is a separate stable promise. [1] Pay verifies the raw callback and attempt correlation, then atomically commits capture identity, balanced journal and outbox e17. Account mechanics belong to Ch24; reconciliation is not a prerequisite for capture.

Write-then-publish loses events after a crash; publish-then-write exposes uncommitted facts. An outbox commits publication intent locally for a relay to recover. [5] Current tables suffice here. Event sourcing makes the event history authoritative and reconstructs state, a separate persistence choice. [11]

Relay reads e17, publishes, and marks its outbox only after a durable broker receipt. Lost ACK or crash before marking causes redelivery with the same ID. Durable pending-row scans and retries are required; a transient wakeup or skipped row cannot guarantee recovery.

Order Adapter validates, then atomically inserts inbox, conditionally updates Order and stores the result before ACK. The idempotent-consumer pattern includes identity in the business transaction. [6] Separate early inbox commit can lose the change; early ACK can remove recovery. Extra workers do not repair either semantic error.

Different event IDs can describe one capture. Pay uniquely identifies the provider/merchant money operation and compares its parameters; Order retains acceptance for p9. Return the original decision for repeats, handle changed amount or excess payments explicitly. Delivery identity differs from money identity and cannot collapse into a transient cache key.

PSP ACK accepts the callback, broker ACK persists delivery, and consumer ACK confirms committed processing/quarantine. None proves the user sees paid. Asynchrony adds lag, compatibility and status obligations. Revisit the workflow if disagreement is forbidden.

5 · 跨线合同：翻译事实，不能复制对象 5 · Contracts: translate facts instead of objects

中文

跨上下文的 Published Language 是双方能稳定解释的合同，适配层让外部表达不污染本地规则。[1] 第五组图把 Pay Relay 发出的 v1 送进 Order Adapter；适配层不是一个万能路由器，它明确知道该合同可以触发哪个订单命令。内部重命名属性、拆分支付聚合，不应逼所有消费者同时发版。与此同时，合同如果改了“金额是申请值还是实际捕获值”，即使 JSON 类型没变，也属于语义不兼容。

本例 e17 至少携带来源、事件 ID、合同版本、租户、p9、o42、支付尝试、10999、USD 与支付版本 3。来源真实性和金额关联必须在可信边界建立，不能只看 payload 中写了 tenant_id。Order 确认该支付尝试确实绑定冻结的 o42、币种相同、金额符合当前收款策略，再交给根决定是否接纳。删掉金额与尝试只留 order_id，会让旧支付单或另一笔部分付款错误地推动当前订单。

“版本 3”只在支付模型里解释，不能拿它与 Order v9 比大小。发生时间用来审计，不是分布式全序；没有同一来源与实体的顺序合同，晚到消息不能盲目覆盖状态。Stripe 的原文明确说明 webhook 可能重复且不保证事件生成顺序。[10] 本章据此设计消费端独立判断前置条件，不声称所有 MQ 都会自动按一个 pay_no 排好，更不以“较新的时间戳”把退款事实覆盖成已收款。

未知合同版本、金额不符或关联找不到，进入持久 Quarantine，保存原消息、原因和处理身份，持久回执到达后才结束这次投递。不能把它当临时网络失败无限重试：原数据没修，重复一千次也不会变成正确金额。隔离队列必须有负责人、告警和受控重放入口；修正关联后重放仍经过根与原去重规则，不能为“修数据”开启无条件 UPDATE paid 的后台接口。

兼容变化可以从新增可忽略字段开始，但必填规则、币种单位和枚举语义要有演进约定。迁移期间消费者可同时理解旧合同与新合同，生产者确认旧消费者退场后再移除旧版本。发事件与发命令也要区分：PaymentCaptured 由 Pay 证明已发生；RefundRequested 是 Order 提出的意图，Pay 仍需校验可退金额并决定执行。把命令命名为 Refunded，再在后台慢慢退款，会向下游发布尚未发生的事实。

订单详情可组合双方状态并标明来源与更新时间，它不拥有写权限。浏览器跳转或旧缓存不能证明收款；显示确认中并查询持久结果，背后必须有负责收敛的工作。

English

Published language provides shared interpretation; an adapter protects local rules from external representations. [1] It maps v1 to a specific Order command. Internal refactoring should not force all consumers to deploy, while a meaning change such as requested versus captured amount is incompatible even if JSON types stay unchanged.

e17 carries authenticated source, event ID, schema version, tenant, p9, o42, attempt, 10999, USD and Pay version 3. Trusted correlation must bind the attempt to frozen Order, matching currency and payment policy. Payload tenant alone is not authentication. Order ID alone can misapply an old attempt or partial payment.

Pay version 3 is not comparable with Order v9. Occurrence time supports audit, not a global order. Stripe documents duplicate and out-of-order webhooks. [10] Consumers therefore evaluate preconditions; neither a generic broker nor a newer timestamp can blindly overwrite refund state with capture.

Unknown schemas, mismatched money or missing correlation enter durable quarantine with original input, reason and identity before ACK. Repeating invalid data is not recovery. Assign ownership, alerts and controlled replay through the root and dedup rules, not an unconditional administrative paid update.

Additive ignorable fields may be compatible; required rules, units and enums need an evolution policy. Support old/new versions during migration. PaymentCaptured is Pay’s fact, while RefundRequested is Order’s intent subject to Pay validation. Calling a request Refunded would assert an effect that has not happened.

Compose status with provenance/time without acquiring write authority. Redirects and stale caches are not proof. Expose processing and query durable results, backed by recovery work.

6 · 关单竞态：接纳一笔钱，与证明钱到了分开 6 · Expiry race: acceptance differs from capture

中文

第六组是领域状态图，不是省略网络节点的 RPC 图。结账冻结后的 o42 为 pending_pay v9，合法收款与到期任务都争同一个条件版本；最多一条迁移提交成功。收款先赢，进入 paid，后到的关单不能释放已接纳订单的库存。关单先赢，进入 closed，后到的回调仍要把钱的事实交给 Pay 记录。把 Order 关闭当成“PSP 没扣”会使资金证据和订单生命周期互相覆盖。

图中从 pending_pay 指向 paid 和 closed 的两条线是互斥结果。数据库条件要同时限制状态与预期版本，受影响 0 行后读取权威状态和既有处理结果。[8] paid 且 accepted_pay_no=p9，重复消息返回原接纳；closed 则拒绝接纳；paid 却又来了 p10，属于额外收款，不允许再创建一份履约。不要把所有 0 行都报成功，也不要把所有 0 行都丢到死信，那样会遗漏正常重复或真正的多收。

关单先赢时，迟到 p9 的处理事务要保存 inbox、拒收结果与退款意图，并维持 Order closed。否则先记“e17 处理完”，再从内存发退款 RPC，进程死在中间就有已收款、已关单、无人退款。退款意图带 p9、原因、金额与唯一身份 r17，Outbox 随同事务写入。它表示订单模块已经承担“把这笔钱交回支付处理”的义务，不能把这个动作当作一段用完即丢的日志。

这里有两条不同的不变量：订单不能在释放库存后复活进入履约；支付不能对同一退款重复返还，也不能退款超过可退余额。前者由 Order 的状态决定保证，后者由 Pay 的退款账本和渠道合同保证。跨上下文的补偿并不是把所有已发生动作自动回滚，退款会经历 pending、UNKNOWN 和 confirmed 等过程。若渠道暂不支持自动退，仍保留人工处理的持久工单与审计出口，不能把流程直接标成完成。

事件和关单扫描都可能重复。根用已提交处理结果判断，退款工作按 p9 的拒收身份唯一，重复 e17 不能新建 r18 再退一次。消息回放更不能重做已确认退款或发货；回放用途若是重建读视图，就隔离写者权限，不让投影代码顺手调用钱的 API。业务动作身份、消息身份和状态版本三件事各有用途：防重执行、防重投递、防旧写覆盖，缺任意一个都可能在恢复时翻车。

差异窗口由产品与资金负责人约定：已扣款但未接纳，展示确认中或退款处理中，并能按 o42/p9 查询。监控 Outbox、隔离和 UNKNOWN 最老年龄。A10 的 120 秒是吞吐估算，不是每笔资金都可等待的 SLA。

English

This is a domain state diagram. Frozen o42 is pending_pay v9; capture and expiry race on the same condition, with at most one committed winner. Paid blocks expiry release; closed does not erase later capture evidence in Pay. Closing Order does not prove the PSP never charged.

The paid and closed edges are exclusive outcomes. Predicate on state and expected version; after 0 rows, inspect authoritative state/result. [8] Paid with accepted p9 is a repeat, closed rejects acceptance, and another p10 is excess money. Treating every 0-row outcome as success or dead letter loses those distinctions.

After expiry, late p9 atomically persists inbox, rejection result, refund identity r17 and outbox while Order stays closed. Early dedup followed by an in-memory refund RPC can abandon captured money after a crash. The durable intent records ownership of the recovery obligation, not merely a log line.

Order prevents resurrection after release; Pay prevents duplicate/excess refunds. Compensation is a new business action, not automatic rollback. It can remain pending, UNKNOWN or confirmed; unavailable automation requires durable manual work and audit rather than a false completion.

Both expiry scans and events repeat. Reuse committed decisions and unique rejection/refund identity; e17 must not create a new r18. Replaying projections must not trigger money or fulfillment APIs. Business identity prevents repeated execution, message identity duplicate delivery, and versions stale overwrite.

Product/financial owners define disagreement windows. Show processing or refund pending with o42/p9 lookup and oldest outbox/quarantine/UNKNOWN ages. A10’s 120 s is a throughput estimate, not a money SLA.

7 · 退款与恢复：未知结果要继续有主人 7 · Refund and recovery: own unknown outcomes

中文

Order Relay 从持久退款意图和 Outbox 取 r17，经 Bus 交给 Pay Adapter。Pay Adapter 一次本地事务写 inbox、唯一退款身份和执行意图，提交后 ACK。这次消费者确认只表示支付模块接管工作。它可以在 ACK 后由持久扫描和执行者继续跑；没有可恢复意图时 ACK 就会丢义务。收到重复 r17，返回原工作身份；同一个 r17 换金额则拒绝，不能当成第二次合法退款。

调用 PSP 前先持久记录动作和参数，以原 r17 发起退款。成功回执返回 Pay Adapter，结果保存到 Pay Store，再由 Pay Relay 走前面的 Outbox→Bus→Order Adapter 路径通知订单。图上直接调用 PSP 的执行责任归 Pay Adapter，它包含持久恢复工作，生产实现可拆 worker 但不能漏掉这个责任。PSP 事实、支付本地记录与订单展示有三处确认，不能在最外层消费者 ACK 时全部写成 refunded。

退款请求发出后超时，PSP 可能已成功，状态是 UNKNOWN。重试必须遵循提供商的身份和参数合同，优先用原动作查询；合同不支持稳定查询时转人工核账，而非创造一个新退款身份。Stripe 的幂等文档说明同键保存首次执行结果，并允许至少 24 小时后清理，清理后重用键可能成为新请求。[9] 这个 24 小时是该接口的具体合同，不是所有渠道的通用窗口，更不能充当 UNKNOWN 自动过期的理由。

重复扫描也可能让两个执行者同时发 r17。执行租约和版本用于防止本地旧执行者覆盖新状态，外部动作仍要靠 PSP 端同键防重或可查回执；一个本地锁不能挡住已经飞到渠道的旧请求。只在确认实际退款结果后记退款分录与 confirmed，再发布事实。失败是确定拒绝时可以按业务规则重评估，未知时不能“释放全部退款额度再来一笔”。部分退款还要按 p9 累计已退和预占，保证总额不超可退金额。

恢复容量按净能力算。A10 新入 300/s，消费 600/s，积压 36000 条：净排空=600−300=300/s，追赶=36000/300=120 s。若误按 600/s 除，只报 60 秒，漏掉追赶时继续进入的新消息。如果消费者能力降到 250/s，积压每秒增长 50 条，没有有限追赶时间。加 worker 还受 Order 热行、Pay 数据库和渠道额度限制；不能把消息并发无限拉高又让同一实体的状态决定失去顺序和版本保护。

A11 的七天三份裸载荷为 1.08864 TB，不能因此把 inbox 和资金身份七天后统一删除。事件留存是允许回放的输入窗口，幂等结果要覆盖重试及回放合同，未终结退款意图要活到得到确定结果或被人工可靠接管。观测至少分开已捕获、订单已接纳、退款待处理、UNKNOWN 和已确认退款，结合最老年龄找卡住的一跳。DDD 的交付物是这些规则和责任可被解释、可被恢复；Event Sourcing、CQRS 或拆微服务都要另有约束支持。

English

Order Relay sends r17 through Bus. Pay atomically persists inbox, unique refund identity and execution intent before ACK, accepting responsibility rather than proving success. Durable workers/scans continue afterward. Return the original identity on repeat and reject a changed amount under r17.

Persist action/payload before PSP I/O. Store the returned outcome in Pay, then publish through Pay Relay, Bus and Order Adapter. Pay Adapter includes durable execution/recovery responsibility, which may be a separate worker in production. Provider outcome, Pay record and Order presentation are distinct confirmations.

A timed-out refund may have succeeded: persist UNKNOWN. Query or retry under the provider’s identity contract; lacking stable lookup requires reconciliation/manual handling, not a fresh identity. Stripe documents retained initial results and pruning after at least 24 hours, after which reuse can be a new request. [9] That is its contract, not a universal timeout or safe UNKNOWN expiry.

Two workers can race on r17. Leases/versions stop stale local writes, while the PSP contract controls already-dispatched requests. Confirm the actual outcome before booking/publishing refund. Re-evaluate definite rejection, but never release all held refund budget on UNKNOWN. Partial refunds require cumulative and held amounts within p9’s refundable balance.

A10 net drain is 600−300=300/s, so 36000/300=120 s, not 60 s from gross capacity. At 250/s consumer capacity, backlog grows 50/s indefinitely. Extra workers are bounded by hot rows, DBs and provider limits; preserve entity preconditions and version control.

A11’s seven-day payload retention of 1.08864 TB does not impose seven-day inbox or financial identity expiry. Dedup covers replay/retry contracts; unresolved refunds survive until resolved or durably handed to operators. Observe captured, accepted, refund pending, UNKNOWN and confirmed separately. Other architecture patterns require their own constraints.

Order 接纳付款，Pay 证明资金。根用事务与版本守总额和状态；Pay 的 Outbox 经翻译与 inbox 交给 Order。关单 v9 先赢就持久留 r17，PSP 超时保留 UNKNOWN，查原身份并发布确定结果。每条确认只证明自己的事实。
