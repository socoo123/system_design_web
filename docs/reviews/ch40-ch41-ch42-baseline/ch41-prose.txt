Primary independently streams WAL to A/B. Log position orders changes; an idempotency key identifies an operation. Async success does not wait for replicas and can lose unshipped writes if the primary and its durable media are permanently lost. [2] Receive, flush and apply are different. Durable but unapplied logs can recover during promotion while remaining invisible to reads. In PostgreSQL 17, remote_write does not ensure durability against standby OS failure; on waits for remote durability when synchronous standbys are configured, and remote_apply additionally waits for application. The setting alone does not create a synchronous standby. [1] 为什么等待：耐久与可用性必须一起定 中文 A2 故意采用串行教学预算：主库处理及持久 4 ms，再支付副本 6 ms RTT 和 2 ms flush，合计 12 ms。远端 RTT 换成 80 ms，就变成 4+80+2=86 ms。真实实现可把部分落盘与网络重叠，不能把这个加法当数据库 p99；需要同样事务体量、并发和故障域的测量。它只说明为什么主线里同城同步和跨洲异步会产生不同选择。 本章强耐久合同固定等 Replica A，A 不可用时不给成功，必要时返回不可用或结果未知并查询幂等记录。若改为“任意一个副本确认”，提升协议必须找到包含所有已确认写的可恢复前缀，不能随便选 B。MySQL 8.4 半同步等副本 relay log 落盘后 ACK，并不等副本执行；默认等待点和超时退异步行为都有具体配置语义。[4] 所以“开了半同步”不是端到端零丢失证明。 English A2 deliberately uses a serial budget: 4 ms local processing/durability plus 6 ms replica RTT and 2 ms flush gives 12 ms. An 80 ms distant RTT gives 86 ms. Real implementations overlap work and require measurements for matching transaction size and concurrency; these are not p99 figures. Our strong-durability contract waits specifically for A and refuses successful acknowledgment when A is unavailable, resolving uncertain results through idempotency records. An any-replica policy requires promotion to recover every acknowledged write. MySQL 8.4 semisync acknowledges a flushed relay log without waiting for execution, and its wait-point and timeout-to-async behavior matter. [4] 故障窗口：复制位点之外还要监测日志保留 中文 监测至少区分主库生成、副本已持久、副本已应用三个位点，并看缺口字节与增长速度。以 apply lag 报警可以保护副本读体验，以持久缺口分析灾难丢写，不能让一张“lag 2 秒”仪表盘代替两个判断。空闲期间位点没变化，也不能仅用最后更新时间宣判故障。 Replication slot 保留日志能让落后副本续追，却会把压力推回 Primary。PostgreSQL 文档提醒 WAL 保留可能填满磁盘。[2] 因此设保留与磁盘余量告警，超过可恢复窗口时重建副本并移出读池；不要把“永不清日志”当可靠性设计。三副本也不会替代备份：误删可同时复制到所有在线副本，历史恢复仍需独立的备份与回放窗口。 English Track generated, durable and applied positions plus gap bytes/growth. Apply lag diagnoses stale reads; durable gaps diagnose catastrophic loss. Idle positions alone do not establish failure. Slots retain logs for catch-up but may fill the primary’s disk. [2] Alert on retention/headroom and rebuild replicas outside the recovery window rather than retaining forever. Replication is not backup: accidental deletion can propagate to every live replica. 41.2 · 刚写就读：token、历史与新快照 [existing animation] 正常路径：只有应用屏障通过才发查询 中文 提交回执带 token，含集群与日志历史身份、提交位点；图中简写 T:1042。App 内的 Read Gate 选择同一历史的 Replica A，先查它已应用的连续前缀，再建立读快照。满足 replay≥1042 才执行这次查询。若应用了更晚事务而中间那笔没包含，单独看“最大序号”就不成立，位点必须具有连续前缀语义。 不要在旧 Repeatable Read 事务里等副本追平再重跑 SELECT。该事务仍使用既有快照；PostgreSQL 文档明确该级别后续查询看到同一事务快照。[7] 正确做法是屏障之后开新读事务，或者使用契约清楚的新语句快照。图里的“replay+fresh query”把这两个条件放在一起，查询结果经 Gate→App→Client 返回。 English A commit token includes cluster/history and commit position, abbreviated T:1042. A partitioned Primary may still accept writes. [3] Recover and replay an eligible candidate containing the required acknowledged prefix, promote it, then publish a higher route epoch and reconnect clients.
