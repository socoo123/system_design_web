30 秒开场：先固定契约，再选默认The 30-second opening: fix the contract first

我先确认 ID 用在内部主键，要求全局唯一、正数 64 位、趋势递增，允许空洞，峰值按每秒一万，部署两份。如果这些成立，我把 Snowflake 放在应用进程内：时间、worker、序列组成唯一元组，每号没有网络协调。接下来先证明位预算，再讲 worker 的独占与重启、时钟回拨。若要短码或不用墙钟，我换号段；若接受 128 位且不想分配 worker，我比较 UUIDv7；若要全局严格递增，我承认需要统一串行化，重新讨论延迟与可用性。

本章不是把所有方案各背一遍。题目里“有序”含糊时先问：是索引插入大致靠近尾部，还是后完成的创建必须拿到更大的 ID？两个跨机时钟可以相差几毫秒；即使没有回拨，较晚到达慢钟机器的请求，也可能拿到较小的 Snowflake。唯一性和全局顺序没有自动绑定。

“连续”还要另问一次。即使中心服务按 1、2、3 顺序发号，拿到 2 的进程先提交，拿到 1 的进程稍后失败，业务表也不会连续。必须无空洞的财务凭证，通常要在最终记账事务内编号，并接受串行化、失败恢复与审计规则。普通订单主键允许空洞，发号服务无需代替账本。

trace_id 也不默认套用 64 位订单号。追踪协议可能规定 128 位，外部资源标识还可能要求不可猜测。先看消费端契约，再决定长度、排序与随机性。ID 不等于访问权限，任何可预测标识都不能替代鉴权。下面所有流量、阈值、窗口与库存都是面试假设；位数及引用里的定义才是外部事实。

I first confirm internal primary keys: globally unique, positive 64-bit, roughly time ordered, gaps allowed, 10,000 IDs/s peak, two deployments. Under that contract I embed Snowflake in each app: timestamp, worker, and sequence form the unique tuple, with no per-ID network coordination. Then I prove the bit budget and cover ownership, restart, and rollback. For short codes or independence from wall time I consider segments; for 128 bits without worker allocation, UUIDv7. Strict global ordering requires shared serialization and a new latency/availability discussion.

Do not recite every scheme equally. Ask what “ordered” means: mostly append-oriented index insertion, or a larger ID for every later completed creation? With a few milliseconds of clock skew, a later request on the slower clock can receive a smaller Snowflake even without rollback. Uniqueness does not imply global order.

Gaplessness is a separate question. Even if a central service issues 1, 2, 3, the owner of 2 may commit first while 1 fails. The business table remains noncontiguous. Gapless accounting numbers usually belong in the final posting transaction with serialization, recovery, and audit rules. Ordinary order keys allow gaps; the generator need not become a ledger.

Do not automatically reuse a 64-bit order ID as a trace_id. A tracing protocol may require 128 bits, and external resource identifiers may require unpredictability. Start with consumer requirements for length, ordering, and randomness. An ID is not authorization. Validate CPU, contention, and pauses at the latency target. The layout allows 4,096/ms but guarantees neither four million actual IDs/s nor order-database capacity. It removes the necessity of per-ID central coordination, not the need for availability testing.

本地 Snowflake：发出 ID 不代表订单已经存在Local Snowflake: an ID is not a committed order

正常路径与唯一性证明The normal path and uniqueness proof

第一组从 POST /orders 开始。App 1 先调用进程内 Generator 1，取到整数，再向 Primary 插入订单，收到提交确认后返回 201。图上的 SDK 调用与返回是进程内边界，不是多了一台远端 ID 服务。注册中心不出现在这个请求里；业务库只负责订单事务，不负责每次分配 Snowflake。这个区别能避免把“零协调”误讲成“整个业务请求不访问数据库”。

唯一性的证明按字段拆开：不同相对毫秒，时间字段不同；同毫秒不同 worker，机器字段不同；同毫秒同 worker，受保护的序列字段不同。三项共同组成一一对应的元组。证明还藏着前提：一个 worker 只有一份状态，序列不会重复，时间字段不溢出，同一 worker 重启不能重入历史时间片。任何一项被破坏，位布局本身都救不了它。

如果单个应用进程里多个线程同时调用 nextId()，必须把读取 last、比较时间、更新 seq、写回 last 当作同一临界区。只把 seq 做成原子自增不够：线程 A 发现新毫秒并归零，线程 B 同时用旧 last，可能把新旧状态组合错。Twitter 的历史 IdWorker 用 synchronized 保护这整个状态更新，序列掩码对应 12 位。[1] 这说明生成器有本地状态，不能称为无状态服务。

Deck one starts with POST /orders. App 1 calls its in-process Generator 1, inserts the order into Primary, and returns 201 after commit confirmation. SDK arrows are local calls, not an extra remote ID service. Registry traffic stays outside this request. The database handles the order transaction rather than allocating each Snowflake. Zero coordination for minting does not mean a database-free business request.

Prove uniqueness by fields: different relative milliseconds differ in time; different workers within a millisecond differ in namespace; one worker within a millisecond differs by its protected sequence. The tuple maps injectively into the ID. Preconditions include one state machine per worker, no reused sequence, no timestamp overflow, and safe restart. Breaking a precondition defeats the layout.

Concurrent nextId() callers must protect reading last, comparing time, updating sequence, and saving last as one critical section. An atomic sequence alone cannot stop a new-millisecond reset from racing an old timestamp. Twitter’s historical IdWorker synchronizes the whole update and uses a 12-bit sequence mask. [1] The generator has local state even though it has no per-ID network coordination.

为什么选它，代价落在哪里Why choose it, and where the cost lands

按 A1，正数 64 位、允许空洞、只需趋势递增，Snowflake 的本地元组适合。业务要扩应用时，每个进程独占 worker 就能平行发号。收益是从 ID 热路径拿掉网络往返；付出的代价是身份管理与时钟纪律。若平台不愿承担这两件事，直接使用数据库自增或号段也可能更简单，不能因为“分布式”三个字就强制选雪花。

UUIDv4 是另一种本地方式，但标准格式是 128 位，其中 122 位用于随机数据，另有版本和变体字段。[4] 它的唯一性是优质随机源下的概率保证，Snowflake 是约束成立时的确定性保证。本题固定 64 位后，v4 不满足长度，直接排除即可；若题目允许 128 位，就应讨论随机主键的插入局部性与空间，而不是说 UUID 永远不能当主键。

InnoDB 的二级索引记录也携带主键，所以主键变宽的成本会扩散到多个索引。[5] 用 A10 做一笔仅载荷的账：二进制 UUID 16 字节比 8 字节整数多 8 字节，100M 行 × 8 = 800 MB；三个二级索引再多 100M × 8 × 3 = 2.4 GB，总差约 3.2 GB。这没有包含页头、填充、其他列与副本，也没有把 UUID 字符串的 36 字符表示当成二进制。随机写到底有多慢仍要在实际数据上测。

Under A1, positive 64-bit values, allowed gaps, and rough ordering make a local tuple suitable. Each app process can mint under its own worker. The benefit is removing a network hop per ID; the cost is ownership and clock discipline. Database auto-increment or segments may be simpler if the platform will not own those responsibilities. A distributed workload does not mandate Snowflake.

UUIDv4 is another local design, but its standard format is 128 bits with 122 random bits plus version and variant fields. [4] Its uniqueness is probabilistic under a good random source; Snowflake’s is deterministic under its preconditions. A hard 64-bit requirement excludes v4 on length. If 128 bits are allowed, discuss insertion locality and storage rather than declaring UUID unusable as a primary key.

InnoDB secondary-index records also carry the primary key, spreading a wider key across indexes. [5] Under A10, a 16-byte binary UUID adds 8 bytes over an 8-byte integer: 100M × 8 = 800 MB, plus 100M × 8 × 3 = 2.4 GB in three secondary indexes, about 3.2 GB total raw key payload. This excludes headers, fill factors, other columns, and replicas. It compares binary UUIDs, not 36-character strings; measure insertion performance on real data.

故障窗口：唯一主键与幂等请求各守一件事Failure windows: uniqueness and request idempotency

进程取到 ID 后崩溃，订单还没插入，这个号成为空洞，安全地丢弃。库已提交而响应丢失时，客户端重试若每次拿新 ID，会创建两张内容相同、主键不同的订单。唯一 ID 没有解决重复业务操作。应该按创建请求的幂等键保存结果；相同幂等键重新到达，就返回已经存在的订单 ID。它与发号状态是两份不同的正确性约束。

对外传输还要守住精度。RFC 8259 的跨实现整数互操作范围是 −(2^53)+1 到 (2^53)−1。[6] 一些 Snowflake 大于 9,007,199,254,740,991，经过 JavaScript Number 可能丢低位。API 把 ID 编成十进制字符串，前端需要整数运算时显式使用 BigInt；禁止转浮点再拼回字符串。后端 BIGINT 存对了，客户端显示或回传却变了，同样会造成查错记录。

A crash after minting but before insert leaves a safely discarded gap. If the database commits but the reply is lost, retrying with a fresh ID can create two equivalent orders under different keys. Unique IDs do not provide business idempotency. Store creation results by an idempotency key and return the existing order ID on retry. That is separate from generator correctness.

Preserve transport precision. RFC 8259 identifies −(2^53)+1 through (2^53)−1 as the interoperable integer range. [6] Large Snowflakes exceed 9,007,199,254,740,991 and can lose low bits in JavaScript Number. Send decimal strings; use BigInt explicitly when integer arithmetic is needed. A correct database BIGINT does not help if client display or round-trip serialization changes the ID.

位预算：算的是可用空间，不是压测吞吐Bit budgeting: capacity space is not measured throughput

每一段为什么占这些位Why each field gets its bits

经典正数布局是 1 符号 + 41 相对时间 + 10 worker + 12 序列。Twitter 历史实现把 worker 拆成 5 位 datacenter 和 5 位 worker，代码左移量分别是 22、17、12，序列取 12 位掩码。[1] 把两段机器字段合成 10 位，是编码等价的教学写法。只有真的需要按机房划身份，才在白板上拆成 5 + 5；32 × 32 仍是 1,024 个组合。

拼接公式是 ID = ((now − epoch) << 22) | (worker << 12) | seq，其中 0 ≤ 时间 < 2^41，0 ≤ worker < 2^10，0 ≤ seq < 2^12。字段超范围要拒绝，不能靠掩码静默截断。这里符号位始终 0，结果在有符号 long 正数范围内；时间或机器位越界会改变高位，破坏前面已经证明的元组映射。

单 worker 一毫秒可用 2^12 = 4,096 个序列值，范围是 0 到 4095，不是从 1 数到 4096。乘每秒一千毫秒得到 4,096,000 槽/秒。10,000 ÷ 4,096,000 ≈ 0.244%，看起来很宽；但一万次请求若全部挤进同一毫秒，仍超过 4,096。平均除法只能说明长期位空间，不能证明突发延迟或实现的 CPU 吞吐。

把一万次同毫秒突发平均分到两实例，各 5,000 次，超过单实例窗口。理想化忽略计算时间，各实例第一片发 4,096，下一片再发 904，需要 ceil(5,000 ÷ 4,096) = 2 个时间片。若全落到一个实例，ceil(10,000 ÷ 4,096) = 3 个时间片。两份部署仍须按最坏路由测，不能只报全站乘出来的八百多万槽位。

The positive layout is 1 sign + 41 relative timestamp + 10 worker + 12 sequence. Twitter’s historical implementation splits worker into 5 datacenter and 5 worker bits, with shifts of 22, 17, and 12 and a 12-bit sequence mask. [1] Combining machine fields into 10 bits is encoding-equivalent for teaching. Split into 5 + 5 only when datacenter namespaces matter; 32 × 32 still yields 1,024 combinations.

Pack ID = ((now − epoch) << 22) | (worker << 12) | seq, subject to 0 ≤ time < 2^41, 0 ≤ worker < 2^10, and 0 ≤ seq < 2^12. Refuse out-of-range fields rather than silently masking them. The sign remains zero and the result fits a positive signed long. Overflow corrupts the injective tuple mapping.

One worker has 2^12 = 4,096 sequence values per millisecond, from 0 through 4095. At 1,000 milliseconds per second that is 4,096,000 slots/s. 10,000 ÷ 4,096,000 ≈ 0.244%, but all 10,000 requests in one millisecond still exceed 4,096. Averages describe long-term layout space, not burst latency or CPU throughput.

Split a 10,000-request millisecond burst evenly across two instances: each has 5,000, exceeding one window. Ignoring processing time, each uses 4,096 slots then 904 in the next slice, ceil(5,000 ÷ 4,096) = 2 slices. All traffic on one instance needs ceil(10,000 ÷ 4,096) = 3. Test adverse routing rather than quoting a fleet-wide multiplied ceiling.

寿命、扩机器与 epoch 迁移Lifetime, more workers, and epoch migration

2^41 = 2,199,023,255,552 ms，除以 1,000 × 60 × 60 × 24 × 365.25，约 69.68 年；用 365 天算约 69.73 年，口播可说约 70 年。自定义 epoch 把时间空间留给上线之后，但所有历史与新 ID 必须共享它的解释。到期前要设计新的位布局或带版本的新命名空间，并迁移消费端；只把 epoch 重置会重新发出与历史相同的数值。

如果需要 2,048 个同时活跃 worker，可以把布局改成 1 + 41 + 11 + 11。2^11 = 2,048 个身份，同时每实例只剩 2,048/ms，理论槽位减半。时间长度不变。另一种选择是减少时间位来换机器位，直接损失寿命。必须把这笔交易写出算式，而不是说“动态领 worker”就消除了十位身份的上限。

位数改变会改变解码规则，也可能与旧数值空间重叠。不要在滚动发布中让半数进程用旧布局、半数用新布局却共用同一主键域。可以另建带版本的键域、用更宽标识，或证明新旧数值空间互斥再迁移。位布局是长期数据协议，数据库和日志里已经写出的 ID 比某次部署活得更久。

2^41 = 2,199,023,255,552 ms; dividing by 1,000 × 60 × 60 × 24 × 365.25 gives about 69.68 years. Using 365 days gives about 69.73; say roughly 70. A custom epoch reserves the span for service life, but old and new IDs share that interpretation. Plan a new layout or versioned namespace and consumer migration before expiry. Resetting the epoch alone can reissue old values.

For 2,048 simultaneous workers, use 1 + 41 + 11 + 11: 2^11 namespaces and 2,048 slots/ms per worker, half the original sequence capacity, with unchanged timestamp life. Trading time bits for worker bits instead shortens lifetime. Show the arithmetic; dynamic allocation does not remove a ten-bit namespace limit.

Changing bits changes decoding and may overlap old values. Do not roll out mixed layouts within one primary-key domain without proving disjoint numeric spaces. Use versioned domains, wider identifiers, or a proven disjoint migration. A layout is a long-lived data protocol; stored IDs outlive deployments.

短码边界：编码不会压缩唯一性Short codes: encoding does not compress uniqueness

6 位 base62 一共 62^6 = 56,800,235,584 个组合，大约 568 亿。正数 64 位上限接近 2^63−1，而 62^10 ≈ 8.39 × 10^17、62^11 ≈ 5.20 × 10^19，所以覆盖整个正数范围需要 11 位。并非所有 Snowflake 都从第一天起是 11 位：早期相对时间小，编码会短。长度由实际数值决定，但六位空间装不下它长期增长的数值域。

按 A11 的相对时间 10 年，用 365.25 天换毫秒：t = 315,576,000,000；仅时间部分 t × 2^22 ≈ 1.324 × 10^18，已大于 62^10，因此这个例子要 11 位。取后六位相当于把不同原值映射进同一个小空间，迟早撞号。Ch09 的固定短码用紧凑号段另做容量规划；不能把 Snowflake 截短后沿用其唯一性证明。

Six base62 characters provide 62^6 = 56,800,235,584 values. The positive signed 64-bit maximum is near 2^63−1; 62^10 ≈ 8.39 × 10^17 and 62^11 ≈ 5.20 × 10^19, so covering the full positive range needs 11 characters. Early Snowflakes can encode shorter because elapsed time is small. Length depends on value, but six characters cannot cover the growing domain.

At A11’s elapsed ten years, t = 315,576,000,000 ms using 365.25 days/year. The time field alone gives t × 2^22 ≈ 1.324 × 10^18, above 62^10, so this example needs 11 characters. Taking the last six maps multiple inputs into a smaller space. Ch09 sizes a compact segment domain separately; truncation invalidates Snowflake’s uniqueness proof.

worker 生命周期：领到身份只是第一道门Worker lifecycle: allocation is only the first gate

控制路径为什么不放进每次请求Why ownership stays on the control path

第三组图是一个明确声明的租约式身份管理示例，图上 START、ACTIVE、HALTED、RECOVERY 是状态，箭头不是用户 RPC。Registry 管身份归属和安全时间界 H，Generator 1 只在 ACTIVE 本地发号。可用 etcd、Zookeeper 或事务数据库实现登记，但工具名不是证明。真正要答的是：怎样保证旧 owner 与新 owner 不会同时生成相同时间、worker、序列。

历史 Snowflake IdWorker 通过构造函数接收机器与机房编号，并校验取值范围；这段代码没有替所有部署实现身份注册。[1] Leaf 的 2017 原文描述用 Zookeeper 持久顺序节点分配 worker ID，并缓存本机身份。[2] 两者都不能直接改写成“启动领一个临时租约，到期就随便给别人”。必须说明你讨论的是哪套身份策略以及恢复前提。

一种简单策略是稳定分配、禁止自动重用身份，重启仍在同一受控机器上。它把并发 owner 的问题交给部署纪律，适合规模可控时。代价是机器报废后留下占用，必须审计回收；扩到 1,024 个活跃身份就到布局上限。容器镜像不能携带一个共同的 worker 文件，克隆出的两个进程会同时以同一身份发号。

若选租约重用，就把续约当控制路径，给进程一个保守的本地截止点。A6 假设授权最多 30 s；Registry 不通时，当前进程在剩余窗口内可以继续本地发号，但过截止点必须停。进程暂停后恢复，应先检查有效期再发下一号。超时边界要计入允许的时钟误差与暂停，不是用墙钟 now 加 30 s 就自动安全。

Deck three explicitly illustrates lease-based ownership. START, ACTIVE, HALTED, and RECOVERY are lifecycle states rather than user RPCs. Registry stores ownership and safe time bound H; Generator 1 mints locally only in ACTIVE. etcd, Zookeeper, or transactional storage can register ownership, but the product name proves nothing. Explain how old and new owners avoid the same tuple.

The historical IdWorker receives worker/datacenter IDs through its constructor and checks ranges; that class does not implement deployment-wide registration. [1] Leaf’s 2017 article describes persistent sequential Zookeeper nodes and cached local identity. [2] Neither implies freely reassigning an expired temporary lease. State which ownership policy and recovery assumptions your design uses.

A simpler policy assigns stable IDs and prohibits automatic reuse, restarting only on the controlled host. Deployment discipline handles concurrent owners, suitable at modest scale. Retired hosts consume assignments until audited reclamation; the layout caps active identities at 1,024. Do not clone a worker identity file into multiple containers.

For lease reuse, keep renewal on the control path and enforce a conservative local deadline. A6 grants at most 30 s; if Registry becomes unreachable, the issuer can continue only within its remaining window and must then halt. Check expiry after process pauses before minting. Account for clock uncertainty and pauses; now plus 30 seconds is not itself a safe protocol.

失联、重启与复用：三个窗口分别守住Disconnection, restart, and reuse need separate guards

租约到期表示 Registry 可以撤销归属，不表示分区另一边的进程已经停了。新 owner 入场前，旧 owner 必须可靠停发或被隔离；必要时由落库端拒绝旧 owner 的请求。若下游使用 fencing token，那枚单调 token 必须有验证位置。标准 64 位 Snowflake 的十位 worker 并没有暗含一个无限增长的租约代数，不画消费端也不能声称 token 已经保护落库。

即使从不并发重用，也还有重启撞号。旧进程在时间 1000 发到 seq 42，随后退出；新进程拿回 worker 7，墙钟仍是 1000，内存 seq 从 0 开始，就会重用这个时间片。只在当前进程检查 now < last 没用，因为重启把 last 丢了。最保守的入口是拿到持久安全时间界 H 后，等新时间严格大于 H，才允许这个身份重新发号。

H 不能只偶尔记录“最近发过的 last”，否则崩溃前最后几毫秒未写入，恢复仍有洞。可选按批预留未来时间上界：先持久化 H，才允许在不超过 H 的范围内发；下一批先推进 H。重启等到 now > H。这个上界协议是本章提出的设计选项，图里的 Registry 需要可靠持久化，时间前借的范围也要有界。选择逐号落盘或换全新命名空间，同样能守正确性，只是成本不同。

分区时停发牺牲可用性，是本题默认的选择，因为重复主键会污染业务。不能一边承诺 worker 可即时回收，一边承诺失联的旧进程无限本地发号。真正不重用身份的策略可以弱化 Registry 的运行时依赖，但还要守住时间与重启。把这两类设计分清，面试官问“注册中心挂了怎么办”时才不会用另一个方案的承诺来回答。

Registry expiry revokes ownership but does not prove a partitioned issuer has stopped. Stop or isolate the old owner before admitting a new one; downstream writes may need to reject the old owner. A fencing token works only where validated. The standard ten-bit worker field does not contain an unbounded lease generation, so naming a token without its verifier does not protect writes.

Even nonconcurrent reuse can collide on restart. If the old process issued through sequence 42 at timestamp 1000 and exits, a replacement with worker 7, now still 1000, and sequence reset to zero reuses that slice. In-process now < last checks lose last on restart. A conservative gate reads durable H and admits the identity only when its timestamp is strictly above H.

Occasionally saving the latest last leaves unrecorded issued milliseconds before a crash. One option reserves a future time bound: persist H before issuing within that bound, advance H before the next batch, and after restart wait for now > H. This is a chapter design option requiring durable Registry storage and bounded reservation. Per-ID persistence or a new unused namespace can also protect correctness with different costs.

Halting during a partition trades availability for primary-key correctness. Immediate identity reuse cannot coexist with unlimited disconnected minting by the old owner. A no-reuse identity policy can weaken runtime Registry dependence but still needs clock and restart safeguards. Distinguish the policies when answering what happens if the Registry fails.

时钟回拨：等待、拒绝与逻辑时间各有边界Clock rollback: waiting, refusal, and logical time

正常、等时、回拨先分成三条Separate advance, equality, and rollback

第四组画代码决策，不画无关的存储集群。读 now 后与 last 比较，尚未更新状态。now > last，序列归零；now = last，序列加一；now < last 才进入回拨。任何分支都要遵守 worker 的独占前提。时钟同步服务能降低偏差，不能保证程序每次读到的墙钟单调；睡眠、调度暂停、宿主机迁移或人为校时都值得在设计里留检测出口。

演示状态 last = 1000、seq = 42，读取 now = 998，回拨幅度 1000 − 998 = 2 ms。若重置 last 和 seq，就可能重发之前的 ID。按 A5，小于等于 5 ms 时最多等 5 ms；等待之后重新读钟、重新比较。等到 now = 1000 时继续 seq = 43，直到超过 1000 才能清零。这一等号边界，比口头说“睡到时间回来”更容易暴露实现漏洞。

等待阈值与请求延迟预算要一起讨论。10,000 次/s 均匀到达是假设 A3，5 ms 期间约 10,000 × 0.005 = 50 次到达；两实例正常均分各约 25 次。真实突发可能远多于这个数，积压要限制，不能让所有请求无限占线程。回拨超过阈值或等待预算用完，就明确返回发号失败并报警，业务可以按幂等键重试到健康实例。

Twitter 原始 nextId() 遇到 timestamp < lastTimestamp 会抛 InvalidSystemClock；同毫秒序列回绕时，tilNextMillis 等到时间严格大于 lastTimestamp。[1] 本章“小等大拒”是一个额外策略假设，不能写成原版已有的三档行为。时间同步用慢调还是跳调，由实际环境决定；无论怎样，程序内检测、超时与重启检查都不能删掉。

Deck four draws code decisions. Compare now with last before mutating state: reset sequence for now > last, increment for equality, and enter rollback handling for now < last. Every branch still requires exclusive worker ownership. Clock synchronization can reduce skew but does not guarantee monotonic wall-time reads. Retain checks across sleeps, scheduling pauses, host migration, and manual clock changes.

Take last = 1000, sequence = 42, and now = 998: delta = 2 ms. Resetting last and sequence risks historical duplicates. Under A5, wait at most 5 ms for a rollback of at most 5 ms, then re-read and compare. At now = 1000 continue with sequence 43; reset only after passing 1000. This equality boundary catches bugs hidden by “sleep until time recovers.”

Discuss wait thresholds against latency. Under A3’s uniform 10,000/s, 5 ms sees about 50 arrivals, normally 25 per instance. Bursts can be much larger; bound backlog and avoid indefinite thread occupancy. Refuse minting and alert when rollback exceeds the threshold or the wait budget expires. Business retries can use idempotency keys against a healthy instance.

Twitter’s original nextId() throws InvalidSystemClock for timestamp < lastTimestamp; on sequence wrap, tilNextMillis waits for a timestamp strictly greater than lastTimestamp. [1] Small-wait/large-refuse is a chapter policy choice, not three original modes. Deployment determines gradual versus stepped clock adjustment; neither removes application checks, timeouts, or restart gates.

逻辑时间可以做，但要给出有限预算Logical time needs an explicit finite budget

第三条路是钉住已发出的 last，在回拨期间继续增加 seq，而不退回墙钟。它保住的是同 worker 元组不重复，并非真实时间戳。若 last = 1000、seq = 42，那么留在这个逻辑毫秒里的剩余槽位是 4095 − 42 = 4,053。按单实例 5,000/s 均匀到达，仅序列余量最多能撑 4,053 ÷ 5,000 = 0.8106 s；之后必须等待、停发或推进逻辑时间，不能绕回 0。

允许预借未来时间，就给借出的时间距离设硬上限，并把安全水位持久化。否则大回拨期间一直向未来推进，ID 内时间会离真实业务时间越来越远；进程重启后若只读墙钟，又回到已经用过的空间。下游如果按 ID 解码时间做保留、过期或范围扫描，也会受到影响。逻辑时间带来的吞吐和可用性要用这种消费端代价来换。

“预留一个回拨位”不是完整协议。一次回拨切到备用空间，第二次再切回去，若时间与序列重合，仍会碰撞；重启如果忘了已经使用的备用空间，也一样。要用新位表示命名空间代数，就证明代数何时更新、怎样持久化、何时耗尽和安全复用。面试默认选有界等待与拒绝，能把证明讲完整后再谈逻辑时间扩展。

如果业务必须与墙钟故障解耦，号段是更直接的替代：它依赖数据库中的已提交高水位和本地库存，不读墙钟来决定下一号。不过它把故障从时钟移到领段数据库，并没有消除所有依赖。下一组会把“库挂了仍能发”改成库存除流量的有限窗口，而不是一句笼统的高可用。

A third option pins last and continues sequence while wall time is behind. It preserves tuple uniqueness, not true event time. With last = 1000 and sequence = 42, the logical millisecond has 4095 − 42 = 4,053 remaining slots. At uniform 5,000/s on one instance, that alone lasts at most 0.8106 s. Then wait, refuse, or advance logical time; never wrap to zero.

If borrowing future time, cap how far ahead it can go and persist a safe bound. Unlimited advancement during a large rollback divorces encoded time from event time; a restart based only on wall time reenters used space. Retention, expiry, and range scans based on decoded ID time can also break. Price logical-time availability against those consumer costs.

A reserved rollback bit is not a protocol. Switching spaces on one rollback and switching back on another can collide if time and sequence overlap; losing the bit’s history at restart does the same. A namespace generation needs rules for advancement, persistence, exhaustion, and safe reuse. Default to bounded wait and refusal until the logical-time proof is complete.

Segments are a direct alternative if minting must be independent of wall time: they use a committed database high-water mark and local inventory. They shift failure dependence to the allocation database rather than removing it. The next deck derives the bounded outage window as inventory divided by traffic.

号段与双 buffer：用库存换数据库往返Segments and two buffers: inventory buys fewer round trips

先提交预留，再在内存里消费Reserve durably, then consume in memory

定义段表的 high 为下一个尚未预留的数，step = 1,000。事务把 high 从 1000 推到 2000，并把 [1000, 2000) 授给当前分配器，段内只有 1000 到 1999，刚好一千个。不同实例通过同一条权威记录的原子事务领到互不重叠的段。命名空间可按业务标签分开，但跨标签唯一若也是要求，就必须在 ID 里编码标签或共用一条全局记录。

只有确认事务提交，段才可消费。更新发出后响应超时，你不知道这段是否被提交，不能直接当未发生再发同一区间。可以持久记录领段请求的幂等结果；更保守的做法是丢弃不确定段，重新领一个确定的新段，让可能已提交的旧段成为空洞。两者都不能回退 high。崩溃未发完的段同样不回收，唯一性优先于连续。

按 A2 和 A7，总峰值 10,000/s、步长 1,000，长期平均领段频率是 10,000 ÷ 1,000 = 10 次/s。正常两实例各 5,000/s，约各 5 次/s，每段使用约 0.2 s。这是持续满峰、忽略段丢弃与启动预取的平均数；启动同时领两段可能短时放大，事务冲突与副本故障也需要测试。它说明数据库不必每号写一次，不证明所有数据库都能无条件承受业务写入。

双 buffer 在当前段尚有余量时异步领下一段。A8 演示剩余 10% 触发：一千个号余一百，本实例 5,000/s 时只剩 100 ÷ 5,000 = 20 ms；若领段耗时示例 30 ms，预取来不及，仍会出现约 10 ms 空档。至少要留下 5,000 × 0.03 = 150 个号，再加调度与尾延迟余量。可以提高触发水位，或增大 step；不能只说“用了双 buffer 就不会阻塞”。

Define ledger high as the first unreserved value and step = 1,000. A transaction advances high from 1000 to 2000 and grants [1000, 2000), exactly 1,000 IDs. Atomic updates to one authoritative record give instances disjoint ranges. Business tags can separate domains, but cross-tag uniqueness requires encoding the tag or sharing a global ledger.

Consume only confirmed committed reservations. A timeout after sending an update leaves its commit outcome uncertain; do not reissue that range as if nothing happened. Persist idempotent reservation results or discard uncertainty and claim a new confirmed range, leaving a possible gap. Neither approach rolls back high. Crashed allocators leave unused IDs unclaimed; uniqueness takes priority over continuity.

At 10,000/s total and step 1,000, sustained reservations average 10/s. Two evenly loaded instances reserve about 5/s each and consume a range in 0.2 s. This assumes sustained peak and ignores discarded ranges and startup prefetch. Simultaneous two-range startup can burst, and contention/failure needs testing. It removes one database allocation per ID, not all database-capacity concerns.

Prefetch a next range before the current one empties. Under A8’s 10%-remaining example, 100 IDs at 5,000/s leave 20 ms. A 30 ms refill still creates an approximately 10 ms gap. Leave at least 5,000 × 0.03 = 150 IDs plus scheduling/tail-latency margin; raise the threshold or step. Two buffers alone do not eliminate blocking.

数据库坏了，剩多少库存就只能撑多久Database failure lasts only as long as inventory

Leaf 的 2017 原文说明双 buffer 预取，2019 原文进一步讨论固定 step 与流量变化，并用 Q × T = L 表示段长、流量和消耗周期的关系。[2][3] 本章的 step = 1,000、触发 10%、30 ms 都是演示假设。不能把旧文“常用双 buffer”扩写成库故障时永久可用，也不能拿平均 QPS 调好的段长去承诺突发期间相同的容忍时间。

A9 用一个条件例子算库存：当前余 R = 100，若此前已经提交的下一段还有 N = 1,000，按本实例 q = 5,000/s，最多撑 (R + N) ÷ q = 0.22 s。若下一段尚在请求中，N = 0，只撑 100 ÷ 5,000 = 0.02 s。图中后续领段失败沿 Primary → App 1 → Next range 标明不能补货，先保留此前已提交库存的成功回程；只有库存耗尽，才把失败逐跳传回 App 1 和调用方。

要容忍该实例在同样流量下断库 60 s，至少需要 5,000 × 60 = 300,000 个已提交未消费号，还要留突发余量。两个每段一千的 buffer 装不下这笔库存。大 step 可以减少领段与扩大窗口，代价是崩溃时浪费更多号码、ID 跳跃更大。固定六位短码总空间只有约 568 亿，库存与空洞也要纳入消耗预测。

领段 Primary 切换时，高水位也要守住。若升起的副本没包含最近已经授出的段，新主会再领出重叠区间，应用缓存的旧段却仍在发。要使用满足“不丢已确认领段”的持久化与故障恢复协议，或切换时把 high 推到所有可能已授出的上界之后。不能把异步副本等同于安全发号备份；这条要求比普通允许读旧值更严格。

Leaf’s 2017 article describes two-buffer prefetch; its 2019 article discusses fixed steps under changing traffic and relates range length, rate, and consumption period as Q × T = L. [2][3] Our step 1,000, 10% trigger, and 30 ms refill are illustrative assumptions. Buffering is not permanent database-failure tolerance, and mean-rate sizing does not preserve the same window during a burst.

Under A9, R = 100 current IDs plus N = 1,000 previously committed prefetched IDs at q = 5,000/s buy at most 0.22 s. If the next range is still in flight, N = 0 and the window is 0.02 s. The diagram propagates a later refill failure through Primary → App 1 → Next range while retaining earlier committed inventory; only inventory exhaustion propagates failure through App 1 to the caller.

To tolerate 60 s without the ledger at that rate, this instance needs at least 5,000 × 60 = 300,000 committed unconsumed IDs plus burst margin. Two 1,000-ID buffers cannot provide it. Larger steps reduce reservations and extend windows but waste more IDs on crashes and create larger gaps. With a six-character short code, inventory and gaps consume a domain of only about 56.8 billion.

A ledger failover must preserve the high-water mark. A promoted replica missing confirmed reservations can allocate ranges still being consumed by old allocators. Use durability/recovery that preserves confirmed reservations, or advance high above every possibly granted range during recovery. An asynchronous replica is not automatically a safe allocation backup; the requirement is stronger than permitting stale reads.

顺序反例与方案切换Ordering counterexamples and switching schemes

App 1 持有 [1000, 2000)，App 2 持有 [2000, 3000)。App 2 先发 2000，随后 App 1 发 1001，观察到的数值下降，所有号却仍唯一。段内单一分配器序列可以 +1，多实例没有全局实时递增，更没有订单提交顺序。需要严格全局顺序时，必须让发号或最终提交经过共同串行点，并定义顺序按分配、响应还是提交来比较。

号段适合不想依赖墙钟、希望数值较紧凑的业务；Snowflake 适合本地发号和较粗的时间排序；UUIDv7 在接受 128 位时提供时间前缀与随机空间。RFC 9562 在 2024 年定义 v7 的高 48 位 Unix 毫秒及随机 / 可选单调字段。[4] 时间前缀改善局部性，并不凭空建立跨进程全局顺序。选择表只用来回到契约，不能把三套系统全部画成必须并行部署。

App 1 owns [1000, 2000) and App 2 owns [2000, 3000). App 2 issues 2000 before App 1 issues 1001: values decrease while remaining unique. A range increments locally, not globally in real time or commit order. Strict global order requires shared serialization of allocation or final commit and a definition of which order matters.

Segments suit clock-independent compact integers; Snowflake suits local minting with approximate time order; UUIDv7 offers a time prefix and randomness when 128 bits are acceptable. RFC 9562 (2024) defines v7’s high 48 Unix-millisecond bits and random/optional monotonic fields. [4] A time prefix improves locality without creating cross-process global order. The comparison selects one design from requirements, not three mandatory systems.

“ID 唯一所以请求不会重复”“Unique ID makes a request idempotent”响应丢失后重试可创建不同 ID 的同一业务；用请求幂等键返回原结果。Lost replies can produce equivalent operations under new IDs; an idempotency key returns the original result.

Flickr 在 2010 年的 Ticket 原文明确用两台服务器分奇偶号，auto-increment-increment = 2，offset 分别为 1 和 2。[7] 所以本题可以说单中心写是网络与容量依赖，不能说历史 Flickr 只有单台。机器扩展时重分步长、控制重复空间，也不是无成本。小业务直接用数据库自增完全合理，只有本题已固定的低协调目标才推动本地发号。

合上页面时用一分钟收尾：我的默认是本地 Snowflake，因为只要唯一、正数 64 位和趋势递增；不是全局严格递增。位空间 4,096/ms，寿命约 69.68 年。正确性守住 worker 独占、回拨检测、序列耗尽与重启水位。号段替代墙钟依赖，但故障窗口只由已提交库存决定。客户端用字符串传大整数，创建请求用幂等键。每条话都应该能回指一条箭头或一个算式。

下一步把两个边界接到后续章：短码的编码空间与领段消耗见 Ch09；身份撤销后旧进程怎样停止落库，是 Ch08 分布式锁要讲的 fencing 问题。能生成不重复整数，是业务一致性的一个构件；不能据此承诺订单恰好创建一次、全局按时间提交或任何故障都继续接写。

Flickr’s 2010 Ticket article explicitly uses two servers for odd/even IDs, auto-increment-increment = 2 and offsets 1/2. [7] Per-ID central writes introduce network/capacity dependence, but historical Flickr was not a one-server deployment. Changing stride or reassigning spaces also has cost. Database auto-increment can be appropriate for a small workload; this chapter’s low-coordination contract motivates local minting.

Close in one minute: local Snowflake fits unique positive 64-bit roughly ordered IDs, not strict global order. The layout provides 4,096/ms and about 69.68 years. Guard exclusive ownership, rollback, exhaustion, and restart. Segments replace wall-clock dependence but survive only on committed inventory. Transport large integers as strings and use idempotency keys for creation. Each statement should point to an arrow or a calculation.

Continue with Ch09 for short-code space and segment consumption. Preventing an old owner from writing after revocation is the fencing question in Ch08. A unique integer is one consistency component; it does not guarantee one business operation, globally time-ordered commits, or unlimited write availability.
