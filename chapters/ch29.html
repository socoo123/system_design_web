中文

逐步乘：2 × 32 = 64，64 × 8 = 512，512 × 128 = 65,536，65,536 × 2 = 131,072。2 是 K 和 V。8 个 KV head 是 GQA 式的假设；如果 32 个头都存 K/V，这个数乘 4。权重另算：假设 8×109 参数、每参数 2 字节，是 1.6×1010 字节约 14.9 GiB，请求进来之后不变。量化如果只压权重，KV 公式里的 b 不动，准入上限就不动。

准入不按平均输出赌，因为输出长度事先未知。单请求按 prompt 加上 max_tokens 预留：2,048 + 512 = 2,560 token。2,560 × 131,072 = 335,544,320 字节 = 320 MiB。假设权重和一小块激活放完之后，这张 worker 还剩 40 GiB 给 KV block。40 GiB = 40,960 MiB，40,960 / 320 = 128。所以这条规则是：若每条在线请求都按 2,560 token 占满预留，这张卡同时最多接住 128 条。第 129 条不能再画进同一块无限大的显存，只能进在线队列，或直接拒绝。

这 128 还决定完成速率，而不只是「放不放得下」。占用时间取假设 6 秒：256 个输出 token 若每个 iteration 0.020 秒，decode 是 256 × 0.020 = 5.12 秒，再加上大约 0.15 秒的 prefill 和调度空隙，凑整到 6 秒。饱和时 128 / 6 ≈ 21.3 次完成每秒。峰值到达是 30 req/s，大于 21.3，队列会涨。平均 10 req/s 低于 21.3，所以平均日不是 OOM 日。Little 定律：平均并发 10 × 6 = 60，60 × 320 MiB = 19,200 MiB ≈ 18.75 GiB，低于 40 GiB。峰值若都不拒绝，并发 30 × 6 = 180，180 × 320 MiB = 57,600 MiB ≈ 56.25 GiB，超过 40 GiB。乘法的结果是准入上限，不是吞吐目标。

English

Step through it: 2 × 32 = 64, 64 × 8 = 512, 512 × 128 = 65,536, 65,536 × 2 = 131,072. The leading 2 is K and V. Eight KV heads are a GQA-style assumption; storing K/V for 32 heads multiplies the line by 4. Weights are a separate bill: an assumed 8×109 parameters at 2 bytes is 1.6×1010 bytes, about 14.9 GiB, and that does not move when a request arrives. Weight-only quantization leaves b in the KV formula alone, so the admission cap does not move.

Admission does not bet on the mean output, because the length is unknown ahead of time. Reserve prompt plus max_tokens: 2,048 + 512 = 2,560 tokens. 2,560 × 131,072 = 335,544,320 bytes = 320 MiB. Assume that after weights and a small activation reserve, 40 GiB remain for KV blocks. 40 GiB is 40,960 MiB, and 40,960 / 320 = 128. If every online request holds a full 2,560-token reservation, this card admits 128 of them. Request 129 is not drawn into a larger box. It waits on the online queue, or it is rejected.

The same 128 caps completions, not only fit. Occupancy is an assumed 6 seconds: 256 output tokens at 0.020 seconds per iteration is 256 × 0.020 = 5.12 seconds of decode, plus about 0.15 seconds of prefill and scheduling slack, rounded to 6 seconds. At saturation, 128 / 6 ≈ 21.3 completions per second. Peak arrival is 30 req/s, above 21.3, so the queue grows. The mean of 10 req/s sits under 21.3, so the average hour is not the OOM hour. Little’s law: mean concurrency is 10 × 6 = 60, and 60 × 320 MiB = 19,200 MiB ≈ 18.75 GiB, under 40 GiB. If the peak is never rejected, concurrency is 30 × 6 = 180 and 180 × 320 MiB = 57,600 MiB ≈ 56.25 GiB, over 40 GiB. The product is an admission ceiling, not a throughput target.

Throughput and TTFT are different ledgers. If the mean load completes, output rate is 10 × 256 = 2,560 tokens/s. Saturation is about 21.3 × 256 ≈ 5,450 tokens/s. Those numbers are tokens leaving the card, not how long a new user waits for the first one. TTFT is queue wait plus prefill. With an empty queue and the 0.15-second prefill assumption, TTFT can sit inside the example 800 ms SLO. Once 30 exceeds 21.3 and the queue builds, wait consumes the SLO even while decode elsewhere still emits thousands of tokens per second. The input side is also not QPS: 10 × 2,048 = 20,480 input tokens/s. The gateway’s token bucket charges tokens. A tenant cap of 4,096 input tokens/s allows about two of these 2,048-token prompts per second, so one tenant does not fill the card.

0.020 秒的 iteration、0.15 秒的 prefill、40 GiB 的 KV 预算、6 秒占用，都是为了把 req/s 翻译成显存和等待的假设。它们不是基准测试结果。面试官给了别的 iteration 时间，就替换这一步，128 和 21.3 会一起变。The 0.020-second iteration, the 0.15-second prefill, the 40 GiB KV budget, and the 6-second occupancy exist to translate req/s into memory and wait. They are not benchmark results. If the interviewer substitutes another iteration time, replace that step. Both 128 and 21.3 move with it.

在线路径，以及为什么离线队列不能共用这条 SLOThe online path, and why the offline queue cannot share this SLO

先讲清楚，再把离线画上去

在线用户的 TTFT 示例目标是 p99 小于 800 毫秒，TPOT 示例目标是 p99 小于 50 毫秒。离线作业，例如把一批长文档做摘要，没有 TTFT 目标，一条 8k token 的 prefill 可以占住 iteration 远远超过 50 毫秒。DistServe 的观察是：不短的 prompt 上，prefill 偏 compute-bound，decode 偏内存带宽；把一次较长的 prefill 和正在进行的 decode 放进同一次 batch，decode 那一步会被拉长，TTFT 也会被 decode 拖一点 [3]。所以离线流量如果直接进在线 scheduler 的同一次 iteration，在线 SLO 就不再成立。

因此离线是另一条队列、另一组 worker。它不把 KV 写进在线那张卡，也不借用在线的 128 个预留名额。只在讲完这个原因之后，最后一帧才把这条路径画出来。同池加优先级是替代方案，留在图后的正文里比较，不作为第一张图。

Explain it, then draw the offline path

The example online targets are TTFT p99 under 800 ms and TPOT p99 under 50 ms. An offline job, such as summarizing a pile of long documents, has no TTFT target. One 8k-token prefill can occupy an iteration for far longer than 50 ms. DistServe’s observation is that on prompts that are not brief, prefill leans compute-bound and decode leans memory-bandwidth-bound; batching a long prefill with in-flight decode stretches that decode step, and decode also adds a bit to TTFT [3]. If offline traffic enters the same iteration as the online scheduler, the online SLO stops being a real target.

Offline is therefore a second queue and a second set of workers. It does not write KV onto the online card, and it does not borrow the 128 online reservations. That path is drawn only on the last frame, after this reason. Sharing one pool with priority is the alternative, compared in the prose after the deck, not on picture one.

正常路径

客户端带 Authorization、Idempotency-Key 和 body 里的 messages、max_tokens、stream: true。网关先验签，再按 input token 扣租户的 bucket。这一步还没碰 GPU。bucket 不够就在流开始之前回 429，带 Retry-After，scheduler 里不会出现这条请求。通过之后，网关把请求交给在线 scheduler。scheduler 看的是这张 worker 上还剩多少 KV block，而不是看 HTTP 连接数。按前面的预留，一条 2,048 + 512 的请求要 320 MiB。剩余预算够，才在下一次 iteration 把请求送进 worker：先跑 prefill，写出 prompt 的 KV block，吐出第一个 token，经网关用 SSE 推回客户端；之后每个 decode step 只吃最新这个 token，把新的 K/V 追加进 block，再推一个 delta。权重在进程启动时从对象存储载入显存，之后常驻。生成一个 token 不会再去读那份对象。

队列里的请求和卡上的请求不是一类状态。队列只保存还没分配 block 的描述：request id、prompt、max_tokens、到达时间。KV 从 prefill 成功的那一次 iteration 才出现，而且只出现在接住它的那张卡上。所以网关可以水平加实例，scheduler 也可以把新请求分到还有空闲 block 的 worker。已经开始 decode 的请求不能因为网关扩容就换一张卡。

Happy path

The client sends Authorization, an Idempotency-Key, and a body with messages, max_tokens, and stream: true. The gateway authenticates, then charges the tenant bucket in input tokens. That step does not touch a GPU. If the bucket is short, the response is 429 with Retry-After before any stream, and the scheduler never sees the request. Otherwise the gateway hands it to the online scheduler. The scheduler looks at free KV blocks on the worker, not at the HTTP connection count. Under the reservation above, a 2,048 + 512 request wants 320 MiB. Only when that budget remains does the next iteration take the request onto the worker: prefill writes the prompt’s KV blocks and emits the first token, which the gateway pushes to the client as SSE; each later decode step consumes only the newest token, appends K/V into a block, and pushes one delta. Weights load from object storage into GPU memory when the process starts, then stay resident. Emitting a token does not read that object again.

A queued request and an on-card request are different states. The queue stores a description that has no blocks yet: request id, prompt, max_tokens, arrival time. KV appears on the iteration where prefill succeeds, and only on the card that ran it. The gateway can add instances, and the scheduler can place a new request on any worker that still has free blocks. A request that has already started decode does not move cards because the gateway scaled out.

为什么是这条拆分

10 req/s 的平均负载本身不需要很多 worker：饱和大约 21.3 次完成每秒，一张按 128 个预留算的卡在平均日够用。难的是状态和两个延迟。网关没有 KV，所以它可以无状态扩。worker 有 KV，加载约 14.9 GiB 的权重也不是秒级，所以突发不能靠「马上再起一个进程」接住，先靠有界队列和 429。prefill 和 decode 先画在同一张卡上，是因为这条假设的平均并发只有 60，预留显存约 18.75 GiB，还没到必须为两阶段各买一组卡的地步。等两个 SLO 同时很紧、而且 prefill 开始把 iteration 拉过 50 毫秒，再考虑后面的拆分，那是 DistServe 要优化的 goodput，不是这张总图的第一笔 [3]。

离线不进这 128 个名额，还有一个吞吐上的原因。在线输出若按平均 2,560 token/s 在跑，插入一次上千 token 的离线 prefill，这一次 iteration 的墙钟时间会变成 prefill 的时间。在线那些正在 decode 的会话的 TPOT 一起变差。分开池子的代价是离线那组卡在聊天空闲时可能闲着。负载若几乎没有离线作业，同池也可以，但必须有硬隔离：在线队列非空时，离线请求不能开始新的 prefill；已经在跑的离线 chunk 至少会占满当前这次 iteration。那是替代，不是默认。

Why this split

A mean of 10 req/s does not by itself demand a large fleet: saturation is about 21.3 completions per second, so one card sized at 128 reservations covers the average hour. The hard part is state and the two latencies. The gateway holds no KV, so it scales without state. The worker holds KV, and loading about 14.9 GiB of weights is not a one-second operation, so a spike is caught by the bounded queue and by 429, not by “start a process right now.” Prefill and decode share a card on the first picture because mean concurrency is only 60 and the reservation is about 18.75 GiB. That is not yet a reason to buy a second pool for each phase. When both SLOs are tight and prefill starts stretching the iteration past 50 ms, disaggregation is the later discussion. That is the goodput DistServe optimizes, not the first stroke of this diagram [3].

Keeping offline out of the 128 slots is also a throughput decision. If online decode is emitting the mean 2,560 tokens/s, one offline prefill of thousands of tokens makes that iteration as long as the prefill. TPOT for every in-flight chat session moves with it. Separate pools can leave the offline cards idle when chat is quiet. If offline volume is nearly zero, one pool is workable, but only with a hard fence: while the online queue is non-empty, an offline request must not start a new prefill. A chunk already running still occupies the current iteration. That is the alternative, not the default.

故障窗口和常见错误

有三个窗口要单独说。第一，网关已经扣了 token bucket，scheduler 还没把请求放上卡，客户端超时重试。Idempotency-Key 在「还没有任何 token 写出」时必须命中同一次准入，不能再扣一次、也不能再占一个 320 MiB。第二，prefill 已经写下 block，SSE 的第一个字节还没写进套接字，worker 就没了。block 跟着卡一起消失，客户端看到的是失败，不是半段文章。第三，已经推出若干 delta，连接断了。网关日志里有游标，卡上的 KV 不一定还在。这三窗口的处理放在失败那一节，这里先记住：流式回程是同步推送，不是写进一条可重放的业务数据库。

常见错误是把这条路径画成无状态的 CPU 服务：前面一个负载均衡，后面一排可替换的进程，请求失败就换一台重试，而且不提 KV。那样画会漏掉 320 MiB 的预留、漏掉「decode 进行中不能换卡」，也会把离线作业和聊天放进同一个无界线程池。QPS 在这个图上只是到达率。真正限制同时服务多少会话的，是 2,560 × 131,072 那一次乘法。

Failure windows and a common mistake

Three windows need their own sentences. First, the gateway has charged the token bucket and the scheduler has not placed the request, and the client retries on timeout. While no token has been written, the Idempotency-Key must hit the same admission: no second charge, and no second 320 MiB. Second, prefill has written blocks and the first SSE byte has not reached the socket when the worker dies. The blocks die with the card. The client sees a failure, not half an essay. Third, some deltas were already pushed and the connection drops. The gateway log may hold a cursor. The KV on the card may already be gone. The failure section handles these three. What matters here is that the stream is a push, not a row in a replayable business database.

The common mistake is to draw this as a stateless CPU service: a load balancer in front, a row of replaceable processes, and a retry that lands anywhere, with no KV. That drawing misses the 320 MiB reservation, misses “decode cannot change cards,” and puts offline jobs and chat in one unbounded thread pool. QPS on this picture is only an arrival rate. The cap on simultaneous sessions is the multiplication 2,560 × 131,072.

中文

流还没开始时，错误是普通 HTTP。token bucket 或在线队列满，回 429。没有任何 worker 还能放下 320 MiB 的预留，回 503。这两个码都表示卡上没有这条请求的 KV。流已经开始之后，不再改写状态码，而是发一条终止事件，例如 cancelled 或 upstream_dead，然后关掉连接。客户端要续写，必须再发一次 POST。

Idempotency-Key 表示同一次尝试，不表示「把死掉的 GPU 状态接回来」。一个 token 都还没写出时，重试看到的是原来的准入结果：仍在排队、或原来的 429 / 503。已经写出过字节、而 worker 还活着并且流还开着，就不要再开第二条流。worker 已经死了，或者客户端已经走了，网关回 409，body 里带它成功写过的游标文本和 token 数。想继续，就用「原 prompt 加上这段游标」作为新的 prompt，把 max_tokens 减去已经交付的数量，使用新的 request id。那是一次新的 prefill，会重新分配 block。旧 key 仍绑在失败的那次尝试上，避免客户端盲目重试时把第二篇生成接在半篇文章后面。

因此本章不承诺 exactly-once tokens。网关写进套接字的字节，客户端可能没收到；客户端已经渲染的字节，网关的日志可能还没记下。用短了的游标去续，边界上的 token 会重复；游标记多了，会丢掉一个已经显示过的 token。卡上生成了但还没写出网关的 token，随 worker 一起消失。能承诺的是：取消和失败会归还 block；续写是显式的新请求，不是对旧 KV 的静默附着。

English

Before the stream starts, failures are ordinary HTTP. A full token bucket or a full online queue returns 429. If no worker can reserve 320 MiB, the response is 503. Both codes mean the card has no KV for this request. After the stream has started, the server does not rewrite the status line. It sends a terminal event such as cancelled or upstream_dead, then closes. Continuation is a new POST.

The Idempotency-Key names one attempt. It does not mean “reattach the dead GPU state.” When no token has been written, a retry sees the original admission: still queued, or the original 429 / 503. If bytes were written, the worker is alive, and the stream is still open, do not open a second stream. If the worker is dead, or the client is already gone, the gateway returns 409 with the cursor text and the token count it successfully wrote. Continuation sends “original prompt plus that cursor” as the new prompt, subtracts delivered tokens from max_tokens, and uses a new request id. That is a new prefill and a new block allocation. The old key stays bound to the failed attempt, so a blind retry does not paste a second generation onto a half-finished one.

This chapter does not promise exactly-once tokens. Bytes the gateway wrote to the socket may never arrive. Bytes the client already rendered may be missing from the gateway log. A short cursor repeats the boundary token. A long cursor drops a token the user already saw. A token generated on the card and not yet written out dies with the worker. What you can promise is that cancel and failure return the blocks, and that continuation is an explicit new request, not a silent attach to the old KV.

正常路径

一条请求的 KV 不是预留一整段连续的最大长度。PagedAttention 把 K/V 切成固定大小的 block，逻辑上按 token 顺序排，物理上可以不连续，用 block table 做映射 [2]。prefill 只分配 prompt 实际写下的那些 block。decode 每多一个 token，先填最后一块的空位；最后一块满了，再分配下一块。论文里的例子是 7 个 prompt token、block 里放 4 个 token：prefill 映射两块，第二步 decode 才需要第三块物理页。我们的 2,560 token 预留是准入用的上限账，不是启动时就把 320 MiB 涂满。真正占住的是已经生成的 token。浪费被压到最后一块没填满的那些槽位，论文的说法是一条请求的浪费落在一块之内 [2]。

scheduler 在每次 iteration 之前先问：下一块还在不在水位线以下。在就追加。不在就把新来的 R2 留在在线队列；如果队列再长会让等待超过 TTFT 预算（示例里 800 毫秒减去 prefill），就 429 或 503，并且不为 R2 分配任何 block。图上的外框不变大。128 这个数就是 40,960 MiB / 320 MiB。分页后活 token 往往短于 2,560，平均日可以更密；一旦同时写满 max_tokens，密度回到 128。准入不能拿平均值对外承诺。

Happy path

A request’s KV is not one contiguous reservation of the maximum length. PagedAttention splits K/V into fixed-size blocks, ordered logically by token and free to sit apart in physical memory, with a block table as the map [2]. Prefill allocates only the blocks the prompt actually writes. Each new decode token fills a hole in the last block; when that block is full, the next block is allocated. Their walk-through is a 7-token prompt with 4 tokens per block: prefill maps two blocks, and the second decode step is what needs a third physical block. Our 2,560-token line is the admission ceiling, not a paint-the-whole-320-MiB at start. What is occupied is the tokens already generated. Waste is pushed into the unfilled slots of the last block. The paper’s line is that waste for a request stays inside one block [2].

Before every iteration the scheduler asks whether the next block still sits under the watermark. If it does, append. If it does not, R2 stays on the online queue. If a longer queue would push wait past the TTFT budget — in the example, 800 ms minus prefill — return 429 or 503 and allocate nothing for R2. The outer rectangle does not grow. The number 128 is 40,960 MiB / 320 MiB. After paging, live tokens are often shorter than 2,560, so the average hour can pack tighter; if everyone hits max_tokens together, density falls back to 128. Admission does not promise the mean.

为什么分页，而不是换一种 attention 算法

Kwon 等人测量的是当时的服务系统，包括 Orca 和 FasterTransformer 那一类连续预留：按请求的最大长度切一块连续显存。他们的图里，真正放着 token 状态的 KV 只占预留的 20.4%–38.2%，最低可以只到 20.4% [2]。空着的部分有三类：给未来 token 留的槽、最大长度带来的内部碎片、分配器造成的外部碎片。碎片在，batch 就上不去，而 decode 又是内存带宽绑着的，batch 上不去吞吐就上不去。vLLM 在这篇论文里的增量是这套分页和由此带来的共享，论文报告在相同延迟水平下，相对 FasterTransformer 和 Orca 吞吐约 2–4 倍 [2]。Orca 已经有 iteration-level scheduling。所以这句话不能说成「vLLM 发明了推理」。推理服务在这篇论文之前就存在，这篇论文解决的是 KV 怎么放。

前缀可以共享物理 block，引用计数大于 1，最后一块要写新 token 时做 copy-on-write。论文把这用于并行采样、beam 和相同的 prompt 前缀：前缀的 prefill 可以不再重算 [2]。前缀字节一变，旧 block 就不能当命中，因为同一个 token 出现在不同位置，K/V 并不相同。这只减少 prefill，不减少后缀的 decode 步数，也不是网关里的整段答案缓存。

Why paging, rather than a different attention algorithm

What Kwon et al. measured is the serving systems of that moment, including contiguous reservation in the Orca and FasterTransformer line: one contiguous region sized to the request’s maximum length. In their figure, KV memory actually holding token state is only 20.4%–38.2% of the reservation, and can be as low as 20.4% [2]. The rest is three kinds of empty space: slots kept for future tokens, internal fragmentation from the maximum length, and external fragmentation from the allocator. While that space is empty, the batch cannot grow. Decode is memory-bandwidth bound, so a batch that cannot grow does not raise throughput. The increment vLLM claims in that paper is this paging, and the sharing it makes possible. At the same latency they report about 2–4× the throughput of FasterTransformer and Orca [2]. Orca already had iteration-level scheduling. The sentence is not “vLLM invented inference.” Serving existed before this paper. The paper is about where the KV sits.

A prefix can share physical blocks, with a reference count above 1, and copy-on-write when the last block must take a new token. The paper uses that for parallel sampling, beam search, and a repeated prompt prefix, so prefill of the prefix need not be recomputed [2]. Change one prefix byte and the old blocks are not a hit, because the same token at a different position does not have the same K/V. Sharing cuts prefill only. It does not cut decode steps in the suffix, and it is not a gateway cache of a finished answer.

替代和失败边界

已经放进卡里的请求如果在 decode 中途把 block 用穿，论文给的办法是抢占，而且是整条序列一起赶，不是赶单个 block：把 KV 换到 CPU 内存，或者把已经生成的 token 拼回 prompt 再 prefill 一次 [2]。FCFS 下他们先抢最后来的。换出受 GPU 和主机之间的带宽限制；重算则把 decode 已经花掉的步数用一次 prefill 买回来。对在线聊天，本章的默认是尽量在准入时就拒绝，让 TTFT 不要依赖换出的带宽。替代方案是：已经在跑的长输出越过水位线时，抢占最新的那条，把它的 block 换出或直接释放，其他会话继续。不要把整个 worker 打死。

按最大长度做连续预留，也不会自动消灭碎片。论文的剖面里，这种预留大部分不是活的 token 状态 [2]。KV 也不是可复制的数据库：block table 跟着 worker，复制表并不复制显存里的页。

Alternative and the failure boundary

If a request already on the card runs out of blocks mid-decode, the paper’s mechanism is preemption of the whole sequence, not of one block: swap the KV to CPU memory, or concatenate the tokens already generated back onto the prompt and prefill once [2]. Under FCFS they preempt the latest arrival first. Swap is limited by bandwidth between GPU and host. Recompute spends one prefill to buy back the decode steps already taken. For online chat, this chapter’s default is to refuse at admission, so TTFT does not depend on swap bandwidth. The alternative is: when a long output already running crosses the watermark, preempt the newest one, swap or free its blocks, and keep the other sessions. Do not kill the worker.

Contiguous reservation at the maximum length does not by itself remove fragmentation. In the paper’s profile, most of that reservation is not live token state [2]. KV is also not a database you can copy: the block table stays with the worker, and copying the table does not copy the pages in GPU memory.

Continuous batching：一次 iteration 里有人离开、有人加入Continuous batching: one iteration lets someone leave and someone join

中文

Orca 把调度粒度从「整条请求」改成「一次 iteration」：scheduler 让引擎只跑模型的一轮，这一轮里每个还活着的请求生成一个 token；引擎返回后，已经结束的请求可以马上回给客户端，新请求只要再等一轮就可以被考虑进来，而不必等当前这批里最长的那条跑完 [1]。他们同时提出 selective batching：Attention 因各请求已处理的 token 数不同，输入形状不一样，所以按请求分开算；其他算子仍然组 batch，因为那些算子能分摊读权重的开销 [1]。后来的服务论文把「新请求的 prefill 和正在进行的 decode 放在同一批里」叫做 continuous batching [3]。名字不同，调度上要画的是同一件事：批的成员按 iteration 变，不是三个写着 batch 的静态方框。

English

Orca changes the scheduling grain from “a whole request” to “one iteration”: the scheduler asks the engine to run a single step of the model, and each live request in that step produces one token. When the engine returns, a finished request can go back to the client immediately, and a new request can be considered after waiting only one more iteration, instead of waiting until the longest request in the batch finishes [1]. They also introduce selective batching: Attention sees a different shape per request because each request has processed a different number of tokens, so Attention runs per request, while the other operators stay batched so they can amortize the weight read [1]. Later serving papers call “prefill of a new request batched with decode that is already running” continuous batching [3]. The names differ. What the drawing has to show is the same fact: membership changes per iteration. It is not three static boxes labeled batch.

正常路径

iter t 的 lane 里是 A、B、C，三个人都在 decode，各自只算自己的下一个 token。Attention 按 Orca 的 selective batching 可以按请求分开，线性层则合在一起，好让一次读权重服务多条序列 [1]。这一轮结束，B 抽到 EOS，scheduler 把它移出 lane，SSE 把结尾交给客户端，B 的 KV block 引用计数归零后回到空闲表。A 和 C 留在 lane 里，它们的 block 不动。iter t+1 开始前，scheduler 再看队列：D 的 320 MiB 预留还放得下，就把 D 放进同一条 lane 做 prefill。D 没有等待 A 的剩余几百个 token。iter t+2 时 D 的 prefill 已经结束，D 和 A、C 一起 decode。新来的下一条请求，最多再等一轮，而不是等 A 整段生成完。

这就是 Orca 摘要里的对比：请求级调度一旦把一批交给引擎，就要等整批结束才返回；先做完的回不去，后到的也进不来 [1]。他们在 GPT-3 175B 上相对 FasterTransformer，报告过相同延迟水平下约 36.9 倍的吞吐 [1]。那是他们的实验，不是「任何集群都有 36 倍」，也不是长尾消失的证据。36.9 倍来自少等待、少 padding。一条本来就要生成 512 token 的请求，步数还是 512。

Happy path

At iteration t the lane holds A, B, and C, all in decode, each computing only its own next token. Under Orca’s selective batching, Attention may run per request while the linear layers stay together, so one weight read serves several sequences [1]. At the end of the step, B samples EOS. The scheduler removes it from the lane, SSE delivers the tail to the client, and B’s KV blocks return to the free list once the reference count hits zero. A and C stay. Their blocks stay. Before iteration t+1 the scheduler looks at the queue again. If D’s 320 MiB reservation still fits, D enters the same lane and prefills. D does not wait out A’s remaining hundreds of tokens. By iteration t+2, D’s prefill is done and D decodes beside A and C. The next arrival waits at most one more iteration, not A’s entire generation.

That is the contrast in Orca’s abstract: once request-level scheduling hands a batch to the engine, it waits for the whole batch before returning. A request that finished early cannot leave, and a request that arrived late cannot enter [1]. On GPT-3 175B they report about 36.9× the throughput of FasterTransformer at the same latency [1]. That is their experiment, not “every cluster gets 36×,” and not evidence that the tail disappeared. The 36.9× comes from less waiting and less padding. A request that must generate 512 tokens still takes 512 steps.

为什么要组批，以及批为什么不能无限大

decode 每步都要把权重从显存读进来，却只为一个 token 做很小的计算，所以单条请求会把卡留在内存带宽上。把多条请求的线性层放进同一次 iteration，同一次读权重被多条序列分摊，集群的 token/s 才会从「一条序列的速度」变成前面估的每秒数千 token。一张卡同时只跑一条请求，按 0.020 秒一轮就是大约每秒 50 个 token，而不是饱和时约 5,450 token/s。所以「一请求一卡、完全不 batch」接不住成本。Orca 和后面的分页工作都是在让 batch 能上去 [1][2]。

batch 变大，单次 iteration 的墙钟时间也变长。TPOT 的示例目标是 50 毫秒。若为了塞进第 129 条而把 iteration 拉到 80 毫秒，集群 token/s 也许还在涨，每条会话的出字速度已经破 SLO。显存这边 128 已经是满额预留的上限。正确的说法是：在水位线和 50 毫秒之间取更紧的那个。continuous batching 让空位不必等整批结束才填上，它不提供「越大越好」。

Why batch at all, and why the batch cannot grow without a limit

Every decode step reads the weights from GPU memory and then does little math for a single token, so one request leaves the card on the memory bandwidth. Putting the linear layers of many requests into the same iteration amortizes that read, and cluster tokens/s can move from “one sequence’s rate” to the thousands per second in the estimate. One request per card, at the assumed 0.020 seconds per iteration, is about 50 tokens per second, not the roughly 5,450 tokens/s at saturation. “One request per GPU, no batching” does not carry the cost. Orca and the later paging work are both about making the batch able to grow [1][2].

A larger batch also makes the iteration’s wall time longer. The example TPOT target is 50 ms. If admitting a 129th request stretches the iteration to 80 ms, cluster tokens/s may still rise while every session’s token rate has already missed the SLO. On the memory side, 128 is already the full-reservation cap. The line to say is: take the tighter of the watermark and the 50 ms budget. Continuous batching fills a free slot without waiting for the whole batch to end. It does not mean bigger is always better.

它消不掉的东西

D 加入之后，A 的长输出还在。A 若还要 400 个 token，就还要大约 400 次 iteration。这是长尾的一部分，调度粒度改了也不缩短。另一部分是 D 的 prefill 若和 A、C 的 decode 挤在同一轮，这一轮会比纯 decode 长，A 和 C 的这一步 TPOT 变差。DistServe 把这件事叫做 prefill–decoding interference，并写明：把长 prefill 切成 chunk 再和 decode 拼在一起，只是在 TTFT 和 TPOT 之间做交换，干扰还在 [3]。所以下一节要单独画排队、长输出和慢的一轮。不要在这一节结尾说「上了 continuous batching，p99 就没了」。

常见错误有两个。一是把 FlashAttention 当成这个 scheduler。FlashAttention 是 IO-aware 的精确 attention：用分块减少 GPU 高带宽显存和片上 SRAM 之间的读写，避免把完整的注意力矩阵物化到 HBM [4]。它的论文报告的是训练墙钟加速。PagedAttention 的论文甚至把 Dao 等人 2022 的算法当作 prefill 里可以用的一种常规 attention 内核 [2]。内核决定一轮里面 attention 怎么算；scheduler 决定这一轮有谁。二是把 Orca 的 36.9 倍背成尾延迟消失。那个倍数比的是相同延迟水平下的吞吐 [1]。

What it does not remove

After D joins, A’s long output is still there. If A still has 400 tokens to go, that is still about 400 iterations. That piece of the tail does not shrink because the grain changed. The other piece is D’s prefill sharing an iteration with A and C’s decode: the step runs longer than decode alone, and A and C’s TPOT for that step gets worse. DistServe calls this prefill–decoding interference, and says that slicing a long prefill into chunks and attaching decode only trades TTFT against TPOT. The interference remains [3]. The next section draws queueing, long output, and a slow iteration on their own. Do not end this section with “continuous batching, so the p99 is gone.”

Two mistakes show up here. One is treating FlashAttention as this scheduler. FlashAttention is an IO-aware exact attention algorithm: tiling cuts reads and writes between GPU high-bandwidth memory and on-chip SRAM, and it avoids materializing the full attention matrix in HBM [4]. The speedups reported in that paper are training wall-clock numbers. The PagedAttention paper even treats Dao et al. 2022 as one conventional attention kernel you can run inside prefill [2]. The kernel decides how attention is computed inside a step. The scheduler decides who is in the step. The other mistake is memorizing Orca’s 36.9× as the disappearance of tail latency. That factor is throughput at the same latency [1].

长尾：排队、长输出、慢的一轮，取消后要还 blockThe tail: queueing, long output, a slow iteration, and freeing blocks on cancel

三条尾部怎么叠加

TTFT 的示例上限是 800 毫秒。若 prefill 按假设是 0.15 秒，排队预算大约是 650 毫秒。平均 10 req/s、完成能力约 21.3 次每秒时，水位线以下通常有空位，Orca 说新请求最多等一轮 [1]，这一轮若是 0.020 秒，排队不是 p99 的主体。峰值 30 大于 21.3 时，空位不再及时出现，Q1 的等待会涨过 650 毫秒。这时即使 iteration-level scheduling 工作正常，TTFT 仍然破。这是第一条尾部：队列。

第二条是长输出。A 已经在卡上，KV 按 token 在涨，用户看到的总时间大约是 TTFT 加上 token 数乘 TPOT。256 个 token、每步 50 毫秒的示例上限，光 decode 就可以到 12.8 秒。continuous batching 让 B 不必陪 A 到结束，这改善的是 B 的等待和卡的利用率，不是 A 的 256 步。产品若不能接受这个总时长，要收紧 max_tokens，或者在网关做更早的超时，而不是再把 batch 加大。

第三条是慢的一轮。D 的 prefill，或离线作业漏进在线 lane 的一个 chunk，会把这一步的墙钟时间拉长。A 和 C 在这一步无法吐出下一个 token，TPOT 的 50 毫秒示例一起被击穿。DistServe 写的就是这种干扰：prefill 往往比一个 decode step 长得多，放在一起时 decode 被推迟 [3]。chunked prefill 把长 prompt 拆开，让 decode 不必等完整段 prefill，但论文指出这仍是 TTFT 和 TPOT 的交换，干扰没有消失 [3]。三条会叠在一起。

How the three tails add

The example TTFT cap is 800 ms. If prefill is the assumed 0.15 seconds, the queue budget is about 650 ms. At 10 req/s against about 21.3 completions per second, a free slot usually exists under the watermark. Orca’s claim is that a new request waits at most one iteration [1]. If that iteration is 0.020 seconds, the queue is not the body of the p99. When the peak of 30 exceeds 21.3, slots stop appearing in time and Q1’s wait grows past 650 ms. TTFT misses even though iteration-level scheduling is doing what it was built to do. That is the first tail: the queue.

The second tail is long output. A is already on the card and KV is growing per token. What the user feels as total time is about TTFT plus tokens times TPOT. At 256 tokens and the example 50 ms cap per step, decode alone can be 12.8 seconds. Continuous batching means B does not have to stay until A finishes. That improves B’s wait and the card’s utilization. It does not change A’s 256 steps. If the product cannot accept that total time, tighten max_tokens or time out earlier at the gateway. Do not answer by enlarging the batch again.

The third tail is a slow iteration. D’s prefill, or an offline chunk that leaked into the online lane, stretches the wall time of that step. A and C cannot emit their next token during it, and the example 50 ms TPOT breaks for the whole lane. This is the interference DistServe describes: a prefill step is often much longer than a decode step, and batching them delays decode [3]. Chunked prefill splits a long prompt so decode need not wait for the entire prefill, but the paper says this is still a trade of TTFT against TPOT and the interference does not disappear [3]. The three tails stack.

取消必须归还

客户端关掉 SSE，或服务器的截止时间到了，scheduler 在下一轮边界把该请求移出 lane。正在执行的那次 kernel 可以跑完，因为它可能已经在读这批 block；跑完之后不能再给这条请求分配新 block，也不能再推 delta。然后把这条请求独占的 block 引用计数减到 0，放回空闲表，之后 Q1 才可能使用这些空位。若取消只关了 HTTP、block 仍记在死去的请求名下，128 个名额会被僵尸占满，后面的 503 会变成常态。这和论文里「请求结束后 block 可以还给别人」是同一条生命周期 [2]，取消要走完它，不能停在断连上。

替代是不取消、只降低优先级。长输出继续占着显存和每一轮的算力，TPOT 仍会被它和后来的 prefill 影响。对在线 SLO，超时取消比无限陪跑更符合 800 毫秒和 50 毫秒这两个示例目标。代价是用户看到半段文字加一条终止事件，续写必须走前面的游标和新的 prefill。常见错误是说 continuous batching 已经消除了尾延迟，于是不为队列设上限、不为 max_tokens 设截止、也不在取消时归还 block。三条尾部都还在，而且第三条会因为离线作业混进在线 lane 而更常出现。

Cancel has to give the blocks back

When the client closes the SSE, or the server deadline fires, the scheduler removes that request from the lane at the next iteration boundary. The kernel already in flight may finish, because it may already be reading this batch of blocks. After it returns, it must not allocate another block for this request and must not push another delta. Then the blocks owned only by this request drop to reference count 0 and return to the free list, and only then can Q1 use them. If cancel merely closes HTTP and the blocks stay charged to a dead request, zombies fill the 128 slots and 503 becomes the steady state. This is the same lifetime as the paper’s rule that blocks can be given to someone else when a request finishes [2]. Cancel has to finish that lifetime. It cannot stop at the disconnect.

The alternative is to avoid cancel and only lower priority. The long output keeps its GPU memory and its share of every iteration, and TPOT is still exposed to it and to later prefills. For the online SLO, a timeout matches the example 800 ms and 50 ms targets better than riding along without a limit. The cost is a partial answer plus a terminal event. Continuation uses the cursor and a new prefill. The common mistake is to say continuous batching has already removed tail latency, and therefore to skip a queue cap, skip a deadline on max_tokens, and skip returning blocks on cancel. All three tails are still there, and the third one shows up more often if offline work can enter the online lane.

失败：死在 decode 中途、OOM、队列打满Failures: death mid-decode, OOM, and a full queue

三条失败分别怎么走

worker 在 decode 中途消失时，健康检查把它从 scheduler 的可准入集合里拿掉，新请求不再分到这张卡。正在这条流上的客户端收到 upstream_dead。卡上的 block table 和物理页一起没了，不存在「在另一张卡上从第 38 个 token 接着采样」的静默路径，因为采样状态和 KV 都在死去的那张卡上。客户端用同一个 Idempotency-Key 重试时：若游标还是 0，网关可以在活着的 worker 上做一次新的 prefill，这和第一次尝试的用户可见结果同类，仍然可能和「卡上生成过但没写出」的 token 对不上。若游标大于 0，回 409 和游标文本，续写是新的 request id、新的 prefill。这张卡要重新载入权重大约 14.9 GiB 之后，才能再接新请求。

GPU OOM 应该先被准入挡住。若仍然发生，说明水位线被绕过，或激活的瞬时占用超过了那一小块预留。处理是：失败的是申请下一块的那条请求，释放它的 block，对它的流发错误，其他序列继续。不要重启整个 worker 来「清理显存」，否则所有在途 KV 都变成第一条失败。若分配器已经越界到进程被杀掉，就退回 worker 死亡那条路径。

队列过载时，到达是 30 req/s，单卡完成约 21.3 次每秒，差出来的请求会堆积。队列有上限，等待估计会超过 650 毫秒量级的排队预算时，网关对还没开始的请求回 429，并给出 Retry-After。这些请求没有 block。已经在 decode 的会话继续，直到它们自己结束、超时或被取消。取消仍要归还 block，否则 429 会从「保护 SLO」变成「名额泄漏」。多加一张同样预算的 worker，完成能力大约再增加 21 次每秒，但这只对还没开始的请求有用。它救不了已经死在旧卡上的那条流。

How each failure walks

When a worker disappears mid-decode, the health check removes it from the set the scheduler may admit to, and new requests stop landing there. The client on that stream receives upstream_dead. The block table and the physical pages are gone with the card. There is no silent path that continues sampling at token 38 on another card, because both the sampling state and the KV were on the card that died. On retry with the same Idempotency-Key: if the cursor is still 0, the gateway may run a new prefill on a live worker. That is the same kind of user-visible result as a first attempt, and it can still disagree with a token that was generated on the card and never written out. If the cursor is above 0, return 409 and the cursor text. Continuation is a new request id and a new prefill. That card has to load about 14.9 GiB of weights again before it can take new work.

GPU OOM should have been stopped at admission. If it still happens, the watermark was bypassed, or a transient activation exceeded the small reserve. The response is: fail the request that asked for the next block, free its blocks, send an error on its stream, and keep the other sequences. Do not restart the whole worker to “clear GPU memory,” or every in-flight KV becomes the first failure. If the allocator has already overrun far enough to kill the process, this falls back to the worker-death path.

Under queue overload, arrival is 30 req/s and one card completes about 21.3 per second, so the difference piles up. The queue has a cap. When the estimated wait would pass a queue budget on the order of 650 ms, the gateway returns 429 with Retry-After for requests that have not started. Those requests have no blocks. Sessions already decoding continue until they finish, time out, or are cancelled. Cancel still has to return blocks, or the 429 stops being “protect the SLO” and becomes “leak the slots.” Adding another worker with the same budget adds about 21 completions per second, and that helps only requests that have not started. It does not save the stream that already died on the old card.

不承诺什么

exactly-once tokens 要求三件同时成立：每个生成出来的 token 恰好送达一次，重试不会改变已经展示的前缀，死掉的卡上的采样状态还能在别处继续。这三件里第三件做不到，因为 KV 不是持久存储。第一件和第二件也会在游标边界失败。所以对外的说法是：网关成功写出的字节至少有机会被客户端看到，重试要么命中同一次尚未写出的准入，要么带着游标做新的 prefill；重复和丢失都可能出现在边界上。观测至少看四组：TTFT 和 TPOT、输出 token/s 与完成速率、KV block 占用相对 40 GiB、以及 429、503、取消后归还的延迟、upstream_dead 的次数。归还延迟变长，比 GPU 利用率下降更能预告下一次 OOM。

常见错误是无感切到另一副本、OOM 时把显存框画大，或用无限队列换掉 TTFT。30 req/s 高于约 21.3 次完成每秒，无限队列的等待没有上界。

What is not promised

Exactly-once tokens would need three things at once: every generated token delivered once, a retry that does not change the prefix already shown, and the sampling state of a dead card continued somewhere else. The third one is unavailable, because KV is not durable storage. The first and second also fail at the cursor boundary. The public line is: bytes the gateway managed to write have a chance to be seen by the client; a retry either hits the same admission that has not written anything, or runs a new prefill with the cursor; both duplicates and losses can show up at the boundary. Watch at least four groups: TTFT and TPOT, output tokens/s and completion rate, KV blocks used against the 40 GiB budget, and the count of 429, 503, time-to-free after cancel, and upstream_dead. Time-to-free growing long predicts the next OOM more clearly than a dip in GPU utilization.

The usual mistakes are a silent cut to another replica, a larger memory box on OOM, or an unbounded queue in place of the TTFT target. At 30 req/s against about 21.3 completions per second, wait on an unbounded queue has no ceiling.

同卡干扰，以及什么时候才把 prefill 和 decode 拆开Same-card interference, and when prefill and decode actually split

中文

第一张总图把 prefill 和 decode 放在同一张 worker 上，是因为本章的平均负载约 18.75 GiB 的预留，一张卡的 40 GiB 预算盖得住，而且完成能力约 21.3 次每秒高于 10 req/s。拆开是替代，不是起点。DistServe 的理由是：同卡把两个阶段的资源计划和并行度绑在一起，而且同一次 batch 里的 prefill 会干扰 decode。他们把 goodput 定义成在 TTFT 和 TPOT 都满足、并且达标比例达到目标（他们举例 90%）时，每张 GPU 能接住的请求速率 [3]。裸的 token/s 可以在破 SLO 的情况下仍然很好看。

English

Picture one puts prefill and decode on the same worker because this chapter’s mean load is about 18.75 GiB of reservation, inside a 40 GiB budget, and about 21.3 completions per second is above 10 req/s. Splitting is the alternative, not the start. DistServe’s reason is that colocation ties the resource plan and the parallelism of the two phases together, and a prefill in the same batch interferes with decode. They define goodput as the request rate per GPU that still meets both TTFT and TPOT at a target attainment, and their example attainment is 90% [3]. Raw tokens/s can look fine while the SLO is already missed.

什么时候从左边走到右边

同卡加上 iteration-level scheduling，解决的是「短请求陪长请求等整批结束」。它没有解决「这一轮里有一段 prefill，于是所有 decode 的这一步都变长」。若在线 TPOT 的示例 50 毫秒经常被这种轮次打穿，而 TTFT 的 800 毫秒又不允许把 prefill 无限往后排，同卡上的旋钮就只剩两个：少接请求，让 batch 变小，iteration 变短；或者把 prefill 切成 chunk，让每一步没那么长。前者降低大约 5,450 token/s 那一档的饱和吞吐，后者按 DistServe 的说法是用 TTFT 去换 TPOT [3]。两个 SLO 都要守、而且流量大到超配也很贵时，才把 prefill 和 decode 分到不同的 GPU，各自扩，中间用一次 KV 搬运接上。

他们的论文报告，在多种模型、应用和延迟目标下，相对当时的系统，请求数大约是对照的 7.4 倍，或把 SLO 收紧约 12.6 倍，同时让超过 90% 的请求落在延迟约束里 [3]。这些倍数是他们的评测，不是本章 10 req/s 假设的预测。文中还有一个 13B、输入 512、输出 64 的例子，用来说明同卡时 goodput 被两个延迟里更紧的那个卡住，阶段分开之后每张卡的请求速率可以更高 [3]。引用时要带上这个条件。不要把 7.4 背成「拆开就快七倍」。搬运本身要带宽。带宽不够，或 prefill 池太小，拆开的 goodput 可以比同卡更差。本章的 40 GiB 预算和 18.75 GiB 的平均占用，还没有给出必须拆的证据。

When you walk from the left side to the right

Colocation plus iteration-level scheduling fixes “a short request waits until the long one ends the batch.” It does not fix “this iteration contains a prefill, so every decode in the step gets longer.” If the example 50 ms TPOT is often broken by those steps, and the 800 ms TTFT does not allow prefill to be postponed without a limit, two knobs remain on one card: admit less, so the batch and the iteration get shorter; or slice prefill into chunks, so each step is less long. The first lowers the saturation tier around 5,450 tokens/s. The second, in DistServe’s words, trades TTFT for TPOT [3]. When both SLOs must hold and the traffic is large enough that over-provisioning is expensive, put prefill and decode on different GPUs, scale them apart, and connect them with one KV transfer.

Their paper reports that across models, applications, and latency targets, they can serve about 7.4× more requests or meet about a 12.6× tighter SLO than the systems they compare, with more than 90% of requests inside the latency constraints [3]. Those factors are their evaluation, not a forecast for this chapter’s 10 req/s assumption. The paper also uses a 13B example with 512 input tokens and 64 output tokens to show that colocated goodput is stuck behind the tighter of the two latencies, while separating the phases can raise the request rate per card [3]. Keep that condition attached to the citation. Do not memorize 7.4 as “splitting is seven times faster.” The transfer needs bandwidth. If the fabric is thin, or the prefill pool is too small, disaggregated goodput can be worse than colocation. This chapter’s 40 GiB budget and 18.75 GiB mean occupancy are not yet evidence that a split is required.

失败时拆分更疼

同卡失败，丢的是这一张卡上的在途请求。拆开之后，prefill 已经完成、KV 还在搬运途中或已经在 decode 卡上，失败点变多了。搬运中断和 decode 卡死亡一样：客户端不能从字节偏移静默续写，除非网关有游标并且客户端愿意把前缀重新 prefill。decode 卡死亡时，prefill 卡上的那份 block 如果还留着，理论上可以再搬一次；本章不把这说成默认恢复，因为要额外的引用计数和「页还在不在」的协议，面试里先承认你没有 exactly-once。常见错误是第一张图就拆成两个池，却说不出 10 req/s 和 18.75 GiB 为什么已经不够用；另一个错误是把 FlashAttention 的分块和这里的 chunked prefill 说成同一个调度器。前者是 attention 内核里的 tile [4]，后者是 scheduler 把一条长 prefill 拆成多次 iteration [3]。

A split hurts more when it fails

A colocated failure drops the in-flight requests on that one card. After a split, prefill can already have finished while the KV is in transfer or already on the decode card, so there are more places to fail. A broken transfer is the same shape as a dead decode card: the client cannot silently continue from a byte offset unless the gateway has a cursor and the client is willing to prefill the prefix again. If the decode card dies and the prefill card still holds the blocks, a second transfer is possible in principle. This chapter does not make that the default recovery, because it needs an extra reference count and a protocol for “are the pages still there.” In the interview, say first that you do not have exactly-once. The common mistake is to split into two pools on picture one without saying why 10 req/s and 18.75 GiB are already not enough. The other mistake is to treat FlashAttention’s tiling and chunked prefill as the same scheduler. The first is a tile inside an attention kernel [4]. The second is the scheduler breaking one long prefill into several iterations [3].

English

Treating inference as a stateless CPU service. The gateway really has no KV and can scale out. The worker has a block table, and the weights load in gigabytes. Landing on another machine after a failure is a new prefill, not the next token of the old decode. If offline jobs share an unbounded pool with chat, the example 50 ms TPOT has nowhere to stand.

Ignoring KV. About 14.9 GiB of weights is only the fixed lease. The variable lease is 2 × 32 × 8 × 128 × 2 = 131,072 bytes per token, 320 MiB for a maxed request, and 128 such reservations in 40 GiB. A peak left free to reach 180 concurrent wants about 56.25 GiB, over the budget. The paper’s OPT-13B contrast is a different line: 2 × 5120 × 40 × 2, which they report as 800 KB per token and about 1.6 GB at 2,048 tokens [2]. One request per GPU, with no batching. Every decode step reads the weights. Without several sequences’ linear layers in the same iteration, the card’s output stays at one sequence’s rate and cannot carry the saturation ledger around 5,450 tokens/s. What Orca adds is that a finished request leaves and a new one can enter on the next iteration [1]. The batch is still capped by 128 and by 50 ms.

Treating FlashAttention as the serving scheduler. What it cuts is attention traffic between HBM and SRAM [4]. The PagedAttention paper uses that kind of kernel inside prefill [2].
