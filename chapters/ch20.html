30 秒开场：画日志，并说清不承诺什么The 30-second open: draw a log, and name the guarantees you withhold

中文

我设计一条可重放的分区日志，形态接近 Kafka：producer 按 key 追加到分区 leader，leader 按 acks=all 等到 ISR 都写入才回 offset，消费者组用自己的 next offset 来 fetch。消费不删日志。顺序只在分区内。默认至少一次，消费者用业务幂等键。日志本身不提供 exactly-once。

第一问是范围。经典队列常常是消费即删、broker 记住谁拿走了哪条；这道题要的是留着、可以按 offset 重放、多个独立消费者组各记各的位置。RabbitMQ 的 exchange 路由、流处理作业、死信和重试风暴都不进第一张图。和数据库同一事务提交，用 outbox 把事件写进同一提交，再由单独的投递进程 produce；那是相邻话题，这里只留一个接口，不把白板改成 CDC 平台。

开场就把不承诺的四件事说完，避免后面用组件名把它们补回来。没有跨分区的全局顺序。两条分区上的记录即使带着时间戳，也不能在这套设计里排成一条总序。复制完成前不 ack。本设计选择 acks=all，并且 min.insync.replicas=2；leader 本地追加成功还不算对客户端的成功。没有幂等键的消费者做不到 exactly-once。处理成功而 offset 提交失败时，消息会再次投递。幂等 producer 只避免重试在日志里写两遍；事务才能把「写出的记录」和「offset 更新」绑在一起，而且绑不到日志外面的数据库。默认不是无限留存。过期按时间删 segment，慢消费者会跟不上并丢掉窗口之外的数据。

English

I am designing a replayable partitioned log, Kafka-like: the producer appends by key to the partition leader, the leader waits for the ISR under acks=all before returning an offset, and each consumer group fetches from its own next offset. Consume does not delete the log. Order holds only inside one partition. The default is at-least-once with a business idempotency key. The log alone does not provide exactly-once.

The first question is scope. A classic queue often deletes on consume and remembers who took which message. This prompt keeps the log, replays by offset, and lets independent consumer groups store their own positions. Exchange routing, stream-processing jobs, and dead-letter retry storms stay off the first diagram. A write that must commit with a database row uses an outbox in that same commit, then a separate process produces; that interface is named here and the board does not turn into a CDC platform.

Say the four withheld guarantees before the boxes multiply. There is no global order across partitions. Timestamps on two partitions do not become a total order in this design. There is no ack before the replication policy completes. This design chooses acks=all and min.insync.replicas=2; a local append on the leader is not success for the client. A consumer with no idempotency key does not get exactly-once. If processing succeeds and the offset commit fails, the record is delivered again. An idempotent producer only stops a retry from appending twice; a transaction binds produced records to the offset update, and that transaction does not cover an outside database. Retention is not infinite by default. Segments expire by time, and a slow consumer loses data outside the window.

一次乘法：留存和分区数都从它推出来One multiplication: it chooses both retention and partition count

把秒数约掉

磁盘预算用同一组假设乘一次。平均速率是 100,000,000 / 86,400 = 1,157.407 条/秒。留存窗口是 7 × 86,400 = 604,800 秒。副本因子是 3。平均记录 1,024 字节，这里只算 payload，索引和段文件头另加余量，不混进这一次乘法。

Cancel the seconds

The disk budget is one product of the same assumptions. Average rate is 100,000,000 / 86,400 = 1,157.407 messages per second. The retention window is 7 × 86,400 = 604,800 seconds. The replica factor is 3. The average record is 1,024 bytes of payload; indexes and segment headers are headroom, not terms in this product.

disk = (1e8 / 86400) msg/s × 1024 B × (7 × 86400) s × 3
     = 1e8 × 1024 × 7 × 3
     = 2,150,400,000,000 bytes
     = 2.1504 TB

2.1504 TB 改两处设计

86,400 被约掉之后，剩下的就是日消息量 × 字节 × 天数 × 副本。2.1504 TB 是 7 天、三副本、未压缩 payload 的磁盘。把它换成 365 天：1e8 × 1,024 × 365 × 3 = 112,128,000,000,000 字节，约 112.1 TB。本设计不按这个量采购，所以留存写成 7 天，而不是「磁盘便宜，先无限留着」。论文里的 broker 本来就不知道每个订阅者是否读完，删除靠时间契约，常见例子是 7 天；我们的 7 天同时是假设和这笔账的结果。[1]

同一笔账也决定分区数不按容量去切。日字节 1e8 × 1,024 = 1,024 亿字节，除以 86,400 秒，平均入口约 1.185 MB/s；10 倍峰值约 11.85 MB/s。三副本时 follower 还要再拉大约两份，集群内复制流量峰值约 24 MB/s 这个量级。这是单机顺序写和一块千兆网卡能接住的范围，所以不会为了「把 2 TB 摊开」去建几百个分区。分区数跟着消费并行走：主消费者组预期扩到大约 32 个成员，而一个分区在任一时刻只分给组内一个成员，并行上限就是分区数。预建 48 个，比预期成员多一截，滚动发布时仍有空分区可接。48 × 3 个副本日志放到 3 台 broker 上，每台大约 48 个分区副本、约 16 个 leader。每台 leader 写入大约是 11.85 MB/s 的三分之一，仍然不是磁盘题。

热 key 会推翻「48 已经够并行」的错觉，但不会推翻这笔磁盘账。若假设某个 user_id 占峰值的 20%（这是倾斜假设，不是测量），它仍整键落在一个分区上，大约 2,315 条/秒、约 2.37 MB/s。磁盘依旧轻松，卡住的是那个分区的唯一消费者以及它后面的同步调用。把 48 改成 96 只会改变 hash(key) % N，让几乎所有 key 换分区，并不能把这一个 key 拆开。所以 2.1504 TB 的结论是：留存停在 7 天，分区停在「略多于消费者」的 48，集群停在 3 台 broker。真正要单独画的是热分区、ISR 确认和再均衡，不是再加一排容量方框。

索引、段文件和页缓存会让规划容量大于 2.1504 TB。面试里说「原始乘法是 2.15 TB，规划按大约 1.5 到 2 倍留文件和追赶空间，仍然是数 TB 而不是百 TB」。这个倍数是余量，不是第二套业务假设。压缩会把字节降下来，但留存策略仍按未压缩的上限来挡无限保留；压缩不能成为「默认永久保存」的理由。

What 2.1504 TB changes

After 86,400 cancels, the product is daily messages × bytes × days × replicas. 2.1504 TB is seven days, three replicas, uncompressed payload. Swap in 365 days: 1e8 × 1,024 × 365 × 3 = 112,128,000,000,000 bytes, about 112.1 TB. This design does not buy that, so retention is seven days rather than "disk is cheap, keep it forever." The paper's broker cannot know when every subscriber has finished, so deletion is a time contract, with seven days as the typical example. Our seven days are both the assumption and the result of this product. [1]

The same product also decides that partition count is not a capacity shard. Daily bytes are 1e8 × 1,024. Divided by 86,400 seconds, average ingress is about 1.185 MB/s, and a 10× peak is about 11.85 MB/s. Followers pull about two extra copies, so replica traffic peaks on the order of 24 MB/s. Sequential disk and a gigabit link absorb that, so hundreds of partitions are not how we "spread 2 TB." Partition count follows consume parallelism. The main group is expected to grow to about 32 members, and a partition is assigned to only one member at a time, so parallelism caps at the partition count. Pre-create 48, a step above that membership, so a rolling restart still has a free partition to take. 48 × 3 replica logs on 3 brokers is about 48 partition replicas and about 16 leaders per broker. Each broker's leader writes are about a third of 11.85 MB/s. Still not a disk problem.

A hot key breaks the idea that 48 already means enough parallelism, without breaking the disk budget. If one user_id is 20% of the peak — a skew assumption, not a measurement — it still lands entirely on one partition, about 2,315 msg/s and about 2.37 MB/s. Disk stays comfortable. The single consumer of that partition, and any synchronous call behind it, is what sticks. Moving from 48 to 96 changes hash(key) % N and relocates almost every key; it does not split this one. So 2.1504 TB means: retention stays at 7 days, partitions stay at 48 because that is slightly above the consumer count, and the cluster stays at 3 brokers. The pictures that follow are the hot partition, the ISR ack, and rebalance.

Indexes, segment files, and page cache make planned capacity larger than 2.1504 TB. Say the raw product is 2.15 TB, and planning uses about 1.5× to 2× for files and catch-up, still terabytes rather than a hundred terabytes. That factor is headroom, not a second business assumption. Compression lowers bytes, but retention is still capped by the uncompressed ceiling; compression is not a reason to keep data forever by default.

正常路径

以一条订单事件走完。Producer 调用形如 Produce(topic=orders, key=user-7, value=bytes, acks=all)。客户端用 key 选择分区；在这张示意图里 user-7 落在 P0，这是画出来的分配，不是某个哈希实现的实测结果。请求到达 P0 的 leader。Leader 把记录追加到当前 segment 的末尾，得到一个分区内单调的 offset。按本设计的策略，leader 在 ISR 都追加之前不把这个 offset 当作成功返回；复制的等待画在下一组。成功时响应里带 base_offset，沿绿色回程回到 producer。Producer 把这个 offset 视为「已提交」的证据，而不是「我发出去了」。

Billing 组的成员对 P0 发 Fetch(fetch_offset=next)。Broker 从该 offset 起返回一批已提交记录。消费者处理完，再提交下一个 offset。Audit 组对同一条 P0 或 P1 另有自己的 next offset，可以早于或晚于 Billing，也可以从留存窗口内的旧 offset 重放。两条 fetch 都不在 produce 的 ack 上。Retention 按时间扫 segment，和「有没有消费者读过」无关。

Normal path

Walk one order event. The producer calls Produce(topic=orders, key=user-7, value=bytes, acks=all). The client picks a partition from the key. In the picture user-7 lands on P0; that is an illustrated assignment, not a measured hash output. The request hits P0's leader. The leader appends to the tail of the current segment and assigns a monotonic offset inside that partition. Under this design's policy the leader does not return that offset as success before the ISR has appended; the wait is the next deck. On success the response carries base_offset back to the producer on the green path. The producer treats that offset as evidence of commit, not of "I sent the bytes."

A billing member sends Fetch(fetch_offset=next) for P0. The broker returns a batch of committed records from that offset. The consumer processes them, then commits the next offset. The audit group has its own next offset on the same logs, ahead of or behind billing, and it can replay an old offset inside the retention window. Neither fetch rides on the produce ack. Retention sweeps segments by time, independent of whether a consumer has read them.

为什么是日志而不是消费即删

11.85 MB/s 的峰值让「整段放进内存、读完就删」没有容量上的必要，2.1504 TB 的留存又要求数据在磁盘上按顺序留下来。2011 年的设计把每个分区做成逻辑日志，物理上是大约等长的 segment 文件，写入只追加；消息没有单独的随机访问 id，位置就是日志里的 offset。Broker 不保存「每条消息被每个消费者确认了没有」，否则删除和状态都会回到传统队列的随机写。时间留存是这个选择的直接后果：broker 不知道全体订阅者是否读完，就按契约到期删除。[1]

Pull 而不是 push，是为了让慢消费者自己控速。论文比较过推模型：broker 推得比消费者处理得快时，消费者会被灌满；拉取则可以倒回旧 offset。现行文档仍是消费者发 fetch、在请求里带上自己的 offset，并可以倒回重读。[1][2] 空分区用长轮询，避免空转打满 broker。Push 的延迟可以更低，但本题的慢消费者和 7 天重放都更适合 pull。

Why a log, rather than delete-on-consume

A peak of 11.85 MB/s does not require holding the stream in memory and deleting on read, and a 2.1504 TB retention window requires the bytes to stay on disk in order. The 2011 design makes each partition a logical log, physically a set of similar-sized segment files, append-only. There is no separate random-access message id; position is the offset in the log. The broker does not store "has every consumer acked this message," or deletion and bookkeeping fall back to the random writes of a classic queue. Time retention is the direct consequence: the broker does not know whether every subscriber has finished, so it deletes on the contract. [1]

Pull, rather than push, lets a slow consumer set the rate. The paper contrasts a push model that floods a consumer when the broker outruns processing, and notes that pull can rewind. Current docs still have the consumer send fetch with its offset and rewind when it needs to. [1][2] An empty partition uses long poll so the client does not spin the broker. Push can cut latency, but this prompt's slow consumer and seven-day replay both fit pull.

替代与误答

若产品就是「一条任务被一个 worker 取走后必须从队列消失」，消费即删的队列更简单，broker 代为记住 in-flight。那个模型给不了第二个审计组从头重放，也给不了「处理逻辑写错之后倒回 7 天」。本题的重放需求一旦成立，就不要中途改回删除语义。Share group 那种按条争抢、放弃分区顺序的接口是另一种产品，不进这张图。

常见的残图是一个矩形 MQ，左边 producer、右边 consumer，中间没有日志，也没有两条回程。那样无法回答 offset 存在哪里、ack 代表什么、第二组从哪里读。另一句不完整的话是「先写数据库再顺便 send」。请求线程上的裸 send 和数据库提交不是一个原子操作；要一起成功，应把事件放进同一次数据库提交的 outbox，再异步 produce。这里不展开 outbox 的表结构。

Alternative and the weak answer

If the product is "one worker takes a job and it must disappear," a delete-on-consume queue is simpler and the broker tracks in-flight work. That model does not give a second audit group a full replay, and it does not give a seven-day rewind after a bad consumer deploy. Once replay is a requirement, do not switch back to delete semantics mid-design. A share-group style that competes for individual records and drops partition order is a different product and stays off this diagram.

The incomplete picture is a rectangle labeled MQ, producer on the left, consumer on the right, no log and no two return paths. It cannot say where the offset lives, what the ack means, or where a second group reads. Another incomplete line is "write the database, then send." A naked send on the request thread and the database commit are not one atomic action. If they must succeed together, put the event in an outbox inside that database commit and produce asynchronously. The outbox table is not this chapter.

API 与 offset 数据模型API and the offset data model

三个调用，三种失败

白板至少写清 Produce、Fetch、OffsetCommit 的字段和失败时客户端该做什么。Produce 的成功条件是本设计选定的复制策略，不是「leader 进程还活着」。Fetch 只返回已提交前缀：记录已经复制到全部 ISR，并且当前 ISR 的数量不少于 min.insync.replicas。文档写明，这条可见性规则不随 acks 放宽；acks=1 仍然可能在 follower 复制前对 producer 返回成功，而那条记录随后可能随 leader 一起丢掉。所以本设计把 producer 固定在 acks=all。[2][4]

消费者保存的位置是下一条要读的 offset，不是最后处理完的那条。文档的原话是：每个分区上的位置就是 next message 的 offset。因此处理完 offset 41 之后，提交的是 42。这个数字差一，是重放窗口的边界。[2]

Three calls, three failures

The board should show the fields of Produce, Fetch, and OffsetCommit, and what the client does on failure. Produce succeeds only under the replication policy this design chose, not because the leader process is up. Fetch returns only the committed prefix: the record has been replicated to every ISR replica, and the ISR is at least min.insync.replicas. The docs state that this visibility rule does not relax with acks. acks=1 can still return success to the producer before followers replicate, and that record can then disappear with the leader. This design pins the producer to acks=all. [2][4]

The position the consumer stores is the next offset to read, not the last offset it finished. The docs say the position in each partition is the offset of the next message. After processing offset 41, the commit stores 42. That off-by-one is the edge of the replay window. [2]

字段为什么长这样

分区内的 offset 在本设计里是单调的逻辑序号，Fetch 用它做起点。2011 年论文里的 offset 是字节位置，递增但不一定连续，确认某个 offset 表示该分区里它之前的消息都已收到。现行消费者契约把存储值定义成下一条。面试时跟现行契约走，并主动说明论文里的字节位置是早期实现，避免把两个定义叠在同一次提交里。[1][2]

GroupOffset 的主键是组、主题、分区。Billing 和 Audit 各有一行，所以两组可以相差任意远，只要都还在留存窗口里。没有「全局已消费水位」这一列；谁落后谁自己承担被 retention 删掉的风险。producer_id 和 sequence 属于幂等 producer：broker 用它们丢掉同一次会话里的重试重复追加。它们不记录消费者是否处理过。事务 id 默认不配置；现行 producer 文档里 transactional.id 的默认是空，因此事务不是开箱即用。enable.idempotence 在没有冲突配置时默认为 true，acks 的默认是 all。幂等打开只保证重试不会在同一分区日志里留下第二份；它不把消费者变成 exactly-once。[4]

客户端超时后不能区分「请求没到」和「到了但响应丢了」。没有幂等序列时，重试会把同一业务事件再追加一次，这是 producer 侧的至少一次。有了 producer_id 加序列号，broker 可以丢掉这份重复追加，日志里仍是一条。消费者侧的重复是另一扇窗口，在再均衡那一组里画。

Why the fields look like this

Inside a partition, this design's offset is a monotonic logical sequence and Fetch starts there. In the 2011 paper the offset is a byte position, increasing but not necessarily consecutive, and acknowledging an offset means every earlier message in that partition was received. The current consumer contract defines the stored value as the next offset. Follow the current contract on the board, and say the byte position is the early implementation so the two definitions are not mixed in one commit. [1][2]

The GroupOffset key is group, topic, partition. Billing and audit each have a row, so the groups can sit arbitrarily far apart while both remain inside retention. There is no column for a global consumed watermark; a group that falls behind bears the risk that retention deletes its data. producer_id and sequence belong to the idempotent producer: the broker uses them to drop a retry that would append twice in the same session. They do not record whether a consumer processed the record. A transactional id is unset by default; current producer docs default transactional.id to empty, so transactions are not on out of the box. enable.idempotence defaults to true when no conflicting setting turns it off, and acks defaults to all. Idempotence only stops a retry from leaving a second copy in the same partition log. It does not make the consumer exactly-once. [4]

After a timeout the client cannot tell "the request never arrived" from "the response was lost." Without an idempotent sequence, the retry appends the business event again: at-least-once on the producer side. With producer_id plus sequence, the broker drops that duplicate append and the log still has one copy. The consumer-side duplicate is a different window, drawn in the rebalance deck.

分区与顺序：两条日志，没有总序Partitions and order: two logs, no total order

正常路径

Producer 为需要按用户保持先后的事件选择 user_id 当 key，为需要按 SKU 保持先后的事件选择 sku_id。分区函数是确定性的：同一个 key、同一个分区数，总是进同一条日志。示意图里 user-7 在 P0，连续追加变成 offset 10、11、12；sku-9 在 P1，变成 4、5。组内同时只有一个成员读这条分区，所以这个成员看到的顺序就是追加顺序。论文的原话是：单分区内的消息按序交给消费者，不同分区之间没有顺序保证。[1]

没有 key 的记录可以被客户端粘到某个分区上换吞吐，但那就不要再承诺「这些事件有顺序」。用随机 id 当 key，再口头保证用户级 FIFO，是分区函数和承诺不一致。顺序键必须是业务上要排好的那个字段。

Normal path

The producer uses user_id as the key for events that must stay ordered per user, and sku_id for events that must stay ordered per SKU. The partition function is deterministic: the same key and the same partition count always select the same log. In the picture user-7 is on P0 and appends become offsets 10, 11, 12; sku-9 is on P1 and becomes 4, then 5. Only one member of the group reads that partition at a time, so that member sees append order. The paper states that messages from a single partition are delivered in order, and there is no order across partitions. [1]

Records with no key can be sticky-batched onto a partition for throughput, and then there is no order promise across those records. Using a random id as the key and still saying "per-user FIFO" makes the function and the promise disagree. The ordering key has to be the business field that must stay in order.

为什么不给全局顺序

峰值大约 11.85 MB/s，单分区的顺序写接得住字节，所以「技术上可以收成一个分区」是真的。代价是并行度变成 1：一个 leader、一个消费者、所有热 key 叠在一起。48 个分区存在的理由是主组要扩到大约 32 个成员；收成 1 个分区就把这笔并行直接抹掉。跨分区再做一次归并，要么引入单点 sequencer，回到同一个天花板，要么靠时间戳合并。时间戳会偏、会相同、也会在分区之间以不同延迟到达。本设计不维护那次合并，所以 t=11 出现在 P1、t=12 出现在 P0 并不构成系统保证的先后。

分区数从 48 改到 49 时，hash(key) % N 几乎对所有 key 改变。原先同分区的 user-7 事件可能被切到新分区的两侧，旧 offset 上的顺序还在，新写入的顺序不再和旧分区接在一起。Kafka 风格的日志可以增加分区，收缩会把已有数据的路由打乱，本设计创建时就定 48，扩容先加消费者，直到成员数接近 48 再讨论加分区，并接受一次计划内的 key 重映射。

Why there is no global order

The peak is about 11.85 MB/s, so a single partition's sequential write can take the bytes. "We could collapse to one partition" is true. The cost is parallelism of one: one leader, one consumer, every hot key stacked together. The reason 48 partitions exist is that the main group should grow to about 32 members; collapsing to one partition deletes that parallelism. A merge across partitions either adds a single sequencer, the same ceiling, or merges on timestamps. Clocks skew, collide, and arrive with different delay per partition. This design does not run that merge, so t=11 on P1 and t=12 on P0 are not a system-guaranteed order.

Changing the partition count from 48 to 49 changes hash(key) % N for almost every key. user-7 events that used to share a partition can split across the new modulus. Order on the old offsets remains; new appends no longer continue that partition. A Kafka-like log can grow the partition count; shrinking scrambles routing of existing data. This design fixes 48 at create time, scales by adding consumers first, and discusses more partitions only as membership approaches 48, accepting a planned key remap.

热 key 与误答

20% 峰值落在 user-7 上时，大约 2.37 MB/s 仍在一个分区里。加分区救不了这个 key。可行的替代是把这个用户的契约改掉：例如按 user_id 加一天做 key，换来同一天内有序、跨天无序；或者把超大用户单独放到一个主题。两种都要产品同意顺序范围变窄。无序的日志聚合可以去掉 key，吞吐最高，但本题的订单事件需要分区内 FIFO，所以保留 key。

误答是「我们有 48 个分区，所以整个主题有序」或「带了时间戳就可以全局排序」。48 提高的是并行上限，不是总序。时间戳是记录属性，不是分区之间的共识。另一句误答是组内两个消费者同时读 P0 来加速 user-7。那样必须再协调谁读哪条，顺序不再是追加顺序。论文把分区定为组内并行的最小单位，就是为了避免这把锁。[1]

Hot keys and the weak answer

When 20% of the peak sits on user-7, about 2.37 MB/s is still one partition. More partitions do not save that key. A real alternative changes the contract: key by user_id plus day, ordered within a day and not across days, or move the whale user to its own topic. Both need the product to accept a narrower order. Unordered log aggregation can drop the key and take the highest throughput, but this prompt's order events need partition-local FIFO, so the key stays.

The weak answer is "we have 48 partitions, so the topic is ordered" or "timestamps give a global sort." 48 raises the parallelism cap, not a total order. A timestamp is a field on the record, not consensus between partitions. Another weak answer is two consumers in the group reading P0 to speed up user-7. They would have to coordinate who takes which record, and order would no longer be append order. The paper makes the partition the smallest unit of parallelism inside the group to avoid that lock. [1]

复制：等 ISR，不等落后的那一台Replication: wait for the ISR, not for the lagging replica

正常路径

Produce 41 到达 leader。Leader 追加到本地日志，此时 41 还只在 leader 上。Follower 像普通消费者一样向 leader fetch，把记录追加到自己的日志；文档强调 follower 拉取，这样它们可以自己攒批。[2] Follower 1 追上后，ISR 仍是 {leader, follower 1}，数量等于 min.insync.replicas=2。acks=all 要求当前 ISR 全部确认，于是 leader 把 base_offset 41 返回给 producer。Follower 2 的日志停在 30。它超过 replica.lag.time.max.ms 仍追不上 leader 的末尾，leader 把它移出 ISR。后续 produce 不再等它。[2]

消费者可见的范围比 producer 的 ack 更严一层，而且文档写明与 acks 取值无关：记录必须已经复制到全部 ISR，并且 ISR 数量不少于 min.insync.replicas，才会被读到。因此消费者不会读到「leader 上有、副本上没有、leader 一死就会消失」的尾部。已提交的含义是：只要还有一台 ISR 副本活着，这条已提交记录不会丢。[2]

Normal path

Produce of offset 41 hits the leader. The leader appends locally; 41 exists only there. Followers fetch from the leader the way a consumer does and append to their own logs. The docs call out the pull because followers can batch. [2] After follower 1 catches up, the ISR is still {leader, follower 1}, size equal to min.insync.replicas=2. acks=all waits for the current ISR, so the leader returns base_offset 41. Follower 2's log is at 30. If it cannot reach the leader's end within replica.lag.time.max.ms, the leader removes it from the ISR. Later produces do not wait for it. [2]

What a consumer may read is stricter than the producer ack, and the docs say it does not depend on the acks value: the record must be replicated to every ISR replica, and the ISR must be at least min.insync.replicas. Consumers therefore do not read a tail that exists only on the leader and would vanish with it. Committed means the record will not be lost as long as one ISR replica stays alive. [2]

为什么选 acks=all 和 min=2

2011 年的论文没有内置复制。实验里的 producer 甚至不等 broker 确认，作者把这写成日志采集可以拿耐久换吞吐，并把复制列为未来工作：机器损坏时，还没被消费的消息会丢。本题若停在那篇论文的耐久性上，就还不是一套能 ack 的队列。复制、ISR 和 unclean election 来自现行设计文档，不是来自那篇 NetDB 论文。[1][2]

RF=3、min.insync.replicas=2、acks=all 是一起的。可以坏一台 follower，ISR 仍有两份，写入继续。再坏一台，ISR 只剩 leader，数量小于 2，acks=all 的 produce 被拒绝。这是用「现在写不进去」换「已经 ack 的不丢」。若把 min 设成 3，等于要求每台副本都在 ISR 里，一台抖动就拒绝写入，比本设计更脆。若把 min 设成 1 并且 acks=1，producer 在副本复制前就拿到成功，leader 随后宕机会丢掉已经告诉客户端成功的记录。Producer 配置文档把 acks=1 的这种丢失写明了；acks=all 才是「至少还有一台 ISR 活着就不丢」那一档。现行默认也是 acks=all，但白板要说的是我们选择了这一档，而不是背默认值。[4]

Why acks=all and min=2

The 2011 paper has no built-in replication. In the experiment the producer did not wait for a broker ack; the authors describe that as trading durability for throughput on log collection, and they list replication as future work: if a broker's disk is lost, unconsumed messages on it are gone. Stopping at that paper's durability would not be a queue that can ack. Replication, the ISR, and unclean election come from the current design docs, not from that NetDB paper. [1][2]

RF=3, min.insync.replicas=2, and acks=all belong together. One follower can fail and the ISR still has two copies, so writes continue. One more failure leaves only the leader, the ISR size drops below 2, and acks=all produces are rejected. That trades "writes fail now" for "acked records stay." Setting min to 3 requires every replica to be in the ISR, so one jitter refuses writes, which is more brittle than this design. Setting min to 1 with acks=1 lets the producer succeed before replication, and a leader crash can drop a record the client was told had succeeded. The producer config docs state that loss for acks=1. acks=all is the tier that survives while one ISR replica is alive. The current default is also acks=all, but the board should say we chose this tier, not recite a default. [4]

替代、故障与误答

acks=0 把记录放进套接字缓冲区就算发送，retries 基本不起作用，适合可以丢的指标，不适合订单。acks=1 少一次 follower 往返，延迟更低，耐久只到 leader 本地日志。本题的目标是「ack 过的还在」，所以不选它们。等待提交在文档里大约是 10 ms 量级；我们把健康 ISR 下的 produce p99 目标放在 100 ms 以内，给这次等待和小批量留余量。这个 100 ms 是设计目标，不是测量值。[2]

ISR 缩到 1 时的正确行为是拒绝新的 acks=all，而不是静默改成 acks=1。客户端拿到失败就用同一个幂等序列重试。误答是「三副本，所以写 leader 内存即可」或「ack 了就说明三台都落盘」。三副本是复制因子；确认集合是当前 ISR；磁盘上的持久化还要经过操作系统页缓存，面试里应说明 fsync 策略是另一档延迟，本设计至少把「复制到 ISR」当作 ack 条件，不把页缓存当成已经复制。

Alternatives, failure, and the weak answer

acks=0 treats the socket buffer as sent, and retries barely apply. That fits metrics that can be dropped, not orders. acks=1 skips a follower round trip and lowers latency; durability stops at the leader's local log. This prompt's target is "an ack still means the record is there," so those settings are not chosen. The docs put the commit wait on the order of 10 ms. The working p99 target under a healthy ISR is under 100 ms, leaving room for that wait and a small batch. The 100 ms is a design target, not a measurement. [2]

When the ISR shrinks to 1, the correct behavior is to reject new acks=all produces, not to silently downgrade to acks=1. The client retries with the same idempotent sequence. The weak answer is "three replicas, so a write to the leader's memory is enough" or "an ack means all three disks synced." Three is the replica factor; the ack set is the current ISR. Durability through the page cache and fsync is a further latency tier. This design at least makes "replicated to the ISR" the ack condition and does not treat the page cache as replication.

消费者组：再均衡，以及提交失败后的重投Consumer group: rebalance, and redelivery after a failed commit

正常路径

Billing 组订阅 orders。协调者把 P0 分给 C1、P1 分给 C2。成员数如果超过 48，多出来的成员分不到分区，空转；并行上限就是分区数，这和前面用 2.1504 TB 否定「按磁盘切几百片」是同一句话的后半。C1 对 P0 Fetch(next)。假设 next 是 41，它读到 41，在付款库写入 order 9001，然后 OffsetCommit(next_offset=42)。提交成功之后，C1 崩溃也不会让 41 再被组内另一成员当作未处理记录。

Audit 组不在这张分配图里，但它有自己的 GroupOffset 行，可以以不同的 next 读同一条 P0。组内「一条消息只到一个成员」和组间「每组各读一份」是同一个日志上的两种用法。论文用消费者组同时表达这两种模型，并规定同一时刻一个分区只被组内一个消费者读取，免得读的时候加锁。[1]

Normal path

The billing group subscribes to orders. The coordinator assigns P0 to C1 and P1 to C2. If membership exceeds 48, the extra members get no partition and sit idle. The parallelism cap is the partition count, which is the other half of using 2.1504 TB to refuse "hundreds of slices for disk." C1 fetches P0 at next. If next is 41, it reads 41, writes order 9001 to the payments store, then OffsetCommit(next_offset=42). After that commit succeeds, a crash of C1 does not make another member treat 41 as unprocessed.

The audit group is not in this assignment picture, but it has its own GroupOffset row and can read the same P0 at a different next. "One copy inside the group" and "one copy per group" are two uses of the same log. The paper uses consumer groups for both, and it assigns a partition to only one consumer in the group at a time so readers do not lock each record. [1]

再均衡与那扇窗口

C2 心跳丢失或进程退出，组要重新分配它拥有的分区。经典协议在成员变化时重算所有权，搬移期间该分区的 fetch 会停。现行文档里，Kafka 4.0 起下一代再均衡协议（KIP-848）已经 GA，增量进行，不再依赖一次全局同步屏障；服务端默认可用，客户端却不是默认打开，需要把 group.protocol 设为 consumer。[5] 白板默认仍按「分区从旧成员挪到新成员，新成员从已提交的 next offset 读」来讲。增量协议缩短停顿，不改变提交语义。

窗口本身经常比再均衡的停顿更重要。C2 已经把 order 9001 写入付款库，随后提交 next_offset=42 失败，或进程在这两步之间死掉。Offsets 里 P1 仍是 41。C3 接管后 Fetch(41)，再次看到同一条记录。文档把「先处理、后保存位置」定义成至少一次：崩溃发生在处理之后、保存之前，新进程会拿到已经处理过的消息。反过来，先保存位置再处理，是至多一次：位置已经往前，处理却没发生，消息不会再来。[2] 本设计选择前者，因为丢一笔订单比重复一笔更难补；重复交给幂等键。

Rebalance and the window

When C2 loses its heartbeat or exits, the group reassigns the partitions it owned. The classic protocol recomputes ownership on membership change, and fetch of a moved partition pauses while that happens. Current docs say that since Kafka 4.0 the next consumer rebalance protocol (KIP-848) is GA, incremental, and no longer depends on a global synchronization barrier. The server enables it; the client does not by default. group.protocol must be set to consumer. [5] The whiteboard default is still "the partition moves, and the new member reads the committed next offset." The incremental protocol shortens the pause. It does not change commit semantics.

The window often matters more than the pause. C2 has written order 9001 and then fails to commit next_offset=42, or dies between the two steps. P1's stored offset is still 41. C3 takes over, fetches 41, and sees the same record. The docs define "process, then save position" as at-least-once: a crash after processing and before the save delivers an already-processed record to the new process. Saving the position and then processing is at-most-once: the position moved, the work did not happen, and the record does not come back. [2] This design chooses the former because a missing order is harder to repair than a duplicate; the duplicate is the idempotency key's job.

幂等消费者，以及误答

付款表的唯一键是 order_id 加上事件类型，或者消息里的 event_id。C3 再次写入时冲突，读到已有行，返回成功而不再扣款。键必须落在会产生副作用的那个存储里。进程内存里的 set 在 C2 死亡时一起消失，不能算幂等。论文已经写过：大多数时候组内会恰好收到一次，但消费者不干净地退出时，接管者会看到上次成功提交之后的重复；在意重复的应用要用 offset 或消息里的唯一键自己去重。那比两阶段提交便宜。[1]

自动提交按时间推进位置，处理函数若在间隔中失败，位置可能已经越过未处理的记录，变成至多一次；或者处理完还没到下一次自动提交就崩溃，又回到至少一次。生产路径用手动提交，把提交放在副作用成功之后。误答是「消费者写得很小心，所以是 exactly-once」以及「组内只有一个消费者读分区，所以不会重复」。单消费者保证的是顺序，不是崩溃窗口里的恰好一次。

The idempotent consumer, and the weak answer

The payments table is unique on order_id plus event type, or on an event_id in the record. When C3 writes again it conflicts, reads the existing row, and returns success without a second charge. The key has to live in the store that has the side effect. A set in process memory dies with C2 and is not idempotence. The paper already says delivery is usually once inside the group, but an unclean consumer exit makes the takeover process see duplicates after the last successful commit. An application that cares dedupes with the offset or a unique key in the message. That is cheaper than two-phase commit. [1]

Auto-commit moves the position on a timer. If the handler fails inside the interval, the position may already have passed an unprocessed record: at-most-once. If the process crashes after handling and before the next auto-commit, it is at-least-once again. The production path commits manually, after the side effect succeeds. The weak answer is "the consumer is careful, so this is exactly-once" or "one consumer per partition means no duplicates." One consumer gives order, not exactly-once across the crash window.

故障：只从 ISR 选 leader，留存会砍掉慢消费者Failure: elect only from the ISR, and retention cuts a slow consumer

正常的故障转移

Leader 进程或机器消失。控制器从仍在 ISR 中的副本里选新 leader。Follower 1 的日志包含已提交的 41，提升之后客户端已经拿到的 ack 还在。Follower 2 停在 30，不在候选集里。文档对这条保证的说法是：如果告诉客户端消息已提交，leader 随后失败，新选出的 leader 也必须持有这条消息。这要求提交等待和选举集合有重叠；Kafka 用 ISR 而不是多数派来做这件事，但重叠这一条不能省。[2]

全部副本都死掉时，这条保证结束。文档给出两个行为：等 ISR 中的副本回来再当 leader；或者让第一个回来的副本当 leader，即使它不在 ISR 里。后者就是 unclean leader election。落后日志会变成新的权威，已提交但只存在于死去副本上的记录会没。从 0.11.0.0 起默认是等一致的副本；unclean.leader.election.enable 可以改成优先可用性。本设计保持默认的那一侧，也就是关闭 unclean。可用性的缺口是：ISR 全灭时分区不可写，直到有一份 ISR 副本回来。这是有意的，不是漏画。[2]

A normal failover

The leader process or machine disappears. The controller elects a new leader from replicas still in the ISR. Follower 1's log contains committed offset 41, so after promotion an ack the client already received is still present. Follower 2 stopped at 30 and is not a candidate. The docs state the guarantee as: if the client was told the message is committed and the leader then fails, the newly elected leader must also have that message. The ack wait and the election set have to overlap. Kafka uses an ISR rather than a majority vote, and the overlap is not optional. [2]

When every replica is dead, that guarantee ends. The docs give two behaviors: wait for a replica in the ISR to return and elect it, or elect the first replica that returns even if it is not in the ISR. The second is unclean leader election. The lagging log becomes the new authority, and committed records that lived only on the dead replicas disappear. Since 0.11.0.0 the default is to wait for a consistent replica; unclean.leader.election.enable switches toward availability. This design stays on the default side, with unclean election off. The availability gap is intentional: if the whole ISR is dead, the partition does not accept writes until an ISR replica returns. [2]

留存为什么会丢数据

2.1504 TB 是 7 天乘三副本的预算。磁盘满了，或者 segment 的时间到了，retention 就删旧段。Billing 的 next offset 若还是 20，而 20 所在的段已经被删，Fetch 找不到这批记录。这不是复制 bug，而是留存契约：消费不删除，时间才删除，慢消费者要在窗口内追上。告警看 lag，也就是日志末端减去已提交的 next offset。lag 以天计而留存只有 7 天时，加消费者只能加到还没人读的分区；这个分区若已经有主，加人无效，要加快处理或加分区并接受 key 重映射。无限留存会把乘法里的天数拿掉，磁盘不再有上界，本设计不采用。

元数据不走这条数据面。谁是 leader、ISR 里有谁，由控制器法定人数维护。现行运维文档描述 KRaft：控制器通常选 3 或 5 台，合并在重磁盘 broker 上的 combined 模式不建议用于关键部署。[6] 2011 年论文用 ZooKeeper 保存偏移和触发再均衡；那是当时的协调方式，不是这张数据面图上的业务日志。[1] 面试时把控制器说成「不承载业务字节的元数据仲裁」即可，不必把 ZooKeeper 画进 produce 路径。

Why retention loses data

2.1504 TB is the budget for seven days times three replicas. When the disk fills, or a segment ages out, retention deletes the old segment. If billing's next offset is still 20 and that segment is gone, Fetch cannot return those records. That is the retention contract, not a replication bug: consume does not delete, time does, and a slow consumer has to catch up inside the window. Alert on lag, the log end minus the committed next offset. When lag is measured in days and retention is seven, adding consumers only helps up to the partitions nobody is reading. If this partition already has an owner, another member does nothing; speed up processing, or add partitions and accept a key remap. Infinite retention removes the day term from the product and the disk has no bound. This design does not do that.

Metadata does not ride this data plane. Who the leader is, and who is in the ISR, lives in a controller quorum. Current operations docs describe KRaft: operators typically choose 3 or 5 controllers, and combined mode on a heavy-disk broker is not recommended for critical deployments. [6] The 2011 paper stored offsets and triggered rebalance with ZooKeeper; that was the coordination of that system, not the business log on this data-plane picture. [1] Call the controller a metadata quorum that does not carry business bytes. Do not draw ZooKeeper on the produce path.

误答

「三副本里谁活着谁当 leader」就是 unclean，会丢掉已经 ack 的尾部。本设计拒绝它。「磁盘满了就把留存改成永久」和乘法矛盾，112 TB 量级不是这组假设要买的容量。「慢消费者永远读得到，因为日志还在」忽略了删除是按时间而不是按最慢的组。最慢的组若是审计，它可以落后，但不能落后过整个留存窗口。

Weak answers

"Whoever of the three replicas is alive becomes leader" is unclean election and drops an acked tail. This design rejects it. "The disk is full, so make retention infinite" contradicts the product; about 112 TB is not the capacity these assumptions buy. "A slow consumer can always read because the log is still there" forgets that deletion follows time, not the slowest group. An audit group may lag, and it may not lag past the whole retention window.

交付语义：日志单独给不了 exactly-onceDelivery semantics: the log alone does not give exactly-once

三种语义怎么对上 API

至少一次，本设计的默认：Fetch 41，写入付款，再 OffsetCommit(42)。两步之间失败则 41 重现。至多一次：先 OffsetCommit(42)，再写入付款。提交成功后崩溃，41 不会重现，付款也可能没发生。恰好一次不是第三种 offset 提交顺序能变出来的。文档把问题拆成两半：发布时怎样才算提交，消费时何时保存位置。许多系统把 exactly-once 说满，但一旦生产者或消费者会失败、会有多个消费者进程、或者磁盘上的数据会丢，细字就对不上。[2]

Producer 侧，网络错误无法判断提交发生在失败之前还是之后。0.11 之前重试就会在日志里再写一份，那是至少一次。从 0.11 起，幂等投递给 producer 一个 id，每条消息带序列号，broker 去掉重试造成的重复追加。这保证的是「同一次生产者会话、同一分区的日志里不要因为重试出现两行」。现行配置里，没有冲突项时 enable.idempotence 默认为 true，并且它要求 acks=all。它仍然不知道付款库写过没有。[2][4]

How the three semantics meet the API

At-least-once, this design's default: Fetch 41, write the payment, then OffsetCommit(42). A failure between the steps makes 41 reappear. At-most-once: OffsetCommit(42) first, then write the payment. A crash after the commit means 41 does not reappear, and the payment may never happen. Exactly-once is not a third order of offset commits. The docs split the problem in two: when a publish counts as committed, and when the consumer saves its position. Many systems advertise exactly-once, and the fine print fails when producers or consumers crash, when several consumer processes exist, or when data on disk can be lost. [2]

On the producer side, a network error does not say whether the commit happened before or after the failure. Before 0.11 a retry appended another copy: at-least-once. Since 0.11, idempotent delivery gives the producer an id and a sequence on each message, and the broker drops the duplicate append from a retry. That guarantee is "do not get two log lines in the same producer session and the same partition because of a retry." In current configs, enable.idempotence defaults to true when nothing conflicts, and it requires acks=all. It still does not know whether the payments database was written. [2][4]

事务覆盖哪里

跨分区的原子写入，以及「消费、处理、再写回 Kafka」时把 offset 和输出绑在一起，才用到事务。文档的三点是：消费者靠分区分配保证组内只有自己在处理该分区；producer 用事务让写出的记录和对消费者位置的更新一起提交或一起放弃；若要挡住未提交和已回滚的记录，消费者设置 isolation.level=read_committed，并关闭自动提交。事务的是 producer，它能够顺便原子地更新那个叫作 committed offset 的位置。Kafka Streams 是把这套用起来的一条路径，手写 producer 和 consumer 也可以按同样的方式做。[2]

transactional.id 默认是空，不配置就没有跨会话的事务。幂等 producer 因此是默认能力，事务不是。事务还有协调器和 read_committed 的延迟，小批量、低延迟的日志采集不必开。开了事务，括号也只围住 Kafka 里的记录和 offset。付款库在括号外，图上用红线断开。外部写入仍要 order-9001 这种幂等键，或者先写 outbox 再投递。说「我们开了 Kafka 事务，所以扣款恰好一次」是把括号画大了。

What the transaction covers

Atomic writes across partitions, and binding the offset to the output of consume-process-produce back into Kafka, are what transactions are for. The docs list three points: partition assignment so this consumer is the only member processing that partition; a producer transaction so the records it writes and the consumer position it updates commit or abort together; and, to hide aborted and still-open records, isolation.level=read_committed with auto-commit off. The producer is the transactional client, and it can atomically update the position called the committed offset. Kafka Streams is one way to run this; a hand-written producer and consumer can follow the same pattern. [2]

transactional.id defaults to empty. Without it there is no transaction across sessions. The idempotent producer is the default capability; transactions are not. Transactions also add coordinator and read_committed latency, which a small-batch, latency-sensitive log collector does not need. Even when enabled, the bracket contains Kafka records and offsets. The payments database is outside it, which is why the diagram breaks the line in red. An external write still needs an idempotency key such as order-9001, or an outbox written first. "We enabled Kafka transactions, so the charge is exactly once" draws the bracket too wide.

和论文怎么对齐

论文写的是：Kafka 一般只保证至少一次；恰好一次通常要两阶段提交，他们的应用不需要。不干净退出会造成重复，应用应自己去重。这个结论在「日志加一个普通消费者」的范围内仍然成立。后来的幂等 producer 和事务是在这个结论之上加的机制，不是把论文改成「日志天生恰好一次」。面试时两句一起说：默认路径是至少一次加幂等消费者；若处理结果也写回 Kafka，再加幂等 producer 和事务，并让下游 read_committed；写出数据库仍要业务键。[1][2]

How this lines up with the paper

The paper says Kafka generally guarantees at-least-once only, that exactly-once usually needs two-phase commit, and that their applications did not need it. An unclean exit causes duplicates, and the application dedupes. That conclusion still holds for "a log plus an ordinary consumer." The later idempotent producer and transactions are mechanisms added on top of that conclusion, not a rewrite of the paper into "the log is naturally exactly-once." Say both sentences: the default path is at-least-once plus an idempotent consumer; if the result is also written back to Kafka, add an idempotent producer and a transaction, and have downstream consumers use read_committed; a write to a database still needs a business key. [1][2]

追问Follow-ups

1 · 为什么不用一台数据库当队列？

峰值大约 11.85 MB/s 的追加，一台可以顺序写的存储确实接得住字节。数据库表当队列的问题是删除和「谁取走了」都在变热行，消费者一多就从顺序写变成随机更新。本设计要 7 天重放和第二个消费者组，表还得保留已读行，磁盘故事和日志类似，锁和索引却更重。若只有一个消费者、留存以分钟计、还要和业务行同一事务，队列表或 outbox 更合适。本题的多组和 7 天重放才把日志推到前面。

1 · Why not a database table as the queue?

An append peak of about 11.85 MB/s fits on storage that can write sequentially. A table used as a queue turns "who took this" and deletion into hot rows, so more consumers replace sequential writes with random updates. This design wants seven-day replay and a second consumer group, so the table would also keep already-read rows: similar disk, heavier locks and indexes. One consumer, retention measured in minutes, and a transaction with the business row favor a table or an outbox. Multiple groups and seven-day replay are what put the log in front.

2 · 分区可以随时加吗？

可以加，不该随手加。N 一变，hash(key) % N 变，user-7 的新事件可能离开旧分区，顺序在边界处断开。2.1504 TB 和 11.85 MB/s 都没有要求更多分区。成员接近 48、而且 lag 来自「分区都有主、每个主都满了」时，才计划加分区，并通知生产方一次重映射。热 key 加分区无效。

2 · Can partitions be added whenever?

They can, and they should not be added casually. When N changes, hash(key) % N changes, new user-7 events can leave the old partition, and order breaks at the boundary. Neither 2.1504 TB nor 11.85 MB/s asks for more partitions. Add them when membership nears 48 and lag means every partition has a busy owner, and tell producers about one planned remap. A hot key is not fixed by more partitions.

3 · leader 宕了，已 ack 的还在吗？

在 acks=all、选举只来自 ISR、unclean 关闭的前提下，已提交记录在新 leader 上。只写到 leader、还没进 ISR 的尾部可以丢，但那种尾部本就不该被 ack，也不该被消费者读到。若有人打开 unclean，这个答案不再成立。

3 · If the leader dies, do acked records remain?

With acks=all, election only from the ISR, and unclean election off, a committed record is on the new leader. A tail that reached only the leader and not the ISR can disappear, and that tail should not have been acked or fetched. If someone turns unclean election on, this answer stops holding.

4 · 能不能承诺 exactly-once？

对「写回 Kafka 的处理结果」可以，用幂等 producer、事务和 read_committed，并接受额外延迟。对付款库不行，除非付款写入自己有幂等键，或和 outbox 在同一个数据库事务里。日志文件、三副本、单消费者，三者相加仍是至少一次加顺序。

4 · Can you promise exactly-once?

For a result written back to Kafka, yes, with an idempotent producer, a transaction, and read_committed, and with the extra latency. For the payments database, no, unless that write has its own idempotency key or shares a database transaction with an outbox. The log file, three replicas, and one consumer together are still at-least-once plus order.

5 · 积压了怎么办？

先看 lag 落在哪几个分区。没人读的分区可以加成员。已经有主的热分区加人无效。处理函数里同步调用下游并无限重试，会把下游打满；熔断、隔离和死信是弹性话题，这里只指出它们不该塞进消费者的无限循环。留存窗口快到时，积压会变成丢数据，而不是变成无限排队。

5 · What if the group falls behind?

See which partitions hold the lag. A partition with no owner can take another member. A hot partition that already has an owner does not benefit from another member. A synchronous downstream call with unbounded retries inside the handler will saturate that downstream. Circuit breaking, isolation, and dead letters are a resilience topic; the point here is that they do not belong in an infinite loop inside the consumer. As the retention window approaches, backlog becomes data loss, not an infinite line.

中文

顺序只到分区。复制按 ISR 和 acks=all 才 ack，unclean 不开。消费者是至少一次，幂等键放在付款库；exactly-once 要另加幂等 producer 和事务，并且到不了日志外面的数据库。留存 7 天，因为 1e8 × 1024 × 7 × 3 = 2.1504 TB，分区因此是 48 而不是按磁盘切出来的几百片。

English

Order stops at the partition. Replication acks only with the ISR and acks=all, and unclean election stays off. Consumers are at-least-once, with the idempotency key in the payments store. Exactly-once adds an idempotent producer and a transaction, and it does not reach a database outside the log. Retention is seven days because 1e8 × 1024 × 7 × 3 = 2.1504 TB, so the partition count is 48 rather than hundreds of slices cut for disk.
