分布式消息队列Distributed message queue

约 35 分钟 · 前置：Ch03、Ch06、Ch18 · 从追加确认，讲到消费位点、业务事务与重放~35 min · after Ch03, Ch06, Ch18 · append acknowledgement → completed checkpoints → effects and replay

本章要点：分清四种成功，作出三项决定Core: four successes, three decisions

一条订单事件从写入到处理完成，会经历四种不同的成功：Leader 在本地追加日志、复制满足确认条件、消费者提交业务事务、消费组保存恢复位点。本章围绕三个问题展开：日志何时可读？消费者从哪里恢复？重复消费或再均衡后，由哪个原子边界保护业务结果？

下面六组动画沿着生产者（Producer）→ 分区主副本（Leader）→ 两个独立消费组 → 本地账本展开。输出仍在 Kafka 中时，还可以选择 Kafka 事务。讨论“恰好一次”（exactly-once）之前，要先说明事务究竟覆盖哪些操作。

An order event passes local append, replication acknowledgement, business commit, and checkpoint commit. Follow three decisions: when is the log readable, where does a consumer recover, and which atomic boundary protects an effect after replay or rebalance?

Six retained decks connect Producer → partition Leader → two independent groups → local ledger. A Kafka output transaction is an alternative path. Define its scope before claiming exactly-once.

需求与版本：先约定日志保留与独立重放Contract and version: a replayable log

业务处理和审计需要各自重读七天内的事件；同一订单的事件按订单编号 order_id 分区，保留分区内的追加顺序。副本数设为 RF=3，采用 acks=all、min.insync.replicas=2。消费者主动拉取（Pull）记录，各消费组分别保存下一次恢复读取的位置，也就是 next offset。普通消费先提交本地业务效果，再提交位点；重复投递由同一数据库中的 inbox 去重。KRaft 控制器（controller）负责元数据，不转发业务记录。

本章采用 Kafka 4.1 系列，API 与实现核对到 4.1.2，这里的版本号不表示最新版。教学集群显式设置 eligible.leader.replicas.version=0、unclean.leader.election.enable=false。4.1 新集群默认开启 ELR，此时安全选举候选者可以包含 ISR 之外的 ELR 副本。因此，后文图中禁止落后副本接任 Leader 的条件，需要结合这些配置理解，不能直接推广。[8]

Business and audit independently replay seven days. Partition by order_id with RF3, acks-all, and min.insync.replicas 2. Groups Pull and store separate next offsets. Ordinary consumption commits local effects before checkpoints; a same-database inbox handles replay. KRaft controllers own metadata.

This chapter pins Kafka 4.1, checking APIs and implementation at 4.1.2, without calling it latest. The teaching cluster explicitly sets eligible.leader.replicas.version=0 and unclean.leader.election.enable=false. New 4.1 clusters enable ELR, whose safe candidates can include replicas outside ISR; the pictured election restriction is conditional. [8]

容量：分别估算七天留存与消费并行能力Capacity: separate retention and consumer parallelism

存储按每天的总量估算，吞吐按峰值估算，分区数则要结合处理能力。本例平均每秒 1,157.407 条，十倍峰值为每秒 11,574.074 条，入口流量约 11.85 MB/s。七天日志保留三份，未压缩消息内容合计 2.1504 TB。这些数字由教学假设计算得出，还没有经过压测。

负载均匀时，至少需要 ceil(11,574.074 / 800)=15 个有效消费成员。业务组近期使用 32 个成员、预先创建 48 个分区，是为了预留分配余地，不能据此认为分区越多就自动越快。如果一个 key 占峰值流量的 20%，它每秒约有 2,314.815 条，是单成员每秒 800 条预算的 2.89 倍；增加空闲成员，也无法拆开这个 key 的工作。

Daily totals size storage, peaks size I/O, and processing capacity sizes partitions. Average is 1,157.407 records/s; the tenfold peak is 11,574.074, about 11.85 MB/s ingress. Seven-day RF3 uncompressed payload is 2.1504 TB. These are teaching calculations, not measurements.

Uniform load needs ceil(11,574.074 / 800)=15 effective members. Thirty-two near-term business-group members and 48 pre-created partitions reserve assignment headroom, not automatic speed. A 20% key receives 2,314.815/s, 2.89 times one member’s 800/s budget; idle members cannot split it.

磁盘：每份日志占 100M×1,024×7=716.8 GB，RF3 合计 2,150.4 GB。三台 broker 各有 2 TB 可用磁盘，平均每台的消息内容约占 35.84%；索引、段头、内部主题、备份和增长还要另算。一台故障后暂留两份，只有补入第三台并追上日志，才恢复三份副本；同一分区的三个副本不能放在两台 broker 上。

网络出口：峰值字节率×（两份副本复制＋两组消费）=47.41 MB/s，再加上全群每秒 20 MB 的重放和 20 MB 的修复，共 87.41 MB/s，约为 699.3 Mb/s。即使这些流量全部集中到一台，也只能说明没有超过原始 1 Gb/s 线速；协议与 TLS 开销、p99 延迟仍需在同一负载下联合压测。

磁盘 I/O：RF3 写入 35.56 MB/s，加上修复写入 20 MB/s，共 55.56 MB/s。保守地把复制、消费、重放和修复都按需要读盘的冷读计算，共需 87.41 MB/s。A11 中读、写各 100 MB/s 的预算，必须在同一混合负载下同时成立，还要核对仅剩两台 broker 时的负载分布。页缓存（page cache）占用内存，不增加磁盘的耐久存储容量。[1]

Disk: 100M×1,024×7=716.8 GB/copy, or 2,150.4 GB at RF3. Three 2 TB brokers each use 35.84% for payload; overhead, internal topics, backup, and growth are extra. One loss leaves two copies until a third broker catches up. An RF3 partition cannot place three replicas on two brokers.

Egress: peak bytes×(two replica streams+two groups)=47.41 MB/s; replay 20 and repair 20 raise it to 87.41 MB/s≈699.3 Mb/s. Even worst concentration below raw 1 Gb/s only rejects obvious undersizing; protocol, TLS, and p99 still need joint tests.

Disk I/O: RF3 writes 35.56 plus repair writes 20 gives 55.56 MB/s. Conservatively treating replication, consumption, replay, and repair as cold reads gives 87.41 MB/s. Both 100 MB/s budgets must hold under the same mixed load; check two-survivor placement too. Page cache uses RAM rather than durable capacity. [1]

1 · 日志路径：追加确认为什么不代表业务完成1 · Log path: append acknowledgement is not an effect

先沿完整图看一次写入，再看两组独立读取。生产者 SDK 缓存 Leader 元数据，按 key 选择分区，并把记录成批直接发送给 Leader。只有满足复制条件，Leader 才返回 offset。如果响应丢失，结果就是未知（UNKNOWN）：应保留 event_id，让同一个幂等生产者按协议重试原序列。应用重新调用 send，或重启后重新构造事件，并不会自动归入先前那次协议重试。[2][7]

Billing 与 Audit 读取同一份日志，但分别保存 (group,topic,partition) → committed_next。处理完 41 后保存 42，表示下次从 42 恢复；读取本身不删除记录，因此增加一个消费组主要会增加读取出口流量。主动拉取有助于消费者控制取数速率，但仍要限制批次大小、在途工作量和下游调用。linger=5 ms 为批量发送留出少量等待时间；健康状态下写入 p99<100 ms 仍是待验证的目标。[1][4]

delete 留存策略按符合条件的旧日志段清理，七天不表示每条记录都精确在到期时删除。如果 Audit 需要从 20 读取，但日志起点已经是 41，本例配置 auto.offset.reset=none 会明确失败并告警。改成 latest 或 earliest 都不能找回已经删除的历史；增加分区也不会搬动已有日志段。[3][6][10]

Read the final frame’s two Fetch loops. Producer SDK caches leader metadata, selects a partition by key, and batches directly to Leader. The offset reply follows replication; a lost reply is UNKNOWN. Keep event_id and let the same idempotent producer retry its protocol sequence. A fresh application send or reconstructed event after restart is a different case. [2][7]

Billing and Audit share logs but separately store (group,topic,partition) → committed_next. Processing 41 permits 42; reading does not delete. Another group adds read traffic. Pull still needs bounded batches, in-flight work, and downstream calls. Linger 5 ms trades some waiting for batching; healthy write p99<100 ms remains a target. [1][4]

Delete retention removes eligible old segments, not exact per-record expiry. If Audit needs 20 but log start is 41, auto.offset.reset=none fails and alerts. Neither latest nor earliest restores deleted history, and new partitions do not move old segments. [3][6][10]

2 · 顺序：日志追加、业务处理与扩区各有什么保证2 · Order: append, effects, and expansion

分区函数、编码方式和分区数都固定时，同一个 order_id 会进入同一份日志。图中 h=55 只是示意，55 mod 48=7。多个生产者并发写入时，broker 形成的是追加顺序，不会自动替业务建立因果顺序。消费者还要按 key 串行执行，或校验业务版本，才能约束业务效果的完成次序。生产者幂等也需要兼容的配置：acks=all、retries>0、max.in.flight≤5。[2]

从 48 个分区增加到 96 个时，均匀整数哈希中，一半保留原映射，另一半进入新分区；从 48 增加到 49 时，在完整的均匀周期内有 48/49 改变映射。旧记录仍在 P7，新记录却可能进入 P55，跨分区消费就可能让 v2 先完成，破坏业务顺序。图中的红线表示不安全的设计选择，并不是说 Kafka 会拒绝增加分区的请求。[10]

如果切换必须保序，先撤销旧路由生产者的写入资格（fence），确认旧 key 的最后业务序号已经处理，再发布新路由。图中的迁移门 Migration gate 需要由业务协议实现。若为全局总序只用一个分区，消费组的成员并行上限就降为 1，每秒 800 条接不住每秒约 11,574 条。热点 key 只能通过优化处理、单独分配预算，或经产品同意缩小保序范围来应对；随机加盐后，还要解决归并与顺序问题。

With stable partition function, encoding, and count, an order_id stays on one log. Illustrative h=55 maps to P7 under mod 48. Concurrent producers establish broker append order, not business causality. Per-key serialization or version checks constrain effect order. Idempotence also requires compatible acks-all, retries>0, and max.in.flight≤5. [2]

Uniform hashes move half at 48→96; 48→49 moves 48/49 over a full uniform cycle. Old records stay in P7 while new records may enter P55, allowing v2 to finish first. The red route is an unsafe design choice, not a broker rejection of partition expansion. [10]

An ordered cutover fences old-route producers, verifies the final business sequence was processed, then publishes new routing. Migration gate is an application protocol. One partition for global order caps member parallelism at 1; 800/s cannot handle 11,574/s. Optimize a hot key, isolate its budget, or explicitly weaken its ordering scope; random salting requires merging or weaker order.

3 · 复制：分清 HW、acks、minISR 与磁盘耐久3 · Replication: distinguish HW, acks, minISR, and disk durability

这组图假设副本成员稳定，没有待加入 ISR 的副本，也没有重分配竞态。ISR={Leader,Follower1}。Leader 追加 41 后，日志末端 LEO=42，但 HW 仍为 41。Follower1 先 Fetch 并追加 41，再在下一轮 Fetch(next=42) 中报告进度，Leader 才把 HW 推进到 42，并确认 41。HW 是排他上界，普通读取只能得到 offset<HW 的记录。Follower2 在 ISR 外追赶，不影响这次确认。[1]

acks=all 要等待 ISR 中的全部副本，min=2 则规定成功所需的最低数量，计数包含 Leader。ISR 有三份时，不能任选两份。ISR 副本数低于门槛时，追加前或追加后都可能报 NotEnoughReplicas，因此收到错误不总能说明记录没有写入。在 4.1.2 中，严格的 minISR 规则还会在 ISR 低于 min 时阻止 HW 推进；显式设置 ELR=0，也不会恢复为旧版行为。真实实现还考虑待加入副本等状态，这组图只说明稳定成员下的路径，不是完整的 broker 算法。[3][16]

复制进度不等于磁盘刷写 fsync。各台机器上的日志可能先写入页缓存；acks=all 不能证明每条记录都已在每个副本完成磁盘 flush。它的安全性依赖至少一份安全副本存活、存储没有损坏，以及选举过程安全等条件。电源、磁盘或机房共同失效，需要另行设计耐久性与故障域方案。按 A1 的配置，没有安全候选者时就应等待，不能让缺少已确认记录 41 的落后副本接任 Leader。[1][2][8]

read_uncommitted 的名称只涉及事务过滤，它也不能读取仅在 Leader 上的日志尾部。read_committed 还受稳定事务边界 LSO 限制。例如 HW 已到 46，但 offset 42 处的事务尚未完成，就只能读到 42 之前，连非事务记录 44 也需要等待；已经中止的事务记录也会被过滤。[4][6]

This deck assumes stable membership with no pending ISR admission or reassignment. ISR={Leader,Follower 1}. Appending 41 makes Leader LEO=42 but leaves HW=41. Follower 1 Fetches and appends 41, then a later Fetch(next 42) reports its progress; Leader advances HW=42 and acknowledges 41. HW is exclusive: ordinary reads stop before it. Follower 2 outside ISR does not delay this ack. [1]

acks=all waits for the ISR; min=2 is the minimum success threshold, including Leader. An ISR of three is not any two votes. NotEnoughReplicas can occur before or after append, so errors need not mean absence. In 4.1.2 strict minISR also blocks HW advancement below min, even with ELR0. The implementation considers pending admissions too; this is not a complete broker algorithm. [3][16]

Replication progress is not fsync. Each machine may first hold writes in page cache. An acks-all response does not certify a per-record flush on every disk. Its safety depends on a surviving safe copy, working storage, and safe elections; correlated power, disk, or site loss needs a separate durability and placement plan. Under A1, wait without a safe candidate rather than promote a replica missing acknowledged 41. [1][2][8]

read_uncommitted still cannot read a leader-only tail; its name concerns transactions. read_committed also respects LSO: if HW=46 includes an open transaction starting 42, it stops before 42, can withhold nontransactional 44 too, and filters aborted transactions. [4][6]

4 · 消费位点：按实际交付顺序提交完成前缀4 · Consumer checkpoints: commit the completed prefix

先分清拉取位置 position、实际交付记录的完成前缀，以及已提交位点 committed。一次 poll 拿到 41–50 后，本地 position 可以到 51，但业务效果还没完成，不能据此提交 51。下面的 Java 17 代码只跟踪一个分区的一批实际交付记录，每批最多 500 条。处理期间暂停该分区，poll 线程仍继续处理消费组协议。工作线程把成功结果放入邮箱，由同一个 poll 线程调用 completed；它们不并发调用 KafkaConsumer。[4]

代码的输入是按升序排列的实际 offset，以及本次 poll 的 nextOffsets 终点；输出是第一条未完成记录的 offset，全部完成后才使用终点。offset 可能不连续。例如实际交付 [41,45,50]，终点为 53：先完成 50，仍只能提交 41；再完成 41，可以提交 45；全部完成后才提交 53。不存在的 42–44 无需等待，尚未完成的 45 则不能越过。[14]

Separate position, completion frontier, and committed position. Polling 41–50 can make position 51 before effects finish. This Java 17 tracker holds one bounded batch per partition, at most 500 records. Pause that partition while the poll thread continues membership work. Workers send completion messages to that thread; they never call KafkaConsumer concurrently. [4]

Inputs are sorted delivered offsets and that poll’s nextOffsets terminal. The output is the first unfinished delivered offset, or the terminal after all finish. For [41,45,50] ending 53, finishing 50 leaves 41; finishing 41 permits 45; completing all permits 53. Missing 42–44 are not tasks, and unfinished 45 cannot be skipped. [14]

public List<Ticket> register(List<Long> offsets, long polledNext) {
    // Exactly one bounded batch per partition; pause it until pending is empty.
    if (phase != Phase.ACTIVE || !pending.isEmpty()) throw new IllegalStateException();
    List<Long> copy = List.copyOf(offsets);
    if (copy.size() > maxBatch || polledNext < safeNext) throw new IllegalArgumentException();
    long previous = -1;
    for (long offset : copy) {
        if (offset < safeNext || offset <= previous || offset >= polledNext)
            throw new IllegalArgumentException();
        previous = offset;
    }
    batchNext = polledNext; // from this poll's nextOffsets(), not a later position()
    var tickets = new ArrayList<Ticket>();
    for (long offset : copy) {
        Ticket ticket = new Ticket(this, offset);
        pending.addLast(new Work(ticket)); tickets.add(ticket);
    }
    safeNext = pending.isEmpty() ? batchNext : pending.getFirst().ticket.offset;
    return List.copyOf(tickets);
}

public boolean completed(Ticket ticket) {
    // A fresh tracker is created on assignment; old workers cannot complete it.
    if (phase == Phase.CLOSED || ticket == null || ticket.owner != this) return false;
    Work found = null;
    for (Work work : pending) if (work.ticket == ticket) { found = work; break; }
    if (found == null || found.done) return false;
    found.done = true; // only after the durable effect (or duplicate result) succeeds
    while (!pending.isEmpty() && pending.getFirst().done) pending.removeFirst();
    safeNext = pending.isEmpty() ? batchNext : pending.getFirst().ticket.offset;
    return true;
}

这段代码节选自 PartitionProgress.java，完整类可以编译。它先校验整批输入，再修改状态；校验失败就不领取新工作。这个内存对象只计算安全位点，不提供分布式所有权或持久保存能力。业务效果失败，或结果仍为 UNKNOWN 时，不能标记成功；应暂停该分区，用同一事件重试，不能提交失败记录的后一位来跳过它。

对于只完成一部分的批次，教学适配器使用 new OffsetAndMetadata(safeNext)，省略没有跟踪的 epoch。只有整批完成，才原样复用本次 nextOffsets 元数据；不能把批尾的 epoch 附在更早的完成前缀上。

This teaching excerpt comes from compilable PartitionProgress.java. Validate the entire batch before mutation. This in-memory object computes a checkpoint, not distributed ownership or durability. A failed or UNKNOWN effect never completes its ticket; pause and retry the same event rather than skip it. For a partial prefix, this adapter uses new OffsetAndMetadata(safeNext), omitting the untracked epoch. Only a fully completed batch reuses its nextOffsets metadata; never attach a terminal epoch to an earlier prefix.

撤销与丢失：停止工作后，还要判断能否提交Revoked versus lost: stop work, then determine commit eligibility

使用 4.1 的新协议 group.protocol=consumer，需要客户端显式启用；心跳与会话时间由服务端控制，每个成员都有自己的 member epoch，没有转移的分区可以继续处理。对于需要交接的分区，正常撤销时，旧成员还可以在限定时间内等待在途工作完成，再尝试提交安全位点；所有权已经丢失时，新成员可能早已接手，旧成员不能再提交。必须区分这两个回调。[5][13]

The 4.1 group.protocol=consumer protocol needs client opt-in; server settings control heartbeat and session timing; members have individual epochs, and unmoved partitions may continue. Graceful revocation allows bounded drain/commit; after ownership is lost, another member may already own the partition and no commit is attempted. Distinguish the callbacks. [5][13]

# Teaching adapter: ALL KafkaConsumer calls/tracker changes run on the poll thread.
# Workers post confirmed durable results; UNKNOWN never completes a ticket.
onEveryPoll(records):                                # includes an EMPTY poll
  apply success mailbox to each message's tracker/ticket; closed old trackers ignore it
  for p in records.nextOffsets().keys:
    tasks = tracker[p].register(records[p].offsets, records.nextOffsets()[p].offset)
    terminal[p] = records.nextOffsets()[p]            # save THIS poll's metadata
    consumer.pause({p}); dispatch tasks to bounded per-key-serial workers
  eligible = ALL current-owned ACTIVE trackers         # not only this poll's partitions
  for p,t in eligible: if t.idle(): consumer.resume({p})
  map = safeMap(eligible)
  if map is not empty:
    try: consumer.commitSync(map, bounded_timeout)
    catch TimeoutException: record UNKNOWN; retain work state; do not invent a rollback
    catch ownership_or_rebalance_rejection: discard map; process handoff before rebuilding
  # Never retry a saved map across reassignment; a later commit rebuilds current eligibility.

safeMap(eligible):
  map = {}
  for p,t in eligible:
    safe = t.checkpoint()                              # first unfinished offset or terminal
    if t.idle() and terminal[p] exists and terminal[p].offset == safe:
      map[p] = terminal[p]                            # whole batch: original epoch metadata
    else: map[p] = new OffsetAndMetadata(safe)         # partial/no poll: omit unknown epoch
  return map                                          # includes completions on empty polls

onPartitionsRevoked(partitions):
  stop admitting work for these partitions; tracker.beginRevocation()
  until short drain deadline: apply queued successful completions
  try: consumer.commitSync(safeMap(THESE still-eligible trackers), bounded_timeout)
  catch TimeoutException: record UNKNOWN
  catch ownership_or_rebalance_rejection: replay from the stored checkpoint
  finally: close trackers; cancel remaining work; forget ownership and terminal metadata

onPartitionsLost(partitions):
  close trackers; cancel work; forget ownership and terminal metadata  # NO commit

onPartitionsAssigned(partitions):
  create NEW trackers for newly acquired partitions at recovered next offsets
  retain trackers for partitions whose ownership did not move
# Never use no-argument commitSync(): poll position may exceed completed work.

每一轮 poll，即使没有取到新记录，也先处理完成邮箱，再从全部当前 ACTIVE 分区构造显式位点 map，并实际调用 commitSync；不能只遍历本轮取到新记录的分区。正常撤销时，map 只包含这次撤销且仍有提交资格的分区。位点提交超时，就把结果记为 UNKNOWN；明确被拒绝后，则从可靠检查点重放。

新分配必须创建新 tracker，旧任务的回调不能推进它；broker 仍需按成员 epoch 拒绝过期提交。关闭本地对象或取消工作，都不能撤回已经发出的数据库或 HTTP 调用，重放安全仍依赖下一节的业务去重。交接结束时必须关闭旧 tracker，不能跨分配重试旧快照。

Every poll, including an empty one, applies completions and commits an explicit map from all currently ACTIVE trackers, not just partitions with new records. During revocation the map is limited to those still-eligible revoked partitions. Timeout is UNKNOWN; rejection permits replay from the stored checkpoint. A new assignment gets a fresh tracker, so old callbacks cannot advance it; broker member-epoch fencing remains necessary. Closing/cancelling local work cannot undo DB/HTTP calls already sent. Effect dedupe still matters, and every exit closes the old tracker rather than retry its snapshot across assignments.

5 · 重放：本地业务事务与 Kafka 事务分别保证什么5 · Replay: separate local effects from Kafka transactions

本地账本使用同一数据库的事务，Kafka 位点在这个事务之外。第 2–4 帧先一起提交 inbox 去重记录与 ledger 账本更新，随后才把记录 41 的工作标为成功，并提交 next=42。如果数据库提交成功、位点提交却被拒绝，新成员重放 41 时会读取原结果，不再重复增加账本金额。唯一键采用稳定的 (handler,event_id)，不能随消费组改名而重置；同一个事件还必须校验决定业务效果的完整意图。

下例只给账户余额增加整数分，因此完整意图就是账户 account_id 与增量 delta_cents。数据库提供唯一约束、事务原子性与并发更新保证。如果 ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING 没有插入新记录，就在 READ COMMITTED 下通过下一条 SELECT 读取并发胜出者的记录。该事件行只能由这个事务写入，表结构见 schema.sql。[15]

Use a same-database transaction for the ledger, outside Kafka checkpoints. Frames 2–4 commit inbox and ledger together, then complete work 41 and checkpoint 42. If the checkpoint is rejected, replay 41 returns the stored result without a second increment. Stable (handler,event_id) does not reset when a consumer group is renamed; validate the full effect intent.

This example increments integer cents; complete intent is account_id plus delta_cents. The database supplies uniqueness, transaction atomicity, and concurrent updates. After ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING inserts nothing, the next READ COMMITTED SELECT reads the winner. Only this transaction writes event rows; see schema.sql. [15]

# PostgreSQL 18 READ COMMITTED; one authoritative database, bound parameters.
# Input: trusted handler/event_id + COMPLETE effect intent (account_id, delta_cents).
# Output: APPLIED/ALREADY_APPLIED(result) or failure; no Kafka commit inside this DB txn.
applyEvent(handler, event_id, account_id, delta_cents):
  BEGIN
  inserted = INSERT INTO inbox(handler,event_id,account_id,delta_cents)
    VALUES(:handler,:event_id,:account_id,:delta_cents)
    ON CONFLICT (handler,event_id) DO NOTHING RETURNING event_id
  if inserted is empty:
    old = SELECT account_id,delta_cents,result_balance FROM inbox
      WHERE handler=:handler AND event_id=:event_id
    if old missing or old.result_balance is NULL: ROLLBACK; fail invariant
    if old.account_id != account_id or old.delta_cents != delta_cents:
      ROLLBACK; fail EVENT_ID_REUSED_WITH_DIFFERENT_INTENT
    COMMIT; return ALREADY_APPLIED(old.result_balance)
  result = UPDATE ledger SET balance_cents=balance_cents+:delta_cents
    WHERE account_id=:account_id RETURNING balance_cents
  if result is empty: ROLLBACK; fail UNKNOWN_ACCOUNT
  UPDATE inbox SET result_balance=:result WHERE handler=:handler AND event_id=:event_id
  COMMIT                               # only confirmed commit completes this work
  return APPLIED(result)
# Any statement error: ROLLBACK; do not complete the worker ticket.
# COMMIT timeout/connection loss: discard connection; retry SAME event + full intent.
# Never commit the inbox row separately or call an external bank in this transaction.

正常路径中，初始余额为 100，事件 9001 增加 30，inbox 保存结果 130；确认完成后，消费者提交位点 42。如果数据库 COMMIT 成功，而进程在提交 Kafka 位点前崩溃，重放 9001 仍返回原结果 130；若复用同一 ID，却把增量改成 99，就应拒绝。任何 SQL 失败都要回滚整个事务，包括已经插入的 inbox 记录；COMMIT 结果未知时，应丢弃连接，并用原来的事件身份与完整意图重试。去重记录的保留期必须覆盖所有允许的重放窗口，不能只按七天日志留存决定；备份恢复时，inbox 与 ledger 也必须保持一致。

Normal: balance 100 plus event 9001 delta 30 stores result 130, then permits checkpoint 42. Counterexample: a crash after DB commit but before Kafka commit replays 9001 and still returns 130; reusing the ID for delta 99 is rejected. SQL failure rolls back the inbox too. An unknown commit discards its connection and retries the same identity/intent. Retain dedupe through every permitted replay window, not merely seven-day log retention, and recover inbox and ledger consistently.

Kafka 事务：提交超时后，继续确认同一个决定Kafka transactions: a timeout retries the same decision

输出也留在 Kafka 中时，可以用事务绑定输出记录与安全的 next 位点。这是另一条处理路径，并不是在上面的数据库事务外再包一层事务。对整批记录完成纯转换后，调用 sendOffsetsToTransaction(batch.nextOffsets(), consumer.groupMetadata())，不再单独调用 commitSync。下游使用 read_committed；位点暂存 ACK 和 Produce ACK 都不代表 EndTxn 成功。稳定的 transactional.id 标识逻辑任务，它提供的生产者隔离与消费组成员 epoch 是两套 fencing 机制。[7][11][12]

最容易混淆的是提交超时：commitTransaction() 可能正在完成提交，此时不能改为调用 abort。下例只重试同一次 commit；重试预算耗尽，就关闭生产者和消费者，恢复时重新执行 initTransactions，并从可靠的消费组位点读取。只有在提交之前遇到明确允许中止的错误，才走 abort 路径；如果 abort 本身也没有得到确认，同样不能开始下一笔事务。[7]

When outputs stay in Kafka, a transaction binds them to safe next offsets. This is an alternative to the DB path, not an outer transaction around it. After a pure whole-batch transform, use sendOffsetsToTransaction(batch.nextOffsets(), consumer.groupMetadata()) without a separate commitSync. Downstream uses read_committed. Produce and staged-offset ACKs do not finish EndTxn. Stable transactional.id fences the logical task, separately from member epochs. [7][11][12]

The critical exception is commit timeout: commitTransaction may already be completing, so do not switch to abort. Retry the same commit; if the budget ends, close both clients, reinitialize transactions, and recover stored group offsets. Only an explicitly abortable precommit error takes the abort path. An unresolved abort cannot start a new transaction either. [7]

# Kafka 4.1.2 API-shaped pseudocode; pure transform, no DB/HTTP side effects.
# Stable transactional.id per logical task; auto.commit=false; input/output read_committed.
producer.initTransactions()             # resolve the previous producer incarnation first
batch = consumer.poll(bounded_duration)
try:
  producer.beginTransaction()
  for record in batch:
    producer.send(transformToKafkaRecord(record))
  producer.sendOffsetsToTransaction(batch.nextOffsets(), consumer.groupMetadata())
except fatal_producer_error:            # fencing, authorization, invalid epoch, etc.
  close producer and consumer; stop
except abortable_precommit_error:
  try: producer.abortTransaction()
  except timeout_or_other_error: close producer and consumer; restart
  close producer and consumer; restart  # recover committed offsets, not current position

while bounded_commit_retry_budget_remains:
  try:
    producer.commitTransaction()
    return COMMITTED                   # output and next offsets share this outcome
  except TimeoutException:
    continue                           # retry SAME commit; DO NOT switch to abort
  except other_error:
    close producer and consumer; restart
close producer and consumer; restart   # outcome UNKNOWN; initTransactions resolves it
# Never start the next batch after an unresolved commit/abort.
# Restart is an application recovery loop; it does not assert that UNKNOWN was aborted.

外部业务效果需要另外安排。支付服务商 PSP 或 HTTP 调用都不在 Kafka 事务范围内。如果需要付款，先在同一数据库事务中提交 inbox 与持久付款任务 payment_job，再推进位点。任务工作进程复用业务幂等键；遇到 UNKNOWN 时保留未完成责任，并查询核对付款结果。如果目标端不提供幂等与查询契约，本地唯一记录无法证明只扣款一次。

较长的 Kafka 事务还会增加等待稳定事务边界 LSO 的时间，因此应限制事务大小与时长，并监控尚未结束的事务。

External effects: PSP/HTTP is outside Kafka’s transaction scope. Persist inbox and a payment_job together before checkpointing the obligation. A job worker reuses a business idempotency key and reconciles UNKNOWN. A local unique row cannot prove one charge without destination idempotency/query guarantees. Bound Kafka transaction size/duration and monitor open transactions because long ones delay LSO.

6 · 故障转移：保住历史后，为什么还会拒绝新写入6 · Failover: preserved history does not imply writable state

三台专职 KRaft 控制器需要两票形成多数派，可承受一台失效；它们保存 broker 注册信息、分区与 Leader epoch，以及副本元数据。数据 Produce、消费者 Fetch 和副本 Fetch 仍直连 broker，元数据多数派不能代替 ISR。小环境可以采用 combined 合并部署；关键部署选择专职控制器，有助于隔离 broker 的重磁盘停顿，并分开维护故障域。[9]

A 失联后，客户端自己的计时器超时。仍可用的控制器多数派把保存了已确认记录 41 的 B 提升为新 Leader，客户端刷新元数据后改连 B。此时 ISR 只有 B，1<min2，新发起的 acks=all 写入会被拒绝，HW 也不能继续推进。因此，要分别讨论已经保留的提交前缀与新写入的可用性。恢复写入应等待安全副本追上，不能让健康检查悄悄降低 min。

控制器失去多数派时，已经稳定的 Leader 可能暂时按现有元数据继续处理部分工作，但新选举、ISR 变更等会受阻，不能承诺无限继续。监控至少要分别观察低于最小 ISR 的分区（UnderMinIsr）、拒绝写入、复制与可读水位、每个分区的消费积压 lag、未结束事务，以及元数据提交。数据副本与控制器都要按故障域放置；三台机器不自动代表三个独立故障域。

Three dedicated KRaft controllers need two votes and tolerate one loss. They store broker registration, partition/leader epochs, and replica metadata. Produce, consumer Fetch, and replica Fetch still target brokers; metadata majority is not ISR. Dedicated critical deployments isolate disk-heavy broker stalls and maintenance domains; combined mode suits smaller environments. [9]

A disappears and the client’s own timer expires. A live quorum promotes B, which holds acknowledged 41. After refreshing metadata, the client reaches B. ISR={B} is below min 2, so new acks-all writes are rejected and HW cannot advance. Preserved committed history and new write availability are distinct; restore a safe replica rather than silently lower min.

Without controller majority, stable leaders may continue some work using existing metadata, but elections and ISR changes can stall; continuation is not indefinite. Monitor UnderMinIsr, rejected writes, replication/readable boundaries, per-partition lag, open transactions, and metadata commits separately. Three brokers or controllers do not automatically create three independent failure domains.

接口与失败轨迹：明确结果，再决定从哪里恢复Interfaces and failures: where recovery starts

记录地址 (topic,partition,offset) 与业务事件身份 event_id 是两回事。拉取位置 position、完成前缀和已提交的 committed_next 也要分别记录；两个 offset 的差值不一定等于精确消息条数。下面的契约用于白板讲解，省略了真实协议中的校验和限额字段，不能当作可直接调用的 Java 方法签名。

A record address (topic,partition,offset) is not business identity event_id. Track position, completed frontier, and committed_next separately; an offset delta is not an exact message count. These whiteboard contracts omit protocol validation/quota fields and are not Java signatures.

Produce(topic, partition, records[], producer_id, epoch, sequence)
  → base_offset | explicit rejection | timeout: outcome UNKNOWN
Fetch(topic, partition, fetch_offset, max_bytes, isolation)
  → records[] | long-poll empty | OffsetOutOfRange
OffsetCommit(group, member_id, member_epoch, partitions[].next_offset)
  → persisted | stale member rejection | timeout: outcome UNKNOWN

Record(topic, partition, offset, event_id, key, value, version)
GroupOffset(group, topic, partition, committed_next)
Business DB transaction: UNIQUE inbox(handler, event_id) + ledger update
Kafka transaction: output records + safe next offsets + current group metadata

可以用这些反例检查恢复流程：先提交 42、再处理 41，崩溃后可能永久漏掉这次业务；先单独提交 inbox、再写 ledger，可能把从未执行的业务误认为已经完成；仅取消旧线程，无法撤回远端调用。提交超时不表示确定失败，也不允许换一个身份重做；Kafka 事务提交超时后，更不能直接改为 abort。恢复时必须保留原事件身份，并继续承担尚未确认完成的工作。

Counterexamples: checkpoint 42 before effect 41 can omit work; committing inbox separately can suppress an unperformed effect; cancelling a thread cannot undo a remote call; timeout is not definite failure or permission for a new identity; a Kafka commit timeout cannot switch to abort. Recovery retains event identity and unfinished obligations.

展开扩展追问与积压计算Follow-ups and backlog calculation

同一业务组从 32 个成员增加到 72 个时，48 个分区至多分配给 48 个活跃持有者，另外 24 个成员空闲。若输入均匀，每秒 11,574.074 条，而 10 个成员合计处理 10×800 条，一分钟会积压 214,444.444 条。扩到 20 个成员后，每秒净排空速度为 16,000−11,574.074=4,425.926 条，需要 48.45 秒；不能直接用积压量除以总消费速率，因为新记录还在持续进入。

若留存延长到一个月，消息内容需要 100M×1,024×30×3=9.216 TB，已经超过现有 6 TB；保留一年则需要 112.128 TB。扩留存时，还要同时增加冷读与修复预算。如果积压集中在一个热点 key 上，处理能力仍受单分区每秒 800 条的限制，上述均匀负载的排空公式就不适用。

Growing one business group from 32 members to 72 leaves at most 48 active owners and 24 idle. Uniform ingress 11,574.074/s with 10×800 processing builds 214,444.444 records in one minute. Twenty members drain at net 16,000−11,574.074=4,425.926/s, taking 48.45s rather than dividing by gross throughput.

One month needs 100M×1,024×30×3=9.216 TB, beyond 6 TB; one year needs 112.128 TB. Expand cold reads, retention, and repair together. A hot-key backlog retains its 800/s ceiling, so the uniform drainage equation does not apply.
