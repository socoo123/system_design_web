30 秒开场：先固定日志契约和版本The 30-second open: fix the log contract and version

中文

我设计可重放的分区日志：Producer 按订单 key 写 Leader，满足 acks=all 与 min.insync.replicas=2 才确认；消费者自己 Fetch，两个组分别保存 next offset，读过不会删除。基本路径是至少一次，本地业务用同事务的 inbox 和副作用去重。顺序只到分区日志；控制面是独立 KRaft，业务字节不经过 controller。

需求先问重放、顺序和副作用。若只要一个 worker 取走一条任务，可选传统任务队列；本题要求业务与审计独立重读七天，才把分区日志留下。本文不展开 exchange、流计算或多地域复制。数据库提交必须一起产生事件时，先把业务行与 outbox 同事务写入，再让 relay produce；发送函数本身没有把两个系统原子提交。

本章固定 Apache Kafka 4.1 文档和普通 consumer group，不宣称它是 2026 最新版本。为把传统 ISR 推导讲清，教学集群显式设 eligible.leader.replicas.version=0。4.1 新集群默认开启 ELR；开启时安全候选可包含 ISR 外的 ELR，不能照搬「只能选 ISR」的规则。ELR 是安全恢复集合，不是「谁活着谁当主」的 unclean 选举。[8]

English

I design a replayable partitioned log: Producer writes the key-selected Leader and succeeds under acks=all with min.insync.replicas=2. Consumers Fetch and two groups store separate next offsets; reading does not delete. The base path is at-least-once, with a transactional inbox and local effect. Order stops at the partition log. Dedicated KRaft controllers handle metadata, not business bytes.

Ask first about replay, order and effects. A task queue can serve one worker claiming each task; independent seven-day business and audit replay selects this log. Exchanges, stream computation and multi-region replication are outside scope. If a database write must create an event, commit the row and outbox together and let a relay produce; a send function does not atomically commit both systems.

This chapter pins Apache Kafka 4.1 documentation and ordinary consumer groups without calling 4.1 the latest 2026 release. The teaching cluster explicitly sets eligible.leader.replicas.version=0 for traditional ISR reasoning. New 4.1 clusters enable ELR by default; safe candidates can then include ELR outside ISR. ELR is a safe recovery set, not unclean election of any live replica. [8]

先算留存，再留出消费、重放和修复的预算Compute retention, then budget consumers, replay and repair

中文

日均速率为 100,000,000÷86,400=1,157.407 条/秒；峰值乘十是 11,574.074。入口为 11,574.074×1,024=11,851,852 B/s，约 11.85 MB/s。日增量 102.4 GB，七天一份 716.8 GB，三份合计 716.8×3=2,150.4 GB=2.1504 TB。这是未压缩 payload 下限，不含索引、段头、内部 topic、备份和增长，也不能把十倍峰值当成全天字节积分。

A10 买三台、每台 2 TB 可用盘，总计 6 TB。RF3 分区在三个不同 broker 各放一份，所以每台 payload 716.8 GB，水位 35.84%，余下 64.16% 给文件开销、内部主题、重建和增长。一台消失时，余下两台各有一份，配置 RF3 不变，实际只剩两份；必须补出第三台、按修复预算追上日志，才能恢复三份。不能把 2.1504 TB÷2 当成两台上的合法 RF3 配置，同一分区的三个副本不能挤在两个 broker 上。

正常峰值除了 11.85 MB/s 入口，还有两份副本约 23.70 MB/s，以及两个组各读一份合计 23.70 MB/s。broker 聚合出口约 47.41 MB/s；A12 再加重放 20 与修复 20，合计 87.41 MB/s，约 699.3 Mb/s。即使出口最坏汇到一台，仍低于 1 Gb/s 的原始线速；但协议、TLS、跨故障域和控制流也要占带宽，所以这只是排除明显不够的配置，不能宣称低于线速就满足 p99。

磁盘也做保守检查：三份写入聚合约 35.56 MB/s，加修复写 20 是 55.56；冷读最坏把复制、两组消费、重放和修复都算作盘读，约 87.41 MB/s。两者各低于 A11 的 100 MB/s 假设预算；它要求同一混合负载同时满足读写，不是各自独测的峰值。上线前还要联合压测 CPU、TLS 和两台存活时的分布。Page cache 使用 RAM，不是额外磁盘容量；不能把全量重放当成免费内存命中。[1]

分区数由处理能力反推。均匀时 11,574.074÷800=14.47，至少 15 个成员；近期上限 32，预建 48 个区保留调度余量，32 台可给 25,600 条/秒预算。每个成员可能持有多个区，所以没有「预留分区天然无人读」这回事。若 20% 集中一个 key，11,574.074×0.2=2,314.815 条/秒，超过单成员 800 约 2.89 倍；扩到 48 台也救不了这个 key。

留存从七天变一年，1e8×1,024×365×3=112.128 TB。这个变化可能要求分层存储、备份和冷读预算，不能只改一行 TTL。压缩能减少字节，却不改变副作用吞吐和热 key 顺序；只提高磁盘容量也不改变单消费者的 800/s。每个乘法都要说出它影响的是日志保留、复制、读出口还是业务处理，才不会把一张硬件采购单当成系统设计。

English

Average is 100,000,000/86,400=1,157.407 records/s; the tenfold peak is 11,574.074. Ingress is 11,574.074×1,024=11,851,852 B/s, about 11.85 MB/s. This uncompressed payload floor excludes indexes, headers, internal topics, backups and growth; the peak factor is not applied to all-day bytes.

Normal peak has 11.85 MB/s ingress, 23.70 MB/s for two replica streams and 23.70 MB/s for two consumer groups. Aggregate broker egress is 47.41 MB/s; replay 20 and repair 20 raise it to 87.41 MB/s, about 699.3 Mb/s. Even concentrated on one broker this is below raw 1 Gb/s line rate, but protocol, TLS and control traffic also consume bandwidth. Page cache consumes RAM, not persisted capacity, and full replay is not a free cache hit. [1]

1 · Produce、Fetch、留存是三份不同契约1 · Produce, Fetch and retention are different contracts

中文

正常路径从 Producer 的客户端库开始。它缓存主题和分区 leader 元数据，按 key 选区，攒一个 batch，再直连 Leader。追加结果先来自本地 Partition log；复制策略满足后，Leader 返回 base_offset。这个 offset 证明日志接受成功，没有承诺 Billing 已处理。若响应在网络里消失，Producer 只知道超时，不知道记录是否已经写入；下一笔要用原生产者序列重试，不能随手制造一个新业务事件。

Billing 从自己组的 next offset 发 Fetch，Leader 返回一批记录；Audit 也可读同一段，但有自己的恢复位置。图上的两条绿回程分别是 Produce 成功和 Fetch 记录，不应合成 Broker 推给 Consumer 的一根箭头。一个分区日志可由很多组重读，增加组主要增大读取流量，不需要给每组复制一套七天文件。这正是前面把组数加到出口算式的原因。[1][4]

选择 Pull 是为了把取数据的速率交给消费者；它还得限制 poll 返回条数、在途工作和下游调用，不能把拉到内存等同已处理。空分区长轮询减少空转。A13 的 linger=5 ms 与小 batch 用吞吐换一点排队时间，健康写 p99<100 ms 只是目标，既不是 Kafka 固定十毫秒提交，也不是把同机房 RTT 加几次就能保证。broker 反压会继续拖长等待。[2]

日志按 segment 滚动，delete 留存依据时间或配置的每分区字节上限删除合格旧段，而不是消费者确认后删单条。A6 不设置提前删除的容量上限；七天是保留目标，并非每条记录在第七天那个毫秒准时消失。段粒度和清理周期会有尾差。磁盘意外满时可能报写入失败或使副本不可用，不能擅自删尚未符合策略的业务日志来换可写性。[3]

故障恢复先比较 next offset 与 log start offset。若 start=41，而审计还要 Fetch20，返回越界错误；本题把 auto.offset.reset 设为 none，显式停止并告警，决定从备份恢复还是经业务批准跳过。latest 不是自动修复，它会抹掉读者意识到缺口的机会。加分区只把今后的新写分流，旧段不会自动搬走，所以不能拿加分区修补已过期的审计缺口。[6][10]

English

Producer caches topic and leader metadata, selects a partition by key, batches and directly connects to Leader. Local Partition log returns first; after the replication policy Leader returns base_offset. It proves log acceptance, not Billing processing. If the response disappears, timeout does not reveal whether append occurred; retry with the original producer sequence rather than inventing a new business event.

Billing Fetches from its group checkpoint and receives a batch; Audit reads the same log with independent recovery position. Produce success and Fetch records are separate replies, not one broker push. Many groups reuse one partition log, adding read traffic rather than seven-day files per group; that is why group count appears in the egress equation. [1][4]

Pull lets consumers control retrieval, but they must also bound poll batches, in-flight work and downstream calls. Fetching into memory is not processing. Long polling prevents busy loops. A13 uses linger 5 ms and small batches to trade some waiting for throughput; healthy p99<100 ms is a target, not a fixed Kafka 10 ms commit or a guarantee from adding RTTs. Broker backpressure can increase waiting. [2]

The log rolls into segments; delete retention removes eligible old segments by time or configured per-partition byte limit, not individual acknowledged messages. A6 has no earlier byte-limit deletion. Seven days is a retention target rather than exact per-record expiry; segment granularity and cleanup add slack. Unexpected full disks can fail writes or replicas, not authorize deletion of ineligible data. [3]

Recovery compares checkpoint with log start. If start 41 and Audit Fetches 20, the position is out of range. This design selects auto.offset.reset=none, stops and alerts, then restores from backup or gets business approval to skip. latest silently hides the gap. New partitions route future writes without moving old data, so they cannot repair expired audit history. [6][10]

2 · 分区日志保序，扩容必须交代映射边界2 · Partition-log order and the mapping boundary of expansion

中文

同一业务 key 在分区数、分区函数和编码固定时，落到同一日志。图上 h=55 仅是示意：55 mod48=7，连续版本 v1、v2 放进 P7，返回 offset。多个 Producer 同写一个 key 时，日志的最终追加序是 broker 接收后确定的顺序，未必等于业务产生时间；业务若规定版本先后，就在记录里带版本号，在生产或处理端拒绝跳序，不能只靠同 key 获得跨进程因果顺序。

组内单分区只派给一个成员，保证分配没有两位当前所有者，却不保证处理线程的完成序。C1 将 v1、v2 同时交给线程池，v2 的数据库更新可能先成功。默认每键串行处理并只提交连续完成前缀；并发可放到不同 key，或者让数据库用 expected_version 条件更新。关闭幂等生产又允许多个在途 batch 重试，也可能让后批超前，客户端配置同样属于顺序契约。[2][4]

全局顺序可以把所有事件收进一个区，但此时组内成员并行上限是 1，单成员 A8 的 800/s 接不住 11,574/s。入口字节不大，也不能因此断言单区足够，瓶颈在副作用处理。跨区按时间戳归并还需要处理时钟偏差、迟到和等待水位，并另设最终顺序规则；如果产品不需要总序，这些同步点只有代价，没有解决本题的额外约束。

加区会重新计算新事件的落点，不会重分布旧记录。48→96 时，均匀整数哈希 mod96 的前 48 个余数保持原区，后 48 个余数从旧区移到新增加的区，所以移动比例是 48/96=1/2。48→49 在完整均匀周期上只有 1/49 保持，约 48/49 移动。不能把两个案例都背成「几乎全部迁移」，也不能把 1/2 解读成业务只需要处理一半风险。[10]

若仍要扩区，先 fence 旧路由生产者，确认旧 key 的最后序号已经处理，发布新路由版本，再让新 key 事件进入新区。图上的 Migration gate 是可选业务协议，不是 Kafka 加分区会自动生成的组件。迁移时允许停顿，或同时读旧新两区再按业务序号归并；未确认 drain 就开新路由，会让 v2 先于旧区 v1 完成。新 topic 双写也不能免掉去重和切换水位。

热订单或热账户仍整键落一区。真正可选的是优化该键的处理、把热键隔离到专用消费者预算，或者经产品同意把顺序范围改成可并行子键。单纯改成 user_id+日期不会拆开同一天的 20% 热流量；随机加盐虽然分摊，随后就得重新排序或放弃该键的总序。分区数提供可用的并行单元，不能替产品决定哪些状态更新允许并发。

English

A key stays on one log while partition count, function and encoding stay fixed. Illustrative h55 maps to P7 under mod 48, where v1 and v2 receive offsets. Multiple producers of one key get broker append order, not necessarily business creation order. Required business version order needs a version and gap/order checks; a shared key alone does not establish cross-process causality.

One current member owns each group partition, but parallel handlers need not finish in order. If C1 dispatches v1 and v2 concurrently, v2 can update the database first. Serialize per key and checkpoint only the contiguous completed prefix, or use expected_version conditions; parallelize across keys. Disabling producer idempotence while retrying multiple in-flight batches can also reorder batches. [2][4]

A single partition gives a global log order but caps group member parallelism at 1; the 800/s effect budget cannot absorb 11,574/s. Modest input bytes do not prove it sufficient. Timestamp merge across partitions needs skew, late-arrival, watermarks and a total-order rule; without a product need, these synchronization points add cost without solving a constraint.

Adding partitions changes future routes without redistributing old records. Under uniform integer hashes, half of mod 96 residues retain their old mod 48 partition, so 48→96 moves 1/2. Over a full uniform cycle 48→49 keeps 1/49 and moves 48/49. These are different arithmetic cases, and half moved does not mean half the operational risk. [10]

An ordered migration fences old-route producers, verifies the old key’s final sequence was processed, publishes a routing version, then starts new writes. The optional Migration gate is a business protocol, not an automatic Kafka component. Pause or merge both logs by business sequence; switching before drain lets new v2 finish before old v1. Dual-writing a new topic still needs dedupe and a cutover watermark.

A hot order/account still occupies one partition. Optimize its processing, isolate it with a dedicated budget, or obtain product agreement to use parallel subkeys. Adding a date does not split same-day 20% load. Random salting distributes load but requires reordering or weaker key order. Partition count cannot decide which state updates may run concurrently.

3 · ISR、HW 和确认：发出副本记录还不算完成3 · ISR, HW and ack: sending replica records is insufficient

中文

复制单位是分区，Leader 本身也是 ISR 成员。图中初始 ISR={Leader,Follower 1}，Follower 2 因长期追不上而在集合外。Producer 请求写 offset41，Leader 本地追加后日志末端位置是 42；HW 仍为 41，消费者只能读 offset<41。这里将水位统一表示成排他上界，避免「水位41」一会儿包含41、一会儿不包含的歧义。ISR 成员并非每一瞬间都没有延迟，集合管理有追赶与退出规则。[1][3]

Follower 1 从 Leader Fetch41，拿到记录并追加到本地日志。仅看 Leader 发出那份响应，无法证明远端已经完成追加；下一轮 replica Fetch 报告 next=42，才让 Leader 观察到它已有41。本组第四帧特意加上这次进度请求，再把 HW 推为42、回 ack41。这个确认依赖日志复制位置，不等于每条记录已经在所有副本做 fsync；整机同时失电等相关失败仍要另作耐久假设。[1]

acks=all 等当前 ISR 全部满足，min=2 则是允许成功所需的最小 ISR 数。ISR 有三份时要等三份；ISR 剩两份时等两份；剩一份时不能成功，可能在追加前拒绝，也可能追加后发现条件不满足而报错。后一种情况下记录有可能留在日志里，所以生产者收到错误也不能总当作完全没写。RF=3、min=2 和 acks=all 分别回答放几份、最低门槛、等哪些副本，三个参数不能互换。[2][3]

A1 关闭 ELR 并拒绝 unclean。健康确认后 Leader 消失，仍在安全候选中的 Follower 1 含41，能被提升；若它也不在可用集合，分区等待安全副本，不能让缺41的 Follower 2 任意成为权威。这个等待用写可用性守住已确认历史。改成 acks=1 或打开 unclean，会改变已确认记录的丢失窗口，不是同一套承诺下的「优化性能」。ELR 开启时另按其安全集合选举，不能把本图推广为唯一现代行为。[3][8]

普通非事务读取受 HW 的已复制前缀约束；read_uncommitted 的名字没有允许读 Leader 独有的脏尾，它指是否屏蔽 Kafka 事务。read_committed 还要等稳定事务边界。例如已复制 offset41–45，但42开始的事务未结束，读取会停在42之前，即使44是非事务记录也可能暂时被挡住。使用排他位置可写成 readable_end=min(HW,first_open_transaction_offset)，并过滤已中止事务；没有未完成事务时该边界退回 HW。[4][6]

English

Replication is per partition and Leader is an ISR member. Initially ISR={Leader,Follower 1}; Follower 2 is outside after falling behind. Appending 41 advances Leader end position to 42 while HW remains 41, so consumers only read offsets below 41. We consistently use an exclusive watermark to avoid ambiguity. ISR membership is not zero lag at every instant; admission and removal follow progress rules. [1][3]

Follower 1 Fetches 41 and appends the returned record locally. Leader sending the response does not prove completion. A subsequent replica Fetch(next 42) reports that 41 is stored; frame 4 shows that request before HW 42 and ack 41. The acknowledgement is replication progress, not per-record fsync on every replica; correlated power loss needs separate durability assumptions. [1]

acks=all waits for the current ISR, while min 2 is the minimum size required for success. ISR 3 waits for all 3, ISR 2 for both, and ISR 1 cannot succeed. A request can be rejected before append or fail after append when the condition changes; the latter can leave a record in the log. RF3, min 2 and acks-all answer replica count, minimum threshold and waiting set, respectively. [2][3]

With ELR disabled and unclean election rejected, a surviving safe Follower 1 containing 41 can be promoted. If no safe candidate is available, wait rather than elect lagging Follower 2 as authority. This trades write availability for acknowledged history. acks 1 or unclean changes the loss contract, not merely performance; ELR-enabled elections follow their own safe candidate set. [3][8]

Ordinary nontransactional reads respect the replicated HW prefix. read_uncommitted concerns transaction filtering, not reading a leader-only tail. With replicated offsets 41–45 and an open transaction starting 42, read_committed stops before 42 and can withhold nontransactional 44 too. In exclusive-position notation readable_end=min(HW,first_open_transaction_offset), filtering aborted transactions; without open transactions the boundary is HW. [4][6]

4 · 再均衡交接所有权，位点只跟随完成前缀4 · Rebalance ownership; checkpoint the completed prefix

中文

普通 consumer group 的某一区，任一稳定分配下只给一个当前成员；不同组则可以同时读取同一区。Group coordinator 是某台 broker 的职责，不是 KRaft controller。它管理成员、分配与提交，业务 Fetch 仍去分区 leader。GroupOffset 的键是(group,topic,partition)，值是下次恢复的位置。处理41后提交42；一次 poll 若拿41–50，也不能只因本地 position=51 就说整批已经完成。[4]

并发处理时要维护连续完成前缀。若41还在等待外部确认、42与43已成功，仍只能提交41，不可跳到44；否则重启会漏过尚未成功的41。自动提交按客户端 poll 进度和时间运行，不天然知道工作线程状态，因此 A14 关闭它。同步串行处理后提交容易说明；需要吞吐时用有界线程池、暂停相关分区、记录完成位图，再提交连续安全位置，不能只增加线程而不设计提交规则。

成员退出、会话超时或 poll 超过处理间隔，都会触发交接。经典 eager 协议有全组屏障；经典 cooperative 可增量撤销，本题另显式选择 group.protocol=consumer。4.1 文档说明新协议在服务端可用、客户端仍需 opt-in；心跳与会话相关参数由服务端控制，不能继续照抄旧客户端字段。未移动分区可以继续，不代表被移动的区没有等待、重放或状态加载。[5][6]

交接时旧 C2 先停止接新任务，等可控的在途任务完成或取消，然后交还 P8；若它已失联，C3 从可靠位点恢复。成员 epoch 或经典 generation 能让协调器拒绝旧成员的提交，却没有给远端数据库发送「撤销已完成交易」。旧进程若从长暂停中醒来，仍可能继续 HTTP 扣款；幂等键、业务版本条件或副作用端 fencing 才能挡住这条越界执行。单所有者分配是调度状态，不是所有旧线程都已经消失的证明。

积压要按区看入口与处理速率。均匀总峰值下，如果处理能力只有10×800=8,000/s，净积压约11,574−8,000=3,574/s，一分钟约214,444条。若扩到20个有效成员、均匀总能力16,000/s，净排空16,000−11,574≈4,426/s，上述积压约48.45秒才追完；不能用总消费16,000来除而忽略新消息。若积压集中热 key，增加空闲成员不会改变那个区的800/s。

English

A stable ordinary group assignment has one current member per partition; different groups can read it concurrently. Group coordinator is a broker role, not KRaft controller. It manages membership, assignments and commits while data Fetch targets Leader. GroupOffset is keyed by(group,topic,partition) and stores recovery position. Processing 41 permits 42; polling 41–50 and local position 51 does not prove all work completed. [4]

Concurrent processing requires a contiguous completed prefix. If 41 still waits for an external result but 42 and 43 succeeded, checkpoint 41 rather than 44; restart would otherwise skip unfinished 41. Auto-commit follows client poll progress and timing rather than worker completion, so A14 disables it. For concurrency use bounded workers, partition pauses and completion tracking, then checkpoint the safe prefix.

Leaving, session timeout or a missed poll processing interval triggers handoff. Classic eager uses a group barrier and cooperative classic can revoke incrementally; this design explicitly chooses group.protocol=consumer. In 4.1 the server supports it while clients opt in, and heartbeat/session settings move to server control. Unmoved partitions can continue, but moved ones still wait, replay or load state. [5][6]

C2 stops admitting work and drains or cancels controlled in-flight tasks before returning P8; if lost, C3 resumes from the stored checkpoint. Member epoch or classic generation rejects stale commits without undoing remote effects. A paused old process may resume an HTTP charge; destination idempotency, business version checks or effect-side fencing must reject it. Assignment ownership does not prove all old threads vanished.

At uniform peak,10×800=8,000/s processing yields about 3,574/s net lag and 214,444 records in one minute. At 20 effective members,16,000/s capacity drains at 16,000−11,574≈4,426/s, taking about 48.45s. Divide by net surplus, not gross 16,000. A hot-key backlog still has its one-partition 800/s limit.

5 · 幂等生产、至少一次消费和 Kafka 事务的范围5 · Idempotent production, at-least-once and Kafka transaction scope

中文

普通幂等 Producer 用 producer id、epoch 和每分区序列识别协议重试，避免同一次会话把一个 batch 重复追加；A7 显式要求 enable.idempotence=true、acks=all，并保持兼容的 retries 与在途请求上限。库里存 event_id 是另一种业务去重：应用自己再次调用 send，或重启后重新构造同一事件，不一定被生产者会话去重。事件身份要贯穿 outbox、日志、inbox，不能把 Kafka 默认幂等当成所有业务重复都消失。[2][7]

默认先做副作用再提交位点。Consumer 把 inbox(event_id=9001) 唯一行和 ledger 更新放进同一个 Business DB 事务；两者都成功才提交42。崩溃在副作用之后、位点之前，新成员会再读41，事务遇到已有 event_id，读出原结果并不再更新账本。若两次提交拆开，先有去重行、后无副作用，就变成永久漏执行；若先记位点、后做副作用，崩溃又会跳过消息。这些是提交边界造成的结果，不能靠「写得小心」解决。

图里为了确定画出 next仍为41，选的是协调器明确返回 rebalance rejection。若 OffsetCommit 只是超时，保存42可能已经成功，读回 committed offset 才知道；Producer 的超时同理。有幂等键可以安全面对重放，却不能把未知提交结果改写成确定失败。恢复流程应能查原事件结果，并把重试预算和人工修复分开，避免不断重发把日志和副作用端都堆满。

输出还写回 Kafka 时，可用事务 Producer：beginTransaction，产生输出记录，把连续完成的 next offset 与当前 group metadata 送入 sendOffsetsToTransaction，再 commitTransaction。下游用 read_committed，失败则 abort 并重放。组协调器负责事务位点暂存，事务协调器处理 EndTxn；两者可位于不同 broker，图中 Kafka coordinators 仅汇总职责。输出追加与位点暂存 ACK 都不等于事务完成 [7][12]；事务成功后输出和位点共享提交结果，下游仍受 LSO 限制。stable transactional.id 隔离逻辑任务，Producer epoch fencing 挡旧事务实例；这与消费成员 epoch 是不同防线。[7][11]

远端付款不是这套 Kafka 事务的一部分。若业务必须调 PSP，先在本地事务里插 inbox 和持久 payment_job，位点可以在这份义务落盘后推进；独立 job worker 用固定业务幂等键向 PSP 申请，超时保留 UNKNOWN 并查账。只有 PSP 的幂等/查询契约、任务重试和对账一起成立，才可讨论最终业务效果。单独在本地表建唯一 order_id，却把 HTTP 调用放在事务外，并不能保证银行只扣一次。

事务的代价包括协调器、事务标记、超时恢复和 LSO 等待。长事务会让下游在有 HW 进展时仍读不到新数据，所以要限制事务大小和时长，并同时看可读 lag 与未结束事务。无外部原子需求的日志采集可以保持普通幂等生产；本地账本用至少一次加原子 inbox 更直接。三副本、单成员和事务三个词不能相加成端到端「所有系统免费恰好一次」，要先把图上的原子括号画清。

English

Producer id, epoch and per-partition sequence dedupe protocol retries within an idempotent session. Explicitly use enable.idempotence=true, acks-all and compatible retry/in-flight settings. Business event_id dedupe is separate: an application resending an event or reconstructing it after restart may evade session dedupe. Preserve identity across outbox, log and inbox rather than assume producer idempotence removes all business duplicates. [2][7]

Commit the unique inbox(event 9001) and ledger update together, then checkpoint 42. A crash after effect but before checkpoint replays 41; the transaction finds the existing event and returns its prior result without another ledger update. Separate dedupe/effect commits can permanently omit an effect; checkpointing first can skip a failed effect. These outcomes follow commit boundaries rather than coding care.

The diagram uses an explicit rebalance rejection to establish next 41. An OffsetCommit timeout can follow a successful save 42; inspect the committed position. Producer timeouts are similarly unknown. Idempotency makes replay safer without converting uncertainty into definite failure. Query the original event result and bound retries instead of saturating both log and effect destination.

For Kafka output use beginTransaction, produce outputs, sendOffsetsToTransaction with the safe next offset and current group metadata, then commitTransaction. Downstream reads committed; abort and replay on failure. The group coordinator stages transactional offsets; the transaction coordinator handles EndTxn. They may be on different brokers; Kafka coordinators aggregates their roles in the diagram. Produce and offset-staging acknowledgements do not complete the transaction [7][12]. A successful transaction commits output and offsets together, while downstream reads still respect LSO. A stable transactional.id identifies the logical task and producer epoch fences stale transaction instances, separate from consumer member epochs. [7][11]

A PSP call is outside Kafka transactions. Persist inbox and a durable payment_job locally before checkpointing the obligation. A separate worker sends a stable business idempotency key, retains UNKNOWN on timeout and reconciles. Destination idempotency/query guarantees plus durable retries establish the eventual business effect. A local unique order_id with an external HTTP call does not guarantee one bank charge.

Transactions add coordinator work, markers, timeout recovery and LSO waiting. Long transactions can block reads despite HW progress, so bound size/duration and inspect readable lag plus open transactions. Log collection can use plain idempotent production; a local ledger can use at-least-once with an atomic inbox. RF3, single ownership and transactions do not add up to free exactly-once across every system; define the atomic scope first.

6 · KRaft 管元数据，提升新 Leader 不等于立刻可写6 · KRaft metadata; a promoted leader is not automatically writable

中文

独立 controller quorum 存储集群元数据：broker 注册、主题分区、leader epoch 与副本集合。三 controller 的多数派是 floor(3/2)+1=2，失去一台仍可作决定，失去两台就无法提交新元数据。数据分区 acks=all 不是用这两票代替 ISR；Produce、consumer Fetch 和副本 Fetch 都在 broker 数据面上。图里 quorum 是三台 controller 的聚合表示，不是假装一台控制进程就有法定人数。[9]

Broker A 失联时，客户端只观察计时器，不会收到 A 发出的红色「我超时了」包。可用 quorum 判断故障、在 A1 安全集合内提升 B，增加 leader epoch，brokers 和客户端随后刷新元数据。客户端不能用一份永久静态地址无限重试旧 A；也不能把每次 Produce 先代理给 controller 才找新主。图中失效路径整体变淡，显示它曾是稳态，而新路由绕过 A。

B 持有已经确认的41，因此提升后历史没有因为那次单机故障消失。但若 ISR 从{A,B}变成{B}，size=1低于min=2，新 acks=all 写仍会被明确拒绝。读已有提交前缀和接受新写是两个状态；等 Follower 2 追上或补出新副本、重新达到安全门槛再写。把 min 临时调到1可以恢复部分写可用性，同时把新写的耐久合同降了一档，必须作为另一个被批准的策略，不能隐含在健康检查中。

控制器失去多数派时，已稳定的 broker 数据路径未必立刻全部停掉，已有 leader 可按现有元数据继续一些工作；需要新建主题、重选 leader 或改变 ISR 的操作却可能卡住。不能保证它们永远继续，因为后续数据面故障需要新的元数据决定。监控要分开看元数据提交、active controller、partition under-replication、拒写和 consumer lag，不能用一个 controller 进程是否存活来概括整站健康。

替代 combined 模式让小环境省三份部署，却将重磁盘 broker 的停顿和控制面放在同一故障域，维护时也难分别滚动。A16 选择专职 controller 是为了隔离这些影响；官方 4.1 运维文档同样不建议关键部署混部。[9] 扩 controller 到五台能承受两台失效，不能直接提高数据吞吐，也不能消除机房电源或网络共同故障。副本和 controller 都要按故障域放置，参数上的“三”和三处独立失败不是同一个事实。

English

Dedicated controllers store broker registration, partitions, leader epochs and replica membership. Three controllers need floor(3/2)+1=2 votes; one loss still permits decisions and two block new metadata commits. These votes do not substitute for a data partition ISR. Produce, consumer Fetch and replica Fetch stay on brokers. The quorum box aggregates three controllers, not a single magically fault-tolerant process. [9]

When A disappears, the client sees its timer, not a red timeout packet from A. The available quorum elects B from the A1 safe set and increments the leader epoch; brokers and clients refresh metadata. Do not retry a permanent A address forever or proxy every Produce through controllers. Historical failed paths are muted while the new route bypasses A.

B contains acknowledged 41, so that single failure preserves history. But ISR shrinking from{A,B} to{B} leaves size 1 below min 2 and new acks-all writes are rejected. Reading the committed prefix and admitting new writes are separate states. Restore a caught-up replica first; lowering min 1 changes durability and must be an explicit alternate policy rather than a hidden health-check action.

Losing controller majority does not necessarily stop every stable broker path immediately; existing leaders can perform some work on current metadata. Topic creation, elections and ISR changes may stall, and later data failures need new metadata decisions. Monitor metadata commits, active controller, under-replication, rejected writes and consumer lag separately rather than reduce health to one controller process.

Combined mode saves deployments in a small environment but couples disk-heavy broker stalls to control-plane failure and separate maintenance. A16 chooses dedicated controllers for isolation;4.1 operations likewise discourages combined critical deployments. [9] Five controllers tolerate two failures without directly increasing data throughput or removing shared power/network failure. Counts of three are not proof of three independent failure domains.
