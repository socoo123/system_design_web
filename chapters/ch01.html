面试在评什么What the interview scores

中文

它不是 45 分钟造出微信，也不是知识竞赛。两个工程师把一个模糊问题收成可落地的设计。过程比终图重要。终图漂亮但中途沉默，是低分。过程清楚、图有意做简，是高分。

English

You are not building WeChat in 45 minutes, and it is not a quiz. Two engineers turn a fuzzy prompt into a design you could ship. The process outranks the final picture. A pretty diagram with a silent middle is a low score. A clear process and a deliberately small diagram is a high one.

原书那句仍然成立：最终设计和你在过程里做的工作相比，没那么重要。The line from the book still holds: the final design matters less than the work you do in the process.

Step 1 · 定范围Step 1 · Set scope

中文

别当 Jimmy：题面一出就抢答，零加分，而且是 red flag。短链、Feed 这种题，5 到 7 个问题就停，其余写成你自己的假设。

你问Feed 上的典型回答
App、Web，还是都要？都要
最重要的功能？发帖，看 feed

English

Do not be Jimmy, the student who answers before the question is finished. That scores nothing, and it is a red flag. On a short-URL or feed prompt, stop after five to seven questions and write the rest as your assumptions.

You askA typical feed answer
App, web, or both?Both
The features that matter?Post, and read the feed

Scale and read:write?Reads dwarf writes

Step 2 · 高层，换 buy-inStep 2 · High-level, then buy-in

中文

四个动作：画初始蓝图，边画边问，用数量级看它撑不撑得住，再走通 1 到 2 个用例。

我先用 5 分钟画高层，确认方向。Client 进来，LB 后面是无状态 Web，读多的话前面放 Cache，权威数据在 DB。这一步方向可以吗？

大题（设计搜索）可以先不写 API。中小题在这一步列出 endpoint。拿不准就问一句要不要写。

English

Four moves: sketch the blueprint, ask as you draw, check that the orders of magnitude fit, then walk one or two use cases.

I will spend five minutes on a high-level picture and check the direction. Clients hit a load balancer and a stateless web tier. Reads go through a cache. The database is the source of truth. Is that direction all right?

On a huge prompt such as search, you can skip the API. On a medium one, list the endpoints here. If you are unsure, ask whether they want them.

这一层后面会拆开讲：Ch39 · 负载均衡Ch38 · 缓存Ch37 · 存储。Later chapters split this tier: Ch39 · load balancingCh38 · cacheCh37 · storage.

Step 3 和收尾Step 3 and the close

中文

Deep dive 只挖一块：这题的 hard part（短链是跳转和短码，聊天是连接和 fan-out），或者面试官刚指的那块。深度大于广度。最隐蔽的失败是每个组件点名一遍，哪块都不深。

初级岗把组件、API、schema 讲清。Senior 讲 bottleneck、扩展和失败。Staff 再加 trade-off、多区域和成本。别用资深岗的词堆给初级岗。

2026 的白板多半是共享文档，不是教室里的黑板。中小题主动列 endpoint。收尾时补一句可观测性：延迟、流量、错误、饱和。

English

The deep dive covers one slice: the hard part (redirect and short code for a URL shortener, connection and fan-out for chat), or whatever they just pointed at. Depth beats breadth. The quiet failure is naming every box and going deep on none.

A junior answer makes the components, API, and schema clear. A senior answer adds bottlenecks, scale-out, and failure. Staff adds trade-offs, multi-region, and cost. Do not dump staff vocabulary on a junior loop.

In 2026 the board is usually a shared doc, not a classroom whiteboard. On a medium problem, offer the endpoints. In the wrap-up, add observability: latency, traffic, errors, saturation.

失败和多区域的机制在 Ch36 · CAP / SLOCh42 · 弹性。面试先用上面三个 bottleneck 开口。Failure and multi-region mechanisms are in Ch36 · CAP / SLOCh42 · resilience. In the interview, start from the three bottlenecks above.

追问时怎么接How to take the follow-ups

中文

  1. 还有什么问题？挑两个 bottleneck 和改进，不要说完美。
  2. 流量翻 10 倍？分片、读写分离、缓存。故事在 Ch02。
  3. 一个机房挂了？先说多副本和你愿意牺牲哪一种一致性，再提多区域。
  4. 怎么监控？四个黄金信号，外加一条请求能追下去。
  5. 为什么选 A 不选 B？必须有一句 trade-off，不能只说业界都这么做。
2026 和原书差在哪

原书默认物理白板，现在虚拟白板是常态，提前练共享文档。原书不太推 API，中小题你应该在 Step 2 自己列出来。原书几乎不提模型，现在可能追问向量检索，那是 M5，不是这一章的第一答案。

English

  1. Anything else? Name two bottlenecks and a fix. Do not say perfect.
  2. 10× traffic? Shard, split reads and writes, add cache. That story is Ch02.
  3. One datacenter dies? Start with replicas and which consistency you will give up, then multi-region.
  4. How do you monitor? The four golden signals, plus a way to follow one request.
  5. Why A and not B? You need a trade-off, not "the industry does this".
2026 versus the book

The book assumes a physical whiteboard. A shared doc is the normal board now, so practice that. The book is light on APIs; on a medium problem you should list endpoints in step 2. The book barely mentions models. A vector-search follow-up belongs to M5, not to your first answer here.
