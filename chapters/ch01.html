世界地图与 4 步法World map and the 4-step method

约 35 分钟 · 前置：无 · 一条商品详情读请求贯穿四步~35 min · no prerequisite · one product-detail read through four steps

这一章结束你能做什么What you can do after this chapter

中文

  • 用同一条读请求走完四步：定范围、估算、高层、再深挖一条故障。每一步都在图上多一笔，而不是换一张名词卡。
  • 说出哪个数迫使缓存出现，以及缓存整层挂掉时回源为什么必须有上限。
  • 卡住时把卡点说出来，退回这张高层图，而不是沉默或再堆一个组件。

English

  • Walk one read through four steps — scope, estimate, high-level, then one failure — and add a stroke each time instead of swapping noun cards.
  • Say which number forces the cache, and why a dead cache tier cannot fall through to the store without a cap.
  • When you stall, name the stall and return to this high-level picture instead of going silent or adding another box.

30 秒开场、明确不做、以及写在白板上的假设The 30-second open, explicit non-goals, and assumptions on the board

中文

我用一条读多的商品详情做贯穿例子：GET /v1/products/{id} 返回标题、价格、可售标记和图片 URL。四笔都画在同一张图上。先划信任边界，再用无连线的 scope note 标出本轮不做结账和搜索，两种边界不混。再算峰值 QPS，把它和「10 ms 串行寻道」推出的数学上限比，比完才把缓存加进去。然后分开无状态服务和权威存储，画出同步请求和同步响应，并标明队列和第二机房这轮不连线。最后只深挖一条故障：缓存层不可用时，回源有放行上限，超出的请求 503，而不是把峰值全部压到存储上。

功能需求只有这一条读。调用方把商品 id 交给边缘，边缘转到商品服务，服务返回一份商品 JSON。未知 id 是 404。这是功能：系统正常时，这份 JSON 里有什么、没有什么。非功能是另一列，不要和功能混着说：单机房内服务侧 p99 按假设订在 80 毫秒以内，月可用性按假设订在 99.9%，详情页上的价格允许最多 30 秒陈旧。80 毫秒、99.9%、30 秒都是为了能继续设计而写下的目标，不是某家商店的内部 SLO，面试官可以改。

明确不做：结账、支付、库存预占、搜索排序、推荐、图片字节本身、多机房同时写。图片 URL 可以出现在 JSON 里，像素由对象存储或 CDN 承担，那不是这条 API 的带宽。推荐不在本站主线里，这轮也不要主动画。结账对价格的一致性要求和「浏览可以看 30 秒旧价」不是同一条策略，所以结账留在本轮范围外；它仍可能在同一系统信任边界内。这样才不会用一条缓存策略假装覆盖两种操作。

English

The running example is a read-heavy product detail: GET /v1/products/{id} returns title, price, an availability flag, and image URLs. All four strokes land on that same picture. First draw the trust boundary, then put checkout and search in a separate no-wire scope note; the two boundaries are not the same. Compare peak QPS with the mathematical ceiling implied by an assumed serial 10 ms seek, and only then add a cache. Next split the stateless service from the source of truth, draw the synchronous request and reply, and leave Queue and Region B unwired. The deep dive is one failure: when the cache tier is down, origin admission has a cap and the rest return 503 rather than pouring the peak into the store.

The functional requirement is that one read. The caller sends a product id through the edge to Product Service and gets product JSON back. An unknown id is 404. That is function: what the JSON contains when the system is healthy. Non-function is a separate column: an assumed in-region service-side p99 under 80 ms, an assumed 99.9% monthly availability, and a price on this page that may be up to 30 seconds stale. Those three numbers are working targets so the design can continue, not a retailer’s internal SLO. The interviewer may replace them.

Explicitly out of scope: checkout, payment, inventory reservation, search ranking, recommendations, the image bytes, and multi-region writes. Image URLs may sit in the JSON; pixels belong to object storage or a CDN and are not this API’s bandwidth. Recommenders are not on this site’s spine and do not get a box in this round. Checkout’s price invariant is not the same policy as “a browse may show a 30-second-old price,” so checkout stays out of this round—not necessarily outside the system trust boundary—rather than letting one cache policy pretend to cover both operations.

这些数从哪一步乘出来

日活取 8,640,000 是为了让除法落在整数上，方便口述，不是行业基准。8,640,000 × 10 = 86,400,000 次详情阅读。一天 86,400 秒，而 86,400 × 1,000 = 86,400,000，所以平均读 QPS 正好是 1,000。峰值系数取 5 也是假设：晚间流量高于白天，但还不到后面追问里的十倍增长。1,000 × 5 = 5,000，这才是图上要面对的读压力。

写侧分开算。目录更新按假设每天 50,000 次，50,000 ÷ 86,400 ≈ 0.58，口述约 0.6 次写 QPS。86,400,000 ÷ 50,000 = 1,728，大约 1,700 次读对 1 次写。夜间改价放到 30 倍也只有约 18 次写 QPS，所以这条读路径不加队列。

存储单独算，是为了说明它不改图。200 万 SKU × 1.5 KB ≈ 3 GB 原始行，三副本约 9 GB，加上索引仍是几十 GB。这轮溢出的是寻道速率，不是磁盘装不下。

Where the multiplications come from

8,640,000 DAU is chosen so the division lands on an integer you can say aloud. It is not an industry benchmark. 8,640,000 × 10 = 86,400,000 detail reads. A day has 86,400 seconds, and 86,400 × 1,000 = 86,400,000, so average read QPS is exactly 1,000. The peak factor of 5 is also an assumption: evening traffic is higher than the day, and it is not yet the 10× growth in the follow-up. 1,000 × 5 = 5,000, and that is the read pressure the drawing has to face.

Keep writes in their own line so a read-heavy prompt does not turn into write fan-out. Catalog updates are an assumed 50,000 per day. 50,000 ÷ 86,400 ≈ 0.58, about 0.6 write QPS said aloud. Reads over writes: 86,400,000 ÷ 50,000 = 1,728, roughly 1,700 reads per write. A nightly price job at 30× for a short window is still only 0.6 × 30 ≈ 18 write QPS. That does not earn a queue on this read. The queue appears when someone asks for an asynchronous search-index update.

Storage gets one multiplication so you can see that it does not change this picture. 2,000,000 SKUs × 1.5 KB = 3,000,000 KB, about 3 GB of raw rows. Three replicas are about 9 GB; indexes and backups still leave a tens-of-GB planning range, not a day-one sharding problem. Capacity and QPS are different resources. What overflows here is seek rate, not free disk space.

更完整的估算操练在 Ch03。本章只把一次除法算到它改变架构为止。架构会怎么继续长大，在 Ch02。The fuller estimate drill is Ch03. This chapter follows one division until it changes the architecture. How the picture keeps growing is Ch02.

世界地图：积木是依赖，不是章节名单World map: blocks are dependencies, not a list of chapter titles

全章只有这一组是地图。它回答「不同题为什么长得像」。后面四组都回到同一条商品详情请求，一步只改变一个决定。This is the only map deck. It answers why different prompts look alike. The next four decks return to one product-detail request and change one decision at a time.

读图约定：灰色实线是同步请求，绿色是同步返回，橙色虚线是异步发送，红色虚线是降级或拒绝；箭头尖端永远指向动作的接收方。Visual grammar: solid gray is a synchronous request, green is its reply, dashed orange is asynchronous delivery, and dashed red is degradation or rejection. The arrowhead always points at the receiver of the action.

按依赖把这张图走一遍

读多的 API 先查缓存，命中就不再碰存储；未命中才由 Service 读权威存储并回填。写路径在一次事务里提交业务行与 outbox，Relay 只读已提交记录，再异步发到 Queue；这不是「提交后再顺手 emit」的双写。Worker 消费队列，不占用用户请求的回程。只有真正存在「必须做一次」不变式时才加租约，所以协调只是注记，不是每个请求的默认一跳。

这样画，是因为约束不同，依赖就不同。读多而且单次要快，才会在主路径上方加一层可丢的内存。扇出可以晚几秒，才让 outbox relay 把事件投到队列。重复扣款或重复发号不能靠「大概不会撞」来赌，所以那些操作才可能需要协调。面试里先说自己落在哪一个家族，再决定哪条边是同步的。本章后面用商品详情把读家族走完；短链那种读写都重的题在 Ch09，不要把它的盒子抄到这张地图上充数。

有的读不该走缓存：峰值远低于存储能稳定服务的速率，而且不允许陈旧。那时箭头只指存储。命中率接近零时，缓存只多一个故障域。写路径若必须和扇出同成败，才改用同步调用；否则队列是对的。

把协调器放进读路径，锁一抖动，本来不用加锁的 GET 会一起失败，所以终图没有那根箭头。队列若画成同步，扇出堆积会拖进读的预算；异步边表示这条边失败时，存储里的提交仍然算数。

常见的错法是把地图画成章节名，或把 cache、queue、CDN 当开场词表。没有箭头，就没有依赖。阅读顺序不要画进这张图：本章之后看 Ch02、Ch03，再用小题把四步跑顺，然后做 Ch09 这种完整题。M5 只做推理、RAG 和 Agent 基建，不做推荐。机制后读 Ch37 和 Ch38。这门课不在浏览器里写代码。

Walk the map by dependency

A read-heavy API checks Cache first. A hit does not touch the store. On a miss, Service reads the source of truth and fills Cache after the row returns. The write path atomically commits business rows plus an outbox entry; Relay reads committed outbox rows and only then publishes asynchronously to Queue. That is not a dual write that commits and then casually emits. Worker consumes Queue without occupying the user request’s reply path. Coordination is only a note until a true once-only invariant earns a lease; it is not a default hop on every request.

The drawing is shaped that way because different constraints create different dependencies. A read that is both frequent and latency-sensitive earns a droppable memory branch above the main spine. Fan-out that can be a few seconds late earns an outbox relay and a queue. A double charge or duplicate id cannot be waved off as “probably unique,” so that operation may earn coordination. In the interview, name the family first, then say which edge is synchronous. The rest of this chapter walks the read family on product detail. A prompt where both reads and writes are hard, such as Ch09, is a different request. Do not paste its boxes onto this map to look complete.

Some reads should not go through a cache: the peak sits far under what the store can serve steadily, and the product forbids staleness. That read arrow should point only at the store. A cache is a liability when the hit rate would be near zero or a 30-second-old price would be an incident: you add a failure domain and do not buy the freshness this prompt needs. In the other direction, a write that must succeed or fail together with fan-out should not use a queue. A synchronous fan-out call ties notification availability to checkout, and that is worth drawing only when the product says an order without a notification is a failed order.

The failure window on the map is specific. Put coordination on the read path and a lock-service blip fails GETs that never needed a lock. That is why the finished map has no arrow from the read API to coordination. If the queue edge is drawn as synchronous, fan-out backlog drags commit latency into the read budget. The async edge is the statement that a failure on that edge does not undo the commit already in the store. The cache-versus-store window is calculated on the product example below: the authoritative row can already be new while the cache still returns the old price inside the allowed stale time.

The common mistake is a row of chapter titles, or an opening vocabulary list of cache, queue, CDN, and Redis. A word list does not tell the interviewer who depends on whom, or which edge is synchronous. The correction is to keep only dependencies that have an arrow, and to say why that edge is synchronous. Reading order on this site is a different fact and does not belong on the drawing: this chapter, then Ch02 for how an architecture grows out of pain, then Ch03 until estimates are fluent, then small problems — rate limit, hashing, KV, unique ids, locks — until the four steps are automatic, then a full case such as Ch09. M5 is LLM inference, RAG, and Agent infrastructure, not recommenders. Storage and cache mechanisms in M6 come later; open Ch37 · storage and Ch38 · cache when a design needs them. There is no in-browser coding assignment. A system-design interview is explaining dependencies so they still fit on the board.

第一步 · 定范围：信任边界、范围内的路径、明确不做Step 1 · Scope: trust boundary, the in-scope path, and what is explicitly out

时间盒大约 3 到 8 分钟。问到能写假设就停，不要问满 10 分钟。About 3 to 8 minutes. Stop once the assumptions are written down. Do not interview them for 10 minutes.

问完就写假设，不要追问到烦

范围内的路径是：不可信的客户端把商品 id 送到边缘，边缘只做 TLS 和路由，商品服务读存储里的一行，正常时返回 JSON。未知 id 返回 404。这是功能契约。非功能写在同一张图的系统信任边界里，但是另一句话：p99 80 毫秒、99.9%、价格最多陈旧 30 秒。把「要返回价格」和「价格可以旧 30 秒」说成同一件事，后面缓存的 TTL 就没有依据。30 秒是产品假设。如果面试官说价格必须实时，TTL 策略会整个换掉，结账却仍然在本轮设计范围外，因为实时价格也不等于要在这轮设计支付。

大约问 5 到 7 个就停：同一 GET 覆盖移动端和网页；不做结账；不是个性化价格；浏览可以旧 30 秒；规模用上面的日活；这轮单机房；p99 按 80 毫秒。对方把问题抛回来，就把假设写在白板左侧再进入估算。问太少会加错组件，问太久会吃掉深挖。

最小记录先写在旁边，不单独开一组图：Product(id PK, title, price_cents, currency, availability, image_urls, version, updated_at)。version 是为了后面比较缓存里的价格和权威行，不是为了在第一步引入协调器。id 是主键，按 id 读一行，所以这条路径不是搜索。搜索被标成本轮不做且没有连线，就是为了挡住「那我们顺手做个查询」。

这一步最常见的错是把非功能当成组件名。听到 80 毫秒就画缓存，听到 99.9% 就画三机房，听到陈旧就画队列。80 毫秒只说明同步路径不能做三次串行寻道；99.9% 还没证明需要第二机房；30 秒陈旧只说明浏览读可以接受一个有界的旧值。组件要等估算证明某个预算装不下再加。另一个错是把结账口头排除，图上却从商品服务拉一根线到支付。没有那根线，才算排除。

Write the assumption and stop interviewing them

The in-scope path is: an untrusted client sends a product id to the edge, the edge does TLS and routing only, Product Service reads one row, and a healthy call returns JSON. An unknown id returns 404. That is the functional contract. Non-function is on the same picture, inside the boundary, and it is a different sentence: p99 80 ms, 99.9%, and a price that may be 30 seconds stale. If “return a price” and “the price may be 30 seconds old” collapse into one remark, the later TTL has nothing to hang on. Thirty seconds is a product assumption. If they require a live price, the TTL policy changes, and checkout is still outside, because a live price is not a decision to design payments in this round.

Ask about five to seven questions, then stop. Same GET for mobile and web: assume yes. Checkout in this round: assume no. Personalized price: assume no, one catalog price. May the browse be 30 seconds stale: assume yes. Scale: use the DAU above, do not invent “very large” live. Multi-region: assume one region this round. Latency: 80 ms. When they bounce a question back, write the assumption on the left of the board, say you will proceed and change it if they disagree, and move to the estimate. Too few questions add the wrong box because you never established that reads dominate. Too many questions spend the ten minutes on clarification and the deep dive disappears.

Write the minimum record beside the picture. It does not need its own deck: Product(id PK, title, price_cents, currency, availability, image_urls, version, updated_at). Version exists so a later step can compare a cached price with the authoritative row. It is not a reason to introduce coordination in step 1. The id is the primary key and the read is one row, so this path is not search. Search is marked out of this round with no wire, which stops “let’s add query while we are here” without confusing scope with trust.

The usual miss at this step turns a non-functional phrase into a component name. Hearing 80 ms and drawing a cache, hearing 99.9% and drawing three regions, hearing staleness and drawing a queue. Eighty milliseconds only says the synchronous path cannot pay three serial seeks. 99.9% has not yet shown that a second region is required. Thirty seconds of staleness only says a browse may return a bounded old value. A component waits until the estimate shows a budget that does not fit. The other miss excludes checkout out loud and then draws a wire from Product Service to payments. The missing wire is the exclusion.

第二步 · 估算：一个数把缓存画上去Step 2 · Estimate: one number adds the cache

大约 4 分钟。平均 QPS 不够，要把它和示意性寻道上限比完。Dean 的 10 ms 数量级来自他的大规模系统讲座幻灯片，不是博客转述；它不是当代硬件基准。[2]About four minutes. Average QPS is not enough until you compare it with an illustrative seek ceiling. Dean’s 10 ms order of magnitude comes from his large-systems talk slides, not a blog recap; it is not a benchmark for current hardware. [2]

把除法展开到它改变路径

Jeff Dean 的讲座幻灯片里，同机房往返是 500,000 纳秒，也就是 0.5 毫秒；一次磁盘寻道是 10,000,000 纳秒，也就是 10 毫秒；主存访问是 100 纳秒。[2] 单盘如果串行做随机寻道，一秒钟有 1,000 毫秒，1,000 ÷ 10 = 100，所以在 100% 忙碌、不留排队余量的极端假设下，数学上限才是每秒 100 次。真正的安全 gate 必须更低，并由当代硬件、缓冲池命中、排队与压测数据重算。这里只用公开数量级挡住「存储什么都扛得住」。

平均 1,000 QPS 已经是 100 的 10 倍：1,000 ÷ 100 = 10。峰值 5,000 ÷ 100 = 50。所以即使用平均而不是峰值，也不能让每次详情阅读都去寻道。缓存这一笔是被这个倍数逼出来的。命中走内存：缓存若在另一台机器上，还要加上约 0.5 毫秒的机房往返，仍远小于 80 毫秒的假设预算。未命中才有资格去存储。

98% 不是拍脑袋的命中率。在「未命中都变成 10 ms 串行寻道」这个故意保守的教学假设下，先用≤100/s 作上限，峰值是 5,000，未命中比例最多约 100 ÷ 5,000 = 0.02，所以命中率要到约 98%。验算：5,000 × 0.02 = 100。若你随口说 90%，未命中是 5,000 × 0.1 = 500，仍是这个示意上限的 5 倍。90% 只有在你把实测回源容量提高到至少 500/s（例如加盘，或证明热行已在数据库缓冲池里、未命中不再寻道）时才站得住。面试里要把假设一起说出来，否则 98% 看起来像魔术。

Dean 的缩略图例子里，30 次串行寻道 × 10 毫秒 = 300 毫秒，再加传输大约 560 毫秒；改成并行就落到一次寻道的量级。[2] 所以这轮把价格和可售标记放进同一行，同步路径只有一次存储读。他也写过：要能承受大约 10 到 20 倍的规模变化，但适合眼下的方案常常不适合 100 倍。[2] 十倍增长留到追问。

若热行已经在数据库内存里，寻道预算就不再是 100，缓存的理由改成尾延迟。Dean 和 Barroso 写过，大约 100 毫秒内的响应才显得顺，规模变大后尾部会主导体验。[3] SRE 监控章里，平均 100 毫秒、每秒 1,000 请求时，仍可能有 1% 的请求要 5 秒；页面若等多个后端，一个后端的 99 分位会变成用户看到的典型延迟。[4] 一次同步存储读，就是不让三条尾巴叠进同一次 GET。80 毫秒仍是假设。

替代方案是不加缓存，加盘直到寻道容量盖住 5,000。五块串行盘大约 500 次每秒，仍差一个数量级；大约 50 块盘才对齐。访问若非常均匀、缓存几乎不命中，加盘更好，因为 98% 依赖请求集中在少数 SKU，而那是假设，不是这里引用的测量。面试官若说每个 SKU 一样热，就删掉缓存，只谈盘或副本。

热门 id 同时过期时，未命中会从平稳的 100 变成接近 5,000 的尖峰。同进程合并同一个 id 只能削掉这一台机器上的重复回源，换一台仍会各打一次，所以它代替不了后面的回源上限。另一个窗口是价格：权威行已是新价，缓存仍可能在 30 秒内返回旧值。浏览接受；商家自己的下一次 GET 不接受，要绕过缓存或读主库。

常见错法是算出 QPS 却不改图，或只说「缓存抗住高并发」却不说命中率。3 GB 也不会迫使分片，溢出的是寻道而不是容量。SRE 的过载章还提醒：用 QPS 代表容量经常失真，不同请求耗的资源不一样。[5] 白板上的每秒 100 次寻道是数量级替身，有实测就替换，不要说成生产限流配置。

Expand the division until it changes the path

In Jeff Dean’s talk slides, a round trip in the same datacenter is 500,000 nanoseconds, or 0.5 ms; a disk seek is 10,000,000 nanoseconds, or 10 ms; a main-memory reference is 100 nanoseconds. [2] If one disk serves random seeks serially, a second has 1,000 milliseconds and 1,000 ÷ 10 = 100. That is a mathematical ceiling at 100% busy with no queueing headroom, not a safe production capacity or a benchmark for current hardware. A real gate must be lower and recalculated from the actual device, buffer-pool hit rate, queueing, and load tests. The published order of magnitude is used only to block “the store can take anything.”

Average 1,000 QPS is already 10× that budget: 1,000 ÷ 100 = 10. The peak is 5,000 ÷ 100 = 50. So even the average, not only the peak, forbids a seek on every detail view. The cache stroke is forced by that multiple. A hit is memory. If the cache is another machine, add about 0.5 ms of datacenter round trip, which is still far under the assumed 80 ms budget. Only a miss is allowed to touch the store.

Ninety-eight percent is not a decorative hit rate. Under the deliberately conservative teaching assumption that every miss becomes a serial 10 ms seek, use ≤100/s as the ceiling. The peak is 5,000, so the miss fraction can be at most about 100 ÷ 5,000 = 0.02 and the hit rate has to be about 98%. Check: 5,000 × 0.02 = 100. If you casually say 90%, misses are 5,000 × 0.1 = 500, still 5× this illustrative ceiling. Ninety percent stands only if measured origin capacity is at least 500/s—perhaps through more disks, or because hot rows live in the database buffer pool and misses do not seek. Say the assumption with the result. Otherwise 98% looks like a magic trick.

In Dean’s thumbnail example, 30 serial seeks × 10 ms = 300 ms, and with transfer the total is about 560 ms; in parallel the work falls to one seek. [2] This round keeps price and availability on the same row, one store read on the synchronous path. He also writes that a design should survive about a 10× to 20× change in scale, while the fit for today is often wrong at 100×. [2] A further 10× waits for the follow-up.

If hot rows already live in database memory, the seek budget is no longer 100 and the cache is about tail latency. Dean and Barroso write that responses on the order of 100 ms feel fluid, and that at larger scale the tail dominates. [3] The SRE monitoring chapter notes that at a mean of 100 ms and 1,000 requests per second, 1% of requests may still take 5 seconds, and a page that waits on several backends feels one backend’s 99th percentile. [4] One synchronous store read keeps three tails from stacking on this GET. The 80 ms target is still an assumption.

The alternative is no cache, and enough disks to cover 5,000 seeks. Five serial disks are about 500 per second, still an order of magnitude short; about 50 disks line up. When access is uniform and a cache would almost never hit, disks are the better stroke, because 98% assumes concentration on a few SKUs and that concentration is not a measurement cited here. If every SKU is equally hot, delete the cache and talk about disks or replicas.

When a hot id expires all at once, misses jump from a smooth 100 toward 5,000. Coalescing one id inside a process only removes duplicate origin reads on that machine; the next machine still sends its own, so coalescing does not replace the origin cap. The other window is price: the row is already new, and the cache may still return the old value for 30 seconds. A browse accepts that. The merchant’s next GET does not, and bypasses the cache or reads the primary.

The usual misses are computing QPS without changing the picture, or saying “the cache handles scale” without a hit rate. 3 GB does not force sharding; the overflow is seeks, not capacity. The SRE overload chapter also notes that QPS often misrepresents capacity because requests cost different resources. [5] One hundred seeks per second on the board is an order-of-magnitude stand-in. Replace it when a measurement exists. Do not call it a production throttle.

第三步 · 高层：无状态服务、权威存储、同步来回，以及故意不画的东西Step 3 · High-level: stateless service, source of truth, synchronous round trip, and what stays unwired

大约 10 到 15 分钟。结束的标志是面试官认可这张图，不是你自己画完。About 10 to 15 minutes. This step ends when they buy the picture, not when you finish drawing.

按一次成功的读把高层说完

客户端发出同步 GET。边缘校验路径和 id 形状，转发到任意一个商品服务实例。实例自己不存行：命中则从缓存取出带 version 的商品 JSON，沿绿色回程返回 200；未命中则读存储，把同一份字段填进缓存，再返回 200。未知 id 返回 404，并且不要把「存储超时」写成 404，否则一次故障会被缓存成「商品不存在」。到这里，调用方已经拿到响应。队列没有参加这次往返。

服务保持无状态，是因为 5,000 QPS 要靠加实例，而不是靠会话粘在某一台机器上。实例挂了，边缘把下一跳送到别的实例，缓存和存储都还在。若把商品行放进服务进程，扩容和发布都会变成数据迁移。小规模时服务和缓存客户端可以在同一个进程里，逻辑上仍然是「计算不拥有行」。存储拥有行，所以商家改价必须写存储成功，才算改完；只改缓存不算。

队列这轮不连线，因为写大约 0.6 QPS，没有扇出要从提交里解开。搜索索引以后要异步更新时，再把那条边接上。第二机房也不连线。Dean 的幻灯片里，加州到荷兰再回来大约 150 毫秒。[2] 同步跨大陆读会吃掉 80 毫秒预算。用户真的跨两大洲、而且 150 毫秒不可接受时，才加区域读缓存，并接受新写入的传播窗口。Brewer 写过，「二选三」是误导：没有分区时不必放弃一致性或可用性；分区期间才按操作选择，不是给整张图贴一张 AP。[1]

这一步要停下来要反馈。话术是：「同步 GET 进来，200 从无状态服务回去，行只在存储里，缓存可丢。队列和第二机房我故意没连。这个方向可以吗？」没有这句，你是在自说自话。若面试官说必须全球低延迟读，再把 Region B 连上，并改口陈旧窗口。若他们说改价必须立刻被所有人看见，就把 30 秒假设划掉，缓存要么去掉，要么改成写后主动失效，那是下一笔的材料，不是现在把多机房画满。

常见错法是高层图上每个盒子都点一次名，却说不出哪条边同步、谁拥有行。深度会在下一步只挖缓存挂掉。广度到此为止。另一个错法是响应只停在服务框里，不画回客户端，然后口头说「返回 JSON」。图上没有回程，面试官无法核对超时发生在哪一跳。

Say the high-level picture as one successful read

The client sends a synchronous GET. The edge checks the path and the shape of the id and forwards to any Product Service instance. The instance stores no row: on a hit it takes the versioned product JSON from cache and returns 200 on the green path; on a miss it reads the store, fills the cache with the same fields, and returns 200. An unknown id is 404. A store timeout must not be recorded as 404, or one outage gets cached as “the product does not exist.” At this point the caller has the response. The queue did not join the round trip.

The service stays stateless because 5,000 QPS is absorbed by adding instances, not by sticking a session to one machine. When an instance dies, the edge sends the next hop elsewhere. Cache and store remain. Put the product row inside the service process and every scale-out or rollout becomes a data move. At small size the service and the cache client may share a process; logically, computation still does not own the row. The store owns the row, so a merchant price change counts only after the store write succeeds. Writing the cache alone does not.

The queue stays unwired because writes are about 0.6 QPS and nothing about fan-out has to detach from the commit. An asynchronous search index later is the write-family queue from the map. It turns from “not connected” into an async edge when they ask. Drawing it now pays for a failure domain with no constraint. Region B stays unwired for the same reason. In Dean’s slides a packet from California to the Netherlands and back is about 150 ms. [2] One synchronous cross-continent read spends the entire 80 ms budget and more. One region meets the assumed latency first. Add a regional read cache only when users actually span continents and 150 ms is unacceptable, and then admit a propagation window for new writes. Brewer writes that the “two out of three” poster is misleading: with no partition there is no reason to forfeit consistency or availability, and during a partition the choice is per operation, fine-grained enough to differ by call. It is not an AP sticker on the whole drawing. [1]

Stop and ask. The line is: “Synchronous GET in, 200 back from a stateless service, rows only in the store, cache droppable. I deliberately did not wire the queue or the second region. Is that direction right?” Without that sentence you are talking to yourself. If they require low-latency global reads, connect Region B and restate the staleness window. If they require every reader to see a price change immediately, cross out the 30-second assumption and either remove the cache or switch to invalidation on write. That is material for the next stroke, not a reason to fill the board with regions now.

The common miss names every box and never says which edge is synchronous or who owns the row. Depth in the next step digs only into a dead cache. Breadth stops here. The other miss ends the response at the service box and says “return JSON” out loud. With no return path on the drawing, they cannot see which hop a timeout belongs to.

第四步 · 深挖一条故障：缓存层挂了，回源有边界Step 4 · One deep dive: the cache tier is down, and origin has a boundary

大约 10 到 25 分钟挖这一条，最后 3 到 5 分钟收口。不要换成第二套架构。Spend about 10 to 25 minutes on this one failure, then 3 to 5 minutes to close. Do not switch to a second architecture.

故障时请求实际怎么走

缓存层熔断后，商品服务不再等缓存。白板上先沿用≤100/s 这个教学上限表示「回源必须有界」；生产值必须根据压测、目标利用率、缓冲池命中和安全余量替换，不能把 100 照抄成限流配置。按示例数字，放行且读到行返回 200，没有行返回 404，没放行的在服务侧 503，并带 Retry-After，不要无界重试。5,000 − 100 ≈ 4,900，5,000 ÷ 100 = 50；这个难看的比例说明缓存层是故障域，不是可有可无的加速器。

选 503 而不是无限排队，是因为超时本身就是决定。Brewer 把分区在操作上看成通信的时间上限：一直重试等于选择一致性并放弃这一次调用的可用性。[1] 停下来的是超出预算的 GET。放行的约 100 次仍读权威行，没有用旧值换可用。SRE 的过载章写过：极端过载下应返回错误，尽快拒绝，免得依赖把资源花在完不成的工作上；本地旧副本只在你还握着那份副本时才算降级。[5] 缓存整层都没了，冷实例也没有副本，所以这张图拒绝超出的请求，不发明命中。

进程内存里还有这份商品、而且没超过 30 秒时，直接返回那份旧 JSON，不去打已经过载的存储。窗口过了或本地没有这个 id，就 503，不要偷偷把 TTL 拉长。存储自己挂了是另一条故障：缓存还热时才讨论旧值；未知 id 和超时不能变成 404。等面试官指向存储再加那条红线。

熔断刚打开时，各实例会一起回源。总上限由存储能力算出，但必须由共享限流器或集中配额执行，不能每台服务都以为自己有 100。放行的读看到存储里的当前行；被 503 的请求什么都看不到。

「缓存挂了就查数据库」会把 5,000 打进预算只有 100 的存储，浏览和改价一起死。先说放行数从哪道除法来，再说其余请求的状态码。深挖时不要把队列、第二机房和推荐补上；那些盒子是故意不连的。

What the request does during the failure

After the cache tier opens its circuit, Product Service stops waiting on it. On the whiteboard, ≤100/s remains an illustrative ceiling that proves origin fallback must be bounded. A production value must replace it using load tests, target utilization, buffer-pool behavior, and safety headroom; never copy 100 into a rate-limit configuration. With the teaching number, an admitted request returns 200 when the row exists and 404 when it does not. A request that is not admitted returns 503 inside the service process, preferably with Retry-After, and does not retry without a bound. Subtract 100 from the peak of 5,000 and about 4,900 QPS fail; 5,000 ÷ 100 = 50. The ugly ratio is intentional: the cache tier is a failure domain, not an optional accelerator.

Choosing 503 over an unbounded queue is a timeout decision. Brewer treats a partition, operationally, as a time bound on communication: retrying forever chooses consistency and gives up availability for that call; stopping does the reverse. [1] What stops here are the GETs over budget. The 100 that are admitted still read the authoritative row, so they did not trade a stale value for availability. The overload chapter of the SRE book says that under extreme overload a service may not even have time to build a degraded response and should serve an error, and that rejecting quickly is better than spending the dependency’s CPU on work it cannot finish. It also lists a local copy that may not be fully up to date as one form of degradation, which applies when you still have that copy. [5] If the whole cache tier is gone, the copy is not in that tier. A cold service instance does not have it either. The first failure picture therefore rejects the overflow instead of inventing a hit that is not there.

The alternative is better when a copy is still in process memory. If 30 seconds of staleness is allowed and this instance’s local cache still holds the id inside that age, return that JSON and do not touch a store that is already over budget. You buy availability and you pay the staleness already written down as an assumption. Past the window, or with no local key, return 503. Do not quietly stretch the TTL to “save” availability. The store itself being down is a different failure and does not belong on this same stroke. When the store is down and the cache is still warm, that is the moment to discuss serving an old value inside the stale window. An unknown id and a timeout still must not become 404. Add that red line when they point at the store. Two failure stories on one picture and the close falls apart.

Two concurrency windows remain. First, the instant the circuit opens, every instance falls through together. Store capacity derives one global cap, but a shared limiter or centrally allocated quota must enforce it; 100 per service process would still overload origin. Second, there is no extra magic between a merchant’s just-committed price and an admitted read: the admitted read sees the current row, and a shed read sees nothing. Do not say “the 503 users will be consistent when they refresh” as if that covers the page they cannot load now.

The common wrong line is “if the cache dies, read the database.” At 5,000 against 100, that read punches through the seek budget and the failure spreads from cache to source of truth, taking browse and price updates down together. The correction is to say which division produced the admit count, then which status code the rest receive. The other miss uses the deep dive to bolt on the queue, the second region, and recommendations. Those boxes were left unwired on purpose. A deep dive finishes one red line: who is rejected, who still reads the authoritative row, and how traffic is let back in during recovery. It does not complete a noun list.

收口时准备好的三句瓶颈Three bottleneck lines to keep for the close

不要说设计完美。收口只留三个瓶颈：缓存整层挂掉时只有约 100 QPS 能活，恢复时要慢慢放流量；热 key 会先打满一个缓存分片，饱和常常先看 p99；[4] 权威行和缓存之间最多 30 秒旧价，商家自己的读要绕过缓存。观测收在四个黄金信号：延迟分开成功和失败，流量是这条 GET 的 QPS，错误包括 5xx 和超过 80 毫秒的请求，饱和看回源是否靠近 100。[4]

Do not call the design perfect. Close on three bottlenecks: when the whole cache tier is down only about 100 QPS survive, and recovery must let traffic back slowly; a hot key fills one cache shard first, and saturation often shows up as p99; [4] the row and the cache can disagree for up to 30 seconds, so the merchant’s own read bypasses the cache. Observation closes on the four golden signals: split success latency from failure latency, traffic is this GET’s QPS, errors include 5xx and requests over 80 ms, and saturation is origin QPS nearing 100. [4]

中文

本章主线按 35 分钟排；真实面试若给 45 分钟，把多出的 10 分钟加到第四步追问，不要再铺一套架构。白板左侧写假设和除法，中间画请求与回程，右侧只留一条故障和 trade-off。级别改的是深度：初级说清 GET、404 和「行在存储里」；资深说清 5,000 对 100、共享回源上限和 503；再往上说明哪个操作允许陈旧、为什么第二机房还没连。

卡住时第一句把卡点说出来：「我卡在回源上限该用寻道还是用缓冲池。」然后退回高层那张同步 GET 的图，问面试官要不要换预算，或者换一条故障。要提示不丢人。沉默几十秒等于没有信号。算法题可以安静想；这道题不行。也不要在一个 TTL 公式上花 15 分钟。每 5 分钟看一次时间，深挖只保留缓存这一条红线。

This chapter budgets a 35-minute core. If the interview gives 45 minutes, spend the extra ten on follow-ups inside step 4 instead of spreading a second architecture. A junior walk-through names the GET, the 404, and “the row lives in the store.” A senior one names 5,000 against 100, the shared origin cap, and the 503. 追问：从新约束改图，不从零再画Follow-ups: change the picture from a new constraint

流量再翻 10 倍

现在的峰值是 5,000。再翻 10 倍是 50,000 QPS。寻道预算若仍是 100，需要的命中率是 1 − 100 ÷ 50,000 = 0.998，也就是 99.8%。这已经不是一句「缓存还在」能兜住的。要么把寻道容量加到大约 10 块盘的 1,000 次每秒，命中率需求回到 98%（50,000 × 0.02 = 1,000），要么把缓存按 key 拆开，避免一个热 SKU 打满单分片。仍然先说哪个数溢出，再加盒子。Dean 提醒过，适合当前规模的方案到 100 倍常常不对；50,000 相对最初的 1,000 平均已经是 50 倍，应该主动说这张图的边界到了。[2]

Traffic times ten

The peak is 5,000. Times ten is 50,000 QPS. If the seek budget is still 100, the required hit rate is 1 − 100 ÷ 50,000 = 0.998, or 99.8%. “The cache is still there” does not cover that. Either raise seek capacity to about 1,000 per second, on the order of ten disks, which brings the required hit rate back to 98% because 50,000 × 0.02 = 1,000, or split the cache by key so one hot SKU cannot fill a shard. Name the overflow before adding a box. Dean notes that the design that fits the current size is often wrong at 100×. 50,000 against the original 1,000 average is already 50×, so say that this picture has reached its edge. [2]

一个机房挂了

不要说「我们是 AP」。Brewer 的更正是：没有分区时，一致性和可用性可以同时有；分区期间才按操作选择。[1] 分区在操作上就是超时。浏览可以在 30 秒窗口内读副本或尚热的缓存；改价若不能分叉，就在分区期间拒绝写。跨大陆同步读大约 150 毫秒，[2] 所以只有延迟目标改了才连上 Region B，并写明哪种调用继续、哪种失败。

One datacenter dies

Do not say “we are AP.” Brewer’s correction is that consistency and availability can both hold when there is no partition; during a partition you must choose, and different operations may choose differently. [1] Operationally a partition is a timeout. A product browse may read another replica or a still-warm cache inside the assumed 30-second window: bounded staleness bought with availability. A price update that must not fork refuses the write during the partition: availability given up for a single copy. Waiting forever for the other side spends the 80 ms budget and may still get no answer. That is why Region B is not decoration on the first picture. A synchronous cross-continent read is about 150 ms. [2] Connect Region B only after the latency goal changes, and say which call continues during a partition and which call fails.

怎么监控

四个黄金信号就够：延迟、流量、错误、饱和。[4] 延迟把 200 和 503 分开，否则拒绝越快平均越好看。流量是这条 GET 的 QPS。错误包括 5xx，也包括超过 80 毫秒、被你自己的目标定义成失败的请求。饱和看回源离 100 还有多远，以及命中率是否还在约 98% 附近。p99 常常比 CPU 打满更早。[4]

How do you monitor?

The four golden signals are enough to close: latency, traffic, errors, and saturation. [4] Split latency of 200s from latency of 503s, or faster rejects make the average look healthier. Traffic is QPS of GET /v1/products/{id}, broken out by hit and miss when that matters. Errors include 5xx and the requests you defined as failures by missing 80 ms. Saturation is how close origin QPS is to 100, and whether hit rate is still near the 98% this budget needs. Rising p99 is often earlier than a full CPU. [4] Add one way to follow a single request, so a slow store is distinct from a slow network on the way to the store. Do not close by listing vendor product names.

商家刚改完价，自己刷新却看到旧的

这是读自己的写，不是把所有人的 TTL 改成零。PUT 先提交存储，提交成功才算改完。商家随后对同一 id 的 GET 短时间绕过缓存或读主库。其他浏览者仍可能在 30 秒内看到旧价。缓存值要带 version 和 price_cents。若任何人都不许看到旧价，就改成写后删除，并重算命中率，因为失效会制造回源。

The merchant refreshes and still sees the old price

This is read-your-write, not “set everyone’s TTL to zero.” The merchant’s PUT commits to the store first. The change counts only after that commit. Their following GET for the same id bypasses the cache or reads the primary for a short window, so they see the new price. Other browsers may still see the old price inside the assumed 30 seconds. The cached value has to carry version and price_cents, or you cannot tell an expired row from the current one. If the product changes and says nobody may see an old price, the cache stroke becomes delete-on-write or a version check, and the hit-rate assumption has to be recomputed because invalidation creates origin reads. “The cache will eventually be consistent” does not answer this. That sentence has no deadline and does not say who is reading.

Two process failures are not in the table and are just as costly. One is jumping ahead: drawing the cache before scope is written down. The cache earns its place only after 5,000 has been compared with 100. The other is silence while you think about the origin cap. Say “I am comparing seeks with the buffer pool,” and they can replace the budget with the number they care about. Over-engineering is the third: queue, second region, and recommendations appear together, and no edge says why they are synchronous or why they are unwired.
