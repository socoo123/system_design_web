设计新闻 FeedDesign a news feed

先问清楚Ask before you draw

中文

Feed 三个 hard part：fan-out 发生在写时还是读时，大 V 的写放大，timeline 里存什么。系统看起来就是发帖和刷列表。短链是读多写少的 KV，通知是服务推向用户的 fan-out。这题反过来：一条帖要出现在粉丝的 timeline 里，写会被粉丝数放大。

五个问题就停，其余写成假设。问过 10 分钟也是 red flag。没问流类型就画召回和精排，是把推荐系统整章搬上来。

我假设这是关注流：用户发帖，粉丝按时间看。推荐是另一道题，这一轮不做排序模型。秒级最终一致，不保证全世界同一秒看到。媒体是对象存储里的 URL，CDN 不在这题展开。分页用 cursor。我按这个画，不对你打断我。

English

Feed has three hard parts: whether fan-out happens on write or on read, celebrity write amplification, and what the timeline stores. The product looks like post and scroll. A URL shortener is read-heavy KV. Notifications are fan-out from the service to the user. This prompt is the reverse: one post has to show up on every follower's timeline, so writes scale with follower count.

Five questions, then stop. Write the rest as assumptions. Past ten minutes of questions is a red flag too. Drawing recall and ranking before you asked which feed this is means you imported a recommender.

I'll assume a follow feed: people post, followers read by time. A recommender is a different problem. No ranking model this round. Seconds, eventual consistency. I will not promise the whole world sees it in the same second. Media is a URL in object storage. CDN stays off this board. Pagination is a cursor. I'll draw on that — cut me off if it's wrong.

提问的习惯在 Ch01。问关键的，写下假设，继续画。The habit of asking lives in Ch01. Ask the key ones, write the assumptions, keep drawing.

数量级Orders of magnitude

中文

公式在估算章。这里只要证明：发帖本身不贵，贵的是 fan-out。下面全是白板假设，用公开量级，不是某家公司的内部数字。中位数粉丝更低，均值被大 V 拉高，所以平均按 200 来讲。

约 1000 万 DAU，约 10% 的人每天发 1 帖，就是一天约 1e6 帖。每人每天打开 feed 约 20 次。文本大约 1 KB × 1e6，一天约 1 GB，权威在 post 库。timeline 只缓存活跃用户最近约 1000 个 id，内存是几十到一百 GB 这个量级。

千万 DAU：发帖日均大约十几 QPS，读 feed 是千到万。平均 200 个粉丝，纯 push 就是每秒几千次 ZADD。一个百万粉的号发一条，是瞬时一百万次写，摊不进日均。所以不能纯 push。

English

The formulas live in the estimates chapter. Here you only show that posting is cheap and fan-out is what costs. Every number below is a board assumption from public-scale teaching numbers, not a company's internals. The median follower count is lower. Celebrities pull the mean up, so say about 200.

About 10 million DAU, about 10% posting once a day, is about 1e6 posts a day. About 20 feed opens per person per day. Text is about 1 KB × 1e6, about 1 GB a day, and the post store is the source of truth. The timeline caches about the last 1000 ids for active users, on the order of tens to about 100 GB.

On a 10-million DAU board, posts are about a dozen QPS on a daily average. Reading the feed is thousands to tens of thousands. At 200 followers, pure push is thousands of ZADDs per second. One post from a million-follower account is an instant million writes. That does not average into the daily mean. You cannot pure-push.

估算口径见 Ch03 · 粗略估算。The estimate habit is Ch03 · back-of-the-envelope.

高层：先落库，再 fan-outHigh-level: persist, then fan-out

中文

从 App 一笔一笔加。post 库是权威，按作者分片。timeline 是每人一份预计算的 inbox，只存 post_id。写放大走 MQ 异步，API 先返回。请求线程里不扫粉丝列表。

schema 够用就停：post（post_id、author_id、text、media_url、created_at、deleted），follow（follower_id、followee_id），timeline 是 Redis ZSET，score 为时间，member 为 post_id。热点查询是「这个作者的粉丝」，索引在 followee_id。分区和 ISR 不要在这题展开。简单权重（互动 × 时间衰减）可以提一句，面试官说别太呆再加。这题不放模型。

English

Start at the app and add one box per stroke. The post store is the source of truth, sharded by author. The timeline is a precomputed inbox per user, post_id only. Write amplification goes async on the queue, and the API returns first. The request thread does not scan the follower list.

Stop the schema when it is enough: post (post_id, author_id, text, media_url, created_at, deleted), follow (follower_id, followee_id), timeline as a Redis ZSET with time as the score and post_id as the member. The hot query is "this author's followers," indexed on followee_id. Leave partitions and ISR for the queue chapter. You can mention a simple score, engagement × time decay, in one sentence, and add it only if they say the feed is too dumb. No model on this prompt.

post 放哪一类存储，见 Ch37 · 存储选型。缓存和 CDN 在 Ch38 · 缓存与 CDN。分片在 Ch41 · 复制与分片。队列语义在 Ch42 · 消息与弹性。分区课是 Ch20 · 分布式消息队列，这题不展开。Where a post lives is Ch37 · storage choices. Cache and CDN are Ch38 · cache and CDN. Sharding is Ch41 · replication and sharding. Queue semantics are Ch42 · messaging. The partition chapter is Ch20 · message queue. Not this board.

Trade-off · push 还是 pullTrade-off · push or pull

中文

这一套只讲 trade-off：fan-out 在写时还是读时。写放大和读延迟换位置，不是道德选择。失败路径下一套再画，不塞进同一张。

纯 push 对普通人合适：粉丝几十到几千，预计算让刷 feed 变成一次缓存读。纯 pull 对大 V 合适：发帖写一次，读摊在一天里。两边都会在规模上爆，所以 2026 的白板默认 hybrid，而不是先画纯 push、等对方问明星再改。小规模、没有大 V 时，可以说先纯 push，阈值到了再切。那是演进，不是这题的第一答案。

普通人 push，大 V pull，读的时候 fan-in。阈值按粉丝数，教学大约十万，不是魔法数，看写放大和读延迟调。我不会第一笔把纯 push 画死。

English

This pass is only the trade-off: fan-out on write or on read. You are swapping write amplification and read latency. The failure path is the next pass. It does not share these slides.

Pure push fits regular users: tens to thousands of followers, and a precomputed timeline turns the feed into one cache read. Pure pull fits celebrities: one write on post, and the reads spread across the day. Both sides explode at scale, so the 2026 board defaults to hybrid. You do not draw pure push and patch it when they mention a star. At small scale, with no celebrities, you can start on pure push and say you will cut over when the threshold trips. That is evolution, not the first answer on this prompt.

Regular users push, celebrities pull, and the read is a fan-in. The threshold is follower count. Teaching uses about 100k. It is not a magic number. Tune it on write amplification and read latency. I will not lock the first picture to pure push.

通知那道题的 fan-out 是服务推向用户，见 Ch10。队列怎么声明至少一次，在 Ch42 · 消息与弹性。The notification fan-out is service to user, in Ch10. How you declare at-least-once is Ch42 · messaging.

Hard part · 大 VHard part · celebrities

中文

第二个 hard part。粉丝数是幂律：极少账号贡献绝大部分 fan-out。一条百万粉的帖，纯 push 是瞬时 1e6 次 timeline 写。MQ 堆积、Redis CPU、粉丝看到的时间拉开，这是 hot key。再加消费者消不掉根因。写尖峰挤在同一毫秒；读是粉丝在一天里陆续打开 App。把瞬时写换成分散读，尖峰就没了。

教学线大约 10 万粉。低于这条走 push，跨过就停 push、改 outbox。生产按写放大和 p99 读延迟调。账号变成大 V 之后，旧的 push 停掉，已经在 inbox 里的 id 不必立刻清。长期不活跃的粉丝也可以不 push，他们回来时 pull 一页。活跃粉丝限速分批 push 是优化，不是第一张图。每人关注的大 V 通常远少于关注总数，所以 fan-in 的路数可预期。

大 V 我不对全体粉丝 ZADD。只写 outbox，下次读 feed 时 merge。写热点变成读热点，同一份 outbox，缓存吃得下。不保证一亿个 inbox 在同一秒都有这条 id。

English

Second hard part. Follower counts follow a power law: a tiny set of accounts produce most of the fan-out work. One post from a million-follower account, pure push, is an instant 1e6 timeline writes. Queue backlog, Redis CPU, followers seeing it at wildly different times: that is a hot key. More consumers do not remove the cause. The write spike arrives in the same millisecond. The reads are followers opening the app across a day. Swap the instant write for spread-out reads and the spike is gone.

The teaching line is about 100k followers. Below it, push. Cross it, stop the push and switch to an outbox. Production tunes on write amplification and p99 read latency. When an account becomes a celebrity, stop the old push. Ids already in inboxes do not need an instant purge. You can also skip long-inactive followers; when they return they pull one page. A rate-limited batched push to active followers is an optimization, not the first diagram. People follow far fewer celebrities than total accounts, so the fan-in width stays predictable.

I will not ZADD every follower of a celebrity. I write the outbox, and the next feed read merges it. The write hotspot becomes a read hotspot: one shared outbox, and cache can eat that. I will not promise that a hundred million inboxes contain this id in the same second.

写热点为什么改成读热点更吃得下，见 Ch38 · 缓存与 CDN。Why a read hotspot is easier to cache than a write hotspot: Ch38 · cache and CDN.

Hard part · timeline 存什么Hard part · what the timeline stores

中文

第三个 hard part。Feed 是往下翻，同时新帖还在插进来。页稳不稳看 cursor，内容对不对看 hydration。timeline 只存 post_id，读的时候再批量补全。全文塞进每人一份 ZSET，内存差两个数量级，改文案还要改千万份副本。

cursor 是不透明 token，服务端解成 (created_at, post_id)。雪花 id 自带时间序，也可以当这把键。下一页取严格更老的 20 条。首页没有 cursor。客户端不传 page=3。offset 深翻会变慢，翻页之间插入新帖会重复或漏。hybrid 的两路 merge 用同一把有序键切一刀。

分页用 (时间, post_id) cursor。timeline 只存 id，读时 MGET hydrate。用户卡片和计数走各自的缓存，不 JOIN 成一张宽行。媒体只返回 URL。

English

Third hard part. The feed is infinite scroll while new posts keep inserting. Whether the page stays stable is the cursor. Whether the content is right is hydration. The timeline stores post_id only, and you hydrate in batch on read. Stuffing full posts into a per-user ZSET costs about two orders of magnitude more memory, and an edit would have to patch tens of millions of copies.

The cursor is an opaque token. The server decodes it to (created_at, post_id). A snowflake id already carries time order, so it can be that key. The next page is 20 items strictly older than the anchor. Home has no cursor. The client does not pass page=3. Offset gets slower as you go deep, and a post inserted between pages duplicates or skips. The hybrid merge uses one ordered key and one cut.

Pagination is a (time, post_id) cursor. The timeline stores ids only. On read, MGET to hydrate. User cards and counters live in their own caches. Do not JOIN one fat row. Media comes back as a URL.

id 和正文分开放，存储选型见 Ch37 · 存储选型。hydrate 和回源见 Ch38 · 缓存与 CDN。Ids and bodies live apart. Storage choices are Ch37 · storage choices. Hydrate and backfill are Ch38 · cache and CDN.

失败路径Failure path

中文

这一套只讲写到一半、删帖、冷用户。push 还是 pull 已经在 trade-off 那一套里讲完。

队列会重投。worker 写到一半挂了，重试再 ZADD 同一个 post_id，结果一样，所以 fan-out 幂等。删帖不要遍历全部粉丝 inbox。post 上打 tombstone，hydrate 时丢掉，后台慢慢摘 id。取关也一样：读的时候按 follow 集过滤，或异步摘。追求删帖后一亿份 inbox 强一致，是 over-engineering。

我按 at-least-once 讲。删帖不扫粉丝的 timeline。冷用户平时不 push，回来时 pull 一页重建。

English

This pass is only a half-finished write, deletes, and cold users. Push versus pull already had its own trade-off pass.

The queue redelivers. If a worker dies halfway, the retry ZADDs the same post_id again and the ZSET does not change, so fan-out is idempotent. A delete does not walk every follower inbox. Put a tombstone on the post, drop it at hydrate time, and strip ids in the background. Unfollow is the same idea: filter on read by the follow set, or strip async. Chasing strong consistency across a hundred million inboxes after one delete is over-engineering.

I declare at-least-once. A delete does not scan follower timelines. Cold users are not pushed day to day. When they come back, they pull one page and rebuild.

重投和幂等的展开在 Ch42 · 消息与弹性。The longer version of redelivery and idempotency is Ch42 · messaging.
