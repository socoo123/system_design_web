30 秒开场：先分清确认、可见和读页The 30-second open: acknowledgement, visibility and page reads

中文

这是一条按时间排序的公开关注流。用户发帖，粉丝刷新后看到它。Post Store 保存正文和删除状态；普通人的帖子由 Worker 给粉丝 inbox 写指针，大 V 只写共享的 CelebLog。GET 把自己的 inbox 和关注的大 V 日志归并，再按 id 补正文。推荐、召回、精排和双塔不在本题范围；媒体只记录 URL，字节另走对象存储与 CDN。先把这条依赖走通，再谈跨地域。

201 不是「粉丝已经看到」。Post、Outbox 和幂等记录在同一笔事务中提交，API 才返回 post_id。发布 p99 300 ms 是假设 A1，不能让它等 F 个 inbox。粉丝新鲜度另以 5 s 为工作目标，要把 Relay、排队、扇出和 timeline 缓存年龄一起预算。作者读己之写走 Post Store 的作者索引，并在客户端显示 201 带回的帖子，不靠催促 Worker。

200 也不是「所有历史已经到齐」。页面按当前已经物化的候选读取，允许晚扇出在刷新后补进来。读服务端 p99 80 ms 只针对普通缓存命中页，不含手机网络和首次构建重读者视图。删帖约 30 s 生效是另一条弱一致目标，依赖主库读取、回源截止和缓存 TTL；需要立即撤回的敏感内容就必须额外查权威状态。

这些数字都是为了继续设计写下的 assumption，没有冒充线上报表。尤其不能把输入速率叫处理能力，也不能把几段 p99 相加说成整条链的 p99。下面分配的是阶段截止预算；实现时还要测整条链的延迟分布，监控客户端刷新实际看见新帖的时间。

English

This is a public follow feed ordered by time. A user posts and followers see it on refresh. Post Store owns bodies and delete status. Worker pushes ordinary posts as inbox pointers and writes celebrity posts only to a shared CelebLog. GET merges the inbox and followed celebrity logs, then hydrates bodies by id. Recommendation, retrieval and ranking are outside this problem. Media is represented by URLs; bytes use object storage and CDN. Finish this dependency path before discussing regions.

A 201 does not mean followers have seen the post. API returns post_id after Post, Outbox and the idempotency record commit together. The assumed publish p99 is 300 ms in A1 and cannot wait for F inboxes. Follower freshness has a separate 5 s working target, budgeting Relay, queueing, fan-out and timeline-cache age. Read-your-write uses the author index in Post Store and the client displays the post returned in the 201; it does not rush Worker.

A 200 does not mean all history has arrived. A page reads currently materialized candidates; late fan-out may appear on refresh. The server p99 of 80 ms applies to ordinary cache-hit pages, excluding the mobile network and first construction of a heavy-reader view. Delete visibility of about 30 s is a separate weak-consistency target depending on primary reads, origin deadlines and TTL. Immediate retraction of sensitive content requires an authoritative status check.

Every number below is an assumption used to continue the design, not a production report. Offered load is not capacity, and adding component p99 values does not establish an end-to-end p99. The budgets below are stage deadlines; implementation must measure the whole latency distribution and the actual time until a client refresh sees a post.

两次乘法和一次容量除法，决定 hybridTwo multiplications and a capacity division decide hybrid

中文

先算进入系统的工作。A2 的发帖量是 10,000,000 × 0.1 = 1,000,000 条/天，平均 1,000,000 ÷ 86,400 ≈ 11.57/s，五倍峰值约 58/s。按 A3 平均 F=200，纯 push 上界是 1,000,000 × 200 = 200,000,000 行/天，平均约 2,315 行/s；先不舍入再乘五，峰值约 11,574 行/s。混合方案会省掉部分高粉作者的写，这个数可以作为普通扇出的保守总量预算。

读量单独算：10,000,000 × 20 = 200,000,000 次/天，也约 2,315/s、峰值 11,574/s。与扇出平均数相等只是 0.1×200 恰好等于 20。打开次数减半，读量减半，扇出不变。若每次读都探测 200 个关注对象，纯 pull 平均约 2,315×200=463,000 次作者探测/s，峰值约 2.315M/s；它不能因为一次接口叫 batch 就只算成一次数据库工作。

再算单帖尖峰，不能拿日均当容量。百万粉的一帖要求 1,000,000 行写。除以日均输入 2,315 得约 432 s，只是在说它相当于多少背景工作，不能证明完成时长。完成时间要除以 A5 的单任务能力 10,000 行/s，得到 100 s，再加固定工作 0.5 s。即使独占总容量 30,000 行/s，也要约 33.3 s。这两个条件都不满足 5 s，所以大 V 不全量 push。

本轮把普通人阈值定为 F≤25,000：0.5 + 25,000÷10,000 = 3 s，另给排队 1 s、timeline 缓存陈旧 1 s，合成 5 s 的截止预算。总写峰值 11,574÷30,000≈38.6%，还留约 18,426 行/s 供积压与重试；这份余量也要按分片和热用户压测。若沿用 100,000 粉阈值，单任务就是 10.5 s，不能再宣称 5 s。阈值不是某家公司公开常数，是容量与新鲜度一起逼出的决定。

Hybrid 用一次自己的 inbox 读取替代普通作者的逐个探测；轻读者再拉完整的 K 路大 V 尾部。K=8 时平均上界 2,315×8=18,520 次尾部读取/s，峰值约 92,592/s。共享作者 key 可以缓存，但纯 pull 也能缓存作者 key，不能说只有 hybrid 才共享。Hybrid 的直接收益是把普通作者的 200 路归并从每一次请求拿掉，代价是持久派生数据、重试与修复。

English

Start with offered work. A2 gives 10,000,000 × 0.1 = 1,000,000 posts/day, averaging 11.57/s and peaking near 58/s at 5×. With A3 mean F=200, pure push is an upper bound of 200,000,000 rows/day, about 2,315/s on average. Multiply the unrounded rate by five for about 11,574/s at peak. Hybrid removes some high-follower work, so use this as a conservative aggregate fan-out budget.

Size reads independently: 10,000,000 × 20 = 200,000,000 opens/day, also about 2,315/s and 11,574/s at peak. The match is only because 0.1×200 equals 20. Halving opens halves reads without changing fan-out. Probing 200 followees on every read makes pure pull about 463,000 author probes/s on average and 2.315M/s at peak. A batch interface does not turn that into one unit of database work.

Then size one post’s burst; average demand is not capacity. A million-follower post requires a million writes. Dividing by average input 2,315 yields roughly 432 s of background work, not a completion time. Completion uses the A5 per-job capacity of 10,000 rows/s: 100 s plus 0.5 s fixed work. Even monopolizing all 30,000 rows/s would take about 33.3 s. Neither fits 5 s, so do not fully push a celebrity.

Set ordinary push to F≤25,000: 0.5 + 25,000÷10,000 = 3 s, plus 1 s queueing and 1 s timeline-cache staleness for a 5 s deadline budget. Peak offered writes use about 38.6% of 30,000/s, leaving around 18,426/s for backlog and retries; validate that slack per shard and hot reader. Keeping a 100,000-follower threshold would take 10.5 s per job, contradicting 5 s. This threshold is a design consequence, not a published company constant.

Hybrid replaces ordinary-author probes with one inbox read, then reads all K celebrity tails for a light reader. At K=8, the bound is 18,520 tail reads/s on average and about 92,592/s at peak. Author keys can be shared in cache, but pure pull can also cache them. Hybrid’s direct benefit is removing ordinary-author 200-way merging from every request; the cost is durable derived data, retries and repair.

总览：谁提交，谁扇出，谁把页返回Overview: who commits, who fans out and who returns the page

中文

正常写路径沿上半张图走。Client → Feed API → Post Store，提交回程原路返回。Relay 读出已提交的 Outbox，向 Fan-out Queue 发布，Worker 把指针写入 Timeline Store。图先画普通作者分支，第 3 组再补 CelebLog。入口可以是多台无状态 API，这里只画一台请求拥有者；负载均衡与鉴权不改变扇出推导，因此省略而不是暗示没有。

读路径沿另一条依赖走。Feed API 查自己的 Timeline Store，得到候选 id，再查 Post Store 补正文，最后 200 回到 Client。缓存是 API 旁边的支路，不接在两个数据库之间。Post Store 按作者路由或经 post_id 中的路由信息定位，Timeline Store 按读者 user_id 路由；跨片写发生在 Worker，不能把每个读者 GET 变成扫描所有作者分片。

分片先定访问键，再按真实单片预算定台数。A5 是整层 30,000 行/s，不是每片都能写这么多；若一个热门读者关注许多高频作者，它的 inbox 片仍可能偏斜。批量写可降低往返，不会消掉行数。小产品读 QPS 与关注数都小，可以先按作者索引纯 pull；规模变化时加派生 inbox，而不是第一天就照着盒子数量抄。

故障的边界也在图里：正文提交失败就没有 201；队列失败留下 Outbox 积压，不撤销已提交的帖；Timeline Store 失败且无有效缓存就没有完整页。图中没画的对象存储只负责媒体字节，不是第二份 feed 权威。把这些行为说清楚，比再添一个地域更能回答「客户端现在看到什么」。

English

Walk the write path along the top. Client → Feed API → Post Store, with commit returning through the same owners. Relay reads committed Outbox rows and publishes to Fan-out Queue; Worker writes pointers to Timeline Store. This overview starts with ordinary authors and deck 3 adds CelebLog. Multiple stateless APIs can sit behind the entry; only one request owner is shown. Load balancing and authentication are omitted because they do not change fan-out arithmetic.

Reads follow another dependency. Feed API reads its Timeline Store for candidate ids, hydrates from Post Store, and returns 200 to Client. Caches are API side branches, not links between databases. Post Store routes by author or routing information in post_id; Timeline Store routes by reader user_id. Cross-shard materialization belongs to Worker; a reader GET must not scan every author shard.

Choose access keys before dividing by measured shard capacity. A5’s 30,000 rows/s is aggregate capacity, not the capacity of each shard. A reader following many frequent authors may still skew one inbox shard. Batch writes reduce round trips, not row count. A small product with few reads and followees can start with author-index pull, adding derived inboxes as constraints change.

Failures follow the same boundaries: no 201 after a failed body commit; queue loss leaves Outbox backlog without canceling a committed post; loss of Timeline Store with no valid cache prevents a complete page. The omitted object store owns media bytes, not a second feed authority. These behaviors answer what the client sees now.

API 和数据模型：把幂等落到持久键API and data model: put idempotency in durable keys

中文

POST /v1/posts 带 Idempotency-Key 和 text、media_urls。服务端校验失败 400，未登录 401，限流 429 并带 Retry-After，提交不可用 503。数据库用 (author_id, key) 唯一约束，并记录 request_hash 与原响应；同 key 同 body 重试取原 post_id，同 key 不同 body 返回 409。幂等行与帖子、Outbox 同事务提交，两个并发请求不能各创建一帖。A11 保留 24 h，客户端在此窗口内重试；过期之后不能声称仍永久幂等。

最小 Post 行是 post_id、author_id、created_at、text、media_urls、status、version，并有作者时间索引。Timeline 的唯一键是 (user_id, post_id)，有序索引是 (user_id, created_at, post_id)；不能只说排序主键就已保证重复 post_id 不存在。CelebLog 按 author_id 排时间，也是唯一 (author_id, post_id)。Outbox 用 event_id 标识事件，另存作者、帖 id、原创建时间、路由模式代际和 published_at。队列消息不携带百万粉丝列表。

Follow 记录 (follower_id, followee_id)，反向索引供 Worker 按作者分页找粉丝。TAO 原文的对象、关联与按时间排序的关联列表支持这样的访问模型 [1]，本章借的是查询方向，没有声称 TAO 就是这个 feed 实现。取关的读过滤检查当前关注集合；关注集合版本变化使旧 cursor 返回 409，要求刷新。反向索引异步维护时还必须有修复，不能默认两份边永远同时更新。

Redis sorted set 可以表达缓存里的有序成员：ZADD 对已存在 member 更新 score，不新增同名成员；同 score 按成员字节序排 [2]。所以保留原 created_at，并用定长编码的 post_id 让缓存顺序与持久索引一致。不要把大整数 post_id 硬塞到浮点 score 里。本章的持久幂等仍是 Timeline Store 唯一约束；缓存重启不会让这份约束消失。

English

POST /v1/posts carries Idempotency-Key, text and media_urls. Validation fails with 400, missing authentication with 401, throttling with 429 plus Retry-After, and unavailable commit with 503. A unique (author_id, key) stores request_hash and the original response. Same key and body returns the original post_id; a changed body returns 409. Commit the key with Post and Outbox so concurrent requests cannot create two posts. A11 retains the key for 24 h; do not promise permanent deduplication after expiry.

Post holds post_id, author_id, created_at, text, media_urls, status and version, with an author-time index. Timeline has unique (user_id, post_id) and ordered index (user_id, created_at, post_id); a sort key alone does not prevent duplicate post ids. CelebLog orders by author_id and time, with unique (author_id, post_id). Outbox uses event_id and stores author, post id, original timestamp, routing generation and published_at. Queue messages do not carry million-follower lists.

Follow stores (follower_id, followee_id); a reverse index lets Worker page an author’s followers. TAO’s original objects, associations and time-ordered association lists support this access model [1]. We borrow the query direction, not a claim that TAO implements this feed. Read-time filtering checks current followees. A changed follow-set version makes an old cursor return 409 and requires refresh. An asynchronously maintained inverse index also needs repair.

A Redis sorted set can express ordered cached members: ZADD updates an existing member’s score rather than duplicating it, and equal scores use member byte order [2]. Preserve created_at and use fixed-width encoded post_ids to match the durable index order. Do not pack a large integer post_id into a floating-point score. Durable idempotency remains the Timeline Store unique constraint and survives cache restart.

发帖：同事务提交，Relay 补发，Worker 幂等Publish: atomic commit, Relay replay and idempotent Worker

中文

按时间走完一次发布。Feed API 验证请求，在作者分区插入 Post、Outbox、Idempotency 并提交；返回 201 与帖 id。Relay 只扫描已提交且未发布的行，broker 确认接收后才标 published_at。事件被 Worker 接收，按作者模式去写 Timeline Store 或 CelebLog，持久写成功才提交消费位点。三个完成点各有指标：本地提交、入队确认、派生写完成，不能拿一张「成功」仪表盘代替。

Outbox 解决两边提交的缺口。Chris Richardson 的原始模式让业务变化与待发消息同库提交，再由独立 relay 发布；relay 在发送后、记录已发送前崩溃，会重复发布，消费者必须幂等 [6]。本章选择这种本地事务，不把 Post Store 和 broker 做成一次分布式提交。若把 Outbox 移到另一套库，就重新打开双写窗口；叫它 outbox 并不会自动得到事务性。

四个崩溃窗口要逐个说。提交前回滚，三行都没有，不该入队；提交后 201 丢失，按同 key 取原响应；broker 接收但 Relay 没标记，重发同 event_id；Worker 写完一部分粉丝还没 ack，重投同 post_id。前两处由同库事务与发布幂等处理，后两处由持久唯一键和消费检查点处理。只在 Redis 里去重，缓存一丢，永久重复就会进入 inbox。

大任务把粉丝分成有界分页块，任务记录保存 event_id、模式代际、下一分页位点和完成状态。检查点落后可以重复写，检查点领先却会漏写，所以先持久输出再前移。消费中断从位点继续，不以 published_at 当作粉丝完成标志。队列健康恢复后先消费积压；只有未确认发布的 Outbox 才补发。保留 Outbox 与任务记录直到确认可恢复，清理不能先于恢复策略。

Apache Kafka 4.1 官方设计区分了日志内事务与外部系统输出；外部输出要与消费进度协作，不会因设置一个事务参数就涵盖所有 inbox [3]。本章采用至少一次加幂等输出，避免把队列与所有粉丝存储放进 300 ms 发布关键路径。小规模仍可在同进程跑 Relay，只要重启会从持久 Outbox 继续，而不是只保留一段内存回调。

English

Walk a publish in time. Feed API validates, inserts Post, Outbox and Idempotency in the author partition, commits and returns 201 plus the id. Relay scans committed unpublished rows and marks published_at after broker acknowledgement. Worker routes the event to Timeline Store or CelebLog and commits its consumer position after durable output writes. Local commit, broker acceptance and derived-write completion are three separately monitored milestones.

Outbox closes the dual-commit gap. Chris Richardson’s original pattern stores a business change and pending message in one database transaction, then uses a separate relay. A crash after sending but before recording the send can duplicate publication, so consumers must be idempotent [6]. We choose that local transaction rather than one distributed commit spanning Post Store and the broker. Moving Outbox to a separate store reopens the dual-write window.

Name four crash windows. Before commit, rollback leaves no records and must not enqueue. A lost 201 after commit is retried with the same key. Broker acceptance without Relay marking causes same-event replay. Partial follower writes before Worker ack cause same-post redelivery. Local transactions and publish idempotency cover the first two; durable uniqueness and consumer checkpoints cover the others. Redis-only deduplication allows permanent duplicates after cache loss.

Split large jobs into bounded follower pages. Persist event_id, routing generation, next page cursor and completion state. A lagging checkpoint repeats writes; an advanced checkpoint loses them, so write outputs before advancing. Resume consumption from that checkpoint; published_at is not follower completion. After queue recovery, drain backlog and replay only publication-uncertain Outbox rows. Retain recovery records until the cleanup policy is safe.

Apache Kafka 4.1’s official design distinguishes transactions within Kafka from external output, which must cooperate with consumer progress; one transaction setting does not cover every inbox [3]. We choose at-least-once plus idempotent output to keep the broker and all follower stores out of the 300 ms publish path. Relay can share a small deployment if it resumes from durable Outbox after restart rather than relying on an in-memory callback.

两条路径合成一页：push 普通人，pull 大 VTwo paths become one page: push ordinary authors, pull celebrities

中文

Worker 拥有写，Feed API 拥有读。普通事件分页查 Follow Graph，再按粉丝 user_id 写 Timeline Store；大 V 事件不枚举粉丝，只往 CelebLog 写一行。两类事件都已经在 Post Store 提交。GET 从持久 inbox 和所有关注大 V 的日志取候选，按时间与帖 id 归并、去重，再 hydrate。读路径不会让 CelebLog 去查询 inbox，也不会让 Follow Graph 自己投递帖子。

K 约束计算方法，不约束该看的作者。A6 的 K≤8 描述轻读者，不是任意丢掉第九路。关注大 V 更多的人，由后台合并全部相关日志，产出 (user_id, follow_version, snapshot_id) 的完整指针视图，存进 Timeline Store；前台读它的有序页。重视图新鲜度目标是从帖子提交起算 5 s，包含各来源物化水位，而不只是构建时间距今 5 s；与轻读者实时归并分开监控。首次构建或不完整时返回 503、Retry-After，不返回只有前八个作者的「完整」页。

这份重读者视图换来了预计算与额外存储，也会随关注变化失效；只把缓存 TTL 调短不能补回根本没读取的作者。相反，小产品纯 pull 可以省掉 inbox 修复，只要低读量与低关注数允许每次归并。只给活跃粉丝 push 也是替代，但休眠用户回来需要完整补建，不能把缺少的历史当空 feed。任何优化都要先声明完整性与可见窗口。

普通人长成大 V 时，旧帖不会自动进 CelebLog。先选切换位点，从 Post Store 作者索引补齐保留窗口内的旧帖，再切元数据代际，让切换后的事件只写日志；读仍兼容 inbox 里的旧 id，按 post_id 去重。反向切回 push，必须保留切换前的日志范围，直到对应历史都离开读窗口。为避免粉丝数在阈值附近抖动，可高于 25k 转 pull、低于 20k 才申请转回，后台完成补齐后才生效。

作者的立即可见也有自己的成本：持有刚写成功的位点或响应里的帖 id，额外读主库作者索引，最多并入这一页所需的自己候选，再和其他来源去重。A11 最坏按 58 次写后刷新/s ×20 行=1,160 行/s，不能把它藏到「缓存 99% 命中」里。作者看到成功不证明粉丝新鲜度健康；两条链本来就可以有不同延迟。

English

Worker owns writes; Feed API owns reads. Ordinary events page Follow Graph and write Timeline Store by follower user_id. Celebrity events do not enumerate followers and append one CelebLog row. Both types already committed in Post Store. GET collects the durable inbox and all followed celebrity logs, merges by time and id, deduplicates, then hydrates. CelebLog never queries the inbox and Follow Graph never delivers posts itself.

K constrains the computation, not visible authors. A6’s K≤8 describes light readers, not permission to drop the ninth source. Heavy readers use a background merge over every relevant log, producing a complete (user_id, follow_version, snapshot_id) pointer view in Timeline Store. The foreground reads ordered pages from it. Its 5 s freshness target starts at post commit and includes source materialization watermarks; it is not merely five seconds since construction. Monitor it separately from light-reader merging. First construction or incomplete output returns 503 with Retry-After, not a supposedly complete first-eight-author page.

The heavy-reader view costs precomputation and storage and is invalidated by follow changes. Shortening a TTL cannot restore authors that were never read. A small product can use pure pull to avoid inbox repair if reads and follow counts permit merging every time. Pushing only to active followers is another option, but returning dormant users need a complete rebuild; missing history is not an empty feed. Declare completeness and retention before optimizing.

Growing into celebrity status does not populate old CelebLog rows. Choose a cutover position, backfill retained history from the Post Store author index, then publish a new metadata generation so later events use the log. Reads still accept old inbox ids and deduplicate by post_id. When reverting to push, retain pre-cutover log ranges until that history leaves retention. Hysteresis can switch to pull above 25k and request a return below 20k, taking effect only after background repair completes.

Immediate author visibility has its own cost: carry the committed position or returned post id, read the primary author index for up to the page’s needed own candidates, then deduplicate with other sources. A11’s worst case is 58 refreshes/s ×20 rows=1,160 rows/s and is not hidden in the 99% cache-hit assumption. An author seeing success does not prove follower freshness is healthy.

指针、旁路缓存与删除：旧值在哪个窗口回来Pointers, look-aside cache and delete: where old data can return

中文

一份指针不等于一份正文。Timeline 行只带用户、帖 id、作者和创建时间；正文、媒体 URL、status 和 version 只由 Post Store 拥有。缓存拆成 Timeline Cache 的 timeline:{user_id} 指针窗口，以及 Post Cache 的 post:{post_id} 正文。Feed API 查缓存，miss 自己查持久库，再条件回填；缓存不会主动串联到库。Timeline 的缓存写后失效由 Worker 的持久追加触发，不能在追加成功之前先宣称新 id 已存在。

按 A4，为最多 2,000,000 个用户窗口预留容量，每个 1,000 行，每行规划 64 B，合计 2,000,000×1,000×64=128,000,000,000 B=128 GB。若每行只复制 1 KB 正文就是 2 TB，2,000÷128≈15.6 倍，而且还没加指针字段。128 GB 是工作集容量上限，不是说 TTL 0.5 s 时每个键都持续新鲜，更不是 Redis 实测内存。副本、真实对象开销与 RSS 要另算。正文逻辑增量 1M×1 KB=1 GB/天，365 天三份约 1.095 TB，索引、日志、备份与媒体都不在这个数里。

删帖先改权威，再主动失效。DELETE /v1/posts/{id} 校验作者，Post Store 提交 status=deleted、version+1，与失效事件同事务记录，返回 200；API 随即 DEL post:{id}，Relay 重试失效事件。失效重复是幂等操作。inbox 可以保留 id，hydrate 过滤 tombstone，后台慢慢清指针。不存在同步扫描百万粉丝这条关键路径；取关则按当前 Follow 集合过滤，不能用一份已经复制的正文躲过过滤。

简单「提交后 DEL」仍有竞态：读请求先读到旧正文，删帖提交并 DEL，旧请求晚一点 SET，就让删除后的正文又出现。Nishtala 等人的原文使用 demand-filled look-aside；其 lease 会在 delete 时失效，阻止 stale set [4]。本章采用同类的代际 token：miss 获得 token，回填原子检查仍有效；token 已撤销就丢弃旧结果，重新读权威 tombstone。标准 Redis 的 ZADD 不提供这套 lease API，需另外实现原子代际检查。

A9 还覆盖失效消息丢失：旧读只能在回源开始后 2 s 内安装值，安装后最多留固定 25 s，因此从删帖提交算最坏约 2+25=27 s。命中不刷新 TTL，不读落后副本，不在过期后继续服务旧正文，才接近 30 s 目标。Timeline 的同类窗口按 0.5 s 回源截止+0.5 s TTL≤1 s，纳入 A5 的新鲜度预算。singleflight 可以降低负载，却不能自动提供 token 失效；缓存全挂时更无法向它讨一个 lease。

English

A pointer is not a body copy. Timeline holds reader, post id, author and time. Post Store owns text, media URLs, status and version. Split caching into Timeline Cache’s timeline:{user_id} pointer window and Post Cache’s post:{post_id} body. Feed API queries cache, fetches the durable store itself on miss and conditionally refills. Worker invalidates the timeline cache after durable append, never before the id exists.

Under A4, reserve up to 2,000,000 user windows of 1,000 rows at the planning 64 B/row: 128,000,000,000 B=128 GB. Copying only a 1 KB body into every row makes 2 TB, about 15.6× larger even before pointer fields. 128 GB is a working-set capacity bound, not a claim all keys stay fresh at a 0.5 s TTL or measured Redis memory. Count replicas, real object overhead and RSS separately. Bodies grow logically by 1 GB/day; 365 days and three copies give about 1.095 TB, excluding indexes, logs, backups and media.

Delete authority before invalidating. DELETE /v1/posts/{id} verifies the author, commits status=deleted and version+1 with an invalidation event, then returns 200. API promptly deletes post:{id}; Relay retries the invalidation event. Repeated invalidation is idempotent. Inbox ids can remain while hydrate filters tombstones and background cleanup removes pointers. There is no synchronous million-follower scan. Unfollow filtering uses the current Follow set and cannot be bypassed by a copied body.

A simple post-commit DEL still races: a read obtains an old body, delete commits and invalidates, then the old request SETs the body back. Nishtala et al. describe demand-filled look-aside and leases invalidated by delete to prevent stale sets [4]. We use a generation token: miss obtains it; refill atomically checks validity. A revoked token discards the old result and rereads the authoritative tombstone. Standard Redis ZADD does not provide that lease API; atomic generation checks need separate implementation.

A9 also covers lost invalidation: an old read may install only within 2 s of origin start, then retains the value for a fixed 25 s, giving about 27 s from delete commit in the worst case. Hits do not refresh TTL, origins do not read lagging replicas, and expired bodies are not served. The analogous timeline window is ≤1 s from 0.5 s origin deadline plus 0.5 s TTL and belongs in A5 freshness. Singleflight reduces load but does not invalidate tokens, and a dead cache cannot issue leases.

一页怎么切：全序、晚扇出和补页预算Cutting a page: total order, late fan-out and refill budgets

中文

先限定每个来源，再归并。GET /v1/feed?limit=20&cursor= 解出上一次实际消费到的 (created_at, post_id)，每个 inbox 与作者日志只取严格更旧的行，再用同一个排序比较器做 k 路归并并去重。cursor 是服务端签名的不透明串，绑定 user_id、follow_version 与边界；重读者还绑定 snapshot_id。它不是授权凭证，换用户不能复用，关注版本变化返回 409 让客户端刷新。

只比较 created_at 不够。同毫秒有 (1000,0042) 与 (1000,0041)，读完前者，下一页 tuple<(1000,0042) 仍应含后者。PostgreSQL 的行比较按字段从左到右确定顺序 [7]；Post Store 与 Timeline 的索引必须符合这条全序。sorted set 同 score 是字节序 [2]，所以帖 id 必须定长编码，不能让字符串 9 排在 10 后面又与数据库数值顺序不同。

不用 OFFSET，但也不神化 cursor。PostgreSQL 18 文档要求 LIMIT 的次序可预测，并说明 OFFSET 跳过的行仍要在服务端计算 [5]。新帖插入会移动页码，seek 的排他边界避免这种位移。然而一个 created_at 较新的帖因扇出晚到，插进已经越过的上界之上，本轮深翻仍看不到。刷新首页会见到它；如果要求滚动时历史绝不漏，就要固定快照或按分区保存物化水位，单个时间 cursor 不够。

归并候选后批量 hydrate，并检查 deleted 与当前关注关系。如果 A7 的 20 条候选里删了 3 条，不能无条件回 17 条还宣称没有更多；继续消费更旧的候选，最多扫描 5×limit，默认 100 条、limit=50 时最多 250 条。消耗预算后仍不满，响应包含 partial=true 和 continuation；只有各来源确实耗尽时 next_cursor 才为空。源超时则整页 503，不能把未读完当空。

cursor 指实际处理到的最后一个候选，可能是一条已删除帖；否则一整段删除会让下一页重复扫描。预取 100 条、在第 23 条已经凑够 20 条有效正文，就只把 cursor 推到第 23 条，不能直接推到第 100 条。未消费的预取候选要保留或下次重读。源内翻页位点、post_id 去重和 5×limit 的预算共同限制 CPU 与回源。inbox 的 1,000 条保留窗以外要声明历史结束或走归档，不悄悄切成逐个关注者大扫描。

English

Bound each source before merging. GET /v1/feed?limit=20&cursor= decodes the last actually consumed (created_at, post_id). Every inbox and author log reads strictly older rows, then the same comparator merges and deduplicates them. The cursor is a server-signed opaque value bound to user_id, follow_version and the bound; heavy readers also bind snapshot_id. It is not authorization, cannot be reused by another user, and a changed follow version returns 409 for refresh.

created_at alone is insufficient. With (1000,0042) and (1000,0041) in one millisecond, after consuming the former, tuple<(1000,0042) must still admit the latter. PostgreSQL row comparisons decide order field by field [7], and durable indexes must match it. Sorted-set equal scores use byte order [2], so encode ids at fixed width rather than letting strings 9 and 10 disagree with numeric order.

Avoid OFFSET without mythologizing cursors. PostgreSQL 18 requires predictable ordering with LIMIT and notes that OFFSET’s skipped rows still need server computation [5]. Inserts move page positions; an exclusive seek bound avoids that drift. Yet a newer-created post arriving late above a passed bound is still missing from this scroll session and appears on refresh. Guaranteed historical completeness needs a fixed snapshot or materialization watermarks per partition, not merely a time cursor.

After merge, batch hydrate and check deleted status and current follows. If three of twenty candidates are deleted, do not return seventeen and claim exhaustion. Consume older candidates up to 5×limit: 100 by default or 250 at limit=50. If budget is exhausted before a full page, return partial=true and continuation. next_cursor is null only after every source is actually exhausted. A source timeout makes the page 503, not empty.

The cursor names the last candidate actually processed, possibly a deleted post; otherwise a deleted run is scanned again. If 100 ids are prefetched but candidate 23 completes twenty valid bodies, advance only to candidate 23, not 100. Retain or reread unconsumed prefetched ids. Per-source positions, post_id deduplication and the 5×limit budget bound CPU and origin work. Beyond the retained 1,000-row inbox window, declare history exhaustion or use an archive rather than silently scanning every followee.

故障放回路径：滞后、惊群、缓存失效与回源封顶Failures on the paths: lag, herds, cache loss and bounded origin

中文

队列积压先损害新鲜度。帖子已经提交，201 仍成功，粉丝可以继续读旧 inbox。监控未发布 Outbox 年龄、已发布任务的消费 lag、最老未完成分页任务和实际刷新可见延迟；只看 broker 接收或 API 201 都会报绿。Relay 恢复处理未发布行，Worker 恢复消费检查点。若 backlog 有 60,000 行、背景峰值仍是 11,574 行/s，总能力 30,000 的净排空速度约 18,426 行/s，理想下限约 3.3 s；热点片、重试与读争用会让实际更慢。

热帖 miss 与整层缓存失效是两件事。单个 post:{id} 同时未命中，API 进程内 singleflight 把等待者合并；缓存仍健康时可用跨进程 lease 避免重复回填。Memcache 原文在一组易惊群键上观察到数据库峰值从 17K/s 降到 1.3K/s，默认每键 10 s 才发一枚 token [4]，这是 2013 年的机制与测量，不是本章的 25 s TTL、容量或预期收益。热作者日志可以多副本缓存，普通 reader key 的偏斜也要监控，不能说 hybrid 把所有热点消灭了。

A10 按正常一页 20 条、Post Cache 目标 99% 命中算正文点查：11,574×20×0.01≈2,315 次/s，再加 A11 最坏写后读 1,160，合计约 3,475，低于 4,000 预算。删帖密集的补页和首次重视图不属于这个正常页画像，必须共享全局 token 限额。命中率掉到 95%，仅普通页就要约 11,574 次正文点查/s，预算不成立，需要限流、改善工作集或扩容；不能继续背 80 ms。

整层缓存不可用时，11,574 页/s 最坏想读 231,480 条正文/s，是 4,000 的约 57.9 倍。图里的 Origin budget 是 API 侧准入，不是新业务权威：给默认 20 条的完整页最多 200 页/s，并同时限制点查 token、并发和截止时间。limit=50 就最多 4,000÷50=80 页/s，不能照搬 200。Timeline 的回源也有独立预算，最终准入取两份存储预算较小者；图上的 200 是 Timeline 尚可承担时的上界。

仅缓存挂、持久库还健康时，超过预算整页 503；不要返回删掉部分帖的伪完整页，也不在过期后续命旧正文违反删除目标。若正文库也挂，红回程 Post Store → Origin budget → Feed API → Client，连准入的请求也失败。按 A1、A2 请求口径，日错误预算 200M×0.001=200,000；固定峰值下仅缓存故障至少约 11,574−200=11,374 页/s 失败，预算约 17.6 s 花完。限流能保护数据库，不能单独保证 99.9%；还需要缓存故障域、副本和足够快的恢复。

English

Queue backlog first harms freshness. The post committed, the 201 remains successful and followers can read older inboxes. Monitor unpublished Outbox age, consumer lag for published jobs, oldest unfinished page job and actual refresh visibility. Broker acceptance or API 201 alone stays green. Relay recovers unpublished rows; Worker resumes checkpoints. A 60,000-row backlog with ongoing peak load 11,574/s and capacity 30,000/s drains ideally at 18,426/s, about 3.3 s; shard skew, retries and read contention make reality slower.

A hot-key miss differs from loss of the whole cache tier. In-process singleflight coalesces waiters on post:{id}; while cache is healthy, a cross-process lease can prevent duplicate fills. The Memcache paper observed peak queries fall from 17K/s to 1.3K/s on herd-prone keys and configured one token per key per 10 s by default [4]. Those are 2013 mechanisms and measurements, not this chapter’s 25 s TTL, capacity or expected gain. Replicated hot author tails help reads, but reader-key skew still needs monitoring.

For normal twenty-body pages, A10’s 99% Post Cache hit target gives 11,574×20×0.01≈2,315 point reads/s. Add A11’s worst-case 1,160 author reads for about 3,475, below the 4,000 budget. Delete-heavy refill and first heavy-view construction are outside the normal-page profile and share a global token limit. At 95% hits, ordinary pages alone require about 11,574 body reads/s, invalidating the budget. Limit load, improve the working set or add capacity rather than repeating 80 ms.

With the cache tier unavailable, 11,574 pages/s may demand 231,480 bodies/s, about 57.9× the 4,000 budget. Origin budget is API admission control, not another authority. Admit at most 200 complete default twenty-body pages/s, also bounding point-read tokens, concurrency and deadlines. At limit=50, the cap becomes 80 pages/s, not 200. Timeline origin has its own budget; admission takes the smaller of the two. The diagram’s 200 is an upper bound while Timeline remains capable.

With only cache loss and healthy stores, reject over-budget pages with 503. Do not disguise missing bodies as a complete page or extend expired bodies beyond the delete target. If the body store also fails, the error returns Post Store → Origin budget → Feed API → Client, and even admitted requests fail. A1/A2 give a daily request budget of 200M×0.001=200,000. At a fixed peak, cache loss alone fails at least about 11,374 pages/s, exhausting it in 17.6 s. Limits protect the database, but 99.9% also requires cache failure domains, replicas and fast recovery.

“Why not write every follower?” Show 1M÷10k=100 s against 5 s. More consumers add capacity without removing F writes and still need validation of author scans and inbox-shard skew. Move the threshold only when per-job capacity and storage budgets actually rise.

“What about old posts from a new followee?” This public feed allows inbox materialization from follow time. The first page may fetch a bounded, explicitly scoped author history or use background rebuilding of the complete visible window. Immediate access to all history needs rebuild cost and follow_version cutover; inserting a Follow row does not complete the inbox.

“How is storage recovered?” Post Store owns bodies; Timeline Store holds reconstructible but operationally required durable pointers. Cache eviction is acceptable and is not a backup strategy. Replication policy determines the acknowledged-write loss window. If a 201 must survive primary loss, configure the required replica acknowledgements and include the wait in 300 ms. Omitted replication topology does not omit the contract.

English

The follow feed is chronological. 1M posts/day×200 followers offers about 2,315 rows/s, yet a million-follower post takes 100 s, so ordinary authors are pushed and celebrities pulled. The 25k threshold uses a 10k rows/s per-job budget. A 201 waits for atomic Post, Outbox and idempotency commit; Relay replay and durable unique keys close crash windows.

GET merges the complete source set, Timeline stores pointers, and API uses look-aside bodies. Delete revokes refill tokens and bounds old data by 2+25=27 s. The cursor is the last consumed total-order bound, not a guarantee against late fan-out omissions. Outage admission of at most 200 pages/s protects stores while daily error budget can disappear in 17.6 s. The remaining trade-offs are freshness, storage amplification and recovery cost, each tied to a diagram and assumption.
