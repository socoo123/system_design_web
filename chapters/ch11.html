设计新闻 FeedDesign a news feed

主线约 30 分钟 · 前置 Ch02 / Ch07 · 发布、混合扇出、有界读页~30 min core · after Ch02 / Ch07 · publish, hybrid fan-out, bounded reads

本章核心：三个完成点，两个候选来源Core: three completion points, two candidate sources

201 等持久提交，粉丝可见等异步扇出，200 等完整来源归并与正文过滤。Post Store 是正文权威；普通作者 push 指针到读者 inbox，大 V pull 共享作者日志；排序只用 (created_at, post_id)，本章不做推荐。

抓住三件事：用容量推导 push 阈值；用本地事务和持久唯一键封住重试窗口；用全序 cursor 限制读页工作，同时承认迟到扇出可能要刷新才能看到。

A 201 waits for durable commit, follower visibility for asynchronous fan-out, and a 200 for merging every required source and filtering bodies. Post Store owns bodies. Ordinary authors push pointers into reader inboxes; celebrities use shared author logs pulled at read time. Order is (created_at, post_id); recommendation is outside scope.

Focus on three decisions: derive the push threshold from capacity; close retry windows with a local transaction and durable uniqueness; bound page work with a total-order cursor while allowing late fan-out to require refresh.

需求与规模：先把承诺分开Requirements and scale: separate the promises

教学假设：10M DAU，每人每天发 0.1 帖、打开 20 次，峰值为平均的 5 倍。发帖 p99 300 ms，普通缓存命中页服务端 p99 80 ms，发布/读取可用性目标 99.9%。粉丝候选新鲜度目标 5 s；普通删帖在服务端约 30 s 生效。媒体字节走对象存储与 CDN，feed 只保留 URL。

成功发布不等于所有粉丝已见；200 表示所选来源都成功读取，不表示尚未物化的历史已经齐全。作者读己之写可把 201 中的 post_id 合入首页，并以作者主库索引补读。私人内容或立即撤权要求另外走权威权限/状态检查，不能沿用普通正文缓存的宽限。

Teaching assumptions: 10M DAU, 0.1 posts and 20 opens per user per day, peak at 5× average. Target publish p99 is 300 ms, ordinary cache-hit page server p99 is 80 ms, and publish/read availability is 99.9%. Follower candidate freshness targets 5 s; ordinary deletion takes effect at the server within roughly 30 s. Media bytes use object storage and CDN; the feed stores URLs.

Successful publication does not mean every follower has seen it. A 200 means every selected source was read successfully, not that unmaterialized history is complete. Authors merge the 201 post_id into their first page and may read the primary author index. Private content or immediate revocation requires authoritative permission/status checks rather than the ordinary body-cache grace period.

容量决定 hybrid，不背一个粉丝常数Capacity determines hybrid, not a memorized follower count

平均输入与处理能力分开。每天 1M 帖，平均 11.57/s、峰值 57.87/s。只有假设按发帖加权的平均粉丝数为 200，纯 push 才是 200M 指针/天，平均 2,314.81 行/s、峰值 11,574.07 行/s；用户均值不能直接替代它。每天 200M 次打开的读峰值也为 11,574.07/s，数值相等只是本题参数巧合。

再看单帖尖峰。假设单任务可持续写 10k 行/s、整层 30k 行/s，固定处理 0.5 s。百万粉一帖需 100.5 s；即使独占整层也需约 33.8 s。普通作者阈值取 25k：0.5+25k÷10k=3 s，给 Relay/排队留 1 s、timeline 缓存陈旧留 1 s，凑成 5 s 的阶段截止预算。不是把各段 p99 相加来证明整链 p99。

普通扇出峰值占整层 38.58%，理想净余量 18,425.93 行/s；60k 积压至少需 3.26 s 排空。每片倾斜、后台补建、重试和失去一副本后的能力都要另外验证，平均余量不能保证一个热点片。

读侧代价：纯 pull 每次探测 200 个作者，平均约 462,963 次作者探测/s。hybrid 用一次 inbox 查询替代普通作者探测，轻读者再读全部 K 路大 V 日志。K≤8 时平均上界约 18,519 路/s、峰值 92,593 路/s，仍需共享日志缓存。批量降低往返，不能把多行存储工作算成一行。

Separate offered work from capacity. 1M posts/day gives 11.57/s average and 57.87/s peak. Pure push produces 200M pointers/day, or 2,314.81 rows/s average and 11,574.07 peak, only if the post-weighted mean follower count is 200. A user-weighted mean is insufficient. The 200M daily opens happen to have the same peak; that equality is a parameter coincidence.

Then size one burst. Assume 10k rows/s per job, 30k/s aggregate and 0.5 s fixed processing. A million-follower post takes 100.5 s, or roughly 33.8 s even monopolizing the entire tier. Set ordinary push at 25k: 0.5+25k÷10k=3 s, plus 1 s for Relay/queueing and 1 s of timeline-cache staleness. These are stage deadlines for a 5 s target, not a sum of component p99s proving a whole-chain p99.

Peak ordinary fan-out uses 38.58% of aggregate capacity, leaving an ideal 18,425.93 rows/s. Draining 60k queued rows takes at least 3.26 s. Validate shard skew, background rebuilds, retries and replica-loss capacity separately; aggregate slack does not protect a hot shard.

Read cost: pure pull probes 200 authors per request, roughly 462,963 author probes/s on average. Hybrid replaces ordinary-author probes with one inbox read, then reads all K celebrity logs for a light reader. K≤8 gives at most about 18,519 tail reads/s average and 92,593 peak, still requiring shared log caching. Batching saves round trips, not storage work.

全局路径：正文权威，inbox 是派生指针The whole path: authoritative bodies, derived inbox pointers

沿上半图发布，沿下半图读页。同一作者分片的 Post、PublishKey、Outbox 一起提交；Relay 发布已提交事件，Worker 跨读者分片写指针。Feed API 查自己的 inbox、补正文并回到请求的 Client；第 3 组展开大 V 支路，第 4 组展开旁路缓存。

Publish along the top; read along the bottom. Post, PublishKey and Outbox commit in one author shard. Relay publishes committed events and Worker materializes pointers across reader shards. Feed API reads its inbox, hydrates bodies and responds to the requesting Client. Deck 3 expands celebrity routing; deck 4 expands look-aside caching.

Post Store 按作者或 post_id 路由信息定位；Timeline Store 按读者 user_id 路由。无状态 API、鉴权、负载均衡和存储复制拓扑在这张逻辑图省略。要承诺 201 在故障后不丢，实际部署仍须满足相应副本持久确认，并把等待算入发布预算。

Route Post Store by author or routing information in post_id, and Timeline Store by reader user_id. This logical diagram omits API replicas, authentication, load balancing and storage replication. A promise that acknowledged posts survive failures still requires the corresponding durable replica acknowledgements within the publish budget.

数据与 API：唯一键、排序键和权限各做一件事Data and APIs: uniqueness, order and permission have distinct jobs

POST /v1/posts 接受 Idempotency-Key；同作者同 key 同正文回放原 201，不同正文 409。语法错误 400、未登录 401、无权限 403、限流 429；提交结果未知或依赖不可用 503，客户端保持同 key 重试。幂等回执至少保留 24 h；本章不承诺清理后仍永久去重。

Post 保存 post_id、author_id、服务端 created_at、正文、媒体 URL、status、version；创建时间是展示排序键，不能当跨分片提交全序。Outbox 保存 event_id、post_id、原时间和模式代际。Timeline 只存指针：唯一 (user_id,post_id) 防重复，另一有序索引支持 seek。CelebLog 对 (author_id,post_id) 唯一。下列 SQL 给出真实约束位置，不把不同分片伪装成一个事务。PostgreSQL INSERT [8]

POST /v1/posts accepts Idempotency-Key. The same author/key/body replays the original 201; a changed body returns 409. Use 400 for invalid syntax, 401 for no authentication, 403 for denial and 429 for throttling. Unknown commit results or unavailable dependencies return 503; retry with the same key. Retain receipts for at least 24 h; no permanent deduplication is promised after cleanup.

Post stores post_id, author_id, server-created time, body, media URLs, status and version. Time is a display-order key, not a cross-shard commit order. Outbox stores event_id, post_id, original time and routing generation. Timeline stores pointers: unique (user_id,post_id) prevents duplication, while a separate ordered index supports seek. CelebLog is unique by (author_id,post_id). The SQL locates real constraints without pretending different shards share a transaction. PostgreSQL INSERT [8]

-- PostgreSQL 18; the author shard owns Post, Outbox and PublishKey.
CREATE TABLE publish_key (
  author_id bigint NOT NULL, idem_key text NOT NULL,
  request_hash bytea NOT NULL, post_id bigint NOT NULL,
  response_json jsonb NOT NULL, retain_until timestamptz NOT NULL,
  PRIMARY KEY (author_id, idem_key)
);
-- Post has unique post_id; Outbox has unique event_id.
-- They are inserted with publish_key in ONE author-shard transaction.

-- A different store, partitioned by reader; not in that transaction.
CREATE TABLE timeline (
  user_id bigint NOT NULL, post_id bigint NOT NULL,
  author_id bigint NOT NULL, created_at_ms bigint NOT NULL,
  PRIMARY KEY (user_id, post_id)
);
CREATE INDEX timeline_seek
  ON timeline (user_id, created_at_ms DESC, post_id DESC);
-- Bound parameters; same exclusive tuple bound on every source:
SELECT * FROM timeline
 WHERE user_id = :viewer
   AND (created_at_ms, post_id) < (:before_ms, :before_id)
 ORDER BY created_at_ms DESC, post_id DESC LIMIT :batch;

Follow 的正向集合用于读过滤，反向作者索引用于 Worker 分页找粉丝；TAO 原文支持这种对象/关联与有序列表访问模型，不能据此声称 TAO 就是本题 feed。TAO §3 [1] 权限以读时的当前关注/拉黑关系为准，cursor 不是授权凭证；若权限服务不可用则失败关闭，不从旧 inbox 推断仍获授权。

Forward Follow sets support read filtering; reverse author indexes let Worker page followers. TAO supports this objects/associations and ordered-list access model, not a claim that TAO is this feed implementation. TAO §3 [1] Permission follows the current follow/block state at read time; a cursor is not authorization. Fail closed when permission service is unavailable rather than inferring access from an old inbox.

发布：本地原子提交，异步至少一次Publish: atomic local commit, asynchronous at-least-once delivery

三个记录同事务，队列不在事务里。发布只等第 2 帧的 commit；第 3–4 帧 Relay 的 broker ack 只表示入队。发送后标记前崩溃必然可能重复，所以 event_id 不变、消费者幂等。Transactional outbox [6]

Three records share one transaction; the broker is outside it. Publish waits for the commit in frame 2. Relay’s broker ack in frames 3–4 means enqueued only. A crash after sending but before marking can duplicate publication, so preserve event_id and make consumers idempotent. Transactional outbox [6]

publish(authenticated_author, key, request):
  author = authenticated_author
  hash = digest(canonicalizeAndValidate(request))
  prior = primary.readPublishKey(authenticated_author, key)
  if prior exists: return replayOr409(prior, hash)
  post_id, event_id = allocateUniqueIds()
  response = {status: 201, post_id: post_id}
  try:
    BEGIN on the author's PostgreSQL shard
    INSERT PublishKey(author, key, hash, post_id, response, now + 24h)
    mode = SELECT AuthorMode FOR SHARE  // serialized with cutover
    INSERT Post(post_id, author, server_created_at, request, live, v=1)
    INSERT Outbox(event_id, post_id, server_created_at, mode.generation)
    COMMIT
    return response
  catch unique_conflict on PublishKey(author, key):
    ROLLBACK
    prior = primary.readPublishKey(author, key)  // NEW statement
    if prior absent: return 503  // no invented success
    return replayOr409(prior, hash)
  catch commit_result_unknown:
    discardConnection(); return 503  // retry the SAME key
  catch other_database_failure:
    ROLLBACK if possible; return 503

relay(event):
  broker.publish(event)  // same event_id on every retry
  await broker.durableAck()
  primary.markPublished(event.event_id)

数据库唯一键仲裁并发，不靠 API 内存 Set。冲突后先 ROLLBACK，再以 READ COMMITTED 的新语句从主库读胜者并比 request_hash；不可在已失败事务中继续查，也不可把一次不存在当成失败查询。COMMIT 回执丢失表示结果未知：丢弃连接、503、同 key 重试恢复原帖。校验与摘要采用稳定的规范化规则；限流/鉴权在外层执行。

The database unique key arbitrates concurrent requests; an API-local Set cannot. After conflict, ROLLBACK and use a new READ COMMITTED statement on the primary to read the winner and compare request_hash. Do not query inside an aborted transaction or confuse an empty result with a failed query. A lost COMMIT acknowledgement means unknown outcome: discard the connection, return 503, and retry the same key. Use stable canonicalization for validation/digest; outer adapters handle authentication and admission.

Worker：先持久输出，再前移检查点Worker: durable output before advancing the checkpoint

consume(event):
  job = tasks.insertOrLoadUnique(event.event_id, pinned_mode,
                                retained_audience_snapshot, cursor=START)
  while not job.done:
    old = job.cursor
    if job.mode == PULL:
      CelebLog.INSERT(event.author, event.post_id, event.created_at)
        ON CONFLICT (author_id, post_id) DO NOTHING
      page = END
    else:
      page = FollowGraph.page(job.snapshot, old, max=500)
      for reader in page.readers:
        TimelineStore.INSERT(reader, event.post_id, event.author, event.created_at)
          ON CONFLICT (user_id, post_id) DO NOTHING
        // Preserve original time; a replay never moves a post upward.
        TimelineCache.invalidateBestEffort(reader)
    // All outputs above have DURABLE acknowledgements; never reverse.
    tasks.compareAndSetCursor(event.event_id, old, page.nextOrEND)
    job = tasks.reload(event.event_id)
  broker.ack(event)  // published_at alone does not mean job.done

task 表以 event_id 唯一，保存固定模式、可恢复的粉丝快照引用、游标和 done。Graph 页必须来自稳定且可保留到任务完成的同一快照；快照失效要修复/重建，不能静默换成任意当前分页。若反向索引异步维护，还需变更日志和对账补漏。Timeline/CelebLog 的唯一键在各自持久库实现；任务库的 CAS 仅允许预期旧游标前移。二者不跨库原子：输出后崩溃会重做，先写检查点则会永久漏粉丝。

并发重复任务可读同一页，但相同 post_id 写不新增、原时间不变，只有一个 CAS 成功；大 V 分支只写一条作者日志。任务和去重记录的清理要覆盖事件重放窗口，过期事件不得重新插入读窗口外的旧指针。Kafka 事务不会自动涵盖外部 inbox；这里明确采用至少一次加持久幂等。Kafka 4.1 [3]

A task is unique by event_id and retains pinned mode, a recoverable audience-snapshot reference, cursor and done state. Graph pages must use one stable snapshot retained until task completion. Expiry requires repair/rebuild, not silently switching pagination to an arbitrary current graph. An asynchronous reverse index also needs a change log and reconciliation. Each durable output store enforces its own unique key; the task store CAS advances only the expected old cursor. No cross-store atomicity is assumed: a crash after output repeats work, whereas checkpoint-first ordering permanently loses followers.

Duplicate runners may read the same page; repeated post_id inserts add no rows and preserve time, while only one CAS wins. Celebrity routing appends one author-log row. Retain task/dedup state through the replay window and reject expired events outside the readable retention window. Kafka transactions do not automatically cover external inboxes; this design explicitly uses at-least-once plus durable idempotency. Kafka 4.1 [3]

Hybrid：全量来源，而不是只读前八个大 VHybrid: every source, not just the first eight celebrities

普通人写 F 个 inbox，大 V 只写一条 CelebLog。读者归并 inbox 与关注的全部大 V 日志，再补正文。K≤8 是轻读者画像；它决定实时归并预算，不是丢掉第九个作者的许可。

Ordinary posts write F inboxes; celebrity posts append one CelebLog row. Readers merge their inbox and every followed celebrity log before hydration. K≤8 describes light readers and their online merge budget; it never permits omitting author nine.

重读者：后台从全部来源构建不可变 (user_id,follow_version,snapshot_id) 视图，前台读它的有序页。先校验每个来源的物化水位，才能报告从帖子提交起的 5 s 新鲜度；“刚构建完”本身不证明上游已到齐。首建/缺源返回 503 与 Retry-After。cursor 固定引用同一快照，快照原地更新被禁止；过期返回 410 让客户端刷新，不能偷偷换到新快照续翻。

模式切换：author 分片的模式行与发布读锁协调。push→pull 先从作者索引补齐保留窗口，在模式行排他锁下固定最后旧代际提交位点并切新代际；后台补追到这个最终位点才激活新读模式，期间缺源返回 503。旧代际事件仍按旧模式完成。读保留旧 inbox 与新日志、按 post_id 去重。pull→push 必须保留旧日志范围直到读窗口淘汰，并补建新 inbox；阈值可用 25k/20k 滞回，不能只更新粉丝数开关。

关注变化：本章让新关注获得保留窗口内历史；先登记 pending 关注边，供后续新事件的权威反向扫描纳入扇出，再把普通作者历史补入 inbox；追到登记之后选定的作者提交位点才激活新的 follow_version，期间返回构建中。大 V 分支也限定同一保留窗口。取关/拉黑在读时按当前权威关系拒绝；版本变化使旧 cursor 返回 409。这样不需要拿跨库物理时间冒充精确的“关注之后”。

Heavy readers: build an immutable (user_id,follow_version,snapshot_id) view from all sources. Check every source materialization watermark before reporting 5 s freshness from post commit; a recent build timestamp alone proves no upstream completeness. Initial/incomplete builds return 503 with Retry-After. A cursor stays on its fixed snapshot; never mutate that snapshot. Expiry returns 410 and requires refresh rather than silently continuing on a new snapshot.

Mode changes: coordinate the author-shard mode row with publish read locks. For push→pull, backfill retained author history, then fix the final old-generation commit position and switch generation under the mode-row exclusive lock. Activate the new read mode only after catchup through that final position; missing sources return 503 during transition. Old-generation events finish in their pinned mode. Read both old inboxes and new logs, deduplicating post_id. For pull→push, retain the old log range until it ages out and bootstrap new inboxes. A 25k/20k hysteresis can prevent flapping; merely flipping a follower-count flag cannot.

Follow changes: new follows receive history within the retained window. Register a pending edge first so authoritative reverse scans for subsequent events include it in fan-out. Then backfill ordinary-author history and catch up through an author commit position chosen after registration before activating the new follow_version; report building until ready. Celebrity reads use the same retention boundary. Read-time authority denies unfollowed/blocked authors; version changes make old cursors return 409. This avoids pretending cross-store wall-clock timestamps precisely order a follow against a post.

缓存与删除：指针不授予权限，TTL 不续命Cache and delete: pointers grant no permission; TTL never slides

一份共享正文，许多轻指针。2M 用户×1,000 指针×64 B=128 GB 工作集；若每行复制 1 KB 正文则为 2 TB，约 15.6 倍。正文逻辑增量 1M×1 KB=1 GB/天，365 天三份约 1.095 TB。这里用十进制单位，索引、对象开销、副本内存和媒体另算。

Feed API 旁路查 Timeline Cache 与 Post Cache；miss 由 API 回源，数据库不主动推到手机。普通删除在权威库写 tombstone+失效 outbox，再撤销缓存 token，无须同步扫所有 inbox；hydrate 看到 tombstone 就过滤。

One shared body, many small pointers. 2M users×1,000 pointers×64 B gives a 128 GB working set. Copying a 1 KB body into each row costs 2 TB, about 15.6× more. Bodies add 1M×1 KB=1 GB/day, or roughly 1.095 TB for 365 days and three copies. Units are decimal; indexes, object overhead, replica RAM and media are extra.

Feed API uses Timeline Cache and Post Cache as look-aside branches; it owns origin reads on misses. Ordinary deletion commits a tombstone and invalidation outbox, then revokes the cache token without scanning every inbox. Hydration filters tombstones.

谁保证 installIfCurrent？缓存节点以单次原子操作校验 token 的 epoch/代际及服务端安装截止，失效操作同样原子撤销代际并删除。重启或元数据淘汰后旧 token 不能复用。延迟 SET 即使已在网络排队，也必须在执行时拒绝过期 token；API 本地超时不等于远端 SET 被取消。标准 Redis ZADD 没有这套 lease API，要另实现。singleflight 只合并负载。Memcache §3.2.1 [4]

丢失失效消息时才靠硬年龄兜底。只用主库新语句回填，不复用删除前的长事务快照；旧读最迟在 grant 后 2 s 安装，安装后固定 TTL 25 s；正文在缓存中最坏驻留到删帖后约 27 s。再要求 API 从取值到响应发出≤2 s，总计约 29 s，才落在约 30 s 服务端目标内，不包含客户端已显示内容。命中不刷新 TTL、过期不服务旧值。Timeline 同理用 0.5+0.5≤1 s 预算；缓存全挂则绕过 lease，但必须经回源准入。

共享 post:{id} 只能缓存正文和状态，不能缓存“这个用户已获授权”。每次返回仍检查当前关注/拉黑；立即撤回或私密内容走权威检查并计容量，不能拿 29 s 普通删帖承诺当权限边界。已发送/下载的内容也不会因 DEL 被追回。

Who enforces installIfCurrent? The cache node atomically checks token epoch/generation and the server-side install deadline; invalidation atomically revokes generation and deletes the value. Never reuse old tokens after restart or metadata eviction. A SET delayed in the network must still fail its deadline at execution; an API timeout does not cancel a remote SET. Standard Redis ZADD has no such lease API; implement it separately. Singleflight only coalesces load. Memcache §3.2.1 [4]

Hard age bounds cover lost invalidation. Fill with a new primary statement, never a long transaction snapshot from before deletion. An old read installs within 2 s of its grant and stays for a fixed 25 s, bounding cache residence to roughly 27 s after deletion. Require no more than 2 s from value retrieval to sending the API response for roughly 29 s total, fitting the approximate 30 s server target. This excludes content already displayed by clients. Hits never renew TTL; never serve expired values. Timeline similarly budgets 0.5+0.5≤1 s. A failed cache permits a lease-free origin bypass only through admission control.

A shared post:{id} may cache body/state, never “this viewer is authorized.” Check current follow/block state on every response. Immediate revocation or private content requires authoritative checks with an explicit capacity cost; the ordinary 29 s delete bound is no permission boundary. DEL cannot reclaim content already sent or downloaded.

读取实现：全序归并，有预算地补页Read implementation: total-order merge with bounded refill

每个来源在同一个排他上界下读。(1000,0042) 后仍应返回 (1000,0041)，所以索引与比较器必须同时比较时间和唯一 id。Redis 相同 score 按成员字节序，id 要定长编码且不能把大整数塞进 double score；支持 tie 的 tuple seek 才能与库一致。ZADD [2]、行比较 [7]

Read each source below the same exclusive bound. After (1000,0042), (1000,0041) must remain eligible. Index and comparator therefore use time and unique id. Redis breaks equal-score ties by member byte order: encode ids at fixed width and never squeeze large integer ids into a double score. Tuple-aware seek must match the database ordering. ZADD [2], row comparison [7]

下面是可运行 Java 17 兼容内核，对应 Merge→Hydrate。Source 是按同一 cursor 与保留窗口打开的惰性有序流，空值只表示确认耗尽；少任一来源或查询失败都抛错并映射 503。View.requireReady 由外围校验签名、user_id、follow_version、snapshot_id/期限和完整来源集合；末尾重新校验当前权限版本，变化返回 409。它不把签名误当快照或权限。Bodies.load 是有硬截止的批量适配接口，此处逐条表达过滤顺序。

This runnable Java 17-compatible core implements Merge→Hydrate. Each Source is a lazy ordered stream opened under the same cursor and retention bound; null means confirmed exhaustion. A missing or failed source throws and maps to 503. The outer View.requireReady adapter validates cursor signature, user_id, follow_version, snapshot_id/expiry and the complete source set. The final check rereads the current permission version; a change returns 409. Signing creates neither a snapshot nor authorization. Bodies.load is an adapter for bounded batch reads; individual calls expose filter order here.

  public static Page readPage(int limit, View view, List<Source> sources,
                              Bodies bodies) {
    if (limit < 1 || limit > 50) throw new IllegalArgumentException("limit");
    view.requireReady();
    var heap = new PriorityQueue<Head>(
        (a, b) -> b.item.key.compareTo(a.item.key));
    for (Source source : sources) {
      Candidate first = source.next();
      if (first != null) heap.add(new Head(first, source));
    }
    var seen = new HashSet<Long>();
    var out = new ArrayList<Post>();
    Key last = null;
    int scanned = 0;
    while (!heap.isEmpty() && out.size() < limit && scanned < 5 * limit) {
      Head head = heap.remove();
      Candidate candidate = head.item;
      last = candidate.key; // consumed, not merely prefetched
      scanned++;
      Candidate next = head.source.next();
      if (next != null) heap.add(new Head(next, head.source));
      if (!seen.add(candidate.key.postId)) continue;
      Post post = bodies.load(candidate.key.postId);
      if (post == null) continue;
      if (!post.key.equals(candidate.key))
        throw new IllegalStateException("corrupt pointer");
      if (post.live && view.allows(post.author)) out.add(post);
    }
    view.checkStillCurrent(); // also required for an empty page
    boolean more = !heap.isEmpty();
    return new Page(List.copyOf(out), more ? last : null,
                    more && out.size() < limit, scanned);
  }

cursor 指向最后消费候选。默认 limit=20，最多消费 100 个来源行（重复也占预算）；limit≤50。预取 100 条、在第 23 条凑齐 20 条有效正文，cursor 只推到第 23 条，剩下重读；已删/取关候选也前移，避免反复扫描。预算用完但未满页返回 partial=true 和 continuation；只有所有来源确认耗尽才不给 next_cursor。单个不透明 cursor 在外围封装边界及版本。

seek 避免 OFFSET 位移，不承诺零遗漏。新帖不会让页码漂移，迟到扇出却可能落在已跨过的上界之上，本轮深翻仍漏，刷新首页可见。固定快照只稳定已包含的集合；严格历史完整还须先等所选提交位点前的全部来源水位就绪，或直接读权威作者索引。1,000 指针保留窗外明确历史结束或走归档，不悄悄变成无界作者扫描。LIMIT/OFFSET [5]

The cursor names the last consumed candidate. Default limit=20 allows at most 100 source rows, counting duplicates; limit≤50. If candidate 23 completes 20 valid bodies out of 100 prefetched rows, advance only to 23 and reread the rest. Deleted/unfollowed candidates also advance progress. Budget exhaustion with a short page returns partial=true and a continuation; omit next_cursor only after every source confirms exhaustion. The outer layer seals the boundary and version fields into one opaque cursor.

Seek avoids OFFSET shifts, not every omission. New posts do not move page numbers, but late fan-out can land above an already passed bound and remain absent until refresh. A fixed snapshot stabilizes only the set it contains. Strict historical completeness additionally waits for all source watermarks through a chosen commit position, or reads authoritative author indexes. Beyond the 1,000-pointer retention window, report history end or use archives rather than switching to unbounded author scans. LIMIT/OFFSET [5]

故障与预算：保护数据库不等于保住可用性Failures and budgets: protecting storage does not establish availability

队列积压损害新鲜度，缓存失效损害回源预算，正文库失败损害读页。下面三种故障沿各自依赖传播；持久 inbox 已有的页不必依赖队列正在健康消费。

Queue backlog hurts freshness; cache loss consumes origin capacity; body-store failure prevents page reads. Each fault follows its dependency. Already durable inbox pages do not require a healthy live consumer.

稳态按峰值 11,574.07 页/s×20 正文×1% miss≈2,314.81 次点查/s，再加作者写后读最坏 58×20=1,160，合计约 3,474.81，低于假设 4,000/s。命中率降到 95%，光普通页就需约 11,574 点查/s。删帖密集补页、重读者视图和后台补建都共享全局点查 token、并发与截止预算；batch 不是免费。

缓存整层故障时，想要 231,481 正文点查/s，约 57.9 倍预算。完整默认页最多 4,000÷20=200 页/s；limit=50 最多 80 页/s，还要取 Timeline 预算较小者。额外候选过滤多的页按实际点查逐条扣费，不能把 200 当任何页面的固定通行证。超过预算给 503，不把依赖失败伪装成 200 空列表，也不续命已过期正文。

每天读错误预算 200M×0.001=200k 次；固定峰值下缓存故障约每秒失败 11,574.07−200=11,374.07 次，约 17.58 s 即花完。限流只保护库，副本、故障域与恢复速度才影响 99.9%。监控分别覆盖发布提交、Outbox 最老未发布年龄、任务最老未完成页、来源水位、刷新可见延迟、缓存硬年龄和 origin token；不要拿 published_at 当粉丝完成。

At peak, 11,574.07 pages/s×20 bodies×1% misses gives about 2,314.81 point reads/s. Add worst-case author read-after-write at 58×20=1,160 for roughly 3,474.81/s, under the assumed 4,000/s budget. At 95% hit rate, ordinary pages alone need roughly 11,574/s. Delete-heavy refill, heavy-reader views and background backfills share global point-read tokens, concurrency and deadline budgets; batching is not free.

Tier-wide cache loss demands 231,481 body reads/s, about 57.9× budget. Admit at most 4,000÷20=200 full default pages/s, or 80/s at limit=50, further restricted by Timeline capacity. Charge actual point reads for pages with extra filtering; 200 is no unconditional pass for every page. Excess load gets 503, never a fake 200 empty page or expired-body extension.

The daily read-error budget is 200M×0.001=200k. During a fixed peak, cache loss rejects about 11,574.07−200=11,374.07 pages/s and consumes it in about 17.58 s. Admission protects storage; replicas, fault domains and recovery speed determine availability.
