30 秒开场、范围和两条 SLOThe 30-second open, scope, and two SLOs

中文

这是关注流：人发帖，粉丝按时间看。一条帖先写入 Post Store，请求里就返回 post id；扇出异步进行。普通人的粉丝 inbox 会得到一行指针。大 V 不全量推，读的时候把大 V 日志和自己的 inbox 按时间 merge。Timeline 不复制正文。排序模型是以后可能加在已合并 id 上的读时步骤，这轮不设计。

先把产品边界说死，避免画出另一个系统。要做的是发帖、按关注关系出现在粉丝的时间线里、按时间往下翻、删帖之后读的时候不再出现。媒体只存 URL，二进制在对象存储，CDN 不在这张图上。多地域多活、私信、通知的到达保证，都不是这一轮。推荐流、召回、精排、双塔，整段划出范围：面试官如果问 For You，回答那是另一道题，然后回到关注边和物化 inbox。

有两个容易被说成一个数的目标，都必须标成设计假设，不是哪家公司的内部 SLO。发布确认：Post Store 和同一笔事务里的 outbox 提交成功后，在假设的 p99 300 毫秒内返回 201，可用性按 99.9% 来设计。扇出新鲜度：队列健康时，被 push 的普通人帖子在假设的 p99 5 秒内出现在粉丝 inbox。201 发出的时候，粉丝可以还没看到。作者自己要立刻看到，靠读路径额外并上自己刚写的那条，不靠等扇出。

删帖可见性也单独写。假设是：帖子状态在 Post Store 里变成 deleted 之后，hydrate 在大约 30 秒内丢掉它。这 30 秒来自主动删缓存加上同量级的 TTL，不是「全世界 inbox 同一毫秒删掉这个 id」。读 feed 在 timeline 缓存命中时，服务端 p99 按 80 毫秒设计，不含手机网络。这些数字是为了继续画图，面试官可以改，改了就跟着改推拉分界和 TTL。

English

This is a follow feed: people post, followers read by time. A post is committed to the Post Store and the request returns a post id; fan-out is asynchronous. Ordinary followers get one pointer row in the inbox. A celebrity is not fully pushed; the read path merges that celebrity log with the reader's inbox by time. The timeline does not copy the body. A ranking model would be a later read-time step on ids that are already merged. This round does not design it.

Close the product boundary before drawing a second system. In scope: publish, show the post on followers' timelines through the follow relationship, page by time, and hide a deleted post on read. Media is a URL; bytes live in object storage; the CDN is not on this board. Multi-region active-active, private messages, and notification delivery guarantees are out of this round. Recommendation, retrieval, ranking, and two-tower models are out of scope as a block. If they ask about For You, say that is a different problem, then return to follow edges and the materialized inbox.

Two targets are easy to collapse into one number. Both are design assumptions, not a company's internal SLO. Publish ack: after the Post Store and the outbox row commit in one transaction, return 201 within an assumed p99 of 300 ms, designed for 99.9% availability. Fan-out freshness: while the queue is healthy, a pushed post from an ordinary user shows up in a follower inbox within an assumed p99 of 5 seconds. When the 201 leaves, followers may not have it yet. The author sees their own post immediately because the read path unions that one row from the Post Store, not because fan-out has finished.

Delete visibility is its own assumption: after status becomes deleted in the Post Store, hydrate drops the post within about 30 seconds. That window is active cache deletion plus a TTL of the same order. It is not a promise that every inbox loses the id in the same millisecond. A feed read that hits the timeline cache is designed for a server-side p99 of 80 ms, excluding the phone network. These numbers exist so the picture can continue. If the interviewer changes them, the push threshold and the TTL change with them.

一次乘法，决定不能纯 pushOne multiplication decides against pure push

中文

扇出贵不贵，不看发帖 QPS，看帖子数乘粉丝数。下面的输入全部是白板假设，用来推动设计，不冒充任何一家社交产品的线上计数。假设日活一千万。一成的人每天发一条，帖子数是 10,000,000 × 0.1 = 1,000,000 条/天。再假设发帖的人平均有 200 个粉丝。中位数通常比 200 小，均值被少数账号拉高，所以 200 是用来算总写入的均值，不是「典型用户有 200 个粉丝」。

把乘法写全。1,000,000 条帖 × 200 个粉丝 = 200,000,000 次 inbox 写入/天。一天 86,400 秒。200,000,000 ÷ 86,400：86,400 × 2,300 = 198,720,000，余 1,280,000，1,280,000 ÷ 86,400 ≈ 15，所以日均大约 2,315 次写入/秒。再假设峰值是日均的 5 倍，5 × 2,315 = 11,575 次/秒。发帖本身只有 1,000,000 ÷ 86,400 ≈ 12 次/秒，峰值大约 58 次/秒。12 次/秒解释不了架构。2,315 次/秒的预计算写入解释了为什么要有异步 worker，也说明普通人的 push 还在一条流水线扛得住的范围里。

读路径另起一个假设：每人每天打开 feed 20 次。10,000,000 × 20 = 200,000,000 次打开/天，除以 86,400 也是大约 2,315 次/秒，峰值同样大约 11,575。这两个 2,315 相等，只因为假设里 0.1 × 200 = 20，和「每天打开 20 次」用了同一个 20。把打开次数改成 10，读 QPS 减半，扇出写入不变。不要把这个巧合讲成定律。真正否掉纯 push 的是下一档乘法。

假设一个百万粉账号发一条：1 × 1,000,000 = 1,000,000 次 inbox 写入，挤在一次发帖里。1,000,000 ÷ 2,315 ≈ 432 秒，大约七分钟的全日均扇出预算被一条帖占满。就算把峰值 11,575 次/秒整段让给它，1,000,000 ÷ 11,575 ≈ 86 秒。尖峰摊不进日均，所以这条帖不推进每个粉丝的 inbox。教学分界放在大约 100,000 粉：1 × 100,000 = 100,000 次写入，100,000 ÷ 2,315 ≈ 43 秒日均预算，100,000 ÷ 11,575 ≈ 8.6 秒峰值预算。低于这条线走 push；高于这条线只往该作者自己的日志追加 1 行。10 万不是魔法数，流水线变快或新鲜度放宽，线就上移。

纯 pull 会被另一次乘法打回来。若每次读都对 200 个关注对象各取最近帖，2,315 × 200 = 463,000 次作者尾部读取/秒，而且大 V 的同一条帖被每个粉丝各读一次。Hybrid 把普通人收成 1 次读自己的 inbox。大 V 只留少数几路：假设一页最多 merge 8 份大 V 日志，2,315 × 8 = 18,520 次尾部读取/秒，而且这 8 把 key 是所有粉丝共享的，缓存吃得下。所以默认图是 hybrid：push 负责 200 这个均值里的普通人，pull 负责把 100 万这次乘法从写路径上拿掉。

English

Fan-out cost is not post QPS. It is posts times followers. Every input below is a board assumption used to force a design. None of it is an online counter from a real social product. Assume 10 million DAU. If one tenth post once a day, that is 10,000,000 × 0.1 = 1,000,000 posts/day. Assume those authors average 200 followers. The median is usually lower; a few accounts pull the mean up. 200 is the mean used to size total writes, not a claim that a typical user has 200 followers.

Write the multiplication out. 1,000,000 posts × 200 followers = 200,000,000 inbox writes/day. A day has 86,400 seconds. 200,000,000 ÷ 86,400: 86,400 × 2,300 = 198,720,000, remainder 1,280,000, and 1,280,000 ÷ 86,400 ≈ 15, so about 2,315 writes/s on average. Assume the peak is 5× the average: 5 × 2,315 = 11,575 writes/s. Posting itself is only 1,000,000 ÷ 86,400 ≈ 12 requests/s, about 58/s at peak. Twelve per second does not justify the architecture. 2,315 precomputed writes/s is why an async worker exists, and it is still a pipeline that ordinary push can fit.

The read path uses a separate assumption: 20 feed opens per person per day. 10,000,000 × 20 = 200,000,000 opens/day, which is also about 2,315/s, and about 11,575/s at a 5× peak. The two 2,315s match only because 0.1 × 200 = 20 in the assumptions, the same 20 as the open rate. Change opens to 10 and read QPS halves while fan-out writes stay put. Do not promote the coincidence into a law. The multiplication that rejects pure push is the next one.

One post from an assumed million-follower account is 1 × 1,000,000 = 1,000,000 inbox writes in a single burst. 1,000,000 ÷ 2,315 ≈ 432 seconds, about seven minutes of the entire average fan-out budget. Even if the whole peak of 11,575/s is given to that post, 1,000,000 ÷ 11,575 ≈ 86 seconds. The spike does not dissolve into the daily mean, so that post is not pushed into every follower inbox. The teaching threshold sits near 100,000 followers: 1 × 100,000 = 100,000 writes, 100,000 ÷ 2,315 ≈ 43 seconds of average budget, 100,000 ÷ 11,575 ≈ 8.6 seconds of peak budget. Below the line, push. Above the line, append one row to that author's own log. 100,000 is not magic. A faster pipeline or a looser freshness SLO moves the line up.

Pure pull fails a different multiplication. If every read fetched recent posts from all 200 followees, that is 2,315 × 200 = 463,000 author-tail reads/s, and every follower rereads a celebrity post. Hybrid collapses ordinary authors into one read of the reader's inbox. Celebrity legs stay few: assume a page merges at most 8 celebrity logs, 2,315 × 8 = 18,520 tail reads/s, on keys shared by every follower, which a cache can hold. The default picture is hybrid. Push covers ordinary users inside the mean of 200. Pull removes the 1,000,000-write burst from the write path.

怎么把这张图讲完

正常路径。发帖请求进 Feed API，校验文本和媒体 URL 之后，向 Post Store 插入帖子。提交成功才允许 201，响应里是 post id 和 created_at。扇出消息在提交之后进 Fan-out Queue，Worker 消费后向 Timeline Store 追加 inbox 行。刷 feed 走另一条：Feed API 先读 Timeline Cache，未命中再读 Timeline Store，取出 id 之后再去补正文，最后把一页和下一页 cursor 送回 Client。图上的绿色回程就是这条返回，不是装饰。

为什么先画成这样。日均发帖大约 12 次/秒，写放大日均大约 2,315 次/秒，读大约同样量级的打开。所以 API 无状态，可以按读 QPS 扩；Post Store 按作者分片保存正文，是唯一权威；Timeline Store 保存预计算的指针，用来把 463,000 次/秒的纯 pull 探测从热路径拿掉。队列存在，是因为 201 的 300 毫秒预算包不住「给每个粉丝写一行」。小规模时 Feed API 和 Worker 可以是同一进程里的两个函数，依赖方向不变：读路径不调用发号或扇出。

Worker 要读的是关注图，不是推荐特征。普通人的扇出需要「谁关注了这个作者」。这条边可以落成对象和关联：关联由起点、类型、终点标识，同一类型在两点之间至多一条，并带时间，关联列表按时间从新到旧排列，可以用范围查询翻页。Bronson 等人在 TAO 里发表的就是这种社交图存储，系统偏读，并且明确把效率和可用性放在一致性前面 [1]。TAO 不是物化的 per-user inbox，也不是排序模型。我们只借用这个数据模型来表示 FOLLOWS / FOLLOWED_BY。大 V 的粉丝列表会变成一条很长的关联列表，全量 push 就是把这条列表扫完再写 N 次，所以下一张图会把这条扫描从大 V 的路径上拿掉。

替代和故障。如果产品只有十万日活、没有大 V，一张按作者索引的帖子表加按关注人在读时合并，可以是第一版，纯 push 的 inbox 也可以是第一版。那是规模还没碰到 100 万次写入那一档时的选择。这张图的故障方向已经能说：Post Store 提交失败就没有 201，队列失败不取消已经提交的帖，缓存失败不改变正文。作者和粉丝看到的时间可以差几秒，这是新鲜度 SLO，不是发布失败。常见的半张图是只画到队列就停，读路径没有回到 Client，面试官无法追问缓存未命中和空页的区别。

How to talk through the picture

Normal path. A publish enters Feed API, which checks text and media URLs, then inserts the post into the Post Store. A 201 is allowed only after commit, carrying post id and created_at. The fan-out message enters the queue after that commit. The worker appends inbox rows to the Timeline Store. A feed read is the other path: Feed API reads the Timeline Cache, misses through to the Timeline Store, hydrates bodies by id, and returns a page plus the next cursor to the client. The green reply is that return.

Why this shape. Posts average about 12/s, fan-out writes about 2,315/s, and feed opens sit in the same band under these assumptions. Feed API is stateless and scales with read QPS. The Post Store, sharded by author, owns the body. The Timeline Store holds precomputed pointers so the hot path is not 463,000 pure-pull probes/s. The queue exists because a 300 ms budget for the 201 cannot include a write per follower. At small scale, Feed API and the worker can be two functions in one process. The dependency direction stays: the read path does not call fan-out.

The worker reads a follow graph, not ranking features. Ordinary fan-out needs “who follows this author.” That edge fits objects and associations: an association is identified by source, type, and destination, at most one of a given type exists between two objects, it carries a time, and the association list is ordered newest to oldest so a range query can page it. Bronson et al. published TAO as a store for that social graph. It is read-heavy and explicitly favors efficiency and availability over consistency [1]. TAO is not a materialized per-user inbox and not a ranking model. We use the model for FOLLOWS / FOLLOWED_BY. A celebrity follower list is a long association list. Full push means scanning it and writing N rows, which the next picture removes from the celebrity path.

Alternative and failure. At 100 thousand DAU and no celebrity, a post table indexed by author plus a read-time merge over followees can be version one, and so can a pure-push inbox. That choice is for a product that has not met the one-million-write burst. This picture already separates failures: a Post Store failure yields no 201, a queue failure does not uncommit the post, and a cache failure does not change the body. Author and follower can see the post seconds apart. That is the freshness SLO, not a failed publish. A common incomplete board stops at the queue and never draws the read back to the client, so cache miss and empty page cannot be distinguished.

API、幂等和最小数据模型API, idempotency, and the minimum data model

中文

发帖。POST /v1/posts，头里带 Idempotency-Key，体是 text 和 media_urls。校验失败 400，未登录 401，限流 429 并带 Retry-After，Post Store 暂时不可用 503。只有帖子行和事务 outbox 行在同一笔数据库事务里提交成功，才返回 201，体是 post_id 和 created_at。客户端超时后用同一把 key、同一份 body 重试，命中幂等记录就返回原来的 post id，不再插第二行。同一把 key 配了不同 body，返回 409，因为 key 代表一次发帖意图，不代表「相同文本永远同一条帖」。两个活动用了相同文案，仍然是两帖。

读 feed。GET /v1/feed?limit=20&cursor=。没有 cursor 就是最新一页。服务端把 cursor 解成 (created_at, post_id)，下一页取严格更旧的那段。默认 20 条，假设上限 50。成功是 200，items 里是已经 hydrate 的帖子，next_cursor 没有更多时为空。用户确实谁都不关注，inbox 读成功且为空，这是 200 加空列表。Timeline Store 不可用而且缓存也未命中，是 503，不能把故障伪装成「你的 feed 是空的」。删帖是作者调用 DELETE /v1/posts/{id}，Post Store 里 status 变为 deleted 并增加 version 之后返回 200。这个 200 不表示每个粉丝的 inbox 已经摘掉该 id。

最小表。Post 的主键是 post_id，字段有 author_id、text、media_urls、status、created_at、version。Idempotency 的主键是 (author_id, key)，另存 post_id 和 request_hash。Follow 的主键是 (follower_id, followee_id)，另有 (followee_id, follower_id) 这条索引，供 worker 按作者扫粉丝。Timeline 行的主键是 (user_id, created_at, post_id)，带 author_id，不带正文。CelebLog 的主键是 (author_id, created_at, post_id)，每个大 V 一份，不是每个粉丝一份。TxOutbox 的主键是 event_id，带 post_id、author_id、created_at 和可空的 published_at。关注边的形态对齐 TAO 的关联 [1]，inbox 是另一份派生数据。

有序集合只解释访问方式。按用户取一段最新 id，是「一个 key 下面按分数有序的成员」。Redis 的 sorted set 里，ZADD 会加入成员，若成员已存在则更新分数，而不是再插一行 [2]。把 post_id 当 member、时间当 score，扇出重试同一条帖不会变成两行。ZRANGE 按已有顺序取一段，复杂度随返回条数增长，不需要再排序。持久的 Timeline Store 也可以是同一主键的分区表。面试要定的是键和成员，不是厂商。正文缓存键是 post:{post_id}，timeline 缓存键是 timeline:{user_id}，里面只有最近的指针窗口。

English

Publish. POST /v1/posts carries an Idempotency-Key and a body of text plus media_urls. Invalid input is 400, missing auth is 401, rate limit is 429 with Retry-After, and a Post Store outage is 503. Return 201 with post_id and created_at only after the post row and the transactional outbox row commit in one database transaction. A timeout retry with the same key and the same body reads the idempotency record and returns the original post id. The same key with a different body is 409. The key is one publish intent, not a rule that identical text is always one post. Two campaigns can share copy and still be two posts.

Read. GET /v1/feed?limit=20&cursor=. No cursor means the newest page. The server decodes the cursor to (created_at, post_id) and takes the next page strictly older than that pair. Default 20, assumed cap 50. Success is 200: hydrated items and a next_cursor that is empty when the feed is exhausted. A user who follows nobody, with a successful empty inbox read, is 200 and an empty list. If the Timeline Store is down and the cache misses, return 503. Do not disguise the outage as an empty feed. The author deletes with DELETE /v1/posts/{id}. Return 200 after status becomes deleted and version increments in the Post Store. That 200 does not mean every follower inbox has dropped the id.

Minimum tables. Post is keyed by post_id and holds author_id, text, media_urls, status, created_at, and version. Idempotency is keyed by (author_id, key) and stores post_id plus request_hash. Follow is keyed by (follower_id, followee_id) and has a second index on (followee_id, follower_id) so the worker can scan fans of an author. A timeline row is keyed by (user_id, created_at, post_id) and stores author_id, not the body. CelebLog is keyed by (author_id, created_at, post_id): one log per celebrity, not one per follower. TxOutbox is keyed by event_id and stores post_id, author_id, created_at, and a nullable published_at. The follow edge matches TAO's association shape [1]. The inbox is a separate derived structure.

A sorted set describes the access path. "Newest ids for one user" is an ordered set of members under one key. In a Redis sorted set, ZADD inserts a member or, if that member already exists, updates its score instead of inserting a second row [2]. With post_id as the member and time as the score, a fan-out retry does not duplicate the post. ZRANGE walks the existing order; cost grows with the number of returned members, and there is no extra sort. A durable Timeline Store can be a partitioned table with the same primary key. The interview decision is the key and the member, not the vendor. The body cache key is post:{post_id}. The timeline cache key is timeline:{user_id}, holding only the recent pointer window.

发帖时序：先提交，再异步扇出，中间有一个窗口Publish sequence: commit first, fan out after, and name the window

把一次发帖按时间讲完

正常路径。Feed API 先按 author_id 加 Idempotency-Key 查幂等表。命中且 request_hash 相同，直接返回已有 post id。未命中则开一笔事务：插入 Post，插入 TxOutbox，published_at 为空，然后提交。事务成功之后才返回 201。另一个发布器扫描 published_at 为空的 outbox 行，把消息放进 Fan-out Queue，成功后再填 published_at。消息体只有 post_id、author_id、created_at，不带粉丝列表。Worker 收到后，若作者低于粉丝阈值，就按 Follow 索引分页读粉丝，对每个 inbox 追加指针；若是大 V，只追加 CelebLog。追加使用同一 post_id 作为成员，重放不会插出第二行 [2]。

为什么 outbox 和帖子必须同一笔事务。201 的预算是 300 毫秒量级，扇出是每秒两千多次的背景写入，大 V 更是一次百万级。所以请求线程不能扫粉丝。只在应用内存里「提交后再发消息」会留下崩溃窗口：帖子在，消息不在，粉丝永远看不到，而客户端已经拿到 201，不会当失败重试。Outbox 把「必须发出去」写成和帖子一起提交的行。发布器挂了，行还在，补发即可。Kreps、Narkhede 和 Rao 在 2011 年的 Kafka 论文里写的交付语义是至少一次；恰好一次通常要两阶段提交，而且他们的应用不需要 [3]。我们的队列按这个契约用。后来的日志可以在日志内部做幂等生产和事务，那笔事务仍然包不住 Post Store 的提交外加每一个 inbox 写。所以扇出契约是至少一次，加上幂等追加。

还有两个相邻窗口。如果先发队列再提交数据库，事务回滚会扇出一条不存在的帖，hydrate 时变成空洞或错误。顺序必须是先提交、再发布。如果提交成功但 201 在网上丢了，客户端用同一 Idempotency-Key 重试，幂等表把第一次的 post id 交回去，outbox 里仍然只有一行。Worker 扫到一半崩溃，消息会按至少一次再投。普通人只有大约 200 个粉丝，整表重扫的重复工作可以接受；靠近阈值的账号重复工作变贵，这是不把大 V 放进全量扫描的另一个理由。published_at 的更新和队列发送不是同一笔事务，可能重复发布，不可能在行还在的情况下永久丢失，除非有人把 outbox 行删掉。

替代和常见错法。帖子量和 outbox 量都在 Post Store 里时，同库事务就够，不必上分布式事务。若有人把 outbox 放到另一套库，就要讲补偿扫描，不能说「两边同时写」。小规模可以把发布器做成提交后的同进程调用，只要崩溃后仍能从 outbox 表捞起未发布行。常见错法是承诺扇出恰好一次。那会把两阶段提交拉进发帖热路径，201 的延迟和可用性会跟着队列与每一个 inbox 一起掉。另一错法是用「相同文本返回同一帖」代替幂等键，把两次真实发帖并成一条。

Walk one publish in time order

Normal path. Feed API looks up author_id plus Idempotency-Key. A hit with the same request hash returns the stored post id. A miss opens one transaction: insert Post, insert TxOutbox with published_at null, then commit. Only then return 201. A publisher scans unpublished outbox rows, puts a message on the fan-out queue, and fills published_at after the send succeeds. The message carries post_id, author_id, and created_at, not the follower list. If the author is under the follower threshold, the worker pages the Follow index and appends a pointer to each inbox. If the author is a celebrity, it appends CelebLog only. The append uses post_id as the member, so a replay does not insert a second row [2].

Why the outbox shares the post transaction. The 201 budget is a few hundred milliseconds. Fan-out is a background of about two thousand writes a second, and a celebrity is a million-write burst. The request thread cannot scan followers. “Send the message from memory after commit” leaves a crash window: the post exists, the message does not, followers never see it, and the client already holds a 201 so it will not retry as a failure. The outbox stores “this must be sent” in the same commit as the post. If the publisher dies, the row remains and is retried. Kreps, Narkhede, and Rao wrote in the 2011 Kafka paper that delivery is at-least-once; exactly-once typically needs two-phase commit and was not required by their applications [3]. This queue uses that contract. Later logs can do idempotent produce and transactions inside the log. That transaction still does not cover a Post Store commit plus every inbox write. The fan-out contract is at-least-once plus an idempotent append.

Two neighboring windows. Publishing to the queue before the database commit fans out a ghost if the transaction rolls back. Hydrate then sees a hole or an error. Commit first, publish second. If the commit succeeds and the 201 is lost on the network, the client retries the same Idempotency-Key, the idempotency row returns the first post id, and the outbox still has one row. If the worker crashes mid-scan, the message is redelivered at least once. An ordinary user has about 200 followers, so repeating the scan is acceptable duplicate work. Near the threshold that duplicate work gets expensive, which is another reason not to full-scan a celebrity. Updating published_at and sending to the queue are not one transaction. The send can be duplicated. It is not permanently lost while the outbox row exists, unless something deletes that row.

Alternative and the usual mistake. While posts and outbox rows share the Post Store, one local transaction is enough. A distributed transaction is not required. If the outbox lives in a second database, say how the repair scan works. “Write both” is not a design. At small scale the publisher can be an in-process call after commit, as long as a crash can still load unpublished rows. The usual mistake is to promise exactly-once fan-out. That pulls two-phase commit onto the publish path, and the 201 then fails with the queue and with every inbox. Another mistake replaces the idempotency key with “same text, same post,” merging two real publishes.

Push、pull、hybrid 是两条路径，不是两张卡片Push, pull, and hybrid are two paths, not two cards

两条路径怎么合到一页上

正常路径。作者低于大约 10 万粉这条假设线时，Worker 用 FOLLOWED_BY 关联列表分页取出粉丝 [1]，对每个粉丝的 Timeline 行做一次幂等追加，成员是 post_id [2]。作者高于这条线时，Worker 不扫那条长列表，只往 CelebLog 追加一行。读者打开 feed 时，Feed API 读自己的 inbox，再读他关注的每一个大 V 的日志尾部，按 (created_at, post_id) 做 k 路归并，截成一页。归并是按时间，不是模型打分。若读者自己刚发的帖还没扇出完，用 author_id 从 Post Store 取那一条并进首页，保证作者读己之写。粉丝不享受这个并集，他们等新鲜度 SLO。

为什么是 hybrid，而不是先画纯 push 再等追问。日均 2,315 次/秒的 push 写在假设的流水线里讲得通，所以普通人预计算，读的时候一次拿到 inbox。1 × 1,000,000 那次乘法讲不通，所以大 V 的写放大收成 1。读侧代价是每个读者多几次共享日志的尾部读取。假设一页最多 8 路，就是 2,315 × 8 = 18,520 次/秒的量级，而且 key 是作者 id，不是粉丝 id，缓存命中会把这个数再打下来。纯 pull 的 2,315 × 200 = 463,000 次/秒没有这个共享结构。阈值写在白板上，并说明它会随流水线容量和 p99 新鲜度移动。

可行的替代。没有大 V 的小产品可以纯 push，把阈值声明成「一旦有账号越过线再切」。那是演进，不是这组假设下的第一张图。另一个替代是只给最近活跃的粉丝 push：若假设百万粉里一成活跃，仍然是 10 万次写入，和阈值上的普通人同量级，不活跃的人回来时仍要 pull 一页。这是优化，第一张图不画第四套系统。纯 pull 在关注数只有几十、读 QPS 只有几十的时候更简单，因为没有 inbox 修复问题。我们的读 QPS 和 200 的关注数把这条路留在小规模。

故障和讲错的地方。账号从普通人长成大 V 时，停止新的全量 push，已经写进 inbox 的旧 id 不必立刻删，读的时候按时间合并两边，用 post_id 去重。反向也一样：跌破阈值后可以从下一条开始 push。讲错的版本是「大 V 也 push，多加消费者就行」。消费者加得再多，一次要写的行数仍是 100 万，热点在同一把粉丝列表和同一批 inbox 分片上，不会因为消费者个数变成 O(1)。另一个讲错的版本是在这张图旁边画召回和精排，把还没合并出来的 id 当成模型输入。排序如果以后要做，输入是这一页已经按时间合并好的 id，那是读时的另一步，不是这道题的扇出设计。

How the two paths become one page

Normal path. Under the assumed line near 100,000 followers, the worker pages the FOLLOWED_BY association list [1] and idempotently appends one timeline row per follower, with post_id as the member [2]. Above the line, the worker does not scan that long list. It appends one CelebLog row. When the reader opens the feed, Feed API reads their inbox, reads the tail of each followed celebrity log, and k-way-merges by (created_at, post_id) into one page. The merge is by time, not a model score. If the reader's own latest post has not finished fan-out, fetch that one row from the Post Store by author_id and union it into the first page. That is the author's read-your-write. Followers do not get that union. They wait on the freshness SLO.

Why hybrid is the first picture. About 2,315 push writes/s fits the assumed pipeline, so ordinary users are precomputed and the read is one inbox fetch. The multiplication 1 × 1,000,000 does not fit, so celebrity write amplification collapses to 1. The read pays a few shared tail fetches. Cap a page at 8 legs and the order is 2,315 × 8 = 18,520/s, on keys that are author ids rather than follower ids, so cache hits cut that number again. Pure pull at 2,315 × 200 = 463,000/s has no such shared structure. Write the threshold on the board and say it moves with pipeline capacity and the freshness p99.

Alternatives that are real. A small product with no celebrity can pure-push and declare that it will cut over when an account crosses the line. That is an evolution, not the first picture under these assumptions. Another alternative pushes only recently active followers. If one tenth of a million followers are active, that is still 100,000 writes, the same order as a threshold user, and inactive followers still pull a page when they return. That is an optimization, not a fourth architecture on the first board. Pure pull is simpler when follow counts and read QPS are both in the tens, because there is no inbox to repair. Our read QPS and a follow count of 200 leave that design at small scale.

Failure and the weak answers. When an account grows into a celebrity, stop new full pushes. Old ids already in inboxes can stay. Reads merge both sides by time and dedupe on post_id. In the other direction, the next post can start pushing again after the account falls below the line. The weak version says “push celebrities too, and add consumers.” Extra consumers do not change the fact that one post still wants a million row writes. The hot spot is one follower list and one set of inbox shards. Consumer count does not make that O(1). The other weak version draws retrieval and ranking beside this picture and treats ids that are not merged yet as model input. If ranking is added later, its input is the page of ids already merged by time. That is a later read-time step, not the fan-out design of this question.

Timeline 行里是指针，正文只存一份A timeline row is a pointer; the body is stored once

指针为什么比副本便宜

正常路径。Timeline 行是 user_id、post_id、author_id、created_at。Feed API 用 timeline:{user_id} 取最近窗口，未命中再读 Timeline Store，然后用这组 post_id 去 post:{post_id} 批量取正文。缓存未命中时回 Post Store 填充，填充的值带 status、version 和 created_at，这样 hydrate 不必为了判断删帖再查一次库。媒体字段是 URL。图片字节不进 Feed API，也不进 timeline 行。

数量级逼出「不复制正文」。假设两成日活的 timeline 驻在缓存，即 2,000,000 个 key，每个留 1,000 个 id。指针行按 3 个 8 字节再把结构开销算到 64 字节，这 64 是假设。2,000,000 × 1,000 × 64 = 1.28×1011 字节，大约 128 GB。若 1 KB 按 1,000 字节把正文复制进每一行，2,000,000 × 1,000 × 1,000 = 2×1012 字节 = 2 TB。2 ÷ 0.128 ≈ 16 倍。帖子原文本身是 1,000 字节 × 1,000,000 条/天 = 1 GB/天，一年大约 365 GB，三副本大约 1 TB 量级，不含媒体字节。复制进 inbox 之后，存储不再跟帖子数走，跟帖子数乘粉丝数走，删改也要碰到每一份。

失效对齐 look-aside 缓存的做法。Nishtala 等人把 memcache 用作按需填充的旁路缓存：写请求先改数据库，再 delete 缓存键，因为 delete 幂等；缓存不是权威，允许被驱逐 [4]。删帖就按这个顺序：Post Store 里 status=deleted、version 加一，然后 DEL post:{id}。不在每个 inbox 里改一份正文，因为那些副本我们根本没写。inbox 里的 id 留到滑动窗口挤掉，或由后台慢任务摘除。hydrate 看到 deleted 就丢，若这一页不够 20 条，用同一个 cursor 边界再取下一段 id。假设 TTL 30 秒，用来覆盖删除消息丢失；它不代替主动删除。论文里 lease 用来压 thundering herd，他们在一组易惊群的键上测量到，没有 lease 时数据库峰值约 17,000 次查询/秒，有 lease 时约 1,300 [4]。那是他们的数。我们的帖子缓存在单进程里对同一个 post id 合并回源，跨进程再加回源限额，避免把 11,575 的读峰值在一条热帖上打穿 Post Store。

替代和讲错的地方。如果新鲜度允许小时级，可以只靠 TTL、不做主动 DEL，实现更短，删帖会在整个 TTL 里继续出现。我们的假设是大约 30 秒，所以主动删除是主路径，TTL 是保险。取关不扫 inbox：读的时候若 author 已不在关注集合里就滤掉，或异步摘。讲错的版本是「timeline 就是 SELECT 帖子 JOIN 关注」。那条 SQL 是纯 pull，在 2,315 × 200 的探测率上不成立。另一错法是为了删一条帖同步扫描每一个粉丝 inbox。那把删帖变成一次反向扇出，延迟和失败面都比改一行权威状态大。

Why a pointer is cheaper than a copy

Normal path. A timeline row is user_id, post_id, author_id, and created_at. Feed API reads timeline:{user_id} for the recent window, misses through to the Timeline Store, then batch-loads bodies from post:{post_id}. On a body miss, fill from the Post Store. The cached value includes status, version, and created_at so hydrate can drop deletes without a second lookup. The media field is a URL. Image bytes do not enter Feed API or the timeline row.

The size forces “do not copy the body.” Assume 20% of DAU have a resident timeline, 2,000,000 keys, 1,000 ids each. A pointer is three 8-byte fields, rounded to 64 bytes with structural overhead. That 64 is an assumption. 2,000,000 × 1,000 × 64 = 1.28×1011 bytes, about 128 GB. Copy a 1,000-byte body into every row and 2,000,000 × 1,000 × 1,000 = 2×1012 bytes = 2 TB. 2 ÷ 0.128 ≈ 16×. The posts themselves are 1,000 bytes × 1,000,000/day = 1 GB/day, about 365 GB a year, on the order of 1 TB with three replicas, excluding media bytes. Once bodies are copied into inboxes, storage follows posts times followers, and every edit or delete must touch every copy.

Invalidation follows look-aside practice. Nishtala et al. use memcache as a demand-filled look-aside cache: a write updates the database, then deletes the cache key, because deletes are idempotent. The cache is not authoritative and may evict data [4]. A delete follows that order: status=deleted and version+1 in the Post Store, then DEL post:{id}. There is no body to patch inside each inbox, because those copies were never written. The id stays until the sliding window drops it, or a slow background job strips it. Hydrate drops deleted. If the page is short of 20, take the next id window under the same cursor bound. An assumed 30-second TTL covers a lost delete. It does not replace the explicit delete. The paper's leases limit thundering herds. On a set of keys prone to herds, they measured about 17,000 database queries/s at peak without leases and about 1,300 with leases [4]. Those are their numbers. Our post cache coalesces fills for one post id inside a process, and a cross-process origin budget stops a hot post from turning the 11,575 read peak into a Post Store outage.

Alternative and the weak answers. If freshness can be hours, TTL alone without an active DEL is shorter to build, and a deleted post remains visible for the whole TTL. Our assumption is about 30 seconds, so the explicit delete is the main path and TTL is the backstop. Unfollow does not scan the inbox. Filter on read when the author is no longer followed, or strip asynchronously. The weak answer “the timeline is a SELECT of posts joined to follows” is pure pull, and it does not survive 2,315 × 200 probes/s. The other mistake synchronously scans every follower inbox to delete one post. That turns a delete into reverse fan-out, with more latency and a wider failure surface than updating one authoritative row.

读一页：cursor 切开合并后的 id，再补正文Read a page: the cursor cuts merged ids, then hydrate

一页是怎么切出来的

正常路径。首页没有 cursor。Feed API 取出 inbox 的最新 id，加上最多 8 份 CelebLog 里新于窗口下界的尾部，按 (created_at, post_id) 从新到旧归并，取 20 条，再批量 hydrate。下一页的 cursor 编码这一页最后一条的 created_at 和 post_id，请求把它当作排他上界：只要严格更旧的下一段。时间可能相同，所以次序里必须有 post_id，否则同一毫秒的两条帖没有稳定的「下一条」。返回前丢掉 deleted。若丢掉之后不足 20 条，继续向更旧的方向取，直到满页或没有更多 id。作者自己的新帖若 inbox 里还没有，并上 Post Store 的那一条，只并这一条。

为什么不用 OFFSET。Markus Winand 把两种翻页分开：OFFSET 从结果开头重新编号，插入会让页漂移，越往后扫描越多；seek 用上一页最后一条的排序键当边界，只取后面的行，并且排序必须确定，所以要加一个唯一列打破平局 [5]。Feed 是无限下拉，不需要跳到第 40 页，seek 不能随机跳页这个限制正好对上产品。客户端传 page=3 会被拒绝。新帖只出现在刷新后的首页，不会插进你正在看的下一页里造成重复或漏行。Hybrid 的两路在切页之前已经归并成一条有序 id 序列，所以 cursor 只有一把键，不必让客户端分别保存 inbox 和每个大 V 的位点。

替代。如果产品要「跳到第 N 页」而不是下拉，seek 不够，得接受 OFFSET 的扫描成本，或者维护近似页码。关注流不需要这个能力，所以不付这个成本。Snowflake 一类时间有序的 id 可以代替 (created_at, post_id) 这一对，只要全序仍然唯一。不要为了分页去设计一套新的 id，帖子主键已经够用。小数据量时 OFFSET 20 看不出差别，深翻到几千条才会慢；我们仍用 seek，因为插入漂移在第一页之后就会发生，和数据量无关。

讲错的地方。「Timeline 就是一条 SQL」常常写成按关注集合 JOIN 帖子再 ORDER BY 加 LIMIT/OFFSET。JOIN 是纯 pull，OFFSET 在直播式插入下不稳定。正确的读是：先拿预计算或合并后的 id，再按 cursor 切，再 hydrate。另一个错法是把正文放进 cursor 或放进 timeline 缓存值里，于是删帖必须改每一页缓存，而不是 DEL 一个 post:{id} [4]。空列表只在 inbox 和 CelebLog 都成功读完且确实没有帖时返回。任何一段存储超时，整页 503。

How one page is cut

Normal path. The first page has no cursor. Feed API takes the newest inbox ids plus the tails of at most eight CelebLogs that are newer than the window bound, merges them newest-first by (created_at, post_id), keeps 20, and hydrates in a batch. The next cursor encodes the created_at and post_id of the last row on this page. The following request treats that pair as an exclusive upper bound and takes the next strictly older slice. Timestamps can tie, so post_id has to be in the order. Otherwise two posts in the same millisecond have no stable “next.” Drop deleted rows before returning. If that leaves fewer than 20, keep scanning older ids until the page fills or the ids run out. If the author's own new post is not in the inbox yet, union that single Post Store row, and only that row.

Why not OFFSET. Markus Winand separates two paging methods. OFFSET renumbers from the start of the result, so inserts make pages drift and deeper pages scan more. Seek uses the last row's sort key as a bound and fetches only the following rows. The sort must be deterministic, so a unique column breaks ties [5]. A feed is infinite scroll. It does not jump to page 40. Seek cannot jump to an arbitrary page, and that limit matches the product. A client-supplied page=3 is rejected. New posts show up on refresh at the head. They do not insert into the next page you are about to open, which is how duplicates and skips happen. Hybrid's two legs are merged into one ordered id sequence before the cut, so the cursor is one key. The client does not keep a position per celebrity.

Alternative. If the product must jump to page N rather than scroll, seek is not enough. You pay OFFSET's scan or you maintain approximate page numbers. A follow feed does not need that, so it does not pay for it. A time-ordered id can replace the (created_at, post_id) pair if the total order stays unique. Do not invent a new id scheme for paging. The post primary key is enough. On a tiny table, OFFSET 20 looks the same. It gets slow thousands of rows in. We still use seek, because insert drift starts after the first page and does not depend on table size.

Weak answers. “The timeline is just a SQL select” usually means join posts to the follow set, then ORDER BY with LIMIT/OFFSET. The join is pure pull. OFFSET is unstable under live inserts. The read that matches this design takes precomputed or merged ids, cuts with the cursor, then hydrates. Another mistake puts the body in the cursor or in the timeline cache value. A delete then has to rewrite every page cache instead of deleting one post:{id} [4]. An empty list is legal only after the inbox and the CelebLogs were read successfully and there really are no posts. If any of those stores times out, the whole page is 503.

故障：滞后、热点、惊群、读放大Failures: lag, hot keys, stampedes, read amplification

每种失败用户看到什么

扇出滞后。Post Store 里有帖，TxOutbox 里 published_at 仍为空或队列年龄变大，粉丝 inbox 缺这个 id。读者拿到的是更旧的一页，刷新后晚一点出现，看起来像洞。作者若走了读己之写的并集，自己看得到，粉丝看不到，这是符合 SLO 的，只要滞后还在假设的 5 秒 p99 里。超过就报警，报警指标是队列年龄和未发布 outbox 的年龄，不是 201 的延迟。201 在这种故障下仍然是成功的。恢复是把 outbox 补发完，幂等追加把洞填上。不回滚已经返回的 post id。

热点和惊群。全量 push 大 V 会把一条 FOLLOWED_BY 列表打成热 key，并把写入打进许多 inbox 分片 [1]。Hybrid 跳过这条依赖，热写变成 CelebLog 上的一次追加，热读变成所有粉丝共享的同一把日志尾部，Timeline Cache 和 post 缓存都能接住。新帖刚发出时，大量读者同时 miss post:{id}。不合并的话，每个 miss 都打到 Post Store。进程内 singleflight 让一个 leader 回源，等待者共享结果。跨进程用回源预算，和 Nishtala 等人用 lease 限制同一把键的回填是同一类控制 [4]，具体的 10 秒一次 token、17,000 对 1,300 是论文里的配置和测量，不是我们的 TTL。缓存整体不可用时，绕过缓存读 Timeline Store 和 Post Store，同时单飞和限流。Store 也不可用时，已命中的缓存页可以在 TTL 内继续服务；未命中返回 503，不返回空列表。

读放大。如果实现漏了 K 的上限，一个关注了几十个大 V 的读者会把 GET 变成几十次尾部读取，2,315 × 200 那条纯 pull 乘法会从后门回来。页面上把未封顶的 pull 画成红色虚线，表示这条依赖不走。K 用满时，读 p99 会顶到假设的 80 毫秒上面，下一步是给这个重读者做一份短 TTL 的合并缓存，而不是把 K 默默加到 200。队列挂了不影响已经 push 进 Timeline Store 的旧页。大 V 日志挂了，可以只返回 inbox 并在响应里标明不完整，或者整页 503。要选一种并写进假设：这轮选择整页 503，避免把「少了大 V」伪装成完整时间线。

观测和讲错的地方。至少看四条线：201 延迟和错误率、outbox 未发布年龄、扇出队列年龄、post 缓存命中率和 singleflight 等待。只看 201 会在粉丝已经晚了几分钟时仍然报绿。讲错的恢复是「缓存挂了就让所有请求打数据库」。在 11,575 次/秒的读峰值上，这会把 Post Store 和 Timeline Store 一起打满。绕过必须带着单飞、限额和熔断。另一错法是为了填上滞后的洞，在读路径里对每个关注对象现查现合并，等于滞后期间把系统切成纯 pull，放大读而不是隔离写故障。

What the user sees in each failure

Fan-out lag. The post is in the Post Store, published_at is still null or the queue age is growing, and the follower inbox lacks the id. The reader gets an older page. A later refresh shows the post. It looks like a hole. If the author took the read-your-write union, they see it and followers do not. That matches the SLO while lag stays inside the assumed 5 second p99. Past that, page on queue age and on the age of unpublished outbox rows, not on 201 latency. The 201 is still a success during this failure. Recovery republishes the outbox. The idempotent append fills the hole. Do not roll back a post id that was already returned.

Hot keys and stampedes. Fully pushing a celebrity turns one FOLLOWED_BY list into a hot key and spreads writes across many inbox shards [1]. Hybrid skips that dependency. The hot write becomes one CelebLog append. The hot read becomes one shared log tail, which the timeline cache and the post cache can hold. When a new post appears, many readers miss post:{id} together. Without coalescing, every miss hits the Post Store. In-process singleflight lets one leader fill and lets waiters share the result. Across processes, an origin budget is the same kind of control as the leases Nishtala et al. used to limit refills of one key [4]. Their token interval of 10 seconds and the 17,000 versus 1,300 measurement belong to the paper, not to our TTL. If the cache tier is down, bypass it and read the Timeline Store and Post Store with singleflight and a rate limit. If those stores are down too, cache hits may still be served inside TTL. A miss returns 503, not an empty list.

Read amplification. If the implementation forgets the cap K, a reader who follows dozens of celebrities turns GET into dozens of tail reads, and the pure-pull multiplication 2,315 × 200 comes back through the side door. The picture draws the uncapped pull as a red dashed line: that dependency is not taken. When K is saturated, read p99 will push past the assumed 80 ms. The next step is a short-TTL merged cache for that heavy reader, not silently raising K to 200. A dead queue does not hide pages already pushed into the Timeline Store. If the celebrity log is down, either return the inbox and mark the page incomplete, or fail the whole page with 503. Pick one and write it as an assumption. This round picks 503 for the whole page, so a missing celebrity is not disguised as a complete timeline.

What to watch, and the weak recovery. Watch at least four series: 201 latency and errors, age of unpublished outbox rows, fan-out queue age, and post-cache hit rate plus singleflight wait. Watching only the 201 stays green while followers are minutes late. The weak recovery is “cache is down, so send every request to the database.” At a read peak of 11,575/s that saturates the Post Store and the Timeline Store together. A bypass needs singleflight, a limit, and a breaker. The other mistake fills the lag hole by querying every followee on the read path. That switches the system to pure pull for the duration of the outage and amplifies reads instead of isolating a write failure.

四个讲错的答案Four answers that do not hold

中文

把大 V 也 push 给每一个粉丝。1 × 1,000,000 = 1,000,000 次写入，约等于 432 秒的日均扇出预算，或 86 秒的全部峰值预算。多加消费者不改变行数，只改变这些行挤在多少台机器上。热点仍是那一条粉丝关联列表 [1]。大 V 的路径是 CelebLog 追加 1 行，读时 merge，全量 push 在图上是红色虚线。

在白板上把排序模型当成这道题的设计。关注流这一轮的顺序是 (created_at, post_id)。模型如果存在，也只能排已经合并好的 id，那是以后的读时步骤。现在画出召回和精排，扇出窗口、inbox 指针和删帖失效都还没被决定。面试官问 For You 时，说明范围之后回到 hybrid。

Timeline 就是一条 SQL。按关注集合 JOIN 帖子再 LIMIT，是纯 pull。2,315 次读/秒 × 200 个关注 = 463,000 次尾部探测/秒。OFFSET 还会在插入时让页漂移，深翻扫描变长 [5]。这道题的读是 inbox 加有上限的大 V 尾部，cursor 切页，再按 post id hydrate。

扇出要恰好一次。2011 年那篇日志论文给出的契约是至少一次，恰好一次通常意味着两阶段提交 [3]。帖子提交和每个 inbox 写不在同一个事务里。崩溃窗口由事务 outbox 补发填上，补发可能重复，重复被 sorted set 的成员更新吸收，同一 post_id 不会变成两行 [2]。把恰好一次放进热路径，会使 201 依赖队列和全部 inbox。

English

Push a celebrity to every follower. 1 × 1,000,000 = 1,000,000 writes, about 432 seconds of the average fan-out budget, or 86 seconds of the entire peak. More consumers do not change the row count. They only change how many machines the rows pile onto. The hot key is still that one follower association list [1]. The celebrity path appends one CelebLog row and merges on read. Full push stays the red dashed line.

Put a ranking model on the board and call it the design. The order in this round is (created_at, post_id). If a model exists later, it can only rank ids that are already merged. That is a future read-time step. Drawing retrieval and ranking now leaves the fan-out window, the inbox pointer, and delete invalidation undecided. If they ask about For You, restate the scope and return to hybrid.

The timeline is just a SQL select. Joining posts to the follow set and applying LIMIT is pure pull. 2,315 reads/s × 200 followees = 463,000 tail probes/s. OFFSET also drifts when rows are inserted, and deeper pages scan more [5]. The read in this design is the inbox plus a capped celebrity tail, a cursor cut, then hydrate by post id.

Fan-out must be exactly once. The 2011 log paper states an at-least-once contract. Exactly-once usually means two-phase commit [3]. The post commit and each inbox write are not one transaction. The crash window is closed by replaying the transactional outbox. Replay can duplicate. A sorted-set member update absorbs the duplicate, so the same post_id does not become two rows [2]. Putting exactly-once on the hot path makes the 201 depend on the queue and on every inbox.

收尾：三条边界，不说完美Close on three boundaries, not on perfect

中文

最后 20 秒：关注流，按时间。1,000,000 帖/天 × 200 粉丝 ≈ 2,315 次 inbox 写入/秒，所以普通人 push。1 × 1,000,000 约等于 432 秒的日均预算，所以大 V 只写 CelebLog，读时 merge。201 等的是 Post Store 加事务 outbox，不等扇出。Timeline 行是 post id、作者和时间，正文在 post:{id}，删帖 DEL 这把键。cursor 是 (时间, post_id)。至少一次，幂等追加。排序模型不在这张图上。

留下的边界也要说出口。新鲜度和确认被故意拆开，粉丝可以晚于作者几秒。大 V 的读放大用 K 封顶，K 用满以后要的是合并缓存，不是把阈值偷偷取消。缓存可以丢，权威在 Post Store，inbox 指针在 Timeline Store。这三件事讲清，比再加一个地域或一个模型更完整。

English

Last twenty seconds: follow feed, ordered by time. 1,000,000 posts/day × 200 followers is about 2,315 inbox writes/s, so ordinary users are pushed. 1 × 1,000,000 is about 432 seconds of the average budget, so a celebrity writes only the Celeb Log and readers merge. The 201 waits for the Post Store plus the transactional outbox, not for fan-out. A timeline row is post id, author, and time. The body lives at post:{id}. A delete removes that key. The cursor is (time, post_id). At-least-once, idempotent append. No ranking model on this picture.

Say the boundaries that remain. Freshness and ack are split on purpose. Followers can be seconds behind the author. Celebrity read amplification is capped by K. When K is saturated, the next step is a merged cache, not quietly deleting the threshold. The cache can be dropped. The body stays in the Post Store. Inbox pointers stay in the Timeline Store. Those three statements are more complete than adding a region or a model.
