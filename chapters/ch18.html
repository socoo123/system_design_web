设计电商订单系统 Design an e-commerce order system

约 40 分钟 · 前置：Ch03、Ch17 · 6 组演进图，25 帧 ~40 min · after Ch03, Ch17 · 6 evolution decks, 25 slides

这一章结束你能做什么 What you can do after this chapter

从一个订单请求画起，说清哪些事实一起提交，为什么响应丢了也不再占一份货。

沿着预留状态证明不超卖、不重复释放，并算出全站流量很低时为什么单个热门 SKU 仍然会排队。

把付款未知、重复通知和关单竞态串成一条可恢复路径：已关单收到钱就留下退款债务，履约事件通过 outbox 发出。

Start with one checkout request and identify the facts that commit together, including why a lost response never acquires another hold.

Use reservation state to prove no oversell or duplicate release, and calculate why one hot SKU can queue even under modest aggregate traffic.

Connect unknown payment outcomes, duplicate notifications, and closing races into a recoverable path: late money on a closed order creates a refund obligation, while an outbox carries fulfillment intent.

先把范围、单位与承诺写在白板上 Put scope, units, and promises on the board

我先设计普通电商商品单，单地域、单仓，订单和库存放同一个关系库。下单原子地占货并进入待支付，十五分钟未付尝试关单，付款成功消费预留。浏览器跳回不是支付证据，渠道超时也不是付款失败。付款与关单都争同一订单状态；关单先赢，迟到的钱记录为退款债务。副作用通过本地 outbox 投递，重复事件在消费端收住。

先问四个会改图的问题：买的是普通商品还是限量抢购，购物篮能否跨仓，用户多久必须付款，关闭后收到钱能否退款。本章采用单仓购物篮、多种 SKU 都能买、十五分钟付款窗口、迟到收款允许退款。多仓拆单会把一个提交拆成多份承诺，秒杀会改变热门行的容量；这两个条件没有出现时，先把一笔交易讲完整。

订单负责商品、数量、价格快照、收货信息和可执行的状态，不负责证明银行已经结算。钱的证据来自支付渠道，货的证据来自预留记录。订单行不能把这两份证据覆盖成一个真假字段。售后、分账、账本和配送路线分别留给 Ch24、Ch27；本章只把退款请求作为需要兑现的任务，不把退款申请说成用户已经收到钱。

白板上先写三个不变量：可售数量不能为负；每份预留只能消费或释放一次；同一订单只接受一个履约付款结果。最后一个条件仍允许发现两笔真实收款：第二笔不能再次发货，必须留下退款处理记录。把不变量写在 API 的输入旁边，比先列十个服务名更容易检验答案。

I design ordinary e-commerce in one region and one warehouse, keeping orders and inventory in one relational database. Checkout atomically holds stock and enters pending payment. Unpaid orders attempt closing after fifteen minutes; verified payment consumes the hold. A browser redirect is not payment evidence, and a provider timeout is not payment failure. Payment and close compete on one order state. If close wins, late money creates a refund obligation. Local outbox rows carry side effects, and consumers absorb duplicates.

Ask four questions that change the picture: ordinary goods or a limited sale, a basket spanning warehouses, the payment deadline, and whether late money can be refunded. This chapter chooses a multi-SKU basket in one warehouse, a fifteen-minute window, and refunding late captures. Multiple warehouses split one commit into several promises; a flash sale changes hot-row capacity. Without those constraints, first explain one transaction completely.

The order owns product, quantity, price snapshot, shipping details, and executable state. It does not prove bank settlement. Payment evidence belongs to the provider and stock evidence to reservations; one Boolean cannot replace both. After-sales, settlement, ledgers, and delivery routing belong to Ch24 and Ch27. Here, a refund request is an obligation to execute, not proof that the customer already received money.

Write three invariants: available stock never becomes negative; a reservation is consumed or released at most once; an order accepts only one payment for fulfillment. The last rule still allows discovering two real captures: the second cannot ship the order again and needs a refund record. Invariants beside API inputs are easier to test than a list of ten services.

同库下单：承诺从一次提交开始 Same-database checkout: the promise begins at commit

正常路径与幂等身份 Normal path and request identity

请求进 Order API 后，先鉴权并验证正整数数量，金额由服务端价格快照算成整数最小货币单位。把用户、币种、明细和地址版本做请求摘要，再用 (user_id, request_key) 的唯一约束认领这次操作。重复请求不是第二次购买；匹配摘要就返回原 order_id，摘要不同则报键冲突。用户真正想再买一次时需要新键，不能因为第一次网络慢就换键。

一个本地事务内写订单和明细、条件预扣每个 SKU、创建按订单归属的预留、写 outbox 和幂等结果。任一 SKU 缺货，已经改过的行也随整笔回滚，用户不会只买到购物篮的一半。数据库提交前不调用支付渠道，不让跨网络等待把库存行锁拖到两秒。提交后的 pending_pay 是「货已经给这单留好」，不是「后台以后试着抢」。

唯一约束在存储层裁决两个并发请求，应用里先查键不存在再插入不是裁决。PostgreSQL 18 文档说明 UNIQUE 约束约束列组合，而默认 NULL 不被当成相同值，因此幂等身份字段还必须非空 [2]。本章由此选择非空且按用户作用域的请求键；不能全站只按字符串去重，误把另一个用户的购物篮当成重放。

After authentication, validate strictly positive integer quantities and derive the amount from a server-side price snapshot in the smallest currency unit. Hash the user, currency, items, and address version, and claim the operation with a unique (user_id, request_key). Matching replays return the original order_id; changed payloads return a key conflict. A deliberate second purchase needs a new key, but a slow network does not.

One local transaction writes the order and items, conditionally reserves every SKU, creates owned reservations, writes the outbox, and saves the idempotent result. If any SKU is unavailable, earlier updates abort with the transaction rather than selling half the basket. Do not call the provider while this transaction holds inventory locks. Committed pending_pay means the basket already has stock, not that background work will try to obtain it.

Storage uniqueness arbitrates concurrent requests; an application check before insertion cannot do so. PostgreSQL 18 documents composite UNIQUE constraints and the default treatment of NULL as distinct, so identity fields must also be non-null [2]. We therefore use a non-null, user-scoped key instead of accidentally replaying another user’s basket.

为什么暂时保留一个主库 Why the initial design keeps one primary

A1 算出的平均是 1,000,000 ÷ 86,400 = 11.57 单/秒，峰值 ×10 = 115.74，约 116。A2 的成功付款峰值是 116 × 0.70 ≈ 81，按每笔两次通知是 162 次；A3 的未付关单峰值是 116 × 0.30 ≈ 35。因此仅这三种入口尝试就约 116 + 162 + 35 = 313 次/秒。重复通知也要验签和读写幂等记录，不能按「有效付款」八十次来买全部写路径。

这些数字没有证明某个数据库一定扛得住，只说明第一张图不需要先把库存拆成异步服务。单主仍要持久化、备份和故障切换；节点超时后暂停新下单，不用旧副本的库存余额继续卖。我的订单列表可以允许短暂旧数据，付款后详情用主库或带提交版本的读取；页面显示旧不等于允许用旧值作库存决策。读缓存不是预扣的权威。

A10 按 1,000,000 × 4 KB = 4 GB/天，乘 365 = 1.46 TB 原始订单，三份共 4.38 TB。这个数不包括日志、备份、图片和事件。先以保留期做归档与容量规划；是否分片要由压测、索引和存储预算决定。按用户分订单、按 SKU 分库存会把一单拆成跨分片事务，不能把「按 user_id 分库」写在图边上就假装原来的原子承诺还在。

A1 yields 1,000,000 / 86,400 = 11.57 creates/s on average and 115.74, about 116, at the 10× peak. A2 gives about 116 × 0.70 = 81 successful payments/s and 162 notification deliveries/s. A3 gives about 116 × 0.30 = 35 closes/s. These three entry attempts alone total roughly 116 + 162 + 35 = 313/s. Duplicate deliveries still require validation and idempotency work; eighty successful payments do not size the entire write path.

This does not prove any database’s capacity, but it does not force an asynchronous inventory service onto the first diagram. A primary still needs durable storage, backup, and failover. On primary uncertainty, stop new checkout rather than sell from an old replica balance. Order lists may tolerate short staleness, while post-payment details use the primary or a commit-version barrier. Stale display is not permission to make stale stock decisions; a read cache never owns reservation authority.

A10 gives 1,000,000 × 4 KB = 4 GB/day, times 365 = 1.46 TB raw orders, or 4.38 TB for three copies. Logs, backups, images, and events are outside this number. Archive by retention and decide sharding from measured throughput, indexes, and bytes. User-sharded orders and SKU-sharded stock make checkout cross-shard; writing user_id sharding beside the picture does not preserve the original atomic promise.

订单状态：合法边决定副作用的归属 Order state: legal edges own side effects

为什么少一个 created 状态 Why there is no separately visible created state

第一组的订单创建和预留在同库同事务，所以没有提交出来的 created 半成品。事务失败时整单不存在，成功时直接 pending_pay；失败记录可以存在请求日志，但不能让用户给没有库存承诺的订单付款。面试官要求拆服务时才补 creating，并等待库存明确确认后进入 pending_pay，这个边界在最后一组之后讨论。

主要边是 pending_pay → paid → shipped。取消到 cancelled，到期到 closed，两者保存不同原因但都有释放预留的义务。paid 后的用户退货走售后流程，不能用取消接口直接还 available：货可能已经打包甚至寄走，退款和重新上架也不同时发生。图里的终态是本章下单承诺的终态，不是说企业永远不能记录新的售后事实。

一次成功转换必须同时写状态版本和对应副作用。付款成功要消费这份 HELD，关单成功要释放它，paid → shipped 则消费履约证据而不再动预留。事件携带 order_id、事件身份、状态版本和目标动作；迟到的 pending 快照不能覆盖已经提交的 paid，收到低版本通知可以忽略投影更新，但仍记录处理结果供排查。

Checkout and its hold share one transaction, so no committed half-created state is exposed. An aborted transaction leaves no order; success exposes pending_pay. Failure may appear in request logs, but cannot invite payment for an order without a stock promise. Splitting services would add creating and wait for an explicit hold confirmation before becoming payable; the final section discusses that boundary.

The main path is pending_pay to paid to shipped. Cancellation enters cancelled and expiry enters closed, preserving different reasons while both owe a hold release. A return after paid belongs to after-sales; cancellation cannot immediately increase available when goods may be packed or shipped. These terminal states end this checkout promise, not the business’s ability to record later after-sales facts.

A successful transition commits a state version and its side effect together. Payment consumes HELD, closing releases it, and shipping consumes fulfillment evidence without touching the hold again. Events carry order_id, identity, state version, and action. A late pending snapshot cannot overwrite paid. Ignore older projection updates while retaining processing evidence for diagnosis.

条件更新不是先读后写 A conditional update is not read-then-write

白板写 UPDATE orders SET status=:to WHERE order_id=:id AND status=:from，但口述不要停在 CAS 这个缩写。两个写手都针对同一行，数据库把冲突排队，提交后的当前状态重新决定第二个写手是否还有资格。影响零行不是异常地继续下一步，而是先检查已提交状态，判断重放、合法冲突或者该做补偿。读取和副作用没有资格越过这个条件。

这里借 PostgreSQL 18 的 Read Committed 语义说明一条预先定位行的 UPDATE：并发更新提交后，会在更新版本上重新检查 WHERE [1]。这只支持图里的简单单行状态条件，不等于任意多行业务规则自动串行化。库存、订单和预留的关系仍由同一事务以及明确锁顺序维护；遇到死锁或序列化失败应重试整笔事务，不能只重试最后一条减库存。

替代方案是显式锁住订单行后检验合法边，再更新库存；它更便于一次核对多项条件，代价是锁持有时间必须短。条件更新能少一次查状态，但需要检查影响行数。二者都不能把网络调用放进持锁窗口。规则引擎可以描述状态边，却不会替你拿到原子提交、幂等身份或迟到收款的退款责任。

Write UPDATE orders SET status=:to WHERE order_id=:id AND status=:from, then explain more than the acronym CAS. Both writers target one row. The database arbitrates the conflict and the committed current state determines whether the second writer still qualifies. Zero affected rows means inspect committed state for replay, conflict, or compensation; it never authorizes proceeding to a side effect.

PostgreSQL 18’s Read Committed behavior illustrates the narrow case of an UPDATE targeting a known row: after a concurrent update commits, WHERE is re-evaluated against the updated version [1]. This supports the pictured single-row guard, not arbitrary multi-row serializability. One transaction and explicit lock ordering still preserve relations among order, inventory, and reservations. Deadlocks or serialization failures restart the whole transaction, not merely its last stock update.

An alternative explicitly locks the order row, checks the legal transition, then updates stock. This helps validate several conditions but requires short lock duration. Conditional updates can avoid a separate state read, provided the affected-row count is checked. Neither design includes provider calls while holding locks. A rules engine describes edges without supplying atomic commit, identity, or responsibility for late money.

库存：计数器防超卖，归属记录防重复释放 Inventory: counters prevent oversell; ownership prevents duplicate release

三种扣法分别承担什么风险 What each stock policy risks

本章选择预扣，是因为用户进入付款时已经得到商品数量的承诺。付款再扣可以减少未付占货，配合条件更新也能不让可售库存为负，但用户付完才发现无货，业务必须退款。下单直接记为售出则把未付也算销售，后续仍需要关单恢复。不存在适合所有商品的年份默认答案；保留时间、转化率和退款容忍度决定选哪个。

预扣的一条更新是 available=available-q, reserved=reserved+q WHERE available>=q。数量 q 必须是正整数，SKU 必须存在，影响零行就失败。多 SKU 全部按固定 SKU 顺序取锁，任一不足就回滚；否则两个购物篮以相反顺序抢两行，会互相等待。不能只检查一件热门货够了就先提交订单，剩余明细让后台补。

A9 从 A=10、R=0、S=0 开始，买三件后 A=7、R=3、S=0。付款消费预留变为 7、0、3，关单释放则变为 10、0、0，两条分支都满足 A + R + S = 10。这个守恒只在无补货无退货的教学窗口成立；真实系统增加进出库事件时要扩展账务口径，不能把永远等于十当成数据库约束。

We choose holds because entering payment already promises the basket quantity. Decrementing at payment reduces unpaid occupancy and can still guard against negative available stock, but a captured payment may discover no goods and require a refund. Booking stock as sold at checkout counts unpaid goods as sales and still needs reversal on close. No calendar year supplies a universal default; hold duration, conversion, and refund tolerance determine the choice.

The guarded update is available=available-q, reserved=reserved+q WHERE available>=q. q must be a positive integer, the SKU must exist, and zero affected rows means failure. Lock all SKUs in a fixed order and abort the basket if any is insufficient; opposite lock order can deadlock two baskets. Reserving one popular item does not authorize committing the order and obtaining the rest later.

A9 begins A=10, R=0, S=0. Holding three gives 7,3,0. Payment produces 7,0,3; closing produces 10,0,0. Both branches keep A + R + S = 10. This conservation holds only in the example without restock or returns. Actual inbound and return events change the accounting scope; perpetual equality to ten is not a real database constraint.

为什么 reserved 足够仍然会多还货 Why enough aggregate reserved stock can still cause double release

聚合 reserved 只表示所有订单共同占了多少，不能证明某一单还有三件。假设 order 42 已释放三件，order 43 还预留三件；重复释放 42 时，reserved ≥ 3 仍为真。如果据此再加 available，就把 43 的承诺还给别人。仅凭 reserved 条件无法防止重复释放：不出现负数也可能多卖。

因此持久预留按 (order_id, sku_id) 记录数量、状态和过期时间。终结时先争它自己的 HELD → COMMITTED 或 HELD → RELEASED，只有转换成功者修改聚合数。这个转换、订单终结和库存更新共用事务；重放发现已终结就不再加减。订单的状态条件加预留的状态条件分别守住业务和数量，不能只留下其中一个。

A11 假设热门行每次持锁五毫秒，串行上界 1 ÷ 0.005 = 200 次修改/秒，实际还要留队列余量。若 A1 的峰值全部打同一 SKU，预扣 116 加后续终结约 116，稳态合计约 232 次修改/秒，已经超过这个假设上界。全站只有百级下单，不代表一行没热点。先限制热门 SKU 的接入和未付占用，减少事务长度；库存分桶或专门抢购路径只有在重新定义分配规则后才进入下一轮。

Aggregate reserved measures all orders, not whether one order still owns three units. Suppose order 42 released three while order 43 still holds three. Retrying order 42 still passes reserved ≥ 3, and returning those units spends order 43’s promise. An aggregate guard misses this boundary: keeping counters nonnegative does not prevent selling stock twice.

Persist reservation quantity, state, and expiry under (order_id, sku_id). Finalization competes on its own HELD-to-COMMITTED or HELD-to-RELEASED transition; only the winner changes aggregate counters. Reservation transition, order finalization, and counter changes share a transaction. Replays of terminal reservations change nothing. The order guard protects business state, and the reservation guard protects quantity; keep both.

A11 assumes five milliseconds holding a hot-row lock: 1 / 0.005 = 200 mutations/s theoretically, before headroom for queueing. If all A1 peak traffic hits one SKU, 116 reserves plus roughly 116 later finalizations yield about 232 mutations/s at steady peak, above that assumed limit. A hundred creates/s globally does not eliminate hot rows. Limit hot-SKU admission and unpaid occupancy, and shorten transactions first. Buckets or a dedicated flash-sale path require redefining allocation rules in the next design round.

付款：未知结果、真实证据与三种身份 Payment: unknown results, real evidence, and distinct identities

两秒只是等待上限 Two seconds is only a waiting limit

A5 的两秒是用户同步等待预算，不是渠道一定成功或失败的期限。请求可能还没到 PSP，也可能 PSP 收了钱、回包却丢了。后者若立即创建新支付尝试，会向同一用户再收一次；前者若误标成功，会在无钱时发货。于是本地尝试保存 UNKNOWN，订单仍待支付，页面显示处理中，恢复器使用同一渠道对象查询。

Stripe 的低层错误说明，网络错误会让调用方不知道服务端是否收到请求，应复用幂等键和参数重试；服务端 500 也可能已有副作用 [5]。本章据此不把超时或单个 HTTP 状态当业务结论。A6 五秒后首次主动查单，之后退避查询并接收服务端事件；有些渠道异步支付需要更久，只能沿渠道支持的状态契约判断，不凭本地闹钟断定未收款。

每个 order_id 可以记录多个有序付款尝试，但前一笔 UNKNOWN 未解决时禁止用户无提示开启下一笔。允许换渠道必须先结束原尝试，或者承认可能多收并准备退款。渠道接口与本地记录之间没有跨系统事务；先记录稳定尝试再外调，让崩溃后能找回原对象，而不是声称本地数据库 COMMIT 能连 PSP 一起回滚。

A5’s two seconds is a synchronous user-wait budget, not the deadline by which a provider must succeed or fail. The request may never have arrived, or PSP may have captured money and lost its reply. Opening another attempt can charge again; assuming success can ship without payment. Persist UNKNOWN, leave the order pending, show processing, and recover the same provider object.

Stripe’s low-level errors explain that network failures can leave receipt uncertain, recommend reusing the same key and parameters, and warn that a server 500 may already have side effects [5]. Therefore a timeout or one HTTP status does not decide our business outcome. A6 begins an active query after five seconds, then backs off while accepting verified server events. Some asynchronous methods take longer; follow their supported status contract rather than a local timer.

An order can retain several ordered payment attempts, but an unresolved UNKNOWN attempt blocks a silent new attempt. Switching providers must first resolve the old attempt or explicitly accept possible duplicate capture and refund it. The provider and local database share no transaction. Persisting identity before the call makes the original object recoverable; a local COMMIT cannot roll PSP back.

重复通知与重复收款不是一回事 Duplicate delivery is not duplicate capture

至少分清三种身份：request_key 是一次下单；pay_attempt_id 是一次商户付款尝试，对外映射稳定渠道幂等键；provider_event_id 是一次通知事件。另外用 provider_payment_id 标记真实收款，按渠道和商户账户作用域唯一。一笔收款可能发多个事件，两个不同事件号不代表两笔钱；两个真实收款则不能只按 order_id 去重后丢掉第二笔。

Stripe 文档说明 webhook 可能重复、事件不保证顺序；相同事件按事件号识别，两个不同 Event 对象的业务重复还需结合对象身份和类型，执行动作前要验签 [4]。本章在这之上增加金额、币种、商户账户、订单归属和终态核对。只信浏览器 redirect 或未验签的 JSON，都会让一个展示结果直接变成发货许可。

若回调处理足够短，就在一个事务里保存已验真的收款证据、订单转换、预留转换和 outbox；提交后回 2xx。若选择异步处理，接收端必须先持久保存完整 inbox，提交后才确认，worker 再兑现状态。重复键只证明有记录，只有记录已处理或完整事件已经持久待处理，才可以成功确认；不能插入一条 event_id 后崩溃，重试命中唯一键就直接把后续工作吞掉。

Separate identities: request_key is a checkout; pay_attempt_id is a merchant payment attempt mapped to a stable provider idempotency key; provider_event_id is a notification. provider_payment_id identifies a real capture, scoped by provider and merchant account. Multiple events can describe one capture. Two real captures cannot be deduplicated only by order_id and discard the second.

Stripe documents duplicate webhooks, unordered delivery, event-ID deduplication, object-and-type handling for separate duplicate Event objects, and signature verification before acting [4]. We add amount, currency, merchant account, order ownership, and canonical-state checks. A browser redirect or unsigned JSON cannot authorize shipping.

If processing is short, one transaction records verified capture evidence, order transition, reservation transition, and outbox, then returns 2xx. An asynchronous receiver instead commits the complete durable inbox before acknowledgement, and a worker applies state later. An existing key just proves a record exists: success is safe only when already processed or fully persisted for processing. Inserting event_id and crashing cannot let a duplicate acknowledgement silently discard unfinished work.

幂等保留期不能替代业务记录 Provider retention cannot replace business identity

Stripe 的幂等接口保存首次状态码和响应，即使失败也重放；相同键换参数会报错，文档允许至少二十四小时后清理键 [3]。本章没有把二十四小时设成自己的订单或退款窗口，而是保留本地尝试、收款与补偿身份。渠道重试能力有限时，超过保留期先查原对象和业务记录，不能用原键盲目再创建。

Stripe’s idempotency API saves the first status and body even for failure, rejects changed parameters, and allows removing keys once they are at least twenty-four hours old [3]. We do not turn that retention into an order or refund deadline. Keep durable local attempt, capture, and compensation identities; beyond provider retention, inspect the original object and business record before creating anything again.

关单：到期触发与提交竞态分开处理 Closing: separate the timer trigger from the commit race

扫描保证还能找到到期单 A scan keeps expired work discoverable

expire_at 在创建事务里用统一的服务端时间确定，worker 比较数据库时间，不信浏览器倒计时。延迟消息能减少扫描发现等待，却可能重复、晚到或缺席。保留 (status, expire_at, order_id) 索引，每轮固定数据库 cutoff，按截止和游标连续取每批最多 500 个候选，直到本轮 cutoff 前的任务完成或明确留下可重试责任；六十秒是启动周期，不是每分钟只做一批。跨扫描进程与消息 worker 共用全层放行预算，前一轮未完由持久任务继续，不再重复启动全表扫描。

每个候选提交时仍须满足 pending_pay 且 expire_at 已过。每单独立事务，不锁五百单的大事务；支付、扫描与消息 worker 竞争时条件只允许一次释放。失败留下可重试记录，游标推进不删除责任，下一轮从最老未完成到期单找回。A7 新增的是教学容量目标：单单事务均值 ≤20 ms，至少 2 个在途可支撑 100 次尝试/s 的全层上限；全部入口共用它，另假设包含重试后的净有效关单能力 ≥70 单/s，须压测验证，不能由批大小推得。峰值约 35 单/s 每分钟新增 2,100，至少五批才覆盖新增；本目标净完成 4,200 单/分钟，健康的一分钟积压约 2,100÷70=30 秒处理完。最老到期年龄目标 ≤120 秒，发现、数据库争用和重试都算入；超过则告警并限制新增预留。若扫描停止十分钟且峰值稳定，积压约 35×600=21,000，恢复后净追赶 70−35=35 单/s，约 600 秒追平；不是 21,000÷70=300 秒。已实际支付的候选由条件排除，不当作关单成功。

A4 把占用成本算出来：峰值连续十五分钟、所有订单都不付款，116 × 900 = 104,400 份在途预留。若每单都像 A9 买三件，最多占 313,200 件，这是同一保守窗口的假设，不是库里真有这么多货。库存不足会更早拒绝新单；限制同用户未付次数和热门商品数量，防止少量账户把货长时间占住。过期扫描不能恢复未到期的合法承诺。

Set expire_at using authoritative server time inside checkout; workers compare database time. Delayed messages reduce discovery delay but may repeat, arrive late, or disappear. Index (status, expire_at, order_id). Fix a database cutoff each round, continuously page up to 500 candidates at a time, and finish work before that cutoff or retain explicit retry responsibility. Sixty seconds is a round-start interval, not one batch per minute. Scanners and message workers share global admission; unfinished rounds continue as durable work rather than launching overlapping full scans.

Each candidate must still be pending and expired at commit. Use one transaction per order, never a five-hundred-order lock transaction. Payment, scan, and message workers share the guard, allowing one release. Failures retain retry responsibility after pagination and are rediscovered from oldest unfinished expiry. A7 adds teaching capacity targets: mean transaction ≤20 ms and at least two in flight support a global ceiling of 100 attempts/s, shared by every trigger. Net valid completion after retries must reach 70 orders/s, verified by load tests rather than batch size. At 35 new expiries/s, 2,100 arrive per minute, requiring at least five batches just to cover arrivals. The target completes 4,200/min; a healthy minute’s backlog takes about 2,100/70=30 s. Oldest-expired age targets ≤120 s including discovery, contention, and retries; alert and restrict new holds when exceeded. A ten-minute scan outage at steady peak leaves 35×600=21,000 orders. Recovery drains at net 70−35=35/s while arrivals continue, taking about 600 s, rather than 21,000/70=300 s. Already-paid candidates are excluded by the guard, not counted as successful closes.

A4 exposes occupancy: a sustained fifteen-minute peak with no payments gives 116 × 900 = 104,400 active holds. If every basket bought A9’s three units, that bounds occupancy at 313,200 units in the same conservative window, not actual available stock. Insufficient inventory rejects demand earlier. Bound unpaid orders per user and hot-item quantity to resist stock hoarding; a scan cannot revoke valid holds before expiry.

两个提交顺序，两份完整责任 Two commit orders and their complete obligations

图固定演示关单先提交：订单 closed，自己的预留 RELEASED，可售数量加回。稍后收到已验真的收款，先保存付款事实，再尝试从 pending_pay 改 paid，结果为零行。此时不发货、不重新抢库存、不把 closed 改回 paid；同一事务写 refund_due 和退款 outbox，退款任务用固定补偿身份重试，并记录渠道退款的最终结果。

相反顺序也要能讲：付款事务先 paid 并消费预留，关单的条件更新为零行，就不释放任何库存。用户取消使用相同裁决，仅允许 pending；已支付取消转售后申请。不能把「超过十五分钟」当成必须覆盖已支付状态的理由。到期时正在 UNKNOWN 的付款也可能已收款，本章选关单可先完成、迟到收款退款的业务政策，其代价是支付显示与退款通知可能短暂不同步。

这个政策还意味着保存收款证据的事务不能因 paid 的 CAS 失败一起回滚。应在锁住的当前订单状态下选择消费预留或产生退款责任，二者只有一个成立；收款事实、选定责任和 outbox 原子提交。把 CAS 失败当错误直接返回而没持久记账，渠道重试耗尽后就会留下「钱到了但没有人负责」的订单。

补偿是新的业务动作，不能抹掉原来的收款。微软的补偿事务文档指出，恢复原快照可能覆盖并发工作，补偿可能失败，需要可恢复且幂等的步骤 [7]。本章的 refund_due 因此持续存在到兑现或人工处理；渠道不可用时不宣称已经退款。退款余额、结算批次和账本口径在 Ch24 展开，这里只保证责任可找、动作可重试。

The pictured order is close first: closed, its reservation RELEASED, and stock returned. Later verified capture evidence is persisted, then the pending-to-paid transition changes zero rows. Do not ship, reacquire stock, or revive closed. The same transaction records refund_due and refund outbox. A fixed compensation identity makes the refund retryable, and its eventual provider result is retained.

Explain the reverse: payment commits paid and consumes the hold first. Closing changes zero rows and releases nothing. User cancellation uses the same arbitration and only accepts pending; paid cancellations enter after-sales. Fifteen minutes passing never authorizes overwriting paid. A payment still UNKNOWN at expiry may already be captured. We choose a policy allowing close to finish and refunding late money, accepting temporary disagreement between payment display and refund notification.

Therefore a failed paid guard must not abort the capture evidence. Under the locked current order state, select either hold consumption or a refund obligation, never both. Capture fact, selected obligation, and outbox commit atomically. Returning an error on failed CAS without recording responsibility can leave captured money unowned after provider retries end.

Compensation is a new business action, not erasing the capture. Microsoft’s pattern warns that restoring an old snapshot can overwrite concurrent work and that compensation can fail, requiring resumable and idempotent steps [7]. refund_due remains until fulfilled or explicitly handled. Provider failure never becomes a false refund-success claim. Ch24 expands balances, settlement, and ledger scope; here responsibility must be discoverable and retryable.

Outbox：提交后还能推进，重复后仍然只落一份任务 Outbox: durable progress after commit, one task after duplicates

谁负责把已提交意图送出去 Who publishes committed intent

订单付款事务把「需要履约」写进同库 outbox，API 返回只依赖这次持久提交。relay 读取已提交行并发送给 Queue，broker 确认后再标记已发送。AWS 的原文用这种同事务 outbox 消除数据库和消息的双写，并提醒消费者处理重复事件 [6]。图上没有 API 直接 enqueue，因为那会重新打开提交和投递之间的故障缝隙。

relay 在发送前崩溃，下一次还能读到未发送行；发送已成功但标记前崩溃，下一次可能重复投递。先标已发再发送则有永久漏发窗口。标记已发也只代表 broker 接了，不能代表履约完成。需要分别看未投递 outbox 年龄、队列待处理年龄和履约任务年龄，三个指标对应三位负责人，不用一个绿色消息数掩盖后面的卡住。

worker 收到事件后，在 Fulfillment DB 同事务插入 inbox 身份和唯一履约任务，然后确认 Queue。事务提交前崩溃会重来，提交后确认丢失也会重来，但唯一任务不会重复。图承诺的是一次持久任务，不是任意外部发货 RPC 自动 exactly-once；真正调用仓库接口时还要传稳定 shipment_id，并在未知结果后查询同一任务。

The payment transaction records fulfillment intent in the local outbox; API acknowledgement depends on that durable commit. Relay reads committed rows, sends to Queue, and marks sent after broker acknowledgement. AWS describes the same-transaction outbox to remove database/message dual writes and requires duplicate-safe consumption [6]. Order API does not enqueue directly because that reopens the commit/publication gap.

A crash before sending leaves the row discoverable; a successful send followed by a crash before marking can repeat publication. Marking before sending can lose it forever. Sent means accepted by the broker, not completed fulfillment. Monitor unpublished-outbox age, queue waiting age, and fulfillment-job age separately: three owners and three possible stalls.

Worker atomically inserts inbox identity and a unique fulfillment job in Fulfillment DB before acknowledging Queue. A crash before commit retries; acknowledgement loss after commit retries too, without another job. The promise is one durable job, not automatically exactly-once external shipping. A warehouse call still needs stable shipment_id and same-task query recovery after uncertain results.

积压的除法决定何时恢复 Backlog arithmetic sets recovery time

A8 用每单十条事件做保守容量口径，覆盖订单转换及后续任务，而不是声称每单生产上固定发十条。峰值 116 × 10 = 1,160 条/秒。暂停一百二十秒，积压 1,160 × 120 = 139,200 条。恢复时每秒处理两千条，但新事件继续到达，净排空是 2,000 − 1,160 = 840 条/秒，139,200 ÷ 840 ≈ 165.7 秒。旧单到恢复完成可能等 120 + 166 ≈ 286 秒。

这五分钟量级若业务不能接受，需要增加净消费能力、给履约和退款分开预算或拒绝部分新需求；直接把积压除以两千得到约七十秒，漏掉了持续到达的工作。重试要有退避和上限，持久责任不能因达到重试次数就删除。坏事件进入隔离队列并保留原身份，修好后重放，不能让一个字段错误堵住全部订单。

A12 假设每事件一 KB，按每天 1,000,000 × 10 = 10,000,000 条，约十 GB/天；已完成保留七天是七十 GB，三份二百一十 GB。未完成 outbox、退款责任和待处理 inbox 不按年龄直接删；完成记录的清理窗口还要覆盖允许重放的期限。存储只是容量预算，不能拿清理策略消掉幂等所需的历史。

A8 budgets ten events/order conservatively for state changes and subsequent tasks, not a production claim of exactly ten. Peak is 116 × 10 = 1,160 events/s. A 120-second stop accumulates 1,160 × 120 = 139,200. Recovery handles 2,000/s while arrivals continue, so net drain is 2,000 − 1,160 = 840/s. 139,200 / 840 ≈ 165.7 seconds, leaving the oldest affected work about 120 + 166 ≈ 286 seconds late.

If that nearly five-minute window is unacceptable, increase net processing capacity, separate refund and fulfillment budgets, or reject some new demand. Dividing backlog by 2,000 gives roughly seventy seconds but forgets arriving work. Retries back off and have operational bounds, while durable obligations survive exhausted retries. Quarantine malformed events with their identities and replay after repair instead of blocking every order.

A12 assumes 1 KB/event and 1,000,000 × 10 = 10,000,000 events/day, around 10 GB/day. Seven days of completed history is 70 GB, or 210 GB for three copies. Unfinished outbox, refunds, and inbox work cannot simply age out. Retain completed identities across the supported replay window; storage budgeting cannot erase history required for idempotency.

什么时候才把库存拆出去 When inventory actually moves to another service

只有多仓所有权、独立扩容或组织边界逼出拆分时，才承认跨库没有原子的订单加库存提交。此时订单先 creating，订单本地事务写预留命令的 outbox；库存服务按 order_id 认领预留，确认后订单才能 pending_pay。超时取消要留下持久取消意图，晚到的预留成功仍必须识别该意图并释放，不能把已关单重新推进待付。

这是一组可重试的前进动作和补偿动作，各自只在自己的库提交，不是把 Saga 的名字写上去就全局回滚。库存占位失败，订单结束且不允许付款；付款证据在关闭后出现，退款而非复活；补偿暂时失败，暴露未完成责任并继续恢复。若业务不能接受这个窗口，就保留同库或选择有明确阻塞代价的协调事务。先把原子方案讲对，再按新约束付出这份复杂度。

Move inventory only when warehouse ownership, independent scaling, or organizational boundaries force it. Cross-database order creation and stock reservation are no longer one atomic commit. Start the order as creating and write its reservation command in a local outbox. Inventory claims the hold by order_id; only confirmed reservation permits pending_pay. Timeout cancellation leaves a durable cancellation intent, and a late successful reservation must release against that intent rather than revive the order.

These are retryable forward and compensating actions, each committing in its own database, not a global rollback acquired by naming Saga. Reservation failure ends the order without payment. Payment after close refunds instead of reviving. Failed compensation exposes responsibility and continues recovery. If the business cannot tolerate that window, retain the shared database or choose coordination with explicit blocking costs. Explain the atomic baseline before paying for newly required complexity.

三个追问，逐个沿路径回答 Three follow-ups, each answered on the path

「主库故障能继续卖吗？」能读允许旧的订单列表，不应拿旧库存余额接预扣。先恢复权威写和幂等历史，未确认创建让用户处理中或重试原键。故障切换可能丢失已确认提交时，必须先说明复制与持久化承诺，不能用副本这个组件名保证零丢失。

「服务端回调永远没到呢？」主动查询已知渠道对象，并用收款证据补齐同一状态转换；定期核对渠道成功但订单未 paid、订单 paid 但预留未消费、closed 但有未完成退款。检查结果转成带身份的修复任务，不用批量脚本无条件 SET paid，避免把已经关单的货再承诺一次。

「退款任务一直失败怎么办？」订单保留 closed，用户看到退款处理中，任务保留原因、最后一次尝试和下一次执行时间，超过恢复预算告警并人工介入。责任不会因为重试次数耗尽而消失，客服和对账能找到同一收款及其补偿。这才是最终一致中的「最终」如何被推进。

“Can we keep selling after primary loss?” Stale order-list reads may continue, but old stock balances cannot acquire new holds. Restore authoritative writes and identity history. Unconfirmed checkout shows processing or retries its original key. If failover can lose acknowledged commits, state the durability and replication contract instead of promising zero loss just because a replica exists.

“What if the callback never arrives?” Query the known provider object and apply the same transition from canonical evidence. Reconcile provider success without paid state, paid without consumed hold, and closed with unfinished refunds. Produce identified repair tasks rather than unconditional bulk SET paid that promises returned stock again.

“What if the refund keeps failing?” Keep closed, display refund processing, and retain cause, last attempt, and next execution time. Alert and involve an operator beyond the recovery budget. Responsibility survives exhausted retries, and support can locate the capture and compensation. That is how eventual consistency actually progresses.

收尾二十秒：一次同库提交把幂等身份、订单、预留和 outbox 连起来。库存条件挡负数，按订单的 HELD 转换挡重复释放。付款超时保留未知，验真的渠道证据才改单。付款和关单在同一行裁决；关单先赢就留下退款债务，付款先赢就不释放。relay 可以重复，消费端同事务落去重与履约任务。普通负载先不拆库存，热点和多仓再改承诺。

接着读 Ch19 时，问题换成附近哪些点值得查；本章留下的是交易写入边界。需要展开峰值限流去 Ch04，库存抢购去 Ch21，支付与账本去 Ch24，异步事务与补偿去 Ch41。能画对六张最后一帧并说出每个失败由谁接住，再去增加服务。

Twenty-second close: one local commit ties request identity, order, hold, and outbox together. Guarded counters prevent negative stock; the owned HELD transition prevents duplicate release. Payment timeouts remain unknown until verified provider evidence arrives. Payment and close arbitrate on one row: close first owes a refund; paid first releases nothing. Relay can repeat, while consumers atomically create inbox identity and fulfillment task. Ordinary load keeps inventory local; hot items and warehouses change the promise later.

Ch19 moves to nearby-point queries; this chapter leaves a transactional write boundary. Ch04 expands admission, Ch21 flash-sale inventory, Ch24 payment and ledgers, and Ch41 asynchronous transactions and compensation. Be able to draw each final frame and name who absorbs every failure before adding services.
