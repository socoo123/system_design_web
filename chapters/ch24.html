支付系统Payment system

约 40 分钟 · 前置：Ch18、Ch20、Ch41 · 用 6 组、30 帧图追踪同一笔付款~40 min · after Ch18, Ch20, Ch41 · 6 decks / 30 frames tracing one payment

本章要点：保存付款意图，保留未知结果，按资金效果入账Core: preserve intent, retain uncertainty, post each effect

设计支付系统时，要分清一次 API 请求、一次渠道付款尝试，以及一次收款或退款产生的资金效果。读完本章，你应该能说清楚：系统分别用什么键识别它们；渠道已经扣款但回复丢失时，谁负责恢复；账本、退款额度和通知又分别在哪个提交点成立。

Answer three questions: which keys identify a request, a payment attempt, and a financial effect? Who recovers a lost reply after a charge? At which commits do the journal, refund capacity, and notifications become valid?

本章沿着付款意图（payment）→ 渠道尝试 a7（attempt）→ 渠道证据 → 入账处理（Posting）→ 订单服务（Order）展开。六组图分别展示其中的机制，配套说明关键实现，以及进程崩溃后可以依靠哪些持久记录继续处理。

Follow payment intent → attempt a7 → provider evidence → Posting → Order. Six existing decks remain, with the implementation and crash-recovery records beside each mechanism.

先约定范围，再分清三种成功与处理规模Define three successes and scale

本例设计商户收款：用户在支付服务提供商（PSP）托管的页面付款，只使用人民币 CNY，每笔付款只做一次完整收款（capture），之后可以多次部分退款。服务先持久保存受理记录，再调用渠道；调用超时就保留结果未知（UNKNOWN）。经验证的渠道结果统一进入入账入口，入账后通过待发送记录（outbox）通知订单，再用渠道查询和结算对账处理尚未确认的结果。

Design merchant pay-in: PSP-hosted collection, CNY, one full capture, and multiple partial refunds. Persist acceptance before calling the provider, retain UNKNOWN on timeout, route verified results through one Posting gate, notify orders through outbox, and resolve gaps with queries and settlement reconciliation.

本例假设每天创建 100 万笔付款，其中 70% 成功，每笔成功付款平均收到两次回调，峰值流量为平均值的 10 倍。本地受理的 p99 延迟目标为 300 ms，等待渠道回复的预算为 2 s。这些数字都是教学假设。钱包双花、换汇、出款与争议处理暂不展开；订单关闭与付款之间的竞争见 Ch18。这里的 payment 表示本地业务中的付款意图，不一定对应某家 PSP 的同名对象。

All scale values are teaching assumptions: 1,000,000 creates/day, 70% success, two receipts per success, 10× peak, 300 ms local-acceptance p99 target, and 2 s channel wait budget. Wallet double-spend, FX, payouts, and disputes are outside scope; Ch18 owns order-close races. Our payment is a local domain intent, not necessarily a provider object of the same name.

展开容量算式、持久性条件与记录保留范围Capacity arithmetic, durability, and retention

按 10 倍峰值估算，创建付款的速率为 1,000,000 ÷ 86,400 × 10 ≈ 115.74 次/秒，讲解时可取整为 116 次/秒；成功收款约为 700,000 ÷ 86,400 × 10 ≈ 81 次/秒；接收回调约为 1,400,000 ÷ 86,400 × 10 ≈ 162 次/秒。这三项对应不同操作，估算时不能混用各自的 TPS。先测量一套关系数据库在这些事务混合运行时能承受的负载，再决定是否分片；同一笔付款的渠道尝试、账本流水和退款记录应放在同一个财务分区。

Creates: 1,000,000/86,400×10≈115.74/s, rounded to 116; successful captures: 700,000/86,400×10≈81/s; receipts: 1,400,000/86,400×10≈162/s. These are different rates. Measure mixed transactions on one relational authority before sharding; colocate a payment’s attempts, journal, and refunds in one financial partition.

假设后台处理程序每秒能处理 500 次回调，按取整后的每秒 162 次接收速率估算，停机十分钟会积压约 162 × 600 = 97,200 次。恢复后，如果新回调仍以该速率到来，处理能力也仍为每秒 500 次，净消化能力就是 500 − 162 = 338 次/秒，约 288 秒可以追平。这依赖取整和持续速率假设，不能当作最坏情况下的上界。收件箱（inbox）去重可能减少工作量，额外查询和锁争用也可能降低处理能力。

如果另假设 0.1% 的创单结果未决，每天就有 1,000 笔需要继续核查。应跟踪未决笔数、金额和最久等待时间；等待变长可以触发核查，不能自动把付款改成失败。

At an assumed 500 receipts/s worker budget, a nominal 162 receipts/s gives an estimated 162×600=97,200 backlog after ten minutes. If arrivals sustain that nominal rate and workers retain 500/s capacity, spare capacity is about 338/s and drain time about 288 s. These rounded sustained-rate assumptions are not hard bounds. Inbox dedupe can reduce work, while queries and locks can lower capacity. Assumed 0.1% uncertain creates means 1,000/day; track count, amount, and oldest age without turning age into failure.

按十进制单位估算存储：每条付款记录 1 KB，每笔成功收款有两条分录、每条 200 B，每次回调投递的审计记录为 1 KB。按上述日流量计算，一年分别需要 365 GB、102.2 GB 和 511 GB，合计 978.2 GB，保存三份则为 2.9346 TB。这里只计算三类原始记录，渠道尝试、索引、WAL、备份、手续费、退款及更长保留期所需空间都要另算。511 GB 是按上述平均投递次数估算的投递审计量，不是去重后 inbox 的行数；365 天也只是本例的容量假设。

With decimal units, a 1 KB payment, two 200 B capture entries, and 1 KB per-delivery audit yield 365 GB, 102.2 GB, and 511 GB/year; total 978.2 GB, or 2.9346 TB for three copies. This excludes attempts, indexes, WAL, backups, fees, refunds, and longer retention. 511 GB estimates per-delivery audit storage under the assumed average rate, not deduplicated inbox rows; 365 days is only a planning assumption.

这里假设本地响应缓存保留 24 h，但缓存寿命不能决定付款身份的寿命。请求身份记录或用于拒绝旧键的墓碑记录、付款意图、结果未决的渠道尝试和资金效果键，都必须保留到足以覆盖业务重试与审计的范围。缓存清空不能成为再次扣款的理由。过期请求应先查询原结果或被拒绝；即使调用方换了请求头中的键，也仍要找到同一个业务意图。

24 h is only a local response-cache assumption. Request identities or old-key rejection tombstones, payments, uncertain attempts, and effect keys must cover business retry/audit boundaries; cache expiry cannot authorize another charge. Expired requests query or reject, while changed headers still resolve the same business intent.

本章假设财务事务返回 COMMIT 时，本地 WAL 与指定同步副本都已持久落盘。在 PostgreSQL 18 中，这依赖 fsync、同步副本配置以及 synchronous_commit=on 等条件，不能只凭“有三个副本”就认定已满足，也不能据此认定副本已经能查到提交。切换主节点时，要阻止旧主继续写入，并且只提升保存了已确认提交的节点；无法确认时，先暂停财务写入。[10]

Financial COMMIT assumes durable local WAL and designated synchronous standby flush. In PostgreSQL 18 this requires the corresponding fsync, synchronous-standby, and synchronous_commit=on configuration. Three copies alone do not provide it or guarantee standby query visibility. Fence the old primary and promote only a node carrying acknowledged commits; pause financial writes if that cannot be established. [10]

1 · 先保存付款意图，再明确每种身份的唯一范围1 · Persist intent and scope each identity

服务先把付款意图、渠道尝试和渠道命令一起提交，再调用 PSP。 第 1 组图的第 2 帧标出这个本地事务 T1，第 3 帧才开始调用外部渠道。后台付款程序（Pay worker）负责恢复同一次尝试 a7，不会因为任务重跑就创建新的付款。

Commit payment, attempt, and channel command before touching the PSP. T1 in deck 1 frame 2 is the local boundary; frame 3 crosses it. Pay worker recovers a7 rather than creating a new payment.

用唯一约束处理并发竞争，再核对请求摘要Let UNIQUE arbitrate races, then compare digests

-- All scope/key columns in schema.sql are NOT NULL.
CREATE UNIQUE INDEX intent_identity
  ON payment(environment, merchant_id, order_ref, business_version);
CREATE UNIQUE INDEX request_identity
  ON request_key(environment, merchant_id, operation, request_key);
CREATE UNIQUE INDEX channel_command_identity
  ON attempt(environment, channel, account_id, channel_key);
CREATE UNIQUE INDEX receipt_identity
  ON inbox(environment, channel, account_id, event_id);
CREATE UNIQUE INDEX financial_effect_identity
  ON posting(environment, channel, account_id, object_id, kind);
-- One outbox event_id is reused on every delivery.
-- Cross-row journal balance is checked by the posting transaction,
-- not by a row CHECK, and these indexes do not dedupe PSP effects.
createPayment(authenticatedMerchant, key, body):
  quote = verifyOrderAndPrice(authenticatedMerchant, body.order)
  require body.businessVersion == quote.authorizedBusinessVersion else CONFLICT
  require body.method == quote.authorizedMethod else CONFLICT
  digest = canonicalHash(quote.orderRef, quote.amount, quote.currency,
                         quote.authorizedMethod, quote.authorizedBusinessVersion)
  begin transaction
    old = findRequest(merchantScope, "CREATE", key)
    if old exists:
      require old.digest == digest else CONFLICT
      commit; return accepted(old.payNo)
    // UNIQUE arbitrates both request-key and business-intent races.
    payment = insertOrReadIntent(merchantScope, quote.orderRef,
                                quote.authorizedBusinessVersion, quote.amount, quote.currency)
    require payment.immutableDigest == digest else CONFLICT
    if payment was newly inserted:
      a7 = insertAttempt(payment, chosenChannelAccount, freeze(quote))
      insertOutbox("channel:" + a7, command(a7))
    insertRequest(merchantScope, "CREATE", key, digest, payment.payNo)
  commit
  return 202(payment.payNo)  // acceptance, not capture
on relevant UNIQUE conflict:
  rollback; restart transaction and compare the winning record
on lost commit response:
  resolve by the same key/intent on the authoritative DB
  // Never generate a different intent to escape uncertainty.

调用方首先要通过商户鉴权。服务端的 verifyOrderAndPrice 返回不可变报价，其中包含经授权的业务版本 authorizedBusinessVersion 和支付方式 authorizedMethod。服务先核对请求体中的版本与支付方式，再只根据这份报价生成意图键、请求摘要和固定的渠道参数。因此，浏览器把版本从 1 改成 2，会在写库前被判定冲突，不能借此创建第二个付款意图。

insertOrReadIntent 依靠非空字段上的唯一约束（UNIQUE）处理并发竞争。发生冲突后，要回滚整个事务，重新开始事务，读取成功写入的记录并比较摘要。PostgreSQL 的事务一旦因唯一冲突而中止，就不能继续在其中执行 SELECT。这里仅重试请求身份和业务意图这两类已识别的冲突，其他约束错误仍应报出。[6]

Authenticate the merchant. verifyOrderAndPrice returns an immutable server quote with authorizedBusinessVersion/authorizedMethod. Compare the body’s version/method at the call site, then derive intent identity, digest, and frozen provider parameters only from quote facts. Changing body version 1 to 2 conflicts before writing instead of creating another intent. insertOrReadIntent uses non-null UNIQUE constraints; after conflict, roll back, restart, and compare the winner. A PostgreSQL transaction aborted by a unique violation cannot simply continue with SELECT. Retry only these identified identity conflicts, not arbitrary constraint errors. [6]

受理响应丢失后，调用方应沿用原键查询并确认原提交；响应缓存过期也不会抹掉付款意图。换一个请求键不能授权另一笔付款。Outbox 把本地付款意图和待发送命令放进同一个 T1 事务，但 PSP 的资金动作仍在事务之外，所以转发或处理程序重复执行时，仍要遵守渠道的幂等契约。也可以选择同步调用渠道，但同样要先保存尝试记录，并把渠道的尾延迟计入等待成本。[8]

Resolve a lost acceptance reply through the same identity. Cache expiry never erases the intent, and a new key cannot authorize another payment. Outbox binds local intent and command in T1; PSP effects remain outside it, requiring the provider contract on repeats. Synchronous calling is an alternative only after persisting the attempt and accepting provider tail latency. [8]

2 · UNKNOWN 表示结果尚不确定，不能当作扣款失败2 · UNKNOWN is uncertainty, not a failed charge

后台程序先持久保存 SENT，再发起外部 I/O；迟到的超时结果只能更新仍未决的尝试。 第 2 组图展示的是本地对结果的认知如何变化，箭头不代表渠道执行操作的顺序。收款成功的证据统一交给 Posting 处理，浏览器跳转到成功页不能改变财务状态。

Persist SENT before I/O; a late timeout may update only an unresolved attempt. Deck 2 shows knowledge transitions, not provider execution order. Successful evidence enters Posting; browser redirects cannot authorize financial transitions.

dispatchCommittedAttempt(a7):
  begin transaction
    a = lockAttempt(a7)
    if a.state in {CONFIRMED, DECLINED}: commit; return
    if a.firstSendAt is null:
      a.firstSendAt = databaseNow()
      a.retryBefore = a.firstSendAt + adapter.safeReplayWindow
    if databaseNow() >= a.retryBefore:
      commit; enqueueResolver(a7); return
    a.state = SENT  // commit BEFORE I/O: the request may leave
    request = a.frozenPayload; key = a.channelKey
  commit
  // Adapter must enforce send deadline and account/operation contract.
  // DB/outbox cannot fence a stale sender at the PSP.
  result = adapter.send(request, key, deadline=a.retryBefore)
  if result is authenticated captured evidence:
    postCapture(result)  // same gate as webhook/reconciliation
  else:
    begin transaction
      a = lockAttempt(a7)
      if a.state in {CONFIRMED, DECLINED}: commit; return
      if result proves final no-charge AND no future execution:
        a.state = DECLINED
      else:
        a.state = UNKNOWN  // includes timeouts and ambiguous 5xx
      saveEvidence(result)
    commit
  // A new attempt requires separate authorization and an irrevocably
  // non-chargeable old command/object; elapsed time is not that proof.

首次发送时间 firstSendAt 和重试截止时间 retryBefore 都只设置一次，后续重试不能延长截止时间。示例把处理中（pending）、网络中断和无法判定结果的 5xx 都保留为 UNKNOWN；只有渠道明确证明未扣款且以后也不会执行，才记为 DECLINED。如果并发回调已经把 a7 确认为 CONFIRMED，旧后台程序的超时结果就不能再将它改回 UNKNOWN。为此，程序必须在持有数据库锁时比较当前状态，不能仅按最后写入时间决定结果。

Set firstSendAt and retryBefore once; retries never extend them. This model keeps pending, disconnections, and ambiguous 5xx as UNKNOWN; DECLINED requires proof of no charge and no future execution. If a concurrent webhook already confirmed a7, a stale worker timeout cannot overwrite it. Compare state under the DB lock instead of trusting last-write timestamps.

本章引用的是 Stripe API v1 的行为：操作开始执行后，渠道会保存首次响应，包括 500，并可能在至少 24 h 后清理幂等键。官方错误处理说明建议，只在原请求后的前 24 h 内使用同键、同参数重试；超过窗口仍无法确认结果，就停止重复发送并核实原结果。API v2 的重放行为和时间窗口不同，这条规则也不能直接套用到其他渠道。[1] [2]

The cited Stripe API v1 retains the first executed response, including 500, and may prune keys after at least 24 h. Its error guide limits same-key/parameter retries to the first 24 h, then requires reconciliation if unresolved. API v2 has different replay behavior and a different window; do not generalize this v1 rule to v2 or other providers. [1] [2]

本地设置发送截止时间，不能阻止 PSP 执行过时请求。 即使发送前检查通过，后台进程也可能暂停很久，或者请求长时间停留在网络中，之后才到达渠道。代码中的安全重放窗口 safeReplayWindow，依赖渠道保证、时钟裕量，以及调度和传输延迟上界同时成立。无法确认这些条件时，只查询已经绑定的对象，或保留待核查状态（Review），不自动重新发送创建请求。应优先依靠渠道端稳定的对象和状态约束来阻止重复扣款；本地租约本身无法保证任意故障下业务恰好执行一次。

A local deadline does not fence the PSP. A pre-send check cannot stop a suspended worker or a delayed in-flight request arriving later. safeReplayWindow is conditional on provider guarantees, clock margin, and scheduling/transport bounds. Without those bounds, query the bound object or retain Review rather than automatically replay create. Prefer provider-enforced stable-object/state constraints; a local lease does not establish exactly-once across arbitrary failures.

Resolver 已知对象 ID 时，就查询原对象。如果创建回复丢失、尚未拿到 ID，只能使用经过验证的商户引用查询、有效窗口内的同键结果恢复，或经认证的回调、账单关联来查明结果。查不到对象、查询失败或等待过久，都不能证明没有扣款。创建新尝试还需要单独授权，并确认旧对象或命令已无法再执行；只要旧后台程序仍可能完成扣款，就不能自动切换渠道。

Resolver queries the original object when its ID is known. After a lost create reply, use only a verified merchant-reference lookup, valid same-key recovery, or authenticated callback/report correlation. Missing objects, failed queries, and elapsed time do not prove no charge. A new attempt requires separate authorization and a non-executable old command/object; never switch providers while an old sender could still charge.

3 · 先持久保存回调，再处理资金效果3 · Receive webhooks durably before applying effects

回调的 ACK 表示系统已持久接管事件，不表示已经入账。 第 3 组图先保存事件，再由 Posting 根据收款或退款对象的身份防止重复入账。即使签名有效，程序仍要核对对象、账户与金额；不能只看到 success 字段就认定付款成功。

ACK promises durable responsibility, not posting. Deck 3 saves events before Posting deduplicates capture/refund effects. A valid signature still requires object, account, and amount validation; a success field alone is insufficient.

receiveWebhook(endpointConfig, rawBody, headers):
  event = providerSDK.verify(rawBody, headers, endpointConfig.secret,
                            endpointConfig.timestampTolerance)
  require event.environment/account matches endpointConfig
  begin transaction
    old = findInbox(endpointScope, event.id)
    if old exists:
      if old.payloadHash != hash(rawBody):
        appendReceiptAnomaly(old, hash(rawBody))
    else:
      insertInbox(endpointScope, event.id, rawBody, state=READY)
  commit
  return 2xx  // responsibility accepted, not money posted
on invalid signature/account: return 400
on unknown DB commit: return retryable error; same event can redeliver

processInbox(claim):
  evidence = validateOrFetchAuthoritativeObject(claim.event)
  // Validate bound attempt, provider account, kind, amount and currency.
  // Missing capture for a refund -> retain WAITING/REVIEW; do not invent it.
  applyEvidenceThroughPostingGate(evidence)
  markDoneIfLeaseMatches(claim)  // a stale claim cannot finish a new lease

回调端点配置 endpointConfig 中的环境、账户和验签密钥，都来自服务端配置，不能采用请求体自行声明的身份。程序验证原始请求体，再提交 inbox，成功后才返回 2xx。如果无法确认数据库是否提交，可以返回可重试错误，让渠道重投同一事件。

验签应使用渠道提供的 SDK，时间容差也按渠道契约选定。Stripe 文档说明了原始请求体验签、异步处理、重复与乱序交付；持久 inbox 是本章为接管事件所作的设计选择。[3]

Server endpoint configuration supplies environment, account, and secret. Verify the raw body, commit inbox, then return 2xx. An unknown DB commit may return a retryable error and accept event redelivery. Use the provider SDK/tolerance contract, not an invented universal signature scheme. Stripe documents raw-body verification, asynchronous processing, duplicates, and unordered delivery; durable inbox is our design choice. [3]

租约用于让其他程序重新领取未完成的工作，但旧租约到期后，原处理程序仍可能继续运行。因此，防止重复入账要靠 Posting 的唯一约束和本地事务。同一 event_id 重投时不重新创建工作，不同事件 evt_9 却仍可能指向同一收款对象 c9。如果资金效果键发生冲突，程序必须核对原付款、渠道尝试或退款、金额和币种，不能把所有唯一冲突都视为已成功处理。

Leases recover work claims; expired workers may still execute, so Posting uniqueness and transactions protect money. Redelivery of one event does not recreate work, while evt_9 may still describe capture c9. Effect conflicts must compare the original payment, attempt/refund, amount, and currency rather than treating every UNIQUE conflict as success.

本地接收时间 received_at 和精度只有秒的事件创建时间 created，都不能还原严格的业务顺序。退款事件先到、本地收款尚未入账时，要保留事件等待关联，并查询权威对象；已确认成功之后，迟到的处理中或失败通知也不能让状态退回。渠道重试窗口有限，事件仍可能最终漏达，所以还要保留恢复扫描、持久异常工单和对账流程。事件进入死信队列（DLQ）后，相关证据仍须保留。

Arrival time and second-granularity created do not reconstruct strict business order. If a refund event precedes local capture posting, retain it for association and query authority. Stale intermediate/failure events cannot regress confirmed success. Provider retry windows are finite, so retain recovery scans, durable exceptions, and reconciliation; dead-lettering is not evidence disposal.

4 · 完整分录、付款状态与通知意图一起提交4 · Commit complete entries, state, and notification together

一次入账必须包含完整的借贷分录，单独一条“已记账”标记不能替代账本。 第 4 组图的事务 T2 一起提交资金效果的唯一记录、完整账本流水、付款状态，以及 payment.paid 的 outbox 记录。订单服务处理缓慢时，只重新投递通知，不重新执行渠道付款命令。

A posting includes complete debit/credit entries; a single posted marker is not a journal. T2 in deck 4 contains the unique effect, complete journal, payment state, and payment.paid outbox. Slow orders replay notifications rather than channel commands.

postCapture(verifiedEvidence):
  e = verifiedEvidence  // trusted adapter result, never browser input
  key = (e.environment, e.channel, e.account, e.captureId, CAPTURE)
  begin transaction
    p = lockPaymentBoundTo(e.attemptId)
    require e.account == p.channelAccount
    require e.amount == p.amount AND e.currency == p.currency
    old = findPosting(key)
    if old exists:
      require old.payment/attempt/amount/currency == p/e.attemptId/e.amount/e.currency
      commit; return old  // different event, same effect
    require p.captured == 0  // this chapter permits one full capture
    require attemptIsBoundToThisPayment(e.attemptId, p)
    j = insertPosting(key, p.payNo, e.amount, e.currency)
    appendEntry(j, PSP_RECEIVABLE, DR, e.amount, e.currency)
    appendEntry(j, MERCHANT_PAYABLE, CR, e.amount, e.currency)
    require atLeastTwoEntries(j)
    require sumDebits(j, e.currency) == sumCredits(j, e.currency)
    // All entry currencies/accounts must match this validated template.
    p.captured = e.amount
    markAttemptConfirmed(e.attemptId, e.captureId)
    insertOutbox("paid:" + p.payNo, paymentPaid(p, j))
  commit
  return j
on mismatch or a second distinct capture:
  rollback; retain verified evidence in durable REVIEW
  // Never discard external money; exceptional suspense handling is separate.

这里从平台视角记账，并假设资金尚未结算、也未向商户出款。收款金额为 10,000 个最小货币单位时，借记 PSP 应收资产（PSP receivable）10,000，贷记商户应付负债（merchant payable）10,000。商户资金应记为平台的负债，不是平台收入。科目性质决定借贷方向；借贷差为零只是必要条件，因为两边写成同一个错误金额也能平衡，所以代码先核对真实金额和付款关联。[9]

These simplified platform books precede settlement/payout: captured 10,000 minor units debits the PSP receivable asset and credits the merchant payable liability. Merchant funds are not platform income. Account type determines direction. Zero debit/credit difference is necessary, but equally wrong amounts still balance, so validate amount and binding first. [9]

appendEntry 通过同一个关系数据库连接插入分录。受控的 Posting 流程在一次 COMMIT 前，检查至少有两条分录、每个交易 tx_id 在每种币种 currency 下借贷相等，以及科目模板与业务金额是否匹配。普通的行级 CHECK 无法约束跨行求和。生产中应限制直接写入账本的权限，统一经过该流程，或实现可靠的延迟约束。示例中的 SQLite 触发器只阻止修改和删除历史记录，不能替代完整分录校验。[6]

appendEntry inserts through the same DB connection. The controlled Posting process checks at least two entries, per-transaction/currency balance, account template, and business amount before COMMIT. Row CHECK cannot enforce cross-row SUM. Production must restrict raw journal DML to this process or implement reliable deferred constraints. SQLite example triggers prohibit historical UPDATE/DELETE; they do not establish complete-entry balance by themselves. [6]

金额要使用带币种的整数最小单位，或精确十进制数 decimal。输入是十进制字符串时，先精确检查小数位数和取值范围，不要经过浮点数 float。CNY 100.00 对应 10,000 个最小单位，JPY 100 则对应 100 个；具体渠道操作还可能有币种特例。[4] 如果平台承担 300 的手续费，可以另记一组“借费用 300、贷应收 300”的分录，此时净应收 9,700 + 费用 300 = 应付 10,000。手续费按自己的对象身份和操作类型入账，净结算与毛收款也分别对账。

Use currency-tagged integer minor units or exact decimal. Validate decimal-string precision/range without float. CNY 100.00 is 10,000; JPY 100 is 100, with provider operation exceptions. [4] A platform-borne fee of 300 can append debit expense/credit receivable: net receivable 9,700 plus expense 300 equals payable 10,000. Fees have their own effect identity/type; reconcile net settlement separately from gross capture.

Order relay 只读取已提交的 outbox。订单服务在自己的事务中按 event_id 去重，核对 pay_no、订单版本和金额，再应用状态机并返回 ACK。每笔付款只做一次完整收款时，payment.paid 可以用 paid:pay_no 作为稳定事件 ID；退款则用 refunded:refund_id。如果订单已经关闭，仍要保留真实收款事实与退款责任，不能重新打开订单。账务更正通过追加冲正流水并引用原流水完成；退款则是外部资金动作，两者不能互相替代。

Order relay reads only committed outbox. Orders deduplicate event_id, validate payment/order version and amount, apply their state machine, then ACK. For one full capture, paid:pay_no is a stable payment.paid event ID; refunds use refunded:refund_id. A closed order retains the real receipt and refund obligation without reopening. Corrections append linked reversals; a refund is an external funds movement, not an interchangeable internal correction.

5 · 先预留退款额度，结果未知时继续保留5 · Reserve refund capacity and retain it during UNKNOWN

可退金额 = 已收款 captured − 已退款 refunded − 已预留 reserved；所有退款入口都要锁定同一笔付款的聚合记录。 第 5 组图中，原收款为 10,000，r0 已退 3,000，r1 预留 2,000，因此还可退 5,000。渠道受理退款还不代表退款成功；即使调用超时，r1 的预留额度也不能释放。

Available = captured − refunded − reserved; every entry point locks the same aggregate. In deck 5, r0 refunded 3,000 and r1 reserved 2,000, leaving 5,000 of 10,000. Provider acceptance is not success, and timeout cannot release r1’s capacity.

reserveRefund(merchantScope, key, payNo, amount, currency):
  require amount is a positive integer minor-unit amount
  digest = canonicalHash(payNo, amount, currency)
  begin transaction
    p = lockPaymentOwnedBy(merchantScope, payNo)
    old = findRequest(merchantScope, "REFUND", key)
    if old exists:
      require old.digest == digest else CONFLICT
      commit; return accepted(old.refundId)
    require currency == p.currency
    require amount <= p.captured - p.refunded - p.reserved
    r1 = insertRefund(p, amount, currency, state=RESERVED)
    p.reserved += amount
    insertRequest(merchantScope, "REFUND", key, digest, r1)
    insertOutbox("refund-command:" + r1, frozenRefundCommand(r1))
  commit
  return 202(r1)
on relevant UNIQUE conflict: rollback; restart and compare winner
// Every refund entry point uses this same DB aggregate lock/bound.
// A crash after commit preserves both reservation and dispatch intent.
applyRefundResult(verifiedEvidence):
  e = verifiedEvidence
  begin transaction
    p = lockPayment(e.boundPayment)
    r = lockRefund(e.boundLocalRefund)
    require e.account/object/amount/currency matches r and its capture
    if r.state in {SUCCEEDED, FAILED}:
      compareOrRetainContradictoryEvidence(r, e)
      commit; return  // never release or post twice
    if e.provesRefundSuccess:
      j = insertPostingUnique(e.scope, e.refundObjectId, REFUND)
      appendEntry(j, MERCHANT_PAYABLE, DR, r.amount, r.currency)
      appendEntry(j, PSP_RECEIVABLE, CR, r.amount, r.currency)
      require completeValidatedTemplateAndBalanced(j)
      p.reserved -= r.amount; p.refunded += r.amount
      r.state = SUCCEEDED
      insertOutbox("refunded:" + r.id, refundSucceeded(r, j))
    else if e.provesFinalNoRefundAndNoFutureExecution:
      p.reserved -= r.amount
      r.state = FAILED
    else:
      r.state = UNKNOWN  // pending/timeout retains its reservation
    saveEvidence(e)
  commit
// Refund entries assume unsettled PSP receivable, no merchant payout,
// platform-borne fees, and no returned processing fee.

预留事务提交后返回 202 + r1。后台程序读取已提交命令，使用 r1 对应的稳定渠道键、固定金额和原收款对象的绑定关系请求 PSP。收款与退款属于不同 API 操作，身份范围也分开。Stripe 允许针对同一笔扣款多次部分退款，但累计不能超过原金额；本地同样需要额度检查，避免两个并发请求同时用掉最后一份可退金额。[5]

Reservation returns 202 + r1. The worker uses committed intent, r1’s stable provider key, frozen amount, and original capture binding. Create/refund API operations have different identity scopes. Stripe allows multiple partial refunds within the original total; enforce the bound locally so concurrent requests cannot spend the same remaining capacity. [5]

insertPostingUnique 是在同一事务中执行受资金效果唯一约束保护的 INSERT，发生冲突时仍须核查，不能直接忽略。退款证据必须把本地 r1 与外部 refund_id 绑定起来；同一笔外部退款不能被用来结转两笔本地退款的预留。成功时，两条与收款方向相反的分录、reserved 的减少、refunded 的增加、r1 的最终状态和通知事件一起提交。只写了一半分录或提交失败时，整笔事务都回滚。重复成功通知或迟到的失败通知不会再次结转额度；互相矛盾的证据则持久保存，进入 Review 核查。

insertPostingUnique is an INSERT under the effect UNIQUE constraint in the same transaction, without swallowing conflicts. Bind local r1 to its external refund_id; one external refund cannot settle two local reservations. Success commits both reverse entries, reservation decrement, refunded increment, terminal state, and event together. Partial journal or failed commit rolls back all of them. Duplicate success/stale failure never converts capacity twice; retain contradictory evidence in Review.

退款为 UNKNOWN 或仍在处理时，继续保留预留额度。只有明确证明没有退款、以后也不会再执行，才能释放一次。按付款串行处理退款队列可以减少锁竞争，但任务重投、人工操作和多个实例发起的退款，仍要遵守数据库里的额度约束。

示例使用“借商户应付、贷 PSP 应收”的分录，前提是 PSP 尚未结算、平台尚未向商户出款、手续费由平台承担，且不考虑手续费返还。资金已经结算时，要改用相应的现金或清算科目；已经向商户出款时，还要处理资金回收责任。

UNKNOWN/pending retains reservation; release once only after proof of no refund and no future execution. A per-payment queue may reduce contention, but redelivery/manual/multi-instance entry still requires DB bounds. Debit merchant payable/credit PSP receivable assumes no PSP settlement, no merchant payout, platform-borne fees, and no fee reimbursement. Settled funds need cash/clearing accounts; payouts add recovery obligations.

6 · 对账用原效果键修复记录，不能再次扣款6 · Reconcile books and knowledge without charging again

先固定外部结算批次与内部快照，再按对象比较，最后用原资金效果键修复。 第 6 组图中，修复程序（Repair）要等 Payment DB 确认提交，才向工单返回成功。工单尚未关闭，只能说明流程还有待完成，不能成为再次产生资金效果的依据。

Fix the external batch and internal snapshot, compare by object, and repair with the original effect key. Deck 6 waits for Payment DB commit before acknowledging repair; an open case is never permission to move money twice.

reconcile(batchHash, revision, rules, internalSnapshotW):
  run = persistRunIdentity(batchHash, revision, rules, internalSnapshotW)
  require verifiedReportCompletenessOrKeepRunOpen(run)
  compare identities/counts/gross/fee/net/currency at matching scope
  for difference in run.differences:
    c = upsertCase(run, difference.businessKey, difference.kind)
    if difference is external_only:
      e = queryAndValidateAuthoritativeObject(c)
      if e is verified capture/refund:
        applyEvidenceThroughPostingGate(e)  // original effect key
        markCaseClosedAfterKnownCommit(c)
      else: retainReview(c)
    else:
      retainReview(c)  // investigate lateness, cutoffs and mismatches
// A crash after posting but before closing c only repeats the same gate.
// No reconciliation branch creates another charge or guesses a reversal.
// Report revisions append evidence; an absent row is not final failure.

Normalizer 保存批次 batch_id、文件哈希与版本、原始行、时区和结算区间。Adyen 的 Settlement details report 覆盖已结算付款与交易费用，不能当作当天所有已受理请求的最终清单。[7] 内部水位 W 则必须对应可重现的一致快照或已保留的数据集，不能直接把 max(id) 当作提交水位，因为并发事务可能先取得 ID，稍后才提交。

Normalizer retains batch ID, file hash/revision, raw rows, timezone, and settlement interval. Adyen’s Settlement details report covers settled payments and transaction costs, not every accepted request for that calendar day. [7] Internal W must identify a reproducible consistent snapshot or retained dataset; max(id) is not automatically a commit watermark because concurrent transactions can allocate IDs before committing.
