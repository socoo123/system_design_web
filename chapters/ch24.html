支付系统Payment system

约 40 分钟主线 + 15 分钟深挖 · 前置：Ch18、Ch36、Ch41 · 6 组 / 30 帧演进图~40 min core + 15 min deep dives · after Ch18, Ch36, Ch41 · 6 evolution decks / 30 frames

这一章结束你能做什么What you can do after this chapter

从一条支付意图画到渠道请求、异步回调、账本和订单通知，指出每次持久提交发生在哪里。支付的核心是同一笔钱产生几次效果，以及不知道结果时谁能继续动作。每组最后一帧都能顺着箭头讲完正常路径，再指出故障时行为和被否决的动作。

Draw a payment intent through a channel request, asynchronous callback, ledger and order notification, naming every durable commit. The core questions are how many effects one operation produces and who may act when its outcome is unknown. Walk the complete normal path, then state failure behavior and the actions rejected.

你需要分清三个边界：我方受理成功，不等于渠道收款成功；渠道确认收款，不等于我方账本已入；账本提交成功，不等于订单已经收到通知。用不同记录和不同幂等键保存这些事实，再用查询与对账把未决窗口关上。

Separate three boundaries: local acceptance is not a successful charge; confirmed capture is not a completed local posting; a committed posting is not an applied order notification. Separate records and idempotency keys preserve those facts; queries and reconciliation close uncertainty.

30 秒开场与全部假设The 30-second open and assumptions

我先设计商户收款，走 PSP 托管支付页面，单币种 CNY、一次完整 capture，可多次部分退款。每天一百万次创单，七成成功，成功单平均两次回调，峰值十倍。先把 payment、attempt 和渠道命令同库提交，再调用渠道；超时留 UNKNOWN，不换渠道再扣。回调验签后先持久收进 inbox，按业务动作去重入账，复式流水与订单 outbox 同事务。订单通知可重试，渠道结果靠查询与结算对账核实。

I design merchant pay-in via a PSP-hosted page: CNY, one full capture and multiple partial refunds. Assume one million creates/day, 70% success, two receipts per success and a 10× peak. Commit payment, attempt and channel command before the call. Keep timeout UNKNOWN rather than charging through another channel. Authenticate callbacks into durable inbox, deduplicate the business effect, and commit balanced journal plus order outbox together. Query and reconcile channel outcomes.

澄清问题只问会改图的：收款还是钱包转账，是否走托管页，授权与捕获是否分开，币种与部分退款要求，订单关单竞态由谁处理，渠道支持什么查询和幂等期限。假设表不是公开生产数据。钱包双花、证券撮合、多币种兑换与商户出款留在边界外；本章只给 Ch18 两个可靠出口：已入账的付款通知，或关闭订单所需的退款操作。

Ask only questions that change the design: pay-in or wallet transfers, hosted collection, separate authorization and capture, currencies and partial refunds, ownership of order-close races, and channel query/idempotency capabilities. These assumptions are not production measurements. Wallet double-spend, securities matching, FX and merchant payouts stay outside scope; Ch18 receives posted-payment notifications or refund operations for closed orders.

先把除法写出来：1,000,000 ÷ 86,400 = 11.574 次创单/秒，乘十是 115.74，口播 116。700,000 次成功/天是 8.102 次/秒，峰值约 81；1,400,000 次回调接收/天是 16.204 次/秒，峰值约 162。三种速率不能说成同一个 TPS，失败、重投、退款还有不同写放大。预算要用混合事务压测，不能说“任何数据库都一定够”。

Show the division: 1,000,000 / 86,400 = 11.574 creates/s, times 10 gives 115.74, spoken as 116. Successful captures are 700,000/day or 8.102/s, about 81 at peak. Receipts are 1,400,000/day or 16.204/s, about 162 at peak. These are different rates; failure, redelivery and refunds add different writes. Measure the mixed transaction load rather than asserting any database will suffice.

容量按三类原始行估上界：支付单一年 1M × 1 KB × 365 = 365 GB；流水 700K × 2 × 200 B × 365 = 102.2 GB；每次接收另记审计 1.4M × 1 KB × 365 = 511 GB。合计 978.2 GB，三份 2,934.6 GB = 2.9346 TB。inbox 按 event_id 去重后行数通常更少，这 511 GB 估的是接收审计上界。attempt、outbox、索引、WAL、备份、退款与保留更久的财务行都没算进去，不能把“不到一 TB 原始”说成一年总盘量。

An upper bound for three raw row types: payments are 1M × 1 KB × 365 = 365 GB/year; journal is 700K × 2 × 200 B × 365 = 102.2 GB; separate per-delivery receipt audits are 1.4M × 1 KB × 365 = 511 GB. Total 978.2 GB, three copies 2,934.6 GB = 2.9346 TB. Deduplicated inbox has fewer rows; 511 GB bounds receipt audits. Attempts, outbox, indexes, WAL, backups, refunds and longer financial retention are excluded, so raw rows are not total disk.

先落支付意图，再碰渠道Persist intent before calling the channel

正常路径：一笔受理保存三个事实Normal path: accept three durable facts

Client 带商户身份、订单引用、金额、币种与 Idempotency-Key 到 Pay API。服务端从订单侧校验金额，不让浏览器自由改价；先占用幂等键、写 payment 和 attempt a7、再写渠道命令 outbox，一次事务提交后回 202 与 pay_no。这个返回只承诺系统接受了操作，用户可按 pay_no 查状态；托管 URL 稍后由 Pay worker 创建并记录，不能在图上假装它已经随第一次受理返回。

Client sends merchant identity, order reference, amount, currency and Idempotency-Key. Pay API validates price against the order, claims the key, and atomically records payment, attempt a7 and channel outbox. It returns 202 plus pay_no, promising acceptance only. The client queries progress; Pay worker creates and records a hosted URL later, so acceptance cannot already include it.

一份 payment 表示业务收款意图，一份 attempt 表示某次外部尝试，outbox 是“必须把 a7 送给指定渠道”的持久命令。三者同库是为了让崩溃后的 worker 知道该恢复哪个请求；命令记录渠道、商户账户、稳定请求摘要、尝试键与版本。数据库提交失败就不调 PSP，重复创单回原 pay_no。持久受理要先于外部动作，因为 PSP 不在我方关系库事务内，回滚本地 SQL 无法撤销已经扣走的钱。

Payment is the business intent, attempt an external try, and outbox the durable command to send a7 to a chosen provider. Keeping them local lets workers recover the exact request after a crash; record provider, merchant account, immutable payload digest, attempt key and version. Failed local commit means no PSP call. SQL rollback cannot undo an already executed external charge.

决策与替代：把重试范围写清Decision and alternative: define retry scope

创单键唯一范围是 merchant_id + operation + key；退款与创单是不同 operation，不共享一条记录。请求摘要至少含订单引用、金额、币种、支付方式与业务版本。同键同摘要回原受理结果；同键不同摘要拒绝，不偷偷使用旧金额。并发时由 UNIQUE 约束仲裁，一个事务赢，另一个读已有结果或收到我方定义的处理中冲突。所有键列必须非空；先 SELECT 再 INSERT 只是优化，挡不住两次同时读空。[1][6]

Scope a create key by merchant_id, operation and key; refund is another operation. Hash order reference, amount, currency, method and business version. Same key and digest return the original acceptance; changed payload is rejected. A non-null UNIQUE key arbitrates concurrency; the loser reads the result or gets a locally defined in-progress conflict. SELECT-before-INSERT alone races. [1][6]

A8 的 24 小时只保留我方创单响应缓存，过期不删除 payment、未决 attempt 或业务效果键。过期键不得自动启动第二笔钱；让调用方提供新键并明确引用原操作，先查其状态。业务意图按 merchant_id+order_ref+明确业务版本唯一，保留成功终态；换 header 不造新意图。确认未扣款后的新 attempt 仍附原意图，退款不自动许可再次收款。Stripe 的至少 24 小时可清理政策属于该提供商，参数变化会被拒绝，首个执行结果还可能是 500；这不等于所有渠道永久幂等。[1]

A8 retains only our response cache for 24 hours, not payment, unresolved attempts or effect keys. An expired key must not automatically move money again; require an explicitly new operation referencing the old one and inspect its state. Uniquely bind intent to merchant_id+order_ref+explicit business version and retain successful terminal state. A changed header creates no new intent. A confirmed no-charge retry adds an attempt to the same intent; refund does not automatically authorize recharging. Stripe’s at-least-24-hour pruning policy is provider-specific, compares parameters and caches even an initial 500; it is not permanent universal dedupe. [1]

失败窗口：两个提交不能装成一个Failure window: two commits are not one

Pay worker 从已提交命令取 a7，在渠道有效期限内使用相同尝试键请求 PSP。worker 取到命令就崩溃，命令仍待执行；PSP 已执行但回复丢了，恢复到 UNKNOWN 而不是创建 a8。改为同步 API 调渠道也可以，但仍要先持久保存 attempt，并保留恢复流程；它额外让 HTTP 受理耗时受渠道尾延迟影响。本章把渠道等待移出 300 毫秒受理目标，付出的是轮询、任务积压与进度页面复杂度。

Pay worker reads committed a7 and uses its stable channel key within the provider window. A pre-send crash leaves durable work; a lost response after execution creates UNKNOWN, not a8. A synchronous API is an alternative only if it first persists intent and preserves recovery. It couples acceptance to PSP tail latency; asynchronous dispatch protects the 300 ms target at the cost of polling, backlog and progress UI.

Outbox 解决本地意图与命令的双写，不能代替渠道幂等。relay 可能投递两次、worker 也可能执行两次，所以调用端仍用 a7，提供商必须支持相应操作的去重或稳定引用。教学峰值只有 116 次创单/秒，先用一套关系库守住这些约束，压测后再决定分片；拆成 payment 库、attempt 库和命令库会把一个本地事务拆坏。命令积压时显示处理中，保持恢复可能性，并按年龄报警。[8]

Outbox closes local intent-versus-command dual writes but does not provide provider idempotency. Redelivery and repeated worker execution still require a7 and verified provider capability. At about 116 peak creates/s, start with one relational authority and measure before sharding. Splitting payment, attempt and command stores destroys the local atomic boundary. Backlog remains processing and recoverable, with age alerts. [8]

超时保留 UNKNOWN，确认后再推进Keep timeout UNKNOWN until resolved

正常路径：把知识状态和业务结果分开Normal path: separate knowledge from outcome

第二组是 attempt 状态图，不是 HTTP 时序。CREATED 是持久意图，SENT 表示请求可能已送出；SENT 不说明渠道成功。A4 的两秒是我方等待期限，不是扣款撤销期限。网络超时、连接断开或不可判定的服务端错误把知识状态置为 UNKNOWN：我方不知道结果，渠道可能已经 capture。用户页面继续显示处理中，不能用 500 页面、浏览器关闭或回跳 success 参数来改财务状态。

The second deck is an attempt state graph, not HTTP ordering. CREATED is durable intent, SENT means the request may have left, and neither proves success. The two-second wait is not a cancellation deadline. Network failure or indeterminate server error makes our knowledge UNKNOWN; PSP may already have captured. Keep processing UI; HTTP errors, browser closure and redirect parameters cannot mutate financial truth.

Resolver 根据已经拿到的渠道对象 ID 查询当前状态，或利用验证过的稳定商户引用关联对象。若 create 的回复丢失、尚无 channel_txn_id，A9 不能假定任意 PSP 都能按我方 a7 查单；可在仍有效的同键重试窗口取回已缓存结果，或者等已验签回调与账单按稳定商户引用关联。若这些证据都拿不到，就保留未决工单。渠道查询本身失败也只是没有新证据，不是扣款失败。

Resolver queries a known provider object ID or a verified stable merchant reference. If create response is lost and no channel_txn_id exists, do not assume every PSP supports a7 lookup. Recover a cached result through valid same-key retry, or correlate authenticated callbacks/reports by stable merchant reference. Without evidence, keep an open case. Query failure adds no evidence and does not prove no charge.

决策与替代：什么事实允许下一次扣款Decision and alternative: when another charge is permitted

权威成功证据确认 capture c9 后，attempt 进入 CONFIRMED，再由 Posting 入账；“确认收款”与“已入账”仍是不同字段。明确渠道拒绝、对象取消且确认不会再扣款等终态，才可进入 DECLINED 并允许业务发起新 attempt。不同渠道的中间态和最终态要映射成自己的类型，不能把每个非 success 值统一叫失败；异步付款可能在处理中很久，授权未捕获也不能记成已收款。

Authoritative capture c9 evidence makes the attempt CONFIRMED, after which Posting records it. Confirmation and local posting remain separate fields. Final decline or confirmed cancellation with no future charge permits a new attempt. Map provider intermediate and final states explicitly; non-success is not uniformly failed. Long-running asynchronous processing or authorization without capture is not captured money.

换渠道可以作为下一次明确的新尝试，但不能当 UNKNOWN 的自动故障转移。旧渠道若晚来成功，新渠道也已扣款，两个唯一键都合法，内部幂等挡不住两笔外部效果。替代是只查询并向用户解释等待，或在提供商允许时申请取消并确认取消结果，然后再开始新尝试。代价是短期转化率和体验；换来的约束是同一业务意图不能凭一场网络误判产生第二次扣款。

Provider failover can serve a clearly new attempt, not automatic UNKNOWN recovery. A late old success and a new success carry different valid keys, so local dedupe cannot prevent both external charges. Query and explain the wait, or request and confirm a supported cancellation before retrying elsewhere. This sacrifices conversion and immediacy to avoid a second charge caused by uncertainty.

失败窗口：重试期限与迟到证据Failure window: retry expiry and late evidence

Stripe 官方把部分 500 视作 indeterminate，并提醒换新键重试可能遇到已有副作用；同一幂等键缓存的首个 500 还可能一直返回同一 500。[2] 因此“不断重试就一定查到成功”不成立。记录首发时间与提供商幂等有效窗口，超过窗口不得把原扣款请求当安全重放；转成查询、回调关联和对账。worker 崩溃恢复前也重新检查期限，不因为队列终于出队就自动再次移动钱。

Stripe calls some 500 outcomes indeterminate and warns that new-key retries can encounter existing side effects; the same key can keep returning its cached initial 500. [2] Retry does not guarantee recovery. Record first-send time and provider validity window. After expiry, stop replaying the original charge as if safe; query, correlate callbacks and reconcile. Recovery checks expiry before acting on delayed work.

A5 以创单为分母：1M × 0.001 = 每天 1,000 笔未决，并非回调错误率。未决堆积用数量、最老年龄与金额观察，不给它套一个“过十分钟全失败”的扫描。迟到失败通知不能把已经确认成功的 capture 降回 DECLINED；只记录原事件并核查对象，后续退款或争议是新的业务效果。单库失去可确认提交能力时停止财务写，渠道可能仍收款，由 inbox、查询和对账在恢复后收口；不能靠异步副本宣布未看到就是未发生。

A5 uses creates as denominator: 1M × 0.001 = 1,000 unresolved/day, not callback error rate. Track count, oldest age and amount rather than expiring all to failure. A late failure event cannot downgrade an already confirmed capture; preserve evidence and inspect current object. Refunds/disputes are distinct effects. Stop financial writes if authoritative commit is unavailable; absence on a lagging replica does not prove absence of payment.

回调先收进 inbox，再按业务效果去重Receive callbacks durably, then deduplicate the effect

正常路径：ACK 承诺的是接收Normal path: ACK promises receipt

PSP 请求进入 Webhook，先用该渠道的认证方案验证原始 body、签名与允许的时间窗口，校验商户账户与订阅事件类型，再把原始事件、接收时间、对象引用、认证结果保存到 durable inbox。数据库提交回到 Webhook，才回 2xx 给 PSP。这次 ACK 的含义是我方已经接管处理责任，不是订单已支付。入账线程不占着回调等待；若 inbox 事务失败就不成功 ACK，交付可重试，不能先回 200 再只把事件留在内存队列。

Webhook validates provider authentication over raw body, signature and permitted timestamp window, checks merchant account/event type, and saves event evidence to durable inbox. Only after commit does 2xx return. ACK promises ownership of processing, not paid order state. Posting stays outside the callback wait. A failed inbox commit must not ACK success or leave the only copy in an in-memory queue.

本章用 Stripe 做公开例子：原始 body 不能先被解析器重写，官方要求尽快返回 2xx 并异步处理；其事件可能重复或乱序。[3] 认证只回答“谁发的”，不替代业务校验：读取 amount、currency、capture/refund 对象与我方 payment 的绑定。认证通过但金额不符的事件仍可持久接收并进异常工单，却不得进入成功入账。各渠道签名协议不同，本章不把 HMAC、时间戳格式或重试期限写成所有 PSP 的统一标准。

Stripe is the public example: do not rewrite raw body before verification; return 2xx quickly and process asynchronously; delivery can duplicate or reorder. [3] Authentication identifies the sender but does not replace amount, currency and object-binding checks. An authenticated amount mismatch can be durably accepted into an exception case but cannot post success. Signature schemes and retry windows are provider-specific.

决策与替代：两层唯一键有两种职责Decision and alternative: two different unique scopes

inbox 唯一键是 channel + merchant_account + event_id，处理同一事件的多次投递。Posting gate 则按 channel + merchant_account + capture_id + kind 唯一，处理“不同事件说的是同一笔 capture”。refund 使用 refund_id 与独立 kind。一个 pay_no 可有收款、退款和冲正，扩展为多次 capture 时还可有多个 capture_id；只在 pay_no 上建 UNIQUE 会把合法后续动作挡掉，单用 event_id 又会让不同事件双入金额。[3]

Inbox uses channel, merchant_account and event_id for repeated delivery. Posting gate uses channel, merchant_account, capture_id and kind for distinct events describing one capture. Refund has its own refund_id and kind. pay_no aggregates capture, refunds and corrections and may later hold multiple captures; pay_no-only uniqueness blocks valid operations, while event_id-only uniqueness can duplicate money. [3]

worker 领取 inbox 行使用可恢复的处理状态，租约过期可重做，最终正确性由业务唯一键与同事务入账守住。已完成的 event 再投递，读到原有持久 inbox 后 ACK；业务 posting_key 撞键，要比较原金额、币种与对象是否一致，一致才视作已处理，不一致是异常，不能“任何 UNIQUE 冲突都 200 且当钱已对”。直接在回调内做全部本地入账是另一种可选实现，但外部通知仍必须 outbox，且复杂处理更容易撑爆回调超时。

Use recoverable inbox claims; expired leases can be redone, while business uniqueness plus local transaction protects correctness. Repeated completed events ACK after finding durable inbox. On a posting-key conflict, compare stored amount, currency and object; matching means already applied, mismatch is an exception. Synchronous local posting in webhook is an alternative but still requires notification outbox and increases timeout exposure.

失败窗口：顺序与积压都不改账Failure window: order and backlog do not change truth

乱序不能靠 received_at 排回去，因为它只是到达时间；事件里的秒级 created 也不一定唯一或代表严格顺序。先来的退款成功找不到本地 capture 时，保存待关联并查权威对象，不先伪造一笔收款来让关系成立。成功事件晚来可以补入账，已成功后晚来旧的处理中事件只保留证据，不回退状态。合法消息最终也可能在提供商重试窗口外漏掉，所以“至少一次风格的交付”不是一定送达承诺，对账仍不可删。

received_at orders arrival, not business history, and second-granularity event timestamps do not provide strict ordering. A refund success arriving before its capture stays pending association while querying authority; do not fabricate capture. Late success can backfill, while stale intermediate events cannot regress success. Provider retry windows do not guarantee eventual receipt, so reconciliation remains necessary.

按 A3，worker 停十分钟、每次峰值投递都形成工作时，上界是 162 × 600 = 97,200 条；inbox 去重会更少。恢复仍有新流量，净消化是 500 − 162 = 338/秒，清空约 97,200 ÷ 338 ≈ 288 秒，约 4.8 分钟；这是假设容量未被查询与数据库锁争用压低。扩 worker 时要一起量入账事务预算，不能只看队列长度。超重试阈值进持久异常队列并报警，保留原事件以便人工重放，不把 DLQ 当垃圾桶。

At A3, a ten-minute outage at 162 receipts/s gives an upper bound of 162 × 600 = 97,200 work items if every receipt creates work; inbox dedupe can reduce it. Recovery has 500 - 162 = 338/s spare and drains in about 97,200 / 338 ≈ 288 s, or 4.8 minutes, assuming database contention and queries do not lower capacity. Scale against the posting transaction budget too. Persistent exceptions retain original evidence for replay; dead-letter storage is not disposal.

账本提交后，outbox 才通知订单Commit the ledger before notifying orders

正常路径：在平台视角写出借贷Normal path: name accounts in platform books

第四组固定平台的会计视角。收到 CNY 100.00 元、最小单位整数 10,000：借 PSP receivable 10,000，资产增加；贷 merchant payable 10,000，对商户的负债增加。这不是把商户资金记成平台收入。借方合计减贷方合计是 10,000 − 10,000 = 0。科目性质决定借贷方向，不能只背“正负两行”；TigerBeetle 的官方会计说明给出资产、费用借增，负债、权益、收入贷增的角色，本章不因此引入专用账本库。[9]

Use platform books consistently. Captured CNY 100.00 is integer 10,000 minor units: debit PSP receivable, increasing assets; credit merchant payable, increasing liability. Merchant funds are not platform revenue. Debit minus credit is 10,000 - 10,000 = 0. Account nature determines direction, not a memorized positive/negative pair. TigerBeetle’s accounting explanation supports the roles; no specialized ledger database is added. [9]

金额类型保存 currency 与 amount_minor，并按币种 exponent 精确转换，拒绝多余小数位、溢出和币种不符。CNY exponent 为 2，100.00 乘 100 得 10,000 分；JPY 是零小数币种，100 日元是整数 100，不是 10,000 分。渠道还可能有操作特例，需要适配器核对。[4] API 可传十进制字符串，内部用足够范围的整数或精确 decimal；不要先把输入转 float 再乘 100。不同币种不把数值相加来“凑账平”，复式断言按 tx_id 与 currency 分组。

Store currency and amount_minor, converting exactly by currency exponent and rejecting extra precision, overflow or mismatch. CNY exponent 2 turns 100.00 into 10,000; zero-decimal JPY 100 is integer 100, not 10,000 cents. Check provider-specific operation exceptions. [4] Transport can use decimal strings, with sufficient integer range or exact decimal internally. Never parse float before scaling. Balance per tx_id and currency, never by adding currencies.

决策与替代：本地事务关住什么窗口Decision and alternative: the local atomic boundary

Posting 在一个本地事务写非空 posting_key、完整 journal_entries、支付的已入账状态和 payment.paid outbox。金额、币种、科目与借贷平衡都通过，才提交。跨行 SUM 不能靠一条普通行级 CHECK 自动保证，PostgreSQL 明确 CHECK 不支持依赖其他行的约束；本章要求受控 posting 过程在事务内验完整分录，或可靠的延迟约束触发器。[6] 账平也不是充分条件：两边同时多写 100 元仍平，错误科目也能平，后面仍需支付单与外部账对照。

Posting atomically writes non-null posting key, complete entries, posted-payment state and payment.paid outbox after validation. A row CHECK cannot guarantee cross-row SUM; PostgreSQL disallows CHECK dependencies on other rows. [6] Use a controlled posting procedure or a reliable deferred trigger. Balanced entries alone are insufficient: equally wrong amounts or wrong accounts can still balance and need internal/external reconciliation.

Order relay 只读取已提交 outbox，投递 payment.paid；订单按事件业务键去重，应用自己的状态机，持久完成后 ACK relay。relay 在 ACK 前崩溃会重投，订单必须能重复消费。付款入账不等待 Order 的 RPC，更不等待发货。Outbox 的代价是通知短时滞后与重试监控，换来账本已提交就总有通知意图可恢复；如果提交后直接从内存 enqueue，再碰到崩溃，账在、通知无，订单永远不知道付过款。[8]

Order relay reads committed outbox and sends payment.paid. Order durably applies its own deduplicated transition before ACK. Relay can crash and redeliver. Posting waits for neither Order RPC nor fulfillment. Outbox adds notification delay and retry monitoring but preserves notification intent after commit; in-memory enqueue after commit can lose the only notification in a crash. [8]

失败窗口：手续费、冲正与订单关单Failure window: fees, corrections and closed orders

A10 假设平台承担渠道手续费 300 分，净应收是 10,000 − 300 = 9,700。可先按毛额记收款，再追加借费用 300、贷应收 300；合并表示就是借应收 9,700 + 借费用 300 = 贷应付 10,000。若合同改成商户承担费用，商户应付的数也要变，不能沿用本例。对账要按 gross、fee、net 各自比较；渠道净结算 9,700 不等于收款只成功了 9,700，也不意味着内部多记 300。

With platform-borne fee 300, net receivable is 10,000 - 300 = 9,700. Post gross then debit expense/credit receivable 300, or show equivalent net receivable 9,700 + expense 300 = payable 10,000. Merchant-borne fees would change payable. Reconcile gross, fee and net separately; a 9,700 settlement is not a 9,700 capture or an unexplained 300 overposting.

更正必须追加有原流水引用的冲正，不 UPDATE 历史金额，也不 DELETE 为了让汇总好看。余额投影可以重建，投影延迟不能反过来改流水。订单已关闭时收到迟到付款，支付账本仍如实记收到的钱，订单不复活，由 Ch18 返回需要退款的业务命令，再走下一组独立退款。退款是外部业务动作，内部冲正是记账更正，两者不是任何情况下都能互换；钱已经退了吗，要看渠道证据。

Corrections append a linked reversal, never UPDATE or DELETE historical amounts. Rebuild balance projections; lag cannot alter journal truth. If an order is closed, record real received funds without reopening it, and let Ch18 request a separate refund. External refund and internal accounting reversal are distinct operations and are not universally interchangeable; whether money returned requires channel evidence.

部分退款独立建键，并发先预留Key partial refunds independently and reserve first

正常路径：可退金额不是一个无锁读Normal path: refundable amount is not an unlocked read

Refund API 带 refund_id r1、pay_no、金额、原因和调用幂等键进入系统。在付款行或退款余额聚合上锁，计算 captured − succeeded_refunds − pending_reservations；通过后同事务记录退款意图、预留与 refund outbox，回 202 和 r1。A11 的例子是 capture 10,000，r0 已成功退 3,000，r1 正预留 2,000，可再退 10,000 − 3,000 − 2,000 = 5,000。新 6,000 请求在锁内拒绝，不能让两个并发调用各自看见未扣掉的余额。

Refund API receives r1, pay_no, amount, reason and a caller key. Lock the payment/refund aggregate, calculate captured minus successful refunds minus pending reservations, and atomically persist refund, reservation and refund outbox before 202. A11 has 10,000 captured, r0 refunded 3,000 and r1 reserved 2,000, leaving 5,000. Reject a new 6,000 under lock, preventing concurrent callers from spending the same amount.

渠道允许一笔 charge 多次部分退款，但累计不能超过原扣款额，这是 Stripe 官方约束。[5] 本章在本地也执行限额，避免把所有错误都推给 PSP。收款的 capture c9 与退款 r1 有不同业务效果键；同 r1 重发回原退款进度，不同 r2 才是另一笔合法部分退款。退款输入币种必须等于原 capture，金额必须为正整数最小单位，金额精度规则与收款相同。pay_no 用来关联，不能代替独立 refund_id。

Stripe permits multiple partial refunds on one charge, with total not exceeding capture. [5] Enforce the same bound locally rather than outsourcing validation. Capture c9 and refund r1 use separate effect keys; repeated r1 returns its progress while r2 is another operation. Refund currency and precision must match capture and amount must be positive. pay_no correlates operations but cannot replace refund_id.

决策与替代：受理退款与钱退成功分开Decision and alternative: acceptance is not success

Pay worker 从 refund outbox 读 r1，使用 r1 对应的渠道尝试键请求 PSP。渠道“已接受”可能仍 pending，不释放预留也不先记退款完成。权威结果成功后，Posting 用 refund:r1 的业务键追加反向分录并把预留转成成功退款发生额；明确失败则释放预留并保留原因。若进程在渠道成功后、记账前崩溃，回调或 Resolver 用同一 r1 恢复。本地记录成功、结转预留与追加流水同事务，做法与第四组一致，不能另起一条先改余额的捷径。

Pay worker reads refund outbox and sends the stable r1 provider key. Acceptance may remain pending: keep reservation and do not declare refund completion. Authoritative success posts refund:r1 and converts reservation into successful refund amount; confirmed failure releases it with cause. A crash after external success recovers through callback/Resolver using r1. Record local success, convert reservation and append the journal in the same transaction, following the ledger pattern.

可以串行化同一 pay_no 的退款队列，减少锁竞争，但队列顺序不是余额限额的最终防线：重投、人工入口和另一个实例仍能同时操作，所以数据库中的预留与累计约束必须保留。也可以把退款放到独立服务，但要保持限额权威与 journal 的事务边界，或明确补偿协议。按商户拆片时，一笔 payment 的收款与退款留在同一财务分区，先解决正确性，再讨论横向容量。

Serializing refunds per pay_no can reduce contention, but queue ordering does not protect against redelivery, manual entry or another instance. Keep database reservation and cumulative bounds. A separate refund service still needs an authoritative limit and journal atomicity or an explicit compensation protocol. When sharding by merchant, colocate a payment’s capture and refunds in one financial partition before discussing scale.

失败窗口：UNKNOWN 不能归还余额Failure window: UNKNOWN cannot release capacity

退款超时与收款超时一样，可能外部已执行。r1 UNKNOWN 时保留 2,000 预留，继续查询同一退款对象，不能释放后让 r2 再退，也不能换一个新 key 重发 r1。保留预留牺牲了短期可退额度；这是为了不让渠道里可能已完成的退款在本地又被花一次。超过渠道重试有效期之后，保留人工核实路径，不把“超过一天”直接写成退款失败。后台扫描看的是未决年龄与金额，不是自动造一个终态。

A refund timeout can follow completed external execution. UNKNOWN r1 retains its 2,000 reservation and queries the same refund object. Do not release it for r2 or send r1 with a fresh key. This reduces temporary refund capacity to avoid spending possibly returned money again. After provider retry expiry, keep review rather than marking failed because a day elapsed. Aging observes uncertainty rather than inventing final state.

本例限定 PSP 应收尚未结算成现金、商户尚未出款，平台承担手续费且忽略费退还。r1 确认退 2,000 后，借 merchant payable 2,000 减少负债，贷 PSP receivable 2,000 减少资产；原 capture 与费项仍在，退款累计从 3,000 变 5,000，可退余量仍 5,000，因为原 2,000 预留同步清零。若 PSP 已结算现金，即使商户尚未出款也要贷相应现金或退款清算科目；商户已出款则资金回收也不同，需要新的业务设计，不能拿本例直接当所有阶段的退款账。

Assume PSP receivable has not settled into cash and merchant payout has not occurred, with platform-borne fees and no fee reimbursement. Confirmed r1 2,000 debits merchant payable and credits PSP receivable, reducing liability and asset. Preserve capture and fee entries. Successful refunds rise 3,000 to 5,000 while reservation clears, leaving the same 5,000 capacity. If PSP already settled cash, credit the relevant cash/refund-clearing account even before merchant payout. After payout, fund recovery differs too.

对账固定水位，差异先找证据Fix reconciliation watermarks and investigate evidence

正常路径：同一快照对同一批次Normal path: one snapshot against one batch

PSP 的结算文件或报告先进入 Normalizer，保留 batch_id、文件哈希、原始行、报告时区、结算区间与下载版本，再转成带 capture/refund 引用、gross、fee、net 和 currency 的标准记录。Adyen 的结算对账文档描述报告用于核对交易、成本及结算，具体字段和批次规则需要跟渠道适配。[7] 文件并非整个平台所有付款的同一天最终真相：缺文件、修正版和跨日迟到都必须处理。文件名相同、行数看似够了，都不能证明已完整。

Normalizer retains batch ID, hash, raw rows, timezone, settlement interval and download revision before extracting capture/refund references, gross, fee, net and currency. Adyen documents reports for transactions, costs and settlement, with provider-specific fields and batch rules. [7] A file is not universal same-day final truth: missing reports, revisions and late rows matter. Filename and plausible row count do not prove completeness.

Recon 记录内部一致性快照水位 W，只读该水位内已经提交的流水。比较键至少包括渠道账户、币种、外部业务对象、动作类型；pay_no 是关联辅助，不足以区分部分退款。先做三刀：每个 journal 借贷是否平，成功支付与成功退款的聚合是否等于对应 journal，再把同范围流水与外部权威记录比。记录数量也要对，金额总和恰好相同可能只是两笔错单相互抵消；不能只用一张总金额表宣布全部对齐。

Recon records a consistent internal snapshot W of committed journal rows. Match provider account, currency, external business object and operation kind; pay_no alone cannot distinguish partial refunds. Check journal balance, payment/refund aggregates against journals, then comparable journals against external records. Compare identity and count too: equal totals can hide offsetting wrong operations.

决策与替代：差异分类后才修复Decision and alternative: classify before repairing

渠道有、内部无，先查权威对象确认收款或退款、核对绑定与金额，满足证据条件才交 Repair，用原业务 posting_key 补入账。内部有、渠道无，先检查是否在途、渠道尚未结算、报告修订或水位错位，查询与后续窗口仍未知就留 Review，不直接冲正。双方都有却金额不等，先拆毛额、费、净额与币种，无法解释进人工工单；任何人工结论也保留证据与更正引用。修复都回到同一 Posting 约束，不能开一条绕过唯一键的“财务快捷入口”。

For external-only effects, verify authoritative state, binding and amount before Repair backfills with the original posting key. For internal-only effects, investigate in-flight settlement, revisions and mismatched cutoffs; unresolved queries stay in Review, not immediate reversal. Amount mismatch first separates gross, fees, net and currency, then escalates with evidence. All repairs use the same Posting constraints, including manual repairs.

日切批次能稳定比对范围，但有发现延迟；实时查单与增量对账更早发现问题，却不能假定提供商完整实时流。两者可并行：实时处理未决，结算报告核清收款、退款、费用与后续结算。跨切点记录放滚动复核窗，报告完整后推进外部水位，内部则按 W 保持可复跑。没有一个固定 T+1 承诺能代替渠道的完整性标记与实际最终状态，本章的日切是工作安排，不是每笔钱都一天结清的事实。

Daily batches stabilize comparison sets but detect later; live queries and incremental recon detect sooner without assuming complete real-time provider streams. Combine live uncertainty recovery with reports for captures, refunds, fees and settlement. Revisit cross-cutoff rows in rolling windows; advance external watermark only with verified completeness and preserve internal W for reruns. T+1 is a schedule, not proof all funds finalize in a day.

失败窗口：重跑只能重复证据，不能重复钱Failure window: reruns repeat evidence, not money

对账作业也要幂等：run_id 绑定来源批次哈希、版本、对账规则和内部水位；原行导入以来源行键去重，差异工单以业务对象与差异类型去重，补记以 posting_key 去重。报告修正版开新版本，不覆盖旧证据；同一差异的新证据可以追加。worker 在补入账提交后崩溃，重跑撞业务唯一键得到原结果；不能因为“上次工单还没标完成”就再补一笔金额。无法解释的差异停在 Review，而不是让 job 为清零指标随便冲正。

Recon is idempotent too: run ID binds batch hash/revision, rule version and internal W; source rows, cases and corrections have separate dedupe keys. Revisions append evidence rather than overwrite it. A crash after repair commit is safe on rerun through the posting key; an unmarked case is not permission to post again. Unexplained differences remain in Review rather than forcing a zero-difference metric through arbitrary reversals.

图上补记走 Cases→Repair→Payment DB，数据库提交再沿 Payment DB→Repair→Cases 返回；reject 注记否决“内部缺一笔就重新扣一次”。Review 的 held 只表示工单保留。对账修的是账与状态，移动外部资金必须是单独授权的收款或退款业务操作。最终监控看未决笔数、未决金额、最老年龄、报告缺失与修复率；暂时对不上可以解释为有证据的在途窗口，永久无人处理不能叫最终一致。Ch25 的任务调度接手定时查询与对账运行，本章把它们每次执行的财务边界讲清。

Repair follows Cases→Repair→Payment DB; COMMIT returns Payment DB→Repair→Cases. The reject note prohibits charging again for a missing internal row. Review held is only a retained-case receipt. Recon repairs books and knowledge; money movement is a separately authorized payment/refund operation. Monitor unresolved count, amount, age, missing reports and repair rates. Explain evidence-backed in-flight windows, but unmanaged permanent differences are not eventual consistency. Ch25 schedules these jobs; this chapter defines their financial boundaries.

追问与翻车点Follow-up questions and failure traps

面试官把约束改了，哪笔随之改变Which stroke changes when constraints change

如果问“需要部分 capture 呢”，把一次 pay_no 下的 capture_id 扩展成多个，每个独立金额效果键，累计捕获额度锁在同一聚合；refund 必须引用相应已捕获金额。授权与捕获拆开后，授权成功不走本章的收款 journal。如果问“数据库分区了”，拒绝不可确认的财务提交，inbox 和外部状态保留恢复证据；不要仅靠一个 CAP 标签代替解释已确认写的持久性与切主前栅栏。

For partial capture, add multiple capture IDs under pay_no, each independently keyed, and lock cumulative capture bounds; refunds reference captured amounts. Authorization alone does not post a capture journal. During database partition, refuse unconfirmable financial commits and retain evidence. CAP labels cannot replace durability and failover-fencing explanations.

如果问“订单通知慢会不会重新扣钱”，指第四组：只重投 payment.paid，不重放渠道命令。如果问“账都平了为什么还有差异”，举两边同时写错金额的反例，再指出第五、六组的金额与外部核验。如果问“余额要快读呢”，用 journal 投影与明确更新时间；投影可重建但不替代流水。分片、专用账本引擎与多地域只能在事务容量、热点或容灾目标改变后再加，不用更复杂的图掩盖未决付款的责任边界。

Slow order notifications replay payment.paid, never the channel charge. A balanced journal can still have equally wrong amounts, requiring amount and external checks. Fast balance reads use rebuildable projections with clear freshness, not replacement authority. Add shards, specialized ledgers or regions only when measured capacity, hotspots or recovery goals demand them.

收尾：三种成功，三个恢复入口Wrap-up: three successes and three recovery paths

合上正文沿六张终图口述：创单把意图与命令持久化，调用渠道时尝试键稳定；不知道结果就保持 UNKNOWN。回调先收进 inbox，再按业务效果入账；账本、支付状态与 outbox 一个提交，订单通知晚到可重试。退款自己建键，锁下预留，超时不放回额度；对账固定批次和水位，补记用原 posting_key，迟到和不明差异留工单。三种“成功”分别是受理、渠道确认、账本提交，用户和下游各自只能消费与自己承诺对应的事实。

Walk the six complete boards: persist intent/command, keep stable provider keys and retain UNKNOWN. Receive callbacks durably, then post business effects. Commit journal, payment state and outbox together; retry delayed order notifications. Independently key and reserve refunds. Reconcile fixed batches/watermarks, repair with the original posting key and review uncertainty. Acceptance, channel confirmation and ledger commit are distinct successes.
