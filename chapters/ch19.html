30 秒开场、范围和会改变设计的数The 30-second open, the scope, and the numbers that change the design

中文

邻近服务给定一个点、一个半径和实体类型，返回半径内按真实距离截断的候选。公开地点的坐标可以旧一点；正在移动的车或打开位置共享的人，新鲜度是秒级。写入是校验、限频、upsert 最新位置，再异步更新索引。查询先算圆覆盖的格子，再按距离过滤。精确经纬度留在信任边界内，陌生人只看到地点图钉或移动端的距离桶。

第一版停在这条读写闭环。地图瓦片、路网、骑手撮合和推荐都不进第一张图。题目若被改成其中一种，先承认差别，再问还要不要半径召回。超过十分钟还在加范围，就把假设写上白板。

下面每一组数字都标成教学假设，用来删复杂度，不是某家地图公司或打车公司的内部监控。服务区域按北纬 40° 附近的一座城市来算，1° 按 111 km 粗算，经度再乘 cos 40° ≈ 0.766。格子用长度 6 的 geohash 前缀当默认键：30 个交错比特里经纬度各 15 位，赤道上大约 1.22 km × 0.61 km；乘上 0.766 之后，经度边约 0.93 km，纬度边仍约 0.61 km。

English

A nearby service answers one question: given a point, a radius, and an entity type, return candidates inside the radius, cut by true distance. I serve two kinds of entities. A restaurant or store pin can be a bit old. A moving car, or a person who opted into location sharing, needs freshness on the order of seconds. Writes validate, throttle, upsert the latest position, then update the spatial index asynchronously. Reads compute the cells the circle covers, then filter by distance. Exact coordinates stay inside the trust boundary. A stranger sees a place’s published pin, or a distance bucket for a mover.

Version one stops at that read/write loop. Map tiles, road networks, courier matching, and recommendation ranking stay off the first diagram. Tiles are another cache and projection problem. Matching is a two-sided state machine. Ranking is a model. If the interviewer turns the prompt into one of those, name the difference and ask whether radius recall is still the question. Past ten minutes of scope, write the assumptions down and draw.

Every number below is a teaching assumption used to delete machinery, not an internal dashboard from a maps or ride-hailing company. The service region is a city near 40°N. One degree is taken as 111 km, and longitude is multiplied by cos 40° ≈ 0.766. The default key is a length-6 geohash prefix: 30 interleaved bits, 15 for each axis, about 1.22 km by 0.61 km at the equator. After the cosine, the longitude edge is about 0.93 km and the latitude edge stays about 0.61 km.

一次把热路径从「存轨迹」改成「只留最新点」的乘法The multiplication that removes traces from the hot path

中文

先写移动端的更新。80,000 × 4 = 320,000 次接受的更新每分钟，再除以 60，得到每秒约 5,333 次写入。这个 4 次/分钟不是礼貌，是预算。若客户端按 1 Hz 上报，同样 8 万实体就是每秒 8 万次写入，大约 15 倍。限频把多余的点丢在 Location Service 里，存储只看见被接受的那一次。被丢掉的点不进队列，也不进索引。

再写字节。一条最新位置按 96 字节估算。热路径若把每次接受的更新都追加成历史行，就是 5,333 × 96 × 86,400。先算 5,333 × 96 = 511,968 字节/秒，再乘 86,400 秒，大约 4.42 × 1010 字节，约 44 GB/天。三副本大约 133 GB/天，邻近查询不读这些旧点。只留最新一行是 80,000 × 96 = 7,680,000 字节，约 7.7 MB。所以主键是 entity_id，写入是按 observed_at 的条件 upsert。完整轨迹不在这条路径上。

查询侧温和。800 万次/天除以 86,400，平均约 93 QPS；12 倍峰值约 1,110 QPS。按 4 KB 的响应，1,110 × 4 KB 约 4.4 MB/s，带宽不是矛盾。矛盾是格子。高峰里 20% 的查询以同一格为中心，0.20 × 1,110 ≈ 222 QPS 打在一个键上。该键若有 1 万个 id、每个 16 字节，一次未命中约 160 KB，222 × 160 KB ≈ 35 MB/s，查询还要再读周围十几格。缓存键必须是格子 id，例如 cell:wx4g0b。用原始经纬度的话，GPS 抖动会打出无限多个键。

地点写入用来对比，不拿来撑架构。200 万个公开地点、每个一年改两次，大约是 2 × 106 × 2 / (365 × 86,400) ≈ 0.13 次/秒。餐馆坐标可以按小时甚至按天新鲜；它不会制造 5,333 次/秒的写。同一套格子键两边都用，SLO 分开写。

English

Start with mover updates. 80,000 × 4 = 320,000 accepted updates per minute. Divide by 60 and the write rate is about 5,333 per second. Four per minute is a budget, not etiquette. At 1 Hz the same 80,000 entities would write 80,000 times a second, about 15× more. The throttle drops the extra points in Location Service. Storage sees only an accepted update. Dropped points do not enter the queue or the index.

Then bytes. Assume 96 bytes for one latest position: entity id, type, latitude, longitude, client observation time, server accept time, cell id, version, and a little index overhead. If the hot path appended every accepted update, the product would be 5,333 × 96 × 86,400. First, 5,333 × 96 = 511,968 bytes per second. Times 86,400 seconds, that is about 4.42 × 1010 bytes, roughly 44 GB per day of raw appends. Three replicas are about 133 GB per day, and nearby queries do not read those old points. Keeping one latest row for the same entities is 80,000 × 96 = 7,680,000 bytes, about 7.7 MB. The Location Store’s primary key is therefore entity_id, and the write is a conditional upsert on observation time. A full trace is not on this path. An audit log, if anyone truly needs one, is a separate cold log, off by default, and not an input to radius queries.

Queries look mild. Eight million a day divided by 86,400 is about 93 QPS average. A teaching 12× peak for lunch and a match is about 1,110 QPS. At an assumed 4 KB of JSON, 1,110 × 4 KB is about 4.4 MB/s. Bandwidth is not the fight. Cells are. Assume 20% of peak queries are centered on one downtown or stadium cell: 0.20 × 1,110 ≈ 222 QPS on one cell key. If that key holds 10,000 ids at 16 bytes each, one uncached read is about 160 KB, and 222 × 160 KB ≈ 35 MB/s, before the query also reads a dozen surrounding cells. The cache key has to be the cell id, for example cell:wx4g0b, not the raw latitude and longitude. GPS jitter would turn the latter into unbounded keys.

Place writes are the contrast, not the architecture. Two million published places edited twice a year is about 2 × 106 × 2 / (365 × 86,400) ≈ 0.13 writes per second. A restaurant pin can be fresh on the order of hours or a day. It does not create 5,333 writes per second. Both sides share the cell key. Their SLOs stay separate.

格子边长怎样把半径 API 定下来How the cell edge sets the radius API

中文

长度 6 是 6 × 5 = 30 比特。交错之后经纬度各 15 位。纬度范围 180°，一格是 180 / 32,768 × 111 km ≈ 0.61 km。经度范围 360°，赤道上一格是 360 / 32,768 × 111 km ≈ 1.22 km；在 40°N 再乘 0.766，约 0.93 km。这是我们自己的几何，用来选扇出；Gustavo Niemeyer 2008 年的公开说明建立的是另一件事：这种编码有任意精度，相近位置倾向于共享前缀，从末尾去掉字符会损失精度，算法放在公共领域 [1]。比特怎样交错、半径怎样覆盖 1+8 个区间，本章用 Redis GEOADD 文档里写明的实现来对照 [2]，不把这些句子算到那篇博客上。

从格心向北，紧邻格的远边距格心是 1.5 × 0.61 ≈ 0.92 km。半径 1 km 已经大于 0.92 km，所以圆会碰到紧邻格再往外的那一格。经度方向 1.5 × 0.93 ≈ 1.40 km，大于 1 km，左右通常只到紧邻格。格心处的典型覆盖大约是纬度 5 格、经度 3 格，共 15 个长度 6 的格子。把直径 2 km 的外接正方形以最坏对齐放上网格，大约会碰到 5 × 4 = 20 格。实现按「格子与圆相交」选择，k 在这个量级，而不是一个写死的 8。

半径再放大，扇出换档。2 km 的外接盒在长度 6 上最坏大约 8 × 6 = 48 格，还允许，但必须有格子缓存。5 km 若仍走长度 6，外接盒大约 18 × 12 = 216 格，一次查询太散。长度 5 的格子大约 3.7 km × 4.9 km，5 km 的外接盒最坏大约 4 × 4 = 16 格，所以 5 km 这一档改查更短的前缀。50 km 仍用长度 6 的话，外接盒大约 166 × 109 ≈ 1.8 万格。1,110 QPS 乘这个扇出，索引会被一次恶意或错误的半径打穿。因此 API 只接受 500、1,000、2,000、5,000 米，更大直接 400，在算出那 1.8 万个键之前就拒绝。

一座 40 km × 40 km 的城区，长度 6 的格子大约 (40 / 0.93) × (40 / 0.61) ≈ 2,800 个。大多数是空的。容量问题不是「全国有多少格」，而是其中一个格在球赛时同时吃下查询和移动端写入。后面的热点图只处理那一个键。

English

Length 6 is 6 × 5 = 30 bits. After interleaving, each axis has 15 bits. Latitude spans 180°, so one cell is 180 / 32,768 × 111 km ≈ 0.61 km. Longitude spans 360°, so at the equator one cell is 360 / 32,768 × 111 km ≈ 1.22 km; at 40°N, times 0.766, about 0.93 km. That geometry is ours, and it chooses the fan-out. What Gustavo Niemeyer’s 2008 public note actually establishes is different: the encoding has arbitrary precision, nearby positions tend to share a prefix, dropping characters from the end loses precision, and he placed the algorithm in the public domain [1]. How bits are interleaved, and how a radius checks 1+8 areas, is taken from the behavior Redis documents for GEOADD [2]. Those sentences are not attributed to the blog post.

North from a cell center, the far edge of the immediate neighbor is 1.5 × 0.61 ≈ 0.92 km. A 1 km radius is already larger than 0.92 km, so the circle reaches the cell beyond that neighbor. On longitude, 1.5 × 0.93 ≈ 1.40 km, which is more than 1 km, so east and west usually stop at the immediate neighbor. A typical cover from the center is about five cells of latitude by three of longitude, 15 length-6 cells. A 2 km bounding square, worst-case aligned to the grid, touches about 5 × 4 = 20 cells. The implementation selects cells that intersect the circle. k sits in that range. It is not a hardcoded 8.

Larger radii change tier. A 2 km bounding box on length 6 is about 8 × 6 = 48 cells at worst. That is allowed, and it is why the cell cache exists. Five kilometres still on length 6 is about 18 × 12 = 216 cells, too wide for one query. A length-5 cell is about 3.7 km by 4.9 km, and a 5 km bounding box touches about 4 × 4 = 16 of them, so the 5 km tier queries the shorter prefix. Fifty kilometres on length 6 is about 166 × 109 ≈ 18,000 cells. Multiply that fan-out by 1,110 QPS and one bad radius saturates the index. The API therefore accepts only 500, 1,000, 2,000, and 5,000 metres. Anything larger is a 400, rejected before those 18,000 keys are computed.

A 40 km by 40 km urban box holds about (40 / 0.93) × (40 / 0.61) ≈ 2,800 length-6 cells. Most are empty. Capacity is not “how many cells exist nationwide.” It is one cell taking both queries and mover writes during a match. The hotspot deck deals with that one key.

高层架构：写入和查询在同一张终图上High-level architecture: write and query on one final frame

顺着终图走一遍

客户端把 PUT /v1/locations/{entity_id} 和 GET /v1/nearby 交给 Edge。Edge 卸下 TLS，做粗准入，内存里没有坐标。权威只有 Location Store 里的最新一行。

Location Service 拥有规则。更新时它检查范围、执行每分钟 4 次的限频、比较 observed_at，再对 Location Store 做条件 upsert。查询时它取与圆相交的格子，用存下来的经纬度算距离，丢掉圆外的点，按实体去重，然后决定哪些字段可以离开信任边界。绿色回程上已经是候选列表。

Location Store 的主键是 entity_id，行里有 lat、lng、updated_at、cell_id。Spatial Index 是可重启的派生结构，键是 cell_id，所以画成圆柱。它允许比 Store 旧大约 2 秒：换格进 Cell Queue，只有 Index Writer 改成员。200 可以带 index=pending，表示行已落盘，邻居查询还在窗口里。

异步边来自 5,333 次/秒。若每次 upsert 都同步等待旧格删除和新格插入，上报延迟就绑在索引上。2 秒落在 30 秒新鲜度和 100 米距离桶里，盖不住刚好跨格的那一下，所以窗口要画出来。地点编辑约 0.13 次/秒，付得起同步写；第一版仍走同一条队列，避免两套协议。对 24 小时的地点 SLO，这点延迟没有产品差别。

Walk the final frame

The client sends PUT /v1/locations/{entity_id} and GET /v1/nearby through the edge. The edge terminates TLS, applies a coarse admission check, and holds no coordinates. The authority is the latest row in the Location Store.

Location Service owns the rules. On an update it checks the range, enforces four accepts per minute, compares observed_at, and conditionally upserts the Location Store. On a query it loads the cells that intersect the circle, measures distance from the stored coordinates, drops points outside the circle, dedupes by entity, and decides which fields may leave the trust boundary. The green reply is already a candidate list.

The Location Store’s primary key is entity_id. The row holds lat, lng, updated_at, and cell_id. The Spatial Index is a derived structure that survives restart, keyed by cell_id, so it is a cylinder. It may lag the store by about two seconds: the cell move enters the Cell Queue, and only the Index Writer changes membership. A 200 may carry index=pending. The row is durable, and a neighbor query can still be inside the window.

The asynchronous edge comes from 5,333 writes per second. If every upsert waited for the old-cell delete and the new-cell insert, report latency would stick to the index. Two seconds sits inside the 30-second freshness target and the 100-metre bucket. It does not hide a point that just crossed a cell edge, so the window is drawn. Place edits are about 0.13 per second and could be synchronous. Version one still uses one queue, so there are not two protocols. Against a 24-hour place SLO, that lag is not a product difference.

只剩餐馆目录时，写入可以同步：一次事务更新地点行和格子成员，队列不必出现。那是 0.13 次/秒。仍有移动端时，服务、队列和 Writer 可以住在同一进程，甚至用内存通道。只要 upsert 和换格不是同一个提交，窗口就在。图是为了让窗口可见。

单键 Redis GEO 是原型替代。GEOADD 把经纬度交错成 52 位整数放进有序集合，半径查询检查 1+8 个区间再丢掉外部点，距离是球面 Haversine，文档写明最坏约 0.5%，可索引纬度约到 ±85.05112878° [2]。一个键承担不了 5,333 次/秒再加 222 QPS。默认仍是一格一个键，由服务扇出。多地域和把响应缓存在边缘都不进第一张图：格子键已经按城市分布。第一版只索引 ±85° 以内，超出返回 422，因为 k 是在 40°N 算的；极区同样的公里数会跨过更多经度格。若引擎是 Redis GEO，还要遵守它自己的纬度上限 [2]。

English

With only a restaurant catalog, the write can be synchronous: one transaction updates the place row and its cell membership, and the queue can disappear. That is 0.13 per second. With movers still present, the service, the queue, and the writer can share a process, even an in-memory channel. The window remains unless the upsert and the cell move are one commit. The diagram exists to show that window.

A single-key Redis GEO index is the prototype alternative. GEOADD stores interleaved latitude and longitude bits as a 52-bit sorted-set score. A radius query checks 1+8 areas and discards outsiders. Distance is Haversine on a sphere, with a documented worst case around 0.5%, and indexed latitude runs to about ±85.05112878° [2]. One key cannot take 5,333 writes per second plus 222 QPS. The default stays one key per cell, with the service doing the fan-out. Multi-region replication and an edge-cached response stay off the first picture: cell keys are already arranged by city. Version one indexes only within ±85° and returns 422 outside it, because k was computed at 40°N. Near the pole the same kilometre radius crosses more longitude cells. A Redis GEO engine also has to obey its own latitude ceiling [2].

A lone Nearby API box does not say who owns the latest coordinate, which edge returns the result, or whether the index update is synchronous or queued. The four edge types on the final frame are the answer: solid requests, the green candidate list, the authoritative row, and the orange cell-move.

API 和数据模型API and data model

中文

更新用实体自己的 id 做路径，避免把「同一次意图」和「同一个坐标」混成一个幂等键。移动端的 GPS 会在几秒内给出一串不同的点，它们都是新观测，不是同一次重试。重试的判定是：同一个 entity_id，加上同一个 observed_at，或者一个更旧的 observed_at。服务已经存了更新的观测时间时，返回 200 和当前行，不移动格子。这是条件写，不是先读再写。两个副本同时处理乱序包时，输的那个看见存储里的 observed_at 已经不比自己旧，upsert 不生效。

限频也写在这个请求上。移动端滚动 60 秒内已经有 4 次被接受的更新，第 5 次返回 429，带 Retry-After。计数放在 Location Store 的同一行里（窗口起点和窗口内次数），用同一次条件更新改掉，避免两台 Location Service 各数各的。地点编辑走商户凭证，不走这道 GPS 限频；它仍然是一行 upsert。纬度不在 [-90, 90]、经度不在 [-180, 180] 返回 400。纬度超出 ±85° 的可索引带返回 422，行可以不写，以免索引几何和前面算的 k 对不上。Store 暂时不可用返回 503，客户端用同一 observed_at 重试是安全的，因为条件 upsert 不会把旧点盖掉新点。

English

The update path uses the entity’s own id. That avoids mixing “one intent” with “one coordinate.” A mover’s GPS emits a series of different points in a few seconds. Those are new observations, not one retry. A retry is the same entity_id with the same observed_at, or an older observed_at. If the service already stored a newer observation time, it returns 200 and the current row, and it does not move the cell. That is a conditional write, not a read followed by a write. When two replicas handle reordered packets, the loser sees an observed_at that is already at least as new, and the upsert does not apply.

The throttle sits on the same request. After four accepted mover updates in a rolling 60 seconds, the fifth returns 429 with Retry-After. The counter lives on the same Location Store row (window start and count) and moves in the same conditional update, so two Location Service replicas do not keep private counts. A place edit uses a merchant credential and does not pass through the GPS throttle. It is still one-row upsert. Latitude outside [-90, 90] or longitude outside [-180, 180] is 400. Latitude outside the ±85° indexed band is 422, and the row is not written, so the index geometry stays the one used to compute k. If the store is unavailable the response is 503. Retrying with the same observed_at is safe, because the conditional upsert will not replace a newer point with an older one.

Anything larger is 400 with radius_rejected. It does not return a silently truncated fake result. Type filtering happens after the distance filter. An empty list means the circle truly has no entity inside the freshness window.

The response forks by type. That is the privacy contract, not a display detail. A place may include its published lat/lng because the merchant published the pin. A mover has a distance and a 100-metre bucket, and no coordinates. Reading one’s own latest position does not go through nearby. It is GET /v1/locations/{entity_id}, and it requires that entity’s credential. That read hits the Location Store directly, skips index lag, and satisfies “I just reported a point and I need to see it.” A stranger’s nearby call does not get that shortcut.

中文

CellMember 上的 lat/lng 是索引应用那一刻的副本，用来在不回表的情况下做距离过滤。它可能比 Location 行旧最多一个索引延迟。新鲜度判断用 updated_at，不把「人还在这个格子里」当成「坐标是现在的」。地点若还需要店名、类目、营业时间，那些列在 Place 详情里，nearby 的列表只在候选已经按距离留下之后才按 id 去补。详情缺失时可以只返回 id 和距离，不要为了店名把整次查询失败掉。

热路径上没有 LocationHistory。44 GB/天的追加一旦进了主库，备份、压缩和主键都会跟着涨，而半径查询的工作集仍然是那 7.7 MB 量级的最新行加上格子成员。版本号用来让缓存和成员行对齐：Writer 先写新格、再删旧格之后，把 Location.version 递增。缓存值若比这个版本旧，就当未命中。不要用原始经纬度当缓存键，也不要把整段轨迹塞进 CellMember。

English

The lat/lng on CellMember is a copy from the moment the index applied the move. It lets the distance filter run without a join back to the store. It can be older than the Location row by at most one index lag. Freshness uses updated_at. Membership in a cell is not proof that the coordinate is current. If a place also needs a name, category, and hours, those live on a Place detail row. The nearby list hydrates them by id only after distance has already kept the candidate. A missing detail can still return the id and the distance. Do not fail the whole query because a shop name is absent.

There is no LocationHistory on the hot path. Once a 44 GB/day append lands in the primary database, backups, compaction, and the primary key all grow with it, while the radius query’s working set is still the latest rows, on the order of 7.7 MB, plus cell members. The version lines the cache up with membership: after the writer adds the new cell and removes the old one, it bumps Location.version. A cache value older than that version is a miss. Do not key the cache by raw coordinates, and do not stuff a trace into CellMember.

写路径：最新行先落盘，索引可以短暂不同意Write path: the latest row commits first, and the index may briefly disagree

按真实顺序走一遍

以实体 m_481 从 wx4g0b 走进北邻 wx4g0c 为例。客户端提交 lat、lng 和 observed_at。Location Service 先看范围：这个点在 ±85° 里，继续。再看该行上的限频窗口。滚动一分钟内已接受的次数小于 4，就把次数和窗口一起放进即将写入的条件更新；否则 429，Store 和队列都不变。然后比较 observed_at。新于存储中的值才允许改 lat、lng、cell_id 和 updated_at。成功后服务已经可以返回 200 和 index=pending。客户端超时后用同一个 observed_at 重试，会读到同一行，不会再搬一次格。

与此同时，一条换格事件进入 Cell Queue：entity_id、from=wx4g0b、to=wx4g0c、lat、lng、updated_at、version。Index Writer 先在 wx4g0c 插入 CellMember，再从 wx4g0b 删除。插入和删除之间，同一个人会出现在两格里。查询若两格都在覆盖范围内，按 entity_id 去重，留下 updated_at 更新的那条。若 Writer 在插入之后崩溃，修复任务看见 Store 的 cell_id 是 wx4g0c、旧格仍有成员，把删除补上。若先删后插，崩溃会把人从索引里抹掉，直到全量对账。5,333 次/秒的移动不接受这种空洞，所以顺序固定为先加后删。

队列若丢了事件，Store 已是新格，索引会停在旧格。对账按 version 找出 cell_id 对不上的行再重放，可以是分钟级，不改变 200 已经返回。应答在网上丢了，客户端用同一 observed_at 重试，条件 upsert 读回同一行。主键是 entity_id，不会插入第二行。

Walk it in execution order

Take entity m_481 moving from wx4g0b into the northern neighbor wx4g0c. The client submits lat, lng, and observed_at. Location Service checks the range. The point is inside ±85°, so it continues. It then checks the throttle on that row. If the rolling minute still has fewer than four accepts, the count and the window go into the same conditional update. Otherwise the response is 429 and neither the store nor the queue changes. Next it compares observed_at. Only a newer value may change lat, lng, cell_id, and updated_at. After that succeeds the service may already return 200 with index=pending. A timeout retry with the same observed_at reads that row and does not move the cell again.

Meanwhile a cell-move enters the queue: entity_id, from=wx4g0b, to=wx4g0c, lat, lng, updated_at, version. The Index Writer inserts the CellMember in wx4g0c and only then deletes wx4g0b. Between those two writes the same person is in both cells. If a query covers both, it dedupes by entity_id and keeps the newer updated_at. If the writer crashes after the insert, repair sees Store.cell_id = wx4g0c while the old cell still has the member, and it finishes the delete. Delete-then-insert would erase the person from the index until a full reconciliation. At 5,333 moves a second that gap is unacceptable, so the order stays add, then remove.

Two narrower windows remain. The queue can lose the event: the store is on the new cell and the index stays on the old one forever. A reconciler scans rows whose Store.cell_id disagrees with every CellMember and replays the move. That scan can be minute-scale. It does not change the fact that 200 already returned. The other window is a lost response. The client retries, the conditional upsert sees the same observed_at, and it returns the committed row. There is no second row, because the primary key is entity_id. That is the concurrency benefit of not appending history on the hot path: a retry means “what is that row now,” not “insert another one.”

为什么不把轨迹写在热路径上

每次 GPS 都 INSERT 历史，再按实体取最大时间，会把 7.7 MB 的工作集变成约 44 GB/天，还要一次聚合。半径查询要的是现在谁在圆里。历史还会把「一个当前格」变成可重建的轨迹。以后真要轨迹，另开冷路径，默认关，nearby 不读。

同步双写能让窗口消失：Store 和 Index 一次提交。代价是 5,333 次/秒都要跨实体主键和旧格、新格。索引一慢，上报就慢，80 ms 的更新 p99 保不住。地点编辑付得起，移动端付不起。第一版用队列把窗口限制在 2 秒，先加后删把失败从「消失」改成「重复」，查询去重吸收重复。

面试里一个常见的不完整答案是「把坐标 append 到日志，附近查询扫最近一分钟」。最近一分钟在 5,333 次/秒下大约是 5,333 × 60 ≈ 32 万行，而且它们不按格子聚集。扫这一分钟再算距离，就是把半径查询做成了时间范围扫描。最新一行加格子键，才把「附近」变成对 k 个 cell_id 的定位。

Why the trace stays off the hot path

The design that looks more complete inserts a history row on every GPS fix and lets the query take the max timestamp per entity. The write then stops being a 7.7 MB working set and becomes the earlier product: about 44 GB per day, plus an aggregate to find the latest row. A radius query asks who is in the circle now, not the polyline this person walked today. History also changes privacy from “store one current cell” into “store a reconstructable trace,” which collides with the trust boundary below. If a product later needs traces, that is another cold path, off by default, with its own retention, and nearby does not read it.

A synchronous dual write is the other clean-looking alternative: commit the store and the index in one distributed transaction and the window disappears. The cost is that each of 5,333 writes a second commits across two key spaces, the entity primary key and the old and new cells. When the index slows down, the report slows down, and that ties itself to the 80 ms update p99. Place edits can pay that cost. The mover budget cannot. Version one uses the queue to cap the window at two seconds, and add-then-remove turns the failure mode from “missing” into “duplicate.” The query dedupes the duplicate.

A common incomplete answer is “append coordinates to a log and scan the last minute for nearby.” One minute at 5,333 per second is about 5,333 × 60 ≈ 320,000 rows, and they are not clustered by cell. Scanning that minute and then computing distance turns a radius query into a time-range scan. A latest row plus a cell key is what turns “nearby” into a lookup of k cell ids.

读路径：圆被格子边界切开，所以要扇出Read path: the cell edge cuts the circle, so the query fans out

按执行顺序走一遍 1 km 查询

客户端请求 lat=39.9051、lng=116.4056、radius_m=1000、types 含 place 和 mover。Location Service 确认半径在允许档里，把查询点编成长度 6 的格子。用同一套交错比特，39.9042°N、116.4074°E 这个示例落在 wx4g0b；它的北邻是 wx4g0c，东邻是 wx4g10，南邻是 wx4fbz，再往北一格是 wx4g0f。这些字符串是按「经度比特与纬度比特交错、每 5 位一组」算出的例子，用来让扇出可核对。Niemeyer 说明的是前缀和精度这组性质 [1]，不是这张邻格表。

服务不发 cell_id LIKE 'wx4g0%'。wx4g0b 和南邻 wx4fbz 的公共前缀只有 wx4。南边整行在这条 LIKE 外面，而圆会切进南格。长度 3 的 wx4 能罩住南边，但 15 比特的格子纬度约 180/128×111 ≈ 156 km，40°N 的经度再乘 0.766 约 120 km。用百公里前缀回答 1 km，就变回扫描。正确的读是逐个取与圆相交的长度 6 格子：格心约 15 个，外接盒最坏约 20 个。图上的 wx4g0f 和 wx4fbz 就是「8 邻居」和「一个前缀」最容易漏的两格。

成员取回后做精确距离。格子是矩形，圆是圆，格内点可以在半径外。距离用存下的 lat/lng 算。若改走 Redis 单键，文档用的是球面 Haversine，最坏约 0.5%，够附近列表，不够测量 [2]。服务自己丢弃圆外点。同一 entity_id 出现两次时留较新的 updated_at。mover 老于 30 秒就从陌生人结果去掉；place 在 24 小时内保留。按距离截断一页，脱敏后沿回程返回。

Walk a 1 km query in order

The client requests lat=39.9051, lng=116.4056, radius_m=1000, and types including place and mover. Location Service checks that the radius is an allowed tier and encodes the query point as a length-6 cell. Under the same interleaved bits, the example point 39.9042°N, 116.4074°E falls in wx4g0b. Its north neighbor is wx4g0c, its east neighbor is wx4g10, its south neighbor is wx4fbz, and one cell further north is wx4g0f. Those strings are an example computed by interleaving longitude and latitude bits and grouping them by fives, so the fan-out can be checked. Niemeyer’s note is about the prefix and precision properties [1], not this neighbor table.

The service does not issue one cell_id LIKE 'wx4g0%'. wx4g0b and wx4fbz share only the prefix wx4. The fourth character already differs. The whole southern row sits outside that LIKE, and a 1 km circle near the border cuts into the southern cell. The length-3 prefix wx4 would cover the south, but three characters are 15 bits: latitude is about 180/128×111 ≈ 156 km, and longitude at 40°N times 0.766 is about 120 km. Answering a 1 km question with a hundred-kilometre prefix removes the fan-out and brings the scan back. The correct read computes the length-6 cell ids that intersect the circle and loads each one. From the center that is about 15. The bounding square can touch about 20. The drawing includes the second cell north and the southern cell with the broken prefix because those are the cells an “eight neighbors” slogan or a single prefix misses.

After the members return, exact distance runs. A cell is a rectangle and the query is a circle, so a point in the cell can sit outside the radius. Distance uses the stored lat/lng. If this path were a single Redis key instead, the documented distance is Haversine on a sphere, with a stated worst-case error around 0.5%, fine for a social nearby list and not fine for survey work [2]. This service computes distance itself and drops points outside the circle. It does not treat “in the cell” as “in the radius.” If add-then-remove left the same entity_id twice, the newer updated_at wins. A mover older than 30 seconds is removed from stranger results. A place younger than 24 hours stays. The service sorts by distance, truncates to a page, redacts by type, and returns along the reply.

扇出、空结果和两种替代

1,110 QPS × 约 15 格 ≈ 1.7 万次格子读取每秒，摊在约 2,800 个城区格子上。平均值低，所以按格子键做缓存和拆分，不按平均 QPS 分库。5 km 若仍用长度 6，外接盒约 216 格，1,110 × 216 会到二十多万次读取每秒，所以这一档改走长度 5，把 k 压到约 16 以内。圆里点少时不要默默放大到 50 km。空的 candidates 是合法结果。

有空间索引时，lat BETWEEN 加 lng BETWEEN 再滤距离，可以当只有餐馆、写入可忽略时的第一版。在约 1,110 QPS、表上又没有空间索引时，纬度范围会扫出一条宽带，经度只是再滤一次。两条独立 B 树不是「附近」。格子键把这个谓词收成 k 次一维定位。

只查当前格在点远离边界时看起来对，边界把圆切开时对面几米的店会消失。Redis 在单个有序集合里仍检查 1+8 个区间再丢掉外部点 [2]，圆外过滤省不掉。多键索引把这 1+8 换成「所有相交格子」：长度 6 的纬度边约 0.61 km，1 km 会超出那一圈。把 1+8 背成一切半径的常数，就会漏掉外面那一格。

Fan-out, empty results, and two alternatives

About 1,110 QPS times about 15 cells is about 17,000 cell reads per second, spread across about 2,800 urban cells. The average is low, so the architecture does not shard on average QPS. It prepares a cache and a split for the cell key. If the 5 km tier stayed on length 6, the bounding box is about 216 cells, and 1,110 × 216 would be a couple of hundred thousand cell reads per second. That is why 5 km moves to length 5 and keeps k on the order of 16 or fewer. When the circle feels empty, do not silently grow the radius to 50 km. An empty candidates list is a legal result. A larger radius is another request, and it changes the fan-out tier.

One real alternative is a two-dimensional range in a database: lat BETWEEN and lng BETWEEN, then a distance filter. With a spatial index that can be version one, especially when the catalog is restaurants and writes are negligible. At this QPS, on a table with no spatial index, it does not work. Each of 1,110 queries a second can scan a large latitude band, and the longitude predicate only filters that set again. Two independent B-trees are not the two-dimensional predicate “nearby.” A cell key turns that predicate into k one-dimensional lookups.

The other alternative is to query only the current cell. The implementation is shortest, and it looks right when the point is far from a border. When the border cuts the circle, a restaurant a few metres on the other side disappears. That is a functional bug, not an approximation. Inside one sorted set, Redis still checks 1+8 areas and then discards outsiders [2]. Even when the engine builds the neighborhood for you, the outside-the-circle filter stays. Our multi-key index generalizes that 1+8 into “every intersecting cell,” because at length 6 and 1 km the latitude direction steps past the ring of eight neighbors. Memorizing the documented 1+8 as a constant for every radius misses the next cell on a 0.61 km latitude edge.

索引结构：一次写入改一格，一次查询读 k 格Index structure: a write updates one cell, a read queries k cells

这张格子为什么是默认结构

Niemeyer 公开的性质是：编码可以任意加长以换精度，相近的位置倾向于有相似前缀，从末尾删字符会逐渐变粗 [1]。把这个性质用成索引时，前缀就是缩放级别，长度 6 是我们按 1 km 扇出选的级别，不是编码本身规定的唯一长度。写入只需要知道点落进哪一个最细的存储格，所以成员边只有一条。查询必须覆盖圆切到的每一格，所以读边有 k 条。k 由半径和边长算出来，图上的多条边就是这个算式。

单键 Redis GEO 把同一件事折进一个有序集合：52 位交错整数当 score，查询时检查 1+8 个区间再丢掉外部成员 [2]。原型阶段这是合理的，因为服务不用自己维护邻格表，引擎已经在做。规模上来之后那个 key 无法按格子拆开，5,333 次写入和热点查询共享一个有序集合。多键设计保留它的「查邻域再过滤」语义，把邻域从固定的 9 个区间换成服务算出来的相交集合。没有 GEODEL 这个命令，删除成员用的是有序集合的 ZREM [2]。我们的 Index Writer 删除旧格成员时，对应的就是按 entity_id 从那个 cell 键里移除，而不是依赖一个地理删除原语。

Why this grid is the default structure

The properties Niemeyer published are: the code can be lengthened for more precision, nearby positions tend to share a prefix, and deleting characters from the end coarsens it [1]. Used as an index, the prefix is the zoom level. Length 6 is the level we chose for a 1 km fan-out. The encoding does not crown it as the only legal length. A write only needs the one finest storage cell the point falls in, so there is one membership edge. A query has to cover every cell the circle cuts, so there are k read edges. k comes from the radius and the edge length. The edges on the drawing are that arithmetic.

A single-key Redis GEO index folds the same idea into one sorted set: a 52-bit interleaved integer as the score, then a query that checks 1+8 areas and discards outsiders [2]. That is a sound prototype, because the service does not keep its own neighbor table. The engine already does it. At the write rate in this chapter that key cannot be split by cell. The 5,333 writes and the hot queries share one sorted set. The multi-key design keeps the “search a neighborhood, then filter” behavior and replaces a fixed nine areas with the intersecting set the service computes. There is no GEODEL command. Removing a member is ZREM on the sorted set [2]. When our Index Writer drops the old cell, the matching operation is to remove the entity_id from that cell key, not to call a geographic delete primitive.

四叉树和 S2：用在哪，不替代这张格子图Quadtrees and S2: where they fit, without replacing this grid

中文

Finkel 和 Bentley 1974 年把 quad tree 定义成组合键上的检索结构，并给出区域内检索；删除和合并被他们写成固有的困难操作 [3]。半径查询正是区域检索，所以四叉树对得上题。它不适合 5,333 次/秒的移动：每次跨分割都要删除再插入，而删除正是论文标成困难的那一步。格子键把这次移动变成对新 cell_id 插入、对旧 cell_id 删除。

S2 是球面上的层次。官方说明里，立方体六个面投到单位球，每格再切成四个孩子，边是测地线；级别 0 到 30，叶子约 1 厘米；编号沿 Hilbert 曲线，使靠近的格子有靠近的 id [4]。一个 geohash 字符会拆出 32 个孩子，扇出会跳。S2 一级只加 2 比特、四个孩子。热点拆分只借这四等分，不把 30 级和六面体放进第一版。真要全球均匀格子时再上 S2，并且要讲清 level，而不是只报库名。

默认键仍是 geohash 前缀：去掉末尾就变粗，长度 6 和长度 5 的换档有直接含义 [1]。点四叉树留下区域检索和「删除很难」[3]。S2 的四等分只留给打满的那一格 [4]。图上只有格子和边。

English

In 1974 Finkel and Bentley defined the quad tree as a structure for retrieval on composite keys in two dimensions. They give region retrieval, and they call deletion and merging inherently difficult [3]. A radius query is that region retrieval, so a quadtree fits the question. It is a poor first structure for 5,333 mover updates a second: crossing a split means delete then insert, and deletion is the step they mark as hard. A cell key turns the same move into an insert under the new cell_id and a delete under the old cell_id.

S2 is a hierarchy on the sphere, not a second opinion about the plane. The project’s own guide projects the six faces of a cube onto the unit sphere, then recursively splits each cell into four children whose edges are spherical geodesics. Levels run from 0 to 30. A level-30 leaf is about a centimetre across. Cells are numbered along a loop of six Hilbert curves so that nearby cells get nearby 64-bit ids [4]. A four-way split is what a hot cell wants. One extra geohash character splits a cell into 32 children and the query fan-out jumps. One S2 level adds two bits and four children. The hotspot split in this chapter borrows that “four children of one cell.” It does not pull all 30 levels, the cube, and Hilbert numbering into version one. When the product is global and cell area should stay even across latitudes, an S2 cover fits better than a fixed geohash length. The cost is that the interview then has to explain level and covering, not only the name of a library.

The default key stays a geohash prefix: dropping the tail coarsens it, so the switch from length 6 to length 5 has a direct meaning [1]. The point quadtree keeps region retrieval and the warning that deletion is hard [3]. S2’s four-way split is reserved for the one full cell [4]. The drawing stays cells and edges.

热点格子：热的是这个 cell 键Hot cells: the hot object is this cell key

从 222 QPS 和 400 次写入推出这三笔

教学假设里，高峰查询的 20% 以 wx4g0b 为中心，大约 222 QPS。若这场球赛把 6,000 个移动端留在同一格，6,000 × 4 / 60 = 400 次接受的写入每秒打进同一个 cell 键。邻格可以接近于零。按 entity_id 哈希把人打散，会让一次 1 km 查询变成对所有分片的散射，因为邻居不在「用户 id 的哈希」上相邻。分片键用格子，查询才仍然是那 k 个键。

400 次/秒还在一个键上时，先做四等分，不做第 7 个 geohash 字符。多一个字符是 5 比特、32 个孩子。1 km 查询若被迫改走长度 7，扇出会从大约 15 涨到远大于一页缓存能罩住的数量。两个额外比特、四个孩子，和 S2 每一级的分法相同 [4]，但只作用于这一个热父格。查询发现父格被标记为已拆时，把原本对 wx4g0b 的那一次读取换成对相交孩子的读取。其他城市格子保持长度 6，不为了一个球场改全球精度。

缓存值是成员 id 列表，键是孩子 id，例如 cell:wx4g0b#00。1 万个 id × 16 字节 ≈ 160 KB，222 QPS 不缓存大约就是 35 MB/s。TTL 约 2 秒，加抖动；本地 LRU 不超过这个 TTL。未命中按这个键 singleflight，只一路回源。键若用原始经纬度，格子内挪几米就会全部打穿。

进入热孩子的边上有两道限制。每实体 4 次/分钟去掉 1 Hz 上报。孩子写入预算先按约 300 次/秒设：四等分后 400 次不一定均匀，看台会挤在一个象限。超出的移动端更新丢掉，下一分钟再试。地点编辑不占这道预算。缓存挂了可以回源，但必须留下 singleflight 和并发上限，否则 222 QPS 会把那 35 MB/s 再乘上重试。

The three strokes follow from 222 QPS and 400 writes

Under the teaching assumption, 20% of peak queries are centered on wx4g0b, about 222 QPS. If the match leaves 6,000 movers in that one cell, 6,000 × 4 / 60 = 400 accepted writes per second hit the same cell key. Neighbors can be near zero. Hashing people by entity_id scatters one 1 km query across every shard, because neighbors are not adjacent in the hash of a user id. The shard key is the cell, so the query stays those k keys.

While 400 writes a second still land on one key, split four ways. Do not add a seventh geohash character. One more character is 5 bits and 32 children. If a 1 km query were forced onto length 7, the fan-out would jump from about 15 to far more than one cache page wants to cover. Two extra bits and four children match one S2 level [4], and they apply only to this hot parent. When a query sees the parent marked split, it replaces the single read of wx4g0b with reads of the intersecting children. Every other city cell stays at length 6. One stadium does not change precision worldwide.

The cache value is the member-id list. The key is the child id, for example cell:wx4g0b#00. Ten thousand ids times 16 bytes is about 160 KB, so 222 QPS uncached is about 35 MB/s. The TTL is about two seconds, with jitter. A local LRU is not longer than that. A miss is singleflight on this key: one load. A key of raw coordinates breaks as soon as the point moves a few metres inside the cell.

Two limits sit on the edge into the hot child. Four per entity per minute removes 1 Hz reports. A teaching write ceiling on the child starts near 300 per second: a four-way split of 400 is not even, because the stands clump into one quadrant. Mover updates over the budget are dropped until the next minute. Place edits do not spend that budget. If the cache fails, origin reads stay, and they keep singleflight plus a concurrency cap. Without the cap, 222 QPS turns that 35 MB/s back on and then multiplies it by retries.

故障、陈旧窗口和信任边界Failure, the staleness window, and the trust boundary

每种故障下用户看见什么

Location Service 不可用时，PUT 返回 503，客户端用同一个 observed_at 重试。GET 在 Spatial Index 仍可读时继续，新鲜度不放松：mover 超过 30 秒就不作为「正在这里」；place 在 24 小时内仍返回。30 秒回答的是「服务已经停了，索引里的车还能不能给人看」，不是那 2 秒索引延迟。更老的车会在别人的图上开出半径，删掉比显示一个错位几公里的点合适。索引也不可达时返回 503。空列表只表示圆里没有合格实体。

索引落后是预期窗口，所以和故障画在一起。最多约 2 秒里 Store 是 wx4g0c、Index 仍是 wx4g0b。只盖旧格会把人放在旧位置，只盖新格会暂时看不见，两格都盖到就重复，去重留较新的 updated_at。属主读 Store，才对得上「我刚上报成功」。对账比较 Store.cell_id 和 CellMember，按先加后删重放。积压超过 2 秒时，告警打在索引延迟上。

半径 50 km 在 Location Service 就返回 400。外接盒大约 166 × 109 ≈ 1.8 万个长度 6 的格子。即使每个格子只做一次缓存读取，一次查询也是上万次键访问，1,110 QPS 下这是把索引当成扫描器。拒绝发生在算出键列表之前。5 km 不是同一种失败：它换到长度 5，k 落在大约 16 以内，仍然是定位。客户端把 5 km 的结果再在本地放大解释，是客户端的事，服务不替它扫 50 km。

What the user sees in each failure

When Location Service is unavailable, PUT returns 503 and the client retries with the same observed_at. GET continues only while the Spatial Index is readable, and freshness does not relax: a mover older than 30 seconds is not “here now”; a place younger than 24 hours is still returned. Thirty seconds answers “the service has stopped; may we still show the car last written into the index?” It is not the two-second index lag. An older car can sit kilometres from where other people would see it, so dropping it is the better failure. If the index is unreachable too, return 503. An empty list means the circle has no qualifying entity.

Index lag is an expected window, not an exceptional path, which is why it sits on the same drawing as the failures. For at most about two seconds the store says wx4g0c and the index still says wx4g0b. A query that covers only the old cell places the person at the old spot. A query that covers only the new cell misses them. A query that covers both sees a duplicate, and dedupe keeps the newer updated_at. The owner’s view of their own point has to read the store, or “I just reported successfully” will not match the nearby list. Repair compares Store.cell_id with CellMember and replays add-then-remove. When the queue falls more than two seconds behind, movers in nearby results start to risk the 30-second drop early. The alert is on index lag, not on a later complaint that the pin drifted.

A 50 km radius returns 400 inside Location Service. The bounding box is about 166 × 109 ≈ 18,000 length-6 cells. Even one cache read per cell is tens of thousands of key lookups for a single query, and at 1,110 QPS that uses the index as a scanner. The rejection happens before the key list is built. Five kilometres is not the same failure. It moves to length 5, k stays on the order of 16 or fewer, and it is still a lookup. If a client wants to reinterpret a 5 km result more loosely, that is the client’s choice. The service does not scan 50 km on its behalf.

存什么，返回什么

精确位置不是公开信息流。信任边界包住 Location Store、Spatial Index 和 Location Service 里还拿着原始坐标的那段逻辑。边界内的行有 entity_id、entity_type、lat、lng、observed_at、updated_at、cell_id。边界外，陌生人的 nearby 响应里，place 可以有商户发布过的图钉坐标和距离；mover 只有距离和 100 米桶，没有 lat/lng，也没有可反推到米级的格子 id。cell_id 本身在城市里大约是 1 km 见方，把它回给陌生人等于把粗位置公开，所以响应里也不放。属主用自己的凭证读 Store，那一次响应可以带精确坐标，因为它没有穿过「陌生人」那一侧。

同一条边界也决定不留轨迹。5,333 次/秒的追加能把 m_481 的一天重画成路线。默认不写这张表，nearby 也不读它。以后若要保留，保留期和访问人单独做。

看四组数：更新的 429 和 503、索引延迟 p99 是否仍在 2 秒内、每个热 cell 键的 QPS 和成员条数、radius_rejected。最后一组升高通常是客户端把「全市」发成 50 km。成员条数上万才去拆 #00 到 #11，平均一千 QPS 不是拆所有格子的理由。

What is stored, and what is returned

A precise position is not a public feed. The trust boundary encloses the Location Store, the Spatial Index, and the part of Location Service that still holds raw coordinates. Inside, a row has entity_id, entity_type, lat, lng, observed_at, updated_at, and cell_id. Outside, a stranger’s nearby response may include a merchant-published pin and a distance for a place. A mover has a distance and a 100-metre bucket, no lat/lng, and no cell id fine enough to invert back to metres. A cell id in the city is on the order of a kilometre across. Returning it to a stranger publishes a coarse position, so the response omits it too. The owner reads the store with their own credential. That response may include the exact coordinate, because it does not cross the stranger side of the boundary.

The same boundary is why there is no trace. Appending at 5,333 per second lets anyone with the database redraw m_481’s day as a route. Version one does not write that table, and nearby does not read it. Later retention is a separate period and a separate access list.

Watch four sets: update 429s and 503s, whether index-lag p99 is still inside two seconds, QPS and member count on each hot cell key, and radius_rejected. A spike in the last one is usually a client sending “whole city” as 50 km. Split #00 through #11 when a key’s member count stays in the tens of thousands. An average near a thousand QPS is not a reason to split every cell.

追问和四个不完整的答案Follow-ups and four incomplete answers

1 · 无空间索引的一张表，用经纬度范围框查询，在这个 QPS 上行不行？

不行，当峰值大约 1,110，而且表上没有空间索引的时候。纬度一条 BETWEEN 会命中一条很宽的带，经度再滤一次，两次都是一维范围。8 万个最新点和 200 万个地点混在未按格子聚集的堆里，每次查询都可能碰到远超 k 个格子的行数。有空间索引的矩形查询可以当「只有餐馆、写入可忽略」时的第一版，它仍然要在矩形之外做距离过滤，因为框住圆的正方形四个角在半径外。本章一旦加上每秒五千次的移动写入，聚集键必须是 cell_id，否则热的球场无法被单独拆开。

1 · Can one bounding box on an unindexed table serve this QPS?

No, not at about 1,110 peak QPS on a table with no spatial index. A latitude BETWEEN hits a wide band, and longitude filters it again. Both are one-dimensional ranges. Eighty thousand latest points and two million places sit in a heap that is not clustered by cell, so each query can touch far more rows than k cells. A rectangle on a real spatial index can be version one when the catalog is restaurants and writes are negligible. It still needs a distance filter outside the rectangle, because the four corners of the square around the circle are outside the radius. Once the design adds about five thousand mover writes a second, the clustering key has to be cell_id, or the hot stadium cannot be split on its own.

2 · 只查当前 geohash，或者固定再加 8 个邻居？

只查当前格会在边界处漏掉对面的点。示例里南邻 wx4fbz 和中心 wx4g0b 从第 4 个字符就不同，一条前缀扫描盖不住。固定 8 邻居在格子远大于半径时是够的，长度 6 的纬度边只有约 0.61 km，1.5 × 0.61 ≈ 0.92 km，小于 1 km 半径，圆会碰到北边第二格 wx4g0f。Redis 文档里的 1+8 是它在一个有序集合内部覆盖形状的方法，然后再丢弃外部点 [2]。把它背成一切精度、一切半径的常数，就会漏。服务应按相交关系生成 k，1 km 大约 15 到 20，而不是写死 9。

2 · Query only the current geohash, or always add eight neighbors?

The current cell alone misses the point just across the border. In the example, southern neighbor wx4fbz and center wx4g0b differ at the fourth character, so one prefix scan does not cover it. A fixed eight neighbors is enough when the cell is much larger than the radius. A length-6 latitude edge is only about 0.61 km. 1.5 × 0.61 ≈ 0.92 km, which is less than a 1 km radius, so the circle reaches wx4g0f, the second cell north. The 1+8 in the Redis documentation is how that engine covers a shape inside one sorted set before discarding outsiders [2]. Memorizing it as a constant for every precision and every radius will miss. The service should build k from intersection. At 1 km, k is about 15 to 20, not a hardcoded 9.

3 · 热路径把每次上报都存成历史？

5,333 × 96 × 86,400 大约是 44 GB/天，三副本大约 133 GB/天，查询却只需要每个实体的最新一行，约 7.7 MB。历史还会把隐私从当前格变成可重建的轨迹。重试也会变难：追加模型下，超时重试若没有单独的幂等键，就会插入第二行。最新行的主键就是 entity_id，相同 observed_at 的重试是读回同一行。

3 · Store every report as history on the hot path?

5,333 × 96 × 86,400 is about 44 GB a day, about 133 GB a day with three replicas, while the query only needs each entity’s latest row, about 7.7 MB. History also turns privacy from a current cell into a reconstructable trace. Retries get harder too. Under append, a timeout retry without a separate idempotency key inserts a second row. On a latest-row table the primary key is entity_id, and a retry with the same observed_at reads that same row back.

4 · 把精确坐标返回给陌生人？

移动端没有发布自己的位置。nearby 若把 lat/lng 放进响应，信任边界就形同虚设，任何能调用查询的人都能把车或人的点扫出来。地点的图钉是商户公开的，可以返回。移动端只返回距离和 100 米桶。格子 id 也不返回，因为它本身就是大约一公里的位置。属主读自己的行走另一条有凭证的接口，直接读 Store。

4 · Return exact coordinates to strangers?

A mover did not publish their position. If nearby puts lat/lng in the response, the trust boundary is decorative, and anyone who can call the query can sweep cars or people. A place pin is published by the merchant and may be returned. A mover gets a distance and a 100-metre bucket. The cell id stays out of the response too, because it is itself a position on the order of a kilometre. The owner reads their own row through a different, authenticated call that hits the store directly.

若面试官把上报改成每 3 秒一次

限频从 4 次/分钟变成 20 次/分钟，8 万实体就是 80,000 × 20 / 60 ≈ 2.7 万次写入每秒，大约是现在的 5 倍。44 GB/天的历史会变成大约 220 GB/天，更不能上热路径。2 秒的索引延迟开始接近上报间隔，窗口里会堆着不止一次换格，先加后删必须带 version，去重不能只看「有没有这条 entity」。这时才值得把队列的分区数和 Writer 的并行度写上白板。在 4 次/分钟的假设下，那些旋钮还不是第一张图的主角。

If the interviewer changes reports to once every 3 seconds

The throttle moves from four per minute to 20 per minute. Eighty thousand entities become 80,000 × 20 / 60 ≈ 27,000 writes per second, about five times the current budget. The 44 GB/day history becomes about 220 GB/day, which is even less acceptable on the hot path. A two-second index lag starts to look like the reporting interval, so the window can hold more than one cell change. Add-then-remove has to carry a version, and dedupe cannot merely ask whether the entity is present. That is when the queue’s partition count and the writer’s parallelism belong on the board. Under four per minute, those knobs are not the subject of the first picture.
