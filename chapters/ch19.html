邻近服务 / LBS Nearby / LBS

约 35 分钟 · 前置：Ch03、Ch05 · 从附近查询讲到位置更新与热格迁移 ~35 min · after Ch03 and Ch05 · cover, filter, project and migrate

本章要点：覆盖查询范围，再用权威数据筛选和排序Core: coverage, authority and visibility

一次附近查询可以分成三步：找全查询范围覆盖的格子 → 校验权威状态并计算距离 → 从合格候选中选出最近的 20 个。读完后，你应该能说清楚：为什么边界附近的地点容易漏查，最终使用哪份坐标和权限，以及写入确认、索引处理进度与位置样本年龄分别意味着什么。

本章先设计公开地点查询，假设数据量可以由单库承载；之后再讨论独立索引、持续上报位置的移动实体，以及热格拆分。六组动画依次展示整体查询、半径覆盖、缓存读取、位置投影、热格迁移和故障处理。

A nearby query has three steps: cover every relevant cell → validate authority and measure → select the global nearest 20. Explain border misses, the coordinates and permissions that govern output, and the separate meanings of write acknowledgment, projection progress and sample age.

Start with published places; one database fits the assumed scale. Separate indexes, movers and hot-cell splits are extensions. Six retained decks trace queries, covering, cache, projection, migration and failures.

30 秒开场：先设计附近商家查询，再讨论扩展 The 30-second open: places first, then extensions

客户端提供坐标、地点类型，以及 500/1000/2000/5000 m 中的一个查询半径。服务返回半径内符合条件的地点，最多 20 个，按距离从近到远排序，同距时按 id 排序；没有结果时，不自动扩大半径。第一版在同一数据库快照内查询空间索引并过滤状态。如果改用独立的异步索引，响应就要带上 index_as_of，记录各格快照的消费水位与时间。此时只能保证已召回候选之间的距离顺序，不能保证它们就是当前权威数据中最近的 20 个。

开场先确认会影响方案的条件：查询的是公开地点还是私人移动实体，半径最多多大，是否允许地点稍后才被搜到，以及编辑后多久必须可见。本例假设有 200 万个地点，平均每秒约 0.127 次编辑，峰值每秒约 1,111 次查询；原始索引只有 128 MB，还没有迫使我们分布式拆库。容量和服务目标均为教学假设，未经过压测；下文 GB、MB 均按十进制计算。

Clients supply coordinates, type and a 500/1000/2000/5000 m radius. Return up to 20 eligible places in distance/id order without silently enlarging the radius. A single database can query its index and filter state in one snapshot. A separate async index exposes index_as_of with each cell snapshot’s offsets/time and guarantees distance order among recalled candidates, not the exact top 20 of current authority.

Clarify published places versus private movers, radius limits, eventual recall and edit visibility. Teaching inputs are 2 million places, about 0.127 edits/s and 1,111 peak queries/s. A 128 MB raw index has not forced distributed shards. Capacity and SLOs are assumptions, not load tests; MB/GB are decimal.

再看 A2：平均查询量为 8,000,000 ÷ 86,400 ≈ 92.6 QPS，按 12 倍计算，峰值约为 1,111 QPS。整个服务层的响应出口约为 1,111 × 4,000 ≈ 4.44 MB/s，即约 35.6 Mb/s。不过，平均流量无法说明球场附近的一个热格能否承受压力；还要分别测量查询有多集中、每次读取多少候选字节，以及距离计算消耗多少 CPU。

A1 gives 2,000,000 × 2 ÷ (365 × 86,400) ≈ 0.127 edits/s. Details at one KB each total 2 GB. Sixty-four-byte spatial rows total 128 MB before tree nodes, allocation, replicas and backups. Both fit the raw scale of one cluster. Sharding responds to hot-cell load, not the mere presence of geography.

A2 gives 8,000,000 ÷ 86,400 ≈ 92.6 average QPS and about 1,111 at a 12× peak. 1,111 × 4,000 ≈ 4.44 MB/s, about 35.6 Mb/s, is aggregate service egress. Average traffic cannot prove a stadium cell fits. Measure concentration, candidate bytes and distance CPU separately.

整体查询：索引找候选，权威记录决定结果 Authoritative places and a derived index: complete the query

沿一次读请求看，Client 经 Edge 进入 Location Service，服务先查 Spatial Index，再读取 Location Store 中的权威记录，完成过滤后返回。索引按 cell_id + entity_id 组织候选，权威存储按 entity_id 组织编辑。这里把从索引中找出候选的过程称为“召回”。索引坐标可能已经过时，因此不能先按旧坐标算距离、丢掉 id，再指望读取权威记录时找回它。删除状态、营业状态、受众权限和最终距离，都以本次读到的权威版本为准。

第一版可以放在同一个数据库中。例如，PostGIS 的 geography ST_DWithin 以米为单位，默认按椭球测距，并包含可利用空间索引的外接框比较 [3]。地点记录与数据库维护的索引在同一数据库事务中更新，就不必处理跨系统的事件传播窗口。单独为经纬度建立 B 树是否有效，要看查询选择性和执行计划，不能一概否定。

把索引独立出来，可以隔离召回的读取负载，也便于讨论热格迁移，但要付出事件重放、版本管理和延迟可见的代价。权威记录与 outbox 在同一事务中提交后，由 Relay 重试投递；第 5 帧中的 200 仍只确认权威事务的耐久性。若业务要求基于当前完整数据得到精确结果，就要使用一致的同步索引查询，或承担同步等待索引可见的成本。

The read path is Client → Edge → Location Service → Spatial Index, then Service loads authoritative Location Store rows, filters and replies. The index groups recall by cell_id + entity_id; authority groups edits by entity_id. Index coordinates can be stale: discarding an ID by its old distance prevents later hydration from recovering it. Deletion, business state, audience and final distance use the authoritative version read.

One database is a valid first version. PostGIS geography ST_DWithin takes metres, defaults to spheroidal measurement and includes an index-assisted bounding-box comparison [3]. A transaction updates both places and the database-maintained index. Independent coordinate B-trees depend on selectivity and plans.

A separate index isolates recall load but adds replay, versions and delayed visibility. Commit authority and outbox together, then let relay retry. The 200 in frame 5 confirms only the authority transaction under its durability contract. Current complete exact results need a consistent synchronous index path or an explicit synchronous visibility barrier.

半径覆盖：为什么不能只查同一前缀 Radius and cells: prefixes do not replace covering

长度为 6 的 geohash 共用 30 个比特，经度、纬度各占 15 位。按 A6 的局部平面近似，每格高为 180/32768×111≈0.609741 km，宽为 360/32768×111×cos40°≈0.934178 km。图中的 1 km 圆以格心为圆心，越过了北邻格的远端边界 1.5h≈0.914612 km。15 格外接盒中，四个角格离圆心最近处也至少约为 1.027 km，因此它们不与圆相交，剩下 11 格。11 只适用于图示位置；圆心任意对齐时，保守外接盒的格数上界是 (ceil(2/0.610)+1)×(ceil(2/0.934)+1)=20，不能固定查九格。

空间相邻也不保证字符串前缀相同。例如，wx4g0b 的南邻是 wx4fbz，只查 LIKE wx4g0% 就会漏掉它。服务应枚举完整覆盖：跨反经线时拆分经度区间，查询圆触及极点时覆盖所有经度。北纬 40° 的 20 格预算不能直接用于高纬度。覆盖还要保守地包含边界点；超出预算时，应拒绝查询，或选择已经维护的更粗层级，不能截掉最后几格。

沿用同一局部近似，半径为 2 km、5 km 时，六位 geohash 外接盒的格数上界分别为 48、216。5 km 查询若改用五位、约 3.737×4.878 km 的范围，可把范围读取降到最多 16 次。前提是数据行按完整 geohash 排序，支持前缀范围读取；互相独立的六位 Redis 键不会自动组成五位聚合。从 1 km 扩大到 5 km，面积增加到 25 倍，所以范围请求变少并不表示候选变少。

Redis 内部使用 52 比特编码并动态选择覆盖区间 [1]；H3 的 gridDisk(k) 也不能作为“任意 1 km 查询固定查九格”的依据。H3 中的 k 表示网格跳数，输出不保证顺序 [5]；每层都有 12 个五边形，同层格子的面积也会随位置变化 [4]。

A length-6 geohash has 30 bits, 15 per axis. A6’s local planar approximation gives height 180/32768×111≈0.609741 km and width 360/32768×111×cos40°≈0.934178 km. The centered 1 km circle passes the northern neighbor at 1.5 h≈0.914612 km. Four corners of its 15-cell box start about 1.027 km away, leaving 11 intersecting cells. Eleven is diagram-specific; the arbitrary-center box bound is (ceil(2/0.610)+1)×(ceil(2/0.934)+1)=20, not a fixed nine.

Nearby points may lose a shared prefix: wx4g0b has southern neighbor wx4fbz, outside LIKE wx4g0%. Enumerate the cover. Split longitude intervals at the antimeridian; a cap touching a pole needs every longitude. The 40-degree 20-cell budget does not hold at high latitudes. Include boundary points conservatively; reject excess work or choose a maintained coarser level, never truncate a cover.

The same local approximation gives 48/216 length-6 box cells at 2/5 km. Length-5 ranges are about 3.737×4.878 km and reduce 5 km to at most 16 range reads. That requires rows ordered by full geohash; separate length-6 Redis keys provide no implicit length-5 aggregate. Area grows 25× from 1 to 5 km; fewer ranges do not imply fewer candidates.

Redis’s 52-bit encoding and adaptive internal ranges [1], or H3 gridDisk(k), do not justify fixed nine-cell queries. H3 k counts grid hops with unordered output [5]; each resolution has 12 pentagons and location-dependent areas [4].

实现半径覆盖：先明确采用哪种距离模型Cover implementation: declare the distance model

下面节选自可运行的 Java 17 示例。输入是查询坐标、允许的半径、精度和格数预算，输出是完整覆盖所需的经纬网格集合，再把格坐标交错编码为 geohash。覆盖算法与后续 Haversine 测距都使用半径 R=6,371,008.8 m 的同一个球面模型；这里不承诺椭球距离精度，也不保证坐标等于 GPS 真值。

主方法先保守包住球面查询圆，再由辅助方法枚举闭区间。覆盖超出格数预算就抛错，不返回截断的集合。代码向外加的微小余量用于避免边界舍入造成遗漏，不能用来抵消定位误差。

This runnable Java 17 excerpt takes coordinates, an allowed radius, precision and cell budget, returning a complete grid cover; interleave grid coordinates to form geohashes. Covering and final Haversine distance share the chosen R=6,371,008.8 m sphere. They do not promise spheroidal or GPS ground-truth accuracy. The helper enumerates closed ranges and throws on excess work. Tiny outward padding handles rounding, not location error.

public static Set<Cell> cover(Point q, double radius, int precision, int maxCells) {
    if (!Set.of(500d, 1000d, 2000d, 5000d).contains(radius)
            || precision < 1 || precision > 6 || maxCells < 1)
        throw new IllegalArgumentException("radius / precision / budget");
    int bits = 5 * precision, nx = 1 << ((bits + 1) / 2), ny = 1 << (bits / 2);
    double phi = Math.toRadians(q.lat), lambda = q.lon;
    double angular = radius / R + 1e-12; // Outward rounding guard (~6 micrometres).
    double south = Math.max(-90, Math.toDegrees(phi - angular));
    double north = Math.min(90, Math.toDegrees(phi + angular));
    List<Span> longitude = new ArrayList<>();
    if (Math.abs(phi) + angular >= Math.PI / 2) {
        longitude.add(new Span(-180, 180)); // A cap touching a pole needs all longitudes.
    } else {
        double dl = Math.toDegrees(Math.asin(Math.min(1, Math.sin(angular) / Math.cos(phi))));
        double lo = lambda - dl, hi = lambda + dl;
        if (lo < -180) {
            longitude.add(new Span(lo + 360, 180));
            longitude.add(new Span(-180, hi));
        } else if (hi > 180) {
            longitude.add(new Span(lo, 180));
            longitude.add(new Span(-180, hi - 360));
        } else longitude.add(new Span(lo, hi));
    }
    Set<Cell> cells = new HashSet<>();
    for (Span span : longitude)
        addClosedBox(cells, span, south, north, nx, ny, precision, maxCells);
    return Set.copyOf(cells); // Budget failures throw; never return a truncated cover.
}

See the example contract for the full class and commands. Polar covers at precision six can exceed budget; use a coarser maintained level or an appropriate spherical index. Redis GEO itself accepts only about ±85.05112878° latitude [1]; this full-latitude model does not make Redis accept a pole.

完整查询流程：服务读缓存，筛选完成后再排序 The query loop: side cache and global ranking

第 2–5 帧展示了完整过程：Cell Cache 作为旁路缓存；未命中时，Location Service 自行读取索引快照并回填。服务合并完整覆盖中的成员，按 id 去重，然后依次执行读取权威状态与坐标 → 过滤权限、业务状态和样本年龄 → 计算最终距离 → 从全部合格候选中选出最近的 20 个。迁移时，同一 id 可能出现在两个格子中，因此读取的权威版本至少不能低于候选版本。权威读取失败，也不能当作已经确认实体被删除。

如果每格先任取 20 个再过滤，前 20 家都已关门时，第 21 家本该出现的店就会被漏掉。读取过程若没有经过证明的距离下界，就应继续处理全部候选，或明确失败、标为 partial。Redis GEOSEARCH ASC COUNT 20 之后再过滤，也可能无法返回足够的合格地点；COUNT ANY 更不保证得到最近的那些匹配 [2]。另外，Redis 文档给出的球面距离误差最多约为 0.5% [1]，在 1 km 上约是 5 m 的量级，不能据此承诺严格的椭球距离边界。

Frames 2–5 use a side cache: Service loads an index snapshot and fills on a miss. Merge the full cover, deduplicate IDs, then read authoritative state/coordinates → filter policy, business and age → measure → global top 20. Migration can duplicate an ID across cells. Authority must be at least as new as each candidate; a failed authority read is not confirmed deletion.

Arbitrary twenty-per-cell limits miss the 21st open shop after twenty closed shops. Without a proven distance bound, continue through all candidates or fail/mark partial. Filtering after Redis GEOSEARCH ASC COUNT 20 can also underfill; COUNT ANY does not guarantee the closest matches [2]. Redis documents up to about 0.5% spherical error [1], roughly five metres per kilometre, rather than strict spheroidal boundaries.

实现候选筛选：先读权威版本，再计算距离Filtering implementation: authority before distance

下面的 Java 节选与示例源码一致，输入是完整候选集合和新鲜的权威读取快照。Map 中缺少一个键，表示这条记录尚未完成读取；只有 Optional.empty() 才表示已经确认不存在。输出最多 20 条，按实际球面距离、id 排序。私人移动实体只返回按 100 m 划分的距离桶，不返回坐标。

sendBy 表示整条响应最晚何时发送，clockError 表示已经验证的设备时钟误差上界；如果无法在截止时间前发送，就要重新校验或失败。权限撤回若在本次快照之后提交，会影响后续的权威读取；已经发到客户端的副本无法收回。

The same-source Java takes complete candidates and a fresh authority snapshot. A missing Map key means an unfinished read; only Optional.empty() confirms absence. Output contains up to 20 hits sorted by actual spherical distance/id. Private movers expose only a 100 m distance bucket and no coordinates. sendBy is the response send deadline, and clockError is a verified device-clock error bound. If sending misses the deadline, revalidate or fail. Revocations committed after the snapshot affect later authority reads; already delivered copies cannot be recalled.

public static List<Hit> rank(Point query, double radius, String type, long viewer,
        Collection<Member> members, Map<Long, Optional<Row>> authority,
        long sendBy, long clockError, int maxCandidates) {
    if (clockError < 0 || maxCandidates < 1 || !Set.of(500d, 1000d, 2000d, 5000d).contains(radius))
        throw new IllegalArgumentException("policy");
    if (members.size() > maxCandidates) throw new Unavailable("candidate budget");
    Map<Long, Long> recalled = new HashMap<>();
    for (Member m : members) recalled.merge(m.id, m.version, Math::max);
    List<Ranked> eligible = new ArrayList<>();
    for (var entry : recalled.entrySet()) {
        Optional<Row> value = authority.get(entry.getKey());
        if (value == null) throw new Unavailable("authority missing, not confirmed absent");
        if (value.isEmpty()) continue; // Confirmed absent in the authoritative snapshot.
        Row row = value.get();
        if (row.id != entry.getKey() || row.version < entry.getValue())
            throw new Unavailable("authority behind candidate");
        if (row.deleted || !row.open || !row.type.equals(type)) continue;
        if ((!row.published || row.mover) && !row.audience.contains(viewer)) continue;
        if (row.mover) {
            double ageUpper = (double) sendBy - row.observedAt + clockError;
            if (ageUpper < 0 || ageUpper > 30_000) continue;
        }
        double metres = distance(query, row.point); // Latest permitted coordinates first.
        if (metres <= radius) eligible.add(new Ranked(row, metres));
    }
    eligible.sort(Comparator.comparingDouble(Ranked::metres).thenComparingLong(r -> r.row.id));
    return eligible.stream().limit(20).map(r -> new Hit(r.row.id, r.row.version,
            r.row.mover ? Math.floor(r.metres / 100) * 100 : r.metres,
            r.row.mover ? null : r.row.point)).toList(); // Never serialize private coordinates.
}

即使已读取全部候选，也不代表候选覆盖了当前权威数据全集。例如，一个实体从未查询的旧格移进查询圆，但更新事件尚未到达新格，服务就召回不到它的 id。上面的代码只能校正已经召回的候选，因此独立异步索引仍须保留 index_as_of 和最终可见的约定。缺格或工作量超限时，默认返回 503；即使明确返回 partial，也不能把结果称为当前全局精确的最近 20 个。

缓存键包含格子 cell、路由代次 route_epoch 和格快照修订号 cell_revision。entity_version 属于实体，revision 属于格快照，不能混用。服务在同一次 MVCC 快照中读取修订号和成员，再写入对应版本的缓存键。快照从开始读取索引时就带上绝对截止时间 not_after≤start+2s，迟到回填和缓存命中都不能重新计时。设计时还要明确：每次先读当前版本，还是允许使用有年龄上限的旧快照。索引可见 p99 为 2 秒、缓存最多保留 2 秒，只能解释正常情况下的窗口；发生积压后，并没有 4 秒的硬上界。

热格有 1 万条成员记录，每条 64 B，一份完整快照就是 10,000 × 64 B = 640 KB；每秒读取 222.22 次，约需传输 142.22 MB/s。若达到 90% 的命中率目标，回源可降到每秒约 22.22 次、14.22 MB/s。但移动热格每秒写入 400 次、每次都改变 revision，不能直接沿用这个命中率。缓存全部失效时，每秒 222.22 次回源需求超过整层每秒 100 次的预算，所以仍要合并请求并限流；只在单个进程内合并，不能保证全局只回源一次。

Complete candidates do not imply complete current authority: a mover can enter the circle from an unread old cell before its new-cell event arrives. This code corrects recalled IDs only. Preserve the async index’s index_as_of and eventual-visibility contract. Missing cells or work-limit failures default to 503; explicit partial results cannot claim the exact global top 20.

Cache keys include cell, route_epoch and cell_revision; entity_version belongs to an entity. Read revision and members in one MVCC snapshot, then fill its matching version key. From the start of the index read, retain an absolute not_after≤start+2 s; neither delayed fills nor hits reset it. Choose current-version reads or bounded-old snapshots explicitly. A 2 s projection p99 target plus 2 s cache age explains a healthy window, not a four-second failure bound.

A hot snapshot is 10,000×64 B=640 KB, or about 142.22 MB/s at 222.22/s. A 90% hit target leaves 22.22 origin reads/s and 14.22 MB/s. A 400-write/s moving cell that changes revision per write needs a new measured hit rate. Total cache loss demands 222.22/s against the 100/s aggregate origin budget; coalesce and limit. Process-local coalescing is not globally once.

扩展到移动实体：写入成功为何仍可能搜不到 Mover extension: durable success can still miss the new cell

只有扩展到移动实体时，才需要持续接收位置上报。8 万个实体每分钟最多上报 4 次，得到 80,000 × 4 ÷ 60 ≈ 5,333.33 次/秒；每条最新记录 96 B，共需 7.68 MB。若每次上报都追加保存，每天约产生 44.2368 GB，保存三份约为 132.71 GB/天，还没有计入日志、队列和索引。每分钟 4 次是接受额度，不是均匀每 15 秒一次的保证；如果实体在一分钟开头连报 4 次，随后仍可能因为样本超过 30 秒而被隐藏。

服务要验证上报者是否为实体属主、当前共享政策、设备会话和递增序号。客户端时间只有在时钟误差上界可以验证时，才能用来承诺观测年龄；否则，只能说明服务多久前收到坐标，不能声称 GPS 观测仍然新鲜。稳定的 request_id 要绑定完整请求意图。服务锁住实体后，先查找并返回已经提交的回执，再检查时间和额度，避免响应丢失后的重试被当成第五次上报。

Continuous reports belong to the mover extension. 80,000×4/60≈5,333.33/s gives 7.68 MB of 96 B latest rows. Append-only samples would produce 44.2368 GB/day, or 132.71 GB/day for three copies, before logs, queues and indexes. Four/minute is a quota, not one report every 15 s; clustered reports can leave a mover hidden by the 30 s age threshold.

Validate ownership, sharing, device session and increasing sequence. Client timestamps support an observation-age contract only with a verified clock-error bound. Otherwise promise receipt age, not fresh GPS observations. A stable request_id binds the complete intent. Under the entity lock, recover committed receipts before time/quota checks so a lost response does not turn a retry into a fifth report.

权威提交：最新记录、额度、回执与 outbox 一起保存Authority commit: row, quota, receipt and outbox together

report(auth, request_id, intent):
  authenticate owner; validate request shape and immutable intent hash
  BEGIN on the authoritative entity shard
  row = SELECT Entity WHERE id = intent.entity FOR UPDATE
  require row.owner = auth.owner
  receipt = SELECT Receipt WHERE (entity, request_id) = (...)
  if receipt exists:
    require receipt.intent_hash = hash(intent), otherwise 409
    COMMIT; return receipt.original_response
  require active device session, sequence > row.last_sequence
  require bounded device clock, valid sample age and current sharing policy
  require accepted_count_in_window < 4, otherwise ROLLBACK and 429
  v = row.version + 1
  UPDATE Entity SET point, observed_at, session, sequence, version = v
  INSERT AcceptedReport(entity, accepted_at) // Same shard; charged once.
  INSERT Outbox(event_id, entity, v, old_point, new_point, original_sample)
  INSERT Receipt(entity, request_id, intent_hash, {version:v, index:"pending"})
  COMMIT // Entity lock + unique(entity, request_id) + one DB transaction.
  return 200 with stored response
on unknown commit outcome: retry the SAME request_id and SAME intent
// Authorization/read errors are 503, never absence. A rejected transaction charges nothing.
// Session/sequence, quota, receipt and outbox share this DB. No cross-service atomic claim.

上面的协议依赖同一实体分片中的数据库行锁、唯一回执键和事务提交。连接或提交超时，只能说明结果未知，客户端应使用原来的请求键重试；会话或权限依赖不可用时，返回 503。outbox 中的 event_id 可以用于去重，以 entity_id 作为聚合键有助于保持同分区顺序 [6]。不过，Relay 自己也必须按顺序发布，不能仅凭事件落在同一分区，就认定发布已经有序。

One entity-shard database provides the row lock, unique receipt and commit. A connection/commit timeout leaves an unknown outcome: retry the same identity. Unavailable session/policy checks return 503. Outbox event_id supports deduplication and entity_id as aggregate key supports partition order [6], but the relay must preserve that order; a shared partition alone does not order publication.

索引投影：每格内用事务，跨格更新靠重试完成Projection commit: local cell atomicity and cross-cell retries

apply(event): // Immutable event; route before/after points to old/new cell for this generation.
  if event.new_cell exists:
    put_if_newer(event.new_cell, event.entity, event.version, LIVE, event.sample)
  if event.old_cell exists and event.old_cell != event.new_cell:
    put_if_newer(event.old_cell, event.entity, event.version, TOMBSTONE, null)
  // A delete has no new_cell; a same-cell move updates coordinates without self-deletion.
  ACK source offset only after every required write is durably acknowledged
  // A crash between writes retries both; no distributed transaction across cells.

put_if_newer(cell, entity, version, state, sample):
  BEGIN on this CELL's database shard
  guard = INSERT CellGuard(cell) IF ABSENT
  SELECT guard FOR UPDATE // Serializes revision and all membership changes for this cell.
  old = SELECT Membership WHERE (cell, entity) = (...)
  if old exists and old.version > version: COMMIT; return STALE
  if old exists and old.version == version:
    require identical state and immutable payload, otherwise corruption alarm
    COMMIT; return DUPLICATE
  UPSERT Membership(cell, entity, version, state, sample)
  UPDATE CellGuard SET revision = revision + 1
  COMMIT // Row/state/revision are one local transaction, including tombstones.
// Read revision + members in one MVCC snapshot; cache fills inherit its absolute expiry.
// Retain tombstones while older events/snapshots may replay. GC needs a durable replay fence.
// Migration invokes apply for both generations; unknown-child tombstones copy to all four.
// Queue order helps efficiency; versions and durable retries handle duplicates and re-entry.

索引投影就是把已提交的位置更新写入对应格子。处理一次跨格移动时，Index Writer 先在新格写入 LIVE，再在旧格写入同版本的 TOMBSTONE 墓碑；同格移动只更新坐标，删除则只写墓碑。实体以 v43 返回旧格后，迟到的 v42 不能再删掉它；更老的快照复制也不能把墓碑覆盖成活记录。

事件保留更新前后的坐标，路由目录据此计算各代索引中的目标格。复制父格时，若墓碑没有坐标、无法确定属于哪个孩子，就保守地写入全部四个孩子。每格通过本地事务一起更新成员和 revision，跨格之间没有原子事务；中途崩溃后重放未完成事件，只有全部副作用成功，才能推进连续消费水位。只要还没有可靠的重放边界，就要保留墓碑，不能像普通缓存那样按 TTL 清除。

“先加后删”只消除了事件处理期间先删除造成的空洞，事件传播期间的新格遗漏仍然存在，权威读取也找不回未召回的 id。要保证写后能搜到，必须等待相关消费水位、绕过旧缓存并使用兼容路由，或合并一个容量受限的近期写入召回层；只等 ACK 还不够。按每秒约 5,333 次更新估算，两秒积压约为 10,667 条，但两秒仍只是 p99 工作目标。永久丢失的事件要与权威数据对账并重建。所有队列重试都保留原 observed_at，不给样本重新计算 30 秒寿命。

Write LIVE in the new cell before a same-version TOMBSTONE in the old. Same-cell moves update coordinates without deleting themselves; deletion writes a tombstone. A delayed v42 cannot delete v43 after re-entry, nor can an old copy revive a tombstone. Events retain before/after points so routing resolves target cells for each generation. Copy a tombstone without a child location to all four children. Each cell transaction updates membership and revision together; there is no cross-cell atomic commit. Retry interrupted events and advance the contiguous consumer watermark only after every effect succeeds. Retain tombstones unless a durable replay fence excludes older events/copies.

Add-first closes only an apply-time deletion gap. Delivery lag still omits IDs from the new cell, and authority cannot recover unrecalled IDs. Read-after-write recall needs the relevant watermark, old-cache bypass and compatible routing, or a bounded recent-write recall layer; a queue ACK alone is insufficient. A two-second backlog is about 10,667 events, a p99 target rather than a failure bound. Reconcile permanent gaps from authority. Queue retries retain original observed_at, never a renewed 30 s lifetime.

热格拆分：先复制和追平，再切路由与回收 Hot-cell splits: copy, dual reads, watermark and retirement

只拆经过测量、已经达到容量上限的区域。A3 中的热格每秒约被查 222.22 次，包含 1 万个成员；移动实体扩展中，6,000 × 4 ÷ 60 = 400 次/秒的写入也可能先压满一个键，而邻格仍很空闲。拆分前可以先尝试热点快照缓存、读取副本和请求准入。空间四分不会保证负载四等分：实体都在同一看台时，仍会落入同一个子格；四个逻辑键也不会自动增加物理容量。

本章增加两个空间比特，得到四个子格；增加一个完整 geohash 字符则会多出五个比特、得到 32 个子格，两者不能混同。路由目录要持久保存父格、子格、状态和 epoch，查询方不能自行猜测是否已经拆分。原来最多覆盖 20 格时，只拆一格，稳态最多读取 20−1+4=23 次，迁移双读最多 20+4=24 次；若全部拆分，则可能达到 80 次。

Split only a measured hot region. A3 has about 222.22 reads/s and 10,000 members; 6,000 movers×4/60=400 writes/s can overload one key while neighbors stay cold. Try snapshots, replicas and admission first. Four spatial children need not balance load, and four logical keys do not automatically add physical capacity.

Two extra spatial bits create four children here. One full geohash character adds five bits and 32 children. Persist parent, children, state and epoch in a directory. Replacing one of 20 cells gives at most 20−1+4=23 steady reads and 20+4=24 dual reads; splitting all can give 80.

实现迁移：等待各分区的连续水位，而非最大实体版本Migration: contiguous partition offsets, not maximum entity versions

S、W 都是由来源日志各分区水位组成的向量。每个水位表示：此前连续前缀中的所有事件，其全部副作用都已完成。因此，“见过的最大 offset”或某个实体的 v42 都不能代替它们。

这个教学协议设置了两个短暂屏障，暂停相关的索引投影写入：先等待在途写入结束，并阻止旧写者继续操作，再读取父格快照，避免假定跨数据库天然存在一致快照。如果来源按 entity_id 分片，所有分区都可能产生移入或移出该区域的事件，屏障就可能需要覆盖全部分区，这是简化方案的代价。暂停期间，权威事务仍可提交，积压事件保留在持久日志中，但索引可见性会延后。

S/W are source-log vectors whose contiguous prefixes have all required effects complete, not maximum-seen offsets or entity v42. This teaching protocol briefly pauses relevant projectors at two barriers. Drain/fence old workers before taking the parent snapshot, rather than assume a magic cross-database snapshot. With entity-hashed source logs, every partition may emit a move into this region, so all may participate. Authority can keep committing to the durable log; projection visibility pauses.

split(parent, route_epoch):
  P = ALL source partitions that can emit a move into or out of parent
  pause projectors at hole-free applied offsets S[p] for every p in P
  snapshot = read parent members + tombstones while its writers are fenced
  resume parent projection; COPY live rows by point and tombstones to ALL four children
  // Copy guards compare versions, even if a newer live replay arrived first.
  REPLAY source offsets > S[p] to children; do not skip deletes or cross-border moves
  CAS durable route directory: PARENT(e) -> MIGRATING(e, parent + children)
  // Parent keeps receiving all live changes; copy/replay may safely retry.
  pause every relevant projector at offsets W[p] after its in-flight writes finish
  drain child replay until applied[p] >= W[p] for ALL p in P
  // Advance applied[p] only over a contiguous prefix with all destination effects durable.
  CAS directory: MIGRATING(e) -> CHILDREN(e+1)
  invalidate route/cell-cache pointers; retain their ORIGINAL not_after deadlines
  resume projection to BOTH parent and children through the old-reader grace period
  wait until all old routes expired AND all readers using them finished/expired
  fence old parent writers/readers; retire parent; project only to children
on failure before cutover: keep parent serving; resume from durable migration state
// Pauses affect index visibility, not authority commits; events stay in the durable log.
// An entity version is NOT a cross-entity offset. W is a per-partition vector.
// Leases/epochs are checked by storage; a late worker cannot continue on a retired parent.
// CAS changes one directory record. It does not atomically copy data across shards.

切换前，父格持续接收投影更新；只有子格追到 W，才通过 CAS 发布新 epoch。切换后的宽限期内，仍同时投影到父格和子格，这样使用旧路由的读者才不会读到一份停止更新的父格。缓存指针绑定 epoch，并继承原来的绝对有效期。回收父格必须同时满足旧路由已到期，而且旧读者全部结束，不能把两个条件写成二选一。

如果无法登记每个读者，就必须强制执行“最大路由寿命 + 最大请求期限 + 时钟余量”的等待边界，并让逾期读者停止读取。存储端还要验证读者、写者的代次，拒绝迟到者继续访问。迁移任一步失败，都保留父格和持久迁移状态，再按版本幂等重放。

Index Writer 过载时，不能丢弃已经向客户端确认的事件；应在权威事务提交前限流，或接受并暴露可观测的投影积压。目录 CAS 只会原子更新一条路由记录，不会让跨格复制也变成原子操作。

Keep updating the parent before cutover. Publish a new epoch by CAS only after children reach W. Continue projecting to both generations during grace so old-route readers do not see a frozen parent. Epoch-bound cache pointers keep their original deadlines. Retirement needs old routes expired AND all old readers finished. Without reader registration, enforce maximum route lifetime + maximum request duration + clock margin; expired readers stop reading. Storage rejects late reader/writer epochs.

Failure retains the parent and durable migration state; versioned replay is idempotent. Writer overload must not discard acknowledged events. Reject before authority commit or expose projection backlog. Directory CAS atomically changes one route record, not the cross-cell copy.

故障与隐私：说明何时能返回旧结果 Failures and privacy: stale results need a boundary

先分清故障发生在哪个组件。如果 Index Writer 停止，而 API 和索引仍健康，服务可以继续读取逐渐变旧的索引；如果整层 API 停止，GET、PUT 都会失败。缺格或索引超时，默认返回 503，不能伪装成“完整查询没有结果”。公开餐馆即使一年未改坐标，地点本身仍可有效；A8 的 24 小时约束的是投影或目录同步的落后程度，不是 last_modified 的年龄。

对于移动实体，每次输出都要结合原观测时间、时钟误差余量和发送截止时间检查年龄。缓存刚回填，不代表 GPS 刚采样。Redis EXPIRE 控制的是整个键的寿命 [7]，不会为每个 GEO 成员分别保证新鲜度。后台清理过龄成员可以节省空间，能否显示则由在线年龄检查决定。60 km/h 约等于 16.67 m/s，两秒可移动 33.33 m，30 秒可移动 500 m；100 m 距离桶也不会阻止实体跨格移动。

私人移动实体默认不向陌生人开放。服务在当前权威读取快照中校验受众与共享状态；相关依赖不可用时，就拒绝返回私人结果。属主按 entity_id 读取自己的最新记录，也要经过鉴权。100 m 距离桶不保证匿名：多个查询中心得到的距离环、结果排序，以及实体是否出现在结果中，都可能泄漏位置约束。因此仍需受众授权、共享撤回、限频和短期保留。W3C 的隐私建议属于非规范性说明 [8]，没有承诺某个桶宽能够阻止定位。

观测系统应记录覆盖格数、候选数量与字节量、缺片与 partial 比例、连续投影水位与延迟、样本年龄、路由 epoch 错误，以及回源和拒绝率。使用已知跨格位置样本发起探测，可以发现“HTTP 返回 200，但新格仍搜不到实体”的问题。删除和撤回的保留策略也要覆盖索引缓存、outbox、队列和备份，不能只删除最新记录。

Distinguish components: Writer loss with healthy API/index permits increasingly old recall; total API loss fails GET and PUT. Missing cells/index timeouts default to 503, never complete-empty success. An unchanged year-old restaurant remains valid: A8’s 24 h limit concerns projection/synchronization progress, not last_modified age.

For movers, each output checks original observation time, clock margin and send deadline. Redis EXPIRE applies to a whole key [7], not each GEO member’s freshness. Background cleanup saves space; online age checks determine visibility. At 60 km/h a mover travels 33.33 m in 2 s or 500 m in 30 s; a 100 m bucket does not prevent cell crossings.

Private movers are closed to strangers by default. Check authoritative audience/sharing state in the current read snapshot; unavailable checks fail private results closed. Owners read their entity ID through authenticated authority access. 100 m buckets do not guarantee anonymity: intersecting probe annuli, ranking and membership still leak constraints. Keep authorization, revocation, rate limits and short retention. W3C’s privacy discussion is non-normative [8] and makes no bucket-anonymity promise.

Monitor covered cells, candidate count/bytes, missing/partial rates, contiguous projection offsets/lag, sample ages, route errors, origin loads and rejections. Known cross-border probes detect new-cell misses behind HTTP 200. Deletion/retention covers caches, outbox/queues and backups as well as latest rows.

面试追问：条件改变后，哪些地方要重算 Follow-ups and answers that fail

如果改为每 3 秒上报一次，每个实体每分钟上报 20 次，整体写入量就变为 80,000 × 20 ÷ 60 ≈ 26,666.67 次/秒，是原预算的 5 倍。每条历史记录仍按 96 B 计算，每天约为 221.184 GB；最新记录工作集仍为 7.68 MB。此时要重新估算 Index Writer、队列和热格容量，不能只修改 TTL。另一个常见追问是扩大到 50 km：这已超出当前接口约定，按六位 geohash 的局部外接盒估算，约需 166 × 109 = 18,094 格，应在生成覆盖前拒绝。
