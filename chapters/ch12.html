English

Start with one connection and one send. Explain who owns the socket, when Sent becomes true, and why the receiver may see a repeated message.

Use connection count, peak messages, heartbeats and group fan-out to decide components; separate conversation order, device cursors and user read state.

30 秒开场：先拆连接、提交与可见 The 30-second open: connections, commit and visibility

中文

我按文本单聊、小群和大群来做，支持多端与单聊已读。入口用 WebSocket，Gateway 持有连接，Conn Registry 找到每个设备所在的网关。Message svc 按会话定序，消息、幂等结果和 outbox 原子提交后才给 Sent。后台至少一次推送；断线按设备的连续游标补齐。小群物化 inbox，大群查共享日志，只向当前观看者推送。Presence 单独用设备租约重建。

先问五个问题：是否群聊、是否多端、已读是设备还是用户级、群规模及峰值发言速率、离线历史留多久。我们假设单地域接写，历史保留一年，单聊展示「任一对端设备已送达」，用户任一设备读过可合并已读。大群不展示全员逐条已读。跨地域双活、语音与视频通话不展开；媒体消息只存引用，上传下载另走对象存储。

三个时间点必须分开。Sent 是服务端持久提交，Delivered 是对端设备本地保存并确认，Read 是对端界面上报已读水位。它们没有共用的时限。A1 的服务端提交 p99 200 ms、在线送达工作目标 1 s，都只在依赖健康与预算内成立；对端离线几小时不算发送失败。在线状态的绿点更不是投递保证，路由必须以当前连接和实际 ACK 为准。

所有容量与目标都写成 assumption，不能把「2026 默认」当证明。端到端加密若变成硬约束，服务端存密文与路由元数据；服务端明文搜索和内容审核能力受限，可能改成端侧能力。它改变密钥、多端历史和入群授权，不必改变先持久化再投递的基本顺序。此处先把不丢、去重和会话顺序讲完整。

English

I design text DMs, small groups and large groups, with multiple devices and DM read receipts. WebSocket enters a Gateway that owns the socket; Conn Registry locates each device. Message svc orders per conversation and returns Sent only after the message, dedup result and outbox commit atomically. Background delivery is at least once; devices resume from a contiguous cursor. Small groups materialize inboxes, large groups read the shared log and push only to active viewers. Presence is a separate rebuildable device-lease view.

Ask about groups, multiple devices, device versus user receipts, group size and burst message rate, and history retention. Assume one region accepts writes and history lasts a year. A DM is Delivered when any recipient device acknowledges it; reads on any device can advance user Read. Large groups do not display per-message receipts from every member. Multi-region active-active and calls are out of scope; media messages hold references while bytes use object storage.

Separate three moments. Sent is durable server commit; Delivered is recipient device persistence and acknowledgement; Read is a UI-reported watermark. They do not share a deadline. A1 sets server commit p99 at 200 ms and a 1 s working online-delivery goal under healthy, within-budget dependencies. An offline recipient is not a failed send. A green presence dot is not a delivery guarantee; current routes and actual ACKs determine delivery.

Every capacity and target is an assumption, not evidence from “the 2026 default.” If end-to-end encryption is required, store ciphertext and routing metadata; server plaintext search and moderation become constrained and may move to clients. It changes keys, device history and group authorization, without removing persist-before-deliver. Complete durability, deduplication and conversation order first.

四笔估算：连接、消息、字节与心跳 Four estimates: connections, messages, bytes and heartbeats

中文

消息量只用一套口径。A2 是 10,000,000 × 0.5 × 20 = 100,000,000 条/天。除以 86,400 得平均 1,157.4 条/s，乘五得峰值 5,787 条/s。这是被接受的逻辑消息数，客户端超时重试、Queue 重投与回执要另计。若改成全部 DAU 每天发 20 条，日增与峰值都会翻倍；不能正文用一亿、结论却按两亿买盘。

连接数没有乘消息峰值。10,000,000 × 15% = 1,500,000 同时在线用户，再乘 1.2 个设备是 1,800,000 根连接。按 A3，每台可计划 100,000 × 60% = 60,000 根，向上取整为 30 台。连接增量内存是 1,800,000 × 16,000 B = 28.8 GB，每台计划约 0.96 GB；运行时、内核 socket、TLS、索引和高峰缓冲还要测，不能由这笔内存证明单机连接上限。

A4 用十进制 KB：每天一亿 × 1 KB = 100 GB，一年 100 × 365 = 36.5 TB，一份加另外两份是 109.5 TB。这个下限只含文本记录，不含媒体、inbox、幂等索引、outbox 和备份。旧稿的 200 B × 一亿 = 20 GB 算术本身成立，但只像一截正文，不宜当完整记录预算。按基准平均 2 × 1.2 个设备且接收端全部在线的假设，推送规划值约 5,787 × 2.4 = 13,889 帧/s，1 KB 载荷约 13.9 MB/s，也就是 111 Mb/s 聚合出口；大群尖峰另外算。

心跳往往先压到状态服务：180 万 ÷ 30 = 60,000 次续约/s，约为 5,787 条消息/s 的 10.4 倍，回包又是一批帧。它逼出独立 Presence svc 与分区缓存。发送量相同，用户一直挂着客户端，心跳并不会减少；把它塞进 Message Store 会让「无人在发言」也不断争抢提交资源。容量监控要分别看连接数、新握手、逻辑发送、实际推送和续约，不能统称聊天 QPS。

English

Use one message model. A2 gives 10,000,000 × 0.5 × 20 = 100,000,000 messages/day. Divide by 86,400 for 1,157.4/s average; multiply by five for 5,787/s peak. These are accepted logical messages, excluding send retries, queue redelivery and receipts. If every DAU sends 20, both daily bytes and peak double; do not use one daily total in prose and another in storage.

Connection count does not multiply by message peak. 10M × 15% = 1.5M concurrent users, times 1.2 devices gives 1.8M sockets. A3 plans 100k × 60% = 60k per gateway, giving 30 gateways. Incremental memory is 1.8M × 16,000 B = 28.8 GB, or 0.96 GB per planned gateway. Runtime, kernel sockets, TLS, indexes and burst buffers still require measurement; memory arithmetic alone does not prove capacity.

A4 uses decimal KB: 100M × 1 KB = 100 GB/day; 365 days gives 36.5 TB logical, and three copies give 109.5 TB. This floor covers text rows, excluding media, inboxes, dedup indexes, outbox and backups. The old 200 B × 100M = 20 GB arithmetic was valid but budgeted only a short body, not a complete row. With a baseline mean of 2 × 1.2 recipient devices and all of them online, planned fan-out is about 13,889 frames/s; 1 KB each gives about 13.9 MB/s or 111 Mb/s aggregate payload egress. Group bursts are separate.

Heartbeats can pressure state services first: 1.8M / 30 = 60,000 renewals/s, about 10.4 times the message peak; responses add more frames. This drives a separate Presence svc and partitioned view cache. Quiet users with open clients still heartbeat; writing them into Message Store consumes commit resources even with no conversation traffic. Monitor sockets, new handshakes, logical sends, actual pushes and renewals separately instead of one “chat QPS.”

连接归谁：WebSocket 与按设备路由 Connection ownership: WebSocket and device routing

正常路径与协议选择 Normal path and protocol choice

中文

WebSocket 提供基于 TCP 的双向帧通信，Ping/Pong 用于连接探测，协议本身没有应用消息落库 ACK。[1] Client A 认证后经 Edge 升级到 Gateway A，后续帧一直落在这台。Edge 必须转发两方向的帧、支持连接排空与足够的空闲超时；它也有连接资源和故障域，30 台的除法只计算 Gateway 层。登录与历史查询可以继续 HTTP，不必为了「所有操作一根线」把无状态接口绑进长连接。

短轮询的代价是空查询；long-poll 能收消息，只是请求结束后要重开，发送通常另走 HTTP。SSE + POST 同样能实现聊天，代价是分成两条通道。因此本题选 WebSocket 是因为双向互动和持续推送，不是因为「long-poll 换台就找不到人」。两种方案跨机器投递都需要用户路由或事件总线。TCP 只保证一条活连接上的字节顺序，换连接、服务重试和离线补齐仍须应用 seq。

Conn Registry 的一条记录包含 user_id、device_id、connection_id、gateway_id、epoch 和租约截止时间。一个用户可以有多个设备，一台设备本轮只能有一个当前 epoch。Delivery Worker 拿到路由后，把消息转交给拥有 socket 的网关，而不是随机挑一台。Gateway 验证 connection_id 与 epoch 是否仍匹配；查表到发包之间若发生重连，旧路由可能失效，Worker 刷新一次路由或让补齐兜底，不能无限重试旧机器。

English

WebSocket gives two-way framing over TCP and Ping/Pong connection probes, not application persistence ACKs. [1] Authenticated Client A upgrades through Edge to Gateway A; later frames stay there. Edge needs bidirectional forwarding, draining and adequate idle timeouts, with its own socket capacity and failure domain. The 30-gateway division sizes only the gateway tier. Login and history can remain HTTP rather than binding every stateless operation to a socket.

Polling spends empty queries; long-poll can receive messages but reopens after completion and usually sends over separate HTTP. SSE plus POST can also implement chat using two channels. WebSocket is chosen for bidirectional interaction and sustained push, not because long-poll fundamentally cannot route users. Both approaches need routing or an event bus across servers. TCP ordering applies to bytes on one live connection; reconnects, service retries and sync still need application sequence numbers.

A registry record has user_id, device_id, connection_id, gateway_id, epoch and lease expiry. Users may have multiple devices; each device has one current epoch. Delivery Worker forwards to the socket owner instead of a random gateway. Gateway checks that connection_id and epoch still match. A reconnect between lookup and send can stale the route; refresh it with bounded retries or let sync repair delivery, rather than retrying the old host forever.

扩缩容与故障窗口 Scaling and failure windows

中文

图上的 Gateway B 代表同一池里的其他实例，不意味着账号必须粘在 A。普通 WebSocket 均衡器在建连时选择后端，已建立的连接自然绑定那台；重连可以选择任何健康实例。发布时先停止接新连接，再分批通知重连，最后关旧连接。一个注册键的替换与删除必须比较 epoch：A 的迟到断线通知不能把 B 刚登记的新连接删掉。Registry 不存消息正文，丢失后可由活连接重新登记，重建期间实时投递会变慢。

30 台坏一台，剩余舒适容量是 29 × 100,000 = 2,900,000 根，180 万占约 62%。这个余量只说明别的机器有位置，原机的 6 万根连接已经断了。A9 把它们摊到 15 秒，约 4,000 新连接/s，给正常登录只剩约 1,000/s 的接入预算。客户端要随机抖动、指数退避，服务端给过载端退避提示；恢复还包含认证与补齐，不能用「网关 CPU 空着」证明整条恢复链承受得住。

English

Gateway B represents the rest of the pool; an account is not permanently assigned to A. A normal WebSocket balancer selects a backend at connection establishment; the established socket stays there and reconnect can select any healthy instance. Deployments stop new connections, request phased reconnection, then close old sockets. Registry replacement and deletion compare epochs so A’s late disconnect cannot delete B’s new registration. The registry stores no bodies and rebuilds from live connections, with slower live delivery during recovery.

After one of 30 gateways fails, 29 × 100k = 2.9M comfortable slots remain and 1.8M uses about 62%. Slots do not preserve the 60k lost sockets. A9 spreads recovery over 15 s at about 4,000 new connections/s, leaving about 1,000/s of the 5,000 fleet admission budget for normal logins. Use randomized jitter, exponential backoff and server overload hints. Recovery also loads authentication and sync, so spare gateway CPU does not prove recovery capacity.

先提交再投递：消息、seq 与幂等同一事务 Commit before delivery: message, seq and dedup in one transaction

正常提交到底写什么 What a normal commit writes

中文

客户端发送前把待发消息和随机 client_msg_id 放进本地 outbox。Message svc 认证 sender、检查当前会话成员资格，再按 conv_id 路由到有序提交位置。去重键是 (conv_id, sender_id, client_msg_id)，避免不同用户复用相同键冲突；同键不同正文要拒绝，不是把第一条结果静默套到第二种意图。请求身份、内容摘要、分配的 seq 与返回结果都要持久化，不能只靠网关内存的一张已见集合。

A5 的事务先检查去重，再递增会话计数器，写 Message(conv_id,seq,sender,body)、Dedup(key,seq,result) 与 Outbox(event_id,envelope,policy_version)。并发同键只有一笔能首次插入；其他事务读回相同 seq=108。任何一步失败整笔回滚，所以不会留下「去重说处理过，消息却没有」的半成品。幂等记录与实际变更必须原子完成，这一点也在 AWS 的重试原文中明确。[2]

seq 定义为本会话成功提交的次序，而不是客户端 created_at，也不是数据库把随机 UUID 排序的结果。A5 中计数器与消息同事务、串行化，回滚不留对读者可见的空号。不同会话可以由不同分区并行提交。如果采用会预留序号的方案，必须返回区间水位或空号标记，否则后面的连续游标会永远等一个不存在的 seq。撤回消息也保留 tombstone 与原 seq，不能物理删掉后让设备误判缺口。

Sent 的耐久性还有复制条件。我们要求三份跨故障域日志，按多数派共识规则提交后才响应；一次节点损坏保留已提交记录，失去多数派则拒绝或等待新发送。Raft 原文说明日志提交与多数派、领导者安全规则的关系。[3] 这不是「随便两个节点收到了就成功」，也不是所有副本故障都不丢的承诺。若改成主库本地落盘加异步复制，确认更快，却要承认主库盘坏时已经 Sent 的尾部可能丢。

English

Before sending, the client persists its pending message and random client_msg_id in a local outbox. Message svc authenticates the sender, checks current membership and routes conv_id to ordered commit. The dedup key is (conv_id, sender_id, client_msg_id), avoiding cross-user collisions. Reject a reused key with different content rather than silently applying the first result to new intent. Persist identity, content digest, seq and result instead of an in-memory gateway seen-set.

A5 checks dedup, advances the conversation counter, and writes Message(conv_id,seq,sender,body), Dedup(key,seq,result) and Outbox(event_id,envelope,policy_version) atomically. Concurrent copies of the same key produce one first insert; the others return seq=108. Any failure rolls back the transaction, preventing “dedup says done but message absent.” AWS’s retry article explicitly requires atomic recording of the key with the mutations. [2]

Seq means successful commit order within this conversation, not client created_at or random UUID order. Under A5, counter and message commit serializably together and rollback leaves no visible sequence gap. Different conversations commit in parallel partitions. A scheme that reserves sequences needs gap markers or explicit range checkpoints, or contiguous cursors wait forever. Retractions retain tombstones and original sequence numbers instead of deleting rows and creating apparent gaps.

Sent also has a replication condition. Require three logs across failure domains and acknowledge after majority consensus commit; one node loss retains committed records, while losing a majority rejects or waits on new sends. Raft describes majority commitment together with leader safety. [3] This is not an arbitrary two-node ACK rule or protection against every replica loss. Local-primary durability plus async copies can acknowledge faster but can lose an acknowledged suffix on primary disk loss.

四个崩溃窗口与 outbox Four crash windows and outbox

中文

提交前崩溃：没有 Sent，客户端带原键重试。提交后、ACK 到达前崩溃：结果未知，重复请求读回同一 seq。Sent 后、投递前崩溃：Relay 从已提交 outbox 继续。Relay 发布成功、标记完成前崩溃：Queue 可能收到两次，消费者必须幂等。图上的可靠路线是 Message Store → Relay → Queue → Delivery Worker；Message svc 只负责事务，不在事务后另做一次无保障 publish。[4]

Outbox envelope 可含已提交正文，图里 Worker 不另查正文；若只发引用，就补一条按 seq 读取 Message Store 的路径，并计入读预算。离线设备没有有效连接时不算即时送达，保留权威日志与发现新会话的派生索引，必要时发唤醒通知。通知到达也不是 Delivered。只靠一份会话日志而没有用户到会话的成员索引或 inbox，离线用户连「该查哪个 conv」都不知道。

存储选型先看契约，再看形状：按 (conv_id,bucket,seq) 范围读历史，按去重键唯一读，事务更新会话计数器。可用分片关系库，也可用提供这些原子性的事务 KV；「聊天量大所以关系库不能用」不成立。宽列存正文若不支持上述跨行原子性，就需要持久有序日志做提交权威，历史库成为可重建视图，ACK 不能偷偷改为等待一个最终一致写成功。

A10 的自动重试窗是 24 小时，去重记录至少覆盖整个窗口和迟到处理余量。超过窗口先查历史或提交状态，再让用户决定，不自动换一个新键补发。不重的保证分两层：服务端窗口内同键最多一条逻辑记录，接收端可重复收包但按 (conv_id,seq) 只展示一次。后台事件是至少一次，永久磁盘丢失、历史过期或设备本地清空都不在「免费 exactly-once」这句话里。

English

Before commit, no Sent exists and the client retries the same key. After commit but before ACK, the result is unknown and retry returns the original seq. After Sent but before delivery, Relay resumes committed outbox. After Relay publishes but before marking completion, Queue may receive duplicates and consumers must be idempotent. The durable path is Message Store → Relay → Queue → Delivery Worker, not an unprotected post-transaction publish by Message svc. [4]

Outbox envelopes may carry the committed body, so this diagram’s Worker needs no body read. Reference-only events require a Message Store read by seq and a separate read budget. Offline devices are not immediately delivered; retain the authoritative log and derived conversation-discovery index, with optional wake notifications. Notification arrival is not Delivered. A conversation log without user-to-conversation membership or inbox indexes does not tell an offline user which conv to query.

Choose storage by contract first: range history by (conv_id,bucket,seq), unique dedup lookup, and transactional conversation-counter updates. Sharded relational storage or transactional KV can provide this; chat scale alone does not ban relational databases. If a wide-column body store lacks the required cross-row atomicity, use a durable ordered log as commit authority and treat history as a rebuildable view. Do not silently weaken ACK to one eventual-consistency write.

A10 allows automatic send retries for 24 h; dedup records cover that window plus processing slack. Beyond it, query history or commit status before a user decision, without automatically resending under a new key. Separate two guarantees: one logical server row per key inside the window, and repeated receiver packets merged by (conv_id,seq) for display. Background events are at least once; disk destruction, expired history and cleared device storage are not free end-to-end exactly-once.

断线与乱序：连续游标才能证明补齐 Disconnects and reordering: a contiguous cursor proves sync

中文

图里 B 的手机已保存到 105，却先从实时路径收到了 108。若把游标直接改成 108，下次请求 seq>108，106 与 107 永远不会补回来。正确的 receive_cursor 是本设备已连续持久化的前缀，108 先放缺口缓冲；范围拉取返回 106、107、108 后合并去重，再一次本地事务推进到 108。先 ACK Delivered、后写本地盘也有同样漏洞：进程崩溃后对勾在，消息不在。

重连握手重新认证、登记新 epoch，再做每会话补齐。为消掉实时与历史之间的缝，先建立当前会话的实时订阅并缓冲新包，再从提交权威读水位 H=108，分批查询 (cursor,H]。历史读到 H 后合并缓冲中 H 以上的数据；如果缓冲溢出或订阅曾失效，这轮同步不能宣布完成，要取新水位重跑。拥有者改变后旧网关发来的包也能按 seq 合并，不能用旧 epoch 更新路由。

A7 每页 100 条，不做全历史一次拉完；同一会话多桶时返回可继续的游标。同步必须读取至少达到 H 的副本，否则等待、转权威读或返回稍后重试，不能把副本旧尾部当成「没有新消息」。Matrix v1.11 的 /sync 区分 next_batch 与有限 timeline，并要求客户端处理历史缺口；本章借它说明「同步令牌和完整历史不是一件事」，没有把 Matrix 协议换成我们自己的整数 seq。[5]

多端分别保存 receive_cursor：手机 105、桌面 108 都合理。用户 read_upto 可以是 108，却不能替代手机的接收游标；手机还要拉 106 到 108。新设备先读成员索引拿会话列表，再逐会话拉最近窗口；老设备带上自己的游标。离开群以后每次拉历史都再查权限，成员版本也跟订阅绑定，移除成员时撤销订阅；不能因为旧 socket 仍活着就继续发私密正文。

慢端要给内存上限。A7 每连接最多积压 1 MB、正文约 1 KB，约能放 1,000 条，不含额外对象开销。超过上限停止向这条 socket 堆正文，标记 sync_required 并关闭或降速，客户端重连后补日志。若所有 180 万连接同时顶满，仅载荷就要 1.8 TB，所以还必须设每网关总待发预算与公平调度。Typing 与可重建绿点可丢弃，已提交消息不能从权威日志删掉来「降级」。

故障恢复是两笔限流。6 万连接 / 15 s = 4,000 次握手/s 已经很大；若每次还补 100 条，最坏约 400,000 条读取/s。这个最坏情况假设每条重连都缺一页，不代表日常读量。握手成功后可以先显示本地历史，再按用户和会话分批同步，延长历史追齐时间；把握手限额与历史扫描限额分开，才不会靠保护网关把存储打死。

English

B’s phone has persisted through 105 but receives live 108 first. Advancing to 108 makes the next seq>108 request skip 106 and 107 forever. receive_cursor is the device’s contiguous durable prefix. Buffer 108, fetch 106,107,108, dedup, then atomically persist and advance locally to 108. ACKing Delivered before local persistence has a similar hole: after a crash the status exists but the message does not.

Reconnect authenticates and registers a new epoch, then syncs conversations. Close the history/live gap by subscribing and buffering first, reading committed high-water H=108, then paging (cursor,H]. After reaching H, merge buffered events above H. Buffer overflow or a subscription break invalidates completion and requires a new fence and sync. Old-gateway packets can merge by seq, but old epochs cannot update routes.

A7 uses 100 rows per page and resumable cursors across buckets. Reads must reach H; wait, read authority or ask for a retry when replicas lag, rather than treating an old tail as no new messages. Matrix v1.11 /sync distinguishes next_batch from limited timelines and requires handling history gaps. It supports the distinction between sync tokens and complete history; this chapter does not claim Matrix uses our integer seq protocol. [5]

Devices keep separate receive_cursor values: phone 105 and desktop 108 are valid together. User read_upto=108 does not replace the phone’s receive cursor; it still fetches 106–108. A new device discovers conversations through membership indexes and fetches a recent window, while existing devices use their own cursors. Recheck authorization on history reads and bind subscriptions to membership versions; removing members revokes subscriptions instead of trusting a still-open socket with private bodies.

Bound slow-device memory. A7 caps pending payload at 1 MB per socket, about 1,000 one-KB messages before object overhead. At the cap, stop adding bodies, mark sync_required, and close or throttle; reconnect repairs from the log. If all 1.8M sockets reach their cap, payload alone is 1.8 TB, so add gateway-wide pending-byte budgets and fair scheduling. Typing and rebuildable presence hints may be dropped; do not degrade by deleting committed authority.

Recovery has two admission budgets. 60k / 15 s = 4,000 handshakes/s is substantial; if every reconnect lacks a full 100-row page, the worst case is 400,000 rows/s. That assumption is a recovery bound, not normal reads. After handshake, show local history and stagger user/conversation sync, extending catch-up time. Separate handshake admission from history-scan admission to avoid saving gateways by overwhelming storage.

中文

Delivered 的证明不能是 Gateway 把字节写入操作系统成功，也不能是推送平台接受通知。接收端先把连续消息保存到本地，再通过 Receipt svc 上报 delivered_upto。用户打开会话并按我们的产品规则读到 108，再报 read_upto=108；Read 是界面协议，不能证明人眼认真看过每条文字。若产品要求「所有设备送达」，就必须枚举授权设备并定义长期离线设备的到期规则，不能偷用任一设备的 max。

每个水位列单独做原子 max(old,new)，并验证用户、设备、会话与可访问序号范围。来自同一设备的 read=108 也证明它至少收到了 108，因此可同步提高该设备 delivered 水位；随后迟到的 delivered=107 不能降低它。展示状态按 Read 优先于 Delivered 优先于 Sent。多个设备离线重试时，旧回执不能用 last-write-wins 把蓝勾打回灰勾，也不能把其他设备的 receive_cursor 拉到 108。

单聊每端每次连续批次上报一次水位即可，没必要给每条消息写三个状态行。大群里若 N 人每条都读一次，再向 N 人广播各自回执，工作会接近 N²；只保存用户级 read_upto，返回自己的未读状态，小群按需要查询少量成员状态。未读条数也不能永远用 tail_seq-read_upto：可见性过滤、撤回标记与成员入群窗口都会使差值大于真正未读，需要按产品语义计算或维护可修复计数。

English

Delivered is not a successful gateway socket write or a notification provider accepting a wakeup. The recipient first persists a contiguous prefix locally and reports delivered_upto to Receipt svc. On displaying through 108 under our product rules, it reports read_upto=108. Read is a UI protocol, not proof of human attention. “All devices delivered” requires enumerating authorized devices and an expiry policy for long-offline devices, not any-device max.

Apply atomic max independently to each watermark column, validating user, device, conversation and accessible sequence range. A device’s read=108 implies it received through 108 and can raise its delivered watermark; late delivered=107 cannot lower it. Display Read before Delivered before Sent. Offline device retries must not use last-write-wins to regress status, nor advance another device’s receive_cursor to 108.

Receipt ACK means Receipt Store recorded it; A can sync later. Realtime changes may emit hints, with watermarks as authority and status sync repairing loss. Matrix receipts express read-up-to event positions per user and distinguish public from private reads. This chapter adapts that watermark idea; Delivered is our separate product contract in the table. A DM device can report one watermark per contiguous batch instead of three rows per message. In a large group, broadcasting N readers’ per-message receipts to N members approaches N² work. 群聊：存一份正文，扇出按人数与观看者分开 Groups: one body, separate membership and viewer fan-out

小群指针与大群拉取 Small-group refs and large-group reads

中文

小群先写权威正文，再异步为成员写 Inbox(user_id,conv_id,seq,msg_ref)。重复任务靠这个唯一键收成一条，不能只靠 Worker 内存去重。读取自己的 inbox 拿候选引用，再批量补正文并复核权限；图只展开索引查询，正文补齐仍从 Message Store 回 History API 再经 Edge 回读者。Inbox 是派生索引，可从成员与日志修复，历史权威不是成员各一份正文。

阈值必须带发言速率。A8 单群 20 条/s，小群 200 人要 20 × 200 = 4,000 指针/s，是每群 10,000 预算的 40%。按预算不留余量的数学上限是 10,000 ÷ 20 = 500 人，我们先选 200 留给重试、其他工作与估错。若发言涨到 100 条/s，200 人就要 20,000，已经超预算；这时转为只拉日志或限速，不能坚持「几百人永远 push」。单群预算也不能代替整层预算，许多小群同时热仍会耗尽 Worker。

大群 N=50,000，全量写时扇出是 20 × 50,000 = 1,000,000 指针/s。改成正文一份，按会话与 seq 拉历史，只向当前观看的 M=500 个连接推送，是 20 × 500 = 10,000 帧/s，减少 100 倍投递目标，但这不是零成本。M 是设备连接，不是用户数；若万人都在看，推送一样会涨。Worker 给每个网关发一份事件与本机订阅目标，由各网关向自己的 socket 分发，避免一个 Worker 对全部连接串行写包。

大群冷成员靠成员索引发现会话，再拉自己有权看的 seq 区间，不能一边省掉全员 inbox、一边假设他们靠 inbox 发现新消息。提醒可以按会话合并成「有新消息」标记或摘要，不为每条正文复制一行。模式切换也有历史边界：在提交水位 S、policy version 上切换，新消息按新模式处理；旧 inbox 保留可读，切回 push 时补建需要的区间或让 S 之前继续 pull。重放任务带版本与唯一键，防止迁移复制两次。

English

Small groups commit one body, then asynchronously write Inbox(user_id,conv_id,seq,msg_ref). A durable unique key absorbs repeated jobs, not a Worker RAM set. Read inbox candidates, hydrate bodies in batches and recheck permission; the diagram expands index reads only, while bodies return Message Store → History API → Edge → Reader. Inbox is a repairable derived index, not one authoritative body per member.

Thresholds need a message rate. A8 gives 20/s × 200 = 4,000 refs/s, 40% of a 10,000 per-group budget. The no-headroom ceiling is 10,000/20 = 500 members; choose 200 for retries, other work and uncertainty. At 100 messages/s, 200 members need 20,000 refs/s and exceed budget, forcing shared-log pull or throttling. Per-group budgets do not replace fleet budgets; many hot small groups can saturate workers.

For N=50,000, write fan-out is 20 × 50,000 = 1,000,000 refs/s. Store one body and pull by conversation/seq, pushing only to M=500 viewing connections for 10,000 frames/s. Delivery targets drop 100× but are not free. M counts device sockets, not users, and rises when more viewers are active. Worker sends one event with local subscription targets per gateway; each gateway distributes to its sockets instead of one Worker serially writing all sockets.

Cold large-group members discover conversations through membership indexes and read authorized sequence ranges; removing full inbox fan-out cannot still assume inbox discovery. Coalesce reminders into conversation-level new-message markers or summaries. Policy cutover occurs at committed S with a policy version. New messages use the new mode, old inbox remains readable, and switching back to push backfills required ranges or keeps pre-S pull. Versioned jobs and unique keys make migration replay safe.

中文

历史键可写成 (conv_id,bucket,seq)，bucket 是固定时间窗或有上限的序号区间，防止一年消息都挤进同一巨大分区。Discord 2023 工程原文把 channel 与时间桶组合做分区键，同时明确描述热分区造成延迟问题。[6] 我们借用的是键形状与热点警告，不借它的节点数作为本题容量。按 group_id 哈希只能分散不同群，一个热门群仍在同一当前桶与排序拥有者上。

绿点：设备租约、过期窗口与故障隔离 Presence: device leases, expiry windows and isolation

中文

Presence 的权威是服务端当前有效设备租约，用户在线是任一授权设备仍有有效租约。Gateway B 接心跳时带 device 与 epoch，Presence svc 用自己的时间更新 expires_at；旧 epoch 续约要拒绝，旧 disconnect 只删除自己那版。手机关网、桌面还在，不应该把整个用户设为 offline。`last_seen` 可单独持久化低频变化，但不能把它与每 30 秒续约一起写到消息事务里。

A6 的 30 秒心跳与 90 秒租约允许暂时丢包，代价是绿点不会立刻变灰。从最后一次有效心跳算，查询时到 90 秒应判租约失效，活跃观察端刷新完成间隔最多 5 秒，健康条件下界面约在 95 秒内反映。这里的 5 秒包括刷新请求与展示完成，不是另加一段未知网络延迟；后台被冻结的手机、网断的观察端没有这个界面时限。也别从「用户断网的瞬间」重复加上 90 秒，那与最后心跳是不同起点。

实现时查询直接比较 expires_at 与当前服务端时间，并用周期检查修补变化提示。Redis 原文说明 expired 事件是在实际删键时产生，不保证恰好在 TTL 归零时发出；它的 Pub/Sub 断线期间事件也会丢。[7] 所以订阅只能让绿点更新更快，不能成为离线检测唯一依据。重连先拿当前快照，再接增量；增量有空洞或超预算时回到快照，别把缓存过期通知当可靠队列。

好友可以按授权关系订阅变化并按用户合并，万人群进群或打开成员面板时再按需查，不全员互订阅。60,000 次续约/s 若每次又广播给几百人，哪怕没有任何上下线也会产生巨大空转；只有有效状态发生变化才发布，并对抖动合并。Presence 故障时显示 unknown 或带年龄的 last_seen，不能把查询失败当 offline，也不能拿绿点决定是否把消息持久化。发送主链仍然是 Gateway → Message svc → Message Store。

English

Presence is the server view of valid device leases; a user is online if any authorized device lease is valid. Gateway B includes device and epoch on heartbeat, and Presence svc updates expires_at using its own time. Reject old epochs and delete only the matching version on disconnect. An offline phone with a live desktop must not make the user offline. Persist last_seen separately at lower frequency, not on every 30 s renewal in a message transaction.

A6’s 30 s heartbeat and 90 s lease tolerate transient loss at the cost of delayed offline display. From the last valid heartbeat, queries classify expiry at 90 s; active observer refresh completes at most every 5 s, giving about 95 s under healthy conditions. The 5 s includes request and display completion, not an extra unknown network delay. Suspended or disconnected observers lack this UI bound. Actual disconnection and last valid heartbeat are different clock origins.

Queries compare expires_at with current server time, and periodic checks repair change hints. Redis documents that expired events occur at actual deletion, not exactly at TTL zero, and Pub/Sub loses events during disconnects. [7] Subscriptions improve speed rather than define offline detection. Reconnect obtains a current snapshot and then deltas; gaps or budget exhaustion return to snapshots instead of treating expiry notifications as a reliable queue.

Friends may subscribe to authorized, per-user coalesced changes; large groups query on join or member-panel opens instead of mutual full subscriptions. Broadcasting hundreds of followers on each of 60,000 renewals/s creates waste without transitions. Publish effective changes and coalesce flaps. Presence failure shows Unknown or aged last_seen, not Offline. A green dot does not gate persistence; send remains Gateway → Message svc → Message Store.

中文

面试官问「同一会话两人同时发呢」，回答会话事务串行提交决定 seq，谁先点发送不一定先提交；跨会话不用比较。问「存储丢多数派呢」，回答不确认新的 Sent，客户端保留待发并带同键重试。问「Queue 全挂呢」，回答已提交消息仍在日志，outbox 积压，实时新鲜度下降；设置积压与存储余量阈值，不能积压到磁盘满了还无限接写。

最后二十秒可以这样收：百万长连接按设备路由，连接会断，消息权威不跟着断。Sent 在多数派事务后成立，投递由 outbox 保证还能重试，收端按 seq 合并并补连续缺口。Read 与多端接收游标分开。小群用速率乘人数验写放大，大群只推观看连接，历史按桶查。绿点是可重建租约，失败显示 unknown。合上页面，用一分钟口述第二组完整图，再用 105、108 的例子检验自己有没有把顺序与可靠性混在一起。

English

Concurrent senders in one conversation get sequence order from serial commit, not click time; different conversations need no comparison. Without a storage majority, do not acknowledge new Sent; retain client pending data and retry the same key. With Queue down, committed history remains and outbox grows, harming freshness. Set backlog and free-space limits rather than accepting writes until disks fill.

Close: route million-scale sockets by device; sockets break without losing message authority. Sent follows majority transaction commit, outbox preserves delivery retries, and receivers dedup by seq while filling contiguous gaps. Separate Read from device receipt cursors. Size small-group writes by rate × members; push large groups only to viewers and read bucketed history. Presence is a rebuildable lease view and fails Unknown.
