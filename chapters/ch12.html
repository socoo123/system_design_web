设计聊天系统 Design a chat system

主线约 40 分钟 · 前置 Ch01–Ch03 · 用 6 组演进图讲清连接、提交、补齐、回执、群聊和在线状态 ~40 min core · after Ch01–Ch03 · 6 evolution decks: connections, commit, sync, receipts, groups and presence

本章核心：连接断了，消息仍以已提交日志为准 Core: sockets can break without losing message authority

先分清三项职责：连接网关（Gateway）维持设备连接，消息存储（Message Store）提交会话日志，投递和回执则异步完成。服务端确认已发送（Sent），不等于对端已经收到；在线绿点也不能证明消息已送达。

接下来沿六组图看清三个关键过程：客户端用同一个键重试时，服务端只追加一条消息；设备先收到 108 时，仍要补齐 106、107；用户的已读状态，也不能代替每台设备各自的补齐游标。

Separate three responsibilities: Gateways own device sockets, Message Store commits conversation history, and delivery plus receipts catch up asynchronously. Sent does not mean recipient receipt; presence is not delivery evidence.

Use the six diagrams to explain same-key retries, why seeing 108 must not skip 106–107, and why user Read cannot replace each device’s sync cursor.

需求与承诺：先约定发送成功意味着什么 Requirements: define success first

先设计支持多端的文本单聊、小群和大群，由单个地域接收写入，历史消息保留一年。单聊中，只要任一获得授权的对端设备已经连续保存消息并发回确认，就展示“已送达”（Delivered）；同一用户任一设备的已读状态都可合并为 Read。大群不向全员广播逐条已读回执。媒体文件放在对象存储中，本章只保存引用；跨地域双活、通话和完整的加密密钥协议暂不展开。

Sent 表示服务端已耐久提交，Delivered 表示设备在本地保存后发回了应用层回执，Read 表示界面上报了连续已读到哪里。提交延迟的 p99 目标为 200 ms，健康在线设备的送达目标为 1 秒，这些都是教学目标；对端离线几小时不算发送失败。若要求端到端加密，服务端应保存密文和路由元数据，还需另行设计密钥、多端历史和成员授权，现有的服务端明文搜索、审核方案也不能直接沿用。

核心存储契约 A5 约定：同一会话内，分配消息顺序的普通计数器、消息、用于发送去重的幂等回执和待投递记录（outbox）必须串行提交，并由跨故障域的三个副本按共识规则耐久记录。缓存命中或向连接写入成功，都不能替代这个提交边界。

Design text DMs and small/large groups with multiple devices. One region accepts writes; history lasts a year. A DM is Delivered after any authorized recipient device durably stores a contiguous prefix and acknowledges it. Reads merge across devices; large groups omit all-member per-message receipt broadcasts. Media bytes use object storage, with references here. Active-active regions, calls and a full encryption-key protocol are outside scope.

Sent = durable server commit; Delivered = an application receipt after device persistence; Read = a UI-reported read prefix. Commit p99 200 ms and healthy online delivery within 1 s are teaching targets; offline hours are not failed sends. End-to-end encryption adds key, device-history and membership protocols, and constrains server plaintext search/moderation.

A5 requires serial conversation transactions over an ordinary counter, messages, retry receipts and outbox, durably committed by consensus across three failure-domain-separated replicas. Caches or successful socket writes cannot replace that boundary.

四项估算：分别计算连接、消息、字节和心跳 Four estimates: connections, messages, bytes and heartbeats

逻辑消息：按 A2，10,000,000 × 50% × 20 = 100,000,000 条/天，除以 86,400 秒得到平均每秒 1,157.4 条，再乘峰值系数 5，约为每秒 5,787 条。重试、队列重投和回执都要另计。如果改成所有日活用户每天都发 20 条，日增消息和峰值都会翻倍，不能混用这两套口径。

长连接：10,000,000 × 15% × 1.2 = 1,800,000 条。每台按 100,000 × 60% = 60,000 条规划，正常至少需要 ⌈1,800,000 / 60,000⌉ = 30 台。若希望故障一台后仍守住 60% 的预算，就需要 31 台，因为剩下的 30 × 60,000 = 1,800,000 条仍够用。若只部署 30 台，故障后规划容量仅剩 174 万条；虽然 180 万 ÷ 290 万 ≈ 62.1% 仍低于原始上限，却已超过 60% 的规划预算。

字节：按十进制单位，每条记录 1 KB 对应每天 100 GB、每年 36.5 TB，存三份为 109.5 TB；媒体、索引、幂等回执、outbox 和备份另计。180 万 × 16,000 B = 28.8 GB，是连接带来的增量内存；单台按 6 万条连接计算，对应 0.96 GB。内核、TLS、运行时和缓冲的开销仍须实测。若基准中的接收设备全部在线，每条消息平均推向 2 × 1.2 = 2.4 台设备，峰值约为每秒 13,889 帧，聚合载荷约 13.9 MB/s，即 111 Mb/s；大群流量另算。

心跳：180 万 ÷ 30 = 60,000 次续约/秒，约为消息峰值的 10.4 倍，回包还会增加帧数。因此，在线状态服务（Presence）要独立分区，不能把每次续约都写进聊天事务。监控也要分别统计连接数、握手量、逻辑消息量、实际推送量和续约量。

Logical messages: 10M ×50% ×20 = 100M/day; /86,400 = 1,157.4/s; peak ×5 ≈5,787/s. Retries, queue redelivery and receipts are additional. Making every DAU a sender doubles both totals; use one model throughout.

Sockets: 10M ×15% ×1.2 = 1.8M. Each gateway has a 100k ×60% =60k budget, requiring 30 normally. Choose 31 for N−1 at the same budget: 30×60k=1.8M after one failure. Thirty gateways lose to 1.74M planned capacity after one failure; 1.8M/2.9M≈62.1% is below the raw ceiling but exceeds the 60% budget.

Bytes: decimal 1 KB/row gives 100 GB/day, 36.5 TB/year and 109.5 TB for three copies, excluding media, indexes, retry receipts, outbox and backups. Incremental socket memory is 1.8M×16,000 B=28.8 GB; a gateway’s 60k cap gives 0.96 GB. Kernel, TLS, runtime and buffers require measurement. With 2×1.2=2.4 baseline recipient devices all online, plan about 13,889 frames/s, 13.9 MB/s≈111 Mb/s aggregate payload egress. Group bursts are separate.

Heartbeats: 1.8M/30=60,000 renewals/s, about 10.4× message peak, plus replies. Partition Presence separately from chat transactions. Measure sockets, handshakes, logical sends, actual pushes and renewals independently.

连接由谁维持：WebSocket 与设备路由 Connection ownership: WebSocket and device routing

网关维持连接，存储保证消息耐久 Socket ownership is separate from durability

WebSocket 在 TCP 上提供双向帧传输，Ping/Pong 用来探测连接，并不确认聊天消息已写入数据库。[1] 边缘入口（Edge）在建连时选择网关，此后沿同一条连接双向转发；设备重连时可以选择任一健康网关。登录和历史查询仍可使用 HTTP，SSE+POST 或长轮询也能支持聊天，但都需要跨机器找到对应连接。TCP 只保证同一条有效连接内的字节顺序；重连、重投和补齐时，仍需应用层的会话序号 seq。

连接登记表（Conn Registry）按 (user,device) 查找 (connection,gateway,epoch,lease)，分别对应连接、网关、连接代次和租约。投递 Worker 先查路由，网关再核对连接标识和代次。查询后若设备恰好重连，旧路由就可能失效，此时应刷新路由并有限重试，剩余缺口最终靠历史补齐。连接代次 epoch 与会话序号 seq 含义不同；同一用户的桌面端和手机也各自保留路由。

WebSocket frames two-way traffic over TCP; Ping/Pong probes the connection, without a chat-storage ACK. [1] Edge selects a gateway at connection setup and forwards both directions on that socket; reconnects can choose any healthy gateway. Login/history can remain HTTP. SSE+POST and long polling can also support chat, with cross-machine routing. TCP byte order within one live connection does not replace application seq after reconnect, redelivery or sync.

Conn Registry maps (user,device) to (connection,gateway,epoch,lease). Worker looks it up and Gateway checks connection plus epoch. Reconnect can stale a lookup: refresh with bounded retries, then let sync repair gaps. An epoch is a socket incarnation, distinct from conversation seq; phone and desktop have independent routes.

条件更新：迟到的断线通知不能删除新路由 Conditional updates keep old disconnects from deleting new routes

下面用 PostgreSQL 18 的教学 SQL 说明登记规则。设备获得授权时，先创建设备会话记录 DeviceSession，主键为 (user_id,device_id)。登记时，在权威存储的同一个事务中锁住该行、递增连接代次 epoch 并替换路由，行锁一直持有到事务结束。切换主节点后，已确认的代次和状态都不能回退；可以让路由缓存和在线字段过期，但必须保留代次。登记表缓存丢失后，新连接可以重新登记，却不能从 0 开始复用旧代次。[8]

This is PostgreSQL 18 teaching SQL. Device authorization pre-creates DeviceSession, keyed by (user_id,device_id). One authority transaction locks the row, increments epoch and replaces the route; the lock lasts until transaction end. Confirmed epochs must not roll back on failover. Expire route caches/online fields, retaining the epoch. Lost route caches rebuild through fresh registration, never by restarting the generation at zero. [8]

-- Bound parameters are supplied by an authenticated registration service.
-- DeviceSession is pre-created per authorized device; its epoch never expires.
BEGIN;
SELECT epoch FROM device_session
  WHERE user_id = :user AND device_id = :device FOR UPDATE;
UPDATE device_session
  SET epoch = epoch + 1, connection_id = :connection, gateway_id = :gateway,
      lease_until = :server_now + INTERVAL '90 seconds'
  WHERE user_id = :user AND device_id = :device
    AND epoch < 9223372036854775807
  RETURNING epoch;
COMMIT; -- Install the returned epoch only after confirmed commit.
-- Zero rows: reject. Unknown commit: discard this attempt and register anew.

-- Heartbeat and disconnect are independent conditional atomic statements.
UPDATE device_session SET lease_until = :server_now + INTERVAL '90 seconds'
  WHERE user_id = :user AND device_id = :device AND epoch = :epoch
    AND connection_id = :connection AND lease_until > :server_now;
UPDATE device_session SET connection_id = NULL, gateway_id = NULL,
  lease_until = :server_now
  WHERE user_id = :user AND device_id = :device AND epoch = :epoch
    AND connection_id = :connection;

续约只接受尚未过期的当前连接代次，断线通知也只能清除自己那一代的路由。网关必须等登记事务的 COMMIT 确认后，才能启用返回的代次；若提交结果未知，就废弃本次尝试并重新登记。这些条件可以阻止迟到的删除和续约，却无法强制暂停旧进程。路由缓存过时仍可能导致重复推送，接收端要按 seq 合并；设备被撤销授权后，认证层还须拒绝旧凭据。

发布网关新版本时，先停止接收新连接，再分批排空旧连接。把单台最多 6 万条连接分散到 15 秒内恢复，需要每秒 4,000 次新握手；整层每秒 5,000 次的接入限额，还能为正常登录留下每秒 1,000 次。但这只核算了网关层，Edge、认证和消息补齐也需要各自的准入控制。网关能容纳这些连接，不代表整个系统能足够快地恢复。

Renew only an unexpired current epoch; disconnect clears only its own route. Activate an incarnation after confirmed registration COMMIT; discard an unknown attempt and register afresh. These predicates prevent delayed deletes/renewals, without physically fencing a paused old process. Stale routes may duplicate pushes, absorbed by seq dedup. Device revocation must also invalidate old credentials at authentication.

For releases, stop new connections before draining in batches. Spreading at most 60k failed sockets over 15 s requires 4,000 handshakes/s, leaving 1,000/s of the fleet’s 5,000/s admission cap for ordinary logins. This sizes gateways only; Edge, authentication and sync require separate admission.

先提交再投递：消息、序号和去重结果一起保存 Commit before delivery: message, seq and dedup in one transaction

用行锁和唯一约束落实提交契约 Turn commit promises into locks and constraints

对应第 2–3 帧：客户端先把正文和随机消息标识 client_msg_id 持久保存在本地 outbox 中，每次重试都复用原键。服务端按 (conv,sender,key) 去重，并为规范化后的正文、类型和附件引用计算摘要；若同键请求表达了不同意图，就拒绝。下面的 SQL 展示需要的表和约束，仅靠这些 DDL 并不能获得 A5 要求的共识复制。

Frames 2–3: persist the body and random client_msg_id in the client outbox before sending, reusing the key for every retry. Server dedup uses (conv,sender,key); hash the canonical body, type and attachment references. Reject changed intent under the same key. This SQL expresses constraints; DDL alone does not provide A5 consensus replication.

-- Teaching schema: PostgreSQL 18 syntax; replication is an extra A5 contract.
CREATE TABLE conversation (
  conv_id TEXT PRIMARY KEY, head BIGINT NOT NULL CHECK (head >= 0),
  membership_version BIGINT NOT NULL CHECK (membership_version > 0),
  policy_version BIGINT NOT NULL CHECK (policy_version > 0)
);
CREATE TABLE member (
  conv_id TEXT NOT NULL REFERENCES conversation, user_id TEXT NOT NULL,
  view_epoch BIGINT NOT NULL CHECK (view_epoch > 0),
  first_seq BIGINT NOT NULL CHECK (first_seq > 0), active BOOLEAN NOT NULL,
  PRIMARY KEY (conv_id, user_id)
);
CREATE TABLE message (
  conv_id TEXT NOT NULL REFERENCES conversation, seq BIGINT CHECK (seq > 0),
  sender_id TEXT NOT NULL, body BYTEA NOT NULL,
  PRIMARY KEY (conv_id, seq)
);
CREATE TABLE send_receipt (
  conv_id TEXT NOT NULL, sender_id TEXT NOT NULL, client_msg_id TEXT NOT NULL,
  request_hash BYTEA NOT NULL, seq BIGINT NOT NULL CHECK (seq > 0),
  PRIMARY KEY (conv_id, sender_id, client_msg_id)
);
CREATE TABLE outbox (
  conv_id TEXT NOT NULL, seq BIGINT NOT NULL CHECK (seq > 0),
  policy_version BIGINT NOT NULL, envelope BYTEA NOT NULL,
  published BOOLEAN NOT NULL DEFAULT FALSE,
  PRIMARY KEY (conv_id, seq)
);

下面的事务由 Message Store 实现，覆盖上述五张表中的相关行，不在事务内调用队列。这里用 PostgreSQL 的 READ COMMITTED + FOR UPDATE 表达会话内互斥：发送、入群、退群，以及权限或扇出模式的变更，都先锁住同一条 conversation 记录，直到事务结束才释放；每条消息还会更新普通计数器 head。若底层采用可串行化事务，发生冲突时必须整体重试。[8] 当前成员资格也在持锁期间检查。若已有幂等回执，可以先向原发送者返回自己的最小提交状态，即使他后来被移除也一样，但不能借此返回历史正文。

Message Store implements this transaction over the relevant rows in those five tables; it contains no Queue network call. PostgreSQL READ COMMITTED + FOR UPDATE expresses conversation exclusion: sends, joins/leaves, permission changes and fan-out policy changes first lock the same conversation row until transaction end. Each send also updates the ordinary head row. A serializable substrate retries conflicting transactions in full. [8] Check current membership inside the lock. A prior retry receipt may return the sender’s own minimal commit status after removal, without returning history bodies.

submit(auth, conv, key, request):
  require authenticated sender; validate bounded key and canonical request
  digest = SHA256(canonical request including type and attachment references)
  BEGIN transaction on the current conversation authority
  c = SELECT conversation WHERE conv_id = conv FOR UPDATE
  if c missing: ROLLBACK; return NOT_FOUND
  old = SELECT send_receipt WHERE (conv, auth.user, key)
  if old exists:
    ROLLBACK
    return old.request_hash == digest ? Sent(old.seq) : KEY_REUSED
  m = SELECT member WHERE (conv, auth.user)
  if m missing or not m.active: ROLLBACK; return FORBIDDEN
  s = checked_add(c.head, 1)
  UPDATE conversation SET head = s WHERE conv_id = conv
  INSERT message(conv, s, auth.user, canonical_message_bytes(request))
  INSERT send_receipt(conv, auth.user, key, digest, s)
  INSERT outbox(conv, s, c.policy_version, committed_envelope(request, s))
  COMMIT and wait for A5 durable commit confirmation
  return Sent(s)
on definite_abort_or_deadlock:
  ROLLBACK; bounded retry with the same key, or return RETRY_LATER
on commit_timeout_or_connection_loss:
  discard connection; return UNKNOWN
  // Retry the same key against the authority, never invent a new key.

为什么不会先提交 109，再补上 108？同一会话的下一笔事务，要等前一笔结束后才能读取并更新 head。前一笔若回滚，计数器、消息、回执和 outbox 会一起回滚。不能把它改成事务外的 nextval()：数据库序列不会在事务中止时收回已分配的值，因此可能留下空号。不过，确认响应到达客户端的顺序，仍可能与提交顺序不同。[8] 不同会话可以并行处理；若采用预留号段的方案，还必须另行证明哪些区间已关闭、哪些位置确定为空。

A5 还要求，消息写入及其事务提交决定，由跨故障域的三个副本按共识规则耐久记录；恢复或提升副本为主节点时，不能丢失已确认的日志尾部。失去多数派后，不再确认新的 Sent。Raft 的日志提交和领导者安全规则解释了这层前提，不能简化为“任意两个节点收到就算成功”。[3] 可以选择同时提供事务层和 Raft 复制层的数据库，例如 CockroachDB；事务提交检查与副本共识仍是两个实现层次。[9] 单机 PostgreSQL 事务加异步副本不满足 A5，若这样部署，就必须降低 Sent 的耐久承诺。本例没有实现或实测这套分布式存储。

Why can’t 109 commit while 108 remains pending? The next transaction reads and updates head only after its predecessor finishes; abort rolls back the counter, message, receipt and outbox together. An external nextval() is unsuitable: database sequences do not reclaim aborted values and can leave holes. Client ACK arrival order can still differ from commit order. [8] Conversations run independently; reserved ranges require separate closed-range/gap proofs.

A5 additionally requires durable consensus replication of the writes and transaction decision across three failure-domain-separated replicas, with no confirmed-suffix rollback on recovery/promotion. Without a majority, do not acknowledge new Sent. Raft’s commitment and leader-safety rules explain this premise; arbitrary two-node receipt is insufficient. [3] A transactional, Raft-replicated store such as CockroachDB is one option; transaction commit checks and replica consensus are distinct layers. [9] Local PostgreSQL transactions plus asynchronous standbys do not meet A5 and require a weaker Sent promise. This example neither implements nor experimentally verifies that distributed store.

四个故障窗口：如何恢复发送和投递 Four failure windows and durable retries

如果在提交前崩溃，客户端没有拿到 Sent，就用原键重试。如果已经提交但确认响应丢失，结果是未知，仍用原键找回同一个 seq。如果在返回 Sent 后、投递前崩溃，Relay 会重新扫描已提交的 outbox。如果发布成功后、标记完成前崩溃，队列就可能收到两次。Relay 只有拿到队列的耐久接受确认，才标记 published；在标记前失败，宁可重发也不能丢任务。因此，Worker 和设备都必须幂等，不能把发布到队列与数据库 COMMIT 当作一个原子操作。辅助函数 committed_envelope 只把已校验的正文或引用、发送者 sender、会话 conv 和序号 seq 编码为字节，不调用外部系统；outbox 事件的唯一键是 (conv,seq)。[2][4]

图中的投递载荷 envelope 已经包含正文，Worker 无须再次读取。若载荷只保存引用，就要补上历史读取和权限检查，并把这部分开销计入读取预算。离线设备先通过成员索引或 inbox 发现会话，再补齐日志；唤醒通知本身不算 Delivered。outbox 积压也要设置容量阈值，不能在队列故障时无限接收写入，直到磁盘耗尽。

A10 只约定客户端自动重试 24 小时，本例不删除 send_receipt。消息正文一年后过期，最小发送回执仍可保留。若要回收回执，须先按服务端可验证的发送代次或绝对受理期限，关闭旧请求的受理窗口，再排空有界的在途事务。仅靠客户端停止重试，或按 TTL 删除记录，都无法阻止无限迟到的请求再次插入。超过重试窗口后，若状态仍未知，也不能自动换一个新键。服务端对同键只保存一条逻辑记录，接收端按 (conv,seq) 合并重复消息，这与网络采用至少一次投递并不矛盾。

Crash before commit: no Sent, retry the key. Commit with lost ACK: outcome unknown, recover the original seq using that key. Crash after Sent but before delivery: Relay rescans committed outbox. Publish followed by a crash before marking: Queue can receive duplicates. Relay marks published only after durable Queue acceptance; failure before marking favors duplicates over loss. Workers/devices must be idempotent; publish is not atomic with database COMMIT. committed_envelope only serializes validated body/references, sender, conv and seq, with no external call; (conv,seq) is the unique event key. [2][4]

The diagram’s envelope includes the body; reference-only events add history reads and authorization checks to the read budget. Offline devices discover conversations through membership/inbox indexes and sync history. Wakeup notification is not Delivered. Bound outbox backlog instead of accepting writes until Queue outages fill disks.

A10 sets a 24 h client automatic retry window; this example does not delete send_receipt. Minimal receipts may outlive one-year message bodies. GC requires closing a server-verifiable sending epoch/absolute admission deadline and draining bounded in-flight transactions first. Client retry discipline or TTL deletion alone cannot reject arbitrarily late duplicates. Unknown expired-window status must not automatically get a new key. One server logical record per key and client merge by (conv,seq) coexist with at-least-once transport.

断线与乱序：补齐消息后才能推进连续游标 Disconnects and reordering: a contiguous cursor proves sync

即使 108 先到，游标也仍是 105。设备的接收游标 receive_cursor，表示它已经连续、持久保存到哪个位置。因此，要先缓冲 108，补回 106、107，持久保存后再推进到 108。若直接记录见过的最大序号，下次查询 seq>108 就会永久跳过这两条。设备也必须确认本地事务已提交后，才能发出 Delivered，不能先发回执再落盘。

设备重连后，先认证并登记新的连接代次，再为当前会话建立订阅、缓冲实时事件，然后从提交消息的权威存储取得水位 H，按 (cursor,H] 分页读取，每页 100 条。读取源必须至少追到 H；不能因为旧副本暂时没有更多记录，就认定没有新消息。历史追到 H 后，再合并 H 以上的缓冲事件。若订阅失效或缓冲溢出，应重新取得水位并再做一轮，不能宣布同步完成。Matrix v1.11 对有限时间线和缺口的处理体现了这个区别，但其中的 next_batch 并不是本章的整数序号 seq。[5]

Seeing 108 leaves the cursor at 105. receive_cursor proves the prefix durably stored on this device. Buffer 108, fill 106–107, then advance to 108. Recording the maximum seq makes a later seq>108 query permanently skip them. Send Delivered only after confirmed local commit.

On reconnect, authenticate and register a new epoch, establish a buffered subscription, then obtain committed H and page through (cursor,H] at 100 rows/page. Reads must reach H; a lagging replica tail is not evidence of no new messages. After history reaches H, merge buffered data above H. Subscription loss/overflow requires a new watermark and retry. Matrix v1.11’s limited timelines and gap handling illustrate the distinction; next_batch is not our integer seq. [5]

本地事务：消息记录与连续游标一起落盘 Local transaction: persist entries and the contiguous cursor together

下面是设备端伪代码。本地消息记录的主键为 (conv,view_epoch,seq)，接收游标按设备和授权历史视图 view 分别保存。视图由服务端授权，包含根据入群和历史权限确定的起点 first_seq；新视图的初始游标为 first_seq−1。设备在同一事务内按修订号 revision 有条件地插入或更新记录，检查下一个序号 c+1 是否已有记录，再把正文或标记与游标一起耐久提交。客户端不能自行制造 OMITTED 标记，声称缺口已经覆盖。

This device pseudocode uses local entries keyed by (conv,view_epoch,seq) and a cursor per device/view. The server authorizes view and its first_seq; a fresh view starts at first_seq−1. Inside one transaction, revision-conditional UPSERT entries, check for c+1, then durably commit entries/markers and cursor together. A client cannot invent OMITTED markers to cover gaps.

persist_batch(device, view, entries):
  require authenticated server stream and matching conv + view_epoch
  BEGIN local database write transaction
  c = SELECT receive_cursor WHERE (device, view) FOR UPDATE
  for entry in entries:
    require entry.seq >= view.first_seq
    require entry is MESSAGE, TOMBSTONE, or server-certified OMITTED
    UPSERT LocalEntry(view, entry.seq, entry.revision, entry.content)
      only if revision increases; reject conflicting equal revisions
  while c < MAX_SEQ and LocalEntry(view, c + 1) exists:
    c = checked_add(c, 1)
  if gap_storage_exceeds_budget():
    ROLLBACK; request a bounded fresh sync; return NO_ACK
  UPDATE receive_cursor SET upto = c WHERE (device, view)
  COMMIT using the local database's durable write option
  send Delivered(view, c)  // A lost receipt ACK is safe to retry.
on local_io_or_quota_failure:
  ROLLBACK if still active; do not acknowledge unconfirmed storage

消息被过滤或过期，也要说明缺失序号的去向。在保留期内，撤回或不可见的位置应返回删除标记 tombstone，或经过服务端认证的省略标记，继续覆盖原来的序号。不能过滤掉记录后，只返回一个更大的游标。若游标已经早于保留起点 floor，应返回 RESET_REQUIRED，并给出新授权的起点或有限快照，明确说明旧历史已无法补齐。重新入群时创建新的视图代次 view_epoch，旧页和旧回执不能混入新视图。

能合并修订，不代表能发现修订。修订号 revision 只决定如何合并同一位置上已经收到的修订。假设设备保存到 108 后离线，服务端随后把 106 改为 rev2，仅查询 seq>108 就发现不了这次变化。本章没有实现对游标之前的编辑或撤回进行传播。完整的离线同步还需要在新的日志位置追加控制事件，或维护独立的变更游标 change cursor，不能仅凭支持更高修订号合并，就宣称可以发现全部变更。

手机只保存到 105、桌面端已保存到 108，是合理的多端状态。用户级已读水位 read_upto=108 不能替代手机自己的 receive_cursor。新设备先查成员索引发现会话，再读取获得授权的历史窗口。每一页读取和每次实时投递，都要检查当前权限；离群后旧视图失效，尚未完成的同步也不能再宣布成功。实时推送用于尽快展示，日志始终负责修复缺失消息。

每条连接的待发缓冲上限为 1 MB，按每条约 1 KB 算，大约能放 1,000 条，对象开销另计。超限后标记 sync_required，停止堆积正文，并关闭连接或降低速度。若 180 万条连接都占满缓冲，仅载荷就需要 1.8 TB，因此还要设置网关总预算和公平调度。正在输入提示（Typing）和绿点可以丢弃，权威日志则不能为了降级而删除。恢复时若每秒重连 4,000 次、每次补齐 100 行，最坏会产生每秒 400,000 行的读取。因此，握手和历史扫描需要分别准入，界面可以先显示本地历史，再分批追齐。

Filtering and retention must close ranges too. Within retention, retracted/hidden slots return tombstones or authenticated server omission markers that still cover the original seq. Filtering out rows and returning a larger cursor is unsafe. A cursor before the retention floor gets RESET_REQUIRED with a newly authorized baseline/bounded snapshot, explicitly losing expired history rather than pretending completeness. Rejoining creates a new view_epoch; old pages/receipts cannot enter it.

Applying a revision is separate from discovering a change. Revision merging only applies updates already received for a position. If a device goes offline at cursor 108 and the server revises 106 to rev2, a seq>108 query cannot discover it. This chapter does not implement propagation of edits/retractions behind the cursor. Complete offline synchronization needs control events at new log positions or a separate change cursor; accepting a higher revision does not provide change discovery.

Phone 105 and desktop 108 are valid simultaneously. User read_upto=108 cannot replace phone receive_cursor. New devices discover conversations through membership indexes before fetching authorized windows. Every page and live dispatch checks current permission; leaving invalidates the old view and prevents unfinished sync from claiming success. Push is a fast path; the log repairs it.

A 1 MB/socket pending-send cap holds about 1,000 messages at 1 KB each, excluding object overhead. On overflow, mark sync_required, stop buffering bodies and close or slow the socket. All 1.8M sockets at that cap consume 1.8 TB payload, requiring fleet-local budgets and fair scheduling. Typing/presence can drop; authoritative history cannot. At 4,000 reconnects/s and 100 missing rows each, worst-case catch-up reaches 400,000 rows/s. Admit handshakes and history scans separately, displaying local history before paced catch-up.

图中的 Receipt Store 是一组逻辑表，与 conversation/member 位于同一个权威分区、同一个数据库。Receipt svc 虽是独立服务，却不使用独立的事务数据库。设备回执 DeviceReceipt 的主键包含 (user,device,conv,view_epoch)，私有和公开 Read 的主键各为 (user,conv,view_epoch)。下面的每次插入或更新，都通过数据库单语句 ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE … GREATEST(old,new)，或持有行锁时的更新来取最大值，不能由应用先读旧值，再无条件覆盖。事务还要锁住成员记录并校验当前视图；移除成员或重新入群时更新的也是这条记录。本例中，回执记录的初始化与水位更新也在同一事务内完成。

Read cannot jump a device’s own gaps. An honest client reports D=108 and R=108 only after contiguously persisting and displaying through 108 under product rules; both may share a batch. Socket-write success or a push provider accepting a notification does not prove Delivered. Receipt Store in the diagram is a logical table group in the same authority partition/database as conversation/member. Receipt svc is a separate service, not a separate transactional database. DeviceReceipt is keyed by (user,device,conv,view_epoch); private/public Read each use (user,conv,view_epoch). Each max UPSERT is one database ON CONFLICT DO UPDATE … GREATEST(old,new) statement or a locked-row update, never an unlocked read followed by overwrite. This transaction locks current membership and validates view; removal/rejoin update that same membership record. Receipt-row initialization and watermark updates share the transaction.

record_receipt(auth, view, delivered_candidate, read_candidate):
  require current authorized device identity; validate integer bounds
  BEGIN serializable transaction on the shared conversation/receipt authority
  m = SELECT current membership WHERE (auth.user, view.conv) FOR SHARE
  require m.active and m.view_epoch == view.epoch
  base = m.first_seq - 1
  tail = read current committed conversation head
  require base <= delivered_candidate <= tail
  ensure DeviceReceipt(user, device, conv, view_epoch) row via INSERT
    ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING, initialized to base
  d = SELECT DeviceReceipt WHERE (auth.user, auth.device, view) FOR UPDATE
  next_d = max(d.delivered_upto, delivered_candidate)
  require base <= read_candidate <= next_d
  UPDATE DeviceReceipt SET delivered_upto = next_d
  UPSERT PrivateRead(user, conv, view_epoch) using max(old, read_candidate)
  if current user preference permits public read receipts:
    UPSERT PublicRead(user, conv, view_epoch) using max(old, read_candidate)
  COMMIT and wait for durable confirmation
  return ACK(next_d)  // The sender can fetch authoritative state later.
on abort_or_unknown:
  retry the same watermarks; never regress a stored column

迟到的 D=107 不会降低已保存的 D=108。用户级 Read 可以合并多端状态，却不能修改另一台设备的接收游标。用户关闭公开已读后，仍可保留私有水位，用于计算自己的未读消息。回执确认只说明 Receipt Store 已按其耐久契约保存状态，A 可以随后补读；实时提示丢失时，也能从最新状态恢复，无须让 B 等待 A 上线。Matrix 的用户级 read-up-to 和私有回执体现了这一思路，Delivered 则是本章自定的契约。[5]

若产品要求“所有设备都已送达”，就必须枚举获得授权的设备，并约定长期离线设备何时到期，不能只取任一设备的最大水位。单聊可以批量上报连续前缀；但大群若让 N 人逐条上报已读，再向 N 人广播，工作量就接近 N²。因此，大群只保存水位并返回用户自己的未读状态，小群则可按需查询少量成员。另外，直接用 tail−read 计算未读条数，可能因可见性、撤回和入群窗口而高估；应按产品语义计算，或维护可修复的索引。

Late D=107 cannot lower stored D=108. User Read merges devices without changing any other device’s receive cursor. Public reads may be disabled while private state tracks personal unread. Receipt ACK proves durable Receipt Store state; A can fetch it later. Lost realtime hints repair from current state, without making B wait for A online. Matrix’s user read-up-to/private receipts support this model; Delivered remains our own contract. [5]

“All devices delivered” requires enumerating authorized devices and an expiry rule for long-offline ones, rather than any-device max. Batch prefixes suffice for DMs. Broadcasting N readers’ per-message receipts to N group members approaches N² work; large groups retain watermarks and return personal unread, optionally querying a few small-group members. Visibility, retractions and join windows can make tail−read overcount; compute product-aware unread counts or maintain a repairable index.

群聊：正文只存一份，按成员和观看连接安排投递 Groups: one body, separate membership and viewer fan-out

小群写入消息指针，大群按需拉取日志 Small-group refs and large-group reads

中文

小群先提交权威正文，再异步为成员写入消息收件索引 Inbox(user_id,conv_id,seq,msg_ref)。数据库中的唯一键负责合并重复任务，不能只靠 Worker 内存去重。用户先从自己的 inbox 获取候选引用，再批量读取正文并复核权限。图中只展开索引查询；补齐正文时，数据仍从 Message Store 返回 History API，再经 Edge 返回读者。Inbox 是可从成员记录和会话日志修复的派生索引，历史正文不按成员各存一份权威副本。

选择人数阈值时，必须同时看发言速率。按 A8，单群每秒 20 条消息、200 名成员，需要 20 × 200 = 4,000 个指针/秒，占每群每秒 10,000 个指针预算的 40%。若完全不留余量，数学上限为 10,000 ÷ 20 = 500 人；本例先选 200 人，给重试、其他工作和估算误差留出空间。若发言量升至每秒 100 条，200 人就需要每秒 20,000 个指针，已经超预算，应改为只拉取日志或限制发送速率。不能仅凭人数在几百人以内，就认定始终适合写时扇出。单群预算也不能代替整层预算：许多小群同时活跃，仍可能耗尽 Worker 容量。

大群有 N=50,000 名成员，若为全员写时扇出，每秒要写 20 × 50,000 = 1,000,000 个指针。改成正文只存一份、按会话和序号拉取历史，并只向当前观看的 M=500 条连接推送，就需要 20 × 500 = 10,000 帧/秒，投递目标数降为原来的 1/100。不过，这部分开销依然存在。M 统计设备连接，不是用户；若上万人同时观看，推送量仍会增长。Worker 向每个网关发送一份事件及该网关的订阅目标，由网关再向自己的连接分发，避免让一个 Worker 串行向全部连接写包。

大群中暂时不活跃的成员，通过成员索引发现会话，再拉取自己有权查看的序号区间。省去全员 inbox 写入后，就不能继续假设这些成员依靠 inbox 发现新消息。提醒可以按会话合并为“有新消息”标记或摘要，无须为每条正文复制一行。切换群策略时，也要以已提交水位 S 和策略版本 policy version 为界：新消息按新模式处理，旧 inbox 继续可读；切回写时扇出时，要补建所需区间，或让 S 之前的历史继续按日志拉取。重放任务携带版本，并使用唯一键，避免迁移时重复复制。

English

Small groups commit one body, then asynchronously write Inbox(user_id,conv_id,seq,msg_ref). A durable unique key absorbs repeated jobs, not a Worker RAM set. Read inbox candidates, hydrate bodies in batches and recheck permission; the diagram expands index reads only, while bodies return Message Store → History API → Edge → Reader. Inbox is a repairable derived index, not one authoritative body per member.

Thresholds need a message rate. A8 gives 20/s × 200 = 4,000 refs/s, 40% of a 10,000 per-group budget. The no-headroom ceiling is 10,000/20 = 500 members; choose 200 for retries, other work and uncertainty. At 100 messages/s, 200 members need 20,000 refs/s and exceed budget, forcing shared-log pull or throttling. Per-group budgets do not replace fleet budgets; many hot small groups can saturate workers.

For N=50,000, write fan-out is 20 × 50,000 = 1,000,000 refs/s. Store one body and pull by conversation/seq, pushing only to M=500 viewing connections for 10,000 frames/s. Delivery targets drop 100× but are not free. M counts device sockets, not users, and rises when more viewers are active. Worker sends one event with local subscription targets per gateway; each gateway distributes to its sockets instead of one Worker serially writing all sockets.

Cold large-group members discover conversations through membership indexes and read authorized sequence ranges; removing full inbox fan-out cannot still assume inbox discovery. Coalesce reminders into conversation-level new-message markers or summaries. Policy cutover occurs at committed S with a policy version. New messages use the new mode, old inbox remains readable, and switching back to push backfills required ranges or keeps pre-S pull. Versioned jobs and unique keys make migration replay safe.

历史可以分桶，当前热门会话仍可能成为瓶颈 Buckets do not eliminate the current hot conversation

中文

历史记录的键可以是 (conv_id,bucket,seq)，其中 bucket 表示固定时间窗或有上限的序号区间，避免把一年的消息都放进同一个巨大分区。Discord 2023 年的工程文章使用 channel 和时间桶组成分区键，也明确描述了热分区带来的延迟问题。[6] 这里参考的是键的组织方式和热点问题，不能把文中的节点数直接作为本题的容量依据。按群标识 group_id 哈希，只能分散不同群；同一个热门群，仍会集中访问当前桶和负责排序的服务。

如果热点只发生在投递侧，就先按网关并行分发观看者的消息，并为热门历史尾部增加缓存和限速。只有会话提交本身超过单个排序服务的预算时，才考虑批量分配序号并提交、拆成子日志，或把顺序要求放宽为每名发送者内部有序。多个独立序列需要额外的合并协议，不能任意拆分写入后，还承诺无需代价的会话全序。成员变更时，要更新成员版本 membership_version 和视图代次 view_epoch，并使旧订阅失效；历史 API、Worker 和网关都在授权派发时检查当前成员状态，权限依赖不可用就停止发送正文。已经获得授权并进入网络或设备的数据无法收回。对于被移除的成员，权限变更之后再进行授权检查的读取或投递会被拒绝；但这不代表移除事务一提交，所有在途字节便立即消失。若产品要求撤权响应返回后，旧订阅绝不再发包，就还要等待所有相关网关确认隔离屏障；超时不能报告屏障已经完成。知道群标识，或出现在旧 outbox 的目标列表中，都不代表当前仍有权限。

English

History can use (conv_id,bucket,seq), with fixed time or bounded sequence buckets preventing a year in one huge partition. Discord’s 2023 engineering article combines channels with time buckets and explicitly describes hot-partition latency. [6] We borrow the key shape and warning, not its node count as capacity. Hashing group_id distributes different groups; one hot group still hits one current bucket and ordering owner.

For delivery-only hotspots, first distribute viewers by gateway, cache hot history tails and rate-limit. Only a saturated commit owner calls for batched ordered commits, sublogs or weaker per-sender order. Independent sequences need a merge protocol; arbitrary split writes do not retain free total conversation order. Membership changes update membership_version/view_epoch and invalidate subscriptions. History API and Worker/Gateway check current membership at the dispatch authorization point, withholding bodies when authorization is unavailable. Data already authorized and placed on the network/device cannot be recalled. We reject newly authorized reads/deliveries after the change, without claiming instantaneous removal of in-flight bytes. A stricter no-old-subscription-send-after-revocation-response policy needs an additional acknowledged isolation barrier at every affected gateway; a timeout is not barrier completion. Neither group_id nor old outbox recipient lists authorize bodies.

在线绿点：用设备租约判断状态，并隔离故障 Presence: device leases, expiry windows and isolation

中文

在线状态以服务端当前有效的设备租约为准：只要任一授权设备的租约仍有效，用户就算在线。Gateway B 转交心跳时带上设备标识 device 和连接代次 epoch，Presence svc 用服务端自己的时间更新到期时间 expires_at。旧代次的续约会被拒绝，旧断线通知也只能删除自己那一版路由。因此，手机断网而桌面端仍在线时，不能把整个用户设为离线。最后在线时间 last_seen 可以按较低频率单独持久化，但不应与每 30 秒的续约一起写进消息事务。

A6 的 30 秒心跳和 90 秒租约允许短暂丢包，代价是绿点不会立即变灰。从最后一次有效心跳算起，到 90 秒时，查询就应判定租约失效。若活跃观察端完成刷新的间隔最多为 5 秒，健康条件下，界面约在 95 秒内反映离线。这 5 秒已包括刷新请求和展示完成，不能再另加一段未知的网络延迟。后台被冻结的手机或已经断网的观察端，不受这个界面更新时限约束；实际断网时刻与最后一次有效心跳也不是同一个计时起点，不能混用。

查询时应直接比较 expires_at 与当前服务端时间，并通过周期检查弥补变化提示的缺失。Redis 文档说明，expired 事件在实际删除键时产生，未必恰好发生在 TTL 归零时；Pub/Sub 也会丢失断线期间的事件。[7] 因此，订阅只能加快绿点更新，不能成为离线检测的唯一依据。重连时，先订阅并缓冲变化提示，再读取快照、按当前租约重新判定，遗漏的提示则由定期刷新修复。没有可靠游标的 Pub/Sub 无法保证快照与后续事件无缝衔接，过期通知也不是可靠队列。

好友可以按授权关系订阅变化，并按用户合并通知；万人群则在入群或打开成员面板时按需查询，避免全员相互订阅。如果每秒 60,000 次续约都向几百人广播，即使没有任何上下线变化，也会产生大量无效工作。应只发布实际在线状态的变化，并合并短时间内的反复变化。在线状态服务故障时，显示“未知”，或显示注明距今多久的 last_seen，不能把查询失败当作离线，更不能根据绿点决定是否持久保存消息。发送主流程仍是 Gateway → Message svc → Message Store。

English

Presence is the server view of valid device leases; a user is online if any authorized device lease is valid. Gateway B includes device and epoch on heartbeat, and Presence svc updates expires_at using its own time. Reject old epochs and delete only the matching version on disconnect. An offline phone with a live desktop must not make the user offline. Persist last_seen separately at lower frequency, not on every 30 s renewal in a message transaction.

A6’s 30 s heartbeat and 90 s lease tolerate transient loss at the cost of delayed offline display. From the last valid heartbeat, queries classify expiry at 90 s; active observer refresh completes at most every 5 s, giving about 95 s under healthy conditions. The 5 s includes request and display completion, not an extra unknown network delay. Suspended or disconnected observers lack this UI bound. Actual disconnection and last valid heartbeat are different clock origins.

Queries compare expires_at with current server time, and periodic checks repair change hints. Redis documents that expired events occur at actual deletion, not exactly at TTL zero, and Pub/Sub loses events during disconnects. [7] Subscriptions improve speed rather than define offline detection. On reconnect, subscribe and buffer hints before reading a snapshot and re-evaluating current leases. Periodic refresh repairs lost hints. Pub/Sub without a reliable cursor does not provide a seamless snapshot handoff; expiry notifications are not a reliable queue.

Friends may subscribe to authorized, per-user coalesced changes; large groups query on join or member-panel opens instead of mutual full subscriptions. Broadcasting hundreds of followers on each of 60,000 renewals/s creates waste without transitions. Publish effective changes and coalesce flaps. Presence failure shows Unknown or aged last_seen, not Offline. A green dot does not gate persistence; send remains Gateway → Message svc → Message Store.

追问与收尾：讲清每项保证成立的边界 Follow-ups and closing: state the boundaries

可以用一分钟这样讲述：长连接按设备路由，消息通过会话事务排序并耐久提交。提交前后都可能发生超时，客户端要用原键恢复结果。outbox 保存待投递任务；接收端在同一本地事务中保存消息和连续游标，确认提交后才发回执。已读状态按用户合并，却不能替任何设备补齐消息。小群按“发言速率 × 人数”核算写放大，大群则按当前观看连接安排投递。在线绿点来自可重建的租约视图，查询失败就显示“未知”。

再用三个反例自测：事务回滚后，会不会留下让游标永远等不到的空号？先收到 108，会不会让手机跳过 106、107？旧成员或旧设备的请求迟到时，哪些字段能阻止它修改路由，或在新的授权检查中取得正文？回答时，要分清消息已经提交、提交结果未知、消息尚未投递，以及权限已经撤销。

Explain in one minute: route sockets per device; order and durably commit through conversation transactions. Timeouts can hide success, so recover with the same key. Outbox retains delivery work; receivers commit entries and a contiguous cursor locally before ACK. User Read never substitutes for device catch-up. Size small-group fan-out by rate×members; push large groups by viewing connections. Rebuild presence leases and show Unknown on failure.

Test three counterexamples: can abort leave an unfillable sequence hole; can early 108 skip 106–107; and which fields stop stale member/device packets from mutating routes or obtaining newly authorized bodies? Distinguish committed, unknown, pending delivery and revoked authorization.
