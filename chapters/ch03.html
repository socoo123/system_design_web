粗略估算Back-of-the-envelope estimates

约 40 分钟 · 前置：Ch01、Ch02 · 5 组图，每笔数字改一次设计~40 min · after Ch01, Ch02 · 5 decks, each number changes a design

这一章结束你能做什么What you can do after this chapter

中文

  • 用 2–4 分钟把 DAU 走成峰值 QPS，再除以单机可用 QPS，得到无状态层要画几台。
  • 把 API 小响应和用户内容带宽画成两条不同的线，存储按主库加副本计，并指出 CDN 与日志不在这个数里。
  • 用公开的数量级填一条请求的延迟预算，并用可用性的定义算一条依赖会不会吃光错误预算。

English

  • In 2–4 minutes, turn DAU into peak QPS and divide by usable QPS per box to get the stateless tier.
  • Draw API bytes and user-content bytes on different paths, size storage as primary plus replicas, and leave CDN and logs out of that total.
  • Fill one request’s latency budget with published orders of magnitude, and use the definition of availability to see whether one dependency spends the error budget.

先把单位和假设钉死Lock units and assumptions first

中文

我先做 back-of-the-envelope。假设写在板上：1000 万 DAU，每人每天 20 次读、1 次写，峰值按 3 倍，一天口算先用 105 秒。然后四笔改图：峰值 QPS 决定无状态层几台，带宽把 API 和图片拆开，存储乘副本，延迟和可用性只用数量级当预算。数字是教学假设，不是某家公司的线上报表。

Ch01 管时间盒，Ch02 管盒子怎么加上去。这一章是澄清之后、高层图定稿之前的那几分钟。盒子画得出来，却说不清是 2 千 QPS 还是 2 万 QPS，后面的缓存、分片和多地域都是在猜。估算的产物是一个会改图的数量级，加上你写在板上的假设。算到小数点后三位，说明你已经离开 back-of-the-envelope。

本章容量用 10 的幂：1 KB = 103 字节，1 MB = 106，1 GB = 109，1 TB = 1012。按 1024 进制的单位写作 KiB、GiB、TiB [6]；两套单位在 KB 上差 2.4%，GB 上差 7.4%，TB 上差 10%，都改不了要不要分片。存储留在字节（B），带宽最后写成 bit/s。1 byte = 8 bit。小写 b 是 bit，大写 B 是 byte。100 Mb/s ÷ 8 = 12.5 MB/s。把 100 Mbps 当成 100 MB/s，会把链路放大 8 倍。

一天精确是 86,400 秒。白板除以 105。因为分母更大，这样得到的 QPS 偏小，大约是精确值的 86,400 / 100,000 = 0.864 倍。板上的 QPS 当下限，说完用 86,400 复核一次。两个分母不要混用：用 105 除出来的 QPS，乘回去也要乘 105，才能回到每天的请求数。

English

I’ll start with back-of-the-envelope. Assumptions on the board: 10 million DAU, 20 reads and 1 write per user per day, a 3× peak, and 105 seconds in a day for mental math. Four numbers then change the drawing: peak QPS sets the stateless tier, bandwidth splits the API from images, storage multiplies by replicas, and latency and availability stay orders of magnitude used as a budget. These are teaching assumptions, not a company’s production dashboard.

Ch01 is the clock. Ch02 is how the boxes appear. This chapter is the few minutes after clarify and before the high-level picture freezes. People can draw boxes and still not know whether the system is 2 thousand or 20 thousand QPS, so cache, sharding, and multi-region are guesses. The output is an order of magnitude that changes the drawing, plus the assumptions you left on the board. Three decimal places means you have left back-of-the-envelope.

Capacity in this chapter uses powers of ten: 1 KB = 103 bytes, 1 MB = 106, 1 GB = 109, 1 TB = 1012. The powers-of-1024 units are written KiB, GiB, and TiB [6]. The gap between the two is 2.4% at KB, 7.4% at GB, and 10% at TB, and none of it decides sharding. Storage stays in bytes (B). Bandwidth ends in bits per second. 1 byte = 8 bits. Lowercase b is a bit, uppercase B is a byte. 100 Mb/s ÷ 8 = 12.5 MB/s. Reading 100 Mbps as 100 MB/s inflates the link by 8×.

A day is exactly 86,400 seconds. On the board, divide by 105. The larger denominator makes QPS about 86,400 / 100,000 = 0.864 of the exact value, so the board number is a floor. Check it once with 86,400. Do not mix the denominators: QPS you got by dividing by 105 has to be multiplied by 105 to return to requests per day.

峰值 QPS 落到无状态层：除出来的是台数Peak QPS lands on the stateless tier: the division is a box count

正常路径：从 DAU 除到台数Walkthrough: divide DAU down to a box count

中文

按板上的顺序念。假设 DAU = 10,000,000，每人每天读 20 次、写 1 次。读请求/天 = 10,000,000 × 20 = 200,000,000。写请求/天 = 10,000,000 × 1 = 10,000,000。读:写 = 200,000,000 : 10,000,000 = 20:1。白板把一天收成 105 秒，平均读 QPS = 200,000,000 / 100,000 = 2,000，平均写 QPS = 10,000,000 / 100,000 = 100。日常峰值假设是 3 倍，而且这 3 倍是同一天流量的形状，不是在 2 亿次之外再加一轮。峰值读 QPS = 2,000 × 3 = 6,000，峰值写 QPS = 100 × 3 = 300。

无状态 App 的舒适处理能力假设为 1,000 QPS/台：一次请求主要打缓存、组 20 KB 的 JSON，不是一次跨库大查询。这个 1,000 是你写在板上的数量级，不是压测报告。计划用量取舒适值的 60%，这样峰值时整层 App 还留着空位给发布和估错的倍数。可用 QPS/台 = 1,000 × 0.6 = 600。实例数 = 6,000 / 600 = 10。写峰值 300 分到 10 台上大约每台 30 QPS，不改变 N。读:写 20:1 的设计后果是：App 层按读的峰值买，存储按写的每天积分买，两笔不要互相代替。

用 86,400 复核，避免 105 的偏差被当成精确值。200,000,000 / 86,400 = 2,314.8，约 2,315 平均读 QPS。×3 = 6,944 峰值读 QPS。6,944 / 600 = 11.57，向上取整是 12 台。写的精确平均是 10,000,000 / 86,400 = 115.7 QPS，峰值约 347。10 台和 12 台是同一个决定：一小层无状态服务，前面一个 LB。它不会变成「必须先分库」。分片要等写 QPS 或数据量真正越过单集群，那是后面存储那一笔的事，不是 6,000 读 QPS 这一笔。

English

Say it in board order. Assume DAU = 10,000,000, with 20 reads and 1 write per user per day. Read requests/day = 10,000,000 × 20 = 200,000,000. Write requests/day = 10,000,000 × 1 = 10,000,000. Read:write = 200,000,000 : 10,000,000 = 20:1. With a day collapsed to 105 seconds, average read QPS = 200,000,000 / 100,000 = 2,000, and average write QPS = 10,000,000 / 100,000 = 100. The daily peak assumption is 3×, and that 3× is the shape of the same day, not a second pile of traffic on top of 200 million requests. Peak read QPS = 2,000 × 3 = 6,000. Peak write QPS = 100 × 3 = 300.

Assume a comfortable 1,000 QPS per stateless App box: the request mostly hits cache and assembles a 20 KB JSON body, not a wide database query. The 1,000 is an order of magnitude you write on the board, not a load-test report. Plan to use 60% of comfortable so the fleet still has room for a deploy and a wrong multiplier. Usable QPS/box = 1,000 × 0.6 = 600. Instances = 6,000 / 600 = 10. Peak writes of 300 spread over 10 boxes are about 30 QPS each and do not change N. The design consequence of 20:1 is that the fleet is bought from peak reads, and storage is bought from the daily write integral. Do not swap those two jobs.

Check with 86,400 so the 105 bias is not treated as exact. 200,000,000 / 86,400 = 2,314.8, about 2,315 average read QPS. Times 3 is 6,944 peak read QPS. 6,944 / 600 = 11.57, which rounds up to 12 boxes. Exact average writes are 10,000,000 / 86,400 = 115.7 QPS, and the peak is about 347. Ten versus twelve is the same decision: a small stateless tier behind one LB. It does not become “shard first.” Sharding waits until write QPS or bytes actually outgrow one cluster, which is the storage step, not this 6,000-read step.

为什么是 10 台，而峰值必须落在这层上Why the count is 10, and why peak has to hit this tier

中文

60% 不是一个装饰系数。10 台全部在线时，舒适容量 = 10 × 1,000 = 10,000 QPS，峰值 6,000 只用了 6,000 / 10,000 = 60%。发布或宕掉 1 台之后，剩下 9 × 1,000 = 9,000 的舒适容量，6,000 / 9,000 = 67%，仍低于舒适线。要让峰值顶满舒适值，至少需要 6,000 / 1,000 = 6 台。买 10 台意味着大约还能再掉 4 台才坐到舒适天花板上。如果按天花板买，N = 6,000 / 1,000 = 6，峰值已经跑在 100% 舒适值上，再死 1 台就是 6,000 / 5,000 = 1.2 倍舒适值，队列会开始堆。

App 画成无状态，是因为这个除法要求任何一台都能接 LB 送来的下一笔读。会话、feed 的权威记录、图片字节都不放在这层。权威记录在后面的 Primary 里，图片在对象存储里。所以扩容是加实例，不是把用户粘到某一台上。10 台这个数量级也告诉你第一张图不需要服务网格、跨地域协调器或按用户哈希的分片。那些东西要等约束变了再加。

English

The 60% is the room you are buying. With all 10 boxes up, comfortable capacity = 10 × 1,000 = 10,000 QPS, and the peak of 6,000 uses 6,000 / 10,000 = 60%. After a deploy or one death, 9 × 1,000 = 9,000 remains, and 6,000 / 9,000 = 67%, still under the comfortable line. Sitting exactly on that line would take 6,000 / 1,000 = 6 boxes. Buying 10 means you can lose about 4 before you are on the ceiling. Size to the ceiling instead, N = 6,000 / 1,000 = 6, and one death becomes 6,000 / 5,000 = 1.2 times comfortable. That is where the queue starts to grow.

App is stateless because the division only works if any instance can take the next read from the LB. Sessions, the authoritative feed row, and image bytes do not live here. The row lives in Primary later, and the image lives in object storage. Scaling is adding instances, not pinning a user to a box. A count of about 10 also tells you the first picture does not need a service mesh, a cross-region coordinator, or a user-hash shard map. Those wait for a constraint that actually demands them.

替代：换单机能力，或峰值其实是大促Alternative: a different box, or a peak that is actually a sale

中文

面试官若说「这种 JSON 服务一台舒适能到 5,000 QPS」，不要护着 10 这个结果，重做除法。可用 = 5,000 × 0.6 = 3,000。N = 6,000 / 3,000 = 2。再加 1 台给发布，画 3 台。方法没变，变的是你写上去的单机假设。反过来，若他们坚持 N = 1，那要求舒适容量至少 6,000 / 0.6 = 10,000 QPS，而且这台一挂整个读路径就没了。单机可以当第一版的口头替代，只要你把故障域说清楚：一台的可用性和 10 台不是同一个预算。

若峰值倍数从日常 3 改成大促 10，平均 2,000 × 10 = 20,000 峰值读 QPS。N = 20,000 / 600 ≈ 33.3，向上是 34 台。10 台的舒适天花板只有 10,000 QPS，吃不住 20,000。大促的 10 倍通常不该变成常年开机的 34 台，那是排队、限流和降级的理由。你要先问清这 10 倍是每天的形状，还是一年几次的活动。问清楚之前，板上只留一个倍数。

English

If they say a JSON box is comfortably 5,000 QPS, do not defend the 10. Divide again. Usable = 5,000 × 0.6 = 3,000. N = 6,000 / 3,000 = 2. Add one box for deploys and draw 3. The method is the same; the per-box assumption changed. If they insist on N = 1, that box must be at least 6,000 / 0.6 = 10,000 QPS comfortable, and its death removes the whole read path. One box is a fair first-version alternative only if you say the failure domain out loud: one box and ten boxes are not the same availability budget.

If the multiplier moves from a daily 3× to a sale at 10×, average 2,000 × 10 = 20,000 peak read QPS. N = 20,000 / 600 ≈ 33.3, which rounds up to 34. The 10-box comfortable ceiling is only 10,000 QPS, short of 20,000. A 10× sale is usually a reason to queue, limit, and degrade, not a reason to run 34 boxes all year. Ask whether the 10× is the shape of every day or a few events a year. Until that is answered, keep one multiplier on the board.

常见错误：用平均当峰值Common mistake: treating the average as the peak

中文

只报 2,000 QPS，再除以 600，得到 2,000 / 600 ≈ 3.3，向上 4 台。4 × 600 = 2,400 QPS 的计划容量，峰值却是 6,000，差了 6,000 / 2,400 = 2.5 倍。4 台的舒适天花板是 4,000 QPS，连 6,000 的峰值都接不住。这个错误的图是峰值箭头仍然落在「按平均买的那一小撮机器」上，忙的那一个小时会先把连接和 CPU 打满，缓存超时之后流量又会掉进数据库。收尾时主动说：平时 2,000，峰值我按 6,000 买到 10 台；再高出这 3 倍假设，就限流，不按线性把机器乘上去。

English

Quote only 2,000 QPS and divide by 600: 2,000 / 600 ≈ 3.3, which rounds up to 4 boxes. Planned capacity is 4 × 600 = 2,400 QPS, against a peak of 6,000, short by 6,000 / 2,400 = 2.5×. The comfortable ceiling of 4 boxes is 4,000 QPS, which does not hold 6,000. The broken drawing keeps the peak arrow on a fleet that was bought for the average. The busy hour fills connections and CPU first, cache timeouts follow, and the load falls through to the database. Close with the line: steady state is 2,000, I buy 10 boxes for 6,000, and anything above that 3× assumption is shed rather than multiplied into more boxes.

带宽：小响应和用户内容走两条线Bandwidth: a small response and user content take different paths

正常路径：哪条线真正扛字节Walkthrough: which line actually carries the bytes

中文

API 路径上的字节是 feed JSON，假设一次 20 KB = 20,000 字节。峰值读 QPS 沿用上一笔的白板数 6,000。峰值字节率 = 6,000 × 20,000 = 120,000,000 字节/秒 = 120 MB/s。换成 bit：120 × 8 = 960 Mb/s。这是 10 台 App 合起来的出口，量级约 1 Gb/s，不是说单台要一张跑满的 1 Gb/s 网卡。平均时只有 2,000 × 20 KB = 40 MB/s = 320 Mb/s。链路按峰值 960 Mb/s 买，不按平均 320 Mb/s 买，否则忙的那一秒会先打满网卡。

用户内容是另一条假设：一次打开要拉大约 500 KB 的图片，500 KB = 500,000 字节。这些字节的来源是对象存储里的原件或派生图，经过 CDN 回到 Client。峰值 = 6,000 × 500,000 = 3,000,000,000 字节/秒 = 3 GB/s。×8 = 24 Gb/s。24 Gb/s 除以 API 的 960 Mb/s：24,000 / 960 = 25，图片路径大约是 API 路径的 25 倍。所以图上出现第二条回程，App 的框上仍写 20 KB。CDN 是缓存，双层矩形；对象存储是耐久数据，画成圆柱。请求可以从 Client 直接到 CDN，不必先把图片字节拖进 App。

24 Gb/s 是 CDN 边缘的出口，不是对象存储的出口。按 90% 命中（假设），回源只有未命中的 10%：3 GB/s × 0.1 = 300 MB/s = 2.4 Gb/s，仍是 API 那条线 960 Mb/s 的 2.5 倍。命中率从 90% 掉到 80%，回源就翻倍到 4.8 Gb/s。所以对象存储的出口按命中率可能掉到的下限准备，不按平均命中率准备；CDN 边缘按 24 Gb/s 准备。两个数都和 App 的实例数无关。

单次传输时间解释为什么「包很大」不一定先改延迟预算。Dean 的两份幻灯片都写：在 1 Gb/s 网络上发送 2 KB 大约 20 微秒，这是数量级 [1][2]。20 KB 大约是 2 KB 的 10 倍，按这个数量级大约 200 微秒。纯比特算术是 20,000 × 8 / 109 秒 = 160 微秒，和 200 微秒同一个数量级。相对后面 21 ms 左右的往返预算，0.2 ms 还不到 1%。500 KB 在 1 Gb/s 上是 4 × 106 bit / 109 = 4 ms，开始能看见，但 6,000 路并发根本塞不进一张 1 Gb/s 网卡。带宽这一笔改的是路径，不是把 App 的超时从 21 ms 改成 25 ms。

English

Bytes on the API path are the feed JSON, assumed at 20 KB = 20,000 bytes. Peak read QPS stays at the board figure of 6,000. Peak byte rate = 6,000 × 20,000 = 120,000,000 bytes/s = 120 MB/s. In bits, 120 × 8 = 960 Mb/s. That is the combined egress of the 10 Apps, on the order of 1 Gb/s, not one box saturating its own 1 Gb/s NIC. The average is only 2,000 × 20 KB = 40 MB/s = 320 Mb/s. Buy the link for the peak of 960 Mb/s, not the average of 320 Mb/s, or the busy second fills the NIC first.

User content is a separate assumption: one open pulls about 500 KB of images, and 500 KB = 500,000 bytes. Those bytes come from originals or renditions in object storage and return through the CDN to the client. Peak = 6,000 × 500,000 = 3,000,000,000 bytes/s = 3 GB/s. Times 8 is 24 Gb/s. Divide 24 Gb/s by the API’s 960 Mb/s: 24,000 / 960 = 25, so the image path is about 25× the API path. That is why a second return appears, while the App box still says 20 KB. The CDN is a cache, drawn as a double rectangle. Object storage is durable, drawn as a cylinder. The client can go to the CDN directly. The image bytes do not have to be dragged through App.

24 Gb/s is the CDN edge’s egress, not the object store’s. At an assumed 90% hit rate, origin sees only the 10% that miss: 3 GB/s × 0.1 = 300 MB/s = 2.4 Gb/s, still 2.5× the API line’s 960 Mb/s. If the hit rate falls from 90% to 80%, origin doubles to 4.8 Gb/s. So size the object store’s egress for the lowest hit rate you expect, not the average one, and size the CDN edge for 24 Gb/s. Neither number depends on the App instance count.

Transmit time is why a large body does not automatically rewrite the latency budget. Both of Dean’s slide decks say that sending 2 KB on a 1 Gb/s network is about 20 microseconds, as an order of magnitude [1][2]. 20 KB is about 10× 2 KB, so that order of magnitude is about 200 microseconds. Pure bit arithmetic is 20,000 × 8 / 109 seconds = 160 microseconds, the same order as 200 microseconds. Against the later round-trip budget near 21 ms, 0.2 ms is under 1%. A 500 KB object on 1 Gb/s is 4 × 106 bits / 109 = 4 ms, which you can see, but 6,000 of those concurrent transfers do not fit one 1 Gb/s NIC. This step changes the path, not an App timeout from 21 ms to 25 ms.

为什么图片不能算进 API 的出口Why image bytes are not the API’s egress

中文

App 的 10 台实例是按 6,000 QPS 的请求数买的，不是按 24 Gb/s 买的。若把 500 KB 也从 App 返回，每台在峰值要分到 24 Gb/s / 10 = 2.4 Gb/s，外加组 JSON 的 CPU。请求数那一笔的 1,000 QPS 舒适假设立刻失效，因为瓶颈变成了网卡。拆开之后，App 每台分到的 API 带宽大约是 960 Mb/s / 10 = 96 Mb/s，和 1,000 QPS 的 JSON 服务是匹配的。对象存储和 CDN 按字节和命中率扩，和 App 的实例数分开算。

平均带宽也不能代替峰值。图片若按平均 2,000 QPS 估算，2,000 × 500 KB = 1 GB/s = 8 Gb/s，仍然必须离开 App，但链路会在 3 GB/s 的峰值上不够。和 QPS 一样，容量看峰值那一秒，存储才看一天的积分。

English

The 10 App instances were bought for 6,000 QPS of requests, not for 24 Gb/s. If the 500 KB also returned from App, each box would take 24 Gb/s / 10 = 2.4 Gb/s at the peak, plus the CPU to build JSON. The 1,000 QPS comfortable assumption from the request step dies immediately, because the NIC is the bottleneck. After the split, each App’s share of API bandwidth is about 960 Mb/s / 10 = 96 Mb/s, which matches a JSON service at 1,000 QPS. Object storage and the CDN scale on bytes and hit rate, separate from the App instance count.

一条请求上的延迟预算A latency budget along one request

正常路径：把数量级加在同一条请求上Walkthrough: add the orders of magnitude on one request

中文

这条预算只描述一次 feed 读。Client 到附近 Edge 的往返假设为 20 ms，写在板上，标明不是出处里的数。Edge 到 App、App 到 Cache 各用一次同机房往返。两份 Dean 幻灯片都把这个往返印成 500,000 ns，也就是 0.5 ms [1][2]，本章只把它当数量级，而且只在这两跳真的还在同一个机房时使用。主存引用两份材料都是 100 ns。一次缓存查找即使做上千次内存访问，100 ns × 1,000 = 100 微秒 = 0.1 ms，在 20 ms 旁边仍然看不见，所以命中累计写成 20 + 0.5 + 0.5 = 21 ms。绿箭头是响应已经包含在往返里的那一段，不再加一次。

峰值时有多少请求走未命中这条线，由命中率决定。按 95% 命中（假设），6,000 × 0.05 = 300 次/秒回 Primary。按每次未命中一次寻道、每块盘约 1 / 0.010 秒 = 100 次/秒，是 3 块盘的量。命中率掉到 90%，未命中翻倍到 600 次/秒，约 6 块盘。命中率不是锦上添花的监控指标，它直接决定 Primary 要几块盘；也因此缓存整层故障时，不能让 6,000 次/秒原样落到 Primary 上。

English

This budget describes one feed read. The client-to-nearby-edge round trip is assumed at 20 ms and labeled as not from the source. Edge to App, and App to Cache, each take one same-datacenter round trip. Both of Dean’s slide decks print that round trip as 500,000 ns, which is 0.5 ms [1][2]. This chapter uses it only as an order of magnitude, and only while those hops really stay in one datacenter. Both sources print a main-memory reference as 100 ns. Even a thousand memory touches inside a cache lookup are 100 ns × 1,000 = 100 microseconds = 0.1 ms, still invisible next to 20 ms, so the hit cumulative is 20 + 0.5 + 0.5 = 21 ms. The green arrow is the return already inside the round trip. It is not added a second time.

A miss visits Primary: another 0.5 ms round trip, plus the seek. Both slide decks print a disk seek as 10,000,000 ns = 10 ms. 21 + 0.5 + 10 = 31.5 ms, drawn as about 32 ms. 10 ms / 0.5 ms = 20, so one seek is about 20 same-datacenter round trips. 10 ms / 100 ns = 100,000, so a seek is about one hundred thousand memory references. Those divisions are why the hit and the miss are different lines, and why both are still smaller than one 150 ms intercontinental packet.

How many requests take the miss line at the peak is set by the hit rate. At an assumed 95% hit rate, 6,000 × 0.05 = 300 reads/s reach Primary. At one seek per miss and about 1 / 0.010 s = 100 seeks/s per disk, that is 3 disks’ worth. If the hit rate falls to 90%, misses double to 600/s, about 6 disks. The hit rate is not a nice-to-have dashboard number; it decides how many disks Primary needs. It is also why a whole-cache failure cannot drop 6,000 reads/s onto Primary unchanged.

故障窗口：串行 RPC 和寻道叠在同一条关键路径上Failure window: serial RPCs and seeks stacked on one critical path

中文

同机房 10 次串行往返是 10 × 0.5 ms = 5 ms。相对 20 ms 的接入，5 ms 开始能看见；相对 150 ms，它仍然很小。窗口出在你把这 10 次 RPC 再叠上跨洲，或者叠上寻道的时候。一次未命中若在应用里串行做 10 次寻道，仅寻道就是 100 ms，用户看到的就不再是 32 ms。峰值若因为缓存故障整批掉进这条路径，6,000 次寻道/秒对着大约 100 次/秒的单盘，差了 60 倍。所以缓存故障不能等于「全部改读盘」。下一节的可用性图会把这条退路加上限流。

English

Ten serial same-datacenter round trips are 10 × 0.5 ms = 5 ms. Next to a 20 ms access hop, 5 ms is visible. Next to 150 ms, it is still small. The window opens when those 10 RPCs are stacked on an intercontinental hop, or stacked on seeks. A miss that does 10 serial seeks inside the app spends 100 ms on seeks alone, and the user no longer sees 32 ms. If a cache failure dumps the peak onto that path, 6,000 seeks/s against about 100 seeks/s per disk is a factor of 60. A cache failure cannot mean “read disk for everyone.” The availability drawing adds a limit on that fallback.

可用性：一条依赖的错误率会吃掉预算Availability: one dependency’s error rate spends the budget

正常路径：先写定义，再让一条依赖去对账Walkthrough: write the definition, then bill one dependency

中文

Google SRE 书的 Embracing Risk [3] 把四个 9 的例子写成：99.99% 的目标在一年里最多大约 52.56 分钟不可用。这个数等于 0.0001 × 365 × 24 × 60 = 0.0001 × 525,600 = 52.56 分钟。同一套定义往下乘：99.9% 是 0.001 × 8,760 小时 = 8.76 小时/年；99.999% 是 0.00001 × 525,600 = 5.256 分钟/年，约 5.26 分钟；99% 是 0.01 × 365 = 3.65 天/年。这些是定义算出来的允许窗口，不是 feed 已经达到的运行数据，也不是对外合同。

同一章更常用请求成功率，而不是「全站黑了多久」，因为全球服务往往还在部分可用。书里的例子是：一天 250 万次请求、日目标 99.99%，允许的错误是 2,500,000 × 0.0001 = 250 次。Service Level Objectives [4] 把可用性说成服务可用时间的比例，也常定义成格式正确的请求里成功的比例，称为 yield。没有明确后果的目标是 SLO，不是 SLA。本章的 99.9% 是工作 SLO：请面试官改。它不是「我们承诺 99.9%」。

把它用到 feed 的白板读量上。每天 200,000,000 次读 × 0.001 = 200,000 次允许失败。Cache 若有 0.1% 的调用失败，而且失败对用户可见，失败次数同样是 200,000,000 × 0.001 = 200,000。这一跳花掉 100% 的三个 9 预算，Edge 和 App 的发布、自己的缺陷都没有余额。若工作目标改成四个 9，允许次数是 200,000,000 × 0.0001 = 20,000/天。200,000 / 20,000 = 10，Cache 的错误是这个预算的 10 倍。书里错误预算的算法是同一类除法：目标 99.999% 时失败率预算是 0.001%，一次造成 0.0002% 失败的问题花掉 0.0002 / 0.001 = 20% 的季度预算。我们只是把被除数换成了 Cache 的错误率。

English

The Embracing Risk chapter [3] of Google’s SRE book states the four-nines example as: a 99.99% target may be unavailable for up to 52.56 minutes in a year. That figure is 0.0001 × 365 × 24 × 60 = 0.0001 × 525,600 = 52.56 minutes. The same definition scales: 99.9% is 0.001 × 8,760 hours = 8.76 hours/year; 99.999% is 0.00001 × 525,600 = 5.256 minutes/year, about 5.26 minutes; 99% is 0.01 × 365 = 3.65 days/year. These are allowed windows from the definition. They are not measured feed uptime, and they are not a contract.

That chapter prefers request success rate over “how long was the whole site black,” because a global service is often partly up. Its example is 2.5 million requests in a day at a 99.99% daily target, which allows 2,500,000 × 0.0001 = 250 errors. Service Level Objectives [4] describes availability as the fraction of time a service is usable, and often as the fraction of well-formed requests that succeed, called yield. A target with no explicit consequence is an SLO, not an SLA. The 99.9% in this chapter is a working SLO: ask them to correct it. It is not “we promise 99.9%.”

Apply it to the feed’s board read volume. 200,000,000 reads/day × 0.001 = 200,000 allowed failures. If Cache fails 0.1% of calls and those failures are user-visible, the count is also 200,000,000 × 0.001 = 200,000. That hop spends 100% of a three-nines budget. Deploys of Edge and App, and their own defects, have nothing left. If the working target moves to four nines, the allowance is 200,000,000 × 0.0001 = 20,000/day. 200,000 / 20,000 = 10, so the cache errors are 10× that budget. The book’s error-budget division is the same kind of step: at a 99.999% target the failure-rate budget is 0.001%, and a problem that fails 0.0002% of queries spends 0.0002 / 0.001 = 20% of the quarterly budget. Here the numerator is the cache error rate.

为什么红线必须在用户面前停住Why the red line has to stop before the user

中文

时间定义和请求定义回答的不是同一句人话。0.1% 的错误率在时间公式里对应 8.76 小时/年，但 Cache 的 0.1% 通常不是连续黑 8.76 小时，而是全年大约每 1,000 次读失败一次。SRE 书强调全球服务要用请求成功率，就是因为系统往往还部分活着。图上用的是 20 万次/天，时间上的 8.76 小时写在旁边，标明它是同一个 0.1% 的另一种写法。

失败关闭把 Cache 放进关键路径，用户可见错误率至少是这一跳的错误率。绕路把红虚线停在 App，成功响应改从 Primary 回来，Cache 就不再单独吃掉那 20 万。绕路要分清两种故障的量级。0.1% 的零星错误在峰值只是 6,000 × 0.001 = 6 次/秒，绕到 Primary 远低于一块盘约 100 次/秒的寻道，放心绕。整层 Cache 宕机是另一回事：6,000 次/秒全部想绕，正是上一节那约 60 块盘的缺口。所以绕路要封顶（这里约 100 次/秒）并按键合并，不能在 Cache 挂掉的那一秒把峰值原样打到 Primary。

封顶之外还剩约 5,900 次/秒。产品若允许 feed 旧 30 秒，就返回 App 本地还留着的、仍在这个窗口里的旧值，用户可见失败不增加；不允许，就回 503。回 503 的账很难看：200,000 ÷ 5,900 ≈ 34 秒，峰值下半分钟就花完一天的预算。这就是为什么缓存层本身也要按故障域拆开、要有副本，而不是只靠 App 里的一行 fallback。30 秒是产品假设，支付类的请求不能沿用这句。

English

The time definition and the request definition do not answer the same sentence. A 0.1% error rate matches 8.76 hours/year in the time formula, but a 0.1% cache error is usually not a continuous 8.76-hour blackout. It is about one failed read in 1,000 across the year. The SRE book prefers request success rate for global services because the system is often partly alive. The drawing uses 200,000/day. The 8.76 hours sit beside it as the other way to write the same 0.1%.

Fail-closed puts Cache on the critical path, so the user-visible error rate is at least that hop’s error rate. The bypass stops the red dashed line at App and returns success from Primary, so Cache alone no longer spends those 200,000. Tell the two failure sizes apart. Scattered 0.1% errors are only 6,000 × 0.001 = 6 reads/s at the peak, far under the roughly 100 seeks/s of one disk, so bypass them freely. A whole-cache outage is different: all 6,000/s want to bypass, which is the previous section’s gap of about 60 disks. So cap the bypass (about 100/s here) and coalesce by key. Do not drop the peak onto Primary in the second the cache dies.

About 5,900/s remain above the cap. If the product allows the feed to be 30 seconds stale, return the copy App still holds locally inside that window, and the user-visible failure does not grow. If not, return 503. The 503 bill is ugly: 200,000 ÷ 5,900 ≈ 34 seconds, so half a minute at the peak spends the whole day’s budget. That is why the cache tier itself needs separate failure domains and replicas, not just one fallback line in App. Thirty seconds is a product assumption. A payment request does not inherit that sentence.

替代：把多段可用性乘在一起时要停手Alternative: stop when multiplying availabilities together

中文

若 Edge、App、Cache 各自成功 99.95%，并且失败关闭、失败互不重叠，粗算错误率约 0.05% × 3 = 0.15%。精确一点：0.9995 × 0.9995 = 0.99900025，再乘 0.9995 得 0.99850075，大约 99.85%。不可用比例约 0.001499。0.001499 × 8,760 小时 ≈ 13.1 小时/年，已经多于单个 99.9% 目标的 8.76 小时。0.001499 × 200,000,000 ≈ 30 万次失败/天。这个乘法只说明一条串行失败关闭的链会比其中最差的那一跳更差。

SRE workbook 的 Implementing SLOs [5] 直接否定下一步幻想：单区域 99.9% 的服务，为了凑 99.95% 就再部署一个区域，并宣称两个区域独立所以变成 99.9999%。书里写明，这假设两边完全独立，而共享依赖、共享故障域和控制面几乎总会同时打到两边。本章不把 10 台 App 乘成更多的 9。10 台改变的是峰值 QPS 有没有地方去，不是把 99.9% 自动加成五个 9。

English

If Edge, App, and Cache each succeed 99.95% of the time, fail closed, and their failures do not overlap, a rough error rate is 0.05% × 3 = 0.15%. More tightly: 0.9995 × 0.9995 = 0.99900025, times 0.9995 is 0.99850075, about 99.85%. The unavailable fraction is about 0.001499. 0.001499 × 8,760 hours ≈ 13.1 hours/year, already more than the 8.76 hours of a single 99.9% target. 0.001499 × 200,000,000 ≈ 300,000 failed requests/day. The product only shows that a fail-closed serial chain is worse than its weakest hop.

The SRE workbook’s Implementing SLOs [5] rejects the next fantasy: a service at 99.9% in one zone, a second zone added to reach 99.95%, and a claim that independence turns two zones into 99.9999%. The workbook says this assumes the two are wholly independent, while shared dependencies, shared failure domains, and control planes almost always hit both. This chapter does not multiply 10 App boxes into more nines. 10 boxes mean the peak QPS has somewhere to go. They do not turn 99.9% into five nines.

常见错误：把定义说成已经达成的承诺Common mistake: speaking the definition as a promise already kept

中文

「我们做到五个 9」和「五个 9 的定义是一年大约 5.26 分钟」不是同一句。前者要有测量窗口、成功的定义、排除项和后果；本章一个都没有。面试里先说工作目标 99.9%，再把 8.76 小时和每天 20 万次失败算出来，然后指出 Cache 的 0.1% 已经把它花完。全站五个 9 会把每一跳的错误率都压到远小于 0.001%，成本曲线在 SRE 书里就写了不是线性的。feed 读可以用三个 9 当工作目标，写路径和支付不要自动继承同一个数。

English

“We run at five nines” and “the definition of five nines is about 5.26 minutes a year” are different sentences. The first needs a measurement window, a definition of success, exclusions, and a consequence. This chapter has none of those. In the interview, state a working target of 99.9%, compute 8.76 hours and 200,000 failures a day, then show that a 0.1% cache error has already spent it. Site-wide five nines pushes every hop far below a 0.001% error rate, and the SRE book already says that cost curve is not linear. A feed read can use three nines as a working target. The write path and payments do not automatically inherit the same number.

同一套方法再走一遍短链The same method, a second time, on a short link

中文

短链用 Ch09 已经公开的教学假设，不另起一套流量：每天创建 1,000,000 次，每次创建对应 100 次跳转，峰值倍数用那一章的 10 倍，而不是 feed 的 3 倍。记录按 0.5 KB = 500 字节，302 响应也按 0.5 KB。这些仍是假设。单机舒适 1,000 QPS、按 60% 使用得到 600，是本章的单机假设，Ch09 没有公布过实例数。下面每一笔都是同一套除法。

写请求/天 = 1,000,000。平均写 QPS = 1,000,000 / 86,400 = 11.57，取 11.6。峰值按 10 倍是 11.57 × 10 = 115.7，取 116 QPS。读请求/天 = 1,000,000 × 100 = 100,000,000。平均读 QPS = 100,000,000 / 86,400 = 1,157，取 1.16K。峰值读 = 1,157 × 10 = 11,570，取 11.6K。实例数按取整后的 11.6K 来除：11,600 / 600 = 19.33，未取整的 11,570 / 600 = 19.28，都向上到 20 台无状态跳转实例。116 / 20 = 5.8 次写/秒/台，不改变 N。和 feed 一样，读的峰值决定台数。20 台仍然是一小层，不是分片的理由；Ch09 把写路径的困难放在唯一和幂等上，和 116 QPS 这个量级一致。

带宽只算 302，不算目标页。11.6K × 0.5 KB = 11,600 × 500 字节 = 5,800,000 字节/秒 = 5.8 MB/s。×8 = 46.4 Mb/s。这条线是跳转服务回到浏览器的那一小段响应。若把目标页按 1 MB 算进短链出口，11,600 × 1 MB = 11.6 GB/s，×8 = 92.8 Gb/s。那是 feed 里「500 KB 图片穿过 App」的同一个错误：API 没有返回页面。页面字节在浏览器和目标站点之间，不在短链的 App 上。

存储用创建次数，不用跳转次数。一年行数 = 1,000,000 × 365 = 365,000,000。×500 字节 = 1.825 × 1011 字节 = 182.5 GB，约 183 GB 原始行。×3 副本 = 547.5 GB，约 0.55 TB。Ch09 把索引、放大和备份之后的规划区间写成大约 0.6–1 TB/年。0.55 TB 是原始行乘副本；0.6–1 TB 是在此之上留出来的规划范围，不是第三个测量值。跳转产生的访问日志、CDN 上的缓存，都不在这 183 GB 里。目标网页的图片更不在短链的盘上。

延迟和可用性沿用同一条链，不再换一套常数。命中缓存的跳转仍是一次接入往返加上机房内的 0.5 ms 数量级，10 ms 寻道不在命中路径上。若映射存储以失败关闭的方式放在跳转关键路径上，并且错误率是 0.1%，它会像 feed 的 Cache 一样吃掉一个 99.9% 的请求预算。所以读路径把存储留在未命中上，并且存储故障不能变成对用户可见的 0.1% 而无绕路或限流。这一遍没有新的公式，只是把 feed 上已经改过图的四笔，换成短链的假设再除一次。

English

The short link uses the teaching assumptions already published in Ch09, not a new traffic story: 1,000,000 creates per day, 100 redirects per create, and that chapter’s 10× peak rather than the feed’s 3×. The record is 0.5 KB = 500 bytes, and the 302 response is also 0.5 KB. Those are still assumptions. A comfortable 1,000 QPS per box, used at 60% to get 600, is this chapter’s per-box assumption. Ch09 does not publish an instance count. Each step below is the same division.

Writes/day = 1,000,000. Average write QPS = 1,000,000 / 86,400 = 11.57, taken as 11.6. A 10× peak is 11.57 × 10 = 115.7, taken as 116 QPS. Reads/day = 1,000,000 × 100 = 100,000,000. Average read QPS = 100,000,000 / 86,400 = 1,157, taken as 1.16K. Peak reads = 1,157 × 10 = 11,570, taken as 11.6K. Using the rounded 11.6K, instances = 11,600 / 600 = 19.33. The unrounded 11,570 / 600 = 19.28. Both round up to 20 stateless redirect instances. 116 / 20 = 5.8 writes/s/box, which does not change N. As with the feed, peak reads set the count. Twenty boxes are still a small tier, not a reason to shard. Ch09 puts the write-path difficulty on uniqueness and idempotency, which matches a 116 QPS order.

Bandwidth counts the 302, not the destination page. 11.6K × 0.5 KB = 11,600 × 500 bytes = 5,800,000 bytes/s = 5.8 MB/s. Times 8 is 46.4 Mb/s. That line is the small response from the redirect service back to the browser. Charging a 1 MB destination page to the shortener makes 11,600 × 1 MB = 11.6 GB/s, times 8 is 92.8 Gb/s. That is the same mistake as hauling the feed’s 500 KB images through App: the API does not return the page. Page bytes travel between the browser and the destination, not through the shortener’s App.

Storage uses creates, not redirects. Rows/year = 1,000,000 × 365 = 365,000,000. Times 500 bytes = 1.825 × 1011 bytes = 182.5 GB, about 183 GB of raw rows. Times 3 replicas = 547.5 GB, about 0.55 TB. Ch09’s planning range after indexes, amplification, and backups is about 0.6–1 TB/year. 0.55 TB is raw rows times replicas. 0.6–1 TB is the planning range reserved on top of that, not a third measurement. Redirect access logs and CDN cache are not inside the 183 GB. The destination page’s images are not on the shortener’s disks.

Latency and availability reuse the same chain. A cache-hit redirect is still one access round trip plus in-datacenter 0.5 ms orders of magnitude. The 10 ms seek is not on the hit path. If the mapping store sits fail-closed on the redirect critical path at a 0.1% error rate, it spends a 99.9% request budget the way the feed Cache did. The read path keeps the store on the miss, and a store failure cannot become a user-visible 0.1% without a bypass or a limit. This pass adds no new formula. It divides the short-link assumptions through the same four steps that already changed the feed drawings.

追问怎么接Follow-ups

1 · 「这个百万是 DAU 还是注册总量？」

只有 DAU 进 QPS。若 1000 万是注册总量，而日活是它的 10%，读请求变成 1,000,000 × 20 = 20,000,000/天，平均约 200 QPS，峰值 600，600 / 600 = 1 台量级。图从 10 台收成一两台。先问清单位，再除。

1 · “Is the million DAU or total signups?”

Only DAU enters QPS. If 10 million is total signups and daily actives are 10% of that, reads become 1,000,000 × 20 = 20,000,000/day, about 200 QPS average and 600 peak. 600 / 600 = 1 box of capacity. The drawing shrinks from 10 boxes to one or two. Ask the unit, then divide.

2 · 「单机能到 5,000 QPS 呢？」

可用 = 5,000 × 0.6 = 3,000。feed 峰值 6,000 / 3,000 = 2，再加一台给发布，画 3 台。短链 11,600 / 3,000 ≈ 3.9，向上 4 台。结果随单机假设变，除法不变。

2 · “What if one box does 5,000 QPS?”

Usable = 5,000 × 0.6 = 3,000. Feed peak 6,000 / 3,000 = 2, plus one box for deploys, so draw 3. The short link is 11,600 / 3,000 ≈ 3.9, which rounds up to 4. The result follows the per-box assumption. The division does not change.

3 · 「用户在另一个洲呢？」

把 20 ms 换成 Dean 表上的 150 ms，命中累计约 151 ms。11 TB 不变。变的是那个洲要有一套自己的命中路径（Edge、App、Cache），App 台数按各区域自己的峰值分别除。只把 TLS 终结点挪过去，取数据仍要跨洋。单机房承诺全球几十毫秒，和这 150 ms 对不上。

3 · “What if the user is on another continent?”

Replace 20 ms with the 150 ms on Dean’s table, and the hit cumulative is about 151 ms. The 11 TB stays. What changes is that the other continent needs its own hit path (Edge, App, Cache), and the App count is divided per region from that region’s peak. Moving only the TLS terminator there still sends the data across the ocean. A promise of global tens of milliseconds from one datacenter does not match that 150 ms.

4 · 「三年后呢？」

流量若每年翻倍，今天的峰值读 6,000 到第三年是 6,000 × 4 = 24,000，实例 24,000 / 600 = 40 台。存储若行数据每年翻倍，三年行数据 25.55 TB，×3 ≈ 77 TB。先说曲线是假设，再乘。不要只把今天的 10 台和 11 TB 乘 3。

4 · “What about three years out?”

If traffic doubles every year, today’s peak of 6,000 reads is 6,000 × 4 = 24,000 in the third year, and instances are 24,000 / 600 = 40. If rows double every year, three years of rows are 25.55 TB, times 3 ≈ 77 TB. Say the curve is an assumption, then multiply. Do not only multiply today’s 10 boxes and 11 TB by 3.

5 · 「99.99% 是承诺吗？」

不是。52.56 分钟是四个 9 的定义。工作目标要单独说，并说明没有合同后果就是 SLO。Cache 0.1% 对四个 9 是 10 倍预算，所以要么拿掉这条硬依赖，要么把目标改回三个 9。

5 · “Is 99.99% a promise?”

No. 52.56 minutes is the definition of four nines. A working target is stated separately, and without a contractual consequence it is an SLO. A 0.1% cache error is 10× a four-nines budget, so either remove that hard dependency or move the target back to three nines.

English

Feed board: 10M DAU × 20 / 105 = 2,000, times 3 is 6,000, divided by 600 is 10 boxes. API peak is 960 Mb/s. Images at 24 Gb/s go through the CDN, and at 90% hits origin sees 2.4 Gb/s. Metadata is 3.65 TB × 3 ≈ 11 TB, and images, the CDN, and logs are outside it. A hit is about 21 ms and a miss about 32 ms; at 95% hits Primary absorbs only 300 seeks/s, and the 150 ms intercontinental hop needs a hit path near the user. 99.9% allows 200,000 failures a day, a 0.1% cache error spends that, and the red line must not reach the user; a whole-cache outage needs a capped bypass. The short link uses the same division: 11.6K peak reads are about 20 boxes, the 302 is only 5.8 MB/s, and raw rows are about 183 GB.

When the next chapters build components, the rate-limit threshold comes from this peak. Ch04 asks who is rejected above 6,000. Ch09 turns the short link’s 11.6 and 11.6K into read and write paths. The machinery of availability, SLOs, and operating an error budget waits for the trade-off chapter, Ch36. Here the definition and one arithmetic step are enough.
