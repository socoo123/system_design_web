粗略估算Back-of-the-envelope estimates

约 30 分钟 · 前置：Ch01、Ch02~30 min · prereq: Ch01, Ch02

这一章结束你能做什么What you can do after this chapter

中文

  • 用 2–4 分钟在白板上走完：单位 → 从 DAU 到 QPS → peak → 存储 → 带宽。
  • 每个数取整、带单位，读写分开，peak 写在平均旁边。
  • 用数量级决定缓存、分片、要不要多 Region。数字是公开教学假设。

English

  • Spend 2–4 minutes on the board: units, DAU to QPS, peak, storage, bandwidth.
  • Round, label units, split read and write, and write peak next to the average.
  • Let the order of magnitude pick cache, sharding, and multi-Region. The numbers are public teaching assumptions.

这 2–4 分钟怎么开口How you open those 2–4 minutes

中文

Ch01 管时间盒，Ch02 管盒子怎么一个个加上去。这一章是 Step 1–2 里面那几分钟数字。多数人能画框，说不出是 1 千还是 10 万 QPS。gap 在这里。

我先做 back-of-the-envelope。单位先说死：一天按 10^5 秒，存储用 Byte，带宽用 bit。假设写在白板上：DAU、人均操作、read:write、peak 倍数。然后走 QPS、存储、带宽、机器，取整、标单位。peak 用平均 ×2–3。算完用一句话接上架构。

说完就写假设。面试官要的是推理。这几分钟夹在澄清和高层之间。本章把它拆开练，上场时压回这张表。

节拍你在做什么停的信号
30 秒开口，写下 4 个假设对方点头，或改一个数
1–2 分钟QPS，含 peak，读写两行平均和 peak 都在板上
1 分钟存储 × 副本；带宽看 B 和 b数量级落到 GB / TB
30 秒机器量级，加一句成本说清是两台还是一千台

纪律：86400 按 10^5 秒。每个数带单位。假设留在板上，后面回查。算到小数点后三位，就不是 back-of-the-envelope。收尾必须有一句把数字接到设计上，例如 read:write 大约 10:1，所以先缓存。

English

Ch01 is the clock. Ch02 is how the boxes show up one at a time. This chapter is the few minutes of numbers inside step 1–2. Most people can draw boxes. They cannot say whether it is 1K or 100K QPS. That is the gap.

I’ll start with back-of-the-envelope. Units first: a day is 10^5 seconds, storage is bytes, bandwidth is bits. Assumptions on the board: DAU, ops per user, read:write, peak multiplier. Then QPS, storage, bandwidth, and boxes. I round, and every number has a unit. Peak is average times 2 to 3. I close with one sentence that ties the numbers to the design.

Then write the assumptions. They want the reasoning. On a real prompt this slot sits between clarify and the high-level picture. This chapter unpacks it. In the room you compress back to this table.

BeatWhat you are doingStop when
30 secOpen, write the four assumptionsThey nod, or they change a number
1–2 minQPS, including peak, read and writeAverage and peak are both on the board
1 minStorage times replicas; bandwidth watches B vs bThe order lands on GB or TB
30 secMachine order, plus one cost lineYou said two boxes or a thousand

Discipline: treat 86400 as 10^5 seconds. Every number has a unit. Leave the assumptions on the board so you can look back. Three decimal places is not back-of-the-envelope. Close with one sentence that hooks the numbers to the design, such as read:write around 10:1, so cache first.

先约定单位Lock the units first

中文

估算是数量级。先把单位写上，再代公式。面试里 1 KB 就当 10^3 字节，1 MB 当 10^6，1 GB 当 10^9。和 1024 差大约 2%，改不了架构。TB 是 10^12，PB 是 10^15，数字跨过去再写。

一天精确是 86400 秒。口算一律除以 10^5。存储用 Byte（B），带宽用 bit/s（bps）。小写 b 是 bit，大写 B 是 Byte，差 8 倍。混在一起是经典 red flag。

English

An estimate is an order of magnitude. Write the units, then the formula. In the room, 1 KB is 10^3 bytes, 1 MB is 10^6, 1 GB is 10^9. The gap versus 1024 is about 2%. It does not change the design. TB is 10^12 and PB is 10^15. Write those when the number crosses them.

A day is exactly 86400 seconds. Mental math always divides by 10^5. Storage uses bytes (B). Bandwidth uses bits per second (bps). Lowercase b is a bit, uppercase B is a byte, and they differ by 8×. Mixing them is a classic red flag.

从 DAU 推 QPSFrom DAU to QPS

中文

先问再算。问 4 个就够开工，其余自己假设并写上。对方说「你来假设」，就写下这句然后动笔：1000 万 DAU、读多写少、peak ×3、先单 Region。错了对方会改。

你问为什么写到板上的默认
百万是 DAU、MAU，还是注册总量？只有 DAU 进 QPS10M DAU
每人每天几次读、几次写？没有人均就没有 QPS10 次读，1 次写
read:write 大概多少？直接改架构重心先按 10:1
peak 倍数？有没有大促？漏 peak 是 red flag日常 ×2–3，大促 ×10

单条多大、留多久、用户跨不跨洲，有空再补：文本 1 KB，媒体另算，留 1 年，先单 Region。禁止编「某厂线上 37 万 QPS」。下面整组幻灯片用这一套公开教学假设，一口算到底。

公式先写再代：平均 QPS = DAU × 人均操作 / 10^5。读和写分成两行。

English

Ask, then calculate. Four questions are enough to start. Assume the rest and write it down. If they say “you assume,” write this and move: 10M DAU, read-heavy, peak ×3, one Region first. If it is wrong, they will correct it.

You askWhyDefault on the board
Is “a million” DAU, MAU, or signups?Only DAU enters QPS10M DAU
Reads and writes per user per day?No per-user ops, no QPS10 reads, 1 write
Rough read:write?It moves the architectureStart at 10:1
Peak multiplier? Any promo?A missed peak is a red flagDaily ×2–3, promo ×10

Record size, retention, and cross-continent users come next if you have room: text 1 KB, media separate, keep 1 year, one Region first. Do not invent “company X runs 370K QPS in prod.” The slides use this one public teaching assumption and carry it all the way through.

Write the formula, then plug in: average QPS = DAU × ops per user / 10^5. Read and write go on two lines.

再带上 peakPut peak on the board

中文

平均 QPS 回答「平常能不能活」。peak 决定余量和要不要削峰。早晚高峰用平均 ×2–3。大促、晚会用平均 ×10。×10 是削峰和降级的理由，把全年机器都按 ×10 买齐，是成本上的 red flag。

只报平均，等对方问「高峰呢」再补，也是 red flag。wrap-up 主动说：高峰按 ×3 留余量；再高就限流和降级。

存储用平均写 QPS，因为一天的字节是写请求积出来的。带宽和机器用 peak，因为管道和连接数要扛得住那一下。

English

Average QPS answers “can we live on a normal day.” Peak decides headroom, and whether you shed load. Morning and evening are average ×2–3. A promo or a live event is average ×10. ×10 is why you queue and degrade. Buying every box at ×10 for the whole year is a cost red flag.

Quoting the average and patching peak only when they ask is a red flag too. In the wrap-up, say it yourself: peak headroom is ×3; past that, rate-limit and degrade.

Storage uses average write QPS, because the bytes in a day are the integral of writes. Bandwidth and boxes use peak, because the pipe and the connection pool have to survive the spike.

限流阈值从这组 peak 来，下一章是 Ch04 · 限流器。削峰和降级在 Ch42 · 弹性。The rate-limit threshold comes from this peak. Next chapter is Ch04 · rate limiter. Shedding load lives in Ch42 · resilience.

存储Storage

中文

日增量 = 平均写 QPS × 10^5 秒 × 单条大小。总量 = 日增量 × 天数 × 副本。副本先 ×3：多 AZ 各留一份，备份也别漏。对方问三年后，再乘一个增长系数，别只报今天。

媒体和元数据分开。1 MB 的图走对象存储，写进行存里会把 TB 估爆。热、温、冷分层点到后面的存储章，这里只把文本行算清。

English

Daily growth = average write QPS × 10^5 seconds × record size. Total = daily growth × days × replicas. Replicas start at ×3: a copy per AZ, and do not forget backups. If they ask about three years from now, multiply a growth factor. Do not quote today alone.

Media and metadata stay apart. A 1 MB image belongs in object storage. Stuffing it into the row blows the TB number. Hot, warm, and cold tiers wait for the storage chapter. Here you size the text row.

介质和行存、对象存储怎么选，在 Ch37 · 存储选型。热温冷分层在 Ch43 · 分层。How you pick the medium, the row store, and object storage is Ch37 · storage choices. Hot, warm, and cold tiers are Ch43 · tiers.

带宽和机器Bandwidth and boxes

中文

bps = peak QPS × 单次 Byte × 8。上行和下行分开。文本读路径常常很小；把媒体放进同一次读，经常会得到「要 CDN」。这就是估算的用处。

机器 ≈ max(QPS / 单机 QPS, 数据 / 单机盘) × 1.5–2。单机能力用公开数量级：无状态 web 大约万级 QPS 一台，缓存实例到十万级，NVMe 上的单库读比 HDD 年代的直觉高一个数量级。×1.5–2 是 failover，不是把数字做大。

成本说到「每月小几千美金，还是小几十万」就停。报内部折扣，或背一串云 SKU，都不是这一章。

读 QPS 的手感，当校准，不当 SLA：大约 1 万，单机加 NVMe 往往够，上分布式要讲理由；大约 10 万，要 LB 和多机，read:write 决定要不要缓存；大约 100 万，缓存、CDN、分片进高层图；大约 1000 万，先找 bottleneck，再谈加机器。

English

bps = peak QPS × bytes per request × 8. Split ingress and egress. A text read path is often small. Put media on that same read and you often land on “this needs a CDN.” That is the point of the estimate.

Boxes ≈ max(QPS / per-box QPS, bytes / per-box disk) × 1.5–2. Per-box capacity is a public order of magnitude: a stateless web box is tens of thousands of QPS, a cache node is hundreds of thousands, and a single DB on NVMe reads an order of magnitude above HDD-era intuition. The ×1.5–2 is failover, not a bigger number for its own sake.

Stop the cost line at “a few thousand USD a month, or a few hundred thousand.” Internal discounts and a cloud SKU list are not this chapter.

Read-QPS feel, for calibration, not as an SLA: around 10K, one box plus NVMe often holds, and going distributed needs a reason; around 100K, an LB and several boxes, and read:write decides cache; around 1M, cache, CDN, and sharding enter the high-level picture; around 10M, name the bottleneck before you add machines.

缓存和 CDN 的机制在 Ch38 · 缓存。1 MB 在 100 Gbps 上的发送时间大约 0.08 ms，别把它和 RTT 加成一个数。Cache and CDN mechanisms are Ch38 · cache. Send time for 1 MB on 100 Gbps is about 0.08 ms. Keep that separate from RTT.

同一套步骤，再走一个短链Same steps, one short-URL pass

中文

换题只换假设，不换顺序。下面都是公开教学假设。短链的完整设计在 Ch09，这里只口算数量级。

English

A new prompt changes the assumptions, not the order. All of these are public teaching assumptions. The full short-URL design is Ch09. Here you only size it.

PromptAssumptionOrder of magnitudeWhat you say next

Divide the short URL by 86400 and you get about 600 writes, 5,200 reads, and about 280 TB with ×3 over 10 years. Same order as the 10^5 row. A feed at 10M DAU, 2 posts and 20 refreshes, is about 200 write QPS and 2,000 reads, peak read about 6,000: cache belongs in the picture, sharding does not yet. If one refresh then loads 20 items, that amplifies downstream. At estimate time, get request QPS right first.

Writes far above reads (logs, IoT) go to a queue and batched flush. Reads and writes close together (chat, collab) do not end at “add a cache.” Say consistency on its own. The book’s 2010 social-product figures are teaching assumptions in the same way.

延迟表和几个 9Latency bands and the nines

中文

数字决定要不要分片之后，面试官会问「那延迟你用多少」。背 2026 的五档，别背成某厂 SLO。锚点来自 Jeff Dean / Norvig 那张表的现代修订，加上云上跨 AZ、跨 Region 的公开 RTT 量级。

锚点量级你怎么用
DRAM~100 ns所有比较的基准
NVMe 随机读 4 KB~100 μs（区间 10–100 μs）比内存慢约 1000×
同城 RTT~0.5 ms服务间调用按这个
跨 AZ RTT~1–2 ms同步复制会进写延迟
跨洲 RTT~150 ms（大约 80–200）光速地板，CDN / 多 Region

三句就够。缓存比 NVMe 上的库大约快 1000×，缓存是为了热点、跨洲 RTT、卸掉查询，HDD 寻道不是 2026 的第一理由。同城 10 次串行 RPC 大约 5 ms，该并行或合并。跨洲 150 ms 改不了，不要承诺单 Region 全球 50 ms。对象存储首字节常常几十毫秒，和 NVMe 不是一类延迟。

几个 9 是一年允许多少停机。必背 3、4、5。从 3 个 9 到 4 个 9，停机少 10 倍，钱不是少 10 倍，是多 AZ、自动 failover、演练。全站 5 个 9 是 over-engineering。核心交易可以谈 4 个 9，边缘功能 3 个 9。RPO / RTO 一句带过：社交可以秒级丢失、分钟级恢复，支付更严。机制留给后面的 trade-off 章。

可用性一年停机什么时候说
99%（2 个 9）~3.7 天内部工具
99.9%（3 个 9）~8.8 小时常见底线
99.99%（4 个 9）~53 分钟核心业务
99.999%（5 个 9）~5 分钟金融、电信；成本再上一档

English

Once the numbers have decided sharding, they ask what latency you are using. Memorize five 2026 bands. They are not some company’s SLO. The anchors are a modern revision of the Jeff Dean / Norvig list, plus public cross-AZ and cross-Region RTT.

AnchorOrderHow you use it
DRAM~100 nsBaseline for every comparison
NVMe random read, 4 KB~100 μs (range 10–100 μs)About 1000× slower than memory
Same-city RTT~0.5 msService-to-service calls
Cross-AZ RTT~1–2 msSync replication lands on the write
Cross-continent RTT~150 ms (about 80–200)Speed-of-light floor; CDN or multi-Region

Three sentences are enough. Cache is about 1000× faster than a DB on NVMe. You want it for hot keys, cross-continent RTT, and offloading the query. HDD seek is not the 2026 reason. Ten serial RPCs in the same city are about 5 ms, so parallelize or batch. Cross-continent 150 ms does not move. Do not promise 50 ms worldwide from one Region. Object-storage first byte is often tens of milliseconds. That is not the same class as NVMe.

A few nines means how much downtime you allow in a year. Memorize 3, 4, and 5. From 3 nines to 4, downtime drops 10×. The money does not drop 10×. You are paying for multi-AZ, automatic failover, and drills. Site-wide 5 nines is over-engineering. Core payments can talk 4 nines. An edge feature can live at 3. One line on RPO / RTO: social can lose seconds and recover in minutes; payments are stricter. The mechanism waits for the trade-off chapter.

AvailabilityDowntime / yearWhen you say it
99% (2 nines)~3.7 daysInternal tools
99.9% (3 nines)~8.8 hoursA common floor
99.99% (4 nines)~53 minutesCore product
99.999% (5 nines)~5 minutesFinance and telco; cost steps up

追问时怎么接How you take the follow-ups

中文

  1. 磁盘延迟用多少？先问介质。NVMe ~100 μs。HDD ~10 ms 放在折叠里，不当第一答案。
  2. 内存比 NVMe 快多少？~100 ns 对 ~100 μs，约 1000×。HDD 年代那句「10 万倍」已经过时。
  3. 同城和跨洲？0.5 ms 对 ~150 ms，约 300×。
  4. 100 Mbps 是多少 MB/s？12.5。B 和 b。
  5. 三年后呢？乘增长。存储和分片提前说，别只报今天的 12 TB。
  6. 这是某厂真实 QPS 吗？不是。白板上的都是教学假设。编内部数字更糟。
2010 的盘，现在放哪

原书把 HDD 寻道 ~10 ms、同机房 0.5 ms、跨大陆 150 ms 当主记忆。现在正文用 DRAM / NVMe / 同城 / 跨 AZ / 跨洲。跨洲 150 ms 仍然成立，变了的是盘。HDD 只当冷归档。原书常只估 QPS 和存储；现在补 peak、B 和 b、机器量级、成本量级。估完主动说 read:write，再选缓存、队列或分片。原书那组社交产品的月活和 100:1，是教学假设，用来论证媒体带宽和 CDN。

English

  1. What disk latency are you using? Ask the medium. NVMe is ~100 μs. HDD at ~10 ms stays in the fold. It is not the first answer.
  2. How much faster is memory than NVMe? ~100 ns versus ~100 μs, about 1000×. The HDD-era line “100,000×” is outdated.
  3. Same-city versus cross-continent? 0.5 ms versus ~150 ms, about 300×.
  4. 100 Mbps is how many MB/s? 12.5. B versus b.
  5. Three years from now? Multiply growth. Say storage and sharding early. Do not stop at today’s 12 TB.
  6. Is this some company’s real QPS? No. Everything on the board is a teaching assumption. Inventing an internal number is worse.
Where the 2010 disk goes now

The book memorized HDD seek at ~10 ms, same-datacenter 0.5 ms, and cross-continent 150 ms as the main list. The body now uses DRAM / NVMe / same-city / cross-AZ / cross-continent. Cross-continent 150 ms still holds. What changed is the disk. HDD is cold archive. The book often stopped at QPS and storage. You now add peak, B versus b, machine order, and cost order. After QPS, say read:write, then pick cache, a queue, or sharding. The book’s social-product MAU and 100:1 are a teaching assumption, used to argue media bandwidth and a CDN.
