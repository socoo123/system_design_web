本章要点：分清副本、写入归属与提交保证Core: copies, ownership and commit

  • 复制保存副本：副本收到日志（receive）、持久保存日志（flush）、应用日志（apply），以及新快照能读到变更，是不同的阶段。先说明成功回执承诺完成了哪一步。
  • 分片分配归属：根据查询方式和必须共同成立的业务约束选键。切换流量前，要先隔离旧写者，再证明新写者已经拥有完整历史。
  • 事务约束共同提交：本地条件更新负责守住业务约束。跨库的两阶段提交（2PC）、Outbox 中的投递意图，以及 Saga 的业务恢复，各自提供不同的保证。
  • Replication owns copies: receipt, flush, apply and visibility in a new snapshot differ. Define the success acknowledgment first.
  • Sharding owns placement: choose by queries and invariants. Fence the old writer before proving the new writer has the complete history.
  • Transactions own atomic change: conditional updates guard local invariants. Separate the shared 2PC decision, Outbox intent and Saga recovery.

30 秒开场：先问成功回执承诺什么Opening: define the success contract

先把三件事分开：复制为同一份数据保存多个副本，分片为不同数据确定唯一的写入归属，事务让相关变更共同成功或失败。普通列表可以允许读到副本上的旧值；提交请求收到成功确认时，则要问清楚：数据只在主库持久保存，还是已在远端持久保存，又是否已经可读？超过单机能力后，尽量把订单和订单项放在同一片；需要跨库共同提交时再评估 2PC，跨服务的长流程则用 Outbox 和持久化的 Saga 状态来恢复。

设计前先问：已经确认成功的写入，最多允许丢失多少？读取最多允许滞后多久？要应对的是一台机器故障，还是整个地域失效？哪些查询会带租户标识 tenant_id，哪些扣减和唯一性约束必须一起成立，外部服务能否凭稳定的操作键查回原回执？本章默认单地域，每个键范围只有一个写者；所有数字都是教学假设。

Replication keeps copies of the same data; sharding assigns different data to a unique writer; transactions make related changes succeed together. Lists may tolerate stale replicas, while confirmation distinguishes local durability, remote durability and visibility. After exceeding one node, co-locate Order and Items. Evaluate 2PC across controlled databases, and recover cross-service workflows with Outbox and persistent Sagas.

Ask first: how much acknowledged data may be lost, how stale may reads be, and is the failure domain a machine or a region? Which queries carry tenant_id, which invariants must commit together, and can external services retrieve stable operation receipts? Assume one region and one writer per range. Every numeric example is a teaching assumption.

41.1 · 复制：先分清收到、持久与可见41.1 · Replication: distinguish acknowledgments

正常路径：主库处理写请求，各副本独立接收日志Normal path: primary writes, independent log streams

中文

客户端经 App 向 Primary 提交一次业务事务，结果再沿 Primary→App→Client 返回。主库把预写日志（WAL）分别发送给 Replica A 和 B，两个副本各自接收，不经过 A→B 的接力链。本地处理与日志复制可以重叠；服务何时返回结果，取决于采用的确认策略。日志位点描述变更的顺序，业务幂等键标识同一次操作，两者不能互相替代。本章的“异步”只是不等待远端副本，仍要求本地 WAL 持久保存，不能理解成 PostgreSQL 的 synchronous_commit=off。[1] 因为回执不等副本，所以主库及其本地持久介质一起永久丢失时，尚未发出的已确认写入可能没有任何可恢复副本。[2]

判断“副本已有数据”，要分清三个阶段：receive 是收到字节，flush 是日志已进入可恢复的持久介质，apply 是按日志更新了数据。如果日志已落盘但尚未应用，切主时可以通过回放恢复，普通查询却暂时看不到这些变更。PostgreSQL 17 的 remote_write 不能保证日志在副本操作系统故障后仍然持久；配置了同步 standby 时，on 等待远端持久保存，remote_apply 还会等待应用完成。若没有配置同步 standby，单独设置这些选项也不会获得远端保障。[1]

English

Client→App→Primary commits once and returns Primary→App→Client. Primary independently streams WAL to A/B. Log position orders changes; an idempotency key identifies an operation. Here async means replication without a remote wait, with local durable WAL still required; it does not mean PostgreSQL synchronous_commit=off.[1] Async replication can lose unshipped writes if the primary and its durable media are permanently lost. [2]

Receive, flush and apply are different. Durable but unapplied logs can recover during promotion while remaining invisible to reads. In PostgreSQL 17, remote_write does not ensure durability against standby OS failure; on waits for remote durability when synchronous standbys are configured, and remote_apply additionally waits for application. The setting alone does not create a synchronous standby. [1]

为什么要等待：同时约定耐久性与故障时的可用性Why wait: durability and availability are coupled

中文

A2 为便于推导，采用串行过程：主库处理并持久保存用 4 ms，随后等待副本的 6 ms 往返时间（RTT）和 2 ms 落盘时间，合计 12 ms。若远端 RTT 为 80 ms，预算就变为 4+80+2=86 ms。真实实现可能让部分落盘与网络传输重叠，因此不能把这个加法结果当成数据库的 p99；还需在相同事务体量、并发量和故障域下测量。这组数字只用于解释，同城同步和跨洲异步为什么可能成为不同的设计选择。

本章的强耐久约定固定等待 Replica A。A 不可用时，服务不能确认成功；必要时返回不可用，或保留结果未知，再查询幂等记录。若改成“任意一个副本确认即可”，提升协议就必须找到包含所有已确认写入的可恢复日志前缀，不能任意选择 B。MySQL 8.4 的半同步复制等待副本 relay log 落盘后返回 ACK，并不等待副本执行；默认等待点以及超时后退回异步的行为，也都有具体的配置语义。[4] 因此，仅仅“开启半同步”还不能证明端到端零丢失。

English

A2 deliberately uses a serial budget: 4 ms local processing/durability plus 6 ms replica RTT and 2 ms flush gives 12 ms. An 80 ms distant RTT gives 86 ms. Real implementations overlap work and require measurements for matching transaction size and concurrency; these are not p99 figures.

Our strong-durability contract waits specifically for A and refuses successful acknowledgment when A is unavailable, resolving uncertain results through idempotency records. An any-replica policy requires promotion to recover every acknowledged write. MySQL 8.4 semisync acknowledges a flushed relay log without waiting for execution, and its wait-point and timeout-to-async behavior matter. [4]

故障窗口：既要看复制进度，也要看日志能保留多久Failure window: retain recovery logs

中文

监控至少要分别记录主库已生成、副本已持久保存、副本已应用的三个位点，同时观察缺口有多少字节、增长有多快。应用延迟（apply lag）用于判断副本读会有多旧，持久日志缺口用于分析灾难时可能丢失多少写入。一张“延迟 2 秒”的仪表盘无法替代这两个判断。系统空闲时，位点本来就可能不变，也不能只凭最后更新时间判断副本故障。

复制槽（Replication slot）保留日志后，落后副本可以继续追赶，但保留压力也会留在 Primary。PostgreSQL 文档提醒，保留的 WAL 可能填满磁盘。[2] 因此，要对日志保留量和磁盘余量设置告警；副本超出可恢复窗口时，将其移出读池并重建，不能靠“永不清理日志”换取可靠性。三个副本也不能代替备份：误删可能被复制到所有在线副本，恢复历史数据仍需要独立备份和相应的日志回放窗口。

English

Track generated, durable and applied positions plus gap bytes/growth. Apply lag diagnoses stale reads; durable gaps diagnose catastrophic loss. Idle positions alone do not establish failure.

Slots retain logs for catch-up but may fill the primary’s disk. [2] Alert on retention/headroom and rebuild replicas outside the recovery window rather than retaining forever. Replication is not backup: accidental deletion can propagate to every live replica.

41.2 · 刚写就读：提交凭据、日志历史与新快照41.2 · Read-your-writes: token, history and fresh snapshots

确认日志已应用后，在同一服务器打开新快照Bind the apply barrier and snapshot to one destination

图 2 要证明的是，这次查询包含了刚才的写入。服务签发 token 并验证其身份，凭据同时绑定集群、日志历史和提交位点；T:1042 只是图中的缩写。服务先在选定副本上确认同一历史的连续应用前缀，再在同一服务器打开新快照并查询。如果连接池换库、发生切主或连接失效，就要重新检查。接收位点、落盘位点，以及乱序日志中的最大序号，都无法证明查询快照已经包含所需的连续前缀。

在旧的 Repeatable Read 事务中等待日志追平，再执行 SELECT，仍然读的是旧快照。[7] 下面的 Java 17 代码只判定读取资格和路由。可信引擎适配层要取得一个与有效读连接及本次查询快照绑定的视图，对应 FreshView；视图中的 visiblePrefix 表示该快照实际包含的连续前缀，不能直接填写监控上的当前回放位点。这不是 PostgreSQL 原生 API，代码也没有实现等待、网络调用或复制。

Deck 2 asks whether this write is included, rather than whether a number is large enough. The service authenticates a token bound to cluster, history and commit position; T:1042 is shorthand. Verify the continuous applied prefix on the chosen server, then open a fresh snapshot and query that same server. Recheck after pool reassignment, promotion or connection loss. Receive/flush positions and a maximum out-of-order sequence are insufficient.

Waiting for replay inside an existing Repeatable Read transaction does not refresh its snapshot.[7] This Java17 code only checks eligibility and routing. A trusted engine adapter supplies FreshView, bound to a still-valid connection and the query snapshot. Its visiblePrefix is what that snapshot actually includes, not a current replay metric. This is neither a native PostgreSQL API nor an implementation of waiting, networking or replication.

import java.util.Objects;

/** Eligibility only. FreshView is a trusted, pinned engine snapshot. */
public final class ReadGate {
    public record Token(String cluster, String history, long commitPosition) {
        public Token {
            Objects.requireNonNull(cluster); Objects.requireNonNull(history);
            if (commitPosition < 0) throw new IllegalArgumentException("position");
        }
    }
    public record FreshView(String cluster, String history,
                            long visiblePrefix, boolean newlyOpened) {}
    public enum Route { REPLICA, WAIT, PRIMARY, UNAVAILABLE }

    public static boolean covers(Token token, FreshView view) {
        return view != null && view.newlyOpened()
            && token.cluster().equals(view.cluster())
            && token.history().equals(view.history())
            && view.visiblePrefix() >= token.commitPosition();
    }

    public static Route choose(Token token, FreshView replica, FreshView primary,
                               boolean waitBudgetLeft, boolean primaryAdmitted) {
        Objects.requireNonNull(token);
        if (covers(token, replica)) return Route.REPLICA;
        if (waitBudgetLeft) return Route.WAIT;
        if (primaryAdmitted && covers(token, primary)) return Route.PRIMARY;
        return Route.UNAVAILABLE;
    }
}

A3 给副本最多 20 ms 的等待时间，转到主库仍要获准。返回 WAIT 时，适配层在剩余预算内重新取得新视图；返回 PRIMARY 时，主库必须仍然包含 token 对应的提交；返回 UNAVAILABLE 时，不能拿旧行当成功结果。代码保守地只接受相同的 history。若要支持继承历史，适配层还要验证分叉点和所需前缀的包含关系，不能直接比较不同 timeline 上的 LSN。同源示例检查了正常追平、旧快照、不同历史上的更大位点，以及转主库未获准的情况。

20 ms 只是等待副本的预算，不是整个 API 的 SLO，而且仍受请求截止时间约束。大量请求等待超时后一起转主库，还需要限流。更简单的方案是直接读主库并开新快照，但同样要确认它是合法写者，且所需提交没有丢失。“读到自己的写入”只保护本次会话已知的写入，不能自动提供全局线性一致性，也不能证明另一个服务的投影已经更新。

A3 waits at most 20 ms, then still requires primary admission. WAIT asks the adapter for a new view within the remaining budget. PRIMARY requires inclusion of the token; UNAVAILABLE must not serve stale success. The code conservatively accepts equal histories only. Accepting an inherited history requires validated fork points and prefix inclusion, never bare cross-timeline LSN comparison. The same-source example checks catch-up, old snapshots, larger fork positions and denied fallback.

The 20 ms wait is not the API SLO and remains bounded by its deadline. Rate-limit fallback surges. A fresh primary read is simpler but still depends on legitimate ownership and preservation of the requested commit. Read-your-writes is not global linearizability or proof that another service has updated its projection.

41.3 · 故障切换：先隔离旧主，再开放新路由41.3 · Failover: fence before reopening routes

正常切换：每次推进状态都要有证据Normal transition: evidence for every state

中文

第三组图展示的是四个控制状态：SUSPECT→FENCED→RECOVERED→OPEN。探测失败可能由网络分区引起，此时旧 Primary 仍可能接受部分客户端的写入。控制器进入怀疑故障的 SUSPECT 状态后，先暂停新的写路由，通过可靠机制撤销旧写者的存储写权或将其断电。只有取得写入隔离（fencing）证明，才能开始提升副本。PostgreSQL 官方故障切换文档同样要求避免旧主恢复后与新主同时充当 Primary。[3]

进入 FENCED 后，控制器选择可恢复的副本，回放其持久日志，并验证其中确实包含强耐久约定承诺的日志前缀。然后才将它提升为新 Primary，发布更高的路由代次（route epoch），让客户端重连。如果独立数据库仍允许旧连接提交，App 仅仅在 SQL 中附带 epoch 并不能隔离旧主；实际的写入权威必须校验这个代次，或通过硬隔离阻止旧主写入。本章状态机列出这些约束，不假设普通主从复制会自动实现它们。

English

This deck models control states, not four machines: SUSPECT→FENCED→RECOVERED→OPEN. A partitioned Primary may still accept writes. Pause new routes and obtain real storage write-exclusion or power fencing before promotion; PostgreSQL’s failover documentation also warns against simultaneous primaries. [3]

Recover and replay an eligible candidate containing the required acknowledged prefix, promote it, then publish a higher route epoch and reconnect clients. An application SQL parameter cannot fence an independent DB that still permits old connections. The actual writer must enforce authority or be physically isolated. The state machine specifies requirements, not built-in automatic guarantees.

估算丢写量时，分清持久日志缺口与恢复时间Sizing loss: separate durable gaps and recovery time

中文

A4 假设，主库已确认而候选副本无法恢复的日志，恰好对应最后 2 秒；写入速率稳定为每秒 1,000 次。因此，面临风险的写入约为 2×1,000=2,000 次。如果只是日志应用落后了 2 秒，而对应日志已在候选副本上持久保存，就可以回放恢复，不能据此认定会丢失 2,000 次写入。按时间估算也不是精确保证：写入可能突发，精确对账需要定位到具体事务或业务 ID 集合。

恢复点目标（RPO）约定最多能丢多少数据；恢复时间目标（RTO）约定多久恢复服务。后者还包括探测、隔离、回放、路由发布、客户端建连和失败重试，不能只算 promote 命令的耗时。同步复制可以减少约定故障模型内的丢写风险，但安全隔离与日志回放仍可能延长恢复。这些具体步骤，正是 Ch36 中可用性与一致性取舍的实际体现。

English

Under A4, the unrecoverable acknowledged gap corresponds to 2 s at steady 1,000/s: about 2,000 writes at risk. A 2 s apply lag backed by durable logs can replay and is not that loss. Bursts invalidate steady-rate precision; reconcile transaction/business IDs.

RPO measures acceptable loss; RTO includes detection, fencing, replay, publication, reconnect and retries. Promotion duration alone is not end-to-end recovery. Sync durability may still require lengthy safe recovery, concretizing Ch36’s consistency/availability choices.

中文

41.5 · 重分片：拷贝完成后，还要证明可以切流41.5 · Resharding: a full copy is not cutover proof

写端归属检查与冻结操作，必须接受同一套串行仲裁The write guard and fence share one serialization domain

# Teaching contract; range guards are enforced by the write authority.
write(range, revision, operation):
    BEGIN
    guard = lockOwnershipRowSharedUntilCommit(range)
    require guard.owner == thisShard
        and guard.state == ACTIVE and guard.revision == revision
    applyBusinessRowsAndIdempotency(operation)  # same local transaction
    COMMIT                                    # release ownership lock

cutover(moveId, range, expectedRevision = 7):
    # Exclusive ownership lock waits for old write transactions, incl. PREPARED.
    # Commit FROZEN durably; every later old-route writer must reject.
    fence = source.freezeAndDrain(range, moveId, expectedRevision)
    H = fence.finalCommittedPosition
    proof = target.finishCopyAndReplay(range, moveId, fence.history, H)
    require proof.copyComplete and proof.includesDeletes
        and proof.history == fence.history and proof.appliedPrefix >= H
    # Durable linearizable CAS validates the bound fence/proof and old owner.
    grant = directory.publishIfOwnerIs(range, source, expectedRevision,
                                      target, moveId, fence, proof)
    target.activate(grant)  # persist/verify exact range, owner and new revision
    return directory.lookup(range, moveId)  # recover a lost publication reply

每次业务写入都要锁住范围归属行，直到事务提交才释放；对应的操作是 lockOwnershipRowSharedUntilCommit，它在写入权威数据库中取得共享行锁。冻结操作 freeze 则对同一行取得排他锁，等待在途写者完成，再持久提交 FROZEN 状态。这样，旧事务要么先提交并计入最终位点 H，要么在冻结后被拒绝，不会留下“App 先查 rev，隔很久再提交”的空隙。未决的 2PC 事务也必须解决后，才能证明在途写入已排空。引擎若无法约束旧连接，就要撤销写权限或硬隔离；进程内互斥锁、DNS 和 Router 缓存都无法替代这个保证。

目标的完成证明（proof）必须绑定键范围 range、迁移编号 moveId、源日志历史 source history、最终位点 H 和拷贝完成状态，并证明连续应用前缀已经覆盖 H。目录通过持久、线性化的 CAS 校验旧 owner/rev 及这些证据，只签发一次新授权；Target 的写端再校验准确的 owner/rev，并持久保存激活状态。目录发布后、目标激活前，可以暂时拒绝请求。冻结、证明、发布和激活各阶段，都要按同一 moveId 持久保存状态并支持幂等恢复；重启先查阶段状态，发布回执丢失就查原目录，不能重新开放源写者。切流后若要回退，必须反向追平、隔离 Target，再给 Source 新代次，不能重新启用旧 rev=7。

lockOwnershipRowSharedUntilCommit is a shared ownership-row lock in the write authority, held through business commit. Freeze exclusively locks that same row, drains writers and durably commits FROZEN. Each old transaction either commits before H or is rejected after FROZEN; an App precheck followed by a later commit leaves an unsafe gap. Resolve in-doubt 2PC before proving drain. Engines unable to constrain old connections need write-authority revocation or hard isolation; process mutexes, DNS and router caches are insufficient.

Bind target proof to range, moveId, source history, H and complete copy; H must be an applied continuous prefix. A durable linearizable directory CAS validates the old owner/revision and evidence, issuing one new grant. Target verifies the exact owner/revision and persists activation. Requests may temporarily reject between publication and activation. Freeze, proof, publication and activation each persist idempotent stage state under the same moveId. Recover by querying that state and the original directory, without reviving Source. Rollback after new writes needs reverse replay, Target fencing and a new Source generation, never reactivation of rev 7.

Deck 6 separates the upper local transaction from lower 2PC. A one-item DRAFT order already exists. Submission carries only order_id and expectedVersion; tenant/caller authorization is trusted. submit(tenant, caller, key, orderId, expectedVersion):
    request = canonical(tenant, orderId, expectedVersion)
    BEGIN IMMEDIATE  # acquire this DB's writer before the result lookup
    saved = SELECT result WHERE (tenant, caller, key)
    if saved exists:
        require saved.request == request  # otherwise KEY_CONFLICT
        COMMIT
        return saved.response
    order = conditionalSubmit(orderId, tenant, expectedVersion)  # SQL below
    if no order: ROLLBACK; return STATE_CONFLICT
    if conditionalTakeStock(order.sku, order.qty) changed != 1:
        ROLLBACK; return SOLD_OUT
    INSERT outbox(stableEventId(tenant, orderId, order.version), payload)
    INSERT result(tenant, caller, key, request, responseSnapshot(order))
    COMMIT
    return responseSnapshot(order)

2PC 的 YES 不是共同提交结果A 2PC YES is not the shared commit result

图 6 下方，Coordinator 分别向 DB A/B 发送 prepare。每个参与者返回 YES 前，都要持久保存可恢复的 prepared 状态并继续持锁。收到全部 YES 后，协调者先持久保存 COMMIT 决策，再进入第二阶段通知双方；任一参与者回答 NO，就可以决定 ABORT。第二阶段通知的 ACK 丢失时，应沿用原 transaction_id 重发原决定，不能另开一场事务。

参与者已经回答 YES，却无法取得可信决策时，就处于 in-doubt。它不能超时后自行 ABORT，因为另一方可能已经按持久 COMMIT 决策提交。恢复后的协调者或决策日志，必须能按原 transaction_id 查回结论。PostgreSQL 17 的 prepared 状态在连接结束后仍保存在磁盘上，并持续持锁；长期不解决还会妨碍 VACUUM。[8] 因此，需要持久决策、恢复扫描、告警和明确的处理负责人。用户请求超时不决定事务结论。

Coordinator independently prepares DB A/B. Each YES means durable recoverable prepared state with locks retained. After all YES votes, persist the COMMIT decision before notifying participants. Any NO permits ABORT. Retry a lost second-phase acknowledgment with the original transaction_id and decision.

A prepared participant without a trustworthy decision is in-doubt, not free to abort on timeout: another participant may already have committed. Recover the original decision from the coordinator/log. PostgreSQL17 prepared state survives session termination, retains locks and can obstruct VACUUM.[8] Durable decisions, recovery scans, alerts and an owner are required; user timeouts do not decide transaction outcomes.

2PC 负责让各参与者遵循共同决策，本身不提供串行隔离；跨片事务还要依赖引擎的锁、时间戳或冲突控制。设计时可以评估受控的数据库参与者，也可以调整聚合边界，让必须共同成立的业务约束留在同一片。PSP、短信和发货通常不参加这里的 prepare/commit 协议。Outbox 持久保存待投递的意图，Saga 恢复已经提交的步骤，但这两种机制都没有把外部服务纳入本次本地 COMMIT。

2PC coordinates a decision; cross-shard serial isolation still needs the engine’s locks, timestamps or conflict control. Evaluate controlled participants or redesign locality. PSPs, messaging and shipping generally do not join your prepare/commit protocol. Outbox persists delivery intent; Saga recovers already committed steps. Neither brings external services inside the local COMMIT.

正常路径：业务记录与待发事件在同一事务提交Normal path: commit business row and outbox together

中文

把数据库写入和消息发送分成两步，无论先后都有故障窗口：先 COMMIT 后发送，进程可能在中间崩溃，导致事件没发出；先发送后 COMMIT，则可能业务回滚了，消息却已经发出。Order DB 因此在同一事务中写入业务行和 outbox 事件 e17，将“这条事件以后必须发送”纳入成功承诺。独立的 CDC Relay 再读取已提交日志并发布到 MQ，请求线程不用等待投递完成。[10] CDC 是抓取数据库变更；事件溯源则以事件本身为权威记录，两者是不同的模型。

事件应包含稳定的事件标识 event_id、聚合标识 aggregate_id、聚合版本 aggregate_version，以及类型、内容 payload 和结构版本 schema_version。Debezium Outbox Event Router 用唯一事件 ID 支持去重，用聚合键路由消息。[9] 同一订单需要有序处理时，key=order_id 只约定了进入同一消息分区。多个投递程序并发领取同一 outbox 的事件时，还要有序领取，或由消费者设置版本屏障；相同的 key 本身无法证明生产者没有发乱顺序。

English

Bare COMMIT-then-send can lose events; send-then-COMMIT can create ghosts after rollback. Commit row and e17 intent together, then independently publish committed log changes through CDC Relay. [10] CDC is change capture; event sourcing makes events authoritative.

Use stable event ID, aggregate ID/version, type, payload and schema version. Debezium documents event-ID deduplication and aggregate-key routing. [9] order_id partitioning does not alone order concurrent relay claims; use ordered production or consumer version barriers.

两处确认都要等完成：投递游标与消费 ACKTwo acknowledgment boundaries: cursor and consumption

中文

MQ 返回符合约定的持久化 ACK 后，Relay 才能推进持久游标。消息发布成功但 ACK 丢失，或游标持久化失败，恢复后都会重发 e17；如果先推进游标再发布，则可能永远跳过这条事件。轮询式 relay 可以在 broker ACK 后将表中记录标为已投递，CDC connector 通常保存日志 offset，两者记录进度的方式不同。Broker 自身的确认保证、复制和日志保留，也必须满足服务目标，不能假定消息平台在任何条件下都不会丢数据。

Consumer 收到 e17 后，在 Effect DB 的同一个事务里插入唯一 inbox(e17) 并更新业务行，COMMIT 完成才向 MQ 确认。inbox 还要绑定 payload 摘要：同 ID、同内容才算重复，同 ID 却换了内容要报告冲突。遇到相同事件的唯一冲突，先回滚本次尝试，再读取已提交的 inbox 及原结果；不能吞掉任意约束错误就 ACK。若先提交 inbox，再单独提交业务效果，中间崩溃就会留下“已经去重，业务却没做”的状态。如果数据库事务只包含入账行，邮件在事务外发送，inbox 也不能让邮件与入账原子完成。外部调用仍需操作幂等和回执查询。

English

Advance Relay’s durable cursor only after the broker’s agreed durable ACK. Publishing before a lost ACK/checkpoint causes replay; checkpointing before publishing can skip forever. Polling relay marks delivery; CDC stores log offsets. Broker durability/retention must meet the contract too.

Consumer atomically inserts unique inbox(e17) and changes the business row in Effect DB, then ACKs MQ. Bind inbox IDs to payload digests: changed content conflicts. For an identical duplicate, roll back the conflicting attempt and load the committed inbox/result before ACK; do not swallow unrelated constraint errors. Separate inbox/effect commits can lose effects. A DB transaction cannot atomize external email; external actions still require idempotency/receipt recovery.

代价与恢复：算清积压空间，约定重放范围Cost and recovery: bounded backlog and replay

中文

A10 假设每秒 1,000 条事件，每条原始大小为 1 KB，积压 1 小时就是 1,000×1,000×3,600=3.6 GB。若重投使传输体积增加 10%，所需吞吐为 1,000×1,000×1.1=1.1 MB/s。这只是原始逻辑负载，索引、日志、Broker 副本、压缩和峰值都要另外计算。Relay 停机期间，事件仍占用 Order DB 或日志保留空间；请求已经返回，并不表示可以删除 outbox，要按可靠投递位点和约定的重放范围清理。

消费去重记录的保留期，必须覆盖允许的重投和人工重放范围；金融业务效果最好再有长期业务唯一键。历史事件可能使用旧 schema，也可能乱序触发旧状态。消费者要按 aggregate_version 做条件更新，发现版本缺口时，先缓冲、补取或明确拒绝，不能拿更晚事件直接跳过全部业务步骤。捕获位点对应的日志若已被清理，要先告警并重建捕获起点；新快照不能证明每一条历史业务事件都已发出。

English

A10’s 1,000 events/s at 1 KB retains 3.6 GB over an hour. Assumed 10% retransmission overhead gives 1.1 MB/s, excluding indexes, logs, broker replicas, compression and peaks. Retain intents/logs until agreed delivery/replay boundaries.

Dedup retention must cover permitted retries and manual replay; financial effects need enduring business uniqueness. Handle schema versions and aggregate-version gaps by buffering/recovery/refusal rather than skipping steps. If capture logs are pruned, alert and rebuild without pretending a new snapshot reproduces every historical business event.

正常路径：步骤与回执都要持久化Normal path: persist steps and receipts

中文

本例流程是先预占库存，再扣款，最后放行发货。编排器（Orchestrator）先在 Run DB 保存 saga_id、状态、版本、当前步骤、待执行意图、已知回执和下次恢复时间，再调用对应服务。Inventory、Payment 和 Shipping 各自用本地事务维护业务效果与操作幂等。这里的预占截止时间只触发恢复扫描，不自动归还库存；从 HELD 状态转为出库消耗或释放，由 Inventory 互斥仲裁。若改为自动到期回收，还要设计发货前确认与过期回收之间的竞争，不能凭旧 held 回执继续发货。worker 领取运行记录时，可用租约和持有者代次（owner generation）阻止旧执行者推进状态；外部服务也要识别稳定的 operation_key，防止相同动作重复执行。

某一步响应丢失时，Run DB 保留待执行或 UNKNOWN 状态。重启后按原操作查询持久回执，确认成功才写入下一步意图。保存结果与推进运行状态，在编排库的同一事务中完成。如果外部服务已经执行，编排库却还没记录结果，就通过相同操作键查询或幂等重试来恢复。各服务没有共同的一次 COMMIT，因此这些恢复步骤本来就是主流程设计的一部分。

English

Use reserve→capture→release shipment. Run DB stores saga ID/state/version, current step, dispatch intent, known receipts and next recovery time before service calls. Each service locally preserves effect/idempotency. Here reservation deadlines trigger recovery scans, not automatic stock release; Inventory arbitrates fulfillment consumption versus release from HELD. Automatic expiry would additionally require pre-shipment confirmation competing with expiry; an old held receipt is insufficient. Worker lease/generation protects run advancement; remote stable operation keys protect external effects.

Lost responses leave pending/UNKNOWN state. Query the original operation receipt on recovery and confirm before writing the next intent. Store receipt and run advancement together in the orchestration DB; cross-service windows recover through the same operation key, not an imaginary shared COMMIT.

失败路径：补偿按业务依赖执行Failure path: compensation follows business dependencies

中文

只有 Shipping 在权威存储中进入不可再发货的终态失败，并能拒绝迟到的放行请求，编排器才持久保存退款和释放库存的意图。一次“未找到回执”，或一次普通查询显示尚未发货，都不足以证明这一点。Payment 要针对原 capture，使用独立的 refund_key 退款；Inventory 只能释放该订单仍处于 HELD 的预占，不能释放已经转成履约消耗的库存。图中两条红线都是编排器发出的补偿调用，Shipping 的错误先返回编排器，再由它决定后续动作。

业务允许时，退款与库存释放可以分别推进；若要求退款成功才能关闭订单，就把这个依赖写入状态。补偿顺序取决于资源依赖，不能机械地倒序执行所有 RPC。Sagas 原论文讨论了长事务拆分与补偿。[11] 但今天的跨服务补偿与数据库 rollback 不同：退款会新增账本条目，已经发出的通知无法收回，出库货物则要走召回或售后。补偿不会抹去用户已经看到的中间状态。

English

Only terminal failure in Shipping’s authoritative storage that prevents delayed releases from shipping permits refund/release intents. A missing receipt or an ordinary not-shipped query is insufficient. Payment refunds the original capture under a separate stable refund key. Inventory releases only that order’s still-HELD reservation, not stock already consumed for fulfillment. Red commands originate at Orchestrator; shipping errors return there first.

Compensations may advance independently if business permits; encode dependencies such as refund-before-close. Order follows resources, not mechanical reverse RPC order. The Sagas paper motivates long-transaction decomposition/compensation. [11] Modern refunds create new ledger entries; notifications and physical shipments cannot be rolled back from history.

结果未知、补偿失败与并发请求怎样处理UNKNOWN, failed compensation and concurrent observations

中文

扣款超时不能证明未扣款，发货超时也不能证明未发货。结果为 UNKNOWN 时，先查同一操作的回执；查询也不可用，就保留未知，不能因为持久意图已到期而重新执行一次。退款失败时沿用同一 refund_key 重试，记录尝试次数、原因、下次执行时间和恢复预算；超出自动恢复范围后，转入有明确负责人的人工队列。补偿本身也可能结果未知，此时仍需查回执恢复，不能删除运行记录了事。

Saga 没有全局隔离，其他请求可能看到“已扣款、未发货”的中间状态，因此订单应显示 PROCESSING。取消与发货要在 Shipping 的权威状态行上，依据版本和状态条件竞争，并拒绝旧步骤迟到的放行请求；只有持久保存了 CANCELLED，才能安全启动退款。仅在 Run DB 标记取消，挡不住已经发出的 RPC；如果发货先成功，就要转售后路径。事件协同（choreography）可以用每一步的 Outbox 事件推进流程，减少中心决策，但依赖会分散到各服务；集中编排则统一决策，仍然需要本地事务和可靠消息。无论选择哪种，都要说明服务重启后由谁找出未完成步骤并继续恢复。

English

Payment timeout does not mean unpaid, and shipping timeout does not mean unshipped. Query the same operation and retain UNKNOWN if unavailable. Never expire intent as permission to repeat. Retry refunds under the same key with attempts/reasons/schedule/budget; escalate beyond automatic recovery to an owned manual queue. Unknown compensation also requires receipt recovery.

Saga lacks global isolation: users may observe paid-but-unshipped. Show PROCESSING; arbitrate cancel/ship on Shipping’s authoritative row using expected version/state and reject delayed releases from old steps. Only durable CANCELLED permits refund; changing Run DB alone cannot stop an issued RPC. Shipment winning requires after-sales handling. Choreography uses per-step Outbox events with dispersed dependencies; orchestration concentrates decisions but still needs transactions/reliable delivery. Both need restart recovery ownership.
