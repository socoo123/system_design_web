30 秒开场：先问成功回执承诺什么Opening: define the success contract

中文

我先把三件事分开：复制让同一份数据多放几份；分片让不同的数据各有归属；事务让一组变更一起满足不变量。读多写少可先异步复制，写成功只承诺主库持久提交。强耐久要等指定副本，并让切主过程恢复那段已确认日志；刚写就读要检查应用位点与历史身份。容量超出单机再按查询选分片键，订单和订单项尽量同片。跨服务先把业务行与事件意图放本地事务，再用 Outbox 推进；长流程持久记录步骤和回执，用带条件的补偿恢复。2PC 要看参与者与故障预算，不用一句“分布式事务都不能用”代替设计。

这章沿用 Ch02 的逐笔演进和 Ch03 的假设推导。图内数字是本章 assumption，不代表数据库产品基准测试。

澄清先问：成功写能丢多少、允许读旧多久、故障是一台机器还是整个地域；请求天然带哪个实体 ID，哪些唯一性和扣减必须一起成立；外部服务支持幂等或查回执吗？默认单地域、单写者。无主 KV 的冲突合并回 Ch06，共识锁回 Ch08，Kafka 分区回 Ch20。

English

Separate replication, ownership through sharding, and transaction invariants. Async success promises local durability. Strong durability waits for selected replicas and recovers the acknowledged prefix during failover. Read-your-writes checks applied progress and history. Shard by queries after exceeding single-node capacity and co-locate Order with Items. Commit event intent locally through Outbox; persist long-running steps and receipts and use conditional compensations. Choose 2PC by participant capability and failure budgets.

Following Ch02’s incremental diagrams and Ch03’s assumption arithmetic, the diagrams use teaching assumptions rather than product benchmarks.

Ask about acceptable acknowledged-write loss, stale-read duration, machine versus region failures, entity IDs and atomic invariants, and external idempotency/receipt APIs. Assume one region and one writer. Ch06 covers leaderless merging, Ch08 consensus locks and Ch20 Kafka partitions.

41.1 · 复制：把三种 ACK 拆开41.1 · Replication: distinguish acknowledgments

正常路径：写请求回主，日志独立发给副本Normal path: primary writes, independent log streams

中文

Client→App→Primary 提交一次业务事务，结果沿 Primary→App→Client 返回。Primary 再把 WAL 分别流给 Replica A/B，副本不串成 A→B 的接力链。复制流中的日志位点描述顺序，业务幂等键描述同一次操作，两者不能互相替代。异步回执不等副本；主库和本地持久介质一起永久丢失时，未送出的已确认写可能没有可恢复副本。[2]

“副本已有”必须拆开说。receive 是收到字节，flush 是进入可恢复持久介质，apply 是按日志更新数据。flush 已完成而 apply 落后，切主可通过回放恢复，普通查询却暂时看不到。PostgreSQL 17 的 remote_write 不保证副本操作系统故障后持久，on 在配置同步 standby 时等远端持久，remote_apply 进一步等应用；没有配置同步 standby，设置名字本身不会变出远端保障。[1]

English

Client→App→Primary commits once and returns Primary→App→Client. Primary independently streams WAL to A/B. Log position orders changes; an idempotency key identifies an operation. Async success does not wait for replicas and can lose unshipped writes if the primary and its durable media are permanently lost. [2]

Receive, flush and apply are different. Durable but unapplied logs can recover during promotion while remaining invisible to reads. In PostgreSQL 17, remote_write does not ensure durability against standby OS failure; on waits for remote durability when synchronous standbys are configured, and remote_apply additionally waits for application. The setting alone does not create a synchronous standby. [1]

为什么等待：耐久与可用性必须一起定Why wait: durability and availability are coupled

中文

A2 故意采用串行教学预算：主库处理及持久 4 ms，再支付副本 6 ms RTT 和 2 ms flush，合计 12 ms。远端 RTT 换成 80 ms，就变成 4+80+2=86 ms。真实实现可把部分落盘与网络重叠，不能把这个加法当数据库 p99；需要同样事务体量、并发和故障域的测量。它只说明为什么主线里同城同步和跨洲异步会产生不同选择。

本章强耐久合同固定等 Replica A，A 不可用时不给成功，必要时返回不可用或结果未知并查询幂等记录。若改为“任意一个副本确认”，提升协议必须找到包含所有已确认写的可恢复前缀，不能随便选 B。MySQL 8.4 半同步等副本 relay log 落盘后 ACK，并不等副本执行；默认等待点和超时退异步行为都有具体配置语义。[4] 所以“开了半同步”不是端到端零丢失证明。

English

A2 deliberately uses a serial budget: 4 ms local processing/durability plus 6 ms replica RTT and 2 ms flush gives 12 ms. An 80 ms distant RTT gives 86 ms. Real implementations overlap work and require measurements for matching transaction size and concurrency; these are not p99 figures.

Our strong-durability contract waits specifically for A and refuses successful acknowledgment when A is unavailable, resolving uncertain results through idempotency records. An any-replica policy requires promotion to recover every acknowledged write. MySQL 8.4 semisync acknowledges a flushed relay log without waiting for execution, and its wait-point and timeout-to-async behavior matter. [4]

故障窗口：复制位点之外还要监测日志保留Failure window: retain recovery logs

中文

监测至少区分主库生成、副本已持久、副本已应用三个位点，并看缺口字节与增长速度。以 apply lag 报警可以保护副本读体验，以持久缺口分析灾难丢写，不能让一张“lag 2 秒”仪表盘代替两个判断。空闲期间位点没变化，也不能仅用最后更新时间宣判故障。

Replication slot 保留日志能让落后副本续追，却会把压力推回 Primary。PostgreSQL 文档提醒 WAL 保留可能填满磁盘。[2] 因此设保留与磁盘余量告警，超过可恢复窗口时重建副本并移出读池；不要把“永不清日志”当可靠性设计。三副本也不会替代备份：误删可同时复制到所有在线副本，历史恢复仍需独立的备份与回放窗口。

English

Track generated, durable and applied positions plus gap bytes/growth. Apply lag diagnoses stale reads; durable gaps diagnose catastrophic loss. Idle positions alone do not establish failure.

Slots retain logs for catch-up but may fill the primary’s disk. [2] Alert on retention/headroom and rebuild replicas outside the recovery window rather than retaining forever. Replication is not backup: accidental deletion can propagate to every live replica.

41.2 · 刚写就读：token、历史与新快照41.2 · Read-your-writes: token, history and fresh snapshots

正常路径：只有应用屏障通过才发查询Normal path: query after the replay barrier

中文

提交回执带 token，含集群与日志历史身份、提交位点；图中简写 T:1042。App 内的 Read Gate 选择同一历史的 Replica A，先查它已应用的连续前缀，再建立读快照。满足 replay≥1042 才执行这次查询。若应用了更晚事务而中间那笔没包含，单独看“最大序号”就不成立，位点必须具有连续前缀语义。

不要在旧 Repeatable Read 事务里等副本追平再重跑 SELECT。该事务仍使用既有快照；PostgreSQL 文档明确该级别后续查询看到同一事务快照。[7] 正确做法是屏障之后开新读事务，或者使用契约清楚的新语句快照。图里的“replay+fresh query”把这两个条件放在一起，查询结果经 Gate→App→Client 返回。

English

A commit token includes cluster/history and commit position, abbreviated T:1042. Read Gate within App checks the same-history replica’s continuous applied prefix, then creates the read snapshot. replay≥1042 must mean inclusion of the entire prefix, not merely a maximum sequence number.

Waiting inside an existing Repeatable Read transaction does not refresh its snapshot. PostgreSQL documents the fixed transaction snapshot. [7] Start a new transaction after the barrier or use a clearly defined fresh statement snapshot. Results return Gate→App→Client.

为什么限时：一致性不能变成无限占槽Why bound waiting: consistency must not monopolize slots

中文

A3 给副本等待 20 ms，它是局部等待预算，不是整个 API 的 SLO。追平不了就尝试仍包含 token 历史的 Primary，并受请求剩余 deadline 与主库准入限制；主库不可用或不包含那次提交，就明确不可用。大量副本慢读同时回主会压垮写路径，所以回主也要限流、合并查询或只保留关键操作，不把每个超时自动变成主库新负载。

最简单替代方案是这次读直接打 Primary，省掉 token 屏障实现；代价是主库读压力。会话暂时粘主也能改善体验，但 TTL 到期不是副本已追平的证明。普通 Feed 可明确允许旧值；“我刚保存的地址”要 read-your-writes。密码撤销或余额授权还需权威语义，不能把只对自己写入的保证推广为所有用户读写的线性一致。

English

A3’s 20 ms is a local waiting allowance, not the API SLO. On expiry, eligible Primary fallback remains subject to deadline/admission. If no eligible history is available, return unavailable. Bound fallback load rather than flooding the writer.

Reading Primary directly avoids the barrier but increases primary read load. Sticky-primary TTL is not proof of catch-up. Feed can allow staleness; saved-address reads may require read-your-writes. Password revocation and balance authorization need stronger authority semantics; read-your-writes is not global linearizability.

切主后的边界：更大数字不代表包含旧提交After failover: a larger number need not include the write

中文

异步提升若丢了已确认的 T:1042，新主可能从另一历史继续产生更大位点。Gate 必须识别“旧 token 不在当前历史”，不能见到数值更大就放行。合法恢复可以证明新历史继承了该前缀；否则该读取失败，同时将丢写按业务合同对账。让 token 带历史身份也是为了在连接池换服务器时仍能判断语义，不靠某个 App 的内存记忆。

read-your-writes 的 token 应由服务生成并绑定授权上下文，不能接受客户端自造高位点造成长时间等待。跨服务写若最终改变了另一个库，原库 token 也不能证明下游投影已经应用；要有该投影自己的完成回执。设计题里把“等日志位点”说完整，常比只报“走主库保证强一致”更有价值。

English

A lagging promotion may lose T:1042 and then generate numerically larger positions on another history. Gate requires proof of prefix inheritance; otherwise refuse and reconcile the loss under the business contract. History identity survives pool/server changes.

Generate tokens server-side and bind authorization so fabricated positions cannot force unbounded waits. A source-DB token does not establish downstream projection completion; that requires its own acknowledgment.

41.3 · failover：先隔离旧主，再开放新路由41.3 · Failover: fence before reopening routes

正常切换：四个状态各有证据Normal transition: evidence for every state

中文

第三组是控制状态图，SUSPECT→FENCED→RECOVERED→OPEN，而不是四个机器。探测失败可能来自网络分区，旧 Primary 仍能接受某些客户端写。控制器进入 SUSPECT 后暂停新写路由，先通过可靠机制撤销旧写者的存储写权或断电，取得 fencing 证明，再谈提升。PostgreSQL 官方 failover 文档也要求避免旧主恢复后同时自认 Primary。[3]

FENCED 之后选可恢复副本，回放候选持久日志，并验证强耐久合同承诺的前缀确实包含在候选中。随后提升为新 Primary、发布更高 route epoch，客户端重连。单靠 App 把 epoch 写进 SQL 不能 fence 一台仍允许旧连接提交的独立数据库；epoch 必须由真正的写入权威检查，或用硬隔离补足。本章状态机表达约束，不假定原生主从自动提供这些机制。

English

This deck models control states, not four machines: SUSPECT→FENCED→RECOVERED→OPEN. A partitioned Primary may still accept writes. Pause new routes and obtain real storage write-exclusion or power fencing before promotion; PostgreSQL’s failover documentation also warns against simultaneous primaries. [3]

Recover and replay an eligible candidate containing the required acknowledged prefix, promote it, then publish a higher route epoch and reconnect clients. An application SQL parameter cannot fence an independent DB that still permits old connections. The actual writer must enforce authority or be physically isolated. The state machine specifies requirements, not built-in automatic guarantees.

怎么估丢写：持久缺口与恢复时间分开Sizing loss: separate durable gaps and recovery time

中文

A4 假设主库已确认、候选又无法恢复的日志恰对应最后 2 s，且写率稳定 1,000/s，风险写量约 2×1,000=2,000。若只是 apply 落后 2 s，但相应日志已在候选持久保存，就能 replay，不能算成必然丢 2,000 写。按秒估量也不等于保证：实际写率可能有突发，精确对账应定位事务或业务 ID 集合。

RPO 讨论能丢多少；RTO 讨论多久恢复服务。RTO 还包含探测、隔离、回放、发布、客户端连接建立与失败重试，不能把“promote 耗时”当全链路恢复。同步复制减少符合故障模型的丢写风险，却可能为了 fencing 或回放等待更久。这是 Ch36 里可用性与一致性取舍落到实际步骤的地方。

English

Under A4, the unrecoverable acknowledged gap corresponds to 2 s at steady 1,000/s: about 2,000 writes at risk. A 2 s apply lag backed by durable logs can replay and is not that loss. Bursts invalidate steady-rate precision; reconcile transaction/business IDs.

RPO measures acceptable loss; RTO includes detection, fencing, replay, publication, reconnect and retries. Promotion duration alone is not end-to-end recovery. Sync durability may still require lengthy safe recovery, concretizing Ch36’s consistency/availability choices.

未知回执与共识边界Unknown outcomes and the consensus boundary

中文

数据库可能已经 COMMIT，但连接在返回前断开。App 返回超时不等于事务失败；重试同一业务幂等键应查询原结果。幂等记录与业务写必须同事务，其耐久和复制策略也必须一样。若异步灾难连幂等记录都丢了，“查不到”只是未知或合同内丢失，不能据此保证外部扣款从未发生；外部回执仍需恢复。

Raft 的共识日志比“异步主从+探测切主”有更强的选举与提交规则。论文对通过复制数推进提交还限定当前任期条目，旧任期条目通过后续提交间接确认。[12] 面试口述可以说 leader 接写、多数派提交，但别把任意多数副本存了某条日志当完整安全证明。这里以主从合同为主，复制控制器采用共识存储也不自动让业务数据库成为 Raft 数据库。

English

A COMMIT may succeed before the response connection fails. Retry/query the same idempotency key; store its result with the business transaction under the same durability policy. If async disaster also loses that record, absence does not prove an external charge never happened.

Raft has stronger election/commit rules than async primary/replica plus probes. Its replication-count commitment rule applies to current-term entries, indirectly committing earlier entries. [12] A majority holding arbitrary logs is not the complete safety proof. Consensus control storage does not automatically turn the business DB into a Raft database.

41.4 · 分片键：查询、不变量和热点一起选41.4 · Shard keys: queries, invariants and hotspots

正常路径：不同键分开，同一个聚合留在一起Normal path: split keys, keep aggregates together

中文

先写三条主要查询：按 tenant 列最近订单、按 tenant+order_id 点查、按 order_id 查全局客服记录。前两条占主线，选择 hash(tenant_id) 的键空间范围归属，再由目录映射到 Shard 0/1。图只展开两片；A5 实际假设八片，每片拥有不同租户集合。Router 不随机挑库，同一键必须在当前目录修订下找到唯一写者，响应按 Shard→Router→App 回来。

订单、订单项和同聚合幂等记录共用路由键，才能留住本地事务。全局 order_id 搜索需目录索引或异步投影，不能假设 tenant 分片之后它还天然单片。投影要保留事件版本与删除标记，并在读取时承认延迟；主行才负责订单事实。跨片唯一约束也需显式机制，例如集中 ID 发行或目录登记；每片各有 UNIQUE(order_id) 并不等于全局唯一。

English

Start with tenant order lists, tenant+order point queries and global support lookup. The first two motivate tenant-hash ranges mapped by a revisioned directory. Two illustrated shards represent eight assumed shards. Router resolves one writer per key rather than selecting randomly; replies return Shard→Router→App.

Co-route Order, Items and aggregate idempotency records for local transactions. Global order lookup requires an index/directory or lag-aware projection with versions/deletes. Local UNIQUE constraints do not alone create global uniqueness; define ID issuance or registration explicitly.

为什么高基数不够：一个值仍只能落一片Why cardinality alone is insufficient

中文

MongoDB 选键文档分别考虑 cardinality、frequency、单调性和查询模式。[5] status 值少，很快就把同状态的写压到一起；递增时间范围方便扫历史，却让新写热在尾部；随机 hash 有利均匀，却会拆散范围查询。把一个热 tenant 哈希成一个点，仍是那一个点，不能把它的请求均分给八片。

A5 均匀时 8,000÷8=1,000/s，每片比舒适预算 1,200/s 留 200/s；8×1,200=9,600/s 是均衡且同工作量分布的条件容量。A6 热 tenant 占 40%，即 3,200/s，单片超出 2,000/s。其余 4,800/s 若理想摊到七片，每片约 686/s，空闲容量也不能替热片提交它拥有的那行。

English

MongoDB’s shard-key guidance separates cardinality, frequency, monotonicity and query patterns. [5] Low-cardinality status keys concentrate writes; time ranges concentrate new writes at the tail; hashes scatter range queries. Hashing a hot tenant still places its one value at one point.

Balanced A5 gives 8,000/8=1,000/s per shard, 200/s below 1,200/s. Aggregate 9,600/s assumes balance and matching work. A6’s hot tenant needs 3,200/s, exceeding its shard by 2,000/s. Ideally distributing the remaining 4,800/s across seven shards gives about 686/s each; their idle capacity cannot transact the hot shard’s owned row.

替代方案与故障行为Alternatives and failure behavior

中文

可把热租户放独立高容量池，先不改变事务边界；再热才按 tenant+bucket 拆实体，承担列表散查与跨桶规则。八个 bucket 理想时是 3,200÷8=400/s，但同一个热 SKU 的严格扣减仍由其权威行串行仲裁，不能只加后缀便声称库存约束仍原子。选择 bucket 要证明业务可拆，而不是为了让除法好看。

用户列表若必须散查，结果还需全局排序、稳定分页与预算。不能简单拿每片 offset 翻页，写入变化会重复或遗漏。某片不可用时，金融或订单事实查询不能把部分结果当完整成功；可接受不完整的搜索应返回明确 partial 标志。这些代价决定是否值得分片：本地读索引和缓存足够时，先保留单主，而不是为了“水平扩展”提前引入路由和迁移。

English

An isolated high-capacity tenant pool preserves transaction boundaries. tenant+bucket splits entities at the cost of scatter queries and cross-bucket rules. Eight ideal buckets yield 400/s each, but a strict hot-SKU decrement still needs authoritative serialization. Prove business partitionability rather than adding suffixes for nicer division.

Scatter lists require global ordering, stable pagination and budgets; per-shard offsets can omit/duplicate under concurrent writes. Order/financial fact queries cannot label partial results complete. Search may explicitly mark partial. These costs may favor retaining one primary with indexes/cache until needed.

41.5 · 重分片：完整拷贝不是切流资格41.5 · Resharding: a full copy is not cutover proof

正常搬迁：快照与增量覆盖同一条历史Normal migration: snapshot and changes cover one history

中文

Source Shard 在 rev=7 下保持唯一写权威。迁移先获取一致快照 S，并保证 S 之后的事务日志从开始就被保留，再导出到 Target。Snapshot Copier 不把多次普通 SELECT 拼成“完整快照”，否则同一订单与订单项可能取自不同时间。Change Relay 读取 S 之后的有序变化，包含更新、删除和必要的事务边界，让 Target 恢复到连续可验证前缀。

拷贝与变更可能同时到达：若一条新版本已回放，后到的旧快照行不能覆盖它；可以采用分阶段屏障、逐范围完成标记或引擎已验证的版本合并协议。删除也要留下防复活信息，不能只重放 INSERT/UPDATE。Vitess 的 Reshard 工作流将建目标、复制与追变更、SwitchTraffic、ReverseTraffic 分开，切写后支持反向复制。[6] 本图抽出这些阶段的约束，不声称通用脚本就能替代引擎。

English

Source remains the sole rev-7 writer. Capture a consistent S and retain subsequent logs from the start. Ordinary SELECTs stitched together are not a consistent snapshot. Relay ordered updates/deletes and transaction boundaries to produce a verified continuous target prefix.

Concurrent copy/replay must prevent old snapshot rows from overwriting newer versions through barriers/range tracking or verified merging. Deletes need anti-resurrection information. Vitess separates workflow creation, copy/catch-up, SwitchTraffic and ReverseTraffic, with reverse replication after write cutover. [6] Our diagram extracts requirements rather than replacing an engine.

算时间：追赶速度要减去持续产生的日志Time arithmetic: subtract ongoing generation

中文

A7 全量 400 GB÷100 MB/s=4,000 s≈66.7 min，已经是有效速度假设，不含额外重试和源端读取压测成本。A8 回放 60 MB/s、新日志 20 MB/s，净追赶 40 MB/s；120 GB backlog÷40 MB/s=3,000 s=50 min。全量和追日志可重叠，只有明确哪个阶段结束时测得 backlog，才可给总时长，不能直接相加说 116.7 分钟。

回放能力若降到 20 MB/s，净速度为零；低于它 backlog 继续涨，永远等不到“追平即可切”。需要限源写、提高回放、缩小迁移范围，或明确短暂停写窗口。还要给日志保留、Target 额外空间和源库复制读留余量：迁移往往在旧集群已接近容量时启动，搬迁流量却会再吃旧集群资源。

English

A7: 400 GB/100 MB/s=4,000 s≈66.7 min at assumed effective speed, excluding retries/source-load costs. A8: 60−20=40 MB/s net catch-up; 120 GB/40 MB/s=3,000 s=50 min. Copy/replay may overlap; total time depends on when backlog is measured and is not automatically 116.7 min.

At 20 MB/s replay, net catch-up is zero; below it, backlog grows. Throttle writes, raise replay, shrink scope or budget a pause. Reserve logs, target space and source read capacity, especially when the source is already full.

切写与回退：两边都不能凭旧路由继续写Cutover and rollback: old routes cannot retain write rights

中文

最终切写先停止相关源范围的新写并等在途事务完成，取得屏障 H。Target 必须完成该范围快照且已应用到 H，再在持久控制面发布 rev=8 的新归属。Source 写端拒绝旧 rev=7；过期 Router 收到迁移错误后刷新目录并带同一幂等键重试。不能靠广播“所有 Router 应该更新了”证明单写者，因为进程可能离线后带旧缓存回来。

发布回执丢失要查询控制面现有 rev，不能重新分配一次所有权。切后 Target 已接受新写时，立即改回 Source 会丢切后变更；必须反向复制到回退屏障、隔离 Target，再恢复 Source 写权。源数据清理还要等旧读者排空与回退窗口结束。

English

Fence new source-range writes, drain active transactions and record H. Require complete target copy and application through H before durably publishing rev 8. Source rejects rev 7; stale Routers refresh and retry with the same idempotency key. Broadcast expectation is not write fencing.

If publication acknowledgment is lost, query the existing revision. Once Target accepts writes, rollback requires reverse replay to a rollback barrier and fencing Target before Source resumes. Clean Source only after old-reader drain and rollback-window expiry.

41.6 · 本地事务与 2PC：prepared 不能自己猜结论41.6 · Local transactions and 2PC: prepared is not a decision

先守本地不变量，再讨论跨库Preserve local invariants before crossing databases

中文

订单和订单项同片时，用一个本地事务写业务行、幂等结果和后续 outbox。库存扣减写成条件更新并核受影响行数，或在相应隔离与锁规则下检查后修改。仅写“ACID”不会自动防止应用先读到足够库存、两次请求再各自扣成负数。Serializable 也可能让事务报冲突，应用要以同一逻辑操作重试；遇到约束错误还要区分真实业务拒绝与可重试冲突。[7]

跨库 2PC 的第一阶段是 Coordinator 请求各参与者 prepare，参与者持久化可提交状态、保留锁并回答 YES。所有 YES 之后，Coordinator 持久化 COMMIT 决策，再发第二阶段；任一 NO 则持久化 ABORT 并通知。第二阶段 ACK 丢失应重复同一个 transaction_id 的决策通知，不能创建一场新事务。图聚焦 prepared 故障窗口，上方保留本地事务作为比较基线。

English

Locally commit Order, Items, idempotency result and outbox. Inventory needs a checked conditional update or proper locks/isolation; “ACID” alone does not repair read-then-decrement application races. Serializable can fail on conflicts and requires logical-operation retries; distinguish business rejection from retryable conflicts. [7]

In 2PC, participants persist prepared state, retain locks and vote YES. After all YES votes, Coordinator persists COMMIT and broadcasts phase two; any NO yields durable ABORT. Repeat a lost phase-two acknowledgment using the same transaction ID. This deck focuses on the prepared failure window, compared with local transactions above.

故障窗口：超时不知道 COMMIT 是否已经持久Failure window: timeout cannot identify the decision

中文

某参与者已 YES，但还没拿到最终决定，协调者又不可达，这就是 in-doubt。它不能“超时默认 abort”，因为协调者可能已持久 COMMIT 并通知另一参与者。要向可信决策日志或恢复的协调者查原 transaction_id，确定后才 COMMIT PREPARED 或 ROLLBACK PREPARED。协调者可以有复制决策日志，故障时也可恢复；2PC 的代价是这个窗口中仍可能等待与持锁，不是“协调者挂了永远没法恢复”。

PostgreSQL 17 明确 PREPARE TRANSACTION 把状态存盘，连接结束不会自动解开，并持续保留锁；长期 prepared 会影响清理。[8] 所以受控参与者需要决策日志、恢复扫描、告警与运维所有权，不能拿用户请求 timeout 管事务寿命。图中红线否决的是跳过可信决定的 abort，不是禁止所有跨分片提交。

English

A YES voter without the final decision is in-doubt if Coordinator is unreachable. Timeout-abort is unsafe because durable COMMIT may already have reached another participant. Recover the original decision from a trustworthy log/coordinator, then commit or roll back prepared work. Coordinator logs can be replicated/recovered; the cost is potential waiting/locks, not permanent impossibility.

PostgreSQL persists prepared state and retains locks beyond session termination; long prepared transactions affect cleanup. [8] Controlled participants need decision recovery scans, alerts and ownership, not user-request timeouts. The red path rejects decision-free abort, not every cross-shard commit.

选型边界：原子性、隔离与外部副作用是三回事Selection: atomicity, isolation and external effects differ

中文

2PC 解决多参与者的共同提交决定，不单独提供串行隔离；引擎还需锁、时间戳或冲突控制。受控数据库引擎在明确延迟和故障预算下可采用分布式事务，也可以选择重新聚合数据让事务留在同片。别为微服务划分先拆掉本地不变量，再用复杂协议把它拼回去。

PSP、短信或发货通常不参加你的数据库 prepare/commit 协议。它们的操作可能不可撤销，所以用户路径应有幂等动作、持久回执和业务补偿。Outbox 解决“提交事实和事件意图一起持久”，Saga 解决“已提交的多步如何恢复业务结果”，两者不把所有服务变成一次 ACID 提交；对中间状态的观察与业务隔离要另设计。

English

2PC provides an atomic decision, not serial isolation by itself; engines still need concurrency control. Controlled distributed databases may use it within budgets, or co-locate aggregates instead. Avoid splitting local invariants merely to satisfy service boundaries.

PSP, SMS and shipping usually lack your prepare/commit protocol and may be irreversible. Use idempotent actions, receipts and compensation. Outbox atomically persists fact plus event intent; Saga recovers multi-step business outcomes. Neither makes every service one ACID transaction or supplies business isolation.

41.7 · Outbox：保证意图，效果还要再过一次事务41.7 · Outbox: durable intent, transactional effect

正常路径：业务行与 outbox 同提交Normal path: commit business row and outbox together

中文

裸双写无论顺序都留窗口：先 COMMIT 后 send，进程在两者间崩溃会少事件；先 send 后 COMMIT，业务回滚却已有幽灵事件。Order DB 同事务写 row 与 outbox e17，把“将来必须发送”的意图纳入成功承诺。独立 CDC Relay 读已提交日志，再向 MQ 发布；请求线程不承担投递完成。[10] CDC 抓取数据库变更，事件溯源把事件本身当权威记录，两种模型不要混称。

事件包含稳定 event_id、aggregate_id、aggregate_version、类型、payload 与 schema_version。Debezium Outbox Event Router 文档把唯一事件 ID 用于去重，以聚合键路由消息。[9] 同一订单需要顺序时，key=order_id 只是进入同一消息分区的必要约定；多投递器抢同一 outbox 时仍需有序 claim 或消费者版本屏障，不能以“都用同一个 key”证明生产者从未发乱。

English

Bare COMMIT-then-send can lose events; send-then-COMMIT can create ghosts after rollback. Commit row and e17 intent together, then independently publish committed log changes through CDC Relay. [10] CDC is change capture; event sourcing makes events authoritative.

Use stable event ID, aggregate ID/version, type, payload and schema version. Debezium documents event-ID deduplication and aggregate-key routing. [9] order_id partitioning does not alone order concurrent relay claims; use ordered production or consumer version barriers.

两条确认线：cursor 与消费 ACK 都不能提前Two acknowledgment boundaries: cursor and consumption

中文

MQ 返回符合约定的 durable ACK 后，Relay 才推进持久 cursor。发布成功但 ACK 或 cursor 持久化失败，恢复会重发 e17；先推进 cursor 再发布则可能永久跳过。轮询 relay 可在 broker ACK 后标已投递，CDC connector 通常保存日志 offset；不要在正文里把轮询表标记与 CDC 位点当同一个操作。Broker 自身的确认合同、复制和日志保留也要符合服务目标，消息平台并不是无条件不会丢。

Consumer 收到 e17 后，在 Effect DB 同事务插入唯一 inbox(e17) 并更新业务行，COMMIT 完成才 ACK MQ。重复消息触发唯一冲突时返回已处理结果，不再做第二次效果。若先提交 inbox 再提交效果，中间崩溃会“去重成功、业务没做”；若事务只包入账行但邮件在事务外发出，inbox 也不能把邮件变成原子效果。外部调用继续用操作幂等与回执查询。

English

Advance Relay’s durable cursor only after the broker’s agreed durable ACK. Publishing before a lost ACK/checkpoint causes replay; checkpointing before publishing can skip forever. Polling relay marks delivery; CDC stores log offsets. Broker durability/retention must meet the contract too.

Consumer atomically inserts unique inbox(e17) and changes the business row in Effect DB, then ACKs MQ. Duplicates return the recorded result without another effect. Separate inbox/effect commits can lose effects. A DB transaction cannot atomize external email; external actions still require idempotency/receipt recovery.

代价与恢复：积压有容量，重放有边界Cost and recovery: bounded backlog and replay

中文

A10 每秒 1,000 条、每条裸 1 KB，1 h 积压为 1,000×1,000×3,600=3.6 GB。假设重放体积加 10%，吞吐为 1,000×1,000×1.1=1.1 MB/s；这是裸逻辑负载，索引、日志、Broker 副本、压缩与峰值另算。Relay 停机期间事件仍占 Order DB 或日志保留空间，不能因为“请求已经返回”就删掉 outbox；按可靠投递位点与重放合同清理。

消费去重保留期必须覆盖允许的重投和人工重放范围，金融效果最好再有长期业务唯一键。历史回放可能跨 schema 版本，也可能乱序触发旧状态；消费者对 aggregate_version 做条件更新，发现缺口时缓冲、补取或明确拒绝，不拿更晚事件直接覆盖所有业务步骤。日志位点被清掉时先报警和重建捕获起点，不把新快照假装成每一个历史业务事件已发过。

English

A10’s 1,000 events/s at 1 KB retains 3.6 GB over an hour. Assumed 10% replay volume gives 1.1 MB/s, excluding indexes, logs, broker replicas, compression and peaks. Retain intents/logs until agreed delivery/replay boundaries.

Dedup retention must cover permitted retries and manual replay; financial effects need enduring business uniqueness. Handle schema versions and aggregate-version gaps by buffering/recovery/refusal rather than skipping steps. If capture logs are pruned, alert and rebuild without pretending a new snapshot reproduces every historical business event.

41.8 · Saga：每一步成功，整体仍可能要恢复41.8 · Saga: local success can still need recovery

正常路径：步骤与回执都要持久化Normal path: persist steps and receipts

中文

流程固定为库存预占→支付扣款→释放发货。Orchestrator 在 Run DB 保存 saga_id、状态、版本、当前步骤、待发 intent、已知回执和下一次恢复时间，再调用对应服务。Inventory、Payment、Shipping 各用本地事务维护业务效果与操作幂等。worker 领取 run 可用租约和 owner generation 排除旧执行者推进状态；外部服务也必须识别稳定 operation_key，不能只靠编排器租约排重。

每一步的响应若丢失，Run DB 保留待执行或 UNKNOWN，重启后查询该 operation 的持久回执，确认成功才写下一步 intent。结果记录与推进 run 在同一编排库事务中完成；动作在另一个服务已经发生的窗口靠相同操作键查询或幂等重试弥合。没有“所有库共用一次 COMMIT”的幻想，恢复路径才是正常设计的一部分。

English

Use reserve→capture→release shipment. Run DB stores saga ID/state/version, current step, dispatch intent, known receipts and next recovery time before service calls. Each service locally preserves effect/idempotency. Worker lease/generation protects run advancement; remote stable operation keys protect external effects.

Lost responses leave pending/UNKNOWN state. Query the original operation receipt on recovery and confirm before writing the next intent. Store receipt and run advancement together in the orchestration DB; cross-service windows recover through the same operation key, not an imaginary shared COMMIT.

失败路径：补偿按业务依赖执行Failure path: compensation follows business dependencies

中文

若 Shipping 确定没有发货且出现终态失败，编排器持久化 refund 与 release 意图。Payment 按原 capture 及独立 refund_key 退款，Inventory 只释放该订单仍处于 HELD 的预占，不能把已转成履约消耗的库存当可释放。图中两条红线是编排器下发的补偿调用，Shipping 的错误先回编排器，不能画成 Shipping 直接去扣款库。

退款与释放可以在业务允许时分别推进；若业务要求退款成功才能关闭订单，就把这个依赖写进状态。补偿顺序取决于资源依赖，不是机械倒序所有 RPC。Sagas 原论文讨论长事务拆分与补偿，[11] 但数据库 rollback 和今天跨服务补偿不同：退款会产生新的账本条目，通知无法收回，已出库货物要召回或售后。不要说 Saga 把过去的可见中间状态从所有用户记忆里抹掉。

English

Confirmed terminal non-shipment creates persistent refund/release intents. Payment refunds the original capture under a separate stable refund key. Inventory releases only that order’s still-HELD reservation, not stock already consumed for fulfillment. Red commands originate at Orchestrator; shipping errors return there first.

Compensations may advance independently if business permits; encode dependencies such as refund-before-close. Order follows resources, not mechanical reverse RPC order. The Sagas paper motivates long-transaction decomposition/compensation. [11] Modern refunds create new ledger entries; notifications and physical shipments cannot be rolled back from history.

UNKNOWN、补偿失败与并发观察UNKNOWN, failed compensation and concurrent observations

中文

扣款 timeout 不代表未扣，发货 timeout 不代表未发。UNKNOWN 先查同一 operation；查询也不可用时保留状态，不把持久 intent 过期就当“可以再来一次”。退款失败继续以同一 refund_key 重试，记录 attempt、原因、下一次时间和恢复预算；超过自动恢复范围进入有人负责的人工处理队列。补偿也可能 UNKNOWN，其恢复仍是查回执，不是删 run。

Saga 没有全局隔离：其他请求可能看见“已扣款、未发货”的中间状态。订单要显示 PROCESSING，取消与发货用期望版本和状态条件仲裁；取消赢后阻止后续释放，发货赢后转售后路径。协同 choreography 可用每步 Outbox 事件推进，少中心但依赖分散；编排把决策集中，仍需本地事务与可靠消息。选哪种都要回答“是谁在重启后找到未完成的那一步”。

English

Payment timeout does not mean unpaid, and shipping timeout does not mean unshipped. Query the same operation and retain UNKNOWN if unavailable. Never expire intent as permission to repeat. Retry refunds under the same key with attempts/reasons/schedule/budget; escalate beyond automatic recovery to an owned manual queue. Unknown compensation also requires receipt recovery.

Saga lacks global isolation: users may observe paid-but-unshipped. Show PROCESSING; arbitrate cancellation versus shipment with expected version/state. Cancellation blocks later release; shipment moves cancellation to after-sales handling. Choreography uses per-step Outbox events with dispersed dependencies; orchestration concentrates decisions but still needs transactions/reliable delivery. Both need restart recovery ownership.

中文

追问一：等 A 的 flush 后 A 和 Primary 同时永久丢失，能承诺什么？先说故障域超过合同，不能拿 B 的存在补保证。追问二：目录 rev=8 发布 ACK 丢了怎么办？查询持久目录，旧源已 fence，禁止盲目再分配写者。追问三：MQ 说 exactly-once，消费完邮件发了但数据库回滚呢？效果不在同一个事务域，仍需外部操作去重或接受明确重复合同。追问四：扣款与发货都 UNKNOWN，能直接退款关单吗？先恢复回执，状态未知不能当未发货。

English

Follow-ups: losing both Primary and acknowledged A exceeds the chosen fault contract; B alone does not repair it. Lost rev-8 publication ACK requires reading durable ownership, not reallocating. Exactly-once MQ does not atomize external email and DB rollback. Unknown capture/shipment requires receipts before refund/closure.
