English

  • Separate “the API accepted the intent” from “a provider accepted a send,” and show which reply actually returns to the caller.
  • Draw one write as validate, idempotency, a notification row plus recipient snapshot, an outbox row in the same database transaction, then a queue publish that lives outside that transaction and has a repair.
  • Show how the fan-out multiplier forces per-channel queues, a filter before the provider call, and tenant quotas, without treating a provider success code as proof the user saw the message.

中文

通知系统接的是「给这个用户发这条消息」的意图，不是一次已经完成的投递。我先假设 Push、Email、SMS 都要，事务和营销都有。调用方调用 POST /v1/notifications，我们校验、按租户幂等键落库，返回 202 Accepted。真正打到 FCM、APNs、邮件服务商和短信聚合商的动作在各通道队列之后。可靠性按 at-least-once 讲：队列会重投，供应商超时也无法把结果收成恰好一次。用户侧少看到重复，靠通知 id 和客户端去重。

第一版不做全球多活，不做打开率漏斗，不自建短信中心，也不把推荐排序塞进发送路径。短链那道题的难点是读多写少的定位；这道题的难点是一次意图在扇出之后变成很多次外部调用，而且外部调用的成功码并不等于人眼看见。打开率、多 Region 和计费对账留到面试官改约束再加。

English

A notification system accepts an intent to message a user. It does not complete delivery inside that request. Assume Push, Email, and SMS, and both transactional and marketing traffic. The caller hits POST /v1/notifications. We validate, persist under a tenant-scoped idempotency key, and return 202 Accepted. The calls to FCM, APNs, the mail provider, and the SMS aggregator happen after per-channel queues. Reliability is at-least-once: the queue redelivers, and a provider timeout does not collapse into exactly-once. Fewer duplicates on the device come from the notification id and from client-side suppression.

Version one skips global active-active, open-rate funnels, a homegrown SMS center, and any ranking model on the send path. A URL shortener is dominated by read-heavy lookup. This prompt is dominated by one intent turning into many external calls after fan-out, and by those calls’ success codes not meaning a person saw the message. Open analytics, multi-region, and billing reconciliation wait until the interviewer changes the constraint.

为什么先算扇出

每天一千万日活、每人十条推送，是白板假设，用来删掉不该第一天就画上的组件。一天是一亿条推送意图。100,000,000 / 86,400 = 1,157.4，所以平均大约 1157 QPS；日常再乘 5 倍峰值，大约 5787 QPS。事务验证码往往小得多：若每天二十万次，平均只有约 2.3 QPS。接口同步压力跟生产者调用次数走，不跟最终响了多少下手机走。把 1157 当成「API 必须同步打进 FCM」会把整张图画反。

扇出才是乘数。一次营销在接口上是一行活动。收件人一千万，假设平均 1.4 台仍登记的设备，推送侧是 1,400 万次供应商调用。塞进十分钟就是 14,000,000 / 600 ≈ 23,333 次/秒。旁边再写一个配额假设：单项目稳定打到推送供应商的速度按 2000 次/秒规划，这个数要换成真实配额，它不是 FCM 或 APNs 的公开额定值。14,000,000 / 2,000 = 7,000 秒，约 1.9 小时。活动完成时间因此是排水时间。请求线程里循环用户，会把这一次 HTTP 挂住接近两小时，调用方超时后再用同一把幂等键进来，看到的仍应是原来那一行，而不是第二场活动。

分区也从排水速度推出来。再假设在我们的消费方式下，一个分区舒适地处理大约 500 条/秒。这是规划假设，不是某版消息系统的官方基准。2,000 / 500 = 4，留一倍余量，推送主题大约 8 个分区就够排水，突发时队列变深，而不是把分区数乘到 23,333 / 500。分区键用收件人 user_id 的哈希。若用 tenant_id，一场活动只打满一个分区，其余分区空着，撞上同一分区的其他租户一起排队。租户之间的公平放在调用供应商之前的限流上，不放在「一个租户一个分区」上。

存储会改保留策略。0.5 KB × 1 亿条 ≈ 每天 50 GB；30 天 ≈ 1.5 TB 原始日志；三副本 ≈ 4.5 TB，索引和 Attempt 还要再涨。事务通知量小，主库可以留完整行、收件人快照和幂等键。营销不把每个设备的模板正文复制进在线事务库：活动一行，收件人状态用短记录，只有失败和未知结果才写详细 Attempt，超过重试窗口的日志退出主库。1.5 TB 解释的是保留边界，不是第一天就要上全球分片库。

Why fan-out comes first

Ten million DAU and ten pushes per person per day is a board assumption, used to delete components that do not belong on day one. That is 100 million push intents. 100,000,000 / 86,400 = 1,157.4, so about 1157 QPS on average, and about 5787 QPS at a 5× daily peak. Transactional one-time codes are usually much smaller: 200,000 a day is about 2.3 QPS. Synchronous API load follows how often producers call us, not how often a phone vibrates. Treating 1157 as “the API must call FCM inline” inverts the diagram.

Fan-out is the multiplier. One marketing call is one campaign row. Ten million recipients times an assumed 1.4 still-registered devices is 14 million provider calls. Forced into ten minutes, that is 14,000,000 / 600 ≈ 23,333 per second. Next to it, write a quota assumption: plan on 2,000 stable push calls per second per project, and replace that number with the real quota. It is not a published FCM or APNs rating. 14,000,000 / 2,000 = 7,000 seconds, about 1.9 hours. Campaign completion is drain time. Looping users on the request thread holds that HTTP call for nearly two hours. When the caller times out and retries the same idempotency key, it must see the original row, not a second campaign.

Partitions fall out of the drain rate. Assume one partition comfortably handles about 500 messages per second under our consumer model. That is a planning assumption, not an official benchmark for a given broker version. 2,000 / 500 = 4; with 2× headroom the push topic wants on the order of 8 partitions. During a burst the queue gets deep. The partition count does not jump to 23,333 / 500. The partition key is a hash of recipient user_id. A tenant_id key pins one campaign to one partition, leaves the others idle, and queues every other tenant that hashed onto that partition. Fairness across tenants sits in the rate limit before the provider call, not in “one partition per tenant.”

Storage changes retention. 0.5 KB × 100 million rows is about 50 GB per day, about 1.5 TB raw over 30 days, about 4.5 TB with three replicas, before indexes and attempt rows. Transactional notifications are small enough to keep a full row, recipient snapshot, and idempotency key in the primary store. Marketing does not copy every device’s rendered body into that store: one campaign row, short per-recipient status, detailed Attempt rows for failures and unknown outcomes, and logs that age out of the primary store after the retry window. The 1.5 TB figure sets a retention boundary. It does not require a global sharded database on day one.

接受延迟为什么不能包含供应商

Dean 和 Barroso 在《The Tail at Scale》里写过：一次请求若要等一大批子操作里最慢的那个，组件延迟的波动会被放大成服务延迟。他们举的例子是，单机 99 分位 1 秒、扇出 100 台时，多数请求都会撞上这 1 秒；同一篇文章也指出排队会放大波动，少量昂贵请求会堵住大量正在等待的短请求。[7] 通知接口如果同步等 Push、Email、SMS 三家都返回，接受延迟就由最慢的那一家决定，短信聚合商的一次抖动会写进登录接口的 p99。所以 202 的时钟停在本地提交，供应商调用离开这条请求。这也是营销队列和验证码队列分开的理由：批量发送是昂贵请求，验证码是有人正在等的短请求，不能共用一条先进先出。

这些 SLO 是为了把设计做下去而写在边上的假设。面试时要说「请按你们的产品改这几个数」。它们不是 FCM、APNs 或任何邮件服务商的合同，也不是某家公司的内部看板。验证码若 5 分钟过期，30 秒的 p95 还有意义；若重试预算长过验证码寿命，用户看见的是一条已经不能用的短信。worker 在调用供应商前读 not_after，过期就标记为不再发送并确认队列消息。调用方用 GET /v1/notifications/{id} 看到的是我们这边的状态，仍然不是用户已经阅读。

Why accept latency cannot include the providers

In “The Tail at Scale,” Dean and Barroso show that a request which waits for the slowest of many sub-operations magnifies component latency into service latency. In their example a 1-second 99th percentile on one machine becomes the common case at a fan-out of 100, and they also note that queueing amplifies variability so a few expensive requests block many short ones a user is waiting on. [7] If the notification API waited inline for Push, Email, and SMS, accept latency would be the slowest of the three, and one SMS hiccup would land in the login API’s p99. The 202 clock therefore stops at the local commit. Provider calls leave the request. The same point splits the marketing queue from the one-time-code queue: a bulk send is the expensive request, a code is the short request a person is waiting on, and they do not share one FIFO.

These SLOs are assumptions written down so the design can continue. Say in the interview, “change the numbers to match your product.” They are not a contract from FCM, APNs, or any mail provider, and they are not an internal dashboard. A 30-second p95 still means something if the code expires in five minutes. If the retry budget outlives the code, the user sees an SMS they can no longer type. The worker reads not_after before the provider call, marks the row as not to be sent, and acks the queue item. GET /v1/notifications/{id} shows our status. It still does not show that a person read the message.

高层架构：接受回程和投递回程不是同一条线High-level architecture: the accept reply and the delivery reply are different lines

正常路径

生产者带着模板、收件人和幂等键调用 API。API 不渲染一千万封邮件，也不在这条请求里打开到 FCM 的连接。它校验调用方身份和模板，在 Notif Store 提交通知行，然后返回 202 和 notification_id。outbox relay 是另一个进程，读出尚未发布的行，按通道放进 Push Q、Email Q 或 SMS Q。worker 从自己的队列取租约，调用边界外的供应商，把结果写成 Attempt。用户的手机不在这张图的回程上。没有一条从 FCM 指回生产者的同步箭头。

Walkthrough

The producer calls the API with a template, a recipient, and an idempotency key. The API does not render ten million emails and does not open an FCM connection on this request. It checks the caller and the template, commits the notification row in Notif Store, and returns 202 with a notification_id. The outbox relay is a different process. It reads unpublished rows and places them on Push Q, Email Q, or SMS Q. A worker leases from its own queue, calls a provider outside the boundary, and writes an Attempt. The user’s phone is not on the reply path. There is no synchronous arrow from FCM back to the producer.

为什么这样选

Notif Store 拥有「我们答应过要处理这条意图」。Redis 式缓存、队列和供应商都不拥有这句话。队列拥有的是「待发送工作」，丢了可以靠 outbox 再放进去；供应商拥有的是「这一次 HTTP 我收没收下」。把状态放在 Edge 或放在 worker 内存里，进程一重启就说不清 202 之后到底有没有这条通知。create 和 redirect 在短链里拆开，是因为读延迟和写校验不该互相绑架；这里拆开的是接受路径和投递路径，原因是两边的延迟预算差了两个数量级。小规模时 API、relay 和 worker 可以同仓库甚至同进程部署，依赖方向不变：投递失败不能把已经返回的 202 改成没发生过。

三条队列是估算逼出来的，不是消息队列课的目录。短信按条计费、失败模式和超时都跟推送不同。共用一个队列时，worker 被短信的慢调用占满，验证码推送的租约也过不来。邮件活动若按假设每秒 200 封来排，三百万封要 15,000 秒，大约 4.2 小时，比推送的 1.9 小时更长。活动「完成」必须按通道记，不能用一个全局 sent 位表示三家都结束了。

Why this shape

Notif Store owns “we committed to handle this intent.” A cache, a queue, and a provider do not own that sentence. The queue owns “work waiting to be sent,” and a lost item can be republished from the outbox. The provider owns “I accepted this HTTP call.” State in an edge cache or in worker memory disappears on restart, and nobody can say whether a notification exists after the 202. The accept path and the delivery path are split because their latency budgets differ by orders of magnitude. At small scale the API, relay, and workers can ship in one repository or even one process. The dependency direction stays: a delivery failure cannot unwind a 202 that already returned.

Three queues are forced by the estimate, not by a catalog of messaging features. SMS is billed per message and fails differently from push. On one shared queue, a slow SMS call occupies the worker and the one-time-code push never gets a lease. If email is planned at an assumed 200 messages per second, three million messages take 15,000 seconds, about 4.2 hours, longer than the 1.9-hour push drain. Campaign completion is recorded per channel. One global sent bit cannot mean all three providers are finished.

替代方案

小租户、每天几千条事务通知时，可以不先上独立消息集群：数据库里的 outbox 表加一个定时扫表的 relay 就够把工作交给几个进程。代价是扫表延迟和锁竞争，一场营销就会把这张表变成热点。范围若被收成「一个服务、只发验证码短信」，也可以由该服务在落库之后直接调用聚合商，但供应商耗时仍不能写进 202。

Alternative

For a small tenant and a few thousand transactional sends a day, a separate messaging cluster can wait: an outbox table and a relay that scans it are enough to hand work to a few processes. The cost is scan latency and lock contention, and one campaign turns that table into a hot spot. If the scope shrinks to one service and one SMS code, that service can call the aggregator after the commit, but provider time still stays out of the 202.

故障窗口

202 已经返回、relay 还没把消息放进队列的这段时间，用户什么也收不到，调用方却已经认为「系统收下了」。这不是丢意图：行在库里，状态仍是 accepted。用户能感知到的是验证码来晚了。若这段空白长过验证码的 not_after，晚到的短信本身也没用，所以过期检查在 worker，不在已经返回的 202 上。供应商整家不可用时，另一条通道的队列继续被消费；图上没有一条跨通道的同步边。

Failure window

Between the 202 and the relay’s publish, the user receives nothing while the caller already believes the system accepted the intent. The intent is not gone: the row is committed and still accepted. What the user notices is a late code. If that gap outlives not_after, a late SMS is useless too, so the expiry check sits in the worker, not inside the 202 that already left. When one provider is down, the other channels’ queues keep moving. The diagram has no synchronous edge across channels.

常见错误：把 FCM 成功响应里的 message name，或 APNs 的 HTTP 200，画成回到生产者的那条线。FCM HTTP v1 成功时返回的是消息资源名，表示 FCM 收下了这次发送；APNs 的状态表把 200 标成 Success，表示通知请求被 APNs 接受。[1][3] 两条都不是「用户看见了」。Common mistake: drawing the FCM message name, or APNs HTTP 200, as the arrow back to the producer. A successful FCM HTTP v1 response is a message resource name, which means FCM accepted the send. APNs lists HTTP 200 as Success, which means APNs accepted the notification request. [1][3] Neither one means the user saw it.

API、幂等和三张最小的表API, idempotency, and three minimum tables

中文

租户身份来自调用方认证，不来自请求体里可以随便填的 tenant_id。幂等键的作用域是这个租户加 Idempotency-Key。同一把键、同一份请求摘要，无论第一次还是超时后的重试，都返回同一个 notification_id 和同一份 202 表示。同一把键配上不同的正文摘要，返回 409，避免把「重试」和「换了一个收件人」混成一次成功。键的保留时间要长过调用方的最大重试窗口，这里按 24 小时假设。

状态码告诉调用方能不能原样重试。正文不合法是 400，未认证是 401，这个租户不能用该通道是 403，键冲突是 409，租户或用户配额不够是 429 并带 Retry-After，存储暂时不可用是 503。429 和 503 用同一把键重试。不要用 HTTP 200 加一段「failed」字符串表示所有结果，调用方的重试库认不出来。

English

Tenant identity comes from caller authentication, not from a tenant_id the body can set to anything. The idempotency scope is that tenant plus Idempotency-Key. The same key and the same request digest return the same notification_id and the same 202 representation on the first call and on a timeout retry. The same key with a different body digest returns 409, so a retry is not silently merged with “this is actually a different recipient.” Keep the key longer than the caller’s maximum retry window; the assumption here is 24 hours.

Status codes tell the caller whether to retry the same bytes. A malformed body is 400, missing authentication is 401, a channel the tenant may not use is 403, a key reused with a different body is 409, a tenant or user quota miss is 429 with Retry-After, and a store that is briefly unavailable is 503. Retry 429 and 503 with the same key. An HTTP 200 wrapped around a string that says failed is invisible to a normal retry library.

幂等键不是模板，也不是手机号

键代表一次发送意图。用户连点两次「获取验证码」如果共用一把键，第二次应该看到第一次的 notification_id，而不是两条短信。若产品希望两次点击就是两次码，客户端必须换键。用手机号或模板名当幂等键，会把同一用户下一小时的合法验证码合并掉。营销活动另有自己的键：活动 id 加收件人，在扇出展开时写入，而不是让活动创建请求本身携带一千万个键。

The key is not the template or the phone number

The key identifies one send intent. If the user taps “send code” twice and the client reuses the key, the second call returns the first notification_id instead of a second SMS. If the product wants two taps to mean two codes, the client must mint a new key. Using the phone number or the template name as the key collapses a legitimate code an hour later. A campaign gets its own keys at expansion time: campaign id plus recipient. The create-campaign request does not carry ten million keys.

快照里放什么

recipient_snapshot 在事务通知接受时写下 user_id、locale、IANA 时区和当时决定要走的通道。邮件和短信地址在展开或接受时抄进快照，重试仍打原来的地址：密码重置不该因为用户中途改了邮箱就跟到新地址。推送的 device token 不抄进这份快照。token 会失效、会轮换，发送当时再解析仍为 active 的设备。快照里保留的是「这条意图允许走 push」以及 user_id。偏好表是退订和静默时段的权威；发送前的缓存如果存在，只能是它的短寿命副本。

Attempt 跟 Notification 分开，因为一次意图会有多次外部调用：超时未知、明确拒绝、换一台设备、换一个短信聚合商。outcome 至少要能区分 accepted、rejected、timeout_unknown 和 rate_limited。provider_message_id 记下 FCM 返回的 message name 或 APNs 返回的 id，用来和事后投诉对上，不用来宣称用户已读。状态机停在 provider_accepted 就是停在「供应商收下了」。再往前的「已展示」「已点击」不在这张最小模型里，因为 Push 没有一条可靠的用户已读回执可以当权威。

What the snapshot holds

recipient_snapshot at transactional accept stores user_id, locale, the IANA time zone, and the channels chosen for this intent. Email and SMS addresses are copied at accept or expansion time, and retries keep that address: a password reset must not follow an email change made halfway through. Push device tokens are not copied into this snapshot. Tokens die and rotate, so send time resolves devices that are still active. The snapshot keeps “push is allowed for this intent” and the user_id. The preference table is the authority for unsubscribe and quiet hours. A cache on the send path, if it exists, is only a short-lived copy.

Attempt is separate from Notification because one intent makes several external calls: unknown timeout, explicit reject, another device, another SMS aggregator. outcome has to distinguish accepted, rejected, timeout_unknown, and rate_limited. provider_message_id stores the FCM message name or the APNs id so a later complaint can be joined. It does not store “the user read this.” The state machine stops at provider_accepted, which means the provider accepted the call. Displayed and clicked are outside this minimum model, because push has no reliable user-read receipt to treat as authority.

写路径：提交在库里，发布在事务外面Write path: the commit is in the database, the publish is outside it

正常路径

校验通过后，API 用认证得到的租户和 Idempotency-Key 查 Notification。未命中时开启一个数据库事务：插入通知行（含收件人快照、body_hash、status=accepted），并插入 published_at 为空的 outbox 行，然后提交。唯一约束挡住并发的第二次插入。提交成功才返回 202。relay 用 SELECT … FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED 领走未发布的 outbox，把消息放进对应通道队列，发布确认之后再把 published_at 写上，并把状态改成 queued。SKIP LOCKED 避免两个 relay 同时领走同一行；它盖不住「发布已经成功、更新 published_at 之前进程崩溃」。

Walkthrough

After validation, the API looks up Notification by the authenticated tenant and the Idempotency-Key. On a miss it opens one database transaction: insert the notification row (recipient snapshot, body_hash, status=accepted) and insert an outbox row with published_at null, then commit. The unique constraint stops a concurrent second insert. The 202 is returned only after that commit. The relay claims unpublished outbox rows with SELECT … FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED, publishes to the channel queue, and only then sets published_at and moves status to queued. SKIP LOCKED stops two relays from claiming the same row together. It does not cover a crash after the publish has succeeded and before published_at is updated.

为什么是 outbox，而不是跨库的一句承诺

通知行和队列消息住在两个系统里，没有一个参与者能同时提交两边。把「写库」和「入队」放进口头上的同一步，崩溃点仍然在两次网络往返之间。outbox 把可恢复的事实收进数据库事务：只要提交成功，就一定有一行等着被发布。relay 是补偿进程，允许重复发布。Kafka 的设计说明写得很直接：对 Kafka 以外的目的地，恰好一次通常需要对方系统配合，否则默认是至少一次。[6] 我们不把 FCM 拉进这个数据库事务。

Why an outbox

The notification row and the queue message live in two systems. No single participant commits both. Calling “write the database” and “enqueue” one step still leaves a crash between two network round trips. The outbox pulls the recoverable fact into the database transaction: if the commit succeeded, some row is waiting to be published. The relay is the repair process, and it is allowed to publish more than once. Kafka’s design document says exactly-once toward destinations outside Kafka generally requires those systems to cooperate; otherwise delivery is at-least-once by default. [6] FCM is not enrolled in this database transaction.

故障窗口

提交之前崩溃，调用方看到超时或 503，库里没有行，用同一把键重试会重新插入，不会发出半条通知。提交之后、响应丢失：行已经在，重试查到同一 id，relay 仍会发布，用户不会因为这次丢响应而收到两套意图。发布成功但 published_at 没写上：队列里已经有消息，relay 会再放一次。这是至少一次的重复消息，留给 worker 按 notification_id 处理，不在 API 里假装没发生。验证码若在这个窗口里超过 not_after，worker 不再调用供应商，用户看到的是「没收到码」，登录页应允许再要一次，而不是把过期码补发出去。

Failure window

A crash before commit looks like a timeout or 503. Nothing is stored, and a retry with the same key inserts once. There is no half-sent notification. A lost response after commit finds the same id on retry, and the relay still publishes. The user does not get two intents because one HTTP response disappeared. A successful publish whose published_at update is lost leaves a message already on the queue, and the relay publishes again. That duplicate is at-least-once, handled later by notification_id in the worker, not denied inside the API. If a one-time code passes not_after inside this window, the worker does not call the provider. The user sees “no code arrived.” The login screen should offer another code instead of sending the expired one.

常见错误：先调用供应商，成功后再插通知行。供应商已经收下、数据库插入失败时，重试会再打一次，而且没有任何一行可以用来对账。另一个常见替代是用缓存 SETNX 当幂等，再写数据库。两处成功条件一分开，就会出现「键占上了但行没有」或「行在而键没有」。幂等约束和通知行放在同一张表的唯一索引里，就是为了少一次这种裂缝。缓存去重可以做业务层的短窗口防连点，不能代替这次提交。Common mistake: call the provider first and insert the row only after success. If the provider accepted the call and the insert then fails, the retry sends again and there is no row to reconcile against. Another frequent alternative is a cache SETNX as the idempotency lock, followed by a database insert. Split success conditions produce “the key is taken but no row exists” or “the row exists and the key does not.” The idempotency constraint lives in the same unique index as the notification row so that crack is smaller. A cache can still suppress double-clicks in a short business window. It does not replace this commit.

投递：租约、尝试记录，以及超时和明确拒绝Delivery: the lease, the attempt, and timeout versus an explicit reject

正常路径

Push worker 从 Push Q 领取一条消息，可见性 30 秒。它按 user_id 解析仍为 active 的设备，用 FCM HTTP v1 发送：Authorization: Bearer 短时 OAuth 访问令牌，请求打到 POST /v1/projects/{project}/messages:send。成功时响应体是消息资源名，例如 projects/…/messages/…。worker 把这个名字写进 Attempt，outcome 记为 accepted，然后才 ack。APNs 走它自己的 HTTP/2 接口时，200 同样只表示 APNs 接受了请求。[1][3] 邮件侧对应的是 SMTP 在 DATA 结束时的 250：接收方从此承担投递或事后退信的责任，不是收件人已经阅读。[4]

Walkthrough

The push worker leases one item from Push Q with 30 seconds of visibility. It resolves devices still marked active for the user and sends with FCM HTTP v1: an Authorization: Bearer short-lived OAuth access token, posted to POST /v1/projects/{project}/messages:send. On success the body is a message resource name, such as projects/…/messages/…. The worker stores that name on the Attempt with outcome accepted, and only then acks. When APNs is called on its own HTTP/2 interface, HTTP 200 likewise means APNs accepted the request. [1][3] On email, SMTP’s 250 after the end of DATA means the receiver accepts responsibility to deliver or to report failure later. It does not mean the person read the mail. [4]

超时和明确拒绝

5 秒内没有任何 HTTP 响应，Attempt 记 timeout_unknown，不把通知标成已送达，也不把 token 标成失效。租约到期或 worker 主动放回后，消息会再被投递。这次重试可能和一次其实已经到达供应商的请求叠在一起，所以未知结果的重试允许用户看到两条。明确拒绝相反。FCM 的 UNREGISTERED 是 HTTP 404，文档说明应用实例已从 FCM 注销，token 不再有效，应当从服务端删掉并停止使用。[2] APNs 的 410 表示该 device token 对这个 topic 已不再活跃，文档要求不要再向这枚 token 推，除非应用以后又登记了同一枚；同一页写明 5xx 可以在大约 15 分钟后退避重试，Unregistered 不要重试，429 则延迟后再试。[3] 把 410 当成「用户卸载了所以去发短信」会误伤仍安装着、只是 token 轮换过的设备。

Timeout versus an explicit reject

No HTTP response within 5 seconds records timeout_unknown. The notification is not marked delivered, and the token is not marked dead. When the lease expires or the worker releases the item, it is delivered again. That retry can overlap a request that actually reached the provider, so an unknown retry is allowed to show up twice. An explicit reject is the other case. FCM UNREGISTERED is HTTP 404. The error-code document says the app instance was unregistered, the token is no longer valid, and the server should delete it and stop using it. [2] APNs 410 means the device token is no longer active for the topic. The response document says not to keep pushing that token unless the app registers it again; it also says 5xx responses may be retried after about 15 minutes with backoff, Unregistered is not retried, and 429 is retried after a delay. [3] Treating 410 as “uninstalled, so send an SMS instead” misfires on a device that is still installed and only rotated its token.

至少一次的重复从哪来

Kafka 文档描述的消费者窗口在这里原样出现：处理完成之后、确认偏移之前崩溃，下一次会再次拿到这条消息。[6] 对通知来说，「处理」包含一次无法回滚的供应商调用。worker 若在写下 accepted 之前崩溃，重试看不到上一次成功，就会再 POST 一次。载荷里带上 notification_id，客户端对已展示过的 id 直接丢掉。供应商的 collapse key 只能替换尚未投递的挂起通知，收不回已经出现在通知栏里的那一条。所以重复是用户可见的失败模式，缓解放在客户端和尝试记录上，不放在「我们保证恰好一次」这句话上。

Where the at-least-once duplicate comes from

The consumer window in the Kafka documentation shows up unchanged: crash after processing and before the offset is committed, and the next worker receives the message again. [6] For notifications, “processing” includes a provider call that cannot be rolled back. If the worker crashes before it records accepted, the retry does not see the earlier success and POSTs again. The payload carries notification_id, and the client drops an id it has already displayed. A provider collapse key replaces a notification that is still pending. It does not recall one already sitting in the notification tray. A duplicate is therefore a user-visible failure mode. The mitigation is the client and the attempt log, not the sentence “we guarantee exactly once.”

常见错误：把供应商 200、FCM 的 message name 或 SMTP 250 写成用户已读。三份文档说的都是接受责任或接受这次发送。[1][3][4] 其后的退信、未展示、用户把手机扣在桌上，都不会改写这个状态码。Common mistake: writing a provider 200, the FCM message name, or SMTP 250 down as “the user read it.” All three documents describe acceptance of responsibility or acceptance of this send. [1][3][4] A later bounce, a notification that was never displayed, or a phone face-down on a table does not rewrite that status code.

扇出：过滤和限流画在供应商调用之前Fan-out: the filter and the rate limit sit before the provider call

正常路径

活动 202 之后，expander 按每批 1000 个 user_id 读取受众，写成 1 万个展开任务，而不是 1 万次供应商调用。每个收件人先过过滤器：营销必须 marketing_opt_in 为真，并且用用户的 IANA 时区判断现在是否落在 quiet_start 到 quiet_end。落在窗口内就把 not_before 设成该时区的窗口结束，任务留在延迟队列，到点再进入限流。通过过滤的任务再看两只桶：这个用户今天的营销条数，以及这个租户当前允许占用的排水速度。两只桶都有余量，才解析设备或地址并调用供应商。验证码走图上方的旁路，不读营销开关，也不等静默时段结束。

Walkthrough

After the campaign 202, the expander reads the audience in batches of 1,000 user ids and writes 10,000 expansion tasks, not 10,000 provider calls. Each recipient hits the filter first: marketing requires marketing_opt_in, and the user’s IANA time zone decides whether now sits between quiet_start and quiet_end. Inside the window, not_before becomes the end of that window in that zone, and the task waits on the delay queue until it may enter the rate limit. A task that passes the filter then checks two buckets: how many marketing messages this user already has today, and how much drain speed this tenant may occupy right now. Only when both buckets have room does the worker resolve a device or address and call the provider. A one-time code takes the bypass above the filter. It does not read the marketing switch and it does not wait for quiet hours to end.

为什么缓存只放在这里

一次活动要读一千万次偏好。若每次都打主库，展开读会比接口写高出几个数量级。缓存按 user_id + channel 放 60 秒副本，TTL 加抖动。退订提交后立刻删键，所以「已经拒绝」不会在缓存里多留。缓存里的「仍同意」最多旧 60 秒：删键丢失时，用户可能再收到一条营销，然后停止。这个窗口要说出来。权威是 Preference 表。验证码路径不读这层缓存。

Why a cache exists only here

One campaign reads preferences ten million times. Hitting the primary on every read puts expansion far above the API write. A cache holds a 60-second copy keyed by user_id + channel, with jitter on the TTL. Unsubscribe deletes that key immediately, so a recorded refusal does not linger. An “opted in” entry can be 60 seconds old: if the delete is lost, the user may receive one more marketing message and then stop. Say that window out loud. The Preference table is the authority. The one-time-code path does not read this cache.

两层限流，两种用户可见结果

入口限流发生在 202 之前：租户每小时能创建的活动次数、验证码每 10 分钟每个用户的次数，超了就 429，调用方可以按 Retry-After 再试。排水限流发生在 202 之后：活动已经被接受，worker 仍按每秒配额把这个租户的营销让出一部分容量给别人。热门租户看见的是活动超过 1.9 小时还没排完，其他租户的验证码仍走独立队列。分区按收件人打散；租户公平靠这道限流，不靠一个租户独占一个分区。

Two rate limits, two visible outcomes

The accept-time limit happens before the 202: how many campaigns a tenant may create per hour, and how many one-time codes a user may request per 10 minutes. Over the cap, the API returns 429 and the caller can retry after Retry-After. The drain-time limit happens after the 202: the campaign is already accepted, and workers still give away part of the per-second quota so other tenants move. The hot tenant sees a campaign that runs past the 1.9-hour plan. Other tenants’ codes still use their own queue. Partitions spread by recipient. Tenant fairness is this limit, not one partition owned by one tenant.

常见错误：先调用供应商，再发现用户已退订或正处于静默时段。那一次调用已经花钱、已经打扰，过滤器形同事后日志。另一个错误是用机房当地时间判断静默时段。同一场活动里，UTC+8 的用户和 UTC−7 的用户会有人在凌晨被叫醒。时区存在 Preference.tz，在过滤器里按人计算 not_before。Common mistake: call the provider and only then notice that the user unsubscribed or is inside quiet hours. That call has already cost money and already disturbed someone. The filter becomes a log written after the fact. Another mistake is judging quiet hours in the datacenter’s local time. Inside one campaign, a user in UTC+8 and a user in UTC−7 will include someone woken at 3 a.m. The zone lives in Preference.tz, and the filter computes not_before per person.

故障：一条通道倒下时，其他通道还在走Failure: when one channel falls over, the others keep moving

四种用户能感知的失败Four failures a person can actually notice

1 · 供应商不可用

短信聚合商连续 5xx 或超时。SMS worker 按退避重试，次数用尽后进入 SMS DLQ 并告警；备用聚合商只在这条通道上切换。用户若同时要了短信和推送，推送仍会到，两份内容是同一条验证码。用户若只走短信，且重试超过 not_after，手机上不会出现过期码，登录页仍停在「没收到」。推送没有第二家 APNs 可接替，降级是推迟和死信，不是自动改道去短信。

1 · Provider unavailable

The SMS aggregator returns 5xx or times out. The SMS worker retries with backoff and, after the budget, parks the item on the SMS DLQ and alerts. A backup aggregator switches only on this channel. A user who asked for both SMS and push still gets the push, with the same code in both. A user who asked only for SMS, once retries pass not_after, does not receive an expired code. The login screen stays on “nothing arrived.” Push has no second APNs to take over, so it degrades by delay and dead letter rather than by an automatic detour into SMS.

2 · 热门租户或热门 token

一个租户的千万级活动会把排水打满。用户侧的差别是：这家店的顾客等到上午晚些时候才看到促销，其他租户的验证码仍在几秒到几十秒内到达，因为 OTP Q 保留了一部分容量。若热的是某一个 device token，APNs 会对该 token 返回 429。[3] 退避应落在这枚 token 上。把整个租户停掉，会让一次客户端 bug 连点变成全员发不出验证码。入口处的 429 则是另一种可见性：活动创建者立刻知道这次提交被拒绝，而不是以为 202 已经代表一千万人开始收到。

2 · Hot tenant or hot token

One tenant’s ten-million campaign fills the drain. The visible difference is that this shop’s customers see the promotion later in the morning, while other tenants’ codes still arrive in seconds to tens of seconds, because OTP Q keeps a reserved share of capacity. If the hot object is a single device token, APNs returns 429 for that token. [3] Backoff belongs on that token. Stopping the whole tenant turns one buggy client into “nobody can get a code.” A 429 at accept is a different kind of visibility: the campaign creator learns immediately that the submit was refused, instead of believing a 202 means ten million people have started to receive it.

3 · 重复展示

用户看见两条一模一样的推送。原因是第一次调用已经拿到 FCM 的 message name 或 APNs 的 200，worker 在写 Attempt 和 ack 之前退出，队列按至少一次把消息再交给另一个 worker。[6] 第二条推送的载荷仍带同一个 notification_id。客户端展示过就丢弃。服务端无法把已经出现在通知栏里的那一条撤回。邮件更明显：两封信都会进邮箱。把重复说成「不可能」会让面试官追问崩溃点；把重复说成「所以不要重试」会让超时变成丢失。

3 · Duplicate display

The user sees two identical pushes. The first call already received an FCM message name or an APNs 200, the worker died before the Attempt and the ack, and the queue handed the message to another worker under at-least-once. [6] The second payload still carries the same notification_id. The client drops it if that id was already shown. The server cannot recall a notification already sitting in the tray. Email makes the duplicate more obvious: both messages land in the mailbox. Saying duplicates are impossible invites the crash-point question. Saying “so do not retry” turns timeouts into loss.

4 · 已经 202，人还没收到

调用方拿到 202 之后向用户展示「验证码已发送」。此时行在库里，outbox 可能还没发布，或者供应商正在退避。用户盯着空短信列表。若空白短于 not_after，码还会到；若 relay 或供应商停得更久，worker 放弃发送，GET /v1/notifications/{id} 变成失败或过期，客户端应把按钮恢复成「重新获取」，而不是继续转圈。静默时段的营销是另一种「收下了但还没发」：用户白天看到促销，凌晨不会被叫醒。用机房时区算窗口时，这个承诺会破，有人仍会在当地凌晨收到。

4 · 202 already returned, nothing in the hand yet

The caller shows “code sent” after the 202. The row is in the store, the outbox may still be unpublished, or the provider may be in backoff. The user stares at an empty SMS list. If the gap is shorter than not_after, the code still arrives. If the relay or the provider stays down longer, the worker gives up, GET /v1/notifications/{id} becomes failed or expired, and the client should turn the button back into “send again” instead of spinning. Marketing inside quiet hours is the other form of “accepted but not sent”: the user sees the promotion in the daytime and is not woken at night. Computing that window in the datacenter’s zone breaks the promise, and someone still gets it at 3 a.m. local time.

常见错误：用一个队列服务所有通道，再靠消费者自己 if/else 区分短信和推送。慢短信占住分区上的消费进度之后，验证码推送没有别的分区可去。Dean 和 Barroso 把这种情况写成头阻塞：少量昂贵请求把大量正在等待的短请求拖慢，并用服务等级把「有人正在等」的工作和批量工作分开。[7] 通道队列和 OTP 队列就是这个分开，不是消息系统的名词表。Common mistake: one queue for every channel, with the consumer branching in if/else between SMS and push. Once a slow SMS holds the consumer’s progress on a partition, a one-time-code push has no other partition to go to. Dean and Barroso describe this as head-of-line blocking: a few expensive requests slow many short ones a user is waiting on, and they separate that interactive work from batch work with service classes. [7] Channel queues and the OTP queue are that separation, not a glossary of messaging products.

退订和伪造：信任边界上只有一扇窄门Unsubscribe and spoofing: one narrow door on the trust boundary

正常路径

营销邮件带两个头：List-Unsubscribe 里有一个 HTTPS URI，List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click。RFC 8058 要求这封信的 DKIM 签名至少盖住这两个头，这样中转途中改掉退订地址的信不会被当成有效的一键退订；邮箱服务商用 POST 打这个 URI，正文是 List-Unsubscribe=One-Click，并且这次 POST 不依赖 cookie 或先前的网页登录。[5][8] URI 的不透明部分是服务端保存的随机能力，或是对 user、channel、list、expiry 的签名。顺序数字 id 不充当这个能力。入口校验令牌，把 Preference 写成 opt-out，删掉 60 秒缓存键，提交成功后再返回 202。重放同一次 POST 仍然是 opt-out，结果不变。

Walkthrough

A marketing message carries two headers: an HTTPS URI in List-Unsubscribe, and List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click. RFC 8058 requires the message’s DKIM signature to cover at least those two headers, so a relay that rewrites the unsubscribe address is not treated as a valid one-click unsubscribe. The mailbox provider POSTs to that URI with the body List-Unsubscribe=One-Click, and that POST does not depend on a cookie or a previous web login. [5][8] The opaque part of the URI is a random capability stored on the server, or a signature over user, channel, list, and expiry. A sequential numeric id is not that capability. The edge checks the token, writes Preference to opt-out, deletes the 60-second cache key, and returns 202 only after the commit. Replaying the same POST stays an opt-out. The result does not change.

为什么是能力令牌，而不是登录页

一键退订的调用方是邮箱服务商的服务器，不是坐在浏览器里的用户。要求登录会让这次 POST 失败。代价是拿到链接的人可以退订该用户的营销，所以令牌要够长、要过期，并且只绑定这一条列表，不能兼作密码重置凭证。验证码和支付通知不走这扇门。系统推送权限被关掉时，下一次发送看到的是 token 失效，更新的是设备表，不是营销开关。

Why a capability, not a login page

The caller of one-click unsubscribe is the mailbox provider’s server, not a person in a browser. Requiring a login makes that POST fail. Anyone who holds the link can unsubscribe that user’s marketing, so the token is long, it expires, and it is bound to this list only. It is not also a password-reset credential. One-time codes and payment notices do not use this door. When the operating system push permission is turned off, the next send sees a dead token and updates the device table, not the marketing switch.

伪造和内部路径

攻击者可以发出看起来来自我们域名的信，并写上退订头。收件方是否采信，取决于 DKIM 是否签住这些头；没被盖住的头可以被中途改写，所以 RFC 8058 把签名覆盖写成必须。[5][8] 我们的入口仍校验令牌，不因为 From 像自己就执行。营销 opt-out 之后，过滤器在供应商调用前停掉营销；同一用户的验证码仍走旁路。把退订做成删除用户，会把登录码一起删掉。

Spoofing and the internal path

An attacker can send mail that looks like our domain and can attach an unsubscribe header. Receivers trust it only when DKIM covers those headers. Uncovered headers can be rewritten in transit, which is why RFC 8058 makes that coverage mandatory. [5][8] Our edge still checks the token and does not act just because From looks like us. After a marketing opt-out, the filter stops marketing before the provider call. The same user’s one-time code still takes the bypass. Turning unsubscribe into “delete the user” also deletes login codes.

追问：从新约束往下改Follow-ups: change the design from a new constraint

1 · 「Kafka 事务不是已经恰好一次了吗？」

Kafka 的恰好一次覆盖的是 Kafka 主题之间的读写：幂等生产者避免重试在日志里写出第二条，事务可以把消费偏移和下游 Kafka 写入放在一起。[6] 文档同时写明，到其他目的地的恰好一次需要那个目的地配合。FCM、APNs 和 SMTP 都不在这事务里。把 transactional producer 打开，只能让 outbox relay 少往队列里放重复消息；供应商那一侧的重复窗口还在。

1 · “Don’t Kafka transactions already give exactly-once?”

Kafka’s exactly-once covers reads and writes among Kafka topics: the idempotent producer avoids a second log entry on retry, and a transaction can commit the consumer offset together with a downstream Kafka write. [6] The same document says exactly-once toward other destinations needs those destinations to cooperate. FCM, APNs, and SMTP are not in that transaction. Turning on the transactional producer only stops the outbox relay from putting extra copies on the queue. The duplicate window on the provider side remains.

2 · 「用户已读怎么做？」

这版不做已读权威。推送的 200 和邮件的 250 都停在供应商接受。若产品要「用户打开了邮件」，那是另一条不可靠的像素或链接回调，容易被预取、被隐私功能挡住，不能写进投递 SLO。支付或验证码若必须确认「人用过这码」，确认点在业务系统的校验接口，不在通知状态机的下一格。

2 · “How do we know the user read it?”

This version has no read authority. Push 200 and mail 250 stop at provider acceptance. If the product wants “the user opened the mail,” that is a separate, unreliable pixel or link callback, easily defeated by prefetch and privacy features, and it does not belong in the delivery SLO. If a payment or a code must confirm that a person used it, the confirmation is the business system’s verify call, not the next state in the notification machine.

3 · 「多 Region 呢？」

先问要的是全球低延迟接受，还是每个 Region 都能独立发出并退订。前者可以单 Region 写入，队列和发送靠近供应商。后者立刻要处理幂等键冲突、偏好复制延迟，以及「退订在 A 区已提交、B 区的 worker 还在用旧缓存」。在没证明单 Region 的 1.9 小时排水或 200 毫秒接受达不到目标之前，不把三套存储画上第一张图。

3 · “What about multiple regions?”

Ask whether the goal is low-latency accept worldwide, or the ability for every region to send and unsubscribe on its own. The first can write in one region and place queues and senders near the providers. The second immediately needs idempotency conflicts, preference replication lag, and the case “unsubscribe committed in region A while a worker in region B still holds the old cache.” Until a single region misses the 1.9-hour drain or the 200-millisecond accept target, three stores do not go on the first diagram.

4 · 「模板在哪渲染？」

在过滤器之后、供应商调用之前，由 worker 按快照里的 locale 渲染。提前渲染会给已被退订或仍在静默时段的人生成正文，白做一千万次模板工作，也把正文提前写进那 1.5 TB 的账。密钥和一次性验证码只存在于发送当时的内存和供应商请求里，不进营销活动的长期日志。

4 · “Where is the template rendered?”

After the filter and before the provider call, the worker renders with the locale in the snapshot. Rendering earlier builds bodies for people who are unsubscribed or still inside quiet hours, wastes ten million template expansions, and writes those bodies into the 1.5 TB account. Secrets and one-time codes exist only in memory at send time and in the provider request. They do not enter the long-lived campaign log.

中文

接受要快，就把供应商赶出 202 的时钟，并用 outbox 补上「行已提交、队列还没有」的洞。投递要少丢，就接受至少一次，把重复留给 notification_id 和客户端。少打扰，就把偏好、静默时段、用户额度和租户额度画在供应商前面，并给验证码单独的队列。一千万用户的活动按假设要排大约 1.9 小时，这是配额下的完成时间，不是接口的 p99。最后 20 秒就说这三句，再加上：FCM 的 message name、APNs 的 200、SMTP 的 250，都还不是用户看见。

English

To keep accept fast, keep providers off the 202 clock and use the outbox to cover “row committed, queue empty.” To lose fewer sends, accept at-least-once and leave duplicates to notification_id and the client. To disturb fewer people, draw preference, quiet hours, the user cap, and the tenant cap in front of the provider, and give one-time codes their own queue. A ten-million-person campaign takes about 1.9 hours under the stated quota. That is completion time, not the API’s p99. The last twenty seconds are those three sentences, plus this: the FCM message name, APNs 200, and SMTP 250 are still not “the user saw it.”
