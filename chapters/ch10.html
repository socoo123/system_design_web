设计通知系统Design a notification system

主线约 30 分钟 + 深挖约 15 分钟 · 前置：Ch01、Ch03 · 6 组 / 31 帧演进图~30 min core + ~15 min deep dives · after Ch01 and Ch03 · 6 decks / 31 evolution frames

本章核心：接得住、发得出、解释得清结果Core decisions: accept durably, send safely, explain outcomes

  • 返回 202 前，先把任务保存下来：通知意图、各端点的投递任务和待发布消息（outbox）在同一个数据库事务中提交，再异步调用供应商。202 只表示本地已接受。
  • 按端点恢复，避免重发已知成功的任务：每个端点任务使用稳定的 delivery_id，领取时记录代次，重试计划也写入数据库。如果供应商是否接受仍未知，恢复时就仍有重复发送的风险。
  • 每次发送前都检查约束：先检查用户偏好、有效期、发送频次和供应商配额，再调用供应商。供应商接受、投递回执、设备展示和用户已读要分别记录。
  • 202 means durable local acceptance: commit intent, endpoint tasks, and outbox before asynchronous provider calls.
  • Recover by endpoint: stable delivery_id, claim epochs, and durable retries suppress known repeats while retaining provider ambiguity.
  • Gate before sending: check consent, expiry, frequency, and provider quota; model acceptance, receipts, display, and reading separately.

范围与估算：先算端点数量，再谈扩容Scope and sizing: count endpoints before scaling

先确认通知需要走推送（Push）、邮件（Email）还是短信（SMS），属于事务通知还是营销通知，以及是单次发送还是批量活动。本例从单地域开始：API 在同一个事务中保存通知意图和后续工作，再返回 202；消费程序（worker）按通道和优先级分配独立资源，每次发送前先过滤、再限流。处理过程按至少一次设计：端点状态能让 worker 跳过已知成功的投递；结果未知时，则由产品策略决定重试还是停止。供应商收下请求，仍不表示用户看到了通知。

Clarify channels, transactional versus marketing, and individual versus campaign sends. Start in one region: commit intent and durable work before 202, isolate channel and priority workers, and gate before sending. Use at-least-once processing; endpoint state skips known success, while product policy chooses retry or stop for unknown outcomes. Provider acceptance does not prove display.

以下数字都是教学假设。按这些假设，日常端点调用的峰值约为每秒 8,102 次；一场活动即使独占每秒 2,000 次的发送配额，也至少需要 1.94 小时。因此，API 应先保存意图，不能在请求线程中循环发送给所有收件人。增加 worker 可以增加处理资源，却不会自动提高供应商配额；这些假设也都需要用实际限额和压测结果替换。

All figures are teaching assumptions, neither vendor quotas nor benchmarks. Daily endpoint peak is about 8,102/s; a campaign needs at least 1.94 hours even at exclusive 2,000/s capacity. Do not loop through recipients in the request thread; adding workers does not raise quota.

按每页 1,000 人展开，1,000 万受众需要 1 万个稳定的分页任务；一页还会生成多条端点消息。若每个分区的消费预算为每秒 500 条，要用满每秒 2,000 次的发送能力，至少需要 4 个消费分区，本例先配 8 个留出余量。这只能说明消费侧的配置，消息队列（broker）能否接住每秒 8,102 条的发布峰值，仍要单独测量。邮件按每秒 200 封计算，300 万封需要 15,000 秒，约 4.17 小时，所以完成时间也要按通道分别统计。

At 1,000 users/page, 10M recipients need 10K stable page tasks, distinct from endpoint messages. A 500/s consumer budget needs four partitions for 2,000/s; eight provide headroom. This does not prove 8,102/s broker ingestion, which needs measurement. At 200 emails/s, 3M emails take 15,000 s≈4.17 h; track completion per channel.

《The Tail at Scale》用独立性近似 1−0.99100≈63.4% 说明同步扇出会放大尾延迟；这个算式用于理解现象，不能直接预测本题三个供应商的延迟。[7]

本地接受的目标是 99.9% 可用性和 p99 < 200 ms，计时只到数据库提交完成。事务通知从返回 202 到健康供应商接受的 p95 < 30 s，要单独统计，同时记录故障期间的过期率。验证码既要预留消费资源，也要预留供应商配额；每秒 200 次的预留额度被用满时，营销只剩每秒 1,800 次，活动耗时就要重新计算。

The independence approximation 1−0.99100≈63.4% in “The Tail at Scale” illustrates fan-out tail amplification, not a forecast for three providers. [7] The 99.9%, p99<200 ms acceptance target ends at local commit. Separately measure p95<30 s to healthy-provider acceptance and outage expiry. Reserve OTP consumers and quota; when its 200/s reservation is used, marketing has 1,800/s and cannot claim 1.94 hours.

整体架构：202 返回生产者，发送结果返回消费程序Architecture: 202 to the producer; provider outcomes to workers

哪些通知还需要处理，以通知数据库（Notif Store）中的持久记录为准。队列负责缓冲并提醒 worker 领取任务；供应商是否接受，则以供应商给出的外部证据为准。第一组图的最后一帧是整体方案；端点任务（Delivery）和单次尝试（Attempt）怎样保存，见第三组图；发送前的检查，见第四组图。

Notif Store is the durable work authority. Queues provide wake-ups and buffering; providers own external acceptance. Use deck 1’s final frame as the overview, deck 3 for persistence, and deck 4 for gates.

API 校验租户和模板后，在同一个事务中写入通知、端点任务与 outbox；数据库确认提交后，才返回 202 和通知编号 notification_id。

relay 领取已经提交、且已到发布时间的 outbox 行，把消息交给 broker，等到耐久确认后再标记已发布。标记时要核对发布代次 publish_epoch，才能写入 published_at，防止旧 relay 覆盖新领取者的状态。不过，如果 relay 收到确认后、写标记前崩溃，恢复后仍可能重复发布。[11]

The API validates tenant and template, co-commits intent, endpoint work, and outbox, then returns 202 and notification_id after DB confirmation. Relay claims due outbox rows, waits for durable broker acknowledgment, then conditionally marks published_at using publish_epoch. Stale relays cannot overwrite successors; crashing after acknowledgment but before marking can republish. [11]

推送、邮件和短信分别使用消费池，各通道内部再隔离事务通知与营销通知。验证码还要预留供应商配额，否则即使队列独立，共享的发送额度也可能被营销耗尽。

如果每天只发几千条通知，可以先轮询 Delivery 表，暂不引入 broker。但数据库中的任务领取、到期索引和恢复扫描仍要保留，内存线程池无法在进程崩溃后恢复这些工作。

Give channels separate pools, then isolate transactional and marketing work within each. Reserve OTP quota too; queues alone do not protect an exhausted shared outlet. At a few thousand daily sends, polling Delivery can replace a broker; retain claims, due-time indexes, and repair scans. An in-memory thread pool cannot replace them.

API 与状态：分别记录意图、端点、尝试和回执API and state: separate intent, endpoint, attempt, and receipt

认证结果决定租户 tenant_id，服务端允许使用的模板决定优先级。调用方不能自行把营销通知标成验证码，借此绕过营销规则。

幂等键的作用范围是 (tenant_id, Idempotency-Key)。同一租户用同一键提交相同摘要时，API 返回原始的 202 回执；摘要不同则返回 409。客户端超时或收到 503 后，仍用原键重试。API 完成语法与权限校验后，要先查已有回执，再检查新请求的有效期；这样，即使通知现在已经过期，客户端仍能恢复当初成功接受的结果。

Authentication determines tenant_id; authorized server templates determine priority. Callers cannot label themselves OTP to bypass policy. Scope idempotency to (tenant_id, Idempotency-Key): replay the immutable 202 for matching content and return 409 otherwise. Retry timeout or 503 with the same key. After parsing and authorization, read the receipt before validating new-request expiry, preserving recovery of old success.

返回字段 requested_channels 表示系统接受了哪些通道的发送意图；是否真正发出，要看后续处理结果。正文非法返回 400，未认证返回 401，越权返回 403，键冲突返回 409，入口配额不足返回带 Retry-After 的 429，存储错误返回 503。调用方通过鉴权后，可用 GET /v1/notifications/{id} 查询当前汇总状态；这不会改写创建时的回执。

新的一次验证码通知必须使用新键，不能拿手机号或模板名代替通知意图的标识。幂等键至少保留 24 小时，而且要长于重试窗口；本例让它随通知一起保留 30 天，因此不能假定 24 小时后就可复用。

requested_channels describes intent, not sends. Return 400 for malformed input, 401 for missing authentication, 403 for unauthorized use, 409 for key conflict, 429 with Retry-After for admission quota, and 503 for storage errors. Authorized GET /v1/notifications/{id} reads the current aggregate without changing the creation receipt. A new OTP needs a new key; phone/template names are not intent keys. Retain keys beyond retries, at least 24 hours; this example retains them for 30 days with the intent and does not promise reuse after 24 hours.

单次通知在接受时固定一小组端点；批量活动则引用已经持久保存的受众快照，在展开每位收件人时固定其端点。邮件和短信使用当时保存的地址快照，之后修改用户资料不会改变这些任务；推送则在每次发送时，查询所选 endpoint_id 当前的 token 和版本。

验证码的加密载荷可以随通知保存在同一数据库中。如果正文或受众快照保存在外部对象存储中，必须先确认对象已持久保存，再在数据库中提交引用。对象写入失败就不接受通知；如果对象写入成功而数据库随后回滚，遗留的孤立对象可以稍后清理。

Freeze a small endpoint set at individual acceptance. Campaigns reference already durable audience snapshots and freeze recipient endpoints during expansion. Email/SMS snapshots do not follow profile edits; resolve current push token/version for the selected endpoint. Encrypted OTP content can share the DB transaction. External payload/snapshot references must be durable before acceptance; object-write failure prevents acceptance, and later DB rollback leaves cleanable orphans.

查询结果分别列出调度状态和外部观察。例如，任务已处于 failed 或 expired，旧尝试的 accepted 证据才迟到，此时仍要显示“调度已停止；供应商已接受”。查询要呈现这项新证据，同时保持调度已经停止。

接收回调时，先验证供应商身份，再关联到原来的 Attempt。重复或乱序回执不能按到达先后直接覆盖事实。某个通道没有可靠回执，或回执无法关联时，就保持结果未知；孤立回执留待对账，不能凭手机号和时间猜测属于哪次尝试。

要认定用户已读，还需要受信客户端报告预先定义的明确用户动作；验证码是否真正使用，则由业务侧的 verify 验证流程判断。

Queries return scheduling state and external observations separately. A late accepted Attempt after failed/expired must display the new evidence, “scheduling stopped; provider accepted,” rather than a bare failure, without reopening scheduling. Authenticate callbacks and bind their Attempt; duplicates and reordering must not overwrite facts by arrival order. If reliable receipts or correlation are unavailable, retain unknown and reconcile orphan observations instead of guessing by phone/time. Reading needs a defined user action from a trusted client, while business verify determines OTP use.

接受路径：事务内保存工作，事务外发布消息Acceptance: record work inside the transaction; publish outside

数据库唯一约束决定并发请求中谁能创建通知；同一个本地事务保证通知被接受时，后续工作也已经保存。第二组图中的预先查询用于快速返回已有回执。如果两个请求同时未命中，它们仍要通过唯一约束竞争插入，队列不参与这个接受事务。

Uniqueness resolves races; one DB transaction prevents accepted intent without work. Deck 2’s lookup is a fast path; two misses still compete for uniqueness. The broker does not join this transaction.

                      status=queued, lease_epoch=0, next_attempt_at=not_before,
                      policy_for(endpoint.channel, request), last_woken_at=DB_NOW())
      INSERT Outbox(delivery_ready, delivery_id, ready_at=not_before,
                    unique_key=(ready,delivery_id))
    COMMIT
    return receipt
  on UNIQUE_VIOLATION(tenant_id, idempotency_key):
    ROLLBACK
    saved = new_PRIMARY_SELECT(tenant, key)  # a new statement / snapshot
    if not saved: return HTTP_503_RETRY_SAME_KEY
    return saved.body_hash == digest ? saved.receipt_202 : HTTP_409
  on COMMIT_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN:
    discard_connection()  # a rollback cannot prove the commit did not happen
    return HTTP_503_RETRY_SAME_KEY
  on other_storage_error:
    ROLLBACK_if_possible
    return HTTP_503_RETRY_SAME_KEY

COMMIT 的响应丢失时，应用无法确认提交结果。此时要丢弃连接，返回 503，要求客户端用原键重试，再从保存的回执中恢复结果；不能认定事务已经回滚。

批量活动的接受接口只在同一个事务中创建 Campaign 和展开活动用的 outbox，不在接口内遍历 1,000 万人。后续使用稳定分页，按 (campaign_id, user_id) 去重，并把页游标、收件人意图、端点任务和新 outbox 放在同一个事务中提交。

A lost COMMIT reply is not a known rollback: discard the connection, return 503 for same-key retry, and recover through the receipt. Campaign acceptance instead commits Campaign plus expansion outbox. Stable pages dedupe (campaign_id,user_id) and co-commit the cursor, recipient intents, endpoint tasks, and new outbox.

relay 用短事务、SKIP LOCKED 和发布代次 publish_epoch 领取 outbox，释放行锁后再调用 broker。收到耐久确认后，只能标记自己所领取的同一代次。没有确认时，任务等待租约到期后恢复；收到确认后、标记前崩溃，则可能重复发布同一个 delivery_id。[10][11]

即使已经标记发布，唤醒消息仍可能因队列保留期或故障而丢失。因此，恢复扫描还必须检查尚未进入终态的 Delivery，从中重建唤醒；只扫描未发布的 outbox 还不够。

Relay claims with short transactions, SKIP LOCKED, and publish_epoch, releasing row locks before the broker call. Mark only the same epoch after durable acknowledgment; absent acknowledgment recovers through expiry, while crashing before marking republishes delivery_id. [10][11] Retention or failure may lose a published wake, so repair must scan unfinished Delivery, not just unpublished outbox.

队列消息只提醒 worker 处理某个 delivery_id；worker 必须在数据库中成功领取任务，才有权开始本次尝试。否则，两个 worker 都可能先查到可发送状态，然后同时发送。第三组图展示调用流程，下面的 SQL 再说明数据库怎样约束本地并发。

A queue message wakes delivery_id; a DB claim authorizes an attempt. Two workers cannot safely use a separate status lookup then send. Deck 3 keeps the original call directions; the implementation supplies local concurrency boundaries.

实现 2：用短事务领取任务，防止旧代次覆盖状态Implementation 2: short claims reject stale task writes

-- PostgreSQL 18; :parameters are bound by the worker, one primary DB.
BEGIN;
SELECT delivery_id FROM Delivery
WHERE delivery_id = :delivery_id AND (
  (status IN ('queued', 'retry_wait') AND next_attempt_at <= clock_timestamp())
  OR (status = 'leased' AND lease_until <= clock_timestamp())
)
FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED;
-- The driver COMMITs and returns NO_CLAIM if SELECT returned no row.
-- Otherwise it continues on the same connection while holding that row lock:
UPDATE Attempt SET outcome = 'timeout_unknown'
WHERE delivery_id = :delivery_id AND outcome = 'started';
UPDATE Delivery SET
  uncertain = uncertain OR EXISTS (
    SELECT 1 FROM Attempt WHERE delivery_id = :delivery_id
      AND outcome = 'timeout_unknown'),
  status = 'leased', lease_epoch = lease_epoch + 1,
  lease_owner = :owner, lease_until = clock_timestamp() + INTERVAL '30 seconds'
WHERE delivery_id = :delivery_id
RETURNING *, clock_timestamp() AS claimed_at;
COMMIT;
-- Install the returned claim only after confirmed COMMIT; unknown => no send.

数据库驱动只有在 SELECT 返回目标行后，才继续执行 UPDATE。数据库报错或 COMMIT 结果未知时必须停止，不能把错误当成“没有可领取的行”。行锁保护 Delivery 以及未完成 Attempt 的归类，提交后即释放；30 秒租约以数据库时间为准，调用供应商时不持行锁。

重新领取任务时，把遗留的 started 尝试标为未知，因为本地无法区分它是从未调用供应商，还是供应商已经接受。若重复唤醒对应的任务已经终止，或尚未到下次执行时间，可以直接确认这条消息，因为持久计划和恢复扫描仍会继续负责后续工作。[10]

Continue UPDATEs only after SELECT returns a row. DB errors or unknown commits stop processing and are not empty results. The row lock protects Delivery and unfinished Attempt classification until commit. The 30-second lease uses DB time, with no lock across a provider call. Reclaim marks leftover started attempts unknown, since “never called” and “externally accepted” are indistinguishable. Terminal or future duplicate wakes may be ACKed because durable schedules and repair remain. [10]

deliver(wake):
  d = claim_using_claim_sql(wake.delivery_id)
  if d == NO_CLAIM: ACK(wake); return  # durable work / repair scan remains
  ctx = pre_send_gates(d)  # evidence, expiry, consent, quota; resolved send context
  decision = ctx.decision
  attempt = NONE
  if decision == ALLOW:
    BEGIN
    current = SELECT Delivery FOR UPDATE WHERE delivery_id = d.delivery_id
    if current.lease_epoch != d.lease_epoch or current.status != leased
       or current.lease_until <= DB_NOW(): ROLLBACK; return
    if DB_NOW() >= current.not_after: decision = EXPIRED
    else if current.uncertain and not current.allow_unknown_retry:
      decision = UNKNOWN_STOP  # no Attempt or count; result transaction stops scheduling
    else if current.send_count >= current.max_sends:
      decision = EXHAUSTED
    else:
      UPDATE Delivery SET send_count = send_count + 1 WHERE delivery_id = d.delivery_id
      attempt = INSERT Attempt(new_id(), d.delivery_id, d.lease_epoch, outcome=started,
                 provider=ctx.provider, attempted_token_version=ctx.token_version,
                 registration_at=ctx.registration_at, started_at=DB_NOW())
    COMMIT  # unknown / failed commit => no call and no ACK
    if attempt != NONE:
      if local_remaining_budget() < HTTP_TIMEOUT + RESULT_BUDGET:
        decision = NOT_SENT  # definitely no call started by this worker
      else:
        decision = ctx.provider.send(ctx.message, timeout=10s)  # timeout => TIMEOUT_UNKNOWN
        # APNs 410 => INVALID_TOKEN only if registration_at + clock_error <= timestamp;
        # otherwise REGISTRATION_RECHECK. Every re-registration increments version.
  BEGIN
  current = SELECT Delivery FOR UPDATE WHERE delivery_id = d.delivery_id
  if attempt != NONE:
    UPDATE Attempt SET outcome=decision, provider_message_id=decision.message_id,
      finished_at=DB_NOW() WHERE attempt_id=attempt.attempt_id
    if decision == INVALID_TOKEN:

        AND token_version=attempt.attempted_token_version
  if current.lease_epoch == d.lease_epoch and current.status == leased:
    current.uncertain |= (decision == TIMEOUT_UNKNOWN)
    next = plan(current, decision, DB_NOW())
    UPDATE Delivery SET status=next.status, next_attempt_at=next.due,
                        uncertain=current.uncertain WHERE delivery_id=d.delivery_id
    if next.status == retry_wait:

    if next.reason == EXHAUSTED: INSERT Outbox(dlq, d.delivery_id, ready_at=DB_NOW(),
                                            unique_key=(dlq,d.delivery_id,d.lease_epoch))
  COMMIT  # failure => no ACK; a stale epoch may record only its own Attempt
  ACK(wake)

发送前，pre_send_gates 先查同一 delivery_id 是否已有供应商接受的 Attempt 证据，再检查有效期、营销同意与静默时段、发送频次和配额。检查结果分别为已接受（ACCEPTED）、无需发送（SUPPRESSED）、推迟处理（DEFERRED）或允许发送（ALLOW）；依赖出错时暂缓处理。DEFERRED 必须给出未来的允许时间 allowed_at，这些结果都通过同一个结果事务保存。

检查返回的上下文 ctx 还包含按固定版本模板渲染的 message、所选供应商、端点 token 版本和登记时间。供应商适配器负责把网络异常归为结果未知。

即使检查已返回 ALLOW，worker 在预留新的 Attempt 前，仍要在行锁内读取当前 Delivery。如果此前结果仍未知（uncertain=true），而产品禁止未知结果重试（allow_unknown_retry=false），本次判定就是 UNKNOWN_STOP：不创建尝试、不增加 send_count，也不调用供应商。随后由结果事务判定停止状态：判定时业务期限未到，则保存 failed；期限已到，则保存 expired。两种情况都保留 uncertain，确认提交后才 ACK。

send_count 限制的是已经预留的尝试次数：started 记录提交后，即使 worker 在真正调用前崩溃，也会消耗一次预算。本地可以约束这个上界，但无法据此精确断言外部调用了多少次。

pre_send_gates checks accepted Attempt evidence, expiry, marketing consent/quiet hours, frequency, and quota. It returns ACCEPTED, SUPPRESSED, DEFERRED with a future allowed_at, or ALLOW; dependency errors defer. ctx also carries the fixed-template message, provider, token version, and registration time; the adapter classifies network exceptions as unknown. All outcomes share the result transaction.

Even after ALLOW, the reservation transaction checks the current Delivery under its row lock. If uncertain is true and allow_unknown_retry is false, it returns the local UNKNOWN_STOP decision without creating an Attempt, incrementing send_count, or calling the provider. The result transaction records failed if its decision is before the business deadline, otherwise expired. Both retain uncertain, and ACK requires a confirmed commit.

send_count bounds reserved attempts: a crash after started commits but before sending consumes a slot, without pretending to know exact external call count.

任意数据库操作失败后，外层处理都要停止新调用，并且不确认队列消息。结果事务将 Attempt、当前代次的 Delivery，以及重试或死信 outbox 一起原子提交。旧代次只允许补记自己的迟到 Attempt，不能覆盖继任者状态。

后续尝试在发送前查到已接受的证据，就跳过发送；如果继任者的请求已经在途，仍可能产生重复。领取代次 lease_epoch 只保护本地状态，供应商不会验证它，因此它无法作为约束外部发送的 fencing token。[6]

The outer handler starts no new call and sends no ACK after DB failure. The result transaction atomically commits Attempt, current-epoch Delivery, and retry/DLQ outbox. Stale epochs record their own late Attempt but cannot overwrite successors. Future gates skip sending after accepted evidence, although a successor already in flight can duplicate. lease_epoch protects local state; it is not provider-enforced fencing. [6]

本地剩余预算 local_remaining_budget 从发出领取请求之前的单调时间起算。根据数据库返回的 claimed_at，把租约截止时间与业务有效期 not_after 中较早的一个换算成本地截止时间，再扣除已耗时间和安全余量；收到领取响应后，不能重新获得完整的 30 秒。这依赖数据库时钟和业务时间之间已知的误差界，无法维持这个前提时就暂停发送。

本例为 HTTP 调用保留 10 秒，遵循 FCM 至少 10 秒的建议，并另留结果保存的预算。[2] 长任务需要按当前所有权有条件地续租，失去租约后不能开始新调用。不过，最后检查之后进程仍可能暂停，在途请求也可能持续到租约过期；时间预算无法撤回已经产生的外部效果。

Start local_remaining_budget from monotonic time before sending the claim request, using claimed_at to bound the earlier lease/business deadline, subtracting elapsed time and margin instead of granting a new 30 seconds on receipt. This requires a known DB/business-clock error bound; pause sending when it cannot be maintained. Reserve ten seconds for HTTP, following FCM’s documented minimum recommendation, plus separate persistence time. [2] Long tasks need conditional renewal; ownership loss stops new calls. A pause after the last check or an in-flight request can outlive the lease. Time budgets cannot recall external effects.

活动扇出与发送前检查：控制数量，也尊重用户偏好Fan-out and gates: interruption policy is a constraint

批量活动按页展开，每个 worker 仍只处理一个端点。受众快照固定，收件人有唯一键，展开中断后就能从游标继续。首次展开时固定端点集合，后续重试保持这组端点，不加入新设备。

Expand campaigns by page; workers still handle one endpoint. Stable snapshots and recipient uniqueness support resumption; initial expansion freezes endpoints and retries add no new devices.

营销发送要按租户、用户、通道和列表（tenant/user/channel/list）查询同意状态，并使用用户的 IANA 时区判断静默时段。处于静默时段或额度不足时，把任务保存为 DEFERRED，重新唤醒后再检查；不要持有租约并休眠等待。

验证码只绕过营销同意和静默规则，仍要检查反滥用限制、业务有效期和实际供应商配额。入口限流可以在返回 202 前拒绝请求，给出 429；任务被接受以后，发送侧限流则要保存延迟计划，因为原来的 HTTP 请求已经结束。

Marketing checks tenant/user/channel/list consent and the user’s IANA time zone. Quiet hours or insufficient quota persist DEFERRED work and recheck on waking; do not sleep with a lease. OTP bypasses marketing consent and quiet hours but retains abuse, expiry, and provider quota checks. Admission 429 is before 202; drain throttling after acceptance persists delays and cannot reply to a completed HTTP request.

用户发送频次按 delivery_id 预占，重复领取同一任务时复用这份预占。若能确认没有发送，可以释放；结果未知时，则保留到频次窗口结束。租户和供应商配额按尝试消耗，在各自的存储中用原子条件扣减或 Lua 实现，保证多个 worker 共用限制。

多层额度如果分属不同存储，可能出现某层已经扣减、另一层拒绝的情况，导致保守地多扣并暂缓任务。要严格组合这些额度，需要由同一个权威调度器协调，或使用可恢复的预留机制。

Reserve user frequency by delivery_id and reuse it on replay. Release definite non-sends; retain unknowns through the window. Consume tenant/provider quota per attempt using store-side conditional updates or Lua, not process-local counters claimed as global limits. Separate stores may conservatively overcharge and delay work; strict combined quota needs one scheduling authority or recoverable reservations.

偏好缓存能让同一用户的多个设备与重试共用读取结果，但 1,000 万名首次读取偏好的用户仍会访问主库，所以展开要分页，回源也要限速。60 秒 TTL 从开始读取权威数据时算起，随机抖动只能缩短它，迟到的回填仍沿用原截止时间。

退订时，把偏好变更与缓存失效 outbox 放在同一个事务中提交，删除缓存失败后可以重试。不过，较早开始的旧读取仍可能在删除后回填旧值；要求更严格时，需要版本栅栏，或在发送前读主库。无论哪种读取方式，都不能撤回在途调用，也不能据此保证退订后只多发一条。

Caches reuse cross-device and retry reads; millions of cold users still hit the primary and need paging and limits. Start the 60-second TTL at authoritative-read initiation; jitter only downward and retain the original deadline on late fill. Co-commit consent and invalidation outbox, then retry failed deletes. Old reads can refill afterward; stricter cases need version fences or primary reads before sending. In-flight calls cannot be recalled, nor are extras capped to one message.

实现 3：按最晚允许时间重试，过期后停止Implementation 3: latest retry bound, expiry first

plan(d, outcome, now):
  # Adapter bounds: FCM retryable failures >=10s; 429 uses Retry-After or 60s;
  # APNs 5xx => 15min. Neither is capped by the ordinary-SMS local cap below.
  if outcome == ACCEPTED: return provider_accepted
  if outcome == SUPPRESSED: return suppressed
  if now >= d.not_after: return expired
  if outcome in {PERMANENT_REJECT, INVALID_TOKEN, REGISTRATION_RECHECK}: return failed
  if outcome == UNKNOWN_STOP or (d.uncertain and not d.allow_unknown_retry):
    return failed(unknown=true, reason=UNKNOWN_STOP)
  if outcome == DEFERRED: due = outcome.allowed_at  # no external call counted
  else:
    if d.send_count >= d.max_sends: return failed(reason=EXHAUSTED)
    provider_floor = outcome.provider_not_before  # defaults to now for ordinary SMS
    if outcome.provider == FCM and outcome.retryable_failure:
      provider_floor = max(provider_floor, now + 10s)  # now is after the observed failure
      if outcome.http_status == 429:
        provider_floor = max(provider_floor, outcome.retry_after or now + 60s)
        if outcome.quota_kind in {overall, topic}: provider_floor = max(provider_floor, now + 60s)
    cap = min(60s, 1s * 2 ** max(0, d.send_count - 1))  # ordinary SMS policy
    due = max(now + uniform(0, cap), provider_floor, d.not_before)
  if due >= d.not_after: return expired(unknown=d.uncertain)
  return retry_wait(due)

    UPDATE Delivery SET wake_no=d.wake_no, last_woken_at=now WHERE delivery_id=d.delivery_id
  COMMIT  # relay later publishes; never reset a terminal status to queued

plan 是纯函数，返回的计划由投递事务保存。普通短信最多预留 4 次尝试，即初次尝试加 3 次重试；随机抖动只影响本地退避。只有普通短信没有额外等待约束时，供应商允许的最早时间 provider_not_before 才默认为当前时间 now。

FCM 的可重试失败，包括没有相关响应头的 500，都至少等待 10 秒；遇到 429，还要与 Retry-After、缺失时的 60 秒，以及整体或 topic 至少 60 秒的要求取更晚时间。结果事务中的 now 晚于观测到失败的时刻，从它开始增加等待是保守做法。取完最大值后，不能再用本地 60 秒上限截短供应商要求的等待。[2]

如果算出的执行时间 due 已达到或超过 not_after，任务直接过期。plan 在处理 DEFERRED 前，也先检查是否必须因未知结果停止；需要停止时，不再生成重试 outbox。其余暂缓处理的 DEFERRED 必须给出未来的 allowed_at，重新唤醒后再做发送前检查；这种暂缓不会增加外部尝试数。

plan is pure and its result is persisted by the delivery transaction. Ordinary SMS allows four reserved attempts. Jitter affects only local backoff. provider_not_before defaults to now only for ordinary SMS without additional bounds. plan explicitly waits at least 10 seconds for FCM retryable failures, including 500 without headers, then applies any later 429 Retry-After/60-second or overall/topic 60-second bound. Transaction time now follows observed failure, so adding the wait there is conservative. Do not reapply the local 60-second cap after max. [2] Expire when due≥not_after. The unknown-stop policy is checked before DEFERRED and, when it applies, no retry outbox is created. Otherwise DEFERRED requires future allowed_at and rechecks gates without adding an external attempt.

恢复程序 repair 只扫描已经到期的 queued/retry_wait 任务，或租约已过期的 leased 任务，并在拿到行锁后重新检查条件。last_woken_at 初始设为接受时间，因此刚入队就丢失的唤醒，也能在 30 秒后进入恢复范围。

repair 把恢复用的 outbox 和递增的 wake_no 放在同一个事务中提交，relay 只发布 ready_at 已到的消息。恢复程序不会把终态任务重新设为待处理。重复唤醒本来就可能发生，最终由领取事务决定谁能处理。[10]

Repair selects due queued/retry_wait or expired leased work and rechecks under the row lock. Initialize last_woken_at at acceptance; even an immediately lost wake recovers after 30 seconds. Co-commit repair outbox and wake_no; relay publishes only due ready_at and never resets terminal states. Repeated wakes are expected; claim transactions resolve them. [10]

监控要覆盖本地接受的延迟和错误、各通道最老任务的等待时间、过期率、未知结果比例、同一 delivery_id 的尝试数、DLQ 和供应商配额。供应商接受量与 delivered 回执量分别统计，按设计投递给多个设备不算重复。

如果产品不允许对未知结果重试，停止后可以查询供应商支持的状态，或交由人工核查。代价是延迟增加或可能漏发；另建一个 notification_id 会丢掉原有恢复依据，不能用它掩盖未知结果。

Monitor acceptance latency/errors, oldest work per channel, expiry, unknown outcomes, attempts per delivery_id, DLQ, and quota. Count acceptance separately from delivered receipts; legitimate multi-device sends are not duplicates. If unknown retries are forbidden, stop and query supported provider status or investigate. The cost is delay/omission, not a new notification_id hiding ambiguity.

退订：分别验证邮件签名与退订令牌，再处理发送窗口Unsubscribe: signatures, capabilities, and send windows

邮箱负责验证邮件签名，退订入口负责验证能力令牌。第六组图把这两个验证放在各自的信任边界上，再展示偏好缓存的失效和回源。两次验证各有责任，不能相互替代。

The mailbox verifies mail signatures; the edge verifies a capability. Deck 6 retains the trust boundary and cache/primary paths. Neither check replaces the other.

RFC 8058 的一键退订使用 HTTPS POST，以及 List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click。请求不依赖 cookie 或 HTTP 登录，也不重定向。邮箱检查 DKIM 是否覆盖两个退订头；DKIM 的 h= 列出已签名的头。[5][8]

退订入口验证不可猜的能力令牌，确认它绑定的租户、用户、通道和列表（tenant/user/channel/list）。令牌只授予退订该列表的权限。邮件预取可能发起 GET，因此 GET 不能修改用户偏好。

RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe uses HTTPS POST and List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click without cookies, HTTP login, or redirects. The mailbox checks DKIM coverage of both unsubscribe headers, listed by h=. [5][8] The edge validates an unguessable tenant/user/channel/list capability granting only list opt-out. A prefetched GET must not change consent.

入口把 opt-out、版本递增和缓存失效 outbox 一起提交后，返回 202，再删除缓存。重复 POST 仍保持退订状态；令牌有效期应覆盖营销邮件的合理寿命。伪造令牌返回 4xx，不写数据库；真实令牌即使泄露，也只允许退订它绑定的范围，不能同时作为删除账户的凭证。

营销退订不会关闭业务验证码。另外，操作系统是否允许推送，与服务端是否保存了设备 token，是两项不同事实。

Co-commit opt-out, version, and invalidation outbox before 202, then delete cache. Repeated POST remains opt-out; capability lifetime covers useful mail lifetime. Invalid capabilities get 4xx without writes. Leaked real capabilities still opt out their narrow scope and must not authorize account deletion. Marketing opt-out does not disable OTP; OS push permission and token registration are distinct.

worker 读完偏好后、真正调用供应商前，用户仍可能并发退订；普通主库读取也无法与远端发送原子完成。如果要求“退订提交后不再开始新发送”，就需要同一个权威调度器协调偏好版本和发送许可，并另外处理提交前已经在途的请求。这里的缓存方案只能限制使用旧偏好的时间窗口，不能承诺退订后立即撤回发送。

Unsubscribe can land between preference read and send; an ordinary primary read cannot atomically include a remote call. Stricter no-new-starts-after-commit policy needs one authority coordinating consent version and admission, plus treatment of already in-flight work. Ordinary caching bounds staleness rather than promising recall.

面试复盘：沿着每次提交，讲清通知的结果Interview recap: follow the commit boundaries

追问：Kafka 恰好一次与多地域部署Follow-ups: Kafka exactly-once and regions

Kafka 事务可以协调 Kafka 主题写入和消费偏移；外部目的地通常还需要配合，FCM、APNs 和 SMTP 调用仍在事务之外。[6] 因此，更换 broker 无法消除“外部已成功，本地仍未知”的情况。扩展到多个地域前，要先确定由谁负责权威写入、同一个幂等键由谁处理，以及偏好版本怎样复制；增加地域也不会提高供应商配额。

Kafka transactions coordinate topic writes and offsets; external destinations generally need cooperation and FCM/APNs/SMTP remain outside. [6] Replacing the broker does not remove external success with local ambiguity. Regions need write authority, idempotency ownership, and consent-version replication; region count does not increase provider quota.

口述复盘：API 在同一个事务中保存意图和后续工作，再返回 202。worker 按端点领取任务，完成发送前检查，调用供应商并提交结果，最后确认队列消息。outbox 和恢复扫描负责重新唤醒任务，领取代次防止旧 worker 覆盖本地状态；外部结果未知时，保留 uncertain。活动至少 1.94 小时的计算假设独占配额，供应商接受、投递回执与用户已读也始终分别判断。

Recap: co-commit intent/work before 202; claim, gate, call, and commit before ACK. Outbox and scans restore wakes; epochs reject stale local writes and uncertain stays explicit. The 1.94-hour bound assumes exclusive quota; acceptance, receipts, and reading stay separate.
