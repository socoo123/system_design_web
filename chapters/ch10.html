设计通知系统Design a notification system

主线约 30 分钟 + 深挖约 15 分钟 · 前置：Ch01、Ch03 · 6 组 / 31 帧演进图~30 min core + ~15 min deep dives · after Ch01 and Ch03 · 6 decks / 31 evolution frames

本章核心：接得住、发得出、解释得清结果Core decisions: accept durably, send safely, explain outcomes

  • 202只承诺本地接受：通知、端点任务与outbox同事务提交，再异步调用供应商。
  • 按端点恢复：稳定delivery_id、带代次的领取和耐久重试处理已知重复；供应商未知结果保留重复风险。
  • 发送前过门：偏好、有效期、频次与供应商配额在调用前检查；接受、回执、展示、已读分别建模。
  • 202 means durable local acceptance: commit intent, endpoint tasks, and outbox before asynchronous provider calls.
  • Recover by endpoint: stable delivery_id, claim epochs, and durable retries suppress known repeats while retaining provider ambiguity.
  • Gate before sending: check consent, expiry, frequency, and provider quota; model acceptance, receipts, display, and reading separately.

范围与估算：先算端点，再谈扩容Scope and sizing: count endpoints before scaling

先确认Push / Email / SMS、事务或营销、单发或活动。单地域起步：API同事务留意图与持久工作后回202；worker按通道和优先级隔离，发送前过滤并限流。可靠性按至少一次设计，端点状态跳过已知成功，未知结果由产品选择重试还是停止；供应商收下不等于用户看见。

Clarify channels, transactional versus marketing, and individual versus campaign sends. Start in one region: commit intent and durable work before 202, isolate channel and priority workers, and gate before sending. Use at-least-once processing; endpoint state skips known success, while product policy chooses retry or stop for unknown outcomes. Provider acceptance does not prove display.

以下均为教学假设，不是厂商限额或压测结果。日常端点峰值约8,102/s；活动独占2,000/s也至少要1.94小时。API不在请求线程里循环收件人，供应商配额也不会因加worker自动增长。

All figures are teaching assumptions, neither vendor quotas nor benchmarks. Daily endpoint peak is about 8,102/s; a campaign needs at least 1.94 hours even at exclusive 2,000/s capacity. Do not loop through recipients in the request thread; adding workers does not raise quota.

架构：202回生产者，供应商结果回workerArchitecture: 202 to the producer; provider outcomes to workers

Notif Store是持久工作的权威。队列负责唤醒与缓冲，供应商掌握外部接受事实。第一组终图作总览；Delivery / Attempt在第三组展开，过滤在第四组展开。

Notif Store is the durable work authority. Queues provide wake-ups and buffering; providers own external acceptance. Use deck 1’s final frame as the overview, deck 3 for persistence, and deck 4 for gates.

API校验租户与模板，同事务提交通知、端点任务和outbox，数据库确认后回202与notification_id。relay领取已提交且到期的outbox，发布后等broker耐久确认，再按publish_epoch条件标记published_at。旧relay不能覆盖新领取者；确认后、标记前崩溃仍可能重复发布。[11]

The API validates tenant and template, co-commits intent, endpoint work, and outbox, then returns 202 and notification_id after DB confirmation. Relay claims due outbox rows, waits for durable broker acknowledgment, then conditionally marks published_at using publish_epoch. Stale relays cannot overwrite successors; crashing after acknowledgment but before marking can republish. [11]

Push、Email、SMS各有消费池，通道内再隔离事务与营销。OTP另有预留配额，避免只拆队列却共享耗尽的出口。每天几千条时，可轮询Delivery而暂缓broker；领取、到期索引与恢复扫描始终保留，内存线程池不能代替它们。

Give channels separate pools, then isolate transactional and marketing work within each. Reserve OTP quota too; queues alone do not protect an exhausted shared outlet. At a few thousand daily sends, polling Delivery can replace a broker; retain claims, due-time indexes, and repair scans. An in-memory thread pool cannot replace them.

API与状态：意图、端点、尝试、回执分开API and state: separate intent, endpoint, attempt, and receipt

认证决定tenant_id，服务端允许的模板决定优先级，调用方不能自称OTP绕过营销规则。幂等作用域是(tenant_id, Idempotency-Key)：同键同摘要回放原始202，异摘要409；超时或503仍用原键。语法/权限校验后先查回执，再校验新请求有效期，避免已成功请求因现在过期而无法恢复。

Authentication determines tenant_id; authorized server templates determine priority. Callers cannot label themselves OTP to bypass policy. Scope idempotency to (tenant_id, Idempotency-Key): replay the immutable 202 for matching content and return 409 otherwise. Retry timeout or 503 with the same key. After parsing and authorization, read the receipt before validating new-request expiry, preserving recovery of old success.

requested_channels表示接受了通道意图，不表示已发出。非法正文400、未认证401、越权403、键冲突409、入口额度不足429（带Retry-After）、存储错误503。鉴权后的GET /v1/notifications/{id}查当前汇总，不改变创建回执。新验证码必须换键，手机号或模板名不是意图键。键至少保留24小时且长于重试窗口，本例随通知保留30天，不承诺24小时后可复用。

requested_channels describes intent, not sends. Return 400 for malformed input, 401 for missing authentication, 403 for unauthorized use, 409 for key conflict, 429 with Retry-After for admission quota, and 503 for storage errors. Authorized GET /v1/notifications/{id} reads the current aggregate without changing the creation receipt. A new OTP needs a new key; phone/template names are not intent keys. Retain keys beyond retries, at least 24 hours; this example retains them for 30 days with the intent and does not promise reuse after 24 hours.

实现1：先恢复旧意图，再创建Implementation 1: recover before creating

accept(authenticated_tenant, key, request_bytes):
  request = parse_and_authorize(authenticated_tenant, request_bytes)
  tenant = authenticated_tenant
  digest = hash(canonical_business_fields(request))
  saved = PRIMARY.SELECT Notification WHERE (tenant_id, idempotency_key) = (tenant,key)
  if saved: return saved.body_hash == digest ? saved.receipt_202 : HTTP_409
  template_version = validate_new_request(request)  # expiry, allowed template / priority
  endpoints = freeze_small_recipient_snapshot(request)
  payload = encrypt(request.data)  # persisted in the same DB below
  not_before = request.not_before or DB_NOW()
  not_after = request.not_after
  receipt = immutable_202(new_notification_id(), request.channels)
  BEGIN READ COMMITTED
  try:
    INSERT Notification(receipt.notification_id, tenant, key, digest, receipt,
                        endpoints, payload, template_version, not_before, not_after)
    for endpoint in endpoints:
      delivery_id = new_delivery_id()
      INSERT Delivery(delivery_id, receipt.notification_id, endpoint, not_before, not_after,
                      status=queued, lease_epoch=0, next_attempt_at=not_before,
                      policy_for(endpoint.channel, request), last_woken_at=DB_NOW())
      INSERT Outbox(delivery_ready, delivery_id, ready_at=not_before,
                    unique_key=(ready,delivery_id))
    COMMIT
    return receipt
  on UNIQUE_VIOLATION(tenant_id, idempotency_key):
    ROLLBACK
    saved = new_PRIMARY_SELECT(tenant, key)  # a new statement / snapshot
    if not saved: return HTTP_503_RETRY_SAME_KEY
    return saved.body_hash == digest ? saved.receipt_202 : HTTP_409
  on COMMIT_OUTCOME_UNKNOWN:
    discard_connection()  # a rollback cannot prove the commit did not happen
    return HTTP_503_RETRY_SAME_KEY
  on other_storage_error:
    ROLLBACK_if_possible
    return HTTP_503_RETRY_SAME_KEY

这是伪代码，对应第2–4帧。PostgreSQL18主库唯一索引覆盖Notification的租户/键；Notification、Delivery、Outbox在同事务内插入，任一步失败全回滚。唯一冲突先ROLLBACK，再用新READ COMMITTED语句查赢家；不在失败事务中查询，也不假设同一CTE快照能看见并发赢家。[10] 摘要包含收件人、通道、模板标识、参数与时间边界，不含重试时新生成的ID。

This pseudocode implements frames 2–4. A PostgreSQL 18 primary unique index covers tenant/key; Notification, Delivery, and Outbox inserts roll back together. After uniqueness failure, ROLLBACK and read the winner in a new READ COMMITTED statement. Do not query an aborted transaction or assume one CTE snapshot sees a concurrent winner. [10] The digest includes recipient, channels, template identity, parameters, and time bounds, excluding newly generated retry IDs.

COMMIT回包丢失不是确定回滚：丢弃连接，503要求原键重试，从原回执恢复结果。活动接口改为同事务创建Campaign与expand outbox，不循环千万人；稳定分页按(campaign_id,user_id)去重，页游标、收件人意图、端点任务和新outbox同事务提交。

A lost COMMIT reply is not a known rollback: discard the connection, return 503 for same-key retry, and recover through the receipt. Campaign acceptance instead commits Campaign plus expansion outbox. Stable pages dedupe (campaign_id,user_id) and co-commit the cursor, recipient intents, endpoint tasks, and new outbox.

relay用短事务、SKIP LOCKED和publish_epoch领取，释放行锁后调用broker。耐久确认后只标记同代次；没确认就租约恢复，确认后标记前崩溃则重复发布delivery_id。[10][11] 已发布唤醒可能因队列保留期或故障丢失，恢复扫描还必须查未终态Delivery，不能只扫未发布outbox。

Relay claims with short transactions, SKIP LOCKED, and publish_epoch, releasing row locks before the broker call. Mark only the same epoch after durable acknowledgment; absent acknowledgment recovers through expiry, while crashing before marking republishes delivery_id. [10][11] Retention or failure may lose a published wake, so repair must scan unfinished Delivery, not just unpublished outbox.

投递路径：领取、发送、结果提交之后ackDelivery: claim, send, commit, then ACK

队列消息只唤醒delivery_id，数据库领取才授权本次尝试。两个worker不能仅靠先查status再发送；第三组调用方向保留，下面补本地并发边界。

A queue message wakes delivery_id; a DB claim authorizes an attempt. Two workers cannot safely use a separate status lookup then send. Deck 3 keeps the original call directions; the implementation supplies local concurrency boundaries.

实现2：短事务领取，拒绝旧代次覆盖Implementation 2: short claims reject stale task writes

-- PostgreSQL 18; :parameters are bound by the worker, one primary DB.
BEGIN;
SELECT delivery_id FROM Delivery
WHERE delivery_id = :delivery_id AND (
  (status IN ('queued', 'retry_wait') AND next_attempt_at <= clock_timestamp())
  OR (status = 'leased' AND lease_until <= clock_timestamp())
)
FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED;
-- The driver COMMITs and returns NO_CLAIM if SELECT returned no row.
-- Otherwise it continues on the same connection while holding that row lock:
UPDATE Attempt SET outcome = 'timeout_unknown'
WHERE delivery_id = :delivery_id AND outcome = 'started';
UPDATE Delivery SET
  uncertain = uncertain OR EXISTS (
    SELECT 1 FROM Attempt WHERE delivery_id = :delivery_id
      AND outcome = 'timeout_unknown'),
  status = 'leased', lease_epoch = lease_epoch + 1,
  lease_owner = :owner, lease_until = clock_timestamp() + INTERVAL '30 seconds'
WHERE delivery_id = :delivery_id
RETURNING *, clock_timestamp() AS claimed_at;
COMMIT;
-- Install the returned claim only after confirmed COMMIT; unknown => no send.

驱动拿到SELECT行才继续UPDATE；数据库错误/COMMIT未知必须停止，不能伪装成空行。行锁覆盖Delivery及其未完成Attempt的归类，提交后释放；30秒租约用数据库时间，网络期间不持锁。重领将遗留started记为未知，因为无法区分“未调用”与“外部已接受”。终态或尚未到期的重复唤醒可ack，持久计划和扫描仍在。[10]

Continue UPDATEs only after SELECT returns a row. DB errors or unknown commits stop processing and are not empty results. The row lock protects Delivery and unfinished Attempt classification until commit. The 30-second lease uses DB time, with no lock across a provider call. Reclaim marks leftover started attempts unknown, since “never called” and “externally accepted” are indistinguishable. Terminal or future duplicate wakes may be ACKed because durable schedules and repair remain. [10]

pre_send_gates先查同delivery_id的accepted Attempt，再查有效期、营销同意/静默、频次与配额；给出ACCEPTED、SUPPRESSED、DEFERRED或ALLOW，依赖错误暂缓而不放行。ctx同时携带固定版本模板渲染的message、供应商、端点token版本与登记时间；适配器将网络异常归为未知。所有结果共用落库事务，DEFERRED给出未来allowed_at。send_count是预留尝试上界：started提交后、调用前崩溃也消耗一次预算，不假称知道外部精确调用数。

pre_send_gates checks accepted Attempt evidence, expiry, marketing consent/quiet hours, frequency, and quota. It returns ACCEPTED, SUPPRESSED, DEFERRED with a future allowed_at, or ALLOW; dependency errors defer. ctx also carries the fixed-template message, provider, token version, and registration time; the adapter classifies network exceptions as unknown. All outcomes share the result transaction. send_count bounds reserved attempts: a crash after started commits but before sending consumes a slot, without pretending to know exact external call count.

任意数据库失败均由外层停止新调用且不ack；结果事务原子提交Attempt、当前代次Delivery及retry/DLQ outbox。旧代次只能记录自己的迟到Attempt，不能覆盖继任者；以后的门查到accepted证据跳过发送，已经在途的继任请求仍可能重复。lease_epoch只保护本地状态，不是供应商会验证的fencing token。[6]

The outer handler starts no new call and sends no ACK after DB failure. The result transaction atomically commits Attempt, current-epoch Delivery, and retry/DLQ outbox. Stale epochs record their own late Attempt but cannot overwrite successors. Future gates skip sending after accepted evidence, although a successor already in flight can duplicate. lease_epoch protects local state; it is not provider-enforced fencing. [6]

local_remaining_budget从发出领取请求之前的单调时间起算，按claimed_at换算租约与not_after的较早截止，扣已花时间和安全余量，不在收到回包后重新给满30秒；依赖数据库时钟与业务时间的已知误差界，无法保证时暂停发送。HTTP预算10秒，遵循FCM至少10秒的建议，另留结果落库预算。[2] 长任务需条件续租，失去租约后不开始新调用。最后检查后的暂停与在途请求仍可能越过租约，时钟预算不能撤回外部副作用。

Start local_remaining_budget from monotonic time before sending the claim request, using claimed_at to bound the earlier lease/business deadline, subtracting elapsed time and margin instead of granting a new 30 seconds on receipt. This requires a known DB/business-clock error bound; pause sending when it cannot be maintained. Reserve ten seconds for HTTP, following FCM’s documented minimum recommendation, plus separate persistence time. [2] Long tasks need conditional renewal; ownership loss stops new calls. A pause after the last check or an in-flight request can outlive the lease. Time budgets cannot recall external effects.

扇出与发送门：少打扰也是约束Fan-out and gates: interruption policy is a constraint

活动按页展开，worker仍只处理一个端点。稳定快照与收件人唯一键保证续读；首次展开固定端点，重试不加入新设备。

OTP bypasses marketing consent and quiet hours but retains abuse, expiry, and provider quota checks. Reserve user frequency by delivery_id and reuse it on replay. Release definite non-sends; retain unknowns through the window. Consume tenant/provider quota per attempt using store-side conditional updates or Lua, not process-local counters claimed as global limits. 故障与重试：停止不等于确定没发Failures and retries: stopping is not proof of no send

实现3：取最晚重试下界，过期优先Implementation 3: latest retry bound, expiry first

plan(d, outcome, now):
  # Adapter bounds: FCM retryable failures >=10s; 429 uses Retry-After or 60s;
  # APNs 5xx => 15min. Neither is capped by the ordinary-SMS local cap below.
  if outcome == ACCEPTED: return provider_accepted
  if outcome == SUPPRESSED: return suppressed
  if now >= d.not_after: return expired
  if outcome in {PERMANENT_REJECT, INVALID_TOKEN, REGISTRATION_RECHECK}: return failed
  if outcome == DEFERRED: due = outcome.allowed_at  # no external call counted
  else:
    if d.uncertain and not d.allow_unknown_retry: return failed(unknown=true)
    if d.send_count >= d.max_sends: return failed(reason=EXHAUSTED)
    provider_floor = outcome.provider_not_before  # defaults to now for ordinary SMS
    if outcome.provider == FCM and outcome.retryable_failure:
      provider_floor = max(provider_floor, now + 10s)  # now is after the observed failure
      if outcome.http_status == 429:
        provider_floor = max(provider_floor, outcome.retry_after or now + 60s)
        if outcome.quota_kind in {overall, topic}: provider_floor = max(provider_floor, now + 60s)
    cap = min(60s, 1s * 2 ** max(0, d.send_count - 1))  # ordinary SMS policy
    due = max(now + uniform(0, cap), provider_floor, d.not_before)
  if due >= d.not_after: return expired(unknown=d.uncertain)
  return retry_wait(due)

repair_due_work():  # short primary-DB transaction; no network inside
  BEGIN
  now = DB_NOW()
  rows = SELECT Delivery FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED
         WHERE (queued_or_retry_due OR expired_lease) AND last_woken_at < now-30s
  for d in rows:
    INSERT Outbox(repair_wake, d.delivery_id, ready_at=now, unique_key=(repair,d.delivery_id,++d.wake_no))
    UPDATE Delivery SET wake_no=d.wake_no, last_woken_at=now WHERE delivery_id=d.delivery_id
  COMMIT  # relay later publishes; never reset a terminal status to queued

plan是纯函数，结果由投递事务保存。普通SMS最多4个预留尝试（初次+3次重试）；抖动只影响本地退避。provider_not_before仅在普通SMS无额外约束时缺省now；plan显式保证FCM可重试失败（含无header的500）至少等10秒，再与429的Retry-After/60秒及整体/topic至少60秒取更晚。结果事务now晚于观测失败时间，保守从now加等待；取max后不能再按本地60秒截短。[2]due≥not_after直接过期。DEFERRED的allowed_at必须在未来，醒来重查门，不增加外部尝试数。

plan is pure and its result is persisted by the delivery transaction. Ordinary SMS allows four reserved attempts. Jitter affects only local backoff. provider_not_before defaults to now only for ordinary SMS without additional bounds. plan explicitly waits at least 10 seconds for FCM retryable failures, including 500 without headers, then applies any later 429 Retry-After/60-second or overall/topic 60-second bound. Transaction time now follows observed failure, so adding the wait there is conservative. Do not reapply the local 60-second cap after max. [2] Expire when due≥not_after. DEFERRED requires future allowed_at and rechecks gates without adding an external attempt.

repair只查到期queued/retry_wait或过期leased，持锁后重检条件。last_woken_at初值为接受时间；刚入队就丢失也在30秒后可恢复。repair outbox与wake_no同事务提交，relay只发布ready_at已到的行，绝不重置终态。重复唤醒是预期，领取事务裁决。[10]

Repair selects due queued/retry_wait or expired leased work and rechecks under the row lock. Initialize last_woken_at at acceptance; even an immediately lost wake recovers after 30 seconds. Co-commit repair outbox and wake_no; relay publishes only due ready_at and never resets terminal states. Repeated wakes are expected; claim transactions resolve them. [10]
