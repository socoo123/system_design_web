这一章结束你能做什么What you can do after this chapter

中文

  • 30 秒讲完三个 hard part，Push 默认 FCM HTTP v1。
  • 可靠性说 at-least-once 加 idempotency，不说 exactly-once。
  • 按白板顺序画完：澄清、数量级、接入到分通道 worker。失败路径和通道 trade-off 分开讲。

English

  • Give the three hard parts in 30 seconds, and default Push to FCM HTTP v1.
  • Say at-least-once plus idempotency. Exactly-once is not the claim.

30 秒开场The 30-second open

中文

通知三个 hard part：通道不一样、不丢不重、别骚扰。我先确认 Push / SMS / Email、事务还是营销、单发还是群发。千万 DAU 的白板上，Push 日均是千级 QPS，营销突发被三方限速卡住。链路是业务方 → 通知 API → MQ → 分通道 worker → 三方。Push 主叙事用 FCM HTTP v1，覆盖 Android 和 Web；iOS 走 APNS，但不是整张图的唯一出口。可靠性是 at-least-once，加上 notification_id 的 idempotency。我不说 exactly-once。

范围就这么大。短链是读多写少的 KV，这题是 fan-out 和投递。全球多 Region 加打开率漏斗，等他们追问再补。还没问通道就只画 APNS、还没分事务和营销就讲分区、声称 exactly-once、在 API 里循环千万用户，都是 red flag。

English

Notifications have three hard parts: channels that do not look alike, don't drop and don't duplicate, and don't spam. I'll confirm Push, SMS, and Email, transactional versus marketing, and one-to-one versus broadcast. On a 10-million DAU board, Push is about a thousand QPS on average, and a marketing burst is gated by the vendor rate limit. The picture is product services, the notification API, a queue, a worker pool per channel, then the vendors. Push leads with FCM HTTP v1 for Android and Web. iOS still uses APNS, but it is not the only door on the diagram. Reliability is at-least-once plus idempotency on notification_id. I will not claim exactly-once.

That is the scope. A URL shortener is a read-heavy KV. This prompt is fan-out and delivery. Leave global multi-region and an open-rate funnel until they ask. Drawing only APNS before you asked about channels, lecturing partitions before transactional versus marketing, claiming exactly-once, or looping ten million users inside the API — those are the red flags.

时间盒在 Ch01。上一道完整题是 Ch09 · 短链。The clock is Ch01. The previous full case is Ch09 · URL shortener.

先问清楚Ask before you draw

中文

没问清楚就画图，又是 Jimmy。五个问题就停，其余写成假设，写在白板边上。问过 10 分钟也是 red flag。

我假设 Push、SMS、Email 都要；事务和营销都有；验证码不能丢；营销要静默时段和退订。Push 用 FCM HTTP v1 做 Android / Web 的主通道。我按这个画，不对你打断我。

English

Drawing before you ask is Jimmy again. Five questions, then stop. Write the rest as assumptions on the side of the board. Past ten minutes of questions is a red flag too.

I'll assume Push, SMS, and Email are all in, both transactional and marketing, OTP cannot drop, and marketing needs quiet hours and unsubscribe. Push uses FCM HTTP v1 as the Android and Web channel. I'll draw on that — cut me off if it's wrong.

提问的习惯在 Ch01。问关键的，写下假设，继续画。The habit of asking lives in Ch01. Ask the key ones, write the assumptions, keep drawing.

数量级Orders of magnitude

中文

公式在后面的估算章。这里只要证明：这是中等吞吐，难点不在打满一台机器。下面全是白板假设，不是某家公司的内部数字。不要编一个精确投递率。

按千万 DAU：Push 日均千级 QPS，日志是 TB 级。营销可以把瞬时量拉到每秒上万，但 FCM 和 SMS 聚合商会限速，所以要 MQ 削峰。bottleneck 不是算力，是送达，以及别骚扰。

English

The formulas live in the estimates chapter. Here you only show this is medium throughput. The hard part is not saturating one box. Every number below is a board assumption, not an internal figure. Do not invent a delivery-rate percentage.

On a 10-million DAU board, Push is about a thousand QPS on average, and logs are TB-scale. Marketing can spike into the tens of thousands per second, but FCM and the SMS aggregators will rate-limit you, so the queue has to absorb the burst. The bottleneck is delivery, and not spamming people. It is not compute.

估算口径见 Ch03 · 粗略估算。The estimate habit is Ch03 · back-of-the-envelope.

高层：接入到分通道 workerHigh-level: ingress to per-channel workers

中文

从接入画起，一笔加一个。MQ 必答四句：三方慢，API 先返回；营销突发要削峰；三方抖一下可以重试；SMS 慢不能拖死 Push。topic 按通道拆，再按优先级拆。分区和 ISR 不要在这题展开。

schema 够用就停。notification 有 id、user_id、channel、status、template_id。device 有 user_id、token、platform、status。用户对设备是一对多。user_preference 放通道、opt-in、quiet hours。不要在白板上铺五张宽表。

English

Start at the ingress and add one box per stroke. Four reasons for the queue, then stop: vendor calls are slow, so the API returns first; marketing bursts need absorbing; a vendor blip can be retried; slow SMS cannot stall Push. Split topics by channel, then by priority. Leave partitions and ISR for the queue chapter.

Stop the schema when it is enough. notification is id, user_id, channel, status, template_id. device is user_id, token, platform, status. User to device is one-to-many. user_preference holds channel, opt-in, and quiet hours. Do not draw five wide tables.

协议对比在 Ch40 · 协议选型。投递和重试的语义在 Ch42 · 消息与弹性。分区课是 Ch20 · 分布式消息队列，这题不展开。Protocol comparison is Ch40 · protocol choices. Delivery and retry semantics are Ch42 · messaging. The partition chapter is Ch20 · message queue. Not this board.

Hard part · 失败路径Hard part · failure path

中文

这一套只讲不丢、重试、去重、死 token。通道之间怎么换，下一套再画，不塞进同一张。

我保证 at-least-once：先落库再发，失败就重试，耗尽进 DLQ。重复用 notification_id 挡住。我不说 exactly-once，三方自己的重试我控制不了。

端到端 exactly-once 跨 FCM、APNS、SMS 做不到。2PC 把延迟和运维成本拉高，通知不值。服务端按 id 去重，客户端按 id 再滤一次。验证码用独立高优 topic，重试更积极。营销丢一条可以接受，但不能拿「能丢」当借口不做落库。SMS 聚合商可以熔断换路；Push 没有另一家等价出口。

English

This pass is only don't-drop, retry, dedup, and dead tokens. How the channels differ is the next pass. They do not share a slide.

I guarantee at-least-once: persist first, then send, retry on failure, and exhaust into the DLQ. Duplicates are blocked by notification_id. I will not say exactly-once. I do not control the vendor's own retries.

End-to-end exactly-once across FCM, APNS, and SMS does not exist. 2PC pulls latency and ops cost up, and notifications are not worth it. Dedup on the server by id, and filter again on the client by id. OTP gets its own high-priority topic and a more aggressive retry. Dropping one marketing message is acceptable. "We can drop it" is not an excuse to skip the write. An SMS aggregator can fail over. Push has no second equivalent door.

投递语义的展开在 Ch42 · 消息与弹性。The longer version of delivery semantics is Ch42 · messaging.

Hard part · 通道 trade-offHard part · channel trade-offs

中文

这一套只比通道，不把上一套的重试步骤再画一遍。2026 的白板：Android 和 Web 的主叙事是 FCM HTTP v1，鉴权是短时 OAuth，不是永久 server key。iOS 仍是 APNS，可以直连，也可以让 FCM 代发。

Android 和 Web 我走 FCM HTTP v1，iOS 走 APNS。我不会把 APNS 画成整题的默认出口，也不会用已经停掉的 FCM legacy key。

SMS 不自建短信中心，走聚合商：按国家、运营商、价格、成功率选路，失败就熔断换备用。DLR 用 webhook 回来，靠 msg_id 对账，所以 SMS 比 Push 好统计「送达到了」。国内还要签名、模板和退订。Email 走 ESP，便宜，适合营销，但要听 bounce、complaint 和退订。协议名词点到为止。

English

This pass only compares channels. Do not redraw the retry steps from the previous pass. On a 2026 board the Android and Web story is FCM HTTP v1, with a short-lived OAuth token, not a forever server key. iOS is still APNS. Talk to it directly, or let FCM proxy.

Android and Web go FCM HTTP v1. iOS goes APNS. I will not draw APNS as the default door for the whole prompt, and I will not use the FCM legacy key. That send API is gone.

You do not run your own SMSC. An aggregator routes by country, carrier, price, and success rate, and fails over when a route breaks. DLR comes back on a webhook, reconciled by msg_id, which is why SMS is easier to count as delivered than Push. In China you still need a signature, a template, and unsubscribe. Email goes through an ESP. It is cheap and fits marketing, and you honor bounce, complaint, and unsubscribe. Name the protocols and stop.

通道协议的词典在 Ch40 · 协议选型。这里不要把 SMPP 讲成一节。The protocol dictionary is Ch40 · protocol choices. Do not turn SMPP into a section here.

Hard part · 别骚扰Hard part · don't spam

中文

第三个 hard part。营销把验证码堵住、凌晨把人叫醒、退订了还发，都是 red flag。限流算法本身不在这题展开，只说放在哪一层，以及事务和营销为什么必须分开。

用户级和通道级都要限。验证码单独高优队列。营销看静默时段和退订，到点再发，不扔掉。我不会为了「必达」把用户叫醒。

四层就够：用户每天或每小时的上限；通道级 SMS 最严；同一模板的短窗口去重；全局配额保住 FCM 和聚合商。社交通知可以合并，十个赞合成一条。偏好读多，短 TTL 缓存，源在数据库。trade-off 要说出来：防骚扰会压低营销到达；重试做猛会增加重复。不是再画十个箭头。

English

Third hard part. Marketing blocking OTP, waking someone at 3am, or sending after they unsubscribed — those are red flags. The limiter algorithm stays in its own chapter. Here you only say which layer, and why transactional and marketing cannot share a queue.

Per-user and per-channel limits both belong. OTP gets its own high-priority queue. Marketing respects quiet hours and unsubscribe: send when the window ends, do not throw it away. I will not wake the user in the name of must-deliver.

Four layers are enough: a per-user daily or hourly cap; per-channel, with SMS the strictest; a short window on the same template; a global cap that protects the FCM and aggregator quotas. Social notices can coalesce, ten likes into one message. Preferences are read-heavy: a short TTL cache, source of truth in the database. Say the trade-off out loud: anti-spam lowers marketing reach, and aggressive retry raises duplicates. More arrows do not say that.

令牌桶和滑动窗口在 Ch04 · 设计限流器。这题只讲放哪一层。Token bucket and sliding window are Ch04 · rate limiter. This chapter only says which layer.
