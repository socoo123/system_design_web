键值存储Key-value store

30 秒开场：先定数据契约，再定 N、W、RThe 30-second opening: define the contract before N, W and R

我设计一个单地域、小 value、按 key 访问的分布式 KV。示例存可覆盖的用户偏好字段，允许竞争更新选一个胜者；不承担余额、库存扣减和登录撤销的权威判断。环上取三台不同物理机，Coordinator 并行发送，默认等两份耐久 ACK。读取两份并比较版本。3/2/2 在固定副本集合内相交，但少数分区会失败；需要继续接写时另谈 sloppy quorum 的代价。节点内用 WAL、memtable、SSTable，后台修复漏写，删除保留墓碑。

I design a single-region distributed KV with small values and key-based access. The example stores replaceable user-preference fields whose competing updates may select one winner; it does not authorize balances, inventory deductions or login revocation. Choose three distinct physical owners, fan out in parallel and wait for two durable ACKs. Read two copies and reconcile versions. 3/2/2 intersects within fixed membership but fails on a minority partition; continued writes require a separate sloppy-quorum trade-off. Locally use WAL, Memtable and SSTables; repair missed writes and retain deletion tombstones.

“键值存储”只规定访问形态，没有自动替你选择 AP、CP 或某种引擎。先问四件事：value 可以覆盖吗，写后读能接受什么结果，分区时哪一侧必须继续服务，删除多久后必须生效。购物车加购需要保留并发意图，购物车快照却可能只保留一个版本；同一个产品名不能代替数据语义。把每个字段的契约说清，比先背系统标签更能推动设计。

“Key-value” describes access shape; it does not select AP, CP or an engine. Ask whether overwrites are acceptable, what a read after a write must return, which partition must keep serving, and when deletion must take effect. Cart additions may need to preserve concurrent intent while a cart snapshot might choose one version. A product label cannot replace field semantics.

本章 API 是 get(key)、put(key,value,mutation_id,ts) 和 delete(key,mutation_id,ts)，单次操作只涉及一个 key。客户端收到超时，状态标成结果未知；可重试同一 mutation，不能据此执行一次新的业务扣减。扫描、二级索引和跨 key 事务先留在边界外。10 KB 上限是本章为了限制复制与尾延迟写下的约束，大 value 仍可以属于 KV；需要上传图片时换成对象引用，并另算对象存储路径。

The API is get(key), put(key,value,mutation_id,ts) and delete(key,mutation_id,ts), one key per operation. A timeout is an unknown outcome: retry the same mutation rather than perform another business deduction. Scans, secondary indexes and cross-key transactions stay outside this round. The 10 KB cap is our replication and latency constraint, not a definition of KV; images can be stored through object references with a separate path.

Dynamo 2007 是高可用写、版本与应用合并的一条设计；Cassandra 使用时间戳 LWW，并提供可调一致性级别。这里采用的是“固定副本 quorum＋可覆盖 value 的 LWW＋LSM”的教学组合，不把两份实现拼成一个万能生产默认。后面专门画一次 Dynamo 风格 sloppy quorum 的替代策略，切换时明确换了成功条件。[1][2]

Dynamo 2007 uses highly available writes, versioning and application reconciliation; Cassandra uses timestamp LWW and tunable consistency levels. Our teaching combination is fixed-owner quorum, LWW for replaceable values and an LSM engine. The later Dynamo-style sloppy alternative explicitly changes the success condition. [1][2]

写路径：三份发送，两份确认Write path: three sends, two acknowledgements

正常路径：角色、放置、耐久分开说Walkthrough: separate role, placement and durability

Client 把一次 PUT 交给 Coordinator，后者按照 Ch05 的成员版本定位这个 key 的三个 owner。虚拟节点可能连续属于同一物理机，所以选副本时要去重，并尽量跨机架。Coordinator 只负责这次请求的聚合，可以由接入节点或客户端侧的路由角色承担；图把它单独画出来方便解释，不强迫新增一层独立服务。副本都是直接接收同一 mutation，既不经 A 转发给 B，也不经 B 转发给 C。

Client gives a PUT to Coordinator, which resolves three owners using the membership version from Ch05. Consecutive virtual nodes can share a physical machine, so deduplicate and spread replicas across racks. Coordinator aggregates this request and can be a receiving node or routing role, without requiring a new service. All replicas receive the same mutation directly, never through an A→B→C relay.

W=2 是等多少份 ACK，不是发多少份请求。成功点只取收到第二份耐久 ACK 的时刻；本例先到 A、再到 B，C 仍在处理。返回以后 Coordinator 仍要尽力完成 C，失败则进入 hint 或后台修复。图上 C 的箭头是在最后一帧补画的，为了突出“晚到不等于没发送”；它在执行上与前两份并行发送，不能照动画顺序写成延后才发第三份。

W=2 counts waited-for ACKs, not sends. Success occurs at the second durable ACK; A and B finish first while C continues. Complete C or repair a failure afterward. The animation draws C’s reply last to emphasize lateness, but all requests were sent in parallel; animation order does not postpone the third send.

为什么这样选：容量落到真正的线和盘上Why: charge capacity to actual links and storage

一亿 key×1,000 B=100 GB value；乘三副本是 300 GB。教学再把 SST 空间系数设为 2，得到约 600 GB 活跃文件预算，未计 key、索引、WAL、备份和 compaction 临时重叠。若要求已规划占用最多到总容量的 80%，则 600÷0.8=750 GB，仍只是同一估算口径的总量下限。不能从“一亿”直接推出机器数，更不能用压缩后的容量再忘记乘副本。

100M keys × 1,000 B = 100 GB of values; three copies give 300 GB. A teaching SST space factor of 2 gives 600 GB of active files, excluding keys, indexes, WAL, backups and temporary compaction overlap. Keeping that planned occupancy below 80% requires 600÷0.8=750 GB, still a scoped aggregate lower bound, not a machine count.

峰值写载荷是 10,000×1 KB=10 MB/s。发送到三份副本，副本总接收约 30 MB/s，也就是 240 Mb/s；W 改成 1 或 3 都不改变这笔。若 Coordinator 自身也存一份，额外跨节点 value 复制约为两份、20 MB/s；若 Coordinator 是独立代理，则三份都穿网络。协议、索引和修复另加。旧稿把 30 MB/s 统称“复制带宽”，容易混淆网络拓扑和集群载荷，图注现在明确计数边界。

Peak write payload is 10,000 × 1 KB = 10 MB/s. Three replica destinations receive 30 MB/s total, or 240 Mb/s, whether W=1 or W=3. If Coordinator stores one copy, extra cross-node value copies are about 20 MB/s; an independent proxy sends all three over the network. Add protocol, indexing and repair separately rather than calling every topology “30 MB/s replication bandwidth.”

故障窗口：超时与重试不是撤销Failure window: timeout and retry do not undo a write

A 已落盘，B 的 ACK 丢了，Coordinator 等不到 W 时客户端可能收到超时；B、C 之后仍可能完成。系统没有把这次写回滚，所以响应里需要区分成功、确定拒绝和结果未知。盲目生成一个新的 mutation_id 重试，会把重复业务动作变成另一笔合法更新。对覆盖值，重试同一内容、同一时间戳与同一 mutation 标识，才能把它视作同一 mutation；条件扣减仍需另一个串行化保证。

A can persist while B’s ACK is lost; the client times out even though B and C may finish later. Nothing rolled back the mutation. Distinguish success, definite rejection and unknown outcome. Retrying with a new mutation_id creates another business action. For an overwrite, retain content, timestamp and identifier; conditional deductions still require serialization.

替代方案按契约选择。W=1 缩短等待，却放大“确认后仅存活一份”的丢失窗口；W=3 等三份，任何一份不可达都不能成功。三台落在同一宿主机或同一供电域，W=3 也挡不住共同故障。先问确认要抵御什么故障，再写副本放置和落盘规则。异步复制只能补传播路径，不能事后把已经丢掉的唯一耐久副本救回来。

Choose W from the contract. W=1 reduces wait but enlarges the window with only one durable surviving copy; W=3 fails if any owner is unreachable. Three ACKs in one host or power domain do not resist correlated failure. Specify the failure, placement and persistence rule; asynchronous propagation cannot recover a lost unique durable copy.

读路径：集合相交，还要正确解释版本Read path: intersect sets, then interpret versions

正常路径：把交集证明写完整Walkthrough: complete the intersection proof

全集 U={A,B,C}，最近一次已完成写被 B、C 确认。一次读取问 A、B，并收齐 R=2 份。若两个集合没有交集，就需要至少 W+R=4 个不同元素，但全集只有 3 个，因此交集至少为 W+R−N=1。图中的 B 同时属于两个集合，它带回 v2。不能因此把任意单份响应当成新值。

Let U={A,B,C}; B and C confirmed the latest completed write. Read A and B and collect both. Disjoint sets would require W+R=4 distinct elements in a universe of 3, so overlap is at least W+R−N=1. B supplies v2. This does not make an arbitrary individual response fresh.

选 v2 还用了 A6：这次写已经完成、没有并发覆盖、版本顺序正确、成员全集一致且成功副本没有丢掉数据。少一条就要重新证明。交集解决“接触到谁”，冲突比较解决“保留哪份”。多个写可以各自过 quorum 并仍互相覆盖；普通 quorum 加 LWW 没有建立所有操作共享的实时顺序，也没有提供 compare-and-set。把这个证明称为完整的线性一致性协议，会漏掉下一组的时间戳反例。

Selecting v2 also uses A6: completion, no concurrent overwrite, correct version order, unchanged membership and retained data. Overlap answers which copy is contacted; reconciliation answers which version wins. Multiple quorum writes may overwrite each other. Ordinary quorum plus LWW supplies neither a shared real-time order nor compare-and-set.

为什么这样选：读放大和热点要一起算Why: account for read amplification and hotspots

图采用两份完整 value 回给 Coordinator 的简化模型。读出口是 50,000×1 KB=50 MB/s，约 400 Mb/s；副本到 Coordinator 却是 50,000×2×1 KB=100 MB/s，约 800 Mb/s。不能只算客户端返回，也不能以为读写比 5:1 就只有五倍存储操作。产品可用摘要加一份完整结果降低正常带宽，摘要不一致再拉数据；图没有画摘要协议，所以这个 100 MB/s 是本图口径，不是 Cassandra 所有读请求的固定值。

The simplified diagram returns two full values. Client egress is 50,000 × 1 KB = 50 MB/s, about 400 Mb/s; replicas send 50,000 × 2 × 1 KB = 100 MB/s, about 800 Mb/s, to Coordinator. A 5:1 client read/write ratio does not mean only five times the storage work. A digest plus one full value can reduce normal bytes, fetching data on mismatch. Our 100 MB/s belongs to the drawn model, not every Cassandra read.

A8 的十台机器在均匀分布下，每台平均 100,000÷10=10,000 次副本读/秒，低于 12,000 的计划预算。若一个共享 key 吃掉 20% 客户端读，它每秒有 10,000 次；剩余 40,000×2÷10=8,000 次普通副本读分到每台。热点固定读两台，这两台各约 8,000+10,000=18,000，超过预算 50%。即使三份均摊热点的两份读取，8,000+20,000÷3≈14,667 仍超预算。多加不拥有这个 key 的机器帮不上忙。

With A8’s ten machines, uniform traffic averages 100,000÷10=10,000 replica reads/s each, below the 12,000 budget. A shared key receiving 20% of client reads gets 10,000/s. Ordinary traffic contributes 40,000×2÷10=8,000 per machine. Two fixed hot targets each get 18,000, 50% above budget. Even spreading two hot reads across three copies yields 8,000+20,000÷3≈14,667. Adding machines that do not own the key does not help.

替代与故障：别让重试把热点继续放大Alternatives and failures: retries can amplify a hotspot

热点可缓存允许旧的可覆盖值，也可合并并发读取、拆分可聚合的计数或增加专用只读副本，但每一种都要说明失效和新鲜度。对余额直接拆成多个 key 不能自动恢复原子扣减。一次请求的超时重试还会把同一热点再问一轮，所以在 Coordinator 限制在途 RPC、设置总 deadline、控制重试次数；总超时到了就返回明确状态，而不是继续无上限占用副本线程。

For suitable values, cache stale-tolerant state, coalesce reads, split aggregatable counters or add read-only copies, specifying freshness and invalidation. Splitting a balance does not restore atomic deductions. Retries multiply the same hotspot, so cap in-flight RPCs, use a total deadline and bound attempts rather than occupy replica threads indefinitely.

图在成功后异步修 A，是本章选定的读修复策略。修复必须保留 v2 的原版本，不能生成新的“修复时间戳”。读修复的覆盖取决于实际读到哪些副本与哪些 key，没人访问的冷键不会自动被它发现。成员版本改变后还要做范围交接和后台比较，不能拿旧的三副本全集证明新位置的数据可读。正常路径、迁移路径和修复路径应当各自有预算。

Our example repairs A asynchronously after success, preserving v2’s original version. Read repair only covers observed copies and keys; cold keys are not discovered. Membership changes also need range handoff and background comparison. An old three-owner proof cannot prove data exists at new owners. Budget normal traffic, migration and repair separately.

分区：严格 quorum 与 sloppy quorum 改了什么Partition: what strict and sloppy quorum change

故障窗口：只剩 A 可达时，先承认失败Failure window: admit failure when only A is reachable

第一条路线维持正常 owner 集合 {A,B,C}。Coordinator 在 A 这一侧，能拿到一份耐久 ACK，B、C 不可达，1<2，所以拒绝确认。这不是“节点数足够、稍后最终一致”就能掩盖的失败；协议明文要求两份正常副本。B、C 一侧如果拥有协调入口，则可在两份仍可达的前提下继续，但 A 一侧不能。可用性必须按这次操作、这个 key 和这个分区位置描述。

The strict path keeps normal owners {A,B,C}. Coordinator on A’s side gets one durable ACK; B and C are unreachable, so 1<2 and confirmation fails. The protocol requires two normal owners. The B/C side may continue if it has a coordinator and both copies remain reachable; A’s side cannot. State availability per operation, key and partition side.

图里的红线是 RPC 结果失败，不代表所有落盘都被取消。超时后的重试要遵守前面的 mutation 标识规则，还要让系统总 deadline 终止额外工作。Coordinator 自己挂掉也会让已经成功写入两份的请求失去响应；客户端不能因为没收到 200 就自动断言值还在旧版本。这个窗口应暴露为可查询或可安全重试的结果，而不是藏在“高可用”标签后面。

Red paths mean failed RPC outcomes, not cancellation of every persisted mutation. Keep mutation identity across retries and terminate additional work at the total deadline. Coordinator can fail after two replicas persist and before replying. No 200 does not prove the old version remains; expose a queryable or safely retryable unknown outcome.

替代：借用临时副本，交集证明也随之改变Alternative: a fallback changes the overlap proof

如果业务明确接受分区时接写，图再切换到 Dynamo 风格的 sloppy quorum：正常 owner 不够时从扩展候选里选可达机器 D，保存带目标副本信息的临时记录。这里假设 A、D 都可达且都耐久保存，才能凑两份后成功；不是任意严重故障都保证成功。D 只是临时承担 B 的数据，最终要交还，不能无限积压。Dynamo 论文 §4.6 描述这一机制。[1]

If the business accepts writes during partitions, switch to a Dynamo-style sloppy policy: choose a reachable D from an extended list and store a temporary copy tagged for its intended owner. Success assumes both A and D can durably store; it is not guaranteed under arbitrary failure. D must eventually hand off. Dynamo §4.6 describes this mechanism. [1]

本例写集合 {A,D}，网络恢复后一次读可能取 {B,C}，两组没有共同元素。W=2、R=2、N=3 的算术看似没变，实际上 D 不在原全集里；相加超过三不能再证明相交。读取要允许旧、能返回冲突，或等待移交恢复原 owner 状态。这是继续接写付出的具体代价，不能一边借 D，一边承诺所有读仍满足正常 3/2/2 的新鲜度。

Write set {A,D} and a later read set {B,C} are disjoint. W=2,R=2,N=3 appears unchanged, but D lies outside the original universe. The sum no longer proves overlap. Reads must accept staleness, expose conflicts or wait for handoff. Continued writes cannot retain the normal 3/2/2 freshness argument for free.

Cassandra 的 hint 记录漏发的 mutation，用来补齐指定副本；通常的 QUORUM 成功仍要正常副本数量满足条件。官方 hints 示例正是三副本里两份 ACK 后成功，第三份由协调节点留 hint。不能把“有 hints”推导成“必然实行 Dynamo sloppy quorum”。相同术语在不同系统里的角色要看成功条件，这也是图把临时 D 与本地 Hint log 分成两组来画的原因。[3]

Cassandra hints retain a missed mutation for its intended owner; ordinary QUORUM success still requires enough owner acknowledgements. Its official example succeeds with two of three replica ACKs and retains a hint for the third. Hints do not imply Dynamo sloppy quorum. We therefore draw temporary D separately from the local Hint log. [3]

为什么这样选：故障检测与成员权威分开Why: separate failure suspicion from membership authority

超时和 Gossip 只能提供节点可能不可达的线索，不能证明机器永久死亡。若每个观察者各自把慢节点踢出，再按自己的三个成员计算 quorum，就会出现彼此不相交的新全集。稳定成员版本、带版本的请求和受控范围交接是放置正确性的接口；本章不展开成员变更协议，但不能省略这个前提。需要不能丢的条件更新时，转向共识与串行化，而不是把 W 拉到 N 就宣称已经解决。

Timeouts and Gossip indicate possible unreachability, not permanent death. Independently removing slow nodes can create disjoint replica universes that each claim quorum. Stable membership versions, versioned requests and controlled range handoff are placement interfaces. For lossless conditional updates, use consensus and serialization rather than declaring W=N sufficient.

冲突：LWW 得到一个胜者，代价是丢掉其他更新Conflicts: LWW selects one winner and discards other updates

正常规则：所有节点比较同一套排序字段Normal rule: compare the same ordering fields everywhere

本章用可覆盖的用户偏好值解释 LWW：mutation 带时间戳，比较较大者；教学实现还需给同戳定义确定性决胜字段，例如 mutation_id 的固定字节顺序。比较规则必须在读、写、修复和删除中一致。Cassandra 文档说明其 mutation 时间戳可来自客户端或 Coordinator，并按单元格执行 LWW；这里把整个 value 当一个决胜单元，是教学接口，不能把它误说成 Cassandra 多列更新总会作为整体胜出。[2]

For replaceable preferences, mutations carry timestamps and larger ones win. Our implementation also needs a deterministic equal-timestamp tie-break, such as a fixed byte ordering of mutation_id, consistently used for writes, reads, repair and deletion. Cassandra documents client/coordinator timestamps and per-cell LWW. Our whole-value winner is a teaching interface, not a claim that a multi-column Cassandra write always wins as a whole. [2]

四帧图只画状态与真实时间先后，箭头不是 RPC，因而不添加伪造的回程。X 的真实时间 1000、写入戳 1200；Y 的真实时间 1100、写入戳 1100。真实先后差 100 ms，X 时钟却快 200 ms，故排序仍是 X>Y。选择 X 会收敛到同一个结果，但业务上更晚的 Y 被覆盖。即使两次写都拿到了足够 ACK，排序规则也不会因为确认数量多就变正确。

The four frames show state and real-time precedence, not RPCs. X has real time 1000 and timestamp 1200; Y has both 1100. Y occurs 100 ms later but X’s 200 ms clock lead makes X>Y. Choosing X converges while discarding the later update. More ACKs do not correct the ordering rule.

为什么这样选：收敛、因果和业务正确是三件事Why: convergence, causality and business correctness differ

物理时间戳容易生成，不需要集中分配全局版本，适合允许覆盖的状态。代价是并发里只保留一个胜者，并且错误时钟可能让后续一段时间内的写都输掉。时间同步、限制异常未来戳可以减小操作风险，但不会把 LWW 变成无损合并。每个节点自己的计数器也不自动构成可比较的全局版本；“时间戳或版本号都行”必须先交代版本从哪里来、顺序代表什么。

Physical timestamps avoid a centralized global version allocator and suit overwrite-tolerant state. They select one winner and a future timestamp may defeat later writes for a while. Time synchronization and bounding future timestamps reduce risk without making LWW lossless. Independent node counters are not automatically global versions; explain where order comes from.

举一个能翻车的业务例子：两个客户端都读到计数 10，一个写 11，另一个也写 11。无论哪个时间戳更大，LWW 最终只是 11，需求里的两次加一已经丢了一次。购物车两个并发快照各新增不同商品，也可能只留下一个快照。可以按独立字段存储降低冲突范围，或对确实可交换、可合并的操作使用合适的 CRDT；涉及“不低于零”“只能卖一件”的约束，仍需协调，不能靠一个集合合并公式代替。

Two clients read counter 10 and each writes 11. Either LWW winner leaves 11, losing one intended increment. Two cart snapshots adding different items can lose one snapshot. Separate independent fields or use a suitable CRDT for mergeable operations. Invariants such as nonnegative stock or selling a unique item still need coordination.

替代：保留冲突意味着应用要承担合并Alternative: retaining conflicts creates a merge obligation

Dynamo 的向量版本用于辨别祖先与并发兄弟，并可把兄弟版本交给应用合并；它不是“落后年代才用的东西”。是否采用，取决于业务能不能定义有意义的合并、版本元数据和读写接口能否承担复杂度。LWW、因果版本、CRDT、条件更新各解不同约束，不能用“生产默认”代替选择。[1]

Dynamo’s vector versions distinguish ancestors from concurrent siblings that applications may reconcile. The choice depends on meaningful merge semantics, metadata and interface complexity. LWW, causal versions, CRDTs and conditional updates address different contracts; a generic “production default” cannot choose among them. [1]

深挖：两条向量怎么手算并发Deep dive: calculate concurrency with two vectors

把省略的分量视作零。版本 [A:2,B:0] 与 [A:1,B:1] 不能按“总数都是 2”判相同：前者在 A 上较大，后者在 B 上较大，谁都不逐项支配谁，所以并发。若合并生成 [A:2,B:2]，它逐项不小于两者且至少一项更大，才可视为两者的后继。检测到并发不等于知道购物车如何合并，那个决定仍属于应用。

Treat absent components as zero. [A:2,B:0] and [A:1,B:1] are not equal because both sums are 2: each is larger in one component and neither dominates, so they are concurrent. A merged [A:2,B:2] dominates both. Detecting concurrency does not decide cart merge semantics; the application still does.

节点内 LSM：前台确认，后台还带宽与空间的账Local LSM: foreground confirmation, background bandwidth and space

正常路径：先解释本地 ACK，再解释 flushWalkthrough: define local ACK before flush

本章刻意选择 WAL 同步后 ACK 的耐久规则。写进操作系统缓存，只能抵御部分进程故障；机器掉电可能丢掉尚未同步的尾部。RocksDB Basic Operations 明确区分默认非同步写和 sync=true，后者等待持久存储。把默认 RocksDB、Cassandra 的某种 commit log 同步配置和本图自己的耐久假设混成一个规则，会让三副本确认的含义变模糊。[4]

Our ACK rule requires WAL sync. OS buffering can survive some process failures but lose an unsynced suffix on machine power loss. RocksDB Basic Operations distinguishes default non-sync writes from sync=true, which waits for persistent storage. Do not conflate that default, Cassandra commit-log settings and our explicit durability assumption. [4]

WAL 之后应用到可读的 memtable，才对协调层回本地 ACK。内存表达到阈值后冻结，后台输出不可变 SSTable，新写进入下一张内存表。新文件安全发布后，才能回收已被持久文件覆盖的日志；崩溃恢复重放仍需的 WAL。RocksDB Overview 把 memtable、sstfile、logfile 列为三个基础结构，并解释 flush 与 compaction 的关系。图只画一次 flush 与下一层输出，层数不是固定只有两层。[5]

Apply the mutation to readable Memtable after WAL, then send the local ACK. Freeze full tables, flush immutable SSTables and accept new writes in a new table. Retire covered logs after safe file publication; replay remaining WAL on recovery. RocksDB Overview describes Memtable, sstfile, logfile, flush and compaction. Our two files illustrate one flush and one next-level output, not a fixed two-level engine. [5]

读路径：Bloom 只能排除某个候选文件Read path: Bloom excludes an individual candidate file

一次本地 GET 先查内存中的候选版本，再查可能含 key 的文件。对于本章按 mutation 戳比较的 value，不能因为内存里碰巧有一份就无条件提前结束：重放或时钟偏斜可能把旧戳写进新文件，墓碑也要参与比较。具体引擎可根据索引、序列与版本规则安全地缩小搜索；Cassandra 的按单元格 reconciliation 和 RocksDB 的序列号快照是不同语义。面试先说比较正确，再讨论优化到少读几个文件。

A local GET checks memory candidates and relevant files. With our mutation-timestamp semantics, a memory hit cannot unconditionally terminate: replay or clock skew may put an older timestamp in newer storage, and tombstones participate. Engines can safely prune using indexes and their own version rules. Cassandra cell reconciliation and RocksDB sequence snapshots are different semantics; establish correctness before pruning.

普通 Bloom 对一个正确构建的文件索引，只会给“肯定不在”或“可能在”；前者跳过该文件，不代表全库不存在，后者还要查索引与数据。教学按 10 个阴性文件、每个 1% 假阳性，期望额外探查 10×0.01=0.1 个文件；若还假设误报独立，至少一次误报概率是 1−0.99¹⁰≈9.56%。这是阴性点查的期望，不是所有查询的 p99。官方表给约 9.9 bit/key 对应约 1% 误报；按 10 bit 口算：100,000,000×10÷8=125,000,000 B=125 MB，三份约 375 MB，未计多版本和开销。[6]

A correctly built ordinary Bloom filter says definitely absent or possibly present in one file. Absence skips that file, not the database; a positive still needs lookup. For ten absent files at 1% false positives, expected extra probes are 10×0.01=0.1. With independent false positives, any false positive occurs with 1−0.99¹⁰≈9.56%, not a p99 bound. The official table gives about 9.9 bit/key for 1%; rounding to 10 gives 100,000,000×10÷8=125,000,000 B=125 MB per filter, or 375 MB for three copies, before versions and overhead. [6]

为什么这样选：吞吐快，不等于写放大小Why: high throughput does not mean low write amplification

顺序日志和内存批次把随机改页的工作移出前台，但 SSTable 会在 compaction 中被读取、合并和重写。A7 把累计 SST 输出字节除以逻辑 value 写入字节定义为教学写放大 10：10 MB/s×3×10=300 MB/s 全集群 SST 输出，约 2.4 Gb/s 等价字节速率。它是磁盘输出口径，不是网络复制需求；如果 WAL 也写相同载荷，再加约 30 MB/s，compaction 的输入读取另算。不要把空间系数 2 当成写放大 2。

Logging and memory batches move random-page work out of the foreground, but compaction reads, merges and rewrites SST data. A7 defines teaching SST write amplification as cumulative SST output divided by logical value bytes: 10 MB/s×3×10=300 MB/s cluster-wide, equivalent to 2.4 Gb/s of bytes, not network replication. Add about 30 MB/s if WAL writes the same payload, plus compaction reads. Space factor 2 is not write amplification 2.

长时间写入时，平均后台处理速率必须盖住输入。否则冻结 memtable 和 L0 文件越积越多，读需要查更多候选，内存与临时空间也上升，最后前台被限速或停写。给 compaction 留独立 I/O 和空间余量、控制并发与流入，比只把“峰值写入很快”写在答案里完整。写压力很小、点查延迟严的场景，可以比较 B+Tree；LSM 的选择是把成本在时间上搬到后台，不是把成本删除。

Under sustained writes, background throughput must cover input. Otherwise immutable Memtables and L0 files accumulate, reads face more candidates and memory/temporary space rise until writes throttle or stall. Reserve I/O and space and control concurrency and inflow. Low-write, tight point-latency workloads may prefer a B+Tree; LSM moves cost to the background rather than deleting it.

修复与删除：旧副本回来以后，不能让数据复活Repair and deletion: returning replicas must not resurrect data

正常路径：短暂漏写留 hint，冷键另做反熵Walkthrough: hints for missed delivery, anti-entropy for cold keys

本图 A、B 已确认，C 不可达；这一种故障严格 quorum 可以继续。Coordinator 记录给 C 的原始 mutation，恢复时重放；确认对方应用成功后再释放已交付 hint。本章把 hint 保留暂定为 1 h，不能抄成产品默认。下载核对的 Cassandra 官方文档写默认 hint 窗口 3 h，超出窗口的差异需要读修复或后台反熵。两者都必须受带宽与存储预算约束。[3]

A and B confirmed while C is unreachable, so strict quorum can continue here. Coordinator retains the original mutation for C, replays it after recovery and releases a delivered hint after application is acknowledged. Our 1 h retention is an assumption; Cassandra’s checked documentation lists a 3 h default hint window and requires read repair or anti-entropy beyond it. Bound both storage and delivery traffic. [3]

读修复只覆盖被读到的差异；hint 可能过期，保留它的机器也可能坏掉。后台反熵按范围比较副本状态，先定位差异再同步相关记录，不必每轮重传完整全集。Merkle tree 的根不同只说明某处有差异，要继续向子范围定位，不能“只发一个 hash 就把数据修好”。图的橙线表示 B、C 之间的后台范围同步，绿色回程表示完成反馈；细节展开留给 Ch41，主路径仍要给它留 I/O 余量。

Read repair only covers observed differences; hints expire and their holder can fail. Background anti-entropy compares ranges, locates differences and synchronizes records. Different Merkle roots indicate a difference but require descending into subranges; a hash alone does not repair data. The orange B/C path represents background range synchronization, with a completion reply. Reserve I/O; details belong in Ch41.

为什么要墓碑：缺失没有表达删除的历史Why tombstones: absence does not record deletion

A、B 都保存 DEL@300，而 C 保留 v2@200，修复按同一版本规则选择墓碑。若 A、B 只是物理抹掉 value，比较时它们只表现为“没有”，C 的旧值就可能重新复制回来。TTL 到期也应留下能传播的删除信息，而不是让一台节点悄悄抹掉本地记录。Cassandra tombstones 文档用三副本缺少删除标记的例子解释 zombie；删除正是一次携带时间戳的写。[7]

A and B retain DEL@300 while C has v2@200, so the same version rule selects deletion during repair. Physical erasure on A/B leaves only absence and lets C’s old value return. TTL expiry also needs propagatable deletion information. Cassandra’s tombstone documentation illustrates zombie resurrection in a three-copy example; deletion is a timestamped mutation. [7]

A11 教学保留墓碑 7 天，后台修复最长 2 天，加上允许离线最长 1 天，是 3 天，3<7 留出 4 天操作余量。这不是证明所有故障都能在 7 天内修完：机器离线更久、修复失败、临时副本遗漏都破坏假设。要监控范围修复是否成功，超过离线边界的副本需要隔离并重建，不能直接把它重新接入普通读写。删除的安全性因此连接到了成员交接与运维边界。

A11 retains tombstones for seven days; a two-day repair bound plus one-day outage gives three days, leaving four days of margin. This does not prove recovery under longer outages, failed repair or forgotten fallbacks. Monitor successful range repair; isolate and rebuild replicas exceeding the outage boundary before normal service. Deletion safety depends on membership and operational limits.

故障窗口：到了时间，也不等于能立刻清理Failure window: elapsed time is not sufficient to purge

文档的 gc_grace_seconds 默认是 864,000 s，864,000÷86,400=10 天；这是核对过的 Cassandra 默认，与本章教学 7 天不同。过期后仍由 compaction 决定何时清理，且要检查同 key 的旧数据是否在参与合并的文件中、是否满足修复安全条件。墓碑若先被丢掉，另一个旧 SSTable 或长离线副本就可能重新提供旧值。把 grace 配成很短，只是减少暂存字节，不能让这些条件自动成立。[7]

The documented Cassandra gc_grace_seconds default is 864,000 s; 864,000÷86,400=10 days, distinct from our seven-day assumption. Expiry still requires compaction and checking older data coverage and repair safety. Premature tombstone removal can let an old SSTable or long-offline replica restore the value. A short grace reduces retained bytes without establishing these conditions. [7]

重放必须保留原时间戳和 mutation 身份。假设旧 hint 中是 PUT@200，键后来已被 DEL@300 删除；若重放时把 PUT 改为当前时间 400，就人为制造了 400>300 的“新写”，删除会被覆盖。正常重放 PUT@200 应输给 DEL@300。图示重放的是原始 DEL@300，同样不能把它变成一个更晚的删除，否则还可能删掉 300 之后合法重建的值。修复是在传递事实，不是在创建新的业务动作。

Replay must retain the original timestamp and mutation identity. An old PUT@200 hint replayed after DEL@300 must lose. Retimestamping it at 400 manufactures a new write and resurrects the value. Likewise replaying DEL@300 as a later delete can erase a legitimate later recreation. Repair transports prior facts, not new business actions.

追问怎么接：让每个答案落回一个条件Follow-ups: connect each answer to a condition

要立刻读到自己刚写的值，怎么改？How do I immediately read my own write?

先问“刚写的”指收到成功还是结果未知。固定全集、版本顺序正确且没有并发覆盖时，本章 3/2/2 可以读到已经完成的那次写；分区替代路径和错误时钟不在这个证明里。需要跨请求的读写会话契约，可以让成功响应携带版本并让读侧检查至少见到该版本，否则等待或返回失败。若要求条件更新和所有客户端的实时顺序，改用相应的协调协议，不能只调 R。

Clarify whether the write succeeded or timed out. Under fixed membership, correct ordering and no concurrent overwrite, 3/2/2 can read the completed write. Sloppy fallback and clock errors are outside that proof. A session may carry a returned version and require reads to observe it or wait/fail. Conditional updates and global real-time order require coordination, not just a larger R.

Redis Cluster 也是 KV，可以直接当这张图吗？Can Redis Cluster replace this diagram?

它有 16,384 个 hash slot，槽由 primary 负责，并异步复制到 replica；这是另一种复制与故障转移契约。官方规范写明已确认写仍有可能在复制尚未到副本、primary 故障后丢失。因此不把它笼统说成“严格 CP”或拿来证明本章的多写副本 quorum。系统都能存 key/value，不代表 Client→Coordinator→三个对等副本的路径能原封不动套上。[8]

Redis Cluster has 16,384 hash slots served by primaries with asynchronous replicas, a different contract. Its specification allows acknowledged-write loss when a primary fails before replication completes. Avoid calling it strictly CP or using it to prove peer-replica quorum behavior. A shared key/value interface does not imply the same replication diagram. [8]

复制放大、空间放大、写放大能只用一个倍数吗？Can one factor represent replication, space and write amplification?

不能。三副本把逻辑 value 数乘 3；SST 空间系数 2 描述同时保留的文件；SST 写放大 10 描述一段时间累计输出。100 GB×3×2≈600 GB 与 10 MB/s×3×10=300 MB/s 的单位不同。新增记录还要看留存与增长，不能用峰值 PUT 数直接乘一年，本章 PUT 中很多是覆盖已有 key。持续吞吐、存量与新增速度是三种预算。

No. Three replicas multiply logical copies; SST space factor 2 describes resident files; SST write amplification 10 describes accumulated output. 100 GB×3×2≈600 GB and 10 MB/s×3×10=300 MB/s have different units. New-record growth and retention are separate; peak PUTs include overwrites, so multiplying them by a year does not estimate retained keys.

确认两份，是不是再也不会丢？Do two acknowledgements guarantee no later loss?

只有在 ACK 真正耐久、故障域独立程度满足需求、剩余副本及时修复的边界内，才能说抵御指定故障。两台机架同时损坏、两份都只有未同步缓存、错误合并覆盖正确值，是不同问题。备份还要覆盖误删与长期灾难，并定期验证恢复；复制把同一个删除发到三份，不能替你保留删除前的历史。收尾时说明这些边界。

Durable ACKs, suitable failure domains and timely repair resist specified failures. Two rack losses, unsynced buffers and incorrect reconciliation are distinct risks. Backups cover accidental deletion and long-term disaster and require restore verification; three replicated deletes do not preserve prior history. State these bounds when closing.
