30 秒开场与边界The 30-second open and scope

中文

我会从一台机器上的应用进程加本地数据库画起，不先画终图。假设日活一百万、每人每天 20 次请求、95% 是读、峰值按平均的 10 倍。平均大约 231.5 QPS，峰值大约 2,315。这个峰值会先打穿单机 CPU 和单库读预算，所以我按顺序加：拆出数据库、负载均衡和第二台应用、副本和缓存、会话外置和异步队列。基本假设下写峰值大约 116 QPS，单主写预算还够，这一轮不分片。多地域双活只点名，不画进第一张图。

这章练的是演进。百万用户先收成三个澄清：日活还是注册总量，读写各占多少、写完会不会立刻读，这一轮是否只做单个地域。问完就把假设写在白板上。面试官改数字，后面的除法跟着改。本章所有 QPS 都是假设算出来的，不是任何一家公司的线上数字。

产品只固定到能把路径走通：一个读多写少的社区。GET /home 看时间线，POST /posts 发帖，再异步通知关注者。账户、帖子和会话要事务，所以第一版权威存储是一台关系库。全文搜索、推荐、计费、静态边缘分发都不在这张图上。多地域双活只点名：分区时两个地域都接写，就要准备合并；单地域的主从超时，已经够把一致性选择讲清楚。

English

I start with one app process and a local database on one machine. I do not open on the final picture. Assume one million daily actives, 20 requests each per day, 95% reads, and a 10× peak. That is about 231.5 QPS on average and about 2,315 at the peak. The peak breaks a single box’s CPU and a single primary’s read budget first, so I add, in order: a separate database, a load balancer and a second app, a replica and a cache, then an external session store and an async queue. Under the base assumptions the write peak is about 116 QPS, still inside one primary’s write budget, so this round does not shard. Multi-region active-active is named and not drawn.

This chapter is the evolution method. It is not a URL shortener and not a recital of one product’s end state. “A million users” collapses into three questions: daily actives or signups, the read/write mix and whether a user reads what they just wrote, and whether this round can live in one region. Write the assumptions on the board. If the interviewer changes a number, the arithmetic changes with it. Waiting in silence for a secret production QPS does not help. Every QPS on this page is computed from labeled assumptions, not from any company’s live traffic.

The product is only fixed enough to walk a path: a read-heavy community. GET /home reads the caller’s timeline. POST /posts creates a post and notifies followers asynchronously. Accounts, posts, and sessions need keys and transactions, so version one keeps authority in one relational primary. Full-text search, recommendations, billing, and edge distribution of static bytes stay off the diagram. Multi-region active-active can be named as a later problem: once both regions accept writes during a partition, you must plan the merge. A single-region primary timeout is already enough to force the consistency choice.

算术要写出除法，并写明它改了哪一笔

一天的请求数是 1,000,000 × 20 = 20,000,000。再除以一天的秒数：20,000,000 ÷ 86,400 = 231.48，口播成平均 231.5 QPS。峰值系数是假设 A4 的 10 倍，231.5 × 10 = 2,315 QPS。读占 95%，2,315 × 0.95 = 2,199；写占 5%，2,315 × 0.05 = 116。后面每加一个盒子，都要能指回这组数。改了日活或每人请求数，就重做这一次除法，不要把 2,315 背成行业常数。

单进程先死在 CPU。假设每个请求在应用里烧掉 4 毫秒 CPU，这是 A5，不是测量。平均 231.5 × 0.004 ≈ 0.93 核，八核机器在平均负载上还剩得下数据库。峰值 2,315 × 0.004 ≈ 9.3 核，应用单独就超过 8 核。被拿掉的部署是「峰值时应用仍和数据库挤在同一台机器上」。换 16 核能再撑一轮垂直扩容，但进程崩溃和单块磁盘这两个单点都还在。

读预算把缓存加进来，写预算把分片先拿掉。假设 A7：读 p99 不超过 200 毫秒时，主库承担 400 次简单读每秒，写预算是 800 笔简单事务每秒。这两个数是设计预算，不是某款数据库的标称上限。2,199 ÷ 400 = 5.5，单主接全部读不成立。假设 A8 把命中率 90% 定为要挣到的目标。若全部读都允许旧，回源读是 2,199 × 0.10 ≈ 220，低于 400；加入写后强读后用下一节的分流口径重算。写峰值 116 低于 800，大约还有 6.9 倍余量，所以基本假设下不分片。

存储也先不分片。假设 A6：注册用户是日活的 5 倍，5,000,000 个账户；每人 20 条帖子；行加上索引按 2 KB 算，每人 40 KB。5,000,000 × 40 KB = 200 GB 原始数据。再复制一份是 400 GB。单主的数据预算我定为 2 TB，400 GB 在预算里面。数据量撑不起「第一天就分片」这个理由。分片要等面试官把写放大，那笔算术放在副本和队列都讲完之后。

Show the division, and say which stroke it changes

Requests per day are 1,000,000 × 20 = 20,000,000. Divide by the seconds in a day: 20,000,000 ÷ 86,400 = 231.48, spoken as 231.5 average QPS. Assumption A4’s peak factor is 10, so 231.5 × 10 = 2,315 QPS. Reads are 95%, 2,315 × 0.95 = 2,199. Writes are 5%, 2,315 × 0.05 = 116. Every later box has to point back at this set. If daily actives or requests per user change, redo the division. Do not memorize 2,315 as an industry constant.

The single process dies on CPU before it dies on “not enough boxes.” Assumption A5 spends 4 ms of app CPU per request. That is not a measurement. The average is 231.5 × 0.004 ≈ 0.93 cores, so an 8-core box still has room for the database at the average. The peak is 2,315 × 0.004 ≈ 9.3 cores. The app alone exceeds 8 cores and the database has nothing left to run queries. The deployment this removes is “at the peak, the app still shares one machine with the database.” A 16-core box can buy one more round of vertical scale. A process crash and a single disk are still there. Vertical scale is a fair alternative for the average or a short campaign. It is not the answer to the peak plus a single point of failure.

The read budget adds the cache. The write budget removes sharding for now. Assumption A7: under our own target that read p99 stays within 200 ms, the primary takes 400 simple reads per second, and 800 simple write transactions per second. Those are design budgets, not a vendor’s nameplate. 2,199 ÷ 400 = 5.5, so the read peak is five and a half budgets. One primary cannot absorb every read. Assumption A8 sets a 90% hit rate as a goal we must earn, not a measurement. If all reads were stale-ok, origin would be 2,199 × 0.10 ≈ 220, under 400; the next section recomputes it with strong-read bypasses. The write peak of 116 is under 800, with about 6.9× of headroom, so the base diagram stops after the queue and does not shard.

Storage does not shard yet either. Assumption A6: five registered accounts per daily active, 5,000,000 accounts, 20 posts each, 2 KB per row including index overhead, 40 KB per account. 5,000,000 × 40 KB = 200 GB raw. One extra copy is 400 GB. The single-primary data budget I am using is 2 TB. 400 GB sits inside it. Bytes are not a day-one sharding argument. Sharding waits until the interviewer turns the write rate up. That division comes after replicas and the queue.

延迟数量级只用来比较，并且标明年份Latency orders of magnitude are for comparison, and they carry a year

Jeff Dean 在 2009 年 LADIS 的 “Numbers Everyone Should Know” 里给出：主存引用大约 100 纳秒，同机房往返 500,000 纳秒也就是 0.5 毫秒，磁盘寻道 10,000,000 纳秒也就是 10 毫秒，加州到荷兰再回到加州 150,000,000 纳秒也就是 150 毫秒。[2] 寻道是当时机械盘的数量级，不是 2026 年的 NVMe。同机房八次串行 SQL 大约 4 毫秒，相对假设的 200 毫秒读目标很便宜，所以拆库是为了分开核和故障域，不是为了省延迟。跨洋 150 毫秒说明第一张图不画第二个地域。

示例目标同样是假设，用来决定故障时保哪一边：读路径可用性按 99.9% 来设计，p99 不超过 200 毫秒；写确认 p99 不超过 300 毫秒，通知可以晚几秒。面试时说「这是我为了继续设计写下的目标，请你改」。不要把它们说成某家社区产品的内部 SLO。

In the “Numbers Everyone Should Know” slide of his 2009 LADIS keynote, Jeff Dean listed orders of magnitude: a main-memory reference around 100 ns, a round trip in the same datacenter at 500,000 ns (0.5 ms), a disk seek at 10,000,000 ns (10 ms), and a California–Netherlands–California packet at 150,000,000 ns (150 ms). [2] The seek line is a spinning-disk figure from that talk, not a 2026 NVMe measurement, so it is not this chapter’s disk latency. Two comparisons still hold. One extra SQL round trip inside a datacenter is on the 0.5 ms step; eight serial queries are about 4 ms, cheap next to a 200 ms read p99. Splitting the database off the app is about cores and failure domains, not about saving that hop. The transoceanic 150 ms is a different step. It is why the first picture has no second region: the round trip alone would spend a large part of the read budget before the app runs. The cache exists to keep hot rows on the memory side of that gap, not to recite 10 ms as today’s disk.

The example targets are assumptions too, used to decide which side a failure protects. Design the read path toward 99.9% availability and a p99 within 200 ms. The write acknowledgement stays within a 300 ms p99. Notifications may take seconds. Say “these are targets I wrote down so the design can continue; please correct them.” Do not present them as a community product’s internal SLO.

这一轮明确不画的东西What this round deliberately does not draw

多地域双活不进第一张图。跨洋同步会把写确认预算吃掉。Brewer 在 2012 年写过：平时可以同时要一致性和可用性，超时出现时才必须为这一次操作二选一；「三选二」太粗，选择可以细到某类操作、某行、某个用户。[1] 双活等于分区时两边都可能写，恢复时要合并。静态边缘分发、搜索和推荐后置，第一天的瓶颈还没指到它们。

Multi-region active-active stays off the first picture. Dean’s 0.5 ms and 150 ms already show that a transoceanic synchronous commit would consume the write budget. Brewer wrote in 2012 that a system can keep both consistency and availability until a partition or a timeout, and only then must choose for that operation. “Pick two of three” is too coarse, and the choice can be as fine as one operation, one row, or one user. [1] Active-active means both sides may write during a partition and must merge afterward. A primary timeout inside one region already forces that choice, so a second region is not required to tell it. Edge caching of static bytes, a search index, and recommendations wait too. They change read semantics, and day-one bottlenecks do not point at them yet.

单进程到负载均衡：会话问题在这里出现One process to a load balancer: the session problem shows up here

把 GET /home 在单机上走完

用户带上 Cookie sid=8f3 请求 GET /home。连接进应用进程，进程在内存里查出 user_id=42，在同一台机器的库里查时间线，把帖子放进 200 返回。回程箭头表示请求结束了；没有它，这张图只是两个名词。库和进程共享一块盘、一组核、一个故障域。进程崩溃或盘损坏，站点不可用，而且没有第二份帖子可以升起来。

拆开的理由是峰值 CPU，不是延迟。9.3 核对 8 核，数据库在峰值时分不到查询时间。拆到两台主机之后，应用和库各用自己的核和盘。代价是每次 SQL 多一次同机房往返。按 Dean 2009 年那张表，这一跳是 0.5 毫秒；首页串行 8 次查询大约 4 毫秒。[2] 相对 200 毫秒的读目标，这 4 毫秒是拆分的账单，不是拆分的理由。

垂直加核在平均负载上成立。0.93 核可以留在一台更大的机器上，省掉内网和第二份部署。它赢的条件是峰值系数没那么高。面试官坚持 10 倍峰值时，16 核仍把 9.3 核的应用和库的查询挤在一起，进程和磁盘的单点还在。口播是：平均可以垂直；峰值加单点故障，就拆主机。

Walk GET /home on one host

The user sends GET /home with Cookie sid=8f3. DNS points the name at this machine, and the connection enters the app process. The process maps the sid to user_id=42 in its own memory, runs the timeline query on the database on the same machine, and returns the posts in a 200 body. The reply arrow is the end of the request. Without it the drawing is two nouns. The database and the process share one disk, one pool of cores, and one failure domain. A process crash, a ruined disk, or the operating system killing both makes the site unavailable, and there is no second copy of the posts to promote.

Peak CPU is the arithmetic that splits them. Latency is not. 9.3 cores against 8 leaves the database without query time at the peak. After the split, the app can fill its own cores, and the database keeps its own cores and its own disk. The cost is one same-datacenter round trip per SQL call. On Dean’s 2009 table that hop is the 0.5 ms step. Eight serial queries on the home page are about 4 ms of round trips. [2] Next to a 200 ms read target, those 4 ms are the bill for the split, not the reason for it. The bill is cheap, so do not stuff the peak and the database back together to avoid one network hop.

Adding cores is a real alternative, and it wins at the average. 0.93 cores can stay on one bigger machine and skip the private network, the second deploy, and the second monitor. It wins when the peak factor is milder, or when the surge is a few days rather than every busy hour. If the interviewer holds the 10× peak, a 16-core box only packs a 9.3-core app next to the database’s queries. The process and the disk are still single points. Say it this way: vertical at the average; split hosts when the peak and a single failure both matter. More RAM is not horizontal scale.

负载均衡一出现，就要承认会话在内存里

第二台应用是 9.3 核逼出来的，不是为了图上看起来「有集群」。一台应用进程消化不了峰值 CPU，客户端却只应该看见一个地址，所以前面放负载均衡：健康检查把死进程摘掉，活着的进程分摊新连接。到这一笔为止，库仍然只有一份。均衡器解决的是应用进程的入口，不是数据的副本。

会话在加均衡器时就要标出来。登录时 App 1 把 sid 和 user_id 放进自己的内存。均衡器若轮询，下一个 GET /home 落到 App 2，内存里没有这张表，用户看起来像未登录。粘滞把同一 Cookie 钉在 App 1 上，能继续看时间线。窗口是：App 1 重启或被健康检查摘掉，内存表一起消失，用户要重新登录。两台机器的 CPU 都在干活，用户状态没有水平扩展。

「加了负载均衡就可以水平扩展」把入口复制和状态复制说成了同一件事。这一阶段的图是两台应用，标着 session in RAM，库上还贴着 SPOF。先不上第二台、只把单进程垂直加大，只在 CPU 预算还够时成立。峰值已经要第二台进程时，粘滞是下一笔要拆掉的状态，不是终点。

The moment the balancer appears, admit the session is in memory

The second app is forced by 9.3 cores, not by a desire to draw a cluster. One process cannot spend the peak CPU, and the client should still see one address, so a load balancer sits in front. Health checks drop a dead process, and live processes share new connections. The database is still one copy. The balancer fixes the app entrance, not the data’s copies.

Mark the session problem when the balancer appears. Do not wait until the second box is drawn and then pretend to notice. At login, App 1 stores sid and user_id in its own memory. If the balancer rotates connections, the next GET /home lands on App 2, which has no such table and treats the user as logged out. Pinning that cookie to App 1 is stickiness. It keeps this user on the timeline. The failure window is concrete: when App 1 restarts or fails a health check, the table dies with it and the user logs in again. Scale-in has to move sessions or the moved users drop offline too. Both machines spend CPU, and the user state has not scaled out.

The short line “a load balancer means we scale horizontally” treats a copied entrance as copied state. The honest picture at this stage is two app boxes labeled session in RAM, and a SPOF still sitting on the database. The alternative is to skip the second app and enlarge one process, which avoids stickiness. That alternative holds only while the CPU budget holds. Once the peak wants a second process, stickiness is the state the next stroke removes, not the place the design stops.

副本和缓存：哪条读命中，哪条仍回主库Replica and cache: which reads hit, which still reach the primary

三条读路径按这个顺序走

GET /home 进负载均衡，因粘滞落到持有 sid 的应用。应用先识别一致性要求，再决定是否查 feed:42。写成功响应携带 min_pos=(shard, history, commit_pos)，作者下一次 GET 带回这个下界。本章写后读直接绕缓存，在当前主库新快照中核对该历史与位点；若副本能证明连续 apply 到下界，也可改由副本服务。没有证明就等待、回当前主库或明确失败。只有允许旧的读先查共享缓存：命中返回，未命中由应用查副本再回填。图画一台应用的分流，另一台遵循相同规则。

写路径不能先改缓存。POST /posts 在主库插入并提交，响应附带最小提交位点；应用尝试删除 feed:42。删除失败要有重试责任，不能把已提交帖子猜成未写。Nishtala 等人的 demand-filled look-aside 也是由应用回源回填，写先 SQL 再 delete，因为删除幂等。[3] 但普通 DEL 不能挡住删除之后的旧读回填；其论文用缓存端原子校验的 lease token，并在 delete 时撤销旧 token。新 token 若从落后副本取得旧值也不自动满足写后读，所以强读仍先按位点分流。短 TTL 约束缓存驻留时间，不证明副本追平。

Walk the three read paths in this order

GET /home enters the load balancer and lands on the app holding sid. The app classifies consistency before consulting feed:42. A successful write returns min_pos=(shard, history, commit_pos); the author carries that bound on the next GET. Here read-your-writes bypass cache and check the bound in a fresh snapshot at the current primary. A replica may serve only after proving continuous apply through that position in the same history. Otherwise wait, use the current primary, or fail explicitly. Only stale-ok reads consult the shared cache first: hits return, misses let the app query the replica and refill. The second app follows the same routing.

The write path cannot update cache first. POST /posts commits on the primary and returns a minimum commit position; the app attempts to delete feed:42. Failed invalidation needs retry responsibility, rather than treating a committed post as absent. Nishtala et al. use application-driven look-aside fills and SQL followed by idempotent delete. [3] Ordinary DEL still permits a late old refill. Their lease token is checked atomically at the cache and revoked on delete. Even a new token can fetch old data from a lagging replica, so read-your-writes still route by position first. A short TTL bounds cache residence, not replication lag.

5.5 倍读预算逼出缓存；副本先解决单份数据

不加缓存时，峰值 2,199 次读全部落到主库，2,199 ÷ 400 = 5.5。命中率 90% 是假设 A8，要靠键和 TTL 挣出来。先按所有读都可缓存比较，回源约 220；写后读绕缓存后的基线约 324.3，见下节。若在前一比较里只给 70%，未命中是 2,199 × 0.30 ≈ 660，660 ÷ 400 ≈ 1.65，一台副本不够，要两台才把未命中放进预算。命中率守不住，副本就不只是可用性备份，而成了读容量，台数要重算。

90% 命中让普通读的回源预算看起来够了，副本仍然要加，因为帖子不能只留一份磁盘。默认同地域异步：201 等主库本地持久提交，不等副本 flush，更不等副本 apply。主库磁盘失效时，未复制的已确认尾部可能丢失。若把「抵御单主机/磁盘失效且不丢已确认写」写成硬约束，必须在成功前等至少一个独立故障域的合格副本持久确认；不足时不能降级返回满足该合同的成功，结果未知时按原请求身份查证。提升只选包含全部已确认前缀的副本，且先隔离旧主。[4][5] receive、flush、apply 是三层：持久确认不等于读可见。MySQL 8.4 的半同步 ACK 在 relay log 刷盘后发出，却会在等待超时后退回异步，因此名称不能充当合同。[6] Dean 的 0.5 ms 是网络往返数量级，实际等待还含刷盘；跨洋 150 ms 仅网络就吃掉 300 ms 预算的一半。[2]

只加副本、不加缓存，是命中目标不存在时的另一条路：2,199 ÷ 400 ≈ 6 台副本才能摊开峰值读。每台都要接日志，最慢的那台把陈旧窗口拉长，主库的写路径也不会变轻。缓存把大多数读留在内存一侧，副本数量按故障和剩余未命中来定。两者解决的约束不同，不能互相代替。

A 5.5× read budget adds the cache; the replica first fixes the single copy

Without a cache, all 2,199 peak reads land on the primary. Divided by the budget of 400, that is 5.5×. That division is why the cache appears, not a sentence like “reads are heavy so the industry adds a cache.” The 90% hit rate is assumption A8. Key design and TTL have to earn it. For the all-cacheable comparison, origin reads are about 220. With strong-read bypasses the baseline is 324.3, computed below. If the all-cacheable comparison allows only 70%, misses are 2,199 × 0.30 ≈ 660, and 660 ÷ 400 ≈ 1.65, so one replica cannot hold the misses. Two replicas are required to put misses inside the budget. If the hit rate cannot be defended, the replica stops being only an availability copy and becomes capacity, and the count has to be recomputed.

A replica is still needed even when ordinary cache misses fit, because posts need another disk copy. Default same-region async returns 201 after local durable commit, without waiting for replica flush or apply. Losing the primary disk can lose its unreplicated acknowledged suffix. If the contract is to preserve acknowledged writes through a single primary host/disk failure, success must wait for durable confirmation from an eligible replica in an independent failure domain. Missing confirmation cannot degrade into success under that contract; resolve uncertain results with the original request identity. Promote only a candidate containing the entire acknowledged prefix, after isolating the old primary. [4][5] Receive, flush, and apply are distinct: durability does not imply read visibility. MySQL 8.4 semi-sync acknowledges flushed relay logs but can fall back to async on timeout, so its name does not prove this contract. [6] Dean’s 0.5 ms is a network order of magnitude; actual waiting also includes flush. A 150 ms ocean round trip alone uses half the 300 ms budget. [2]

Replicas without a cache are a different road, the road where the 90% goal does not exist: about 2,199 ÷ 400 ≈ 6 replicas to spread the read peak. Each one applies the log, the slowest one stretches the stale window, and the primary’s write path is no lighter. The cache keeps most reads on the memory side, so replica count follows failure and the leftover misses, not the entire read peak. The two boxes answer different constraints. One does not stand in for the other.

写完立刻刷新，旧在哪一个窗口

用户发帖，主库提交，201 带回 min_pos。日志还没 apply 时，副本和共享缓存都可能缺这行。作者刷新必须先识别这个下界，再绕过没有足够证明的缓存；本章直接读当前主库的新快照。若切主后的历史不包含这个已确认位点，强读要报告无法满足，不能把回主库说成一定读到。允许旧的读仍可能拿到旧时间线，产品承诺要分开。

回主库的读要算进预算，并先把集合分开。把峰值 2,199 读/s 视为包含 116 次写后读的总量，剩余允许旧的读为 2,199−116=2,083。A8 的 90% 命中率只对这 2,083 次查缓存的读成立：约 208.3 次 miss，加 116 次绕缓存，故障时全回主库约 324.3 读/s，低于 400，不重复计数。若 116 次写后读是题目另加的流量，总读变成 2,315，才是 2,199×10%+116≈336；它不是本章基线。70% 命中下，2,083×30%+116=740.9，超过预算。会话保留并单调推进 min_pos，每次强读都必须证明满足；不能在一次读取后或 TTL 到期时清掉下界；两秒粘主只是体验启发式，TTL 到期不是副本追平的证明，不能替代读己之写合同。

「有了副本就不会丢已确认的写」不成立。异步复制下，主库已经确认、日志还没到任何副本的事务，会在主库磁盘消失时丢掉。副本提供的是一份可以升起来的数据，它可以比客户端看见的 201 更旧。「未命中就查数据库」也不分路径：写后读查副本，用户会看见自己的帖子消失；允许旧的读全部查主库，会把 400 的预算打穿。

Where “I refreshed and it was gone” actually sits

The user posts, the primary commits, and 201 carries min_pos. Until replica apply, both replica and shared cache can lack the row. The author’s refresh must identify the bound before any cache hit; here it bypasses cache and reads a fresh snapshot at the current primary. If the promoted history lacks that acknowledged position, the strong read reports that the bound cannot be met. Returning to a primary alone cannot recover a lost async write. Stale-ok readers retain a separate contract.

Count primary reads using disjoint sets. Treat 2,199 reads/s as the total including 116 read-your-writes; stale-ok traffic is 2,199−116=2,083. A8’s 90% hit rate applies only to those cache lookups: 208.3 misses plus 116 bypasses give 324.3 primary reads/s when every origin read returns to it, under 400 without double counting. If the 116 follow-up reads are extra traffic, total reads become 2,315 and 2,199×10%+116≈336 applies; that is not this chapter’s baseline. At 70% hits, 2,083×30%+116=740.9 exceeds capacity. Retain and monotonically advance the session’s min_pos; every strong read must prove it is satisfied, without clearing the floor after one read or TTL expiry. Two-second stickiness is an experience heuristic; its expiry proves no replica progress and cannot implement the read-your-writes contract.

A common mistake is “a replica means acknowledged writes cannot be lost.” Under async replication, transactions the primary has acknowledged whose log has reached no replica disappear if the primary disk disappears with them. The replica answers “there is a copy that can be promoted.” The promoted copy can be older than the 201 the client already saw. Another mistake is “a miss reads the database,” without splitting read-your-writes from stale-ok reads. Sending read-your-writes to the replica makes a user’s own post vanish. Sending stale-ok reads to the primary turns a large part of the 2,199 into primary traffic and breaks the 400 budget. Label the paths, then talk about consistency.

会话外置，慢工作离开请求Push session out, and let slow work leave the request

会话为什么不能留在主库，也不能留在进程里

登录成功后，应用把 (sid, user_id, expires_at) 写进共享会话存储，而不是写进某台进程的内存。之后任意一台应用收到 GET /home，都用 sid 读这份记录，再去查缓存或库。负载均衡可以按健康和负载分配，不必认 Cookie。图上负载均衡的标注从 sticky 改成 any app：粘滞曾经是问题，现在不再是路径的一部分。

会话若放在主库，每个请求多一次读。峰值 2,315 次请求大约是 2,315 次会话读。2,315 ÷ 400 ≈ 5.8，单是会话就把读预算用掉五倍多，约 324.3 次内容回源（含绕缓存强读）还没算。会话要离开主库，单独放进一个内存型的共享存储。它挂了，所有应用都无法把 sid 换成 user_id。所以会话存储至少两副本，超时预算也和主库分开，避免一次抖动把主库打满。

替代是签名令牌：应用本地验签，读路径不访问会话存储。立刻登出需要一份吊销列表，那份列表仍是共享存储；或者把访问令牌压得很短，用刷新令牌换新的。要立刻作废被偷的 sid，共享会话更直接。两种都不把会话放回主库，也不留在某一台应用的内存里。

Why the session cannot live on the primary or in the process

After login, the app writes (sid, user_id, expires_at) into the shared session store, not into one process’s memory. Whichever app receives GET /home reads that record by sid, then consults the cache or the database. The load balancer can place work by health and load and does not need to recognize the cookie. On the diagram the balancer’s label changes from sticky to any app: stickiness was the problem, and it is no longer part of the path.

If the shared session sits on the primary, every request adds a read. The peak of 2,315 requests is about 2,315 session reads. 2,315 ÷ 400 ≈ 5.8, so sessions alone spend more than five read budgets before the 324.3 content origin reads, including strong-read bypasses, are counted. The session store has to leave the primary and be its own shared in-memory state. Its failure window is sharp: no app can turn a sid into a user_id, so a timeline sitting in cache still cannot be attributed. Keep at least two copies of the session store, and give session-store timeouts a different budget from primary timeouts, so a session hiccup does not stampede the primary.

The alternative is a signed token. The app verifies the signature locally and the read path does not touch a session store. Instant logout then needs a denylist, and that list is itself shared state, or the access token lives only briefly and a refresh token rotates it. The token is the better fit when session-store availability should not sit on every GET. The shared session is the better fit when a stolen sid must die immediately. Neither puts the session back on the primary, and neither leaves it inside one app’s memory.

1.6 秒的扇出必须离开 300 毫秒的确认

发帖本身是一行插入，116 QPS 放得进 800 的写预算。离开请求的是通知关注者。假设一条通知要 80 毫秒，中位关注者是 20，都是假设。串行做完是 20 × 80 毫秒 = 1.6 秒，超过写确认 p99 不超过 300 毫秒的目标。并行时请求仍被最慢的下游拖住。所以 201 在主库提交后就返回，通知进队列，由 worker 自己扩。

走一遍 POST /posts。应用验完会话，在同一事务里插入 Post(author_id, body) 和 Outbox(post_id, kind=notify)，提交，删除作者的缓存键，返回 201 和 post_id。relay 从 outbox 读出这一行投进队列；worker 消费队列，以 post_id + follower_id 做幂等键发送通知。队列按至少一次：崩溃后重投，收件方可能看到两次，幂等键收成一次。不要承诺恰好一次。

窗口在「提交成功」和「队列里真有这条消息」之间。若提交之后再从内存里 publish，进程死在两步中间，帖子已经在主库里，通知永远不会发出。outbox 把这两步收成同一事务：提交成功，relay 就能把这行投递出去，投完再标掉。队列不可用时，用户的 201 仍然成立，outbox 会积压。积压用更多 worker 消化，不把通知塞回请求。

队列不是分片的替代。116 QPS 也不该因为「架构都有队列」就上队列。这条队列不提高主库的写上限，只把 1.6 秒的扇出移出 300 毫秒的确认。关注者不超过 3 人时，3 × 80 毫秒 = 240 毫秒，还在 300 毫秒以内，可以在请求里做完；中位数 20 远超这个阈值，所以图上默认入队。另一个错误是 201 什么都不带，客户端立刻 GET /home 打副本，正好踩进上一节的 lag Δ。201 正文里带上刚写的帖子，客户端不必马上读一份可能还旧的时间线。

A 1.6 s fan-out has to leave a 300 ms acknowledgement

The post itself is one insert on the primary. A write peak of 116 QPS fits the 800 write budget. What leaves the request is notifying followers. Assume one downstream notification takes 80 ms and the median post has 20 followers. Both are assumptions. Done serially, 20 × 80 ms = 1.6 s, past the target that write p99 stays within 300 ms. Even in parallel, the request waits on the slowest downstream, and the app process cannot spend that time on other peak CPU. So 201 returns after the primary commits, the notification enters the queue, and workers scale on their own.

Walk POST /posts. The app checks the session, and in one primary transaction inserts Post(author_id, body) and an Outbox(post_id, kind=notify) row, commits, deletes the author’s timeline cache key, and returns 201 with the post_id. A relay reads the outbox row and publishes it to the queue. A worker consumes the queue, notifies followers, and uses post_id + follower_id as the idempotency key. Design the queue as at-least-once. A worker crash redelivers, the receiver may see the notification twice, and the idempotency key collapses the effect to once. Do not promise exactly-once on the acknowledgement path.

The failure window sits between “commit succeeded” and “the queue actually contains the message.” If the app publishes from memory after the commit, and the process dies between the two steps, the post is on the primary and the notification never leaves. The outbox makes those two steps one transaction: if the commit succeeded, the relay can deliver the row, and it marks the outbox row after delivery. When the queue itself is down, the user’s 201 still stands, notifications slow down, and the outbox table grows. A backlog is capacity. Add workers. Do not pull notification back onto the request.

A common mistake is to treat the queue as a substitute for sharding, or the reverse mistake, to add a queue at 116 write QPS “because architectures have one.” This queue does not raise the primary’s write ceiling. It only moves a 1.6 s fan-out out of a 300 ms acknowledgement. Doing the fan-out inline for users with few followers is a fair alternative: at most 3 followers is 3 × 80 ms = 240 ms, still inside 300 ms, so it can stay in the request, and the rest is queued. A median of 20 is far above that threshold, so the default path on the diagram is the queue. Another mistake is an empty 201 that makes the client immediately GET /home against the replica. That steps straight into the previous lag Δ. Put the new post in the 201 body so the client can draw it without reading a timeline that may still be old.

先证明单主写得下，再按写预算分片Prove one primary can take the writes, then shard from the write budget

基本假设下，分片是被拿掉的组件

到队列为止，写峰值是 116，写预算是 800。116 ÷ 800 ≈ 0.15。数据 400 GB，低于 2 TB。再加分片会带来路由、跨片扇出和热点，而没有任何一条假设被单主违反。面试里先说「这一步我不分片」。分片键仍然要会讲，因为面试官下一句就可能改假设。

改写的假设单独标出，不和每天 20 次请求混在一个式子里。每个日活每天写 50 条消息：1,000,000 × 50 = 50,000,000。50,000,000 ÷ 86,400 = 578.7 平均写 QPS，峰值 ×10 = 5,787。5,787 ÷ 800 = 7.23，所以要 8 个写分区。8 × 800 = 6,400，盖住 5,787，并给写后读留一点余量。图上只画两片讲路由；口播说 8，不画八个圆柱。

分片键用 user_id。首页读的是「我的时间线」，行按读者的 user_id 放，GET /home 就落在一片上。发帖在作者自己的片上插入 Post，worker 再按每个关注者的 user_id 把时间线条目写到对方那一片。跨片发生在已经离开请求的队列上，不发生在 200 毫秒的读里面。若时间线只存在作者片上，每次首页都要按关注列表去很多片收集，峰值读会乘上关注数。读占 95% 时，扇出放在写路径的 worker 上。

Under the base assumptions, sharding is the component that gets removed

Through the queue, the primary’s write peak is 116 QPS and the write budget is 800. 116 ÷ 800 ≈ 0.15, about fifteen percent of the budget. The data is 400 GB, under the 2 TB single-primary budget. Adding shards would pay for routing, cross-shard fan-out, resharding, and hot keys, and no base assumption is violated by one primary. Saying “I do not shard at this step” matches the numbers better than drawing eight shards from memory. The shard key, hot keys, and cross-shard queries still have to be speakable, because the interviewer’s next sentence may change the assumption.

Label the changed assumption by itself. Do not mix it into the twenty-requests formula. Assume messaging rather than an occasional post: 50 messages per daily active per day. 1,000,000 × 50 = 50,000,000 writes a day. 50,000,000 ÷ 86,400 = 578.7 average write QPS. Times the 10× peak, that is 5,787 QPS. 5,787 ÷ 800 = 7.23, so at least 8 write partitions are required to keep each one inside 800. 8 × 800 = 6,400, which covers 5,787 and leaves a little room for read-your-writes on that shard’s primary. The diagram draws two shards, enough to show user_id routing. Say the count is 8 out loud. Do not draw eight cylinders to fill the board.

The shard key is user_id. Home reads “my timeline.” If those rows live under the reader’s user_id, GET /home hits one shard. The author inserts the Post on their own shard, and the worker writes a timeline row onto each follower’s shard by that follower’s user_id. The cross-shard work rides the queue that already left the request. It does not ride the 200 ms read. Storing the timeline only on the author’s shard makes every GET /home collect from many shards using the follow list. That is synchronous scatter-gather, and it multiplies the read peak by the follow count. With reads at 95%, putting fan-out on the write-path worker is forced by the read budget.

两片怎么接请求，八片怎么口播

用户 42 的 GET /home 先查会话，再查 feed:42。未命中时，应用算 user_id 落在哪一段。图上按 hash(user_id) 的最高字节示意：00–7f 进片 A，80–ff 进片 B。假设 hash(42) 落在 A，于是只对片 A 的主库或它的副本发查询，200 从这一片返回；hash 落在 B 的用户同样只打片 B。应用进程仍然无状态，片的路由是函数，不是粘在某一台应用上的内存。

写只打作者那一片。POST 在作者片的主库提交 Post 和 outbox。worker 按每个关注者的 user_id 把时间线条目写到对应片。关注者在片 B，虚线就落到片 B，首页请求不为这个扇出等待。队列在分片之后还有第二份工作：它是跨片写入留在同步路径外面的通道。worker 落后时，关注者的首页暂时看不到新帖，作者的 201 里已经有这篇帖子。这是产品窗口，不是写失败。

还没到 5,787 时，更干净的替代是按 user_id 做范围分区，仍放在同一台主库上。那不增加写 QPS 上限，只让大表维护更便宜。基本假设 200 GB、116 写，用这个就够，不需要八台主库。另一条是把单机写预算提高到能盖住 5,787。若单机写预算能到 6,000，5,787 ÷ 6,000 ≈ 0.96，商小于 1，分片再次被拿掉。分片是预算除法的结果，不是百万用户的同义词。

热点还在。某个 user_id 的写入远高于平均数，它那一片会单独打满 800，其余七片还空着。读热点继续用缓存挡住；写热点要把这个逻辑用户拆成多个子键再聚合，或给这一片单独加写预算。单调递增的时间戳当唯一分片键，会把新写入堆在最右边。user_id 避开的是这个堆叠，不是「永远没有热点」。

How two shards take a request, and how eight are spoken

User 42’s GET /home checks the session, then feed:42. On a miss the app computes which range owns the user_id. The diagram sketches the top byte of hash(user_id): 00–7f goes to shard A, 80–ff to shard B. Say hash(42) lands on A, so the query goes only to shard A’s primary or its replica, and the 200 returns from that shard. A user whose hash lands on B hits only shard B. App processes stay stateless. Shard routing is a function, not memory stuck to one app.

Writes hit only the author’s shard. The POST commits the Post and the outbox on the author’s primary. The worker reads follower ids and writes a timeline row onto each follower’s shard. If the follower is on shard B, the dashed arrow lands on shard B, and the home request does not wait on shard B for that fan-out. That is the queue’s second job after sharding. It already moved 1.6 s out of the acknowledgement. It is also the path for cross-shard writes that must stay off the synchronous read. If the worker is behind, a follower’s home is temporarily missing the new post, while the author’s 201 already contains it. Call that a product window, not a failed write.

The cleaner alternative, while the rate is not 5,787, is to range-partition the table by user_id and still keep it on one primary, so index and maintenance work can run per partition. That does not raise the write-QPS ceiling. It only makes a large table cheaper to maintain. The base assumptions, 200 GB and 116 writes, are enough for that, and do not need eight primaries. Another alternative is a larger primary whose write budget covers 5,787. If one machine’s write budget reaches 6,000, 5,787 ÷ 6,000 ≈ 0.96, the quotient drops below 1, and sharding is removed again. Sharding is the result of the division, not a synonym for the words “a million users.”

Hot keys survive sharding. If one user_id is written far above the average, that shard alone fills its 800 while the other seven sit idle. Read hot keys can still be stopped at the cache. A write hot key has to be split into sub-keys and aggregated, or that shard gets its own write budget. A monotonically increasing timestamp as the only shard key piles new writes on the rightmost range. A range split only postpones the pile. A high-cardinality key such as user_id avoids that pile. It does not mean there is never a hot key.

故障：主库宕机、副本延迟、缓存击穿Failures: primary down, replica lag, cache stampede

主库超时：取消，还是带着旧数据继续

片 A 的主库不再响应，只证明通信超时，不能证明它死了。应用先暂停这一片写入；允许旧的读可按产品契约继续，强读不能悄悄退化。Brewer 讨论的是超时后某次操作继续还是取消，[1] 不授予第二个写主。即使业务接受异步复制的 RPO，提升前也必须让旧主丧失写权：由写资源验证的权威/栅栏，或可靠断电、撤销存储访问等硬隔离，必须覆盖已有连接和在途写。只改应用 epoch 或路由不够。拿不到隔离证明就保持这一片不可写，片 B 仍可服务。

安全顺序是：暂停写 → 证明旧主已失去写权 → 核验候选持久日志的历史与连续前缀，并 apply 所需前缀 → 提升 → 发布新路由代次 → 恢复写，再补副本。[4] 旧主恢复只能作为隔离节点核对、重建并加入副本。默认异步下，仅在产品接受丢失未复制尾部时才能选当前合格前缀；丢失的 min_pos 不能继续答复强读成功。零丢已确认写合同则必须证明候选覆盖全部已确认前缀，无法证明就停写，而非选“看上去最前”的副本。该合同只抵御前述单主故障，不能覆盖所有持久副本同时损坏。持久副本确认与单写者隔离缺一不可；半同步超时退回异步仍要受合同控制。[5][6]

Primary timeout: cancel, or continue with older data

Shard A’s primary stops answering, which proves a timeout rather than death. Pause its writes; stale-ok reads may continue under their contract, while strong reads cannot silently degrade. Brewer’s per-operation continue/cancel decision [1] does not authorize a second writer. Even with accepted async RPO, promotion requires resource-enforced write authority/fencing or reliable hard isolation, such as power or storage-access removal, covering existing connections and in-flight writes. An application epoch or route change alone is insufficient. Without isolation proof, keep shard A unavailable for writes; shard B may continue.

The safe order is: pause writes → prove the old primary has lost write authority → verify the candidate’s durable history and continuous prefix, then apply the required prefix → promote → publish the new route generation → resume writes and rebuild replication. [4] A recovered old primary remains isolated until checked and rebuilt as a replica. Default async may use an eligible available prefix only when the product accepts loss of the unreplicated suffix; a lost min_pos must fail a strong read. The zero acknowledged-loss variant must prove coverage of every acknowledged commit, or keep writes paused. The contract covers the stated single-primary failure, not simultaneous destruction of all durable copies. Durable replica acknowledgement and single-writer isolation are both required; semi-sync’s async fallback remains subject to the contract. [5][6]

副本延迟和缓存击穿是两条不同的红线

副本延迟不需要主库宕机。主库确认后、日志 apply 前，允许旧的读可能返回旧时间线，状态仍是 200，错误率看不见。写后读先带 min_pos 绕缓存；副本只有连续 apply 的证明才可服务，否则回当前主库、等待或失败。两秒粘主到期不证明追平。按前面的不交叠口径，90% 命中时约 208.3 次 miss 加 116 次强读，共约 324.3 次回源；故障时这些流量共享 400/s 上限，仍需留余量。

整份缓存失效时，读回到大约 2,199 QPS。2,199 ÷ 400 = 5.5。「缓存挂了就查数据库」会把主库一起打死。时间线键按读者分，单个 feed 键并不热；热键来自共享对象，比如一条爆款帖子 post:9。假设它占峰值读的 20%，大约 0.20 × 2,199 ≈ 440 QPS 打在同一个键上。键一过期，所有应用会在同一瞬间回源。

Nishtala 等人用租约对付回源风暴和旧值写回。未命中时给一个 64 位令牌，回填必须带上。默认同一个键最多每 10 秒一个令牌，其余请求稍等再重试。他们对易击穿的键观察一周：没有租约时数据库查询峰值 17K/s，有租约时 1.3K/s。[3] 这是他们的测量，不是这张图的 QPS。整集群宕机时没有进程发令牌，超过 400 的回源失败或降级，不把 2,199 放进主库。

不要把这三件事说成一句「高可用」。主库超时要在丢掉一段已确认的写、和让这一片暂时不可写之间选择。副本延迟会在主库健康时返回旧的 200。缓存失效会把 5.5 倍的读预算打回原点，必须有租约和封顶。三句话都要能指着同一张图上的红线或时钟说出来。

Replica lag and cache stampede are different red lines

Replica lag does not require a dead primary. Between commit acknowledgement and replica apply, stale-ok readers can receive old timelines with 200, invisible to error rate. Read-your-writes carry min_pos and bypass cache first; a replica serves only with continuous-apply proof, otherwise use the current primary, wait, or fail. Two-second stickiness expiring proves no catch-up. With the disjoint sets above, 90% hits yield about 208.3 misses plus 116 strong reads, 324.3 origin reads/s. During failures these share the 400/s cap and still need headroom.

Cache stampede is a different division. When the whole cache is gone, reads that a 90% hit rate used to absorb return at about 2,199 QPS. 2,199 ÷ 400 = 5.5, five and a half read budgets. “The cache died, so query the database” takes the shard primary down with it. A hot key expiring is the smaller version of the same event. Timeline keys are per reader, so no single feed key is hot; hot keys are shared objects such as a viral post, post:9. Assume that key is 20% of peak reads, about 0.20 × 2,199 ≈ 440 QPS on one key. While the cache holds, those 440 are hits. The moment the key expires, every app reloads together.

The leases in Nishtala et al. target that reload storm, and the stale set that writes an old value back. On a miss the cache gives the client a 64-bit token, and the fill must present the token. Their default returns a token for a given key at most once every 10 seconds. Other requests are told to wait briefly and retry after the token holder fills the value. On a set of keys that were particularly prone to stampedes, over one week, the peak database query rate from those misses was 17K/s without leases and 1.3K/s with leases. [3] 17K and 1.3K are their measurement on that key set, not the QPS of this diagram. The mechanism we can take is: one reload per key, everyone else waits. A total cache outage is beyond the lease, because no cache process remains to issue tokens. Then the origin budget is the control. Reloads above 400 fail or return an explicit degraded page. They do not enter the primary as 2,199. The queue still does not sit on the 200 during this failure. Notifications may be late. Notification retries must not spend the timeline’s read budget.

The common mistake is to collapse the three into “high availability.” A primary timeout chooses between losing a suffix of acknowledged writes and making that shard temporarily unable to write. Replica lag returns an old 200 while the primary is healthy. A cache expiry throws 5.5× the read budget at the origin and needs both a lease and a cap. All three sentences have to point at a red line or a clock on the same picture. They cannot point at a freshly drawn box labeled “disaster recovery.”

追问怎么接Follow-ups

1 · 为什么一百万用户还不分片？

因为基本假设下的写峰值是 116，写预算是 800，116 ÷ 800 ≈ 0.15。数据大约 400 GB，低于 2 TB。分片要等写被改成每天 50 条消息那种假设：50,000,000 ÷ 86,400 = 578.7 平均写 QPS，峰值 5,787，5,787 ÷ 800 = 7.23，才需要 8 个写分区。百万这个词本身不产生分片。

1 · Why not shard at a million users?

Because under the base assumptions the write peak is 116 and the write budget is 800, and 116 ÷ 800 ≈ 0.15. The data is about 400 GB, under 2 TB. Sharding waits for a changed write assumption such as 50 messages a day: 50,000,000 ÷ 86,400 = 578.7 average write QPS, peak 5,787, and 5,787 ÷ 800 = 7.23, which needs 8 write partitions. The word “million” does not create shards.

2 · 发完帖立刻刷新，为什么看不到？

201 带回这次提交的 min_pos。作者刷新先按该下界分流，绕过没有下界证明的缓存；副本只有连续 apply 到所需位点才可服务，否则读当前主库新快照、等待或失败。若异步切主丢失该历史，必须明确报告无法满足。两秒粘主只是减轻体验问题的启发式，不能在到期后把旧结果当读己之写成功。

2 · I posted and refreshed, and the post is missing.

The 201 returns min_pos. Classify the author’s refresh by that bound before any cache hit. A replica serves only with continuous-apply proof; otherwise use a fresh current-primary snapshot, wait, or fail. If async failover lost the history, report the unsatisfied bound. Two-second stickiness is only a heuristic and cannot turn an old result into a successful read-your-writes after expiry.

3 · 主库宕机会丢数据吗？

异步复制可能丢主库已确认、但任何副本都未持久保留的尾部。接受这段 RPO 不代表允许双主：先停写、隔离旧主、核验并应用候选前缀，才提升和发布新路由。零丢已确认写则要求成功前有独立副本持久 ACK、提升覆盖全部已确认前缀，等待不足不退回异步成功；达不到条件停写。[4][5][6]

3 · If the primary dies, do we lose data?

Async can lose the acknowledged suffix not durably retained on any replica. Accepted RPO never authorizes two writers: pause writes, isolate the old primary, verify and apply the candidate prefix, then promote and publish routing. Zero acknowledged loss additionally requires durable replica ACK before success, promotion covering all acknowledged commits, and no async-success fallback. Otherwise keep writes paused. [4][5][6]

4 · 缓存整个挂了，能不能直接查库？

不能无预算地查。回源会从约 324.3 回到 2,199，是 400 的 5.5 倍。热键用租约收成一个回源。Nishtala 等人在易击穿的键上看到数据库查询峰值从 17K/s 降到 1.3K/s，那是他们的测量。[3] 整集群宕机时没有进程发令牌，超过 400 的请求失败或降级。

4 · The whole cache died. Do we just query the database?

Not without a budget. Origin traffic goes from about 324.3 back to 2,199, which is 5.5× the 400 budget. A hot-key stampede uses a lease so one reload fills the key. That is the token Nishtala et al. used in memcache. On keys prone to stampedes they saw the peak database query rate fall from 17K/s to 1.3K/s. That is their measurement. [3] When the whole cluster is down, no process remains to issue tokens, and requests above 400 fail or degrade. Notifications sitting on the queue do not spend this read budget.

5 · 流量再乘 10 会先坏在哪？

先别加机器。峰值读大约 21,990。即使仍有 90% 命中，回源大约 2,199，已是 400 的 5.5 倍。写大约 1,160，1,160 ÷ 800 ≈ 1.5，两片写预算才盖住。应用 CPU 从 9.3 核变成大约 93 核。前提是会话已经在共享存储里；还在内存里的话，加进程只会让粘滞更脆。

5 · Traffic times ten. What breaks first?

Do not start by adding machines. Peak reads become about 21,990. Even with a 90% hit rate, origin reads are about 2,199, already 5.5× the 400 budget, so cache and replica capacity have to be recomputed. Writes become about 1,160, over 800, so the shard the base assumptions skipped now appears. 1,160 ÷ 800 ≈ 1.5, and two write budgets cover it. App CPU goes from 9.3 cores to about 93, and the app process count follows, provided sessions are already in the shared store. If sessions are still in memory, more processes only make stickiness more brittle.

6 · 现在就做多地域双活吗？

不做。Dean 2009 的表里，同机房往返是 0.5 毫秒，跨洋是 150 毫秒。[2] 双活还意味着分区时两边都能写，恢复时要合并，这是 Brewer 说的分区选择，不是多画一个方框。[1] 单地域的超时、延迟和击穿还没讲完时，第二个地域只是把这三份契约再复制一遍。用户若跨洋，先说读可以旧多久，再决定要不要把只读副本放到更近的地方。那一步仍然不是两边同时写。

6 · Should we draw multi-region active-active now?

No. On Dean’s 2009 table a same-datacenter round trip and a transoceanic round trip are 0.5 ms against 150 ms. [2] Active-active also means both sides can write during a partition and must merge afterward. That is the partition mode Brewer describes, not one more box. [1] Until the single-region timeout, lag, and stampede are spoken, a second region only copies those three contracts. If users really are across an ocean, first state how old a read may be, then decide whether a read-only replica should sit closer to them. That step is still not both sides writing.
