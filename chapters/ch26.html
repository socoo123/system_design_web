这一章结束你能做什么What you can do after this chapter

从一个总额固定的群红包画到拆分、领取、入账和过期退款。用同一笔 1,000 分演示：为什么四份恰好加回总额，为什么一个人点两次仍只领一份，为什么最后一抢和到期不能各花掉同一笔剩余。每组最后一帧都能顺着箭头口述，并指出哪个持久记录让进程崩溃后仍能接着做。

Trace a fixed-total group packet through splitting, claiming, credit and expiry. Follow the same 1,000 cents: four shares sum to the total, a repeated user gets one share, and the final claim cannot spend money already reserved for refund. Walk each final frame and name the durable record that supports recovery.

重点是整数金额、两维库存和支付边界。先分清“拿到 Redis 候选”“持久确认领取”“账本已经入账”三个状态，再讨论吞吐。Ch21 的商品库存主要数件数，本章还要守住每一分钱；Ch24 负责资金事实，Ch25 负责到期唤醒。红包页面可以先显示入账处理中，领取承诺必须已有可恢复的凭据。

Focus on integer money, two inventory dimensions and the payment boundary. Separate a Redis candidate, a durably confirmed claim and an applied ledger credit before discussing throughput. Ch21 mainly counts items; here every cent matters. Ch24 owns money facts and Ch25 wakes expiry work. A receipt can say credit pending once the claim is durably recoverable.

30 秒开场与全部假设The 30-second open and assumptions

我先做单地域群红包：固定总额、有限份数，支付资金确认后才能开放。金额用整数分，发时预生成并打乱。Redis Lua 原子给候选；Packet DB 在本地事务里确认唯一用户、唯一份额、剩余库存和 Outbox，提交后才返回领取成功。入账由 Relay 重投固定 claim_id，支付账本在同一事务里做去重和资金效果。到期与领取在同一红包记录上排序，先保存退款快照再关闭。Redis 故障暂停新领取，推高 epoch 后从不可变计划与已确认领取重建。

I start with a single-region group packet: fixed total, finite shares, opened after verified funding. Use integer cents and a pre-generated shuffled plan. Redis Lua chooses a candidate; Packet DB commits unique user, unique share, inventory and outbox before acknowledging a claim. Relay retries a fixed claim_id; the payment ledger commits deduplication with the effect. Expiry serializes with claims and preserves a refund snapshot. On Redis failure, pause new claims, advance epoch and rebuild from the plan and confirmed claims.

先问六件事：群包还是活动投放，随机还是均分，发多少与最多几份，谁有领取权限，什么时候过期，成功提示代表领取还是余额已到账。本章选社交群包，领取前校验登录与群成员资格；均分与拼手气走同一套确认路径。个人红包是份数为一的特例。全国活动峰值、渠道清算与钱包余额双花不改变这里的主线，具体机制回对应章节。

Clarify group packets versus campaigns, random versus even split, total and count, eligibility, expiry, and whether success means a receipt or available balance. We choose a social group, checking authentication and membership. Even and random plans share the confirmation path; a personal packet has one share. Campaign peaks, channel settlement and wallet double-spending belong to their respective chapters.

先把成功领取、失败点击与字节分开算Estimate confirmations, attempts and bytes separately

每日成功领取 = 1,000,000×10 = 10,000,000，平均 10,000,000÷86,400≈115.74/s，峰值 ×10≈1,157/s。每日点击按 A2 是 50,000,000，平均约 578.70/s，峰值约 5,787/s。点击包含抢完、重复、过期与无资格等失败尝试；让所有点击都执行领取事务，会把 1,157 的确认预算变成 5,787 的锁请求，还没算重试。入口先过滤，持久写仍按实际确认量规划。

Daily confirmations are 1,000,000×10=10,000,000, or 115.74/s on average and about 1,157/s at 10× peak. A2 gives 50,000,000 attempts/day, 578.70/s on average and about 5,787/s at peak. Sold-out, repeated, expired and ineligible attempts should not all become claim transactions. Filter at admission while sizing durable writes for confirmations.

A3 把全国总和换成一个包：一秒 10,000 位不同用户争 100 份，至少 10,000−100=9,900 无法成功，占 99%。这不是“多加十台 API”能增加的库存。按 A4，假设每次确认占串行临界区 2 ms，理论服务速率 1÷0.002=500/s，计划只用 60% 就是 300/s。同包放行 100 个新候选/s，只占理论速率的 20%，留下查询、冲突重试与关闭余量。门限限制到达速率，连接池和排队长度还要封顶；存储抖动时降门限或拒绝，而不是把 2 ms 当成延迟保证。

A3 moves the load into one packet: 10,000 distinct users chase 100 shares in one second, so at least 9,900, or 99%, cannot succeed. Extra API instances do not create inventory. At an assumed 2 ms serial critical section, theoretical service is 500/s; 60% planning gives 300/s. Admit 100 new candidates/s, 20% of theoretical service, leaving headroom for queries, conflicts and close. Also bound queues and connections; reduce admission on storage stalls.

存储按一天的积分，别把峰值延续全天。元信息 1M×1 KB=1 GB/天；领取行 10M×200 B=2 GB/天。七天合计 (1+2)×7=21 GB 原始行，三份是 63 GB。预生成计划要另算：若一个金额用 8 B，平均 10 份，那么金额本体 1M×10×8=80 MB/天；这是金额数组下限，还没有 share_id、列表/Hash 开销。Outbox、支付 inbox、索引、WAL、备份与未结束的资金记录都没有装进 63 GB。三份是容量乘数，耐久保证由 A10 单独约束。

Size storage by daily totals. Packet metadata is 1M×1 KB=1 GB/day; claims are 10M×200 B=2 GB/day. Seven days gives 21 GB raw rows and 63 GB across three copies. With 8 B per amount and ten shares on average, amount payload alone is 80 MB/day. Share IDs, Redis structures, outbox, payment inbox, indexes, WAL, backups and unresolved financial records are extra. Copy count sizes capacity; A10 separately constrains durability.

先确认资金，再把红包开放出来Verify funding before opening the packet

正常路径：创建成功与开放成功是两个确认Normal path: creation and opening are separate confirmations

Client 提交总额、份数、模式、群标识和幂等创建键。Packet API 先校验金额与权限，生成 packet_id 和不可变 share plan，在 Packet DB 的同一事务写 pending_fund 与资金意图。返回 202 带 packet_id，表示后续可查。Relay 读取已提交意图，调用 Payment API 的 fund:p9；支付确认 1,000 分进入本包可支配的托管资金口径，红包侧核对同键、同额与成功状态后记 funded。签名正确的回调也要验证金额与资金单身份，不能只信一个 success 字符串。

Client submits amount, count, mode, group and an idempotent creation key. Packet API validates eligibility, creates packet_id and the immutable plan, then commits pending_fund and its funding intent together. 202 carries packet_id for lookup. Relay sends fund:p9; after payment confirms 1,000 cents in the packet’s spendable escrow scope, verify key, amount and status before recording funded. Authenticate callbacks and validate their business identity and amount.

funded 之后先进入 initializing，按计划版本向 Redis 安装 List、份额映射和元信息。初始化只允许“当前 epoch、还未就绪”的条件写入；重投不能覆写已经被消费的 List。投影就绪后再条件更新 DB 的 open、opened_at、expire_at，并让投影进入可预扣状态。两边有先后窗口，但 DB 仍会拒绝未开放的候选，所以不会提前确认领取。每一步都是带条件的可重试动作；实例退出，恢复器从持久状态继续，不能重新拆包或再次扣资金。

After funded, enter initializing and install the versioned list, share mapping and metadata in Redis. Conditional initialization for the current unready epoch must not overwrite a consumed list. Once ready, conditionally persist open, opened_at and expire_at, then enable reservations in the projection. A two-store window remains, but DB rejects candidates before opening. Retry each conditional step from durable state without rerandomizing or funding again.

为什么这样选：资金事实决定库存能否承诺Why: verified funds authorize the inventory promise

支付侧用 fund:p9 把资金扣取与该包绑定；HTTP 超时可能发生在账本提交之后，红包只知道结果未知。此时继续查询或重投原操作，保持 pending_fund；不能另生成 pay_no 再扣一遍，也不能边猜支付成功边开放。AWS Outbox 原文说明业务更新与事件记录要在同一事务，本章将它用于资金意图和领取意图 [3]。它解决进程退出造成的双写洞，不替支付接口提供去重。资金接口拒绝同键不同金额，冲突会成为可审查的错误。

fund:p9 binds funding to this packet. HTTP can time out after ledger commit, leaving an unknown result. Keep pending_fund and query or replay the original operation; never create another debit or open speculatively. The outbox records business change and delivery intent together [3], closing the process-crash gap. Receiver deduplication is still required, including rejection of the same key with a changed amount.

替代与窗口：纯数据库起步，也要保护已确认数据Alternatives and failures: a database-only start still needs durable receipts

普通群包只有几十份，可以先把预生成份额和领取事务都放 DB，省去 Redis 安装与恢复。A2 的全国确认峰值约 1,157/s 是否放得下，应由数据库与访问模式测量，不用“群红包一定不能锁行”代替证据。本章保留 Redis 是为了拦住热包失败尝试并缩短候选路径，最终确认仍有本地事务。权威 DB 若只是异步切主，已经确认的资金意图或领取可能回退；WAL 说明持久日志先于数据页的基本原则 [11]，却不能单独证明切主零丢失，所以 A10 还要求不提升落后的权威副本。

A small group packet can start with a database-only plan and claim transaction, avoiding projection installation and recovery. Whether roughly 1,157 peak confirmations/s fit depends on measurements, not a categorical ban on row locks. Redis here filters hot unsuccessful attempts and speeds candidate selection; final confirmation remains transactional. WAL establishes logging-before-data principles [11], but asynchronous promotion can still roll back receipts. A10 therefore also constrains failover.

把总额拆成可重建的整数份额Split the total into reproducible integer shares

正常路径：每抽一份，都给后来者留一分Normal path: every draw preserves a cent for each later share

初始金额 M、剩余份数 N，要满足 M≥N≥1。N>1 时，本章示例上界 U=min(⌊2M/N⌋, M−(N−1))，均匀取整数 a∈[1,U]，再更新 M←M−a、N←N−1；N=1 时取剩余 M。第二项给后来者保留至少 N−1 分：a≤M−(N−1)，所以新 M≥新 N。这是每一步能继续的证明。所有份额都减自同一剩余，最后取尽，归纳得到总和等于原金额；不需要用浮点随机数再四舍五入补差。

Require M≥N≥1. For N>1, use U=min(floor(2M/N),M−(N−1)), draw an integer a uniformly in [1,U], then subtract a and one share. At N=1, take all remaining M. Because a≤M−(N−1), the next state still has at least one cent per remaining share. Each amount is removed from the same remainder, and the last share exhausts it, proving conservation without floating-point rounding repairs.

图里 1,000 分四份：第一次 U=500，示例取 300；剩 700、3 份，U=min(466,698)=466，取 400；剩 300、2 份，U=min(300,299)=299，取 100；最后 200。加总 300+400+100+200=1,000。均分则 q=⌊1,000÷4⌋=250，余数 0，四份都是 250；若 1,003 分四份，q=250、余 3，三份 251、一份 250，总额仍是 1,003。两种都输出带固定 share_id 的计划，领取算法不因模式改变。

For 1,000 cents and four shares: bound 500, draw 300; bound min(466,698)=466, draw 400; bound min(300,299)=299, draw 100; then take 200. The sum is 1,000. An even split gives four 250-cent shares. For 1,003 cents, quotient 250 and remainder 3 give three 251-cent shares and one 250-cent share. Both modes produce fixed share IDs for the same claim path.

为什么预生成：随机离开高峰，身份留在持久记录Why pre-generate: move randomness out of the peak and preserve identity

创建时计算一次、验证 count=N 和 sum=total，然后均匀打乱这 N 个固定份额。计划版本与每份金额持久保存，Redis List 可以只排 share_id。重试创建复用同一 packet_id 与计划；重建加载原计划中未确认的份额，不能再随机生成一组“也等于总额”的钱。否则已领 s2 的 400 分与新 s2 的金额不一致，唯一索引也救不了支付重投。N≤100 时预生成成本很小；代价是每个开放包都要保存计划，长期留存仍按独立预算计算。

Compute once, validate count and sum, then uniformly shuffle fixed shares. Persist version and amounts; Redis may queue share IDs only. Creation retries reuse the plan, and reconstruction reloads its unconfirmed shares rather than another equal-sum random plan. Otherwise an already claimed s2 may acquire a new amount, breaking payment replay despite uniqueness. With N≤100 computation is small, but plan storage remains a separate cost.

均匀打乱让每个位置对这组既定金额有相同的分配机会，期望为 total/N。它没有证明生成金额在所有合法拆分之间均匀，也没有证明每份独立；金额总和固定，份额本来就有关联。真实请求先到先拿，网络延迟、脚本排队、账号资格和风控会影响谁先被接纳。若产品要求抽签公平，要另定义报名截止、抽样规则与可审计随机性，不能给二倍均值加一句 shuffle 就宣称解决所有公平问题。

Uniform shuffling gives each position the same assignment chance within the fixed plan, with mean total/N. It does not make all legal partitions equally likely or shares independent. Network timing, queues, eligibility and abuse controls affect admission. A lottery fairness requirement needs explicit registration and auditable randomization rules rather than a blanket fairness claim about this algorithm.

替代与错误：现场随机仍然要两维原子推进Alternative and mistakes: on-demand randomization still updates both dimensions

现场随机省掉金额 List，把随机选择放进领取临界区；随机结果必须随候选身份记录，超时重试不能重抽。它也要保护后续最低金额、整数上界与最后一份。本章选预生成是一个明确权衡，没有“到了 2026 所有团队都这样做”的证据。脚本运算还要核精确整数范围与参数类型，任何错误先校验再写；Lua 出错不是自动回滚已执行写命令的数据库事务。计划校验失败就不开放，不能把零分或负分偷偷推给最后一个人。

On-demand randomization saves a list but moves drawing into the critical section. Persist each candidate’s draw so timeouts cannot redraw it. Bounds, minimum amounts and the final share still matter. Pre-generation is an explicit tradeoff, not a claimed universal industry default. Validate numeric precision and types before writes: a script error is not database rollback. A bad plan must never open or hide zero/negative amounts in the last share.

Lua 给候选，持久事务给领取凭据Lua selects a candidate; a durable transaction gives the receipt

正常路径：把领取拆成预扣与确认Normal path: reserve, then confirm

Packet API 鉴权后先查同用户的持久领取；已有 c7 就返回原结果，即使红包已经过期。没有记录才尝试 Lua。脚本检查当前投影 epoch、open、截止点、用户候选映射与库存，重复候选直接返回；新用户取一个 share_id，扣 remain_n 与 remain_cents，并保存候选身份。Redis 官方说明脚本执行期间其他活动不能交错 [1]。所有涉及的键用同一 hash tag，如 pkt:{p9}:meta、pkt:{p9}:shares、pkt:{p9}:users；Cluster 多键操作须同槽 [5]。这让同包预扣互斥，却不能把一个热包拆到多个槽自动并行。

Packet API authenticates and first looks up the user’s durable claim; return c7 even after expiry if it exists. Otherwise call Lua to check epoch, open, deadline, user mapping and inventory. Return a repeated candidate, or pop a share, debit count/cents and store its identity. Scripts execute without interleaving [1]. Keys use a shared tag such as pkt:{p9}:meta, shares and users so multi-key operations share a cluster slot [5]. This serializes one packet rather than parallelizing it across slots.

候选进入 T_claim，先锁红包行，再查询已有领取；有则返回原 claim_id。否则检查状态 open、投影已就绪、epoch 匹配，以及锁内读取的当前 DB 时间仍早于 expire_at。按持久计划查 share_id 的正确金额，不信客户端报的分数；在一个事务内写 claim、扣 durable remaining、插 credit Outbox。对用户和份额分别做唯一约束，全部提交后才回 200。行锁保证领取与关闭按次序观察这条记录 [4]；唯一约束挡同人双领和同份双占 [8]。条件失败或唯一冲突就整体回滚，不允许“钱扣了，领取行没写成”。

T_claim locks the packet, then rechecks for an existing user claim. Otherwise validate open, ready projection, current epoch and actual DB time read under the lock before expire_at. Read the amount from the persisted share plan rather than client input. Commit claim, durable inventory debit and credit outbox together. Separate user/share uniqueness prevents double claims and share reuse [8]; the row lock serializes claim versus close [4]. Any failed condition or conflict rolls the entire local transaction back.

为什么多这一跳：200 不能早于可恢复的承诺Why the extra step: acknowledgment requires a recoverable promise

旧稿画成 Lua → 金额 → 200 → 异步 Outbox。若 API 死在 200 与写库之间，用户已被告知领取 400 分，却没有任何可恢复的入账意图。SQL Outbox 不能跨 Redis 与 DB 自动提供事务，补一个 UNIQUE 只能挡重复，造不出丢失的记录。本章改成候选 → T_claim → 200。DB 提交后网络断了，主库查询会找到 c7；DB 未提交时不会对用户承诺领取。界面遇到超时显示“结果查询中”，不要把结果未知显示成失败再换身份重抢。

The old Lua → amount → 200 → later outbox path can acknowledge 400 cents and then lose every credit intent in an API crash. SQL outbox cannot atomically span Redis and DB; uniqueness cannot recover a missing record. Use candidate → T_claim → 200. A lost response after commit is recovered by primary lookup. An unknown result should remain queryable rather than becoming a fresh claim identity.

故障与代价：候选会暂时占位，最终确认仍串行Failures and cost: candidates may strand inventory; confirmation still serializes

Lua 成功但 T_claim 未提交，Redis 的份额可能暂时被占。这里宁可暂缓新领取，也不在超时后随手 LPush 回去：另一个 API 可能正拿着同候选提交。恢复按第六组关闭确认入口、推进 epoch、核持久领取，再重新加载未领取份额。少领一会儿是可用性代价，不是永久少退；到期快照仍由 DB 剩余决定。Redis 的剩余代表尚未预扣，DB 剩余代表尚未确认，两者有在途差额，不能在每一毫秒要求两个数相等。

A successful Lua reservation with an uncommitted T_claim may strand a candidate. Do not blindly push it back after timeout: another API may still confirm it. Recovery closes confirmation, advances epoch and reloads unclaimed shares. Temporary underavailability is the cost; DB remaining still determines refunds. Redis counts unreserved inventory while DB counts unconfirmed inventory, so in-flight reservations legitimately make the two differ.

锁持有期内只做本地校验与写入，不调 Payment API，不生成随机数，也不等队列投递。A4 只是展示如何用服务时间给入口预算；冲突、刷盘或连接饱和时 2 ms 不再成立，就必须降速。Redis 失效也不放任全部点击直接锁 DB：维持有界接纳，优先已确认结果查询，暂停新候选或采用已压测的 DB-only 路径。需要更大单包确认吞吐时，可评估按包分区的持久有序命令与批处理，但要重新定义成功确认点，不能在本图上悄悄删掉持久提交。

Keep the lock around local validation and writes only, with no payment RPC, randomness or broker wait. A4 illustrates admission budgeting; stalls invalidate its 2 ms assumption and require lower admission. Redis loss must not send every click into DB locks. Prioritize receipts, pause new candidates or use a measured bounded database-only mode. Higher per-packet throughput may justify ordered durable commands and batching, with a newly explicit acknowledgment boundary.

领取成功之后，钱怎么只入账一次Deliver one money effect for each confirmed claim

正常路径：两库各自提交，操作身份跨越边界Normal path: two local commits, one stable operation identity

T_claim 已经保存 c7、u42、s2 与 400 分，并留下 credit:c7 的投递意图。Relay 拉取未完成 Outbox，调用 Payment API；资金侧以 claim_id 为业务幂等身份，建立 inbox 与金额、收款人、币种的绑定。Payment Ledger 在一个本地事务里验证资金来源与去重键，记录从本包托管资金到领取人应收/余额的平衡分录，保存 applied 结果，再回复。Relay 收到匹配结果后更新红包侧入账进度。图里 c7 是简写，具体接口可以用 credit:c7，但所有重试必须指向同一业务操作。

T_claim persists c7, u42, s2, 400 cents and a credit:c7 intent. Relay sends it to Payment API. The receiver binds the business key to recipient, amount and currency. Payment Ledger atomically validates source funds and deduplication, posts balanced escrow-to-recipient entries, stores applied and replies. Relay then updates progress. The diagram abbreviates the operation as c7; an interface may use credit:c7, with all retries identifying the same effect.

本地 T_claim 与支付 T_pay 是两个事务。200 返回后钱可仍在途，但有一条持久入账责任；页面展示金额与“入账处理中”，查询领取结果与查询支付进度也分开。Outbox 积压不会让已经确认的 c7 消失，不能为缩短等待而重复分配另一份。Relay 不一定需要再接一个 MQ：本章选择直接轮询与重投，少一个组件；若支付端需要削峰，可以在这条通道上加队列，去重与资金事务仍在接收端完成。

T_claim and T_pay are separate local transactions. A receipt may precede credited funds, but its delivery obligation is durable. Display the amount with credit pending and expose separate receipt and credit-status queries. Backlog cannot erase c7 or justify another share. Direct polling and replay avoid an extra broker here; adding a queue for buffering leaves deduplication and money atomicity at the receiver.

为什么 Outbox 之后仍需 inboxWhy an outbox still requires receiver deduplication

Relay 发送后、标记完成前退出，会再次发送；Payment Ledger 已提交而 applied 回包丢了，也会再次发送。AWS 原文明确提醒 Outbox 消息可能重复，消费者仍须幂等 [3]。inbox 只允许一个 credit:c7，重复且载荷相同就返回原 applied；同键改成 u43 或 500 分则报冲突。只在 Relay 内存去重没有用，重启会忘；先写 inbox 再独立入账会永久漏钱，先入账再独立记 inbox 会重复入账。收件方的去重与效果必须同一事务。

Relay can resend after a crash before checkpointing or after a lost applied response. Outbox consumers must still handle duplicates [3]. A repeated identical credit:c7 returns stored applied; changed recipient or amount conflicts. Sender memory is insufficient. Separating receiver deduplication from posting can either lose or duplicate money. Commit them together in the receiving ledger transaction.

Stripe 的公开幂等接口示例会保存同键首次执行的结果、核对参数，并允许在至少 24 小时后清理键 [9]。这是接口设计的参考，不是本章支付服务的保证。群红包过期本身就假设 24 h，恢复与争议可能更久，所以不能直接照抄该最短保留窗口。七天展示删除也不能删除仍能重投的 credit:c7 或 refund:r9。归档时保留操作唯一性或不可重放的关闭证明，明确谁还能发送旧命令。

Stripe illustrates stored first results, parameter comparison and keys removable after at least 24 hours [9]. That is an interface example, not our payment contract. Our packet lasts 24 h while recovery and disputes can last longer. Neither that minimum nor seven-day display retention should erase replay protection. Archive operation uniqueness or a nonreplayable closure record and define who can still send old commands.

故障窗口：超时查原结果，持续失败保留责任Failure window: query the original effect and retain unresolved obligations

图上红虚线从 Payment API 回到 Relay，表示它看到超时，而非证明账本没动钱。重试同键时接收端查询已保存结果；资金端暂不可达，红包保持 credit_pending。明确不可入账的业务拒绝要转可审查异常，告知用户并按业务规则处理，不能把属于已确认领取人的钱自动退给发送方。限次重试后进入人工接管只是停止自动循环，Outbox 与领取责任还在。告警看最老在途年龄和未处理金额，比只看 Relay QPS 更能发现几百份钱被卡在角落。

The red Payment API → Relay path means timeout, not proof that the ledger did nothing. Replay the same key to retrieve the stored result; keep credit_pending while unavailable. A definite business rejection requires review and authorized handling, not an automatic sender refund of a confirmed recipient’s money. Exhausting automatic retries leaves the obligation and outbox intact. Monitor oldest pending age and unresolved amount, not only Relay throughput.

最后一抢与过期：先保留退款依据，再关包The last claim versus expiry: preserve the refund basis before closing

正常路径：T_expire 和 T_claim 在同一行决胜Normal path: T_expire and T_claim serialize on one record

Scheduler 到点调用 Packet API 的 close:p9，漏调用也由到期索引扫描补上。T_expire 锁住与 T_claim 相同的 packets 行，锁内读取当前数据库时间；未到期就退出，已关闭就返回原退款操作。到期且仍 open，保存当下 remaining_cents 与剩余份数的快照，生成固定 refund:r9，把状态改 expiring、可领取库存改零，并在同一事务保存退款 Outbox。关闭成功后再通知 Redis 停止预扣；通知丢失也没关系，迟到候选仍会被 DB 状态和时间拒绝。

Scheduler invokes close:p9, with a due-index scan covering missed wakeups. T_expire locks the same packet row as T_claim and reads actual DB time under the lock. Early calls exit; closed packets return their existing refund operation. At expiry, atomically snapshot remaining amount/count, persist refund:r9 and outbox, enter expiring and zero available inventory. Later disable Redis reservations. A lost notification cannot authorize a claim against the closed DB state.

p9 已确认 s2=400 分，DB 剩余 600 分、3 份。关闭事务保存 refund_pending=600 与 closed_unclaimed_n=3，然后 available_cents=0、available_n=0。金额式从 open 时 1,000=400+600，变成关闭后 1,000=400+0+600；600 只是换了分类，没有消失。若所有四份都已确认，remaining=0，关闭不生成零额支付命令，但仍留终态。退款命令永远读持久快照，不能重试时再看已归零的 Hash，也不能每次换一个 refund_id。

After confirmed s2=400, p9 has 600 cents and three shares remaining. Close persists refund_pending=600 and closed_unclaimed_n=3, then makes available amount/count zero. Conservation changes from 1,000=400+600 to 1,000=400+0+600: the 600 changes category. If nothing remains, close without a zero-value payment command. Refund retries read the durable snapshot with the same refund_id, never the zeroed Redis hash.

竞态只有两种合法结果The race has two legal outcomes

领取先取得锁且锁内判定未到期，就提交 c7、扣 400，再由关闭看到 600 分并保存退款。关闭先取得锁，则状态已 expiring，哪怕 Redis 之前给过 s2 候选，领取也不能提交，退款快照保留原剩余。本章截止判定的决策点是领取持锁校验的时刻；事务可能稍后才提交，这要在产品规则中写明。调用在截止前发出、却排队到截止后才取得锁，不算赢。数据库里有些“当前时间”函数固定为事务开始时间，不能用它替代持锁后的实际时间：PostgreSQL 可在拿锁后用 clock_timestamp() 再判截止点 [12]。

If claim obtains the lock and passes the deadline check, commit c7 and debit 400; close later snapshots 600. If close obtains the lock first, expiring blocks confirmation even for an earlier Redis candidate. Eligibility is decided at the locked check; commit may finish slightly later, an explicit product rule. A predeadline request acquiring the lock after expiry loses. A transaction-start timestamp must not replace actual time read under the lock; PostgreSQL clock_timestamp() supplies actual time [12].

为什么 TTL 不能完成退款Why TTL cannot complete the refund

Redis EXPIRE 描述的是键的生存时间与删除规则 [7]，删除不是支付退款，也不是业务关闭事务。把 TTL 恰好设成 24 h，可能让退款作业先丢掉依据，初始化遗漏还会让开放包早于业务期限消失。本章开放库存不靠淘汰维持余额，到期由 UTC deadline 与 DB 裁决，关闭后才在可恢复记录仍在的前提下清理投影。缺键让 API 暂停新候选并启动恢复，不能因为“键没了”就重新创建同一总额的红包。

Redis EXPIRE concerns key lifetime and deletion [7], not a payment refund or a durable close transaction. A 24 h TTL can erase the basis before work runs, and initialization errors can delete an open projection early. Use UTC deadlines and DB arbitration, then clean up after recoverable closure. Missing keys trigger paused claims and recovery rather than recreating a packet with the original total.

refund:r9 走第四组的同一投递与账本去重路径，确认 600 分已退才将 refund_pending 转为 refund_applied，红包进入 expired。退款超时保持 expiring，领取人 400 分即使尚在 credit_pending，也不能混进发送方的 600 分退款。收口只处理未确认份额，已确认领取是一条独立履约责任。若作业积压了两小时，过期后一直不能领是因为 DB 校验仍在起作用；代价是退款迟到，要告警并恢复，不能重新开放来掩饰调度故障。

refund:r9 uses deck 4’s delivery and ledger deduplication path. After verified 600-cent refund, move pending to applied and enter expired. A timeout stays expiring. The recipient’s confirmed 400 remains a separate credit obligation even while pending. Delayed jobs cannot extend eligibility because DB deadline checks remain active; delayed refunds require recovery, not reopening the packet.

Redis 丢失之后，凭什么不重新发出已领份额Recover Redis without reissuing confirmed shares

正常恢复：先封旧工作，再取权威快照Normal recovery: fence old work before taking the snapshot

Redis 缺键、出现异常候选或切主丢了映射，Packet API 停止新候选；已有领取仍走 DB 查询。Recovery 锁红包行，把 epoch=7 改为 8，并把确认入口置 recovering，之后才读持久计划与已确认 share_id。它与 T_claim 共用同一锁：先提交的 c7 已在快照里，后来持 epoch=7 的新确认会被拒绝。快照保留 s1、s3、s4，排除 s2=400，剩余 300+100+200=600。一次重建由当前 epoch 的条件写入完成，重复执行不覆盖已经重新开放的投影。

On missing keys, invalid candidates or lost mappings, stop new candidates and query existing receipts from DB. Recovery locks the packet, advances epoch 7 to 8 and sets recovering before reading the immutable plan and confirmed share IDs. A prior c7 appears in the snapshot; later new confirmations with epoch 7 fail. Reload s1, s3 and s4, totaling 600, while excluding s2=400. Conditional epoch installation must not overwrite a projection already reopened.

Redis 返回 ready e=8 后，Recovery 再在 DB 锁内验证仍是同一 epoch、状态仍 recovering、实际时间尚未到期，才开放确认入口；再启用该版本的预扣。半途退出保持可恢复的 recovering，不允许另一实例跳过安装步骤开包。若恢复期间已经到期，改走 T_expire，按 DB 剩余生成退款，不再把 600 分开放出去。旧 API 的未提交候选被拒绝，但旧用户已经提交的 c7 查询照常成功；epoch 拦的是旧写权限，不能抹掉过去的领取事实。

After Redis reports ready e=8, re-lock DB and verify the same epoch, recovering state and actual preexpiry time before reopening confirmations and enabling reservations. A crash leaves recoverable recovering state. If time expired during recovery, close and refund durable remaining rather than reopen. Stale uncommitted candidates fail, while existing c7 receipts remain queryable: fencing changes write authority, not historical facts.

为什么副本和 WAIT 仍不能代替持久领取Why replicas and WAIT do not replace durable claims

Redis 复制原文说明默认异步复制，某些故障情况下即使等待副本确认也可能丢已确认写 [2]。WAIT 返回收到此前写入的副本数，官方同样说明它不能把 Redis 变成强一致存储，故障转移仍有丢失可能 [6]。所以“Lua 原子 + 开 AOF + WAIT”不能直接推导出资金确认不丢。这里即使 Redis 把 s2 重新放回 List，Packet DB 的份额唯一约束也会拒绝再次确认；恢复器随后按权威记录修正投影。DB 若自身回退已确认行，整个证明就失效，必须遵守 A10 或停止业务写入。

Redis replication is asynchronous, and failure scenarios can lose acknowledged writes even with replica acknowledgments [2]. WAIT reports replica receipt but does not make Redis strongly consistent or eliminate failover loss [6]. Lua plus AOF plus WAIT is therefore insufficient proof of durable money receipts. If Redis reoffers s2, DB share uniqueness rejects reuse and recovery repairs the projection. DB rollback of confirmed rows invalidates this proof, so A10 is essential.

脚本与替代：原子范围要说清，故障恢复要有终点Scripts and alternatives: define atomic scope and complete recovery

脚本原子指一个 Redis 执行窗口不可交错，跨 DB 的提交不在这个窗口里。Redis 的事务文档明确不提供回滚，并说明脚本也具有事务性的执行特征 [10]；本章不能靠“脚本失败会恢复原状”来处理已经出队的份额。写前检查参数与键类型，运行异常按疑似占位处理，持久确认仍能防重复发钱。恢复器必须有截止、进度与失败告警，停在 recovering 的包不能无限等人发现。DB-only 方案省去投影 epoch，却仍需用户/份额唯一、到期锁与资金 Outbox。

A Redis atomic execution window does not include DB commit. Redis documents lack of rollback for its transactions and transactional script execution [10]; do not assume a failed script restored a popped share. Validate arguments and key types before mutation, treating errors as potentially stranded candidates. Durable confirmation still prevents duplicate money authorization. Recovery needs progress and alerts; a database-only alternative drops projection epochs but keeps uniqueness, expiry locking and outbox.

两刀对账：业务分配与资金履约不能混算Two reconciliations: allocation and settlement are different facts

库存自检按持久数据：total_cents = confirmed_claim_cents + available_cents + refund_pending_cents + refund_applied_cents。关闭前 p9 是 1,000=400+600+0+0；退款在途是 1,000=400+0+600+0；已退是 1,000=400+0+0+600。份数另算 total_n = confirmed_claim_n + available_n + closed_unclaimed_n，本例关闭后 4=1+0+3。退款一次退三份合计，不等于发生三笔退款。Redis 投影加未完成候选才能与 DB 可用份额比较，不能把预扣当已确认金额。

Inventory uses durable records: total=confirmed claims+available+refund pending+refund applied. p9 evolves through 1,000=400+600+0+0, then 400+0+600+0, then 400+0+0+600. Count separately: total shares=confirmed+available+closed unclaimed, or 4=1+0+3 after close. One refund covers three unclaimed shares. Add outstanding candidates when comparing Redis projection with DB availability; reservations are not confirmed money allocations.

资金对账再按同一截止点、同一 operation_id 连接 Payment Ledger 的真实结果：本包资金 = 已入账领取 + 领取在途 + 仍可领取 + 退款在途 + 已退款。若账本已记 c7=400，但 ACK 丢失，不能把红包侧 pending 的同一 400 再算成在途；先核资金操作结果，每笔只归一个分类。差额要定位缺意图、丢进度、重复或错误金额，再重投原键或提交经授权的补偿流水。不要改历史 claim、重新扣发送方，也不要用“对账最终会平”替代前面的耐久承诺。

For financial reconciliation, join ledger results by operation_id at a consistent cutoff: funded amount=credited claims+pending claims+available+pending refund+refunded. If c7 already posted 400 but its ACK was lost, the sender’s pending flag must not count that same 400 again. Classify each operation once from authoritative outcomes. Investigate missing intents, progress or amounts, then replay the original key or authorized compensation; never rewrite historical claims or redebit the sender.

追问与翻车点Follow-ups and common failures

“Lua 那么快，为什么还让数据库确认？”“Why confirm in DB when Lua is fast?”

因为这里的 200 是一笔可恢复的资金分配承诺，异步复制的 Redis 回包达不到 A10。普通包可以先 DB-only；热点用 Redis 避免 9,900 个无份可领的请求去挤确认锁。若面试官要求把确认也移出同步请求，要改成“已受理、结果查询中”的协议，只有持久命令和确认结果都能恢复后才升级成领取成功，不能沿用旧的 200 语义。

Because 200 is a recoverable money allocation and Redis acknowledgment does not meet A10. Small packets may start database-only; Redis keeps thousands of unsuccessful attempts away from confirmation locks. Moving confirmation off the request requires an accepted/pending protocol and a durable command/result boundary before claiming success.

“过期已经退了，领取人还没到账呢？”“What if the refund is done but a recipient credit is pending?”

退款只覆盖关闭快照的未确认份额，已确认领取仍归领取人。例子里发送方最多收到 600，c7 的 400 必须继续投递与核实。expired 说明未领取部分退款已结束，不保证所有 credit 都完成；完整 settled 可以作为独立结算标记，等所有资金操作都有可核实终态再置位。

Refund covers only unconfirmed shares in the close snapshot. The sender receives at most 600 while c7’s 400 remains a recipient obligation. expired describes refund completion, not every credit; an independent settled flag can wait for verified terminal outcomes for all money operations.

“十万点击要分片吗？”“Do 100,000 clicks require sharding?”

先分清十万个不同包还是一个包。不同 packet_id 可按包路由，把包、计划、claims 与 Outbox 放同一片；按领取人分片会把同包唯一与过期关闭变成跨片协调。单包只有有限份额，更多分片不能凭空增加钱，先做接纳、短脚本与确认预算。要把一个热包拆成多个子库存，必须提前划定固定份额与资金上限，并设计统一关闭快照，那已是新增复杂度，不能一句“分片”跳过去。

Distinguish many packets from one hot packet. Route packet, plan, claims and outbox together by packet_id; recipient sharding makes packet uniqueness and expiry cross-shard. More shards do not add shares to one packet. Subinventory requires predetermined shares, money caps and a coordinated close snapshot rather than a one-word sharding answer.
