红包系统Red packet system

约 40 分钟 · 前置：Ch21、Ch24、Ch25、Ch41 · 6 组 / 31 帧演进图~40 min · after Ch21, Ch24, Ch25, Ch41 · 6 evolution decks / 31 frames

本章要点：金额要守恒，领取要持久，旧操作要失效Core: conserve money, persist receipts, fence old work

本章沿着同一笔 1,000 分，回答三个问题：拆成四份后，怎样保证总额不变？同一个人换了请求键，能不能再领一份？最后一次领取、到期退款和 Redis 重建，又怎样服从同一个持久裁决？六组演进图依次展开这些过程，相应代码放在机制旁边。

Follow the same 1,000 cents through three questions: how do four shares conserve the total, can a user win again with another request key, and how do the last claim, refund and Redis recovery share one durable authority? Six existing decks remain, with code beside each mechanism.

拿到候选份额、取得领取凭据、资金实际入账，是三个不同的阶段。Lua 只选出候选份额；红包数据库（Packet DB）把领取记录、剩余金额、剩余份数和待发送消息（Outbox）在同一事务中提交后，服务才返回 200；随后，资金账本（Payment Ledger）完成入账。分析故障时，要先说清楚哪一层已经提交、哪一层还在等待。

Candidate ≠ durable receipt ≠ credited funds. Lua selects a share; Packet DB commits the claim, both inventory dimensions and outbox before 200; Payment Ledger later posts the effect. Every failure branch must identify which layer has committed.

先约定成功含义、金额范围和截止时间Define success, numeric range and deadline

先设计一个份额有限的社交群红包：提前生成各份金额，确认资金已预留后再开放。Redis 负责接纳候选，数据库确认领取，Outbox 驱动后续入账。到期时，按数据库中尚未确认的剩余金额退款；Redis 丢失时，先推进代次，让旧操作失效，再重建库存投影。普通红包也可以先只用数据库，测量后再决定是否加入 Redis 投影。

Start with a finite social-group packet: open after verified funding and pre-generate amounts. Redis admits candidates, DB confirms claims, and outbox drives credit. Refund durable remaining at expiry; fence generations before rebuilding Redis. Ordinary packets may start database-only and add the projection after measurement.

These are adjustable assumptions: 2 ms is not a PostgreSQL benchmark, and three copies do not prove receipt durability. The window starts at intent creation. Unknown funding stays pending_fund; funding confirmed after the deadline enters initialization and then closes/refunds the unallocated total, rather than opening or remaining stuck.

容量估算：分清确认量、点击量和单包竞争Capacity: separate confirmations, attempts and contention

每天确认的份额为 1,000,000×10=10,000,000，平均每秒 10,000,000÷86,400≈115.74 次，十倍峰值约为每秒 1,157 次。每天的点击量为 50,000,000，平均每秒约 578.70 次，峰值约为每秒 5,787 次。点击中还包括红包已抢完、重复请求和无资格请求，不能让它们全部进入等待确认锁的队列。

10,000,000 confirmations/day gives 115.74/s average and about 1,157/s at 10× peak. 50,000,000 attempts/day gives 578.70/s average and about 5,787/s peak. Sold-out, repeated and ineligible attempts should not all become confirmation lock waits.

单包热点要另行分析，不把它加到全国峰值上。1 万人抢 100 份，至少有 10,000−100=9,900 人领不到，占 99%。若一次串行确认耗时 2 ms，理论能力就是每秒 500 次；按 60% 利用率规划，预算为每秒 300 次。每秒接纳 100 个新候选，占理论能力的 20%。即便如此，仍要限制排队长度、连接数和锁等待，存储出现抖动时还应降低接纳门限。Lua 执行得快，不能消除持久确认的串行瓶颈。

The separate hot burst leaves at least 9,900 users, or 99%, without a share. A 2 ms serial service assumption implies 500/s theoretical, 300/s at 60% planning, with 100/s admission using 20% of theoretical service. Bound queues, connections and lock waits; lower admission during stalls. Fast Lua does not remove durable serialization.

存储需要多少，还有哪些尚未计入Storage arithmetic and exclusions

每天的元信息为 100 万×1 KB=1 GB，领取记录为 1,000 万×200 B=2 GB。七天的原始记录共 (1+2)×7=21 GB，三份拷贝为 63 GB。金额数组仅本体就至少需要 100 万×10×8 B=80 MB/天，尚未计入 share_id、Redis 结构、Outbox/inbox、索引、WAL、备份和未结束的资金记录。这些数字只用于估算容量；多存几份并不能代替对已确认领取的持久保证。

Metadata is 1 GB/day; claims are 2 GB/day. Seven days gives 21 GB raw and 63 GB for three copies. Amount payload alone is 80 MB/day. Share IDs, Redis structures, outbox/inbox, indexes, WAL, backups and unresolved financial records are extra. Capacity copies cannot establish durable acknowledgment.

先为红包预留资金，再开放领取Reserve funding for the packet before opening

创建请求键以发送方为作用域，并绑定总额、币种、份数、群、模式和 deadline。同键同载荷的重试返回原 packet_id，载荷改变则报冲突；并发创建由非空唯一约束保证只保留一个结果。服务先把不可变计划、pending_fund 状态和 fund:p9 Outbox 同事务保存，再返回 202。重试时读取既有计划，不能重新随机拆分，也不能再次扣款。

Bind a sender-scoped create key to amount, currency, count, group, mode and deadline. Identical replay returns the original packet_id; changed payload conflicts; non-null uniqueness settles concurrent inserts. Commit the immutable plan, pending_fund and fund:p9 outbox before 202. Retry loads the existing plan instead of drawing or debiting again.

资金侧把发送方的可用余额转入本包的托管资金池 escrow，并在同一事务中提交业务去重记录和平衡分录，这才真正预留了资金。红包侧核验资金单身份、币种、金额和 APPLIED 结果后，才记录资金已确认，进入初始化状态（funded/initializing）。Redis 初始化完成后才能开放；重复安装不能覆盖已经消费过的 List。资金确认与投影就绪分别依赖本地提交，两者之间暂时不可领取。

The ledger moves sender availability into packet escrow, committing deduplication and balanced entries together. That is the funding reservation. Verify operation identity, currency, amount and APPLIED before funded/initializing. Open after installation without resetting a consumed list on replay. Funding and projection readiness involve separate local commits and an availability window.

HTTP 超时只能说明结果未知。服务应继续查询或重投 fund:p9，不能换支付键，也不能猜测成功后开放红包。Outbox 与业务状态在同一数据库事务中提交，使发送进程退出后仍有资金意图可供恢复 [3]。WAL 说明了先写日志、再写数据页的持久化基础 [11]，但单靠 WAL，不能保证异步复制后的主节点切换不丢失已确认结果。

HTTP timeout leaves an unknown result: query or replay fund:p9 without a new debit or speculative opening. Committing outbox with state closes the sender-crash gap [3]. WAL explains logging before data pages [11], not lossless asynchronous promotion.

整数拆分：每取一份，都为后面的份额留足最低金额Integer splitting: preserve the minimum for every later share

设剩余金额为 M、剩余份数为 N、最低每份金额为 m，始终保持 M≥N×m。当 N>1 时，先算上界 U=min(⌊2M/N⌋, M−m×(N−1))，再抽取整数 a∈[m,U]。由于取值不超过上界，更新后仍有 M−a≥m×(N−1)，足以覆盖后面每份的最低金额。N=1 时直接取尽剩余金额。按这个过程逐步推下去，就能证明每份都≥m，且总和恰好等于原总额；图中使用 m=1。

For remaining amount M, count N and minimum m, preserve M≥N×m. For N>1 draw integer a∈[m,U], where U=min(floor(2M/N),M−m×(N−1)). The next remainder still covers every later minimum. Exhaust the last share, proving per-share minimum and conservation by induction. The diagram uses m=1.

public static List<Share> split(long total, int count, long minimum,
                                boolean even, RandomGenerator random) {
    if (count < 1 || count > 100 || minimum < 1 ||
        total > MAX_CENTS || total < 1 || minimum > total / count)
        throw new IllegalArgumentException("invalid total/count/minimum");
    var plan = new ArrayList<Share>();
    long money = total;
    for (int left = count; left > 0; left--) {
        long amount;
        if (even) {
            amount = total / count + (count - left < total % count ? 1 : 0);
        } else if (left == 1) {
            amount = money;
        } else {
            long upper = Math.min(2 * money / left,
                                  money - minimum * (left - 1));
            amount = random.nextLong(minimum, upper + 1);
        }
        plan.add(new Share(count - left + 1, amount));
        money -= amount; // money >= minimum * (left - 1)
    }
    if (money != 0 || plan.stream().mapToLong(Share::cents).sum() != total)
        throw new IllegalStateException("non-conserving plan");
    for (int i = plan.size() - 1; i > 0; i--)
        Collections.swap(plan, i, random.nextInt(i + 1));
    return List.copyOf(plan); // persist once; retries must load this plan
}

完整的 SplitPlan.java 可用 Java 17 运行，无需第三方依赖。总额上限 10¹² 分保证 2×money 和 minimum×(left−1) 不会溢出 long；校验最低金额时先比较 total/count，避免校验本身的乘法溢出。测试注入固定随机种子，便于复现；真实随机源和可审计抽签需要另外约定。nextLong(origin,bound) 不包含上界 bound [14]。

The complete SplitPlan.java runs on Java 17 without dependencies. The 10¹² cap keeps intermediate products within long; checking minimum against total/count avoids validation overflow. Tests inject fixed seeds for reproducibility; real randomness and auditable lotteries require a separate contract. nextLong(origin,bound) excludes bound [14].

图中的上界依次为 500、466、299，前三份取 300、400、100，最后剩下 200，合计 1,000 分。若把 1,003 分均分为四份，则得到 251、251、251、250。先固定 share_id 及其金额，再打乱位置，份额身份与金额的对应关系不会改变；重建时也只能加载原计划。均匀置换使固定计划中每个位置的期望金额为 total/N，但不代表所有合法拆分都等概率，也不能消除网络时延和接纳机制带来的机会差异。

The bounds are 500, 466 and 299; draws 300, 400 and 100 leave final 200, totaling 1,000. Even splitting 1,003 yields 251,251,251,250. Shuffle fixed share IDs without changing amounts and recover only from that plan. Uniform permutation equalizes positional expectation within the plan, not all legal partitions or network/admission opportunity.

Lua 选出候选，数据库提交后才发领取凭据Lua selects candidates; a durable transaction issues receipts

候选操作的原子范围：一次 Redis 脚本执行Candidate atomic scope: one Redis execution window

入口先鉴权、限流，再查询该用户的持久领取记录；已有结果时优先返回。下面是可在 Redis 7 执行的教学脚本：KEYS 显式传入 meta/list/amounts/users 四个键，它们使用同一个 {p9} hash tag，落在同一槽中 [1][5]；ARGV 中的用户和 epoch 由服务端提供。同一用户复用候选；缺键、类型错误或库存异常时触发恢复。Redis 时间只用于预过滤，是否真正过了领取截止时间，最终由数据库判定。

Authenticate, gate, then prefer the user’s durable receipt. This Redis 7 teaching script explicitly receives meta/list/amounts/users keys sharing {p9} [1][5]. The server supplies user and epoch. Reuse a user candidate; missing keys, bad types or inventory trigger recovery. Redis time is an advisory filter; DB decides final eligibility.

-- KEYS: meta, list, immutable amounts, user->share; all share one hash tag.
-- ARGV: trusted expected epoch, authenticated user; no client amount.
local function integer(s)
  local n = tonumber(s)
  if not n or n ~= math.floor(n) or n < 0 or n > 9007199254740991 then return nil end
  return n
end
for i, want in ipairs({'hash','list','hash','hash'}) do
  local t = redis.call('TYPE', KEYS[i]).ok
  if t ~= want and not (t == 'none' and (i == 2 or i == 4)) then
    return {'RECOVER', 'key type or missing projection'}
  end
end
local m = redis.call('HMGET', KEYS[1], 'epoch','status','deadline_ms','n','cents')
local epoch, deadline, n, cents = integer(m[1]), integer(m[3]), integer(m[4]), integer(m[5])
if not epoch or not deadline or not n or n > 100 or not cents or cents > 1000000000000 then
  return {'RECOVER','bad meta'}
end
if m[1] ~= ARGV[1] or m[2] ~= 'ACTIVE' then return {'RETRY','epoch or gate'} end
local old = redis.call('HGET', KEYS[4], ARGV[2])
if old then
  local amount = integer(redis.call('HGET', KEYS[3], old))
  if not amount or amount < 1 then return {'RECOVER','bad prior candidate'} end
  return {'CANDIDATE',old,tostring(amount),m[1]}
end
local now = redis.call('TIME')
if tonumber(now[1])*1000 + math.floor(tonumber(now[2])/1000) >= deadline then
  return {'EXPIRED'} -- advisory only; Packet DB makes the final time decision
end
if n == 0 then
  if cents ~= 0 or redis.call('LLEN',KEYS[2]) ~= 0 then return {'RECOVER','bad empty'} end
  return {'EMPTY'}
end
local sid = redis.call('LINDEX', KEYS[2], 0)
local amount = sid and integer(redis.call('HGET', KEYS[3], sid))
if not amount or amount < 1 or cents < amount or redis.call('LLEN',KEYS[2]) ~= n then
  return {'RECOVER','bad inventory'}
end
-- Validation precedes mutations. A runtime error still does not roll them back.
redis.call('LPOP', KEYS[2])
redis.call('HSET', KEYS[1], 'n', tostring(n-1), 'cents', tostring(cents-amount))
redis.call('HSET', KEYS[4], ARGV[2], sid)
return {'CANDIDATE',sid,tostring(amount),m[1]} -- never a durable receipt

Lua 执行期间不会与其他命令交错，但这不表示运行错误会回滚，也不包括数据库提交 [10]。脚本异常可能留下已预留却未确认的份额。此时不能因为超时就用 LPush 放回队列，因为另一个 API 进程仍可能确认它。应先推进 epoch，再通过重建回收这些份额；在此之前，可能暂时少领，但数据库中的剩余金额仍可作为退款依据。Redis 的剩余量指“尚未预扣”，数据库的剩余量指“尚未确认”，在途候选会让两者暂时不同。

Noninterleaving Lua is neither rollback nor DB atomicity [10]. An error may strand a reservation. Never push it back merely after timeout while another API might confirm it. Fence and rebuild to recover availability; durable remaining still supports refunds. Redis tracks unreserved shares; DB tracks unconfirmed shares, with candidates explaining the difference.

领取事务 T_claim：分别约束请求键和业务身份T_claim: request identity and business uniqueness

claim(authenticatedUser, packet, requestKey, internalCandidate):
  authorize membership; never accept a browser-supplied share or amount
  BEGIN READ COMMITTED                         // one Packet DB transaction
  INSERT request(user, key, packet) ON CONFLICT(user, key) DO NOTHING
  r = SELECT request(user, key) FOR UPDATE
  if r.packet != packet: ROLLBACK; return KEY_CONFLICT
  if r.claim_id exists: COMMIT; return its durable receipt
  p = SELECT packets(packet) FOR UPDATE
  c = SELECT claims WHERE packet_id=packet AND user_id=user
  if c exists: bind request to c; COMMIT; return c
  require p.state=OPEN and p.ready and p.epoch=internalCandidate.epoch
  require SELECT clock_timestamp() < p.expire_at // after obtaining the lock
  s = SELECT immutable shares(packet, internalCandidate.share_id, p.plan_version)
  require s exists and no claim already uses s.id
  INSERT claim(c7, packet, user, s.id, s.cents)
    // NOT NULL + UNIQUE(packet,user) + UNIQUE(packet,share)
  UPDATE packets SET remaining_n=remaining_n-1,
    remaining_cents=remaining_cents-s.cents
    WHERE packet_id=packet AND remaining_n>0 AND remaining_cents>=s.cents
  require affected_rows=1
  INSERT outbox(credit:c7, packet, user, currency, s.cents) // frozen payload
  UPDATE request SET claim_id=c7 WHERE user=user AND key=requestKey
  COMMIT; return 200 c7, CREDIT_PENDING
  // Any failed check/write aborts the entire transaction.
  // A lost COMMIT reply means UNKNOWN: query authority with this user/key.

这段伪代码使用 PostgreSQL READ COMMITTED，明确了加锁顺序：先锁请求键行，再锁红包行，两个锁都保持到事务结束 [4]。request 表中的 (user,key) 必须非空且唯一，并绑定一个 packet；claims 表再分别约束 (packet,user) 和 (packet,share) 唯一 [8]。因此，同一用户用同一键并发请求，只会得到一个结果；换键也不能再领一份；把旧键改绑另一红包则报冲突。份额来自内部候选，金额从不可变计划读取，浏览器不能指定领取金额。

The pseudocode makes the PostgreSQL READ COMMITTED order explicit: request row, then packet row, held through transaction end [4]. Non-null (user,key) uniqueness binds the request to a packet; separate (packet,user) and (packet,share) uniqueness protects the business [8]. Same-key races converge, rotated keys cannot win again, and rebinding a user’s key to another packet conflicts. Candidates are internal; amounts come from the immutable plan.

扣减 400 分、保存 claim、绑定 request 和写入 credit Outbox，只要一步失败，整个事务就回滚。若提交回复丢失，应显示“结果查询中”，用原用户和请求键查询权威数据库。只能按已识别的唯一约束冲突处理重复，不能把任意数据库错误都当作成功。持锁期间不调用资金 RPC，也不等待消息投递。已有领取在红包到期或重建后仍可查询；新候选则必须通过当前状态、epoch 和锁内实际时间的检查。

Any failure rolls back the 400-cent debit, claim, request binding and outbox together. A lost commit reply stays query-pending and resolves by the same user/key at authority. Handle identified unique conflicts without interpreting arbitrary DB failures as success. No payment RPC or delivery wait belongs under the lock. Existing receipts survive expiry/recovery; new ones must pass state, epoch and actual locked-time checks.

同一资金池：预留的资金怎样用于入账和退款One pool funds reservation, credit and refund

下面的教学伪代码约定了账本事务如何处理资金。命令必须来自已提交、不可改的 Outbox，并经过服务身份校验；客户端自行编造一个新键，并不会获得转移资金的权限。credit:c7 绑定本包、u42、CNY 和 400 分，refund:p9 则绑定关闭快照和发送方。资金侧还要保证同一业务效果唯一，且资金池余额不被扣成负数。

This teaching ledger transaction consumes authenticated, immutable committed outbox commands. A caller cannot authorize money movement merely by inventing a key. credit:c7 binds packet, u42, CNY and 400 cents; refund:p9 binds the close snapshot and sender. The receiver also enforces effect uniqueness and the escrow lower bound.

apply(verifiedPacketCommand):                  // authenticated service, frozen grant
  require positive integer cents, matching currency and canonical operation ID
  BEGIN                                      // one Payment Ledger transaction
  if kind == FUND: SELECT sender wallet(currency) FOR UPDATE
  else: e = SELECT escrow(packet) FOR UPDATE  // credits and refund share this lock
  old = SELECT inbox(operation_id)
  if old exists:
    require old.payload == command.payload   // mismatch is a conflict
    COMMIT; return old.result
  if kind == FUND:
    require no escrow already exists for packet
    debit sender available balance only if balance >= cents
    INSERT escrow(packet, sender, currency, total=cents, available=cents)
  else:
    require command matches the committed claim or close grant
    require credit recipient OR refund sender matches that grant
    require escrow.available >= cents
    UPDATE escrow SET available=available-cents
    increment the bound recipient/sender balance
  INSERT both balanced journal entries        // debit + credit sum to zero
  INSERT inbox(operation_id, complete payload, APPLIED)
  COMMIT; return APPLIED
  // Any failure rolls back balances, entries and inbox together.
  // Timeout at Relay: keep the obligation; query/replay the same operation ID.

T_claim 和 T_pay 是两个本地事务。账本提交后、Relay 记录进度前，Outbox 仍可能重复投递 [3]。因此，账本内的去重记录、余额变化、完整的两条平衡分录和 APPLIED 结果，必须一起提交，失败时一起回滚。同键更改金额或收款人会报冲突。Relay 只能根据与原命令匹配的权威结果更新进度，不能把投递标记当成已入账的证据。真实的身份校验、资金渠道和受限账本写入权限仍是实现依赖，伪代码中的参数名本身不提供这些能力。

T_claim and T_pay are separate local transactions; outbox can redeliver between ledger commit and Relay checkpoint [3]. Deduplication, balances, the complete entry pair and APPLIED commit together or roll back together. Changed amount/recipient conflicts. Relay updates progress only from a matching authoritative outcome. Authentication, channels and restricted ledger permissions are dependencies, not implemented by a parameter name.

如果先退回尚未领取的 600 分，escrow 中仍应留下 400 分，用来履行已确认的领取 c7。即使红包已是 expired，这笔入账也要继续处理。超时后，原有的入账责任仍然保留，服务应查询或重投原键。若收到明确的业务拒绝，或自动重试次数耗尽，就进入审查流程，不能把已属于领取人的钱自动退给发送方。监控时要看未决金额，以及最久一笔已经等待了多久。

Refunding the unclaimed 600 first leaves 400 in escrow for confirmed c7. Credit must continue even after packet expiry. Timeout retains the obligation and original identity. Definite business rejection or exhausted automatic retries requires review, never an automatic sender refund of a recipient’s confirmed share. Monitor unresolved amount and oldest age.

Stripe API v1 提供了一个保留期限有限的外部例子：端点开始执行后，它保存首次状态码和响应体，包括 500；同键重试时比较参数，至少 24 h 后可以清理，清理后复用该键会成为新请求 [9]。API v2 则可能继续处理失败或部分失败的工作，并更新响应。本章内部的资金业务去重必须覆盖旧命令仍可能执行的完整期间，不能直接采用 v1 的最短窗口，也不能随七天展示记录一起删除。

Stripe API v1 is a finite-retention example: after execution starts it saves the first status/body, including 500, compares parameters and permits pruning after at least 24 h; reuse after pruning starts a new request [9]. API v2 may continue failed/partial work and update the response. Our internal financial deduplication must outlive executable old commands, rather than copy the v1 minimum or seven-day display retention.

最后一次领取与到期竞争：怎样确定退款范围Last claim versus expiry: snapshot, close, then refund

调度器 Scheduler 负责唤醒，按到期索引扫描则补上漏掉的任务。关闭事务 T_expire 与领取事务 T_claim 锁住同一条 packets 记录：如果领取先拿到锁，并在尚未到期时通过检查完成确认，对应金额就不会计入后续退款快照；如果关闭先提交，旧 Lua 候选也不能再确认。能否领取，以持锁校验时刻为准，提交可以稍晚。截止前发出的请求，如果截止后才拿到锁，仍不算领取成功。因此，PostgreSQL 要在拿锁后读取 clock_timestamp()，不能用固定在事务开始时刻的 now() [12]。

Scheduler wakes work and a due-index scan covers missed wakeups. T_expire locks the same packet as T_claim. A predeadline locked claim is excluded from the later refund snapshot; a committed close rejects earlier Lua candidates. Eligibility is decided at the locked check, even if commit finishes later. A predeadline request acquiring the lock after expiry loses. Use post-lock clock_timestamp(), not transaction-start now() [12].

close(packet):
  BEGIN                                      // same Packet DB and lock as claim
  p = SELECT packets(packet) FOR UPDATE
  if p.state in {EXPIRING, EXPIRED}: COMMIT; return original refund result
  if p.state not in {INITIALIZING, OPEN, RECOVERING}: ROLLBACK; return NOT_FUNDED
  if SELECT clock_timestamp() < p.expire_at: ROLLBACK; return TOO_EARLY
  amount = p.remaining_cents; count = p.remaining_n
  UPDATE packets SET ready=false, remaining_cents=0, remaining_n=0,
    closed_unclaimed_n=count, refund_pending=amount,
    state=(amount>0 ? EXPIRING : EXPIRED)
  if amount>0:
    INSERT outbox(refund:packet, packet, sender, currency, amount)
  COMMIT                                     // snapshot and intent survive together
  disable Redis projection for this epoch    // best effort; DB already rejects
  // A matching verified APPLIED result moves pending -> applied, then EXPIRED.
  // An unknown result leaves EXPIRING; confirmed recipient credits still run.

c7 已确认领取 400 分后，关闭事务先保存剩余 600 分和三份未领的快照，再将 available 置零。此时，1,000=400 已确认+0 可领+600 待退。同一事务还保存固定身份 refund:p9，也就是图中简写的 r9；重试始终读取这份快照。若全部领完，就不发送零金额资金命令。initializing 或 recovering 状态的红包到期，也通过同一关闭流程处理；资金结果仍未知的 pending_fund，则先查清原资金操作。

After c7 confirms 400, snapshot 600 cents and three unclaimed shares before zeroing availability: 1,000=400 confirmed+0 available+600 refund pending. Persist refund:p9, the business identity abbreviated r9 in the diagram, in the same transaction. Retries read that snapshot. Fully claimed packets create no zero-value command. Expired initializing/recovering packets close through the same gate; pending_fund first resolves its original funding operation.

退款结果未知时保持 expiring；只有核验到与原操作匹配的 APPLIED，才把退款金额从 pending 转为 applied，并置为 expired。expired 并不保证所有领取款都已到账，完整结清需要另设 settled 标记。Redis EXPIRE 只负责删除到期键 [7]，不会替业务关闭红包或退款。开放期间的库存应禁止淘汰，等关闭并完成对账后再清理。

Unknown refund remains expiring; a matching APPLIED converts pending to applied and marks expired. This does not prove every recipient was credited; settled is separate. Redis EXPIRE only deletes keys [7], never closes the business or refunds money. Protect open inventory from eviction and clean up after closure/reconciliation.

Redis 重建：先让旧代失效，再安装不可变快照Redis recovery: fence the old generation, install the snapshot

Redis 缺键或回退时，先暂停新候选，已持久确认的领取仍可查询。恢复器与领取事务共用数据库行锁，先把代次从 e=7 推进到 8，并置为 recovering，再读取“原计划减去已确认 share_id”。这样，已提交的 s2=400 会被排除；旧 API 进程尚未提交的候选，也会在最终数据库检查时因代次过旧而被拒绝。

Pause new candidates on Redis loss or rollback while durable receipt lookup continues. Recovery shares the DB row lock, advances e=7 to 8 and enters recovering before reading plan minus committed share IDs. Committed s2=400 is excluded; an old API’s uncommitted candidate is fenced at final DB confirmation.

rebuild(packet):
  BEGIN
  p = SELECT packets(packet) FOR UPDATE
  if p.state not in {OPEN, RECOVERING, INITIALIZING}: abort
  if p.expire_at exists and actual DB time >= p.expire_at: abort; run close
  epoch = p.epoch + 1; persist state=RECOVERING, ready=false, epoch
  snapshot = immutable plan MINUS committed share IDs
  require snapshot.count=p.remaining_n and snapshot.sum=p.remaining_cents
  persist token = digest(packet, epoch, plan_version, ordered snapshot, deadline)
  COMMIT                                      // old confirmations are now fenced
  install into pkt:{packet}:e:epoch:{meta,list,amounts,users}
    // One Lua call, every key supplied in KEYS; at most 100 shares.
    // Missing meta: validate all input/types, install snapshot with status=READY.
    // Existing identical token: return its status without replacing any list.
    // Existing different token: reject; never overwrite an ACTIVE generation.
  BEGIN
  p = SELECT packets(packet) FOR UPDATE
  require p.epoch=epoch and p.token=token and p.state=RECOVERING
  if actual DB time >= p.expire_at: ROLLBACK; run close; return
  verify READY(epoch, token); persist state=OPEN, ready=true
  COMMIT
  activate Redis only if epoch/token match     // retry if crash follows DB commit
  // Older installers use older namespaces; old API writes fail the DB epoch.

每一代使用独立键名，但保留同一个 hash tag，例如 pkt:{p9}:e:8:meta。安装 token 绑定代次、计划、未领份额的有序快照和原 deadline。同 token 重投安装时，脚本只返回当前状态，绝不重新填满已经消费过的 List。迟到的 e=7 安装只能访问 e=7 的键空间，不能覆盖 e=8。数据库重新开放前，还要核对代次、token、ready 和实际时间；如果已经到期，就关闭并退款。对应的 安装 Lua 和 激活 Lua 见本章示例。

Each generation has separate key names with one hash tag, such as pkt:{p9}:e:8:meta. The token binds epoch, plan, ordered unclaimed snapshot and original deadline. Same-token installation only returns existing status and never refills a consumed list. A late e=7 installer touches only its own namespace. Recheck epoch, token, readiness and actual time before reopening DB; close/refund if expired. The install and activation Lua remain in the examples.

Redis 默认采用异步复制 [2]。即使用 WAIT 等待副本确认，也不会因此变成强一致系统，主节点切换时仍可能丢失已确认写入 [6]。假如投影回退，又把 s2 发给另一个人，数据库的份额唯一约束会拒绝再次确认。这个保证依赖权威数据库不回退已确认行；无法保证时，就要停止写入。恢复期间崩溃可能导致暂时不可领取，因此还需监控恢复状态已持续多久，以及重试是否在推进。

Redis defaults to asynchronous replication [2]; WAIT does not make it strongly consistent or eliminate failover loss [6]. DB share uniqueness blocks a rolled-back projection from reconfirming s2. That proof requires an authority that preserves acknowledged claims; stop writes otherwise. Recovery crashes may reduce availability, so monitor state age and progress.

对账：先核金额与份数，再核资金进度Two reconciliation equations

先核对业务分配金额：total=confirmed_claims+available+refund_pending+refund_applied。p9 依次经历 1,000=400+600+0+0、400+0+600+0、400+0+0+600。份数要另算，关闭后为 4=1 已领+0 可领+3 关包未领。

资金对账则要按同一截止时点的快照，用 operation_id 对齐两侧记录，再把每笔金额归入已入账、领取在途、仍可领、退款在途或已退款中的一类，每笔只计算一次。即使 ACK 丢失，也不能把已经入账的 400 分，再当作另一笔 pending 400 分计入。

Allocation: total=confirmed claims+available+refund pending+refund applied. p9 moves through 400+600+0+0, 400+0+600+0 and 400+0+0+600. Reconcile count separately: 4=1 claimed+0 available+3 closed unclaimed. Financial reconciliation joins operation IDs at one consistent cutoff, classifying each amount once as credited, pending claim, available, pending refund or refunded. Lost ACK must not double-count posted 400 as another pending 400.

方案取舍与一分钟复盘Trade-offs and a one-minute recap

普通群红包可以先只用数据库，省去安装投影和故障重建的复杂度。不同红包按 packet_id 分区，把红包、份额、claims 和 Outbox 放在同一分片。单个热包的资金总量不会因为随机分片而增加，应先限制接纳量，并使用有界队列。如果还要拆成多个子库存，就必须预先分配固定份额和资金上限，再设计统一的关闭快照。

Start ordinary packets database-only to avoid projection installation/recovery. Partition different packets by packet_id, colocating plan, claims and outbox. Random sharding cannot add money to one hot packet; prefer admission and bounded queues. Subinventory needs predetermined shares, funding caps and coordinated close snapshots.

一分钟可以这样讲：先保存各份金额和身份都固定的整数拆分计划，再把 1,000 分预留到本包 escrow。Lua 预扣 s2 的 400 分，T_claim 提交后才给用户领取凭据 c7，后续资金操作沿用原键。关闭时只退尚未确认领取的 600 分，c7 的 400 分仍须入账。Redis 丢失后，先推进 epoch，再按原计划排除已领份额来重建；旧候选和旧安装都不能绕过当前权威数据库的检查。

One minute: reserve 1,000 cents in packet escrow and persist integer share identities. Lua reserves s2=400; T_claim yields c7, whose money operation keeps its original key. Close refunds only unconfirmed 600 while c7 remains owed. On Redis loss, advance epoch and rebuild the original plan minus claims. Stale candidates and installers cannot overrule current authority.

验证材料和运行方式见 本章示例，其中包括 Java 17 纯函数检查、真实 SQLite 事务与多连接竞争，以及隔离 Redis 环境中的 Lua 检查。SQLite 的写者锁比 PostgreSQL 行锁更粗 [13]；这些检查不能证明 PostgreSQL 切主的行为、真实资金渠道的行为或生产吞吐能力。

See chapter examples for Java 17 pure-function checks, real SQLite transactions/multiple connections, and isolated Redis Lua. SQLite uses a coarser writer lock [13]; these checks do not establish PostgreSQL failover, real payment-channel behavior or production throughput.
